# Local Culture Briefing Skill

Give travelers the cultural knowledge to show up with respect and confidence.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

Ask if not provided:
- **Destination**: Country, region, or city
- **Purpose**: Tourism / Business / Living there / Meeting locals / Dating
- **Specific concerns**: Anything they've heard and want to verify?
- **Background**: Are they from a very different cultural context? (affects what needs more explanation)
