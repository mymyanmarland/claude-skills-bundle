# Gift Idea Generator Skill

Generate thoughtful, personalized gift ideas that actually land.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

Ask in one message if not provided:
- **Who is the recipient**: Age, gender (optional), relationship to giver (friend, parent, partner, colleague, etc.)
- **Occasion**: Birthday / Anniversary / Holiday / Wedding / Baby shower / Graduation / Thank you / Just because / Other
- **Budget range**: Under $25 / $25–50 / $50–100 / $100–250 / $250+
- **Their personality & interests**: Hobbies, passions, lifestyle, things they talk about
- **What NOT to get**: Things they already have, don't like, or you've given before
- **Any constraints**: Lives far away (shipping) / Minimalist / Eco-conscious / Dietary restrictions (for food gifts)

### 🎀 Presentation Tips

**How to make any gift feel special**:
- Handwrite a card — in the age of texts, this stands out enormously
- Match wrapping to the person's aesthetic (minimalist = kraft paper; maximalist = bright)

- Include a note explaining WHY you chose this for THEM — the most meaningful part of any gift

## Step 3 — Occasion-Specific Guides

### 🎂 Birthday
```
**Birthday gift principles**:
- Best gifts reflect who THEY are, not who you are
- For milestone birthdays (30, 40, 50): something more meaningful or experiential
- For children: experiences and consumables often better than more toys
- If unsure: ask their closest friend or family member — not the recipient

**Ideas by age**:
- Kids (5–12): [Age-appropriate experiences, creative kits, books in series they love]
- Teens (13–17): [Tech accessories, experiences with friends, hobby gear]
- Young adults (18–25): [Experiences, subscriptions, items for their new life stage]
- Adults (26–40): [Upgrade to something they have but entry-level, experiences, home]
- Midlife (40–60): [Quality over quantity, time-saving, experiences, hobby deepening]
- 60+: [Comfort, memory-making, connecting with family, hobby support]
```

### 💍 Anniversary
```
**Anniversary gift tiers**:
- 1st year: Paper / modern: Clock — [Framed memory, custom book of your year, experiences]
- 5th year: Wood — [Custom cutting board, meaningful keepsake box, travel experience]
- 10th year: Tin/Aluminum — [Engraved item, major experience, jewelry]
- 25th year: Silver — [Silver jewelry, luxury experience, significant trip]
- 50th year: Gold — [Gold jewelry, family gathering, legacy gift]

**For any anniversary**:
- Experiences > objects (dinner at a meaningful place, trip somewhere on the bucket list)
- Recreate your first date / proposal
- Custom gift referencing your shared history
```

### 🎓 Graduation
```
**Graduation gift ideas by life stage**:
- High school → college: Dorm essentials (elevated), experience for the summer, financial gift
- College → first job: Professional wardrobe piece, work bag, experience before starting work
- Professional degree (law/medicine/MBA): Quality item for their new career, celebration experience
- PhD/advanced degree: Something acknowledging the sacrifice, experience of joy
```

### 👶 Baby Shower / New Parent
```
**What new parents actually need** (vs. what they receive 10 of):
- Meal delivery subscription (first 2 months of meals = gold)
- House cleaning service (2–3 sessions)
- Postpartum care for the mother (often overlooked vs. baby gifts)
- Practical items: white noise machine, swaddle blankets, nursing pillow
- Not useful: stuffed animals, decorative items, overly complex gadgets

**Budget guide**:
- Under $30: Swaddles, baby book, care kit
- $30–75: White noise machine, meal delivery card, baby carrier
- $75–150: Meal service subscription, premium baby monitor accessory, experience
- $150+: Experience for the parents (date night fund), major registry item
```

### 🎄 Holiday / Christmas
```
**Holiday gift strategies**:

**For the person who has everything**:
- Experience they'd never book themselves
- Donation to a cause they care about (in their name)
- Something consumable and premium (exceptional olive oil, rare whisky, artisan chocolate)
- A skill: class, workshop, lesson

**Secret Santa / White Elephant** ($20–30):
- Fancy snack or drink set
- Unique candle or diffuser
- Fun game or activity
- High-quality consumable (coffee, tea, hot sauce set)
- Novelty item tied to a shared office joke

**Last minute gifts** (can be arranged quickly):
- Digital gift cards (instant delivery)
- Experience bookings (many available same-day)
- Subscription gift (streaming, food delivery, audiobooks)
- Handwritten letter + promise of experience to come
```

## Step 4 — Gift-Giving Rules

```
## 💡 Universal Gift Wisdom

**The best gifts**:
✅ Show you listened — reference something they mentioned months ago
✅ Give them something they want but wouldn't buy themselves
✅ Reflect their personality, not yours
✅ Come with a thoughtful card explaining the choice

**Avoid**:
❌ Gifts that imply they need to change (diet books, exercise equipment unless they asked)
❌ Regifting obviously
❌ Gift cards to places they don't shop (lazy + impersonal)
❌ Anything requiring the recipient to do significant work to enjoy

**When budget is tight**:
- Time is the most valuable gift: cook a meal, offer to help with something
- Handwritten letter of appreciation > $15 novelty item
- Group gifting: combine with others for something meaningful

**The card matters more than you think**:
Research shows people remember the card longer than the gift.
Write something specific: not "Happy Birthday!" but "I got you this because I remember
you mentioning X and it reminded me of how much you [specific quality]."
```

Always offer: "Want me to help you write the card message too?" or "Want ideas for wrapping or presentation?"
