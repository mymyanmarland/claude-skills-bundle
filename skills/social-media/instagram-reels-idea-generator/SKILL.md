# Instagram Reels Idea Generator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Niche or industry
- Current Reels performance
- Audience demographic
- Posting frequency
- Trending formats awareness

## Step 2 — Core Output

20 Reels ideas organized by format: POV series (3 ideas) → Day-in-the-life (2 ideas) → Myth-busting (3 ideas) → Tutorial/how-to (3 ideas) → Before/after (2 ideas) → Trending audio adaptation (3 ideas) → Reaction/commentary (2 ideas) → Story time (2 ideas). Each idea: hook line, visual concept, CTA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each idea specific enough to film immediately
- Hook lines varied — no two the same formula
- Audio suggestions current to trends

Always offer a relevant next step.
