# Platform-Specific Video Hook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Video topic
- Platform
- Target audience
- Key insight or hook angle
- Video length

## Step 2 — Core Output

5 hook options per platform. TikTok: pattern interrupt → visual action described → spoken hook line. YouTube: thumbnail moment → spoken hook → what they'll gain. Reels: movement cue → spoken hook → text overlay. Each hook: under 5 seconds, addresses viewer's WIIFM, creates open loop. Performance prediction per hook.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each hook specific to platform behavior
- Open loop created — viewer needs to continue
- No intro of self or channel in first 3 seconds

Always offer a relevant next step.
