# Social Media Crisis Response Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What happened
- Brand responsibility level
- Audience reaction
- Response timeline
- Tone needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Response kit: Acknowledgment tweet/post (under 280 chars — within first hour) → Full response statement (public comment, 100-150 words) → DM response template (take it private) → Internal team FAQ → Follow-up post (24-48 hours — action taken). Each response authentic, non-defensive, acknowledges impact.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acknowledgment response within 2 hours of incident
- No deflection or blame in public responses
- Follow-up shows concrete action taken

Always offer a relevant next step.
