# Live Video Script Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Live video purpose
- Duration
- Platform
- Audience size
- Interactive elements: Q&A/polls/guests

## Step 2 — Core Output

Live script: Pre-live checklist → Opening hook (first 60 seconds — most important for retention) → Agenda announcement → Content segments with time estimates → Engagement prompts (questions to ask audience every 5-7 minutes) → Q&A management script → Closing CTA → Technical cue notes in brackets. Conversational language.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening hook designed for people joining late
- Engagement prompts every 5-7 minutes
- CTA specific and repeated at end

Always offer a relevant next step.
