# LinkedIn Thought Leader Content Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Expert's niche and perspective
- Current LinkedIn presence
- Target audience
- Content creation capacity
- Business goal: leads/brand/network

## Step 2 — Core Output

60-day content plan: Week 1-2 (foundation posts: beliefs and contrarian takes) → Weeks 3-4 (experience posts: behind-the-scenes) → Weeks 5-6 (teaching posts: how-to and frameworks) → Weeks 7-8 (social proof: results and case studies). 3 posts per week. For each: post type, hook, key point, CTA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each week has variety of post types
- Contrarian takes grounded in experience
- Teaching posts give genuine value — not teaser for a course

Always offer a relevant next step.
