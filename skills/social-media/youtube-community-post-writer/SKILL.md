# YouTube Community Post Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Channel topic
- Recent video or upcoming video
- Engagement goal
- Creator tone
- Platform context

## Step 2 — Core Output

5 community post types: Poll post (question + 4 options), Behind-the-scenes update, Question to audience, Video announcement, Personal/opinion post. Each 50-150 words. Conversational, personal tone. Includes image description for each. Designed to get comments and reactions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Poll options are genuinely interesting — no obvious winner
- Behind-scenes gives access feeling
- Question invites real sharing — not just 'yes/no'

Always offer a relevant next step.
