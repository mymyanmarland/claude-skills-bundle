# Social Media Growth Audit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current metrics (followers, reach, engagement)
- Posting frequency and content types
- Platforms
- Niche
- Growth goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit report: Profile optimization score → Content quality assessment → Consistency analysis → Engagement rate vs benchmark → Hashtag/SEO performance → Audience quality check → Top 3 growth bottlenecks identified → Prioritized action plan (week 1 / month 1 / month 3). Specific — not generic tips.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bottlenecks diagnosed from actual metrics — not assumptions
- Action plan prioritized by impact
- Week 1 actions achievable immediately

Always offer a relevant next step.
