# User Generated Content Campaign Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- What you want customers to show
- Incentive structure
- Platform
- Hashtag or challenge concept

## Step 2 — Core Output

UGC campaign plan: Challenge/hashtag name → Campaign concept → Brief for participants (what to create, how to share) → Seeding strategy (creators to launch it first) → Incentive structure → Content moderation guidelines → Repurposing plan (how to use UGC in official channels) → Success metrics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Challenge brief clear enough to create consistent content
- Incentive appropriate to effort asked
- Repurposing plan respects creator credit

Always offer a relevant next step.
