# Employee Advocacy Content Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company news or content to share
- Employees' audiences
- Brand voice
- Sharing platforms
- Comfort level of employees

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advocacy toolkit: 5 pre-written posts employees can share with personal touches → Guidance on personalizing (what to add, what not to change) → Employee story prompt templates → Platform-specific tips → Opt-in messaging. Posts feel like real people — not company announcements.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-written posts feel like individuals wrote them
- Personalization guidance specific
- Opt-in culture — never mandatory

Always offer a relevant next step.
