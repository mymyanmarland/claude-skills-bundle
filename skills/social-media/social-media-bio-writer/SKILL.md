# Social Media Bio Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Who they are
- What they create or offer
- Platform
- Target follower
- CTA goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bios for each platform with character limits respected: Instagram (150 chars), Twitter/X (160), LinkedIn headline (220), TikTok (80), YouTube (500+). Each bio: who you are + what you create + why follow + CTA. Link-in-bio copy for Instagram. Keyword optimization for LinkedIn.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each bio within character limit
- CTA specific and low-friction
- Bios adapted to platform norms — not copy-pasted

Always offer a relevant next step.
