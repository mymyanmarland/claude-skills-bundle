# Behind-the-Scenes Content Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type
- What can be shown
- Audience
- Platforms
- Privacy considerations

## Step 2 — Core Output

BTS content plan: 30 content ideas across categories (process/team/fails/workspace/decisions/day-in-life) → Format per idea (photo/video/story/carousel) → Caption angle for each → Publishing sequence (build familiarity over time) → What NOT to share (privacy/competitive/legal notes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ideas genuinely behind-the-scenes — not staged marketing
- Privacy considerations explicit
- Sequence builds narrative arc over 30 posts

Always offer a relevant next step.
