# Social Media Policy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type
- Key concerns: brand protection/legal/crisis
- Employee social usage context
- Crisis protocol needs
- Tone: strict/flexible/collaborative

## Step 2 — Core Output

Policy sections: Purpose and scope → Personal vs professional use guidelines → Disclosure requirements (FTC, endorsements) → Confidentiality and proprietary information rules → Crisis and negative comment protocol → Disciplinary consequences → Approval process for official posts → Training resources. Legal disclaimer note: recommend legal review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Policy clear enough to follow without asking questions
- Crisis protocol gives step-by-step guidance
- Consequences proportionate and specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
