# Social Media Brand Voice Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand personality
- Platforms used
- Team size
- Common tone mistakes
- Target audience per platform

## Step 2 — Core Output

Voice guide: Core voice (3 adjectives with definitions) → Platform-by-platform tone adjustments → Word/phrase do list → Word/phrase avoid list → Before/after examples (5 pairs per platform) → Response templates for common scenarios. One-page quick reference card included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Platform adjustments genuinely different — not just formal vs casual
- Examples show the difference — not just describe it
- Quick reference card standalone usable

Always offer a relevant next step.
