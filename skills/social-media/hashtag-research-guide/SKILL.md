# Hashtag Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Niche or content type
- Platform
- Account size
- Content style
- Goal: reach/community/niche

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hashtag strategy: Tier 1 (5 niche hashtags under 50K posts) → Tier 2 (5 topic hashtags 50K-500K) → Tier 3 (3-5 broad hashtags 500K-5M) → Branded hashtag suggestion → Hashtag sets for 4 different content types. Usage rules per platform. Rotation schedule to avoid shadow ban risk.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hashtags actually relevant — not just popular
- Tier mix matches account size
- Rotation strategy prevents algorithmic penalty

Always offer a relevant next step.
