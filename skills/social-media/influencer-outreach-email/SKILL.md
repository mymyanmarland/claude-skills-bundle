# Influencer Outreach Email Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand description
- Why this specific creator
- What you're proposing
- Compensation or value exchange
- Brand tone

## Step 2 — Core Output

Email: Subject line (specific, not 'collaboration opportunity') → Genuine opener (why this creator specifically) → Value proposition (what's in it for them) → What you're asking (specific, low-ask first) → Compensation or benefit → Next step (simple ask). Under 150 words. 3 subject line options. Follows up only once template included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opener proves you actually follow them
- Ask is specific — not 'let us know if interested'
- Compensation mentioned upfront — not held back

Always offer a relevant next step.
