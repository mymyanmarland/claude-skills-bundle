# TikTok Caption Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Video topic
- Niche
- Account size
- Caption length preference
- Goal: views/follows/saves

## Step 2 — Core Output

Caption structure: Line 1 (reinforce video hook — not repeat it) → Optional expansion (2-3 lines) → CTA (comment with X / duet this / follow for more) → Hashtag set (3-5 niche + 2-3 trending + 1-2 large). Include 3 caption variations: short (50 chars), medium (150 chars), long (300 chars).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Caption adds context not available in video
- Hashtags include at least 2 under 100K posts
- CTA specific — not generic 'like and subscribe'

Always offer a relevant next step.
