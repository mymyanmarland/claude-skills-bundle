# Social Media Collaboration Brief Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Collaboration type
- Both parties' audiences
- Campaign goal
- Deliverables expected
- Timeline and compensation

## Step 2 — Core Output

Collaboration brief: Campaign overview → Each party's role and deliverables → Content requirements (minimal — preserve authenticity) → Timeline and milestones → Approval process → Usage rights → Compensation structure → Success metrics → Communication plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Requirements list short — over-prescription kills authenticity
- Usage rights specific and fair
- Success metrics agreed before campaign launches

Always offer a relevant next step.
