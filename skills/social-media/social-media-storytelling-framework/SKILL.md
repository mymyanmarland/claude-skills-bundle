# Social Media Storytelling Framework Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand or personal story to tell
- Platform
- Audience
- Story goal: trust/sales/community
- Story type: origin/struggle/result/behind-scenes

## Step 2 — Core Output

Framework: Setup (hook + context in 1-2 sentences) → Rising tension (the problem or obstacle — make it relatable) → Turning point (the decision, insight, or moment that changed things) → Resolution (the outcome — specific and honest) → Lesson (the takeaway for the reader). Apply to: caption version, story frame version, short video script version. Each under 300 words.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tension is genuine — not manufactured drama
- Resolution is specific — not vague success
- Lesson connects directly to audience's own situation

Always offer a relevant next step.
