# Social Media Brand Consistency Checker Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Content to review
- Brand guidelines reference
- Primary concerns
- Platform
- Brand voice description

## Step 2 — Core Output

Review: Brand voice score (1-10) → Specific inconsistencies found → Suggested rewrites for each issue → Pattern diagnosis (systemic vs one-off) → Brand consistency checklist for future content → Quick reference style card. Constructive and specific.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Inconsistencies specific — not 'doesn't sound like us'
- Rewrites show rather than tell
- Checklist actionable for non-expert

Always offer a relevant next step.
