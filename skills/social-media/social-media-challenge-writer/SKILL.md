# Social Media Challenge Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- Target participation
- Prize or motivation
- Duration
- Hashtag concept

## Step 2 — Core Output

Challenge concept: Name and hashtag → Concept overview (what participants do in one sentence) → Participation instructions (3 steps max) → Example content brief → Seeding plan (who goes first) → Content moderation approach → Repurposing rights → Success metrics. Keep entry barrier low — the simpler the challenge concept, the wider the participation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Participation instructions completable in under 2 minutes
- Challenge naturally shows or connects to brand
- Hashtag unique and brand-ownable

Always offer a relevant next step.
