# Personal Brand Social Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Expertise and niche
- Current platforms and following
- Business goal behind brand
- Content creation capacity
- Ideal audience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Personal brand strategy: Positioning statement → Platform priority order (1 primary, 1 secondary) → Content pillars (3-4 topic areas) → Content mix (educational/personal/promotional ratios) → 90-day content plan → Signature content format → Monetization pathway aligned with brand.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Platform choice justified for their niche and audience
- Content pillars distinct and brand-relevant
- 90-day plan sustainable with stated capacity

Always offer a relevant next step.
