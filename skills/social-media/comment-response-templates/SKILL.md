# Comment Response Templates Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platform
- Brand voice
- Comment types to address
- Engagement goal
- Controversial topic handling

## Step 2 — Core Output

Response templates by type: Positive comment (3 templates — genuine, not just 'thank you!') → Question (3 templates — answer + CTA) → Criticism (3 templates — acknowledge + address) → Spam/off-topic (2 templates) → DM redirect (2 templates). Plus: response time guidance, tone consistency notes, when to NOT respond.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Responses feel personal — not copy-pasted
- Criticism responses non-defensive
- Never engage further with trolls — template included

Always offer a relevant next step.
