# Social Media Audit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platforms to audit
- Audit goals
- Time period
- Who will act on findings
- Current pain points

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit framework: Profile completeness checklist per platform → Content consistency score → Engagement rate benchmarks → Best/worst performing content analysis → Audience growth analysis → Competitive comparison template → Priority action list. Includes scoring rubric and data collection guide.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Benchmarks platform-specific — not one-size
- Actions prioritized by impact
- Audit completable in one work day

Always offer a relevant next step.
