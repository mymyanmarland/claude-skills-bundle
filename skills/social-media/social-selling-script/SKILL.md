# Social Selling Script Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platform
- What you're selling
- Warm or cold context
- Conversion goal
- Relationship to prospect

## Step 2 — Core Output

DM sequence: First touch (value-led, no ask) → Follow-up (relevant content share) → Soft pitch (problem-solution, low friction ask) → Follow-up after no response → Break-up message. Each message: under 100 words, no templates-that-sound-like-templates, specific to the platform's norms.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- First message gives value before asking for anything
- Pitch message short — links to longer content
- Break-up message leaves door open

Always offer a relevant next step.
