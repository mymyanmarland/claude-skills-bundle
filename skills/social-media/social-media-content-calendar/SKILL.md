# Social Media Content Calendar Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platform(s)
- Monthly theme
- Posting frequency
- Content mix (educational/promotional/personal)
- Key dates

## Step 2 — Core Output

Monthly calendar: Week-by-week view → Each post: date, platform, content type, topic, format (image/video/carousel/text), copy hook, visual brief. Theme-driven with variety. 20% promotional maximum. Includes reactive content slots for trending topics. Batching recommendations (what to create together).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Promotional posts under 20% of calendar
- Each week has variety of content types
- Calendar achievable with stated resources

Always offer a relevant next step.
