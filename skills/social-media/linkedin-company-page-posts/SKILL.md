# LinkedIn Company Page Posts Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company industry
- Content goal: thought leadership/culture/hiring/product
- Recent news or achievements
- Target audience: buyers/talent/partners
- Brand voice

## Step 2 — Core Output

5 post types for a content mix: (1) Industry insight post. (2) Behind-the-scenes culture post. (3) Customer success story. (4) Team highlight. (5) Product/service spotlight. Each with: hook line, body copy, CTA, 3 hashtags. Each 150-300 words. Company voice — more formal than personal LinkedIn but still human.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Company posts avoid promotional language in insights
- Culture posts feature real employee moments
- Product posts lead with customer outcome not features

Always offer a relevant next step.
