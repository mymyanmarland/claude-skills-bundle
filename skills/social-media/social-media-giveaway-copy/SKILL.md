# Social Media Giveaway Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Prize
- Entry requirements
- Platform
- Duration
- Goal: followers/engagement/UGC

## Step 2 — Core Output

Giveaway kit: Announcement post (hook + what's the prize + how to enter + deadline + legal note) → Reminder post (halfway) → Final day urgency post → Winner announcement template. Entry requirements: ask for minimum friction. Include 3 headline variants with different hooks. Legal disclaimer language.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Entry requirements match the prize value
- Legal disclaimer included and complete
- Urgency in final day post is genuine not fake

Always offer a relevant next step.
