# Social Media Video Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platforms
- Current video capabilities
- Content goals
- Audience
- Video production resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video strategy: Platform-by-platform video format guide → Content series concepts (3 recurring formats) → Production workflow for each format → Publishing cadence → Hook + structure templates per format → Distribution strategy (cross-post vs native) → Performance metrics to track.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Formats matched to production resources
- Series concepts sustainable long-term
- Distribution strategy platform algorithm-aware
