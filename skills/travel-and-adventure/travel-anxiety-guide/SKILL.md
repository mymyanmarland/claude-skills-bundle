# Travel Anxiety Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Destination or trip type
- Duration and dates
- Budget range
- Travel style and preferences
- Any special requirements or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Complete travel plan for travel anxiety guide: Personalized recommendations based on stated preferences → Practical logistics (transport/accommodation/timing) → Money-saving opportunities → Safety and health considerations → Day-by-day or phase-by-phase structure → Packing or preparation notes → Backup plans for common issues → Resources and booking guidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recommendations destination-specific — not generic travel advice
- Budget realistic for the destination
- Safety considerations included without scaremongering

Always offer a relevant next step.
