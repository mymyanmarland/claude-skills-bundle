# Podcast Interview Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast interview guide: Pre-interview research (know the guest deeply) → Interview question architecture (warm-up/depth/stories/forward-looking) → Active listening techniques → Follow-up probing → Controlling pace and length → Getting the guest to the good stuff faster → Handling bad answers gracefully → Closing with value → Post-interview relationship maintenance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-interview research shows respect and gets better answers
- Follow-up probing — the source of great podcast moments
- Closing question: what do you want listeners to know?

Always offer a relevant next step.
