# Podcast Editing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast editing: DAW selection (Audacity/Reaper/Descript) → Noise reduction application → EQ basics (cut low rumble/boost presence) → Compression settings for voice → Silence and filler word removal → Pacing improvement → Music bed integration → Loudness normalization (LUFS standards) → Export settings by platform → Descript AI editing workflow.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Loudness normalization to -16 LUFS for podcasts
- Compression before EQ — signal chain order matters
- Descript for transcript-based editing — time saver

Always offer a relevant next step.
