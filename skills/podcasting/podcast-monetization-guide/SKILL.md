# Podcast Monetization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast monetization: Audience size benchmarks per revenue type → Direct monetization (listener support/memberships/paid content) → Indirect monetization (lead generation/consulting/speaking) → Advertising tiers (host-read/programmatic/sponsorships) → CPM benchmarks by niche → Sponsorship pitch deck → Merchandise strategy → Course and community upsell.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Indirect monetization possible at any audience size
- Host-read ads 3-5x CPM of programmatic
- Sponsorship pitch deck components specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
