# Podcast Show Notes SEO Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast show notes: Episode title with primary keyword → Episode summary (150-200 words) → Key takeaways (scannable) → Timestamps with descriptions → Guest bio and links → Resources mentioned → Transcript (full or partial) → CTA section → Internal links to related episodes → Meta description for SEO.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Primary keyword in episode title and first paragraph
- Timestamps drive listening and SEO dwell time
- Transcript for full keyword coverage

Always offer a relevant next step.
