# Podcast Guest Booking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Guest booking system: Ideal guest profile definition → Outreach email sequence (personalized 3-touch) → Booking calendar and confirmation system → Pre-interview questionnaire → Guest preparation guide (sent to guests) → Recording day protocol → Post-recording thank you and follow-up → Guest promotion coordination → CRM for relationship tracking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-interview questionnaire improves recording quality
- Guest preparation guide reduces technical problems
- Post-recording follow-up maintains relationship for future

Always offer a relevant next step.
