# Podcast Growth Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast growth: Guest swap partnerships → Cross-promotion strategy → Show notes SEO → Clip strategy for social (audiogram/video clip/quote card) → Guesting on other podcasts → Email list building from podcast → Community building around the show → Listener review ask timing → Paid promotion consideration → Charting strategy (New and Noteworthy window).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Guest appearances on other shows — fastest growth tactic
- Clip strategy — each episode becomes 5+ pieces of content
- New and Noteworthy window — first 8 weeks critical

Always offer a relevant next step.
