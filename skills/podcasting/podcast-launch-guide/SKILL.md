# Podcast Launch Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast launch: Concept validation (who is this for and what do they get?) → Niche positioning → Format decision (solo/interview/panel/narrative) → Episode length and cadence → Equipment minimum viable setup → Recording and editing workflow → Hosting platform selection → RSS feed and directory submission (Apple/Spotify/Google) → Launch strategy (batch episodes) → Pre-launch audience building.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Concept validation before equipment purchase
- Batch recording first 3 episodes before launch
- Directory submission timeline — Apple takes 5-7 days

Always offer a relevant next step.
