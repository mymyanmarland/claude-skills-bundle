# Podcast Content Planning Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Podcast topic and format
- Target audience
- Current stage: planning/launched/growing
- Budget and technical level
- Primary goal: audience/revenue/authority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast content planning: Audience question mining → Topic clustering → Series planning (3-5 episode arcs) → Seasonal and evergreen balance → Guest episode vs solo episode cadence → Pillar episode creation → Content repurposing plan → Batch recording schedule → Seasonal peaks and valleys → 3-month rolling content calendar.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audience questions as episode starting points
- Pillar episodes as top-of-funnel content
- Batch recording protects against burnout

Always offer a relevant next step.
