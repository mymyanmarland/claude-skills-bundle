# Comedy Roast Speech Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Roast speech: Opening self-deprecation → Genuine praise setup → Roast section (5-7 specific escalating jokes) → Crowd material → The serious turn (genuine appreciation 90 seconds) → Closing toast. Total: 4-6 minutes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Self-deprecation opens the roaster to roasting
- Serious turn authentic — audience detects fakeness
- Closing toast specific and heartfelt

Always offer a relevant next step.
