# Comedy Character Voice Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Character voice: Physical quality signals → Vocabulary signature (overuse and avoid) → Sentence structure pattern → Obsessions bleeding into all conversation → Status relationship → How they handle not knowing → Sample monologue in voice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Physical quality grounded in character background
- Vocabulary signature specific — list provided
- Status relationship drives how voice behaves

Always offer a relevant next step.
