# Meme Content Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Meme content: Format selection → Relatable observation → Subverted expectation → Caption economy (memes must be instant) → Niche adaptation → Trend vs evergreen balance → Cultural sensitivity check. Meme anatomy: top text sets up, bottom text subverts.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Format matched to joke type
- Caption economy — every word costs attention
- Cultural sensitivity check — memes spread without context

Always offer a relevant next step.
