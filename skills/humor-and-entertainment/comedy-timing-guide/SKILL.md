# Comedy Timing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject
- Target audience and context
- Tone preference
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy timing: The pause (silence is the delivery mechanism for many jokes) → Rhythm and beat in spoken comedy → The rule of threes in timing → When to rush vs when to hold → Physical comedy timing principles → Writing timing into a script (stage directions) → The callback timing (how long to wait) → Reading audience response.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- The pause — silence is often the punchline delivery
- Reading audience response — timing is reactive
- Callback timing — too soon or too late both fail

Always offer a relevant next step.
