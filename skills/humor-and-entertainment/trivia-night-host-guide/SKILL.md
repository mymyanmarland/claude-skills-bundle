# Trivia Night Host Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Trivia night script: Pre-show energy → Rules announcement (fun not bureaucratic) → Round introductions → Between-round banter → Dispute handling with humor → Tie-breaker mechanics → Score announcement drama → Closing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rules announcement entertaining — not just reciting rules
- Dispute handling with humor — deflects without dismissing
- Energy consistency — droops in round 3 lose the room

Always offer a relevant next step.
