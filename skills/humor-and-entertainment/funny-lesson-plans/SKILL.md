# Funny Educational Content Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny educational content: Humor aids memory (bizarreness effect) → Self-deprecating expert → Analogies with comic exaggeration → Absurd example making rule clear → Student laughter as engagement signal → Timing humor with difficult content → Funny professor vs stand-up (very different).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bizarreness effect — humor improves memory encoding
- Timing with difficult content — humor reduces anxiety
- Funny professor vs stand-up — educational humor different purpose

Always offer a relevant next step.
