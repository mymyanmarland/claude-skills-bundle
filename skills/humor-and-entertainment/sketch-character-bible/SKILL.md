# Sketch Character Bible Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject
- Target audience and context
- Tone preference
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Character bible: Core characteristic (the one thing) → The want they always pursue → The method they always use → What always goes wrong → Relationship to other characters → Catchphrase potential (use sparingly) → Escalation behavior → What the character never does → Sample sketches that showcase the character.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Core characteristic singular — one thing
- Escalation behavior defined — how they get worse
- What they never do — limits define the character

Always offer a relevant next step.
