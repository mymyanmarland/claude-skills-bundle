# Funny Acceptance Speech Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject
- Target audience and context
- Tone preference
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Acceptance speech: Thank you efficiency (thank quickly, specifically) → The unexpected observation → Self-deprecating opener → Genuine emotion alongside humor → The callback if you have setup time → The memorable closing line → Time awareness (acceptance speeches go long — be brief). The funniest speeches are also the most heartfelt.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Thank quickly and specifically — no one wants the long list
- Genuine emotion alongside humor — not replacing it
- Time awareness — brevity is the soul of acceptance

Always offer a relevant next step.
