# Comedy Writing Exercises Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy exercises: Premise mining (10 observations in 10 min) → Rewrite the unfunny → Rule of three construction → Callback planting → What if exercise → Persona adoption (same observation as 5 different comedians) → Speed writing (50 setups in 30 min).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Volume before quality — first draft comedy always rough
- Persona adoption — the best comedy education
- Callback planting forces intentional structural thinking

Always offer a relevant next step.
