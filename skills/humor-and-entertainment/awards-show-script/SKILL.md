# Awards Ceremony Script Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Awards script: Opening monologue → Award introductions (each a 30-second performance) → Presenter introductions → Winner moment (build suspense) → Acceptance speech management → Humor moments → Closing. Each award is an event, not just an announcement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening monologue sets the whole night tone
- Each award a mini-event — not just reading names
- Time management for acceptance speeches

Always offer a relevant next step.
