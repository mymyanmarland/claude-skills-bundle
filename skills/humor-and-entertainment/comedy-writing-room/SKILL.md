# Comedy Writing Room Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject
- Target audience and context
- Tone preference
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Writing room: Pitch session structure (quantity over quality early) → Yes-and building on pitches → Punching up jokes collaboratively → The table pass (read-through for timing) → Credit and attribution in a room → Managing the room energy → Minority voice protection → Daily page count targets.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Quantity over quality in pitch phase — safety first
- Table pass reveals what plays vs reads funny
- Minority voice protection — best ideas often quietest

Always offer a relevant next step.
