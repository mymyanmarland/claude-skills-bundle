# Humorous Explainer Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Humorous explainer: Analogy selection → Comic exaggeration of truth → Absurd but accurate example → Comedian confession technique → Tone: knowledgeable friend not textbook → Accuracy check after humor → Memorable punchline encoding the concept.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Analogy must be accurate — humorous explainers can mislead
- Accuracy check after humor — comedy cannot introduce error
- Memorable punchline encodes concept for retention

Always offer a relevant next step.
