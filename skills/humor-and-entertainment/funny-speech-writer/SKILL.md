# Funny Speech Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny speech: Occasion tone calibration → Personal stories with universal resonance → Callback structure → Self-deprecation as trust builder → Rule of three → Emotional pivot (funny to sincere) → Timing notes → Always end on warmth not the laugh.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Occasion tone calibration — funeral humor vs wedding very different
- Emotional pivot from funny to sincere — the most important moment
- End on love not the laugh

Always offer a relevant next step.
