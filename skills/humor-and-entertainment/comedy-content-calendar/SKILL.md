# Comedy Content Calendar Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy content calendar: Awareness dates with comedy potential → Recurring humor formats per platform → Evergreen comedy types → Real-time comedy opportunities → Balance (not every post funny) → Batching → A/B testing → Performance measurement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recurring formats — infrastructure of comedy content
- Not every post funny — range creates contrast
- Real-time slots — some comedy only works immediately

Always offer a relevant next step.
