# Office Party Entertainment Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Office party entertainment: Inclusivity test for all activities → Activity menu (3-4 options at different energy levels) → Icebreaker sequence → Team game design → Clean humor content → Award show parody → Photo opportunities → Evening arc.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Inclusivity first — activities accessible to all
- Clean humor content — professional context
- Collaborative games default — not everyone competitive

Always offer a relevant next step.
