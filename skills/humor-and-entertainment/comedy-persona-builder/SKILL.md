# Comedy Persona Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject
- Target audience and context
- Tone preference
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy persona: Point of view (what is your angle on the world?) → The subject (what do you talk about?) → The attitude (how do you relate to your audience — superior/inferior/peer?) → Speech patterns and rhythms → The signature move → What makes you different from every other comedian → The authentic foundation (persona built on real truth).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Point of view specific — the backbone of all comedy
- Authentic foundation — persona rooted in real experience
- Signature move — the thing audiences wait for

Always offer a relevant next step.
