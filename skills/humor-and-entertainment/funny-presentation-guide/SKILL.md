# Funny Presentation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny presentation: Self-deprecation opener → Relatable observation vs joke → Callback through presentation → Visual humor in slides → The pause and look → When to abandon humor → Humor vs comedy in presentations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Self-deprecation opens permission
- Observation vs joke — observation safer professionally
- Room-reading — knowing when not to be funny

Always offer a relevant next step.
