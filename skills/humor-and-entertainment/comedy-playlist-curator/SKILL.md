# Entertainment Curator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Entertainment curation: Occasion and mood matching → Genre selection → Length and format matching → Entry point recommendations → Deep cuts for fans → Paired sequences → Age filtering → Platform availability.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Occasion matching — comedy for grief vs bad day different
- Entry points — not everyone starts with the classic
- Paired sequences — comedy builds on comedy

Always offer a relevant next step.
