# Comedy Team Building Facilitator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy team building: Why comedy builds teams (shared laughter = trust) → Workshop arc → Improv principles applied to work (yes and/listening/support) → Low-stakes exercises → Application moments → Take-home practices.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety created before comedy attempted
- Improv principles connected to work skills explicitly
- Application moments — workshop sticks when connected to real work

Always offer a relevant next step.
