# Comedy Open Mic Preparation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Open mic prep: Material selection (5 minutes tested) → Set order (strong opener/closer) → Stage craft → Timing for laughs → Bomb recovery → Pre-show preparation → Recording yourself → Post-show analysis.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Material order specific — opener and closer non-negotiable
- Bomb recovery technique — what to say when joke dies
- Record every set — the only way to improve

Always offer a relevant next step.
