# Satirical News Show Format Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Satirical news format: Show premise → Segment types (breaking fake news/expert interview/field report) → Anchor persona → Recurring characters → Punch up not down → Graphics integration → Social media strategy → Episode structure.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Punch up rule — satire targets power not the powerless
- Anchor persona consistency — voice of the show
- Recurring characters — audience investment over time
