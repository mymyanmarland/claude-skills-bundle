# Satirical Essay Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Satirical essay: Target identification → Satirical mode (Horatian gentle vs Juvenalian cutting) → Deadpan voice → Escalation of absurdity → Reveal technique → Specific detail over vague criticism → Closing that crystallizes without stating.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Satirical mode matched to target and audience
- Deadpan commitment — humor comes from seriousness
- Specific targets — satire needs precision

Always offer a relevant next step.
