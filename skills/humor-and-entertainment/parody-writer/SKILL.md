# Parody Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Parody writing: Target understanding → The angle → Accuracy to original form → Comedic deviation → Internal consistency → Fair use principles. Best parodies are love letters that make fun.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Know the original — audiences must recognize it
- Internal consistency — parody world follows own rules
- Fair use vs pastiche distinction

Always offer a relevant next step.
