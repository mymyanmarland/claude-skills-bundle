# Humor Workshop Facilitator Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Humor workshop: Arc (safety/permission/tools/practice) → Opening low-stakes exercise → Principles through experience → Partner exercises → Group exercises → The sharing moment → Application to real contexts → Debrief.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety before permission — humor requires psychological safety
- Principles through experience — lecture kills humor
- Application to real context — workshop must connect to life

Always offer a relevant next step.
