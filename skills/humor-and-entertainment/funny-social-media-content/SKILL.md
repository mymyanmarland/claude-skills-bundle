# Funny Social Media Content Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny social content: Niche humor identification → Platform-specific humor → Relatable observation format → Absurdism for algorithm → Self-awareness as brand voice → Comment section as content → Series concepts → What to avoid.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Niche humor specific — generic humor performs poorly
- Platform matching — humor styles differ
- Trying too hard detection — enemy of funny

Always offer a relevant next step.
