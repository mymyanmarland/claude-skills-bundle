# Comedy Writing Coach Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy coaching: Comic timing → Rule of threes → Callbacks → Misdirection → Specificity as humor → Finding the game → Economy of language → Exercises: rewrite unfunny premises → Premises test (funny or just clever?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specificity rule with examples — abstract is never funny
- Economy exercises — cut 50% and get funnier
- Premises test — clever is not comedy

Always offer a relevant next step.
