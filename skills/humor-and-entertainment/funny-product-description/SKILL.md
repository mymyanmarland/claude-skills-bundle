# Funny Product Description Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny product copy: Product honest assessment → Comedic angle (absurd benefit/self-aware limitation) → Specificity in humor → Serious sell buried in funny → CTA with wit. Funny enough to be memorable, clear enough to communicate.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Product honest assessment first — humor serves information
- Specific details — generic humor kills product copy
- Serious sell present — humor is not substitute for clarity

Always offer a relevant next step.
