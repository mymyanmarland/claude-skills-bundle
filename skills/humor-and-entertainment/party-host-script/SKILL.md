# Party Host Script Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Party host script: Opening energy setter → Guest welcome → Activity transitions → Crowd engagement → Announcements → Award segments → Emergency energy savers → Closing. Host voice: warm, inclusive, energetic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening energy sets the entire evening tone
- Transition scripts prevent awkward silences
- Emergency energy savers — every host needs these

Always offer a relevant next step.
