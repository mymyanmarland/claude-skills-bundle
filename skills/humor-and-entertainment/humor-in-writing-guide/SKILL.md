# Humor in Writing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Humor in writing: Comic timing through sentence structure → Unexpected word technique → Understatement → Absurd simile and metaphor → Footnote joke → Comic digression → Narrator self-awareness → Specificity rule. Writing humor is rhythm as much as content.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sentence structure for timing — the hidden comedy tool
- Unexpected word technique — immediately applicable
- Specificity rule with before-and-after examples

Always offer a relevant next step.
