# Celebratory Obituary Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Celebratory obituary: Person signature humor → Specific funny stories → What they would say about their own death → Genuine tributes alongside humor → What they would hate about the obituary (include it anyway) → Closing honoring grief and celebrating life. Humor in death is love, not disrespect.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Signature humor of the person — not generic comedy
- What they would hate included — honors personality
- Grief acknowledged — not bypassed

Always offer a relevant next step.
