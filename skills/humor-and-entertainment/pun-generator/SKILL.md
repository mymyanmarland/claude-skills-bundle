# Pun and Wordplay Generator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pun generation: Semantic field mapping → Phonetic similarity search → Multiple meaning exploitation → Compound pun construction → Visual pun concepts → Groan scale rating → Context-appropriate selection → Sets of 10 with annotations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Semantic field mapping — systematic not random
- Groan scale is a feature not a bug
- Context matching — work pun vs social media pun different

Always offer a relevant next step.
