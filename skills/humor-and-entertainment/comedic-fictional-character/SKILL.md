# Comedic Character Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedic character: Core comedic trait → Want vs need gap (comic characters want the wrong thing) → Speech pattern → The character blind spot → Relationship to status → Escalation behavior → Sample dialogue in voice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Core trait drives all behavior — multi-trait characters lose focus
- Blind spot — the engine of situational comedy
- Want vs need gap — comedy comes from this tension

Always offer a relevant next step.
