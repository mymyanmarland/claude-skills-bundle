# Funny Email Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny email: Audience calibration → Opening hook with wit → Humor after credibility established → Self-awareness → Understatement → Callback in close → Subject line wit when appropriate → Tone recovery (serious when needed).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audience calibration first — humor that offends is just rude
- Humor after credibility — not before trust established
- Tone recovery — ability to be serious when needed

Always offer a relevant next step.
