# Comedy Podcast Guest Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast guest preparation: Stories prepped (3-5 polished anecdotes) → Listening and building vs performance mode → Callbacks to host points → Self-deprecation as entry → Energy calibration → First five minutes → Post-show amplification. Podcast comedy is a conversation, not a set.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stories polished in advance — not improvised from scratch
- Listening and building — podcast is conversation not performance
- First five minutes determines arc of whole conversation

Always offer a relevant next step.
