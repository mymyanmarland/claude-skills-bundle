# Comedic Debate Format Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedic debate: Motion selection (absurd but arguable) → Structure → Judging criteria (funniest argument wins not most logical) → Time limits → Host management → Audience voting → Best line award.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Motion selection — absurd enough to be funny, arguable enough to sustain
- Judging criteria for humor not logic — frees participants
- Host management — comedy debates need a traffic cop

Always offer a relevant next step.
