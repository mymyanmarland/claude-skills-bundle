# Entertainment Review Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Entertainment review: Hook (start with strongest reaction) → Context → The argument (main claim) → Specific evidence from the work → Comparison → Recommendation (who should and should not engage) → Memorable closing line.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hook as strongest reaction — no plot summary openings
- Argument explicit — review is not a summary
- Recommendation specific — who is this for and not for

Always offer a relevant next step.
