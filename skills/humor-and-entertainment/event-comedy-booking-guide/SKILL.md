# Event Comedy Booking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy booking: Audience profile → Material appropriateness check → Budget ranges by tier → Contract essentials → Briefing the comedian → Backup plan → Post-show feedback. Clean vs blue material decision upfront.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audience profile determines everything
- Material approval process — essential for corporate events
- Clean vs blue decision explicit before booking

Always offer a relevant next step.
