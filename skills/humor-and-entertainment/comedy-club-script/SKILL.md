# Comedy Club Night Script Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comedy club script: Room energy opening → Emcee material (original 3-5 min) → Act introductions (each different) → Between-act material (shorter as night progresses) → Headliner introduction (best intro) → Closing. Emcee sets the room, acts fill it.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Emcee material original — not just reading bios
- Between-act shorter as night goes
- Headliner intro builds maximum anticipation

Always offer a relevant next step.
