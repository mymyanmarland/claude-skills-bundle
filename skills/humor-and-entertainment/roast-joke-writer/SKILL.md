# Roast Joke Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Roast jokes: Subject research (specific details land) → Target: public behavior/known quirks/shared history (never private pain) → Punchline structure → Rule of affection → Self-deprecating balance → Escalation arc → Heartfelt landing (roasts end with genuine love). Specificity is the soul of roasting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specificity rule — generic insults are just mean
- Affection grounding — every joke from love not contempt
- Heartfelt close — roasts always end with genuine warmth

Always offer a relevant next step.
