# Children Comedy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Children comedy: Age calibration (3-5 slapstick / 6-9 wordplay / 10-12 absurdism / teen irony) → Phonetics that sound funny → Incongruity → Unexpected answer → Safe silliness → Repetition as comedy → Character voices for children.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age calibration specific — very different by stage
- Phonetic comedy for young children — sounds matter
- Repetition is feature not bug for children

Always offer a relevant next step.
