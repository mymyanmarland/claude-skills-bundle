# Funny Award Categories Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic, occasion, or subject matter
- Target audience and context
- Tone: gentle/cutting/absurdist/observational
- Constraints or sensitivities
- Goal: entertain/roast/satirize/educate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funny awards: Category naming (amusing not mean) → Description copy → Nomination criteria → Presentation line → Winner reaction suggestion. Awards must be affectionate — recipient should feel celebrated not mocked. 10-15 categories with range.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Affectionate not mocking — recipient should feel celebrated
- Range from universal to personal categories
- Presentation lines ready — hosts ad-lib badly

Always offer a relevant next step.
