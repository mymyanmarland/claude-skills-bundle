# Ebook Outline Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Topic and core promise**: What will the reader be able to do/know after reading?
- **Target reader**: Who is this for? What's their current level of knowledge?
- **Type**: Lead magnet (5-15 pages) / Short ebook (20-50 pages) / Full book (50-200 pages)
- **Goal**: Generate leads / Establish authority / Sell / Educate / Entertain
- **Unique angle**: What makes this ebook different from what's already out there?

## Step 2 — Ebook Outline Structure
```
## Ebook Outline: "[Working Title]"
Type: [X] | Estimated pages: [X] | Reader goal: [What they achieve]

---

### FRONT MATTER
Title page concept: [Working title + subtitle]
About the author (2-3 sentences)
How to use this book (optional — for instructional ebooks)

---

### INTRODUCTION
Hook: [What pulls the reader in — their pain, a bold claim, a story]
The promise: [What they'll get from this ebook specifically]
Why you: [Credibility without bragging — 1 paragraph]
Road map: [Preview of the chapters]

---

### CHAPTER 1: [Title]
Purpose: [What this chapter does for the reader]
Key points:
  - [Point 1]
  - [Point 2]
  - [Point 3]
Ends with: [Chapter summary / exercise / transition to next chapter]

### CHAPTER 2: [Title]
[Same structure]

### CHAPTER 3: [Title]
[Same structure]

[Continue for all chapters — typically 4-8 for ebooks]

---

### CONCLUSION
[Summary of the transformation]
[Call to action — next step for the reader]
[Final inspiring close]

### BACK MATTER
Resources / Further reading
About the author (expanded)
CTA page (for lead magnet ebooks: the offer)

---

### CHAPTER WORD COUNT GUIDE
Introduction: ~[X] words
Each chapter: ~[X] words
Total estimated: ~[X] words / [X] pages
```

## Step 3 — Ebook Strategy Notes
- **Lead magnet ebooks**: End every chapter with a micro-CTA
- **Authority ebooks**: Cite sources and include original research or frameworks
- **Instructional ebooks**: Every chapter should include an actionable exercise
- **The title matters as much as the content** — spend time on it

Always offer: "Want me to write the full introduction chapter based on this outline?"
