# Website Copy Writer Skill

Write website copy that converts visitors into customers.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Page type**: Homepage / About / Service / Product / Landing page / Pricing
- **Business name and what they do**
- **Target customer**: Who lands on this page? What are they hoping for?
- **Unique value proposition**: Why choose this over alternatives?
- **Tone**: Professional / Friendly / Bold / Minimal / Luxurious
- **Primary CTA**: Book a call / Buy now / Sign up / Learn more / Contact
- **Social proof available**: Testimonials, clients, stats, awards?

## Step 2 — Page Templates

### Homepage
```
## ABOVE THE FOLD (what they see without scrolling)

Headline: [What you do + for whom + outcome — 6-10 words]
Subheadline: [Expand on headline — the "how" or "why you" — 1-2 sentences]
CTA Button: [Action verb + value — "Get My Free Quote" not just "Submit"]
Supporting text: [One trust signal: "Trusted by 500+ businesses" / "No credit card required"]

---

## PROBLEM SECTION
[Name the pain the visitor is feeling — 2-3 sentences. They should think "yes, that's me."]

---

## SOLUTION SECTION
Headline: [How you solve it]
[3 benefit pillars — each with icon, bold headline, 1-sentence description]

---

## HOW IT WORKS
[3-step process — simple, clear, reassuring]
Step 1: [Action]
Step 2: [What happens]
Step 3: [The outcome they get]

---

## SOCIAL PROOF
[Testimonial 1 — result-focused, attributed with name/company/photo]
[Key stats: "1,200+ customers | 4.9★ rating | $0 setup fee"]
[Testimonial 2]

---

## ABOUT / TRUST
[2-3 sentences on who you are and why you care. Human, not corporate.]

---

## FINAL CTA
Headline: [Ready to [desired outcome]?]
[Reinforce the CTA — reduce risk: "No commitment. Cancel anytime."]
CTA Button: [Same as hero]
```

### Service/Landing Page
```
[Hero: Problem-aware headline + subhead + CTA]
[Agitation: Expand the pain — cost of not solving it]
[Solution: Your service as the answer]
[Features → Benefits list]
[Social proof block]
[FAQ section — handle objections]
[Final CTA with risk-reversal]
```

## Step 3 — Copywriting Rules
- Lead with WIIFM (What's In It For Me) — always
- Benefits over features: "Saves 3 hours/week" not "Advanced automation"
- Short paragraphs: 2-3 sentences max on web
- Active voice: "We build" not "Websites are built by us"
- One CTA per section — don't confuse visitors with choices

Always offer: "Want me to write a specific section in more detail?"
