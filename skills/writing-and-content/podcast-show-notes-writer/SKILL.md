# Podcast Show Notes Writer Skill

Turn episode content into show notes that attract new listeners and serve existing ones.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Episode title and number**
- **Guest name and bio** (if interview episode)
- **Main topics covered** (bullet points or rough notes are fine)
- **Key takeaways or quotes** from the episode
- **Any links, resources, or sponsors to include**
- **Platform**: Spotify / Apple / Website / All

## Step 2 — Show Notes Structure

```
## Episode [#]: [Episode Title]
*[Podcast Name] with [Host Name]*

---

### Episode Summary (2-3 sentences — the hook)
[What this episode is about and why listeners should care.
Lead with the most compelling insight or guest credential.]

---

### In This Episode
[Host] sits down with [Guest, Title at Company] to explore [topic].
[What problem/question does this episode answer?]
[What will the listener walk away knowing or able to do?]

---

### Key Takeaways
- [Insight 1 — specific and actionable]
- [Insight 2]
- [Insight 3]
- [Insight 4]
- [Insight 5]

---

### Episode Highlights & Timestamps
- **[00:00]** — Introduction and [guest background]
- **[03:45]** — [Topic or question discussed]
- **[12:20]** — [Key insight or story]
- **[24:10]** — [Turning point or surprise moment]
- **[38:00]** — [Practical advice section]
- **[47:30]** — [Rapid fire / closing questions]
- **[52:00]** — Where to find [guest]

---

### Guest Bio
[2-3 sentence bio. Credentials first, human element second.]
Connect with [Guest]:
- Website: [URL]
- LinkedIn: [URL]
- Instagram: [URL]

---

### Resources Mentioned
- [Resource 1] — [URL]
- [Resource 2] — [URL]
- [Book/Tool mentioned] — [URL]

---

### Connect With Us
- Subscribe: [Link]
- Leave a review: [Link]
- Instagram: [Handle]
- Website: [URL]

---
*[Sponsor message if applicable]*
```

## Step 3 — SEO Optimization
- Episode title should include searchable keywords
- First 200 characters are indexed by podcast apps — make them count
- Guest name + their known topic = strong search signal
- Include 3-5 hashtags for social sharing version

Always offer: "Want a social media post to promote this episode?"
