# Blog Post Writer Skill

Write compelling, well-structured blog posts that rank and resonate.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
Ask if not provided:
- **Topic**: What is the post about?
- **Target audience**: Who is reading this?
- **Word count**: 500 / 800 / 1200 / 2000+ words
- **Tone**: Conversational / Professional / Educational / Inspirational
- **Goal**: SEO traffic / Engagement / Lead generation / Brand authority
- **Primary keyword**: (if SEO-focused)
- **CTA**: What should the reader do at the end?

## Step 2 — Blog Post Structure

```
## [HEADLINE — include primary keyword, create curiosity or promise value]
*[Optional subheadline — expand on the promise]*

**[Opening hook — 2-3 sentences]**
[Surprising stat / bold claim / relatable problem / compelling question]
[Why this matters to the reader right now]
[What they'll learn — the promise]

---

## [H2: First Major Section]
[3-4 paragraphs. One idea per paragraph. Short sentences.]
[Include primary or secondary keyword naturally.]

### [H3: Subsection if needed]
[Supporting detail, example, or tip]

---

## [H2: Second Major Section]
[Content]

---

## [H2: Third Major Section — often the "how to" or "what to do"]
[Numbered steps or actionable advice work well here]

1. **[Step/tip 1]**: [Explanation]
2. **[Step/tip 2]**: [Explanation]
3. **[Step/tip 3]**: [Explanation]

---

## [H2: Conclusion]
[Summarize key takeaways — 2-3 sentences]
[Reinforce the value they just got]
[CTA — what to do next: comment, subscribe, download, buy, share]
```

## Step 3 — SEO Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in title, first 100 words, one H2, conclusion
- [ ] Meta description: 150-160 chars, includes keyword, has CTA
- [ ] Internal link opportunities noted
- [ ] Suggested image alt text for featured image
- [ ] Suggested URL slug: /[keyword-phrase]

## Step 4 — Output
Deliver: Full post + meta description + suggested title variations (3 options) + URL slug

Always offer: "Want me to write a social media caption to promote this post?"
