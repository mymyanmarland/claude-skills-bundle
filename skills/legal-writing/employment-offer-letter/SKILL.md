# Employment Offer Letter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Offer letter: Role title and start date → Compensation (salary/hourly/bonus structure) → Benefits summary → Reporting structure → Employment classification (at-will or term) → Contingencies (background check/drug test) → Expiration date of offer → Offer acceptance mechanism → Confidentiality and IP assignment reference. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- At-will vs term employment explicit — jurisdiction matters
- Benefits summary accurate — employment attorney for full benefits package
- Contingencies listed — prevents later disputes

Always offer a relevant next step.
