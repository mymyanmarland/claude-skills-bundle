# Promissory Note Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Promissory note: Borrower and lender identification → Principal amount → Interest rate (or zero interest explicit) → Repayment schedule (lump sum vs installments) → Maturity date → Late payment terms → Default provisions → Prepayment rights → Governing law → Signature requirements. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Promissory notes may have tax implications for zero-interest loans (imputed interest rules).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zero interest explicit — IRS imputed interest rules
- Default provisions — what triggers default, what happens
- Prepayment rights — can borrower pay early without penalty

Always offer a relevant next step.
