# IP Assignment Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

IP assignment: Assignor and assignee identification → Description of IP being assigned (specific) → Consideration (payment or recital) → Representations and warranties (assignor owns what they are transferring) → Scope of assignment (all rights vs limited) → Future works provision → Moral rights waiver (where applicable) → Governing law → Signature block. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scope of assignment explicit — all rights vs specific rights
- Representations that assignor actually owns the IP
- Future works provision if ongoing relationship

Always offer a relevant next step.
