# Speaking Engagement Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Speaking contract: Speaker and organizer identification → Event details (date/location/audience) → Presentation description and duration → Fee and payment terms → Travel and accommodation → Audio-visual requirements → Recording rights → Cancellation policy (both directions) → Marketing and promotion rights → Speaker materials ownership. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recording rights explicit — valuable and often disputed
- Cancellation policy both directions — speaker and organizer
- AV requirements specific — technical failure affects speaker reputation

Always offer a relevant next step.
