# Website Accessibility Statement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Accessibility statement: Conformance status (fully/partially/non-conformant) → Standards targeted (WCAG 2.1 level AA) → Known limitations and workarounds → Alternative access provisions → Feedback mechanism → Response time commitment → Formal complaint procedure → Contact information → Last assessment date. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Website accessibility has legal requirements under ADA — accessibility attorney consultation recommended for high-risk businesses.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Conformance status honest — false claims create liability
- Known limitations acknowledged — honesty reduces complaints
- Feedback mechanism with response time — demonstrates good faith

Always offer a relevant next step.
