# Employment Separation Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Separation agreement: Termination date and circumstances → Severance amount and schedule → Benefits continuation (COBRA notice) → Return of company property → Release of claims (scope) → Non-disparagement → Confidentiality → Reference statement → Cooperation obligation → ADEA notice if applicable (21-day review, 7-day revocation). Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Separation agreements require employment law compliance — attorney review essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- ADEA requirements for over-40 employees — legal requirement
- Release scope negotiated — what claims covered
- 21-day review and 7-day revocation for ADEA compliance

Always offer a relevant next step.
