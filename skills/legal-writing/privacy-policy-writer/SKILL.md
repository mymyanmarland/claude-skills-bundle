# Privacy Policy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Privacy policy: Information collected (explicit list) → How information is used → Third-party sharing → Cookies and tracking → User rights (GDPR/CCPA rights catalog) → Data retention → Security measures → Children's privacy (COPPA if applicable) → Contact information → Last updated date. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Have reviewed by privacy attorney for compliance in your jurisdiction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- User rights section complete — GDPR/CCPA specific
- Children's privacy section if any under-13 users possible
- Contact information real and monitored

Always offer a relevant next step.
