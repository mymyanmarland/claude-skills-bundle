# Influencer Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Influencer agreement: Parties → Campaign description and deliverables → Content specifications → Approval process → Compensation and payment timing → FTC disclosure requirements (mandatory) → Exclusivity period → Usage rights (brand's rights to content) → Term and termination → Morality clause. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FTC disclosure requirements — legal mandate, not optional
- Usage rights explicit — who can use content after campaign
- Morality clause scope — protects brand from association with controversy

Always offer a relevant next step.
