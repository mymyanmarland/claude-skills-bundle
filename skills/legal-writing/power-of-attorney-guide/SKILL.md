# Power of Attorney Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

POA guide: Types of POA (general/limited/durable/healthcare/springing) → When each type is appropriate → Principal and agent responsibilities → Scope of authority (what agent can do) → Limitations and restrictions → Activation conditions → Revocation process → Record keeping requirements → Notarization and witness requirements by state. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: POA requirements vary by state — attorney preparation recommended for durable and healthcare POA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Durable vs general POA — the critical distinction for incapacity
- Activation conditions for springing POA
- State-specific execution requirements

Always offer a relevant next step.
