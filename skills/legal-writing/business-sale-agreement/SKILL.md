# Business Sale Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Business sale agreement: Parties → Assets being purchased (specific list) → Assumed liabilities (explicit) → Purchase price and payment terms → Representations and warranties → Due diligence period → Closing conditions → Non-compete provision → Transition assistance → Allocation of purchase price. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Business sales have significant legal and tax implications — both parties should have attorneys and accountants.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Asset list specific — everything not listed stays
- Assumed liabilities explicit — buyer takes on what is listed
- Non-compete scope and duration — common business sale negotiation point

Always offer a relevant next step.
