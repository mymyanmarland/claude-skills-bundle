# Letter of Intent Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Letter of intent: Transaction description → Key terms agreed (price/structure/timeline) → Binding vs non-binding provisions (usually non-binding except exclusivity and confidentiality) → Due diligence period → Exclusivity provision → Expiration of LOI → Conditions to closing → Good faith obligation. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Binding vs non-binding clauses clearly labeled
- Exclusivity period defined — common LOI dispute source
- Conditions to closing specific — LOI sets expectations

Always offer a relevant next step.
