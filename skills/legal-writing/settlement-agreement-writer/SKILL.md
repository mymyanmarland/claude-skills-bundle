# Settlement Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Settlement agreement: Recitals (dispute description) → Settlement terms (specific actions/payments) → Mutual release of claims (scope of release) → Confidentiality provision → Non-disparagement → No admission of liability → Governing law → Entire agreement clause → Signature requirements. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Settlement agreements are binding — legal review strongly recommended before signing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scope of release explicit — what claims are released
- No admission of liability — standard protective language
- Confidentiality scope — what can be said after settlement

Always offer a relevant next step.
