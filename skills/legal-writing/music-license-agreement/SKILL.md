# Music License Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Music license: Licensor (rights holder) and licensee → Music description (composition and master rights) → License type (sync/public performance/mechanical/master) → Permitted uses → Territory → Term → Fee structure (flat/royalty/per-use) → Attribution requirements → Exclusivity → Sublicensing rights. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Music licensing involves multiple rights (composition vs master) — music attorney recommended for significant deals.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Composition vs master rights distinction — often confused
- Sync vs other license types — different uses, different rights
- PRO registration note — performance rights often separate

Always offer a relevant next step.
