# SaaS Subscription Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

SaaS agreement: License grant (non-exclusive/non-transferable) → Service description and SLA → User accounts and security → Data ownership (customer owns their data) → Data processing and privacy → Payment and auto-renewal → Service modifications → Acceptable use → Limitation of liability → Term and termination → Data export on termination. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Customer data ownership explicit — customers need this assurance
- SLA terms specific if uptime guarantees made
- Data export on termination — customer should not be locked in

Always offer a relevant next step.
