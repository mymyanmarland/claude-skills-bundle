# Software License Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Software license: License grant (scope — non-exclusive/non-transferable/limited) → Permitted use → Restrictions (reverse engineering/sublicensing/competitive use) → Updates and support → Fees and payment → Term and termination → Warranty disclaimer → Limitation of liability → Data and privacy provisions. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- License grant scope specific — what they can and cannot do
- Limitation of liability — often the most negotiated clause
- Data provisions if SaaS processes personal data

Always offer a relevant next step.
