# LLC Operating Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Operating agreement: LLC information (name/state/purpose) → Members and initial contributions → Ownership percentages → Management structure (member vs manager-managed) → Voting rights and requirements → Profit and loss allocation and distribution → Member admission and withdrawal → Transfer restrictions → Dissolution → Tax treatment election. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Operating agreements are state-specific — review with business attorney in your state.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Management structure decision — member vs manager managed
- Voting thresholds for major decisions
- Transfer restrictions — prevents unwanted new members

Always offer a relevant next step.
