# Model and Photo Release Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Model release: Parties → Description of photographs/media → License grant (commercial/editorial/specific uses) → Compensation or consideration → Limitation on objectionable use → No endorsement implied → Perpetual or time-limited term → Parent or guardian signature for minors → Revocation limitations. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Commercial vs editorial use distinction
- Minor signature requirement — parental consent
- Consideration required — free releases can be revoked in some jurisdictions

Always offer a relevant next step.
