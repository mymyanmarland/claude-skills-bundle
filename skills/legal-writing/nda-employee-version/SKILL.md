# Employee Confidentiality Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Employee confidentiality agreement: Confidential information definition → Confidentiality obligation scope → Trade secrets specific protections → Computer and electronic information → Duration (during and post-employment) → Carve-outs (prior knowledge/public domain) → Permitted disclosures (to perform job) → Return of confidential information → Survival of obligations. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trade secrets specific — broader protection than NDA in some jurisdictions
- Post-employment duration reasonable — unreasonable terms unenforceable
- Return obligation on separation

Always offer a relevant next step.
