# Demand Letter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Demand letter: Factual background (neutral, documented) → Legal basis for demand → Specific amount or action demanded → Supporting documentation referenced → Deadline for response → Consequences of non-response → Professional closing. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Demand letters may be used as evidence — professional tone is essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Factual basis documented — not just claims
- Deadline specific and reasonable
- Tone professional — demand letters become exhibits

Always offer a relevant next step.
