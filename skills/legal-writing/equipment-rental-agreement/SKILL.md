# Equipment Rental Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Equipment rental: Equipment description (make/model/serial) → Rental period → Rental fee and deposit → Condition at pickup → Renter responsibilities (care/maintenance/insurance) → Prohibited uses → Return condition requirements → Late return fees → Loss or damage liability → Governing law. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Equipment condition documentation — prevents return disputes
- Renter insurance requirement — protects owner
- Late return fee calculation specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
