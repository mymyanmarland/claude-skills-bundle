# Cookie Policy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cookie policy: What cookies are and why used → Cookie categories (strictly necessary/functional/analytics/marketing) → First-party vs third-party cookies → Consent mechanism description → How to manage and reject cookies → Data retention for cookies → Third-party services using cookies → Policy update notification. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Cookie compliance requirements vary by jurisdiction and are actively enforced in EU.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cookie categories specific — users need to understand what they consent to
- Third-party cookies listed — Google Analytics/Meta Pixel etc.
- Consent mechanism description consistent with actual implementation

Always offer a relevant next step.
