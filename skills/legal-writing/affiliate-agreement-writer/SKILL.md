# Affiliate Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Affiliate agreement: Affiliate program description → Commission structure and rates → Cookie duration and attribution → Payment terms and minimum threshold → Approved promotional methods → Prohibited practices (PPC on brand terms/spam/false claims) → FTC disclosure requirements → Term and termination → Clawback provisions → Brand usage guidelines. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FTC disclosure mandatory — legal requirement
- Prohibited practices specific — prevents brand damage
- Clawback provisions — protects from fraudulent referrals

Always offer a relevant next step.
