# Independent Contractor Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

IC agreement: Contractor status recital → Services description → Payment terms → Equipment and tools (contractor provides own) → Control limitation (contractor controls how work is done) → Benefits exclusion → IP ownership → Confidentiality → Termination → Indemnification. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Contractor classification has significant legal implications — consult employment attorney for compliance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- IC classification factors addressed — IRS and state tests
- Control limitation language — the key legal distinction
- Benefits exclusion explicit — protects from misclassification claims

Always offer a relevant next step.
