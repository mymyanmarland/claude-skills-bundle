# Agency Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Agency agreement: Principal and agent identification → Territory or market segment → Products or services represented → Commission structure → Performance requirements → Exclusivity → Agent authority (what agent can commit principal to) → Reporting obligations → Term and termination → Non-solicitation post-termination. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Agent authority scope — the key legal liability issue
- Exclusivity vs non-exclusive — significant commercial decision
- Termination notice period — statutory minimum in some countries

Always offer a relevant next step.
