# Intellectual Property Policy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

IP policy: Scope (what IP covered) → Company ownership of work-related IP → Employee inventions outside scope (protections) → Prior inventions carve-out → Confidential information obligations → Patent and copyright registration responsibilities → IP violation reporting → Consequences of violation. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Prior inventions carve-out — employee protection provision
- Work-related scope definition — what company owns
- Employee rights preserved — over-reaching policies create problems

Always offer a relevant next step.
