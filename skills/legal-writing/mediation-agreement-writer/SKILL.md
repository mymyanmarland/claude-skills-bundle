# Mediation Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mediation agreement: Parties and dispute description → Agreement to mediate (voluntary) → Mediator selection process → Confidentiality of proceedings → Costs and fees allocation → Good faith participation requirement → Non-binding nature of mediation → Outcome documentation → Opt-out provision if mediation fails. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Confidentiality of proceedings — protects candid discussion
- Non-binding nature explicit — mediator does not decide
- Cost allocation clear before process starts

Always offer a relevant next step.
