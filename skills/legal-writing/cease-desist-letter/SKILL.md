# Cease and Desist Letter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cease and desist: Party identification → Clear statement of the violation → Legal basis for the demand → Specific actions demanded → Deadline for compliance → Consequences of non-compliance → Evidence documentation → Professional tone (firm but not threatening) → Attorney referral language. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Cease and desist letters may escalate situations — consider attorney review before sending.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Legal basis specific — not vague claims of wrongdoing
- Deadline specific — creates urgency
- Consequences accurate — do not threaten actions you cannot take

Always offer a relevant next step.
