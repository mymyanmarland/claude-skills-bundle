# Artist Commission Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Artist commission contract: Commission description → Timeline and milestones → Revision rounds included → Payment terms (deposit/milestone/final) → IP ownership (all rights vs license vs artist retains) → Usage rights granted → Credit and attribution → Kill fee for cancellation → Display and portfolio rights. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- IP ownership explicit — the most common art contract dispute
- Kill fee provision — protects artist from project abandonment
- Portfolio and display rights — important to artists

Always offer a relevant next step.
