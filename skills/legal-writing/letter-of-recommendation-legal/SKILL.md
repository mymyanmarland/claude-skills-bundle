# Legal Letter of Support Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Letter of support: Relationship and credibility establishment → Specific observations and examples → Character and qualifications → Support for the specific proceeding → Availability for follow-up → Professional closing. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Letters for legal proceedings should be reviewed by the attorney handling the matter.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specific examples — not generic character praise
- Relationship and observation basis clear
- Professional credentials of letter writer established

Always offer a relevant next step.
