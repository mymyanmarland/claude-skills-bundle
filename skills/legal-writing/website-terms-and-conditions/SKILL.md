# Website Terms and Conditions Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Website T&C: User acceptance → Account registration terms → Permitted use → Prohibited activities → Intellectual property rights → User content license → Payment terms if applicable → Disclaimer of warranties → Limitation of liability → Indemnification → Governing law and dispute resolution. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acceptance mechanism prominent — browsewrap vs clickwrap
- Prohibited activities specific to the site type
- User content section if UGC platform

Always offer a relevant next step.
