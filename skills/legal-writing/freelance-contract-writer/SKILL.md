# Freelance Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Freelance contract: Parties and recitals → Scope of work (detailed) → Deliverables and milestones → Payment terms (amount/schedule/late payment) → Intellectual property assignment → Confidentiality → Independent contractor status → Revision policy → Termination clause → Limitation of liability. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scope of work specific — vague scope leads to disputes
- IP assignment explicit — work-for-hire or license
- Termination clause mutual — both parties protected

Always offer a relevant next step.
