# Scope of Work Document Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scope of work: Project background and objectives → Deliverables (specific, measurable) → Deliverable acceptance criteria → Timeline and milestones → Out of scope (explicit exclusions) → Client responsibilities → Assumptions (if any do not hold, scope may change) → Change order process → Resources and access required. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acceptance criteria for each deliverable — prevents disputes
- Explicit exclusions — as important as inclusions
- Change order process — scope creep starts without this

Always offer a relevant next step.
