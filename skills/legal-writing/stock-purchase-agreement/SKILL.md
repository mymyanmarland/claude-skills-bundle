# Equity and Stock Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Equity agreement: Parties and recitals → Securities being purchased/granted → Purchase price or grant terms → Vesting schedule (standard 4 years, 1-year cliff) → Acceleration provisions → Repurchase rights → Transfer restrictions (right of first refusal) → Representations → Section 83(b) election note. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Equity agreements have significant tax and securities implications — attorney and accountant review essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Vesting schedule standard — 4 year/1 year cliff explanation
- 83(b) election timing — 30-day window is critical
- Securities law note — offering securities requires compliance

Always offer a relevant next step.
