# Rental Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rental agreement: Parties and property address → Lease term (start and end dates or month-to-month) → Rent amount and due date → Security deposit terms → Utilities responsibility → Maintenance and repair obligations → Pet policy → Guest policy → Subletting policy → Termination and notice requirements → Move-in/move-out inspection. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Rental law varies significantly by jurisdiction — review against local landlord-tenant law.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Jurisdiction note prominent — rental law varies enormously
- Security deposit terms match local law requirements
- Move-in inspection clause — protects both parties

Always offer a relevant next step.
