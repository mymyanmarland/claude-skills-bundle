# Release of Liability Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Release of liability: Activity or service description → Assumption of risk (informed) → Release language (waiver and release of all claims) → Indemnification → Emergency authorization → Governing law → Voluntary execution acknowledgment → Signature and date. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Enforceability of waivers varies significantly by jurisdiction and activity type.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specific activity named — generic releases are weaker
- Voluntary execution — informed consent language
- Jurisdiction note — releases not enforceable everywhere or for negligence

Always offer a relevant next step.
