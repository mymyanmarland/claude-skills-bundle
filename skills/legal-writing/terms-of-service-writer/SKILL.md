# Terms of Service Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Terms of service: Acceptance mechanism → Account terms → Acceptable use policy → Content ownership and license → Payment and refund terms → Limitation of liability → Warranty disclaimer → Governing law and dispute resolution → Termination provisions → Modification of terms. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Limitation of liability — the most legally protective clause
- Content license if user-generated content platform
- Dispute resolution (arbitration vs court) — significant choice

Always offer a relevant next step.
