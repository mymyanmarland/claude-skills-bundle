# Website Disclaimer Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Website disclaimer: Site content nature (informational only) → Not professional advice (specify: legal/medical/financial) → No client or patient relationship created → Accuracy and currency disclaim → Results not guaranteed → External links disclaimer → Jurisdiction and governing law → Use of site constitutes acceptance. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specific profession named — generic disclaimers are weaker
- No professional relationship language — key for liability
- Results disclaimer — important for coaches and consultants

Always offer a relevant next step.
