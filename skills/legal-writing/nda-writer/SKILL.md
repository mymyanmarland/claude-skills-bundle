# NDA Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

NDA draft: Parties identification → Definition of confidential information (broad vs narrow) → Obligations of receiving party → Exclusions from confidentiality (public domain/prior knowledge/independent development) → Term and duration → Remedies for breach → Governing law → Mutual vs one-way NDA selection → Signature block. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Definition of confidential information — the most important clause
- Term specified — perpetual vs time-limited
- Mutual vs one-way decision based on context

Always offer a relevant next step.
