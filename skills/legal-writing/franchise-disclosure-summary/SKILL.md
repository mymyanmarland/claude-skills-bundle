# Franchise Agreement Summary Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Franchise summary: Initial fees and ongoing royalties → Territory rights (exclusive vs protected vs none) → Term and renewal conditions → Performance requirements → Training and support obligations → Approved supplier requirements → Transfer and exit provisions → Non-compete scope → Dispute resolution mechanism → What franchisee can and cannot do. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: FDD review and franchise attorney consultation strongly recommended before any franchise investment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Territory rights — the source of most franchise disputes
- Exit provisions — often unfavorable, must be understood
- Non-compete scope — may affect future business activities

Always offer a relevant next step.
