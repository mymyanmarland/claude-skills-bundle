# Subscription Terms Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Subscription terms: Subscription tiers and pricing → Billing cycle and auto-renewal → Free trial terms → Cancellation policy (how to cancel, timing, refunds) → Pause and resume options → Price change notice → Account suspension and termination → Service level and availability → Data upon cancellation. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Auto-renewal clear and prominent — FTC guidance
- Cancellation process simple and accessible — legal requirements in some states
- Data handling on cancellation — user expects clarity

Always offer a relevant next step.
