# Records Request and Demand for Documents Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Records request: Parties identification → Legal authority for request (statute/contract right/discovery) → Specific documents or categories requested → Privilege log requirement if any documents withheld → Response deadline → Format requirements → Custodian certification → Consequences of non-compliance. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Legal basis for request specific — strengthens the demand
- Specific categories — not overly broad fishing expeditions
- Response deadline reasonable — proportionate to request

Always offer a relevant next step.
