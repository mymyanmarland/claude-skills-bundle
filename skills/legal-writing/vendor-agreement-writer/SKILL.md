# Vendor Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Vendor agreement: Parties and recitals → Products or services description → Pricing and payment terms → Delivery and acceptance → Warranties and representations → Quality standards → Confidential information → Indemnification → Insurance requirements → Termination → Governing law. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acceptance criteria specific — delivery vs acceptance distinction
- Insurance requirements — often overlooked but important
- Termination for convenience vs cause — different implications

Always offer a relevant next step.
