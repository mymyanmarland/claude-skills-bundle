# Content Licensing Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Content licensing: Licensor and licensee identification → Content description (specific) → License grant (exclusive vs non-exclusive) → Permitted uses → Prohibited uses → Territory → Term → Royalty or flat fee → Attribution requirements → Sublicensing rights → Termination for breach. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exclusive vs non-exclusive — the key licensing decision
- Territory and term — often forgotten in content licenses
- Attribution requirements specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
