# Web Development Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Web development contract: Project scope and deliverables → Technology stack and specifications → Development methodology → Milestones and timeline → Payment schedule tied to milestones → Client content provision responsibilities → Testing and acceptance process → IP ownership and license → Ongoing maintenance terms → Source code escrow consideration → Hosting and support post-launch. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acceptance criteria specific — what constitutes completion
- Client content obligations — delays often client-caused
- Source code delivery — client should own or have access to their code

Always offer a relevant next step.
