# Data Processing Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Data processing agreement: Controller and processor identification → Processing purpose and scope → Data subject categories → Processing instructions → Security measures → Subprocessor obligations → Data subject rights assistance → Breach notification → International transfers → Audit rights → Deletion or return on termination. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: DPAs must comply with applicable data protection law — legal review required.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Processing purpose explicit — processor cannot use data beyond instructions
- Security measures specific — not vague security commitment
- Breach notification timeline — 72 hours under GDPR

Always offer a relevant next step.
