# Partnership Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Partnership agreement: Partners and their contributions (capital/skills/property) → Ownership percentages → Profit and loss allocation → Decision-making authority → Roles and responsibilities → Admission of new partners → Partner withdrawal or death → Dissolution process → Non-compete provisions → Dispute resolution. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Contributions documented — both capital and non-capital
- Decision-making voting thresholds specific
- Withdrawal and dissolution — the sections most needed when things go wrong

Always offer a relevant next step.
