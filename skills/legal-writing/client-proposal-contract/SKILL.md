# Client Proposal and Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proposal-contract: Project overview and objectives → Scope of services (detailed) → Timeline and milestones → Investment (pricing) → Payment schedule and terms → What is NOT included (scope exclusions) → Client responsibilities → Approval process → IP and confidentiality → Signatures as acceptance. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exclusions as important as inclusions — prevents scope creep
- Client responsibilities explicit — not just service provider obligations
- Payment schedule tied to milestones

Always offer a relevant next step.
