# Employee Handbook Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Employee handbook sections: At-will employment statement → Equal opportunity policy → Code of conduct → Harassment and discrimination policy → Leave policies (vacation/sick/FMLA basics) → Remote work policy → Technology use policy → Confidentiality and IP → Disciplinary procedures → Acknowledgment page. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Employee handbooks can create legal obligations — review with employment attorney.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- At-will statement prominent — common handbook oversight
- Harassment policy specific — legally required in many jurisdictions
- Acknowledgment page — evidence of receipt

Always offer a relevant next step.
