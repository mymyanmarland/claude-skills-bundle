# Home Contractor Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home contractor agreement: Scope of work (detailed) → Materials specification → Price and payment schedule (avoid large upfront payment) → Timeline → Change order process → Permits and inspections → Cleanup obligations → Warranty on work → Lien waiver requirement → Insurance verification → Dispute resolution. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice. Note: Review against local contractor licensing requirements — unlicensed contractor work may not be covered by insurance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Payment schedule avoid large upfront — consumer protection
- Lien waiver requirement — protects homeowner from subcontractor liens
- Permit responsibility explicit — contractor typically responsible

Always offer a relevant next step.
