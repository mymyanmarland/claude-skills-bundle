# Consulting Agreement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Consulting agreement: Services description (specific) → Consultant independence (control limitation) → Compensation and expenses → Term and termination → Confidentiality → IP ownership (work product) → Non-solicitation → Conflict of interest → Representations and warranties → Limitation of liability. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Services description specific — prevents scope disputes
- IP ownership explicit — who owns the work product
- Non-solicitation scope — employees vs clients varies

Always offer a relevant next step.
