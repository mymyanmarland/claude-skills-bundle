# Event Vendor Contract Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Parties involved and their roles
- Transaction or situation details
- Jurisdiction if known
- Specific terms or requirements
- Purpose and context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Event vendor contract: Event details (date/location/time) → Services description → Deliverables and timeline → Payment and deposit terms → Cancellation and rescheduling policy → Force majeure → Liability limitations → Backup plan provisions → Image and content rights → Vendor substitution restrictions. Important: This skill produces educational drafts and templates only. Always have legal documents reviewed by a qualified attorney before signing or relying on them. This is not legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Force majeure clause — pandemic experience demonstrated importance
- Backup plan for vendor failure — critical for key vendors
- Cancellation policy mutual — both parties have protections

Always offer a relevant next step.
