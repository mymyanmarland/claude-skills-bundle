# Sales Enablement Content Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Competitors to address
- Top objections
- Sales team knowledge gaps
- Format preference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales enablement package: Competitive battlecard (vs specific competitor: their strengths/weaknesses/how to win/landmines to avoid) → Product one-pager (buyer-facing — problem/solution/proof/CTA) → Objection handling cheat sheet → Discovery question bank → Demo outline → Email templates by scenario. Each piece: purpose, when to use, how to use.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Battlecard honest about competitor strengths — rep trust requires honesty
- One-pager buyer-focused — not internal spec sheet
- Discovery questions generate real insight — not checkbox questions

Always offer a relevant next step.
