# Sales Presentation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Presentation purpose (discovery/demo/proposal/executive review)
- Audience size and seniority
- Time available
- Current presentation problems
- Deal stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Presentation guide: The problem with most sales presentations (too much about you) → Structure by presentation type → Executive presentation: lead with outcome, not agenda → Demo presentation: problem → aha → proof → next step → Proposal presentation: their situation → your recommendation → business case → terms → Design principles (60% white space, no bullet walls) → Presenter notes framework.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Buyer-centric structure — their outcomes first
- Design principles specific — not 'make it look good'
- Presenter notes separate from slides — different content

Always offer a relevant next step.
