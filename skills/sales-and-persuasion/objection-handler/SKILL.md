# Objection Handler Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Top 5-7 objections heard
- Price point
- Deal stage where objections arise
- Competitive context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Objection handling guide: For each objection: Acknowledge (validate, don't argue) → Clarify (is this the real objection?) → Respond (address with evidence/reframe/question) → Confirm (did that address it?). Objections covered: 'Too expensive' / 'We're happy with current vendor' / 'Not the right time' / 'Need to think about it' / 'Need to talk to [decision maker]' / 'Send me information' / '[Competitor] is cheaper'. Plus: distinguishing real objection from stall.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acknowledge before responding — arguing creates resistance
- Clarify if it's a real objection or a stall
- Each response specific — not generic 'let me explain our value'

Always offer a relevant next step.
