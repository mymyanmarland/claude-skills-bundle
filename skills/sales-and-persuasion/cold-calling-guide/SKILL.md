# Cold Calling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target prospect type
- Product and value proposition
- Gatekeeper situations
- Call volume expectations
- Current cold calling success rate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cold calling guide: Call reluctance addressing (mindset before tactics) → Gatekeeper navigation → Decision maker opening (permission-based in 10 seconds) → The 'pattern interrupt' opener → Elevator pitch under 10 seconds → Bridge to discovery → Handling early objections ('I'm busy' / 'Send me info' / 'Not interested') → Voicemail strategy → Call follow-up email sent within 5 minutes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Call reluctance addressed first — mindset enables tactics
- Permission-based opening reduces resistance
- Voicemail strategy included — most calls go to voicemail

Always offer a relevant next step.
