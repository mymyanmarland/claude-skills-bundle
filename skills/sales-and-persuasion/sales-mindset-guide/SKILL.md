# Sales Mindset Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sales role and type
- Specific mindset challenges
- Rejection frequency
- Performance goals
- Current mental blockers

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales mindset guide: Rejection reframe (rejection is information, not personal) → Abundance vs scarcity mindset → Detachment from outcomes (control activity, not results) → The numbers game reality (quota is a math problem) → Confidence building practices → Managing the emotional sales cycle → Motivation structure (intrinsic vs extrinsic) → High-performance recovery from slumps.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rejection reframe specific and believable — not toxic positivity
- Activity control vs outcome control distinction concrete
- Slump recovery protocol specific — not 'push through'

Always offer a relevant next step.
