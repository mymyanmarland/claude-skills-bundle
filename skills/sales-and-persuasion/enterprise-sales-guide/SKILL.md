# Enterprise Sales Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Enterprise account type
- Deal size and complexity
- Stakeholder count
- Sales cycle length
- Competitive situation

## Step 2 — Core Output

Enterprise sales guide: Multi-stakeholder management (economic buyer/technical buyer/user buyer/coach) → Enterprise buying process navigation → Security/legal/procurement stages → Executive access strategy → Proof of concept design → Business case development (help them justify internally) → Procurement negotiation → Mutual success plan → Long-term account expansion.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- All four buyer types addressed — not just the champion
- Business case helps them sell internally — your job to make it easy
- Mutual success plan creates accountability post-close

Always offer a relevant next step.
