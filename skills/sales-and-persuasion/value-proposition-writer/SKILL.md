# Value Proposition Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Target customer
- Top 3 benefits delivered
- Primary competitor alternatives
- Proof points available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Value proposition development: Customer job analysis (functional/emotional/social) → Pains addressed → Gains created → Value proposition statement (formula: For [customer] who [need], [product] is [category] that [benefit]. Unlike [alternative], [product] [key differentiator].) → 5 copy variations (headline/subheadline/elevator/one-liner/tagline) → Test against: is it specific? Is it measurable? Can a competitor claim the same thing?

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Customer job analysis before writing — what are they hiring your product to do?
- Competitor comparison included — differentiates
- Specificity test applied — vague value props rejected

Always offer a relevant next step.
