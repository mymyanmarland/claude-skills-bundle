# Demo Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Demo length
- Prospect pain from discovery
- Stage in sales cycle
- Common demo objections

## Step 2 — Core Output

Demo design: Pre-demo preparation (customize to their situation — not generic) → The demo hypothesis (what must they believe after?) → Problem-first opening (their words from discovery) → The aha moment (design for one transformative insight) → Feature selection (fewer features — more impact) → Handling questions during demo → Trial close after key moments → Next step ask.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Customized to their situation — not generic feature tour
- One aha moment designed deliberately
- Trial closes placed after compelling moments — not just at end

Always offer a relevant next step.
