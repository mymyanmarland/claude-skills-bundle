# Closing Technique Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Deal type and size
- Sales cycle stage
- Decision-maker situation
- Objections already handled
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Closing guide: The best close is a great sales process (if you have to hard close, something went wrong earlier) → Trial closes throughout (gauge commitment) → Assumptive close (when confidence is high) → Summary close (recap value agreed) → Next step close (always have one) → Decision deadline (when and how to create genuine urgency) → What to do when they go silent.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Best close = great earlier process — mindset shift
- Trial closes used throughout — not just at the end
- Silence protocol specific — how to re-engage without pressure

Always offer a relevant next step.
