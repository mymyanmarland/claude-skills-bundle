# Account-Based Selling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target account size and type
- Deal size target
- Stakeholders involved
- Current account penetration
- Marketing alignment

## Step 2 — Core Output

ABS strategy: Account selection criteria → Account research framework (org chart/initiatives/budget cycle/tech stack) → Multi-stakeholder mapping (economic buyer/champion/user/blocker) → Personalized outreach by persona → Account-level content → Executive engagement strategy → Champion development → Internal political navigation → Marketing + sales alignment on target accounts.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Multi-stakeholder mapped before outreach
- Champion development explicit — someone who wants you to win
- Executive engagement strategy includes how to get there

Always offer a relevant next step.
