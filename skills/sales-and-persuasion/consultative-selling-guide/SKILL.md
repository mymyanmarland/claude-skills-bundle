# Consultative Selling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service complexity
- Deal size and relationship length
- Buyer sophistication
- Sales cycle stage
- Current selling approach

## Step 2 — Core Output

Consultative selling guide: The mindset shift (advisor not vendor) → Diagnostic questions vs pitch questions → Active listening techniques → Insight selling (teach them something they don't know about their problem) → Recommendation framing (what's right for them, not what you sell) → Building trust over multiple touches → Long-term relationship sales vs transactional.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Diagnostic questions prioritized — diagnose before prescribing
- Insight selling — bring a perspective they haven't considered
- Long-term relationship approach explicit — not just deal-by-deal

Always offer a relevant next step.
