# Sales Interview Prep Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sales role type (SDR/AE/enterprise)
- Company type and stage
- Interview format
- Experience level
- Specific concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales interview prep: Common questions by role type → The 'sell me this pen' variations and how to handle them → Role play scenarios to practice → How to demonstrate sales skills in the interview itself → Questions to ask that prove strategic thinking → How to discuss quota attainment honestly → Reference preparation → Offer evaluation for sales roles (quota attainability check).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Role play scenarios specific to role type
- Questions to ask that demonstrate sales thinking
- Quota attainability check — the most important job evaluation step for salespeople

Always offer a relevant next step.
