# Sales Call Debrief Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Call type (discovery/demo/negotiation)
- What happened on the call
- What went well vs poorly
- Deal stage after call
- Win/loss context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Debrief framework: What did I learn about them (problems, priorities, process, people, timeline, budget) → What went well → What should I have done differently → What's their next step and mine → What's the probability of winning → What do I need to find out → What will I do differently next call. 5-minute process — sustainable if brief.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Debrief done immediately while fresh — not next day
- Learning specific — not 'could have been better'
- Next step and probability updated in CRM immediately

Always offer a relevant next step.
