# Inbound Lead Conversion Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Lead source types
- Lead volume
- Response time current
- Qualification criteria
- Sales cycle

## Step 2 — Core Output

Inbound conversion system: Speed-to-lead (5-minute response = 100× better than 30 min) → Lead scoring and routing → First response script (personal, not automated-feeling) → Qualification questions per lead source → Demo/call booking friction removal → Lead nurture for not-ready leads → Re-engagement for gone-dark leads → Conversion rate by stage tracking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 5-minute response standard emphasized — data is compelling
- First response personal — not obviously automated
- Not-ready lead nurture — don't discard, nurture

Always offer a relevant next step.
