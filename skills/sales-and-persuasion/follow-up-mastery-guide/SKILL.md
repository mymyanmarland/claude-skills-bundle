# Follow-Up Mastery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Deal type and stage
- Follow-up frequency
- What's worked and not worked
- Prospect communication preferences
- Going-dark patterns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Follow-up system: Value-add follow-up (every touch adds something — not 'just checking in') → Timing strategy (when to follow up based on stage) → Channel variation (email/phone/LinkedIn/video) → Bump email (move old thread to top) → Pattern interrupt (something unexpected to re-engage) → The persistence threshold (when to stop) → Long-term nurture for not-now prospects.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every follow-up adds value — 'just checking in' banned
- Pattern interrupt technique specific and memorable
- Long-term nurture for not-now — don't discard them

Always offer a relevant next step.
