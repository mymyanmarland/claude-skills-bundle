# Sales Email Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Target prospect type
- Pain point to address
- Sequence length
- Tone: direct/consultative/casual

## Step 2 — Core Output

5-email sequence: Email 1 (ultra short — insight about their situation, one question) → Email 2 (relevant case study, social proof) → Email 3 (value-add content, soft CTA) → Email 4 (direct ask — specific meeting request) → Email 5 (breakup — genuine, reopens door). Subject lines for each. Each under 100 words. Different angle per email.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each email different angle — not same pitch repeated
- Subject lines designed for cold-list opens
- Breakup email genuine — no fake 'last chance'

Always offer a relevant next step.
