# Sales Process Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model and deal type
- Average deal size and cycle
- Team size
- Current informal process
- CRM in use

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales process design: Stage definitions (each stage has a specific name, buyer action that advances it, and seller action) → Exit criteria (what must be true to move to next stage) → Playbook per stage (what to do, what to learn, what to complete) → CRM stage alignment → Stage probability → Process exceptions (when to skip stages) → Process review cadence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stage exit criteria buyer-action based — not seller-action
- Probability by stage based on historical data
- Process exceptions explicit — rigid process hurts complex deals

Always offer a relevant next step.
