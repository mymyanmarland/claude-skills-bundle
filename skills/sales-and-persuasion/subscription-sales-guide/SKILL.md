# Subscription Sales Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Subscription product type
- Price point and tiers
- Churn context
- Trial or freemium model
- Sales motion: product-led/sales-led/hybrid

## Step 2 — Core Output

Subscription sales guide: Sales motion selection (when product-led works vs sales-led) → Trial conversion optimization → Annual vs monthly conversation → Churn risk in sales (selling to wrong customers churns them) → Expansion revenue in sales context → Multi-year deals (when to offer discounts for commitment) → Net revenue retention as a sales metric.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Churn risk in sales explicit — selling to wrong customer hurts
- Annual vs monthly conversation script included
- NRR as sales metric — expansion is part of the sales job

Always offer a relevant next step.
