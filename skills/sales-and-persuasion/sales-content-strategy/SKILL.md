# Sales Content Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sales stages
- Common buyer questions per stage
- Current content gaps
- Sales team feedback
- Buyer personas

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales content strategy: Buyer question mapping (what do they need to know at each stage?) → Content audit (what exists vs what's needed) → Priority content gaps → Format selection (blog/video/one-pager/case study/template) → Sales-to-content feedback loop → Content usage tracking → Sales-specific vs marketing-general content distinction → Personalization guidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Buyer questions mapped — content serves their needs
- Sales feedback loop systematic — not occasional
- Usage tracking identifies what reps actually use

Always offer a relevant next step.
