# Sales Territory Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Territory size and account count
- Deal size and sales cycle
- Tier A/B/C criteria
- Time available
- Current coverage gaps

## Step 2 — Core Output

Territory plan: Account tiering (Tier A: highest potential + highest probability / Tier B: high potential / Tier C: low potential) → Time allocation (Tier A: 60%, Tier B: 30%, Tier C: 10%) → Coverage model (calls/meetings/events per tier) → White space analysis (unconquered accounts) → Quarterly territory review → Pipeline coverage ratio target.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Time allocation explicit and enforced — Tier A gets the time
- White space analysis finds uncovered opportunities
- Pipeline coverage ratio target gives weekly quota discipline

Always offer a relevant next step.
