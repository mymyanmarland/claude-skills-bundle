# Negotiation Strategy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What is being negotiated
- Your position and goals
- Other party's likely position
- Walk-away point
- Relationship importance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Negotiation strategy: BATNA (Best Alternative to No Agreement — know yours) → Their BATNA (estimate theirs) → Zone of Possible Agreement mapping → Anchor strategy (who goes first and why) → Concession plan (what to give up in what order, trading up in value) → Pressure tactics to expect and responses → Package vs single-issue negotiation → Closing the deal once agreed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- BATNA defined before negotiation — non-negotiable
- Concessions planned in advance — not reactive
- Never split the difference tactics included where relevant

Always offer a relevant next step.
