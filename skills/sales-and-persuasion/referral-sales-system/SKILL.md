# Referral Sales System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Customer base
- Product type
- Current referral volume
- Incentive options
- Sales team capacity

## Step 2 — Core Output

Referral system: When to ask (at moment of success — not randomly) → How to ask (specific request, not 'anyone you know') → Referral ask script → Incentive structure (for referrer and referee) → Tracking and attribution → Closed-loop feedback (tell referrer the outcome) → Systematizing through CRM → Expanding from customers to partners.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Timing specific — peak happiness moment
- Ask specific — not 'do you know anyone'
- Referrer notified of outcome — closes the loop and motivates future referrals

Always offer a relevant next step.
