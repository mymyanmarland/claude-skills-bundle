# Upsell & Cross-Sell Playbook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service portfolio
- Customer base
- Average current deal size
- Expansion triggers
- Customer success data

## Step 2 — Core Output

Expansion playbook: Trigger identification (usage signals, business growth, new hire, funding event) → Product/service adjacency map (what naturally follows what they have) → Expansion conversation timing (after they've achieved value) → ROI case building (from their experience) → Multi-product champion development → Account review meeting design → Expansion quota vs new logo quota balance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Timing after value achievement — not just 90 days post-close
- Trigger signals specific and trackable
- Expansion conversation framed as helping them more — not selling

Always offer a relevant next step.
