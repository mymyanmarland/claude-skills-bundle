# Pricing Negotiation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Deal size
- Pricing flexibility
- Common discount requests
- Competitive situation

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pricing negotiation guide: Anchor high (don't start at your target) → Discount in exchange for something (never give for free) → What to trade: payment terms/case study/expanded scope/faster close → The 'let me check' technique → Quoting without apologizing → Handling 'your competitor is cheaper' → Walking away vs accommodating → Protecting margin culture.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every concession traded — nothing given for free
- 'Let me check' creates time and reduces impulsiveness
- Walking away as negotiating tool — when and how

Always offer a relevant next step.
