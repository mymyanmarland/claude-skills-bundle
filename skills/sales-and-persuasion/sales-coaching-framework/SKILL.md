# Sales Coaching Framework Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Team size and experience
- Common performance gaps
- Coaching time available
- Deal types
- Metrics to improve

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching framework: Weekly 1:1 structure (deal review + skill development + pipeline) → Call recording review protocol (what to look for) → Role play design (specific scenarios) → Skill development prioritization (one skill at a time) → Coaching vs managing distinction → Rep self-assessment before coaching → Progress tracking → How to coach top performers differently from strugglers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One skill at a time — not everything at once
- Rep self-assessment first — more receptive to coaching after
- Top performer coaching different — stretch, not fix

Always offer a relevant next step.
