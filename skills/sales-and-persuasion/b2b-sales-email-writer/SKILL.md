# B2B Sales Email Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target company and persona
- Your product and value
- Specific pain to address
- Personalization available
- Desired next step

## Step 2 — Core Output

B2B email formula: Subject (5 words max, specific to them) → Opening (personalized insight about their company/role/situation — 1 sentence) → Problem identification (their likely pain — in their language) → Proof (brief — case study or metric) → CTA (one specific low-friction ask). Under 100 words. 3 variations at different personalization levels. Subject line A/B pairs.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Under 100 words — busy executives won't read more
- Opening proves you researched them — not template
- One CTA — not three options

Always offer a relevant next step.
