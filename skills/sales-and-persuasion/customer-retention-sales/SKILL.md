# Customer Retention Sales Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Contract length and renewal timing
- Churn risk indicators
- Expansion opportunities
- Relationship status
- Competitive threat level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Retention sales guide: Early warning system (health score signals that trigger action) → Proactive renewal conversation (before renewal date, not at) → Value reinforcement (remind them of ROI achieved) → Expansion as retention (more product = more stickiness) → Competitive threat response → The save playbook (when churn is imminent) → Executive sponsor involvement triggers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Proactive renewal 90 days out — not at renewal date
- Value reinforcement quantified — their specific ROI
- Save playbook for active churn risk — not preventive measures

Always offer a relevant next step.
