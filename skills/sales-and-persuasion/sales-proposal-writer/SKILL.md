# Sales Proposal Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Client and their situation
- Problem and desired outcome
- Your solution and differentiation
- Pricing and terms
- Decision timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proposal structure: Executive summary (lead with their outcome — not your company) → Understanding of their situation (show you listened) → Proposed solution (specific to them — not generic) → Why us (3 specific differentiators) → Investment (pricing framed as ROI) → Implementation timeline → Success metrics → Next steps. Their name appears more than your company name.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Executive summary leads with their outcome — not your company history
- Understanding section proves you listened in discovery
- Investment framed as ROI — cost compared to cost of inaction

Always offer a relevant next step.
