# Champion Builder Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Account and deal context
- Current contacts
- Champion candidate profile
- What they personally gain
- Deal stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Champion development guide: Champion identification (who benefits if you win, who has influence) → Champion vetting (are they real?) → Value exchange (what's in it for them personally?) → Arming the champion (what they need to sell internally) → The internal pitch (help them build it) → Coach vs champion distinction → When your champion goes dark → Building multiple champions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Champion's personal benefit identified — champions help when they gain
- Arming the champion specific — talk tracks, ROI data
- Multiple champions reduce single-point-of-failure risk

Always offer a relevant next step.
