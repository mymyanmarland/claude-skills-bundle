# Sales Account Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Account name and size
- Current relationship and revenue
- Growth opportunity
- Key stakeholders
- Competitive threats

## Step 2 — Core Output

Account plan: Account overview (business, priorities, org structure) → Current state (what we have, what we don't) → Growth opportunity map (whitespace by product/department/geography) → Stakeholder map (current relationships + gaps) → Competitive situation → Strategic objectives (12-month revenue and relationship goals) → Action plan (quarterly milestones) → Executive sponsorship plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Whitespace map specific — by product and business unit
- Stakeholder gaps identified — not just current contacts
- Quarterly milestones make annual plan actionable

Always offer a relevant next step.
