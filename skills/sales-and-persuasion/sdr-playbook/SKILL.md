# SDR Playbook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target market
- Outreach channels
- Daily activity targets
- ICP definition
- Handoff to AE criteria

## Step 2 — Core Output

SDR playbook: ICP definition (specific enough to find in 30 seconds) → Daily activity targets (calls/emails/LinkedIn touches) → Account research protocol (5 minutes max per account) → Multi-channel sequence design → Cold call script → Email templates → LinkedIn strategy → Objection handling for prospecting stage → Meeting qualification criteria → AE handoff template → Metrics to track.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- ICP specific enough to search in 30 seconds
- Research protocol time-boxed — research kills selling time
- AE handoff criteria specific — prevents wasted AE time

Always offer a relevant next step.
