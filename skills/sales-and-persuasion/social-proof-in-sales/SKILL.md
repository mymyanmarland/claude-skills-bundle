# Social Proof in Sales Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Available social proof (case studies/testimonials/references)
- Deal stage
- Prospect industry and size
- Specific objections to address
- Competitive context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social proof deployment guide: Case study selection (match to prospect situation — industry/size/problem) → When to use in sales cycle → How to tell the case study story in conversation (not read from a PDF) → Reference call setup and management → Testimonial collection for objection handling → Logo drops (when appropriate vs when off-putting) → Creating social proof when you don't have it yet.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Social proof matched to prospect situation — not just most impressive
- Reference call setup protects the reference relationship
- Creating social proof when limited — what to do with few customers

Always offer a relevant next step.
