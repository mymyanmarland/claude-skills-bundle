# Sales Team Meeting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Team size and distribution
- Meeting frequency
- Current meeting problems
- Performance metrics to review
- Skill development needs

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales meeting guide: Weekly meeting structure (wins → metrics → pipeline → skill → announcements — in that order) → Win celebration protocol → Pipeline review approach (not deal interrogation) → Skill segment design (one skill, 10 minutes, real scenario) → Meeting time limit (60 minutes max) → Pre-meeting prep requirements → Asynchronous alternatives for info sharing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wins first — sets energetic tone
- Pipeline review is collaborative — not interrogation
- Skill segment every meeting — compound learning effect

Always offer a relevant next step.
