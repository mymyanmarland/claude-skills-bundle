# Procurement Navigation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Deal size
- Procurement maturity of account
- Legal and security involvement
- Timeline pressure
- Current procurement stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Procurement navigation: Getting above procurement (economic buyer relationship essential) → Understanding their process (ask early — don't assume) → Procurement timeline management → Security review preparation → Legal/contract negotiation approach → Standard contract vs your contract → Common procurement delays and prevention → Negotiating final terms without giving back what's already agreed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Economic buyer relationship before procurement engagement
- Timeline mapped early — surprises kill deals
- What's already agreed stays agreed — negotiation discipline

Always offer a relevant next step.
