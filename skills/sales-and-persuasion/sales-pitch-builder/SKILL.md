# Sales Pitch Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Buyer type and their priorities
- Deal stage
- Competitors likely in play
- Time available for pitch

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales pitch: Opening hook (start with their world, not your product) → Situation validation (show you understand their reality) → Problem agitation (make the cost of status quo clear) → Solution introduction → Proof (case study or demo moment) → Investment → Next step. For each buyer type: what they care about most → how to lead with it.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pitch starts with their world — not product features
- Problem agitation quantified — cost of inaction specific
- One clear next step — not multiple options

Always offer a relevant next step.
