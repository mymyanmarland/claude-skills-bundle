# Pricing Page Copy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Pricing tiers and structure
- Target customers per tier
- Most common pricing objections
- Value of each tier
- Competitor pricing context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pricing page: Tier naming (meaningful names — not Starter/Pro/Enterprise as defaults) → Value emphasis per tier (outcomes, not features) → Recommended tier highlighting → Annual vs monthly toggle copy → FAQ section (top 5 pricing objections answered) → Trust signals near price → Money-back guarantee language → Contact sales CTA for enterprise. Reduces friction at highest-friction moment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tier names meaningful — signal who each is for
- FAQ answers pricing objections proactively
- Annual toggle copy shows savings clearly — increases take rate

Always offer a relevant next step.
