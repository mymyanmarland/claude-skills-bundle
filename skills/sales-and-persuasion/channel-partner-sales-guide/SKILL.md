# Channel Partner Sales Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Partner types (resellers/VARs/integrators/consultants)
- Deal size
- Current direct vs channel mix
- Partner incentive budget

## Step 2 — Core Output

Channel program: Partner type selection (which adds value for which customer segment?) → Partner recruitment and vetting → Value proposition to partners (why sell your product?) → Partner enablement (training/certification/tools) → Deal registration → Co-selling vs channel-only decision → Partner incentive structure → Partner account management → Channel conflict management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value proposition to partners explicit — they have choices
- Channel conflict policy proactive — not reactive
- Partner enablement depth determines partner success

Always offer a relevant next step.
