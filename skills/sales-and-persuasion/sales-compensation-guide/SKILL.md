# Sales Compensation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sales role type
- Revenue targets
- Deal size and complexity
- Sales cycle length
- Business priorities

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comp plan design: Base-to-variable split by role (SDR: 70/30, AE: 50/50, enterprise: 60/40) → Quota-setting methodology (realistic is motivating — impossible is demoralizing) → Accelerators above quota → SPIFs for priority products → Clawback provisions → Payout frequency → Non-monetary motivators → Common comp plan mistakes (too complex / perverse incentives / unachievable quota).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Quota achievable by top 70% of reps — or it doesn't motivate
- Accelerators genuinely exciting above quota
- Perverse incentive check — does the plan incentivize the right behavior?

Always offer a relevant next step.
