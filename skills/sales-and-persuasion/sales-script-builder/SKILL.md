# Sales Script Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Call type (cold/warm/demo/closing)
- Product or service
- Target prospect
- Common objections at this stage
- Call goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales script: Opening (permission-based — don't assume they have time) → Elevator pitch (10 seconds — specific, not general) → Bridge question (from pitch to discovery) → Discovery questions (3-5 key questions) → Objection responses (in script) → Transition to next step → Closing for next step. Branching script: if they say X → say Y. Talker-to-listener ratio notes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Permission-based opening — respects their time
- Talker-to-listener ratio target (40/60) noted
- Branching for common deviations — not linear only

Always offer a relevant next step.
