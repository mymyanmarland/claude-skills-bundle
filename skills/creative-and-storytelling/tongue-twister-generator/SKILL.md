# Tongue Twister Generator Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Theme or topic**: (animals / food / jobs / custom / any)
- **Target sound or letter**: (s / sh / th / r / p / b / specific combo)
- **Difficulty**: Easy (kids) / Medium / Hard (speech practice)
- **Length**: One line / Two lines / Four lines / Verse
- **Purpose**: Fun / Speech therapy / Language learning / Performance

## Step 2 — Tongue Twister Craft Rules
- **Consonant clusters** at the start of words create the most difficulty: bl, cr, str, spl
- **Near-homophone confusion**: words that sound almost identical create the best trips
- **Rhythm matters**: twisters work best in iambic or trochaic patterns
- **Alliteration alone isn't enough** — the sound shift between words is the key

## Step 3 — Output: Sets of Twisters
Generate 3-5 twisters per request, ranging from easier to harder:

```
## Tongue Twisters: [Theme/Sound]

### Easy
[Single line, 6-8 words, one dominant sound]

### Medium
[Two lines, more complex sound combinations, some rhyme]

### Hard
[Three or four lines, multiple sound conflicts, fast rhythm]

### Expert Level
[Maximum difficulty — near-homophones, consonant clusters, rhythm traps]

---

### How to practice:
1. Say it slowly 3x — get it right before speeding up
2. Speed up gradually — muscle memory, not rushing
3. Record yourself — you'll hear the mistakes you feel you're making correctly
```

Always offer: "Want twisters in a specific language or accent?"
