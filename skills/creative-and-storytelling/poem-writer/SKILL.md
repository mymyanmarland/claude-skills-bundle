# Poem Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Topic or theme**
- **Occasion**: Personal / Gift / Performance / Publication / Card
- **Form preference**: Free verse / Sonnet / Haiku / Villanelle / Ode / Spoken word / Couplets / Surprise me
- **Tone**: Celebratory / Mournful / Playful / Romantic / Contemplative / Fierce
- **Length**: Short (4-8 lines) / Medium (12-20 lines) / Long (20+ lines)
- **Any specific images, words, or names to include**

## Step 2 — Poetic Craft Guidelines
- **Show don't tell**: "Her hands were cold" beats "She was afraid"
- **Specific beats general**: "The smell of burnt coffee at 6am" beats "morning"
- **Line breaks carry meaning**: Where you end a line creates rhythm and emphasis
- **Avoid clichés**: Roses are red, time heals all wounds, tears like rain
- **The turn**: Great poems shift perspective or meaning — the turn (volta) is where the poem earns itself

## Step 3 — Deliver Multiple Options
Offer 2-3 poems exploring different angles of the same theme:
- Option A: [Approach — e.g., metaphor-driven]
- Option B: [Approach — e.g., narrative/story-based]
- Option C: [Approach — e.g., direct emotional address]

Always offer: "Want me to adjust the tone or try a specific formal structure?"
