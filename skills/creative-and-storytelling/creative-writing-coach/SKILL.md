# Creative Writing Coach Skill
## Step 1 — Identify the Need
- **Mode A**: Feedback on a piece (user pastes their writing)
- **Mode B**: Craft lesson (user wants to learn a specific skill)
- **Mode C**: Exercise (user wants a prompt-based practice)
- **Genre**: Fiction / Memoir / Essay / Poetry / Script

## Step 2 — Feedback Framework (Mode A)
When reviewing a piece:

```
## Writing Feedback: [Title/Excerpt]

### What's Working
[Specific praise — name the technique and why it works]
[At least 2-3 genuine strengths before any critique]

### Top 3 Development Areas
**1. [Issue — e.g., "Telling instead of showing"]**
From your draft: "[quoted line]"
Consider: "[rewritten version demonstrating the fix]"
Why it matters: [Brief explanation of the craft principle]

**2. [Issue]**
[Same structure]

**3. [Issue]**
[Same structure]

### Line-Level Notes
[Specific sentences worth examining — for rhythm, word choice, clarity]

### Big Picture
[One paragraph on the overall effect and the single most important thing to work on next]
```

## Step 3 — Craft Lessons (Mode B)
Teach any craft element:
- Show vs. Tell
- Dialogue mechanics
- Scene structure (goal, conflict, outcome)
- Pacing and tension
- Point of view
- Opening lines
- Endings

Each lesson: Explanation + Bad example + Good example + Practice exercise

## Step 4 — Writing Exercises (Mode C)
Generate targeted exercises:
- Constraint exercises (write without adjectives / in 50 words / in second person)
- Imitation exercises (write in the style of a specific passage)
- Revision exercises (take weak writing and make it specific)

Always offer: "Want me to do a line edit on a specific paragraph?"
