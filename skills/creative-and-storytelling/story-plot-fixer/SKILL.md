# Story Plot Fixer Skill
## Step 1 — Understand the Problem
Ask for:
- **Brief story summary** (2-3 sentences: who, what they want, what's in the way)
- **Where the story breaks down**: Beginning / Middle / End / Specific scene
- **Symptoms they notice**: Feels slow / Characters do nothing / Ending doesn't land / Readers lose interest / Plot holes

## Step 2 — Diagnose Common Problems

| Symptom | Likely Diagnosis | Fix |
|---------|-----------------|-----|
| Story feels slow | Protagonist is reactive, not active | Give them a clear goal with a ticking clock |
| Middle sags | No escalating stakes | Add a midpoint shift that raises the cost of failure |
| Ending doesn't land | Theme not set up early enough | Plant thematic seeds in Act 1 |
| Plot hole | Cause-effect chain broken | Trace backward from the hole to find where logic breaks |
| Characters feel passive | External plot happening TO them | Make protagonist's choices drive events |
| Reader doesn't care | No clear character want + obstacle | Sharpen the core dramatic question |

## Step 3 — Story Structure Check

```
## Plot Diagnosis: [Story Title]

### The Core Dramatic Question
"Will [protagonist] achieve [goal] despite [obstacle]?"
Your story's CDQ: [Identify it — if unclear, that's the first problem]

### Three-Act Audit
Act 1 (first 25%): Does it establish: character want, world rules, inciting incident?
[Yes/No + specific note]

Act 2 (middle 50%): Does it escalate: attempts fail, stakes rise, midpoint shift?
[Yes/No + specific note]

Act 3 (final 25%): Does it deliver: climax tests the theme, resolution earns the ending?
[Yes/No + specific note]

### Top Problems Identified
1. [Problem + specific fix suggestion]
2. [Problem + fix]
3. [Problem + fix]

### One Structural Rewrite to Consider
[Specific, concrete suggestion — a scene to add, move, or cut]
```

Always offer: "Want me to outline a fixed version of the problematic section?"
