# Book Club Discussion Guide Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Book title and author**
- **Group size and type**: (mixed ages / all adults / literary focus / casual)
- **Meeting length**: 1 hour / 2 hours
- **Has everyone finished the book?** (spoiler threshold)

## Step 2 — Discussion Guide Structure

```
## Book Club Guide: [Title] by [Author]

### About the Book (2 minutes of context)
[2-3 sentences: publication context, what kind of book it is, why it matters]

### About the Author
[3-4 sentences: relevant biography, how their life connects to the book's themes]

---

### Opening Question (5 min — get everyone talking)
[A low-stakes, accessible question: "What was your overall reaction — did you enjoy it?
What surprised you most?"]

---

### Themes to Explore

**Theme 1: [e.g., Identity and belonging]**
- [Discussion question — specific to this book]
- [Follow-up question — invites personal connection]

**Theme 2: [Theme]**
- [Question]
- [Follow-up]

**Theme 3: [Theme]**
- [Question]
- [Follow-up]

---

### Character Deep Dives
- Was [protagonist] sympathetic to you? Why or why not?
- Which character did you understand the most — and least?
- Did any character change your mind about something?

---

### Craft & Writing Questions
- How did the author's [writing style / structure / POV choice] affect your reading?
- Which passage or scene stayed with you most? Read it aloud if you have it.

---

### The Big Questions (save for last 20 min)
- What is the book ultimately about — beyond its plot?
- Did this book change how you think about [central topic]?
- Who would you recommend this to, and why?

---

### Activity Ideas
- [Activity 1: e.g., "Share one quote that resonated — read aloud and discuss why"]
- [Activity 2: e.g., "Rate the book 1-5 before and after discussion — did it change?"]

### Pairing Suggestions
- Similar book: [Title] by [Author]
- Film adaptation or related documentary: [If applicable]
- Thematic pairing: [A book on the same theme]
```

Always offer: "Want me to create a guide for your next book club pick too?"
