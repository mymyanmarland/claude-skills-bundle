# Song Lyric Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Theme or story of the song**
- **Genre**: Pop / Country / R&B / Folk / Rock / Hip-hop / Gospel / Indie
- **Mood**: Upbeat / Sad / Angry / Hopeful / Romantic / Nostalgic
- **Structure needed**: Full song / Just chorus / Verse + chorus / Bridge only
- **Rhyme scheme preference**: ABAB / AABB / ABCB / No rhyme / Slant rhyme OK
- **Any specific lines or images they want included**

## Step 2 — Song Structure

```
## [Song Title]
Genre: [X] | Mood: [X]

[VERSE 1 — sets the scene, introduces character/situation]
Line 1 (A)
Line 2 (B)
Line 3 (A)
Line 4 (B)

[PRE-CHORUS — builds tension, leads to the release of the chorus]
Line 1
Line 2

[CHORUS — the emotional peak, most memorable, repeated]
Line 1 ← hook line (title often here)
Line 2
Line 3
Line 4

[VERSE 2 — advances the story or deepens the emotion]
[Same structure as V1]

[CHORUS]

[BRIDGE — contrast, twist, or emotional shift]
[Different rhythm/structure from verses]

[FINAL CHORUS — sometimes with variation]

[OUTRO — optional]
```

## Step 3 — Lyric Craft Tips
- The hook (usually the title) should be memorable in 5 words or fewer
- Verses tell the story; the chorus delivers the feeling
- Avoid perfect rhymes that force unnatural phrasing ("I love you so / wherever you go")
- Count syllables in each line — they should feel singable at a natural rhythm

Always offer: "Want me to write a second verse or an alternative chorus?"
