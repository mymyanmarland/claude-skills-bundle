# Satirical News Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Topic**: What real situation, trend, or behavior is being satirized?
- **Target**: Who/what is the satire aimed at?
- **Style**: The Onion (deadpan absurdist) / Late-night monologue / Fake press release / Fake academic study
- **Length**: Headline only / Short (150 words) / Full article (400-600 words)
- **Tone**: Dry / Absurdist / Biting / Gentle / Political / Pop culture

## Step 2 — Satirical News Structure
```
## Satirical Article: [Topic]

---

HEADLINE: [The entire joke in one line — absurd premise stated with total sincerity]
[Subhead: expands on the absurdity with equally deadpan logic]

[DATELINE, publication date]

[LEAD PARAGRAPH — state the absurd premise as breaking news fact]
"[City] — [Character or organization] [ridiculous action stated completely seriously], sources confirmed [day]."

[PARAGRAPH 2 — quotes from made-up people reacting normally to the absurdity]
"'[Quote that treats the ridiculous as completely routine],' said [made-up but believable person with title]."

[PARAGRAPH 3 — fake statistics or studies that support the satirical point]
"According to a [made-up institution] study of [absurdly specific number] participants..."

[PARAGRAPH 4 — extend the logic to its absurd conclusion]

[CLOSING QUOTE — the best quote for last, the one that crystallizes the satire]
"'[Final deadpan quote that nails the real-world observation],' [character] added."

---

[Optional: Related story links in The Onion style]
• [Fake related headline 1]
• [Fake related headline 2]
```

## Step 3 — Satire Rules
- **Deadpan is everything** — the humor comes from treating the absurd as normal
- **The best satire contains a real observation** — what's actually true underneath the joke?
- **Real-sounding fake names** — "Dr. Kenneth Briggs, Associate Professor of..." not "Bob from Marketing"
- **Never explain the joke** — the satirical piece stands alone

Always offer: "Want 5 satirical headlines on this topic to choose the best angle from?"
