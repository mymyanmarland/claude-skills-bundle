# Short Story Generator Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Genre**: Literary / Romance / Mystery / Sci-fi / Fantasy / Horror / Comedy / Thriller
- **Prompt or theme**: (or generate from scratch)
- **Main character**: (or leave open)
- **Length**: Flash (under 500 words) / Short (500-1500) / Long (2000-5000)
- **Tone**: Dark / Uplifting / Suspenseful / Humorous / Emotional / Whimsical
- **POV**: First person / Third person limited / Third omniscient

## Step 2 — Story Structure
Every story needs: **Hook → Setup → Conflict → Rising tension → Climax → Resolution → Echo**
The echo: a final image or line that resonates with the opening — gives a sense of completion.

## Step 3 — Deliver the Story
Write the full story with:
- A compelling opening line that drops the reader in
- Sensory details (show don't tell)
- A character with a clear want and obstacle
- A satisfying or subversive ending

Always offer: "Want me to expand this into a longer piece or write another in the same world?"
