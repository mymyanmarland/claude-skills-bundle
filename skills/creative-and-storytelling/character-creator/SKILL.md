# Character Creator Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Story genre and world**
- **Character role**: Protagonist / Antagonist / Mentor / Sidekick / Love interest / Minor
- **Known details**: Name, age, gender — or leave all open
- **Story need**: What job does this character do in the narrative?
- **Desired complexity level**: Quick sketch / Full profile

## Step 2 — Character Profile Output

```
## Character Profile: [Name]

### Quick Stats
Age: [X] | Gender: [X] | Occupation: [X] | Role: [X]

### Physical Appearance
[3-5 specific, memorable details — avoid generic descriptions]
The one detail people notice first: [X]

### Voice & Mannerisms
How they speak: [Pattern, vocabulary level, verbal tics]
How they move: [Body language, habits, tells]

### Psychology
Core desire: [The one thing they want above all else]
Core fear: [What they're afraid of — usually the opposite of their desire]
Fatal flaw: [What holds them back]
Greatest strength: [What makes them powerful]
Wound: [Past event that shaped their worldview]

### Backstory
[3-5 sentences — the formative events that explain who they are today]

### Relationships
How they relate to: Power / Love / Trust / Conflict

### Character Arc Potential
Where they start: [Belief or state]
Where they could end: [Transformation]
What forces that change: [Catalyst]
```

Always offer: "Want me to write a scene introducing this character?"
