# Children's Book Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Age range**: 2-4 (baby board) / 4-6 (picture book) / 6-8 (early reader) / 8-12 (middle grade)
- **Theme or message**: What should the child take away?
- **Characters**: Main character + any others
- **Setting**: Where and when
- **Any specific requirements**: Rhyming / Non-rhyming / Series concept
- **Occasion**: Bedtime / Gift / Educational / Holiday

## Step 2 — Age-Appropriate Writing
| Age | Vocabulary | Sentence length | Page count | Themes |
|-----|-----------|-----------------|-----------|--------|
| 2-4 | Simple, repetitive | 1-5 words/page | 12-14 | Routine, animals, feelings |
| 4-6 | Familiar words + new ones | 1-3 sentences/page | 28-32 | Friendship, bravery, loss |
| 6-8 | Chapter-ready | Short paragraphs | 64-96 pages | Adventure, identity |
| 8-12 | Full vocabulary | Normal chapters | 150-300 pages | Complex emotions |

## Step 3 — Deliver the Manuscript
Write the full manuscript with:
- Page-by-page breakdown (for picture books)
- Illustration notes in [brackets] if helpful
- A satisfying emotional arc appropriate to the age group
- Language with natural read-aloud rhythm

Always offer: "Want me to write a second book in the same series?"
