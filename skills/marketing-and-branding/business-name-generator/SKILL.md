# Business Name Generator Skill

Generate business names that are memorable, meaningful, and ownable.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **What the business does** (product, service, industry)
- **Target audience**: Who are the customers?
- **Brand personality**: 3 adjectives (e.g., bold, playful, trustworthy)
- **Naming preferences**: Short / Descriptive / Abstract / Founder name / Made-up word
- **Names to avoid** (competitors or styles that feel wrong)
- **Domain importance**: Do they need a .com?

## Step 2 — Generate Across 6 Naming Styles

```
## Business Name Ideas: [Industry/Niche]

---

### 1. Descriptive Names (say what you do)
[Name 1] — [Why it works]
[Name 2] — [Why it works]
[Name 3] — [Why it works]

### 2. Abstract / Evocative Names (feel over function)
[Name 1] — [Feeling or association it creates]
[Name 2] — [Association]
[Name 3] — [Association]

### 3. Made-Up / Portmanteau Words
[Name 1] — [Components it combines + meaning]
[Name 2] — [Components]
[Name 3] — [Components]

### 4. Metaphor / Analogy Names
[Name 1] — [What it references and why it fits]
[Name 2] — [Reference]
[Name 3] — [Reference]

### 5. Short / Punchy (4-6 letters)
[Name 1] — [Why memorable]
[Name 2] — [Why memorable]
[Name 3] — [Why memorable]

### 6. Human / Personal Names
[Name 1] — [Founder-style or character-inspired]
[Name 2]
[Name 3]

---

## ⭐ Top 3 Recommendations

1. **[Name]** — [Why this is the strongest: memorability, availability potential, brand fit]
2. **[Name]** — [Reason]
3. **[Name]** — [Reason]

---

## Domain & Availability Check
For each top pick, check:
- [ ] [name].com — available/taken
- [ ] @[name] on Instagram
- [ ] Trademark search: USPTO.gov or EUIPO
- [ ] Google search for existing businesses
```

## Step 3 — Naming Criteria Checklist
✅ Easy to spell when heard aloud
✅ Easy to remember after hearing once
✅ Works at small scale and large scale
✅ Doesn't accidentally mean something offensive in another language (check: Google Translate)
✅ Not too similar to established competitors
✅ Domain available (or workable variation)

Always offer: "Want tagline ideas to go with your chosen name?"
