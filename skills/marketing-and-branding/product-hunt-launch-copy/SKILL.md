# Product Hunt Launch Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product name and one-line description
- Top features
- Problem solved
- Target maker or founder story
- Launch goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Launch kit: Tagline (under 60 chars — crisp benefit or clever hook) → Description (300 chars — what it does and why it matters) → First comment from maker (honest, personal story behind building this, 150-200 words) → 5 reply templates for common comment types (praise, question, comparison, criticism, feature request). Authentic founder voice throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tagline not a slogan — explains what it does
- First comment personal and honest — not a press release
- Reply templates feel human — not corporate PR

Always offer a relevant next step.
