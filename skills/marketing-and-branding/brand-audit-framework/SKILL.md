# Brand Audit Framework Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What to audit: visual/verbal/both
- Brand touchpoints
- Stakeholder types
- Timeline
- Output format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit framework: (1) Brand assets inventory — logo, colors, typography, imagery, tone. (2) Touchpoint consistency score (1-5) across 10 key touchpoints. (3) Message clarity test — would a stranger understand what we do? (4) Competitor perception comparison. (5) Internal vs external brand alignment. Deliver audit scorecard template, findings summary template, and priority recommendations structure.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audit covers both internal and external perception
- Scoring criteria defined — not subjective
- Output is actionable: what to fix, in what order

Always offer a relevant next step.
