# Crisis Communications Template Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Crisis type
- Brand role: perpetrator/victim/bystander
- Audience: customers/press/employees
- Severity level
- Timeline for response

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crisis response kit: Initial holding statement (within 2 hours — acknowledge, no details yet) → Full statement template (24-48 hours — acknowledge, explain, apologize if warranted, action steps) → Employee internal communication → Social media response templates → FAQ for customer service team → Recovery communication (1 week later — progress update). Each template with fill-in sections and tone guidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Holding statement acknowledges without committing to facts
- Apology template avoids 'sorry you feel' language
- Recovery communication focuses on action taken not feelings

Always offer a relevant next step.
