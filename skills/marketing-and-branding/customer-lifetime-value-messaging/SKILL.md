# Customer Lifetime Value Messaging Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Average LTV
- Customer relationship stages
- Retention pain points
- Key loyalty drivers
- Brand relationship style

## Step 2 — Core Output

LTV messaging strategy: Stage-by-stage messaging (acquisition/activation/retention/loyalty/advocacy) → Key messages per stage → Retention-focused copy examples → Loyalty program messaging → Win-back messaging. Shift from transaction to relationship in every piece.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every stage message advances relationship — not just sells
- Loyalty messaging makes long-term customers feel valued
- Win-back honest about the relationship gap

Always offer a relevant next step.
