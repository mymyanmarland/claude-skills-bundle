# Employer Brand Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company culture highlights
- What makes the company different to work at
- Target candidate profile
- Current hiring challenges
- Honest culture description

## Step 2 — Core Output

EVP statement → Career page hero copy → Job ad template with culture section → Employee story framework (for authentic content) → Social media employer brand posts (5) → Interview day welcome message → Rejection email template. Each piece authentic — not aspirational fiction. Includes honest culture signal.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- EVP based on real differentiators — not wish list
- Job ad culture section specific — not generic perks
- Rejection email treats candidates as future customers

Always offer a relevant next step.
