# Brand Positioning Statement Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target customer
- Category
- Key benefit
- Key differentiator
- Reason to believe

## Step 2 — Core Output

Formula: For [target customer] who [need/desire], [Brand] is the [category] that [key benefit] because [reason to believe]. Deliver 5 positioning options at different angles: value-based, emotion-based, differentiator-based, category-creation, and aspirational. For each: the statement, what it emphasizes, ideal use context, and how it could evolve. Recommend one with full rationale.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each positioning falsifiable — could not apply to every competitor
- Target customer specific — not 'anyone who...'
- Reason to believe is evidence-based

Always offer a relevant next step.
