# Brand Community Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- Target community members
- Platform preference
- Community goal
- Resources for community management

## Step 2 — Core Output

Community strategy: Platform selection with rationale → Community positioning (what makes it worth joining) → Launch plan → Content categories (80% member value / 20% brand) → Community guidelines → Engagement triggers → Moderation guidelines → Growth strategy → Success metrics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 80/20 rule enforced — community not a marketing channel
- Guidelines set right culture from day one
- Growth strategy builds community — not just list

Always offer a relevant next step.
