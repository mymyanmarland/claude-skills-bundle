# Brand Ambassador Program Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand description
- Target ambassador profile
- Incentive structure
- Program goals
- Content expectations

## Step 2 — Core Output

Program guide: Ambassador criteria and selection process → Application or invitation copy → Welcome kit copy → Monthly mission briefs (template) → Content guidelines (what to post, what not to) → Incentive structure explained → Performance tracking → Community building plan. Makes ambassadors feel special and empowered.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Selection criteria specific and fair
- Content guidelines short — authenticity protected
- Incentive structure transparent from day one

Always offer a relevant next step.
