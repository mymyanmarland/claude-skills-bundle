# Sponsorship Deck Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What is being sponsored
- Audience size and demographics
- Sponsorship tiers
- What sponsors get
- Past sponsor results if any

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deck copy: Audience overview (demographics, psychographics, reach) → Why sponsor this (alignment + ROI argument) → Sponsorship tiers with descriptions → What's included per tier → Audience value alignment proof → Past sponsor results or testimonials → Contact and process section. Numbers-forward. ROI framing throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audience description specific enough to match advertiser ICP
- Tier value clearly differentiated
- Numbers used in ROI argument are real or clearly estimated

Always offer a relevant next step.
