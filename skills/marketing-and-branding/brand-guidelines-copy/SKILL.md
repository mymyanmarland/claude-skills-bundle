# Brand Guidelines Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand elements to cover
- Audiences for the guidelines
- Common misuse patterns
- Examples available
- Scope: verbal/visual/both

## Step 2 — Core Output

Guidelines copy: Brand introduction (purpose and values) → Voice and tone chapter (personality, tone spectrum, examples) → Writing style chapter (grammar rules, formatting, vocabulary) → Logo and mark usage text (when and how to use) → Common mistakes section → Quick reference card. Written to be actually read — not just filed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rules have rationale — not arbitrary
- Examples more useful than rules
- Document is scannable — not a wall of text

Always offer a relevant next step.
