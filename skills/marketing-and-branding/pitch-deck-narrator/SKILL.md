# Pitch Deck Narrator Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Company name and one-line description**
- **Problem being solved**
- **Solution / product**
- **Traction**: Users, revenue, growth rate, key customers
- **Business model**: How it makes money
- **Market size**: TAM, SAM, SOM if known
- **Team**: Key founders and relevant credentials
- **The ask**: How much raising, at what stage, for what use

## Step 2 — Pitch Narrative Arc
```
## Pitch Narrative: [Company Name]
Total pitch time: [X minutes]

---

### Slide 1: COVER (10 seconds)
"We are [Company]. We [one sentence — what you do for whom]."
[Hook them before the first slide change]

### Slide 2: THE PROBLEM (60-90 seconds)
"[Paint the world as it is — the pain, the cost, the frustration.]
[Make the audience feel the problem before you show the solution.]
[Quantify if possible: X million people / $X billion wasted / X hours lost]"

### Slide 3: THE SOLUTION (60-90 seconds)
"[Introduce your product simply — what it does, not how it works]
[The 'aha' moment: show don't tell — demo, screenshot, or before/after]
[One sentence on why now is the right time for this solution]"

### Slide 4: THE MARKET (45 seconds)
"[TAM is $X — here's why that number is real and why you can capture a piece of it]
[Don't inflate this — sophisticated investors will call it out]"

### Slide 5: BUSINESS MODEL (45 seconds)
"[We make money by X. Current price point: $Y. Unit economics: [key metric]]"

### Slide 6: TRACTION (90 seconds — most important slide)
"[The numbers that prove people want this]
[Growth rate > absolute numbers. Show the curve.]
[Key logos, testimonials, or partnerships if you have them]"

### Slide 7: COMPETITION (30 seconds)
"[We know this space — here's why we win]
[Acknowledge competitors — it shows you know the market]
[Your specific, defensible differentiation]"

### Slide 8: TEAM (45 seconds)
"[Why are we the right team for this problem?]
[Domain expertise + execution track record + any unfair advantages]"

### Slide 9: THE ASK (60 seconds)
"[We are raising $X at [stage]. Here's exactly what we'll do with it and why it gets us to [milestone].]
[The milestone should be the next fundable event]"
```

## Step 3 — Pitch Rules
- Investors fund the team first, market second, product third — reflect that
- Traction is the most important slide — spend most time here
- Never apologize for competition — it validates the market
- The ask should be specific: "18 months of runway to reach X ARR / X users"
- Practice so you can do it without slides

Always offer: "Want me to write the one-line elevator pitch for this company?"
