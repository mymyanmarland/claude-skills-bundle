# Marketing Operations Workflow Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Team size
- Tools used
- Campaign types
- Bottlenecks experienced
- Approval structure

## Step 2 — Core Output

Workflow documentation: Campaign brief template → Creative production workflow → Review and approval process → Campaign launch checklist → Performance monitoring protocol → Post-campaign review process. RACI for each stage. Timeline templates per campaign type. Designed to reduce bottlenecks identified.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- RACI clear — single owner per decision
- Approval process realistic — no unnecessary gates
- Templates reduce starting from zero on each campaign

Always offer a relevant next step.
