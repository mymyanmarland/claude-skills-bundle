# Consumer Insights Report Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research data and findings
- Research methodology
- Target audience for report
- Strategic decisions this informs
- Key findings to highlight

## Step 2 — Core Output

Report: Executive summary (5 key insights + implications) → Methodology overview → Finding 1: [Insight + evidence + implication] (repeat for 5-8 findings) → Opportunity areas identified → Recommended actions → Research limitations. Insight language: 'Consumers [do X] because [motivation]' not 'Survey shows 73% of respondents'.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Insights are interpretations — not just data points
- Every insight has a 'so what' for the business
- Limitations section honest about research constraints

Always offer a relevant next step.
