# Product Differentiation Mapper Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Your product features
- Top 3 competitors and their features
- Target buyer priorities
- Your strongest advantage area
- Weaknesses to acknowledge

## Step 2 — Core Output

Differentiation map: Feature comparison table → Prioritized differentiators (ranked by buyer importance × your strength) → Messaging angles for top 3 differentiators → Honest assessment of parity areas → Framing strategies for weakness areas → Recommended primary differentiator with rationale.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Differentiators must be genuinely different — not reframing parity
- Buyer importance ranked from research — not assumption
- Weakness framing is honest — not spin

Always offer a relevant next step.
