# Brand Partnership Pitch Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Your brand description
- Potential partner brand
- Shared audience overlap
- Proposed collaboration type
- Value exchange

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proposal sections: Why us + why you (audience overlap and complementary positioning) → Collaboration concept (specific, not vague) → What each party brings → What each party gains → Audience value (what does this give their audience) → Success metrics → Proposed timeline → Next steps. 500-700 words. Business-casual tone.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value exchange explicitly balanced
- Audience benefit specific — not assumed
- Proposal feels personalized to the specific partner

Always offer a relevant next step.
