# Seasonal Marketing Calendar Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type
- Key seasonal opportunities
- Past seasonal performance
- Resources
- Brand relationship with holidays

## Step 2 — Core Output

12-month calendar: Each month — key dates + campaign concept + channel strategy + lead time required + budget allocation guidance. Themed campaigns vs standalone promotions balanced. Non-commercial moments included (cultural events, industry dates). Annotated with previous year performance notes slots.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lead times built in — not planning day before
- Non-holiday campaigns included for balance
- Budget guidance percentage-based for any budget size

Always offer a relevant next step.
