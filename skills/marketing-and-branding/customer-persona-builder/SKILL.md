# Customer Persona Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Known customer data
- Primary market
- 3-5 customer pain points
- Business model: B2B or B2C

## Step 2 — Core Output

Persona template: Name + photo description + one-line summary → Demographics (age, role, income, location) → Psychographics (values, goals, fears) → Day-in-the-life (relevant context) → Pain points (ranked) → Goals related to your solution → Buying triggers and objections → Preferred content and channels → Quote that captures their mindset. Deliver 2-3 distinct personas.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Personas based on research signals — not stereotypes
- Pain points specific enough to inform copy
- Each persona genuinely distinct — not variations of same person

Always offer a relevant next step.
