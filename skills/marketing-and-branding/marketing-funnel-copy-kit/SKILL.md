# Marketing Funnel Copy Kit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- ICP and pain points
- Price point
- Funnel stages needed
- Tone and brand voice

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funnel copy kit: TOFU (ad headline + hook content intro + social post) → MOFU (email sequence: 3 nurture emails, retargeting ad copy, comparison page bullet points) → BOFU (sales page hero section, objection handler, trial/demo CTA, FAQ) → Post-purchase (onboarding email, upsell email, referral ask). Each piece labeled with funnel stage and channel.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Copy evolves in specificity as funnel deepens
- No stage assumes knowledge from previous stage
- Conversion copy only appears at BOFU

Always offer a relevant next step.
