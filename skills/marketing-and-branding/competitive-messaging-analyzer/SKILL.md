# Competitive Messaging Analyzer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- List of 3-5 competitors
- Your product/service
- Your current messaging
- Market category
- Target audience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Analysis structure: Competitor messaging matrix (for each competitor: headline, key benefit claimed, tone, audience signal) → Whitespace map (what's NOT being said) → Overused claims to avoid → Unique angles available to you → Recommended messaging direction with rationale. Visual: 2x2 positioning map description (axes based on most meaningful differentiators found).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Analysis based on actual competitor copy — not assumptions
- Whitespace genuinely unclaimed
- Recommended angle defensible and ownable

Always offer a relevant next step.
