# Cause Marketing Campaign Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand values
- Cause or charity partner
- Campaign mechanic
- Target audience
- Authenticity concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Campaign copy: Campaign concept (connection between brand and cause — must feel genuine) → Announcement copy → Campaign mechanic explanation → Progress update templates → Impact report copy → Social media series. Authenticity checklist: does the cause connect to your business genuinely? Red flags included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brand-cause connection authentic — not just PR
- Impact reported honestly — including what wasn't achieved
- Cause partner benefits stated explicitly

Always offer a relevant next step.
