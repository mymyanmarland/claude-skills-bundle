# Competitive Intelligence Report Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Competitors to analyze
- Research available
- Focus areas: messaging/product/pricing/channels
- Time period
- Who will read the report

## Step 2 — Core Output

Report structure: Executive summary (key insights in 5 bullets) → Competitor profiles (for each: overview, messaging, pricing, recent moves, strengths/weaknesses) → Comparative analysis → Threat assessment → Opportunity identification → Strategic implications → Recommended actions. Present in executive-ready format.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Insights are actionable — not just descriptive
- Threats and opportunities prioritized by impact
- Strategic implications tied to specific competitor findings

Always offer a relevant next step.
