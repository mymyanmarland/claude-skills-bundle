# Tagline & Slogan Creator Skill

Distill a brand into a line people remember.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Brand/product name and what it does**
- **Target audience**
- **Brand personality**: Bold / Warm / Witty / Serious / Inspiring
- **Core benefit**: The single most important thing the brand delivers
- **Tone**: Functional / Emotional / Aspirational / Humorous
- **Usage**: Logo lockup / Ad campaign / Website hero / Personal brand

## Step 3 — Tagline Quality Test
Run each finalist through:
- **Memorability**: Can someone repeat it 10 minutes after hearing it?
- **Ownable**: Could it only belong to this brand?
- **Timeless**: Will it feel dated in 5 years?
- **Expandable**: Does it allow the brand to grow?
- **Honest**: Does the brand actually deliver on this promise?

Always offer: "Want brand voice guidelines to match this tagline?"
