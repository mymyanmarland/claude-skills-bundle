# Market Entry Strategy Brief Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- New market description
- Current market position
- Resources available
- Competitive landscape in new market
- Timeline and investment appetite

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strategy brief: Market opportunity assessment → Target segment within the market → Competitive differentiation for this market → Go-to-market approach (channels, messaging, partners) → Phased entry plan (3 phases: test, validate, scale) → Resource requirements → Risk assessment → Success criteria. 1000-1500 words executive-ready.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Market sizing is realistic — no optimistic assumptions without basis
- Competitive differentiation specific to new market
- Risk assessment includes mitigation actions

Always offer a relevant next step.
