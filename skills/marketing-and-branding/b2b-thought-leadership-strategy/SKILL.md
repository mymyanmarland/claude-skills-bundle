# B2B Thought Leadership Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Industry and expertise area
- Key executives or spokespeople
- Content creation resources
- Target audience: buyers/peers/media
- Business goal: leads/talent/partnerships

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strategy: POV development (what unique perspective does this brand have?) → Channel selection (LinkedIn/speaking/media/research) → Content calendar (monthly cadence per channel) → Executive voice guide → Ghostwriting workflow → Amplification strategy → Measurement approach. 12-month plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- POV genuinely differentiated — not industry consensus
- Executive voice authentic — not corporate
- Measurement tied to business goal — not just impressions

Always offer a relevant next step.
