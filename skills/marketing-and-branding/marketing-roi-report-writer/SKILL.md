# Marketing ROI Report Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Metrics and results available
- Reporting period
- Executive audience
- What worked and what didn't
- Business context: goals achieved or missed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Report structure: Executive summary (3 bullets: results, wins, concerns) → Investment vs return overview → Channel performance breakdown → Campaign highlights → What worked and why → What didn't and learnings → Next period recommendations → Appendix (raw numbers). Translate every metric into business language: not CTR — but leads generated.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Executive summary tells the story in 30 seconds
- Negative results reported honestly with learnings
- Recommendations specific and budgeted

Always offer a relevant next step.
