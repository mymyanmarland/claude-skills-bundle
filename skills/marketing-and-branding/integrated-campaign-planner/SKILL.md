# Integrated Campaign Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Campaign goal
- Channels available
- Budget
- Timeline
- Core message

## Step 2 — Core Output

Campaign plan: Core message and creative concept → Channel-by-channel role (what each channel does) → Content requirements per channel → Timing and sequencing (what launches first, what follows) → Budget allocation → KPIs per channel → Measurement and attribution plan → Campaign brief for each channel.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each channel has distinct role — not same content on all channels
- Sequencing logical — paid reaches new, owned converts
- Attribution plan acknowledges multi-touch reality

Always offer a relevant next step.
