# Brand Identity Questionnaire Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Client type
- Project scope
- Agency style
- Depth needed
- Number of questions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Question categories: Brand history and background (5Q) → Target audience (5Q) → Competitive landscape (5Q) → Brand personality and tone (5Q) → Visual preferences (5Q) → Goals and vision (5Q) → Stakeholder alignment (3Q). Each question has sub-prompts to get specific answers. Include warm-up questions and deep-dive questions. Format for easy completion.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Questions open-ended — not yes/no
- Each section builds toward actionable insight
- No leading questions — genuinely exploratory

Always offer a relevant next step.
