# Influencer Brief Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Campaign goal
- Brand voice and values
- Key message
- Content requirements
- What to avoid

## Step 2 — Core Output

Brief sections: Campaign overview (why this, why now, why you) → Brand context (who we are in their language) → Campaign objective → Must-haves (non-negotiable — keep short: 2-3 items) → Nice-to-haves (suggestions, not requirements) → What to avoid → Example formats (inspiration, not templates) → Deliverables and timeline → Compliance notes. Keep the must-have list genuinely short — over-restriction kills authenticity.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Must-haves under 5 items
- Brief reads like a collaboration, not a list of restrictions
- Creator's authentic voice explicitly welcomed

Always offer a relevant next step.
