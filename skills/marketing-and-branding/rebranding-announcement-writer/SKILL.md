# Rebranding Announcement Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What changed and why
- Old brand vs new brand
- Primary audience
- Key message
- Timeline of rollout

## Step 2 — Core Output

Three announcement versions: (1) Customer email — personal, benefit-focused, what changes for them. (2) Press release — newsworthy angle, quotes, business rationale. (3) Internal announcement — honest about why, what employees need to know and do. Each addresses: what's changing, why now, what stays the same, what this means for them, what to do next.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 'Why' explained before 'what'
- What stays the same acknowledged explicitly
- Each version appropriate to its specific audience

Always offer a relevant next step.
