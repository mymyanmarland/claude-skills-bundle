# Product Roadmap Announcement Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Roadmap highlights
- Release confidence levels
- Customer pain points being addressed
- Competitive sensitivity
- Communication channel

## Step 2 — Core Output

Roadmap announcement: Theme and vision framing → What's coming (categorized by: coming soon / in development / exploring) → Why we're building it (customer pain it addresses) → What to do to influence it → Caveats and timeline language. Honest about uncertainty — doesn't create false expectations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Confidence levels clear in language ('we're planning' vs 'we're exploring')
- Customer pain explicitly connected to feature
- Expectation-setting honest — no hard dates unless certain

Always offer a relevant next step.
