# Customer Review Responder Skill

Respond to every review in a way that builds trust with future customers.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **The review** (paste the full text)
- **Star rating** (1-5 stars)
- **Platform** (Google, Yelp, Amazon, Etsy, Trustpilot, App Store)
- **Business name and type**
- **Brand tone**: Formal / Warm / Casual
- **Context** (is the complaint accurate? Is it resolved already?)

## Step 2 — Response Templates

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ 5-Star Response
```
[Personalized thank you — reference something specific from their review]
[Reinforce what they praised — adds value for future customers reading this]
[Warm close with invitation to return / refer / connect]
[Name or initials of responder]
```

**Example**:
"Thank you so much, [Name]! We're delighted to hear [specific thing they mentioned]. [Staff name / that detail] is something we really pride ourselves on, so this means a lot to the whole team. We look forward to seeing you again! — [Name], [Team/Brand]"

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ 4-Star Response
```
[Genuine thank you]
[Acknowledge the positive points]
[If they mentioned anything less than perfect: "We'd love to hear more about [concern] — please reach out at [email]"]
[Invitation to return]
```

### ⭐⭐⭐ 3-Star Response
```
[Thank them for taking the time]
[Acknowledge the mixed experience — don't be defensive]
[Address the specific concern briefly]
[Take it offline: "We'd love to make this right — please contact us at [email]"]
[Commitment to improvement]
```

### ⭐⭐ or ⭐ Negative Review Response

**Formula**: Thank → Acknowledge → Apologize (if warranted) → Explain (briefly, not defensively) → Resolve → Take offline

```
"Thank you for sharing your experience, [Name].

We're genuinely sorry to hear that [specific complaint] — this isn't the standard we hold ourselves to, and we understand how frustrating that must have been.

[Optional: brief, non-defensive context if the complaint is based on a misunderstanding]

We'd like to make this right. Please reach out directly at [email/phone] so we can [specific resolution — refund, replacement, call, etc.].

[Name], [Title]"
```

**Rules for negative responses**:
- Never argue publicly — even if they're wrong
- Never reveal personal customer information
- Don't copy-paste the same response to multiple reviews
- Always offer to resolve offline
- Keep it under 150 words

### Fake/Malicious Review Response
```
"Thank you for your feedback. We've reviewed our records and are unable to find a [purchase / visit / account] matching your details. We take all feedback seriously — if there's been an error, please contact us at [email] so we can look into this. — [Name]"
```

## Step 3 — Response Quality Check
- [ ] Starts with thank you (not "We're sorry to hear...")
- [ ] Mentions something specific from the review (shows it was read)
- [ ] Doesn't repeat the negative complaint word-for-word (amplifies it for search)
- [ ] Offers a resolution path
- [ ] Under 150 words (readers skim responses)
- [ ] Signed by a real name, not "Management"

Always offer: "Want me to batch-respond to multiple reviews at once?"
