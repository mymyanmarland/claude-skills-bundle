# Go-to-Market Strategy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Target market and ICP
- Competitive landscape
- Resources available
- Launch timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

GTM document sections: Market opportunity summary → Target customer definition → Value proposition → Competitive differentiation → Channel strategy → Pricing strategy → Launch timeline (phases) → Success metrics → Resource requirements. Each section includes rationale and alternatives considered. 1500-2000 words executive-ready format.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Strategy specific to this product — not generic
- Channels selected based on where ICP actually is
- Success metrics measurable within launch timeline

Always offer a relevant next step.
