# Influencer Marketing Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- Target audience
- Budget range
- Campaign goal
- Previous influencer experience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strategy: Influencer tier selection (mega/macro/micro/nano — with budget guidance) → Vetting criteria → Outreach process → Brief template → Content approval workflow → Rights and usage → Measurement framework → Long-term partnership vs one-off approach guidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tier recommendation budget-appropriate
- Vetting criteria beyond follower count
- Measurement framework tracks actual business impact

Always offer a relevant next step.
