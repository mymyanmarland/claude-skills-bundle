# Marketing Calendar Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Time period
- Channels
- Key dates: product launches/events/seasons
- Budget range
- Team size

## Step 2 — Core Output

Calendar format: Monthly overview (theme + 3 priorities) → Week-by-week content plan (platform + type + topic) → Campaign integration (how all pieces connect) → Key date flags with lead time needed → Resource allocation notes → KPI tracker per campaign. Highlight quick wins vs. major initiatives.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every campaign has a goal and metric
- Major initiatives have sufficient lead time
- Calendar realistic for stated team size

Always offer a relevant next step.
