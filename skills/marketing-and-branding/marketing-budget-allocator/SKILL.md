# Marketing Budget Allocator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Total budget
- Business stage: startup/growth/mature
- Channels available
- Primary goal
- Current performance by channel

## Step 2 — Core Output

Budget framework: Allocation by stage (startup: 70% acquisition / growth: 50-30-20 / mature: 40-40-20) → Channel allocation template → Experiment budget (10-20% rule) → Performance-based reallocation triggers → Monthly budget review process → ROI tracking by channel. Justified with reasoning not just formulas.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Allocation percentages explained — not arbitrary
- Experiment budget explicit and protected
- Reallocation triggers specific — not 'if it's not working'

Always offer a relevant next step.
