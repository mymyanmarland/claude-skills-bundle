# Market Research Survey Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research objective
- Target respondents
- Number of questions
- Quantitative vs qualitative balance
- How results will be used

## Step 2 — Core Output

Survey sections: Screening questions (1-2) → Background/demographic (3-4, only if needed) → Core research questions (8-12) → Satisfaction/NPS if applicable → Open-ended insight questions (2-3). Each question: clear phrasing, appropriate response format (scale/multiple choice/open), avoid leading language. Include survey introduction copy and completion CTA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Under 15 questions for completion rate
- No double-barreled questions
- Scale questions anchored with labels at both ends

Always offer a relevant next step.
