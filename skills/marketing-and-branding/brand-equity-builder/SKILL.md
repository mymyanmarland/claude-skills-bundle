# Brand Equity Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current brand awareness level
- Brand values
- Target brand associations
- Budget for brand vs performance
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Brand equity strategy: Desired brand associations map → Content strategy for association building → Consistency framework across touchpoints → Brand campaign concepts (3 ideas) → Cultural relevance strategy → Brand measurement approach (beyond clicks). 12-month brand building roadmap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brand associations specific and ownable
- Content strategy distinct from performance marketing
- Measurement approach acknowledges long attribution windows

Always offer a relevant next step.
