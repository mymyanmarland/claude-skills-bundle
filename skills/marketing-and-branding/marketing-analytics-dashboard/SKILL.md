# Marketing Analytics Dashboard Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business goals
- Channels
- Reporting audience
- Data sources available
- Reporting cadence

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dashboard framework: Goal hierarchy (business → marketing → channel → content) → KPI set per level → Data source mapping → Weekly vs monthly vs quarterly metrics → Red/yellow/green thresholds → Anomaly detection triggers → Stakeholder-specific views (CEO vs channel manager). Tool-agnostic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- KPIs connect to business goals — no vanity metrics
- Threshold triggers define action — not just awareness
- Stakeholder views genuinely different levels of detail

Always offer a relevant next step.
