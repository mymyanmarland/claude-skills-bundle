# Brand Voice Guide Builder Skill

Define a brand voice so clearly that anyone can write on-brand content.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Brand name and what they do**
- **Target audience** (demographics + psychographics)
- **3-5 brands with voices they admire** (for reference, not copying)
- **3-5 brands whose voices they want to avoid**
- **Current content samples** (if any — to analyze existing voice)
- **Brand values** (if defined)

## Step 2 — Voice Discovery

Ask these questions to uncover voice:
- "If your brand were a person at a dinner party, how would they talk?"
- "What 3 adjectives describe how you want customers to feel after reading your content?"
- "What would your brand NEVER say?"
