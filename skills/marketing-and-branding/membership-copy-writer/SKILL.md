# Membership Copy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Membership benefits
- Pricing and tiers
- Target member
- Community size and social proof
- Transformation promised

## Step 2 — Core Output

Copy sections: Hero (transformation promise) → Who this is for (specific profile) → What members get (benefits list with concrete descriptions) → What membership feels like (experience description) → Social proof section → Pricing presentation → Objection handling (3 most common) → Enrollment CTA. Membership copy sells belonging, not just features.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Transformation promise specific and believable
- Benefits describe experiences — not features
- Objections addressed honestly — not deflected

Always offer a relevant next step.
