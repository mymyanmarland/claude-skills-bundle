# Agency Proposal Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Project scope and deliverables
- Client context
- Your agency credentials
- Pricing structure
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proposal: Executive summary → Understanding of brief (show you listened) → Proposed approach and methodology → Team and credentials → Deliverables and timeline → Investment (pricing framed as ROI) → Case studies section brief → Process and collaboration section → Terms summary → Next steps CTA. Winning proposals show understanding before prescription.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brief understanding shows genuine listening — not generic
- Credentials relevant to this specific brief
- Investment section shows value before showing price

Always offer a relevant next step.
