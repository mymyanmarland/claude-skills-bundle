# Story World Rules Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Genre and world type
- Core concept or hook
- What the world's rules govern
- Story needs these rules must serve
- Tone: serious/playful/gritty

## Step 2 — Core Output

World rules framework: Core rule (the fundamental law of this world) → Costs and limits (every power/element has constraints — this creates drama) → Consequences of breaking rules → Internal consistency map → How rules interact with each other → What the rules reveal about this world's values → 3 story situations the rules naturally create. Brandon Sanderson's first law: the reader's ability to understand the solution is proportional to the satisfaction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every power has a cost — unlimited power kills drama
- Rules internally consistent — established early, paid off later
- Rules serve story — not just cool worldbuilding for its own sake

Always offer a relevant next step.
