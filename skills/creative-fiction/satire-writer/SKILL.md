# Satire Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target of satire: institution/trend/behavior/genre
- Satirical mode: gentle/cutting/absurdist/biting
- Real phenomenon being exaggerated
- Audience who will recognize the reference
- How far to push the absurdity

## Step 2 — Core Output

Satirical piece with: the real thing that's being satirized stated clearly in the subtext, exaggeration that reveals the absurdity of the original, internal consistency (the satire's world follows its own logic), a specific target not a vague gesture, the point landing without being stated. Satire without a point is just mockery.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Target specific — not vague cultural gestures
- Logic internally consistent within the satirical premise
- Point made through showing — not author editorializing

Always offer a relevant next step.
