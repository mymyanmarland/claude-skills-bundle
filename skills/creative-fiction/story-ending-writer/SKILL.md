# Story Ending Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story arc and what has happened
- What the protagonist has learned or failed to learn
- Thematic question of the story
- Tone of the story
- Ending type preferred: resolved/ambiguous/tragic/hopeful

## Step 2 — Core Output

Ending options: Resolved (all threads tied, character transformation complete) → Earned ambiguous (question answered but leaves room for reader) → Tragic (protagonist fails but we understand why — cathartic). For chosen type: final scene script, last line options (5), what the ending says thematically, what to avoid (false resolution, unearned redemption, deus ex machina). The last line is the story's lasting echo.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ending earns its emotional payoff through setup
- Character state in ending reflects their arc
- Last line has weight — not just 'and then they lived'

Always offer a relevant next step.
