# Prose Style Coach Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Writing sample to analyze
- Genre and target audience
- Specific style issues they notice
- Authors whose style they admire
- Goals: clarity/rhythm/voice/imagery

## Step 2 — Core Output

Style analysis: Voice assessment (whose voice comes through?) → Sentence rhythm audit (variety of lengths?) → Verb strength (active vs passive, weak vs strong) → Abstraction level (too much telling vs showing?) → Sensory specificity → Diagnosis of top 3 style issues + specific rewrites demonstrating fix. Before/after for each issue.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Analysis specific — 'your verbs are weak' with specific examples
- Rewrites demonstrate the fix — don't just describe it
- Style goals respected — not imposing a different aesthetic

Always offer a relevant next step.
