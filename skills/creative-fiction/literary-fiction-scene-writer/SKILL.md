# Literary Fiction Scene Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Character and their internal state
- Setting that reflects or contrasts their state
- What the scene is really about (theme/emotion)
- Prose style references (authors they admire)
- What action (if any) frames the interiority

## Step 2 — Core Output

Literary scene with: precise, earned prose (no easy adjectives), access to character's inner experience at a level of detail beyond surface feelings, the exterior world filtered through interior state, theme present in the texture of the prose not stated, something small and specific standing in for something large and universal. The scene that happens externally is almost irrelevant — what matters is what it means.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every adjective earned and precise — no lazy description
- Small specific detail stands in for large universal truth
- Nothing stated that can be shown through sensation or image

Always offer a relevant next step.
