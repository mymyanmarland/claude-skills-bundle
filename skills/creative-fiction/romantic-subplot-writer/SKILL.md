# Romantic Subplot Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Characters involved and their dynamic
- Stage of romance: meet/conflict/near-miss/consummation
- Genre: sweet/steamy/literary
- Obstacles keeping them apart
- What the romance reveals about each character

## Step 2 — Core Output

Romantic subplot plan + scene: The attraction established (specific, physical and intellectual), the obstacle (external or internal — more interesting when both), the almost-moment (interrupted or pulled back), the confession or connection. Scene written with: charged dialogue, body language subtext, each character's fear of vulnerability. Tension sustained through proximity and restraint.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Attraction specific — not generic 'chemistry'
- Obstacle meaningful — not just contrived misunderstanding
- Restraint builds tension better than immediate gratification

Always offer a relevant next step.
