# Fiction Pacing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story excerpt or outline
- Genre and reader expectations
- Current pacing issue: too slow/too fast/uneven
- Scene vs summary balance
- Emotional beats needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pacing analysis: Scene vs summary map → Where the story lingers too long vs rushes → Tools for slowing: interiority, sensory detail, dialogue that circles → Tools for speeding: scene cuts, shorter sentences, action, summary → Recommended pacing rhythm for this genre → Restructuring suggestions for specific sections identified.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Diagnosis specific to actual text — not generic advice
- Slowing and speeding tools demonstrated, not just listed
- Pacing rhythm matched to genre expectations

Always offer a relevant next step.
