# Genre Fiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Genre
- Scene type or story beat
- Setting and world details
- Characters involved
- Genre conventions to honor or subvert

## Step 2 — Core Output

Genre-appropriate scene/story with: genre conventions met (the reader gets what they came for), genre-specific pacing (thriller: short sentences/urgency; romance: emotional beats; horror: dread-building), unique specific details that feel fresh within the genre. Deliver what the genre promises while surprising within those expectations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Genre conventions present — reader gets the experience they want
- Specific details make it feel original — not template
- Pacing appropriate to genre: thrillers move fast, literary slow down

Always offer a relevant next step.
