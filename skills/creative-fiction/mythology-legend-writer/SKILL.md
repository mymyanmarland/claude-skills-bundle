# Mythology & Legend Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- World or culture this myth belongs to
- What the myth explains (origin/value/warning)
- Tone: oral tradition/written epic/folk tale
- Deities or figures involved
- What the culture values or fears

## Step 2 — Core Output

Original myth/legend with: archetypal narrative structure appropriate to form, thematic function (what does this teach/warn/explain?), cultural fingerprint (this people's specific voice and values), symbolic resonance, memorable imagery and repetition (oral tradition elements). Include: how this myth would be told (around a fire, in temples, by children) and what it reveals about the culture.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Myth feels culturally specific — not generic
- Thematic function clear: explains, warns, or celebrates something
- Oral tradition elements present: repetition, rhythm, memorable images

Always offer a relevant next step.
