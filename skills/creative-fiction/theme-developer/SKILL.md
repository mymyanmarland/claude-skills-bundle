# Theme Developer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story premise
- What question the writer wants the story to ask
- Characters and their contradictions
- Key events in the story
- What the writer wants the reader to feel, not think

## Step 2 — Core Output

Theme development guide: Core question (not answer — stories explore, not preach) → Thematic statement as a testable proposition → How each major character embodies a different answer to the thematic question → Scenes that force the theme into action → What the ending says about the theme (tentative answer) → What to avoid: characters stating the theme, authorial intrusion.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Theme expressed as a question — not a lesson
- Every major character holds a different position on the theme
- Ending takes a position without closing all ambiguity

Always offer a relevant next step.
