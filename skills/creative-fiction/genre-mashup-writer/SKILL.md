# Genre Mashup Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Primary genres to combine
- Story concept
- Which genre's conventions to honor vs subvert
- Target audience
- Tone of the mashup

## Step 2 — Core Output

Genre mashup with: both genres' core promises delivered (romance readers get the emotional arc; thriller readers get the tension), the collision point where genres create new possibilities, subverted expectations that come from the mashup, a tone that holds both together. Plus: which genre conventions can flex and which must hold.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Both genres' core promises delivered — not one dominant
- Mashup creates something neither genre alone could
- Tone consistent despite genre collision

Always offer a relevant next step.
