# Dialogue Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Characters and their relationship
- What they need to communicate
- What they're NOT saying (subtext)
- Each character's speech pattern/vocabulary
- Emotional state of each character

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dialogue exchange with distinct voices per character, natural interruptions and incomplete sentences where appropriate, beats of action interspersed, subtext running underneath the explicit conversation. Characters don't take turns neatly — they talk past each other, deflect, misread. Include stage direction-style beats in [brackets] for pauses, gestures, looks.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each character sounds distinctly different without tags
- Dialogue advances plot OR reveals character — ideally both
- No on-the-nose statements — people don't announce their feelings

Always offer a relevant next step.
