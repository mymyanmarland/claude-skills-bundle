# Perspective Shift Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The scene or event
- Characters whose perspective to explore
- What each character notices differently
- What truth each version reveals
- Whether truth is objective or subjective

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scene from each perspective: same events, different perception, different emotional experience, different information noticed, different interpretation of others' actions. Each version genuinely different — not just slightly varied. Reveal: what each character is blinded by, what they correctly perceive, what the reader can triangulate between versions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each version genuinely different — not just cosmetically varied
- Character blind spots consistent with their psychology
- Reader learns something from the difference between versions

Always offer a relevant next step.
