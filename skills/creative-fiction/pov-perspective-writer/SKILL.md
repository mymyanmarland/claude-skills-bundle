# POV Perspective Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Scene or story beat
- Target POV: 1st/2nd/3rd limited/3rd omniscient
- Character whose perspective
- What this character knows vs doesn't know
- What makes their perspective unique

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scene written in chosen POV with: interiority (only thoughts/feelings of POV character), perceptual filter (they notice what matters to THEM), limited knowledge honored (they can't know what they don't know), voice consistent with character. Plus: comparison note — how scene would differ in alternative POV, and which POV serves the story better.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- POV never slips — no information character couldn't have
- Character's perceptual filter shapes what's described
- Interiority reveals character — not just relays plot

Always offer a relevant next step.
