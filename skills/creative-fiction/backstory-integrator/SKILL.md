# Backstory Integrator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Character backstory to reveal
- Current scene or chapter
- What the reader needs to know vs what's nice to know
- How much urgency the current scene has
- Character's relationship to their own past

## Step 2 — Core Output

Backstory integration guide: The iceberg principle (reader senses 90% that isn't shown) → What MUST be revealed vs what can stay submerged → Techniques: triggering memory through current action, revealing through behavior patterns, dialogue that implies history, object as memory → Rewritten passage demonstrating each technique on the actual text.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Backstory revealed through present action — not stopped for explanation
- Reader learns what they need when they need it
- Character's relationship to past affects how they reveal it

Always offer a relevant next step.
