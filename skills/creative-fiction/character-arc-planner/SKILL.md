# Character Arc Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Character's starting state (flaw, wound, belief)
- What the plot will put them through
- What they need to learn
- Whether they achieve transformation (positive/negative arc)
- Genre and story length

## Step 2 — Core Output

Arc plan: Ghost (wound from past that informs flaw) → Lie (false belief character holds) → Want (what they consciously pursue) → Need (what they actually need) → Transformation arc mapped to plot beats → How each major plot event forces engagement with the flaw → Moment of truth (when they must choose: old lie or new truth) → Ending state. Positive arc: they change. Negative arc: they don't.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lie and Need distinct from Want — external vs internal
- Each plot beat makes the arc worse before better
- Transformation earned through genuine difficulty — not sudden epiphany

Always offer a relevant next step.
