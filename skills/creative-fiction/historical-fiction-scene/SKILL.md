# Historical Fiction Scene Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Historical period and location
- Characters and their social position
- The scene's narrative purpose
- Research notes available
- Balance: historical accuracy vs narrative accessibility

## Step 2 — Core Output

Historical scene with: period-accurate sensory detail (what they'd see, smell, hear — specific to era), contemporary mindset (characters think and feel appropriately for their time without being caricatures), historically grounded conflict (arising from the period's tensions), language that suggests the period without being inaccessible, research worn lightly — never lecture mode.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Research evident but never lectured — woven into action
- Character mindset of the period — not modern sensibility in costume
- Language suggests era without alienating modern reader

Always offer a relevant next step.
