# Short Story Structure Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story concept
- Current draft or outline if any
- Target length
- Genre
- What the writer feels is missing

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure analysis and guide: Short story vs novel differences (compressed, one idea, one arc) → The single effect (Poe's principle — every element serves one emotional goal) → Entry point (start as late as possible) → The turn (what changes — must be singular and clear) → The resonance (ending that echoes back to opening) → Common short story problems and fixes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single effect identified and everything serves it
- Story enters as late as possible
- Turn is singular — not multiple revelations

Always offer a relevant next step.
