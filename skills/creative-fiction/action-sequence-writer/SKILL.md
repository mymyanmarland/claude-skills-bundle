# Action Sequence Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Type of action: fight/chase/battle/heist
- Characters involved and their physical attributes
- Setting and environment
- Stakes: what happens if they fail
- Tone: realistic/cinematic/brutal/stylized

## Step 2 — Core Output

Action sequence with: spatial clarity (reader always knows where everyone is), short punchy sentences during peak action, character decisions within the action (not just things happening to them), cost (action has consequences — injury, exhaustion, loss), environment used actively (not just backdrop), an unexpected turn. Slow down the most important moment — don't rush the climax of the action.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reader always oriented spatially
- Characters make decisions — action isn't just done to them
- Slow the most crucial moment — expand narrative time at the peak

Always offer a relevant next step.
