# Narrative Voice Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Narrator type: first person/close third
- Character's background and personality
- Genre and intended tone
- Vocabulary and education level
- What makes this narrator's perspective unusual

## Step 2 — Core Output

Voice development: Vocabulary signature (words they'd use and avoid) → Sentence rhythm pattern → What they notice first (reveals priority and personality) → How they describe other people → Their relationship to the reader (confiding/unreliable/ironic/earnest) → Sample paragraph in this voice with annotation → Common voice-killing mistakes to avoid.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Voice specific enough that any page is recognizable as this narrator
- Voice consistent with character's background and psychology
- Avoids generic 'literary fiction' default voice

Always offer a relevant next step.
