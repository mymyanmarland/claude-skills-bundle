# Conflict Escalation Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current conflict state
- What's at stake
- Characters in conflict and what they each want
- How far the escalation should go
- Genre and tone

## Step 2 — Core Output

Escalated conflict scene/sequence with: clear stakes established upfront, obstacles that compound (each solution creates new problem), ticking clock element, characters making things worse through their own flaws, the moment where it seems impossible — then push further. Sentence length manipulation for pacing (shorter = faster). No easy resolution.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stakes clear and personal — not just abstract
- Each escalation step makes previous situation seem simple
- Character flaw actively makes the conflict worse

Always offer a relevant next step.
