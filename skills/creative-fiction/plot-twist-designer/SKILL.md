# Plot Twist Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story setup so far
- What the reader currently believes
- What characters/objects/events are already in play
- Genre expectations to subvert
- Emotional impact desired

## Step 2 — Core Output

3 twist options at different scales: Small (recontextualizes a character relationship) → Medium (recontextualizes Act 2) → Large (recontextualizes the entire story). For each: the twist itself, how to plant clues readers miss on first read, what false assumptions the reader holds, the moment of reveal, the retroactive re-reading experience. The best twist uses elements already present — not new information.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every twist foreshadowed — reader can find clues on re-read
- Twist recontextualizes what came before — doesn't just add new information
- Emotional impact as important as intellectual surprise

Always offer a relevant next step.
