# Dialogue Subtext Master Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What characters ostensibly talk about
- What they're really talking about
- Each character's emotional agenda
- Why they can't say the real thing directly
- Relationship between characters

## Step 2 — Core Output

Subtext dialogue exchange: Surface conversation (entirely normal topic), beneath it the real conversation (the reader feels both simultaneously), each character's real need bleeding through in word choice and deflection, a moment where the real thing almost surfaces, an ending where nothing is resolved but everything has changed. Pinter and Carver as reference points.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Surface conversation completely plausible in isolation
- Real conversation readable beneath without being obvious
- Something has shifted by end — even if unspoken

Always offer a relevant next step.
