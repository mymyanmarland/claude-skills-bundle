# Children's Fiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Age range: picture book/early reader/middle grade/YA
- Theme or story premise
- Characters (child protagonist important)
- Emotional challenge or lesson
- Tone: funny/adventurous/heartfelt/scary-safe

## Step 2 — Core Output

Age-appropriate fiction with correct: vocabulary and sentence complexity, emotional resonance (children's books deal with real fears), pacing (picture books: each page turn is a beat / MG: faster than adult / YA: adult complexity), child protagonist with real agency (adults help but don't solve), theme that respects the reader's intelligence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Child protagonist has genuine agency — adults don't rescue
- Theme present but never preachy or spelled out
- Age-appropriate complexity — not dumbed down, not over-complicated

Always offer a relevant next step.
