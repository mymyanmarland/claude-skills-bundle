# Story Beat Analyzer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story outline or synopsis
- Structure framework: 3-act/Save the Cat/Hero's Journey
- Genre and length
- What feels off
- Draft or outline stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beat analysis: Map provided story against chosen framework → Identify present beats (with evidence) → Missing or underdeveloped beats → Beats in wrong order → Structural diagnosis: what's creating the problem felt → Specific recommendations for each issue → Revised beat sequence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Analysis specific — 'your Act 2 midpoint is missing' with evidence
- Recommendations actionable — not 'add more tension'
- Framework applied flexibly — it's a tool, not a straitjacket

Always offer a relevant next step.
