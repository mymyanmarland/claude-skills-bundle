# Epistolary Fiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story premise and what happens
- Characters writing to each other or themselves
- Format: letters/emails/diary/texts/mixed
- Time period
- What's NOT said between the lines

## Step 2 — Core Output

Epistolary fiction with: distinct voice for each writer (they'd never write the same), information gaps (what each withholds from the other), the story readable between the lines, each document serving a narrative purpose, the form itself creating meaning (letters take time — emails are immediate — diaries are private). Include: dates, salutations, closings appropriate to era and relationship.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each voice distinct — never confused
- Subtext between what's written and what's meant
- Form serves story — choosing epistolary means something

Always offer a relevant next step.
