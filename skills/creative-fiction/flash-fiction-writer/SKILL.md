# Flash Fiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Genre
- Core conflict or image
- Target word count (100/500/1000)
- Tone
- Any specific ending type: twist/open/resolved

## Step 2 — Core Output

Complete flash fiction with: opening that drops reader mid-scene, compressed rising action, emotional or plot turning point, resonant ending. Every sentence earns its place. No backstory — imply it. Use sensory detail over explanation. The final line should land with weight — often circles back to the opening image.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zero wasted words — every sentence advances story or character
- Reader oriented in first 2 sentences without expository dump
- Ending resonates — doesn't just stop

Always offer a relevant next step.
