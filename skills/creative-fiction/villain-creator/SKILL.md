# Villain Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story genre
- What the villain wants
- What the villain fears
- Their relationship to the protagonist
- How far they'll go

## Step 2 — Core Output

Villain profile: Core desire (what they want and why they believe they deserve it) → Origin wound (what made them this way — without excusing) → Worldview (what they believe that justifies their actions) → Method (how they pursue goals — specific tactics) → Weakness (genuine, not just plot-convenient) → Relationship to protagonist (mirror, foil, dark version) → Most chilling moment concept. A great villain believes they're right.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Villain has coherent internal logic — not just evil for evil's sake
- Origin explains without excusing
- They would be the hero of their own story

Always offer a relevant next step.
