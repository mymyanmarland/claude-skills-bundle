# Opening Line Generator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Genre
- Story premise or theme
- Tone: dark/humorous/mysterious/literary
- Character type
- What feeling to evoke immediately

## Step 2 — Core Output

20 opening lines across 5 styles: Voice-driven (character speaks), In medias res (action already happening), World-establishing (unique world detail), Thematic (the story's heart stated slant), Uncanny (something slightly wrong). For each: why it works, what it promises the reader, what it withholds. Top 3 highlighted with rationale.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every line creates a question the reader needs answered
- No weather openings, no alarm clock openings, no mirror openings
- Each line promises a specific kind of story — genre and tone clear

Always offer a relevant next step.
