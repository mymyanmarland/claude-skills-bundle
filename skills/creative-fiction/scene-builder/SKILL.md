# Scene Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What happens in the scene
- Characters involved and their goals
- Setting and time
- Emotional tone
- What the scene must accomplish narratively

## Step 2 — Core Output

Full scene with: grounding sensory opening (place reader immediately), character movement and dialogue woven together, subtext beneath the surface dialogue, escalating tension even in quiet scenes, a turn or shift at the end. Include: blocking (where people are), interiority (one character's thoughts), beats (natural pauses in action). Show power dynamics through dialogue rhythm.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Characters want something in every scene — even small things
- Dialogue has subtext — people rarely say what they mean directly
- Scene ends differently than it began — something has shifted

Always offer a relevant next step.
