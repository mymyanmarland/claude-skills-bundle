# Story Collection Organizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stories in the collection (titles/summaries)
- Themes across the collection
- Intended reader journey
- Whether linked or standalone
- Length and scope of collection

## Step 2 — Core Output

Collection structure: Opening story criteria (invitation — establishes voice and tone, not the hardest or longest) → Arc across the collection (emotional journey from first to last) → Story sequencing rationale → Pairing principles (contrast and echo) → The anchor story (most ambitious — where to place it) → Closing story criteria (resonant, leaves the reader with the collection's heart) → Section divisions if applicable.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- First story earns the reader's trust — not the most demanding
- Emotional arc across the whole — not random sequence
- Final story resonates with the collection's heart — not just strong in isolation

Always offer a relevant next step.
