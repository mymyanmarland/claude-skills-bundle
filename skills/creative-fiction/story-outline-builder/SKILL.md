# Story Outline Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story concept or logline
- Genre and length target
- Structure preference: 3-act/Save the Cat/Hero's Journey/Story Circle
- Main character and their flaw
- Thematic question

## Step 2 — Core Output

Full outline: Act 1 (setup, inciting incident, lock-in moment) → Act 2a (new world, fun and games, midpoint) → Act 2b (complications, dark night of soul, climax setup) → Act 3 (climax, resolution, new equilibrium). Each beat: what happens + why it matters structurally. Character arc mapped alongside plot. Theme embodied — not stated.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each structural beat has clear narrative function
- Character arc and plot arc intersect at key moments
- Theme expressed through events — never character speeches

Always offer a relevant next step.
