# Horror & Dread Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Type of horror: psychological/supernatural/body/cosmic
- What the reader should fear
- What is visible vs implied
- Setting and atmosphere
- How explicit to be

## Step 2 — Core Output

Horror scene/story with: atmosphere built through wrong details (something slightly off before the obvious threat), the horror of suggestion (what isn't shown), escalating wrongness, a moment of false safety followed by renewed dread, an ending that resonates with unease not closure. Effective horror: the reader's imagination does the work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Something wrong before the threat appears — atmosphere first
- Horror suggested more than shown in psychological horror
- False safety makes the return of threat more effective

Always offer a relevant next step.
