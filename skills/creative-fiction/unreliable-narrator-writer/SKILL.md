# Unreliable Narrator Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What the narrator believes vs what is true
- Why they can't or won't see clearly (self-deception/trauma/denial)
- What truth the reader should sense
- Story genre and tone
- How far the unreliability goes

## Step 2 — Core Output

Unreliable narrative sample with: the narrator's confident voice (they don't know they're unreliable), clues embedded for the observant reader (what they don't mention, contradictions in their account), moments where the truth almost breaks through, gaps and deflections. Plus: a guide to how much the author should signal vs leave unsaid.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Narrator confident — not obviously untrustworthy
- Truth visible to careful reader from subtle signals
- Narrator's blind spots consistent with their psychology

Always offer a relevant next step.
