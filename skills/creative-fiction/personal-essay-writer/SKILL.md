# Personal Essay Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Personal experience or topic
- What the essay is really about (beyond the surface event)
- Tone: confessional/analytical/humorous/meditative
- Target publication or platform
- What the writer wants the reader to feel or understand

## Step 2 — Core Output

Personal essay with: specific scene opening (in the moment, not 'I want to tell you about'), the braided structure (present narrative + reflection + universal connection), the moment of insight (when the writer sees the experience differently), the ending that arrives rather than concludes. The best personal essays: the specific experience opens into the universal.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opens in scene — not explaining why they're writing
- Universal connection earns the specific personal story
- Insight arrived at — not stated from the beginning

Always offer a relevant next step.
