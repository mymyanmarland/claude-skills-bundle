# Fiction Feedback Coach Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fiction excerpt (paste)
- Genre and target audience
- Stage: first draft/revision/nearly final
- Specific concerns
- What kind of feedback: brutal/encouraging/balanced

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structured feedback: What's working (specific, with line citations) → Top 3 issues (specific, with line citations and rewrite suggestions) → Line-level notes (5 specific lines to examine) → Overall assessment → One thing to focus on in next revision. Feedback that teaches, not just evaluates. Show, don't just tell, what the fix looks like.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every point anchored to specific text — no vague observations
- Rewrites demonstrate the fix concretely
- Feedback tone matches requested style: brutal/encouraging/balanced

Always offer a relevant next step.
