# Chapter Hook Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Chapter content and position in story
- What happened in previous chapter
- What happens next chapter
- Tone and genre
- Protagonist's emotional state

## Step 2 — Core Output

For each chapter: Opening hook (3 options — drop into action, voice-first, disorienting image) + Ending hook (3 options — revelation, question, dread). Each option with annotation: what it creates in the reader (anticipation/dread/curiosity), how it connects to story momentum. The chapter-ending hook is THE most important element for page-turning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chapter opening creates a question or continues an existing one
- Chapter ending ends on tension — never resolution
- Reader must have a reason to read the next chapter within last paragraph

Always offer a relevant next step.
