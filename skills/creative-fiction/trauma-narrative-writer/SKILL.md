# Trauma Narrative Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Type of trauma
- Character's relationship to the trauma
- Stage of processing: raw/suppressed/integrated
- Genre and how direct to be
- What the narrative needs from this trauma

## Step 2 — Core Output

Trauma narrative guidance + sample: Non-linear revelation (trauma rarely surfaces linearly) → Body memory (trauma lives in the body — physical reactions) → Dissociation rendered on the page → The approach-avoid pattern → What the character cannot yet say directly → Recovery as non-linear. Sample passage demonstrating these principles with annotation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trauma rendered through physical experience — not just reported
- Non-linear approach mirrors how trauma actually surfaces
- Neither exploitative nor sanitized — respectful and honest

Always offer a relevant next step.
