# Narrative Tension Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story genre and tone
- Current tension level
- What the reader fears will happen
- What the characters fear
- What information is being withheld

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tension toolkit applied to story: Dramatic irony (reader knows more than character — or less) → The ticking clock → False resolution followed by new complication → The thing that almost goes wrong → Withholding: what not to tell the reader and when → Sentence-level tension: fragment. Short. Unexpected stop. Plus: a tension audit of the provided excerpt.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tension from character vulnerability — not just plot danger
- Information management explicit — what reader knows matters
- Sentence-level tension used purposefully — not throughout

Always offer a relevant next step.
