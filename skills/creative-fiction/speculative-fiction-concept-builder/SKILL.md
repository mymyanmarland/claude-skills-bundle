# Speculative Fiction Concept Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Core 'what if' premise
- Genre: hard sci-fi/soft sci-fi/fantasy/alternate history
- Tone and intended audience
- Time period or setting
- Thematic interest

## Step 2 — Core Output

Concept development: The premise fully articulated → Single change principle (change one thing and follow it rigorously) → World implications (how does this change everything?) → Character opportunities (who thrives/struggles in this world and why?) → Plot potential (what conflicts does this premise naturally generate?) → Thematic possibilities → 3 possible story angles from this premise.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Premise follows its internal logic rigorously
- Character opportunities arise from the concept — not imposed on it
- Thematic potential organic to the premise

Always offer a relevant next step.
