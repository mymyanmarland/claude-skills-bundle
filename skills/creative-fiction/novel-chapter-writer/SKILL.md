# Novel Chapter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Story context and what has happened
- Chapter goal: what must happen and what must change
- Characters in chapter
- Setting
- POV and tone

## Step 2 — Core Output

Complete novel chapter (1500-3000 words) with: chapter-opening hook, scene development through action/dialogue/interiority, at least one meaningful complication or surprise, character revelation through behavior, chapter-ending hook that demands the next chapter. Prose style consistent with stated genre and tone.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chapter advances both plot and character arc
- Something changes by chapter end — situation or understanding
- Chapter ending creates forward momentum — reader must continue

Always offer a relevant next step.
