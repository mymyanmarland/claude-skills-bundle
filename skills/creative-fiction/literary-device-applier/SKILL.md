# Literary Device Applier Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Writing sample
- Device(s) to apply or suggest
- Genre and tone
- Skill level
- What effect to create

## Step 2 — Core Output

Applied literary device guide: Device identification in existing text (what's already working) → 3 devices recommended for this piece with examples: extended metaphor, motif, foreshadowing, irony, symbolism, juxtaposition, or others → Demonstration of each device applied to the actual text → How to weave devices in naturally vs forcing them.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Devices serve the story — not deployed to show cleverness
- Applied to actual text — not described abstractly
- Organic integration demonstrated — not bolted on

Always offer a relevant next step.
