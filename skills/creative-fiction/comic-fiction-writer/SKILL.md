# Comic Fiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Comic target: situation/character/language/absurdism
- Characters and their comic dynamic
- Tone: dry/slapstick/dark/romantic comedy
- Scene setup
- What the comedy should reveal about character

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comic scene with: comic timing built into prose rhythm (setup in one sentence — payoff hits after a beat), character-based humor (funny because of who this person is), the rule of three (establish, repeat, subvert), unexpected specificity (funniest when most specific), the comic character who doesn't know they're funny. Humor that arises from character — not situation imposed on character.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Humor arises from character — not situation imposed on them
- Timing built into sentence structure
- Specificity is the engine of comedy — vague isn't funny

Always offer a relevant next step.
