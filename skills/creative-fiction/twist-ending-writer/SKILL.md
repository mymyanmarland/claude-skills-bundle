# Twist Ending Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Genre and tone
- The twist itself or a direction to go
- Length of story
- What false assumption the reader should hold
- How much setup the ending needs

## Step 2 — Core Output

Twist story structure: Setup phase (establish the false assumption naturally — readers shouldn't suspect they're being misled) → Clue planting (at least 3 elements that recontextualize on re-read) → Misdirection (reinforce the false reading) → The reveal (as late as possible) → The reversal (how the story reads differently now). Plus: the story itself, written to specification.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Setup naturally establishes false assumption — no artificiality
- Clues visible on re-read but not first read
- Reveal as late as structurally possible

Always offer a relevant next step.
