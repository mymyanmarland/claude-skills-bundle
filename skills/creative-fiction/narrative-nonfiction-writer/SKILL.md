# Narrative Nonfiction Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- True story or subject
- Key scenes and characters
- Research available
- Target publication
- Balance: story vs information

## Step 2 — Core Output

Narrative nonfiction with: scene-by-scene structure (not essay), real characters with interiority (based on interviews/evidence), the narrative arc of a story applied to real events, information woven into scenes not presented as facts, the writer's presence calibrated (immersive vs. invisible). Truth honored — storytelling deployed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every scene based on evidence — no invented dialogue without basis
- Information emerges from scenes — not inserted as facts
- Narrative arc serves the subject — not imposed on it

Always offer a relevant next step.
