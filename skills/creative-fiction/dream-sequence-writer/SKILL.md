# Dream Sequence Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Character's fears and desires
- What the dream should reveal or process
- Logic level: realistic/surreal/nightmarish
- Connection to story events
- Whether the character remembers it

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dream sequence with: the dream's own internal logic (consistent within its rules), compressed versions of waking fears/desires, transformation of real people and places, the emotional truth delivered through image not statement, a waking moment that connects it to the story. Avoid: obvious Freudian symbols, dream sequences that are just recaps, prophetic dreams that remove mystery.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dream has its own internal logic
- Emotional truth through image — not statement
- Connection to waking story clear but not heavy-handed

Always offer a relevant next step.
