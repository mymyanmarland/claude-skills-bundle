# Management Accounts in Excel Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Excel version and platform
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Specific task or use case
- Data type and approximate size
- Intended output or deliverable

## Step 2 — Core Output

Management accounts: Cover sheet (period / prepared by / approval / version) → Executive summary (1-page summary of key metrics / commentary boxes) → P&L (actuals vs budget vs prior year / variance analysis) → Balance sheet (current period and prior period / key movements explained) → Cash flow (operating / investing / financing activities / opening and closing cash) → KPI page (3-5 operational KPIs / trends chart) → Commentary (text boxes with management commentary — not just tables of numbers) → Distribution (PDF export / protect before sharing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Commentary alongside numbers: tables without explanation are not management accounts
- Prior year comparison: context from prior year is often more useful than budget vs actual
- PDF export before distributing: protects formulas and ensures layout integrity
