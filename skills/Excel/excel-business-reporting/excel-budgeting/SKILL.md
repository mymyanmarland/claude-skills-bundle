# Personal and Business Budgeting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Excel version and platform
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Specific task or use case
- Data type and approximate size
- Intended output or deliverable

## Step 2 — Core Output

Budgeting in Excel: Categories (income categories / expense categories — comprehensive list upfront) → Monthly budget (budgeted amount per category per month) → Actual tracking (enter actual amounts / or link to bank data via Power Query) → Variance (=Actual-Budget / conditional formatting for overspend) → Running total (=SUM of actuals to date vs SUM of budget to date) → Annual summary (SUMIF across all months by category) → Savings rate (=(Income-Expenses)/Income) → Visualisation (waterfall chart showing income minus expense categories) → Zero-based budgeting (every pound/dollar allocated — budget equals income).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zero-based budgeting: assigns every unit of income — no unallocated money
- Running total vs budget: cumulative comparison is more meaningful than month-by-month
- Waterfall chart for budget: shows income minus each expense category visually

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
