# Salary Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Job title and level
- Industry
- Geographic location
- Years of experience
- Company size

## Step 2 — Core Output

Salary research package: Data sources ranked (Levels.fyi for tech, LinkedIn Salary, Glassdoor, Payscale, BLS, industry surveys) → Research methodology (how to triangulate) → Total compensation breakdown (base/bonus/equity/benefits) → Market range for this specific role → Factors that move salary up or down → Your position in the range (justified) → Target number and walk-away number.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total compensation — not just base salary
- Multiple sources triangulated — not one number
- Specific target number derived from data — not a guess

Always offer a relevant next step.
