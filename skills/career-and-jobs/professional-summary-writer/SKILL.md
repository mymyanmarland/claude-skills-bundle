# Professional Summary Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current/target role
- Years of experience
- Top 3 achievements
- Industry keywords
- Career goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

3 summary options at different word counts (50/75/100 words). Formula: [Title] with [X] years in [field] → Top achievement or specialization → What you bring to employer → Optional: what you're seeking. Each version front-loads most impressive credential. Includes keyword density check for ATS. Avoids: 'hardworking', 'team player', 'passionate professional'.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Most impressive credential in first sentence
- Keywords from target JD woven naturally
- Reads like a human wrote it — not a template

Always offer a relevant next step.
