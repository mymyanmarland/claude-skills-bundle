# Workplace Rights & Discrimination Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Type of issue experienced
- Evidence available
- HR relationship
- Company size and culture
- Desired outcome

## Step 2 — Core Output

Guide: Documentation protocol (what to record, how, where to store) → Internal reporting options and considerations → HR complaint process → External options (EEOC, state agencies) → Consulting an employment attorney (when and how) → Support resources → Protecting your employment during process → Decision framework (internal vs external reporting). Note: strongly recommend professional legal advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Legal advice recommendation prominent and repeated
- Documentation protocol specific and practical
- Reader's wellbeing centered throughout

Always offer a relevant next step.
