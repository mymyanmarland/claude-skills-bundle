# Professional Development Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and career goal
- Skills to develop
- Time available for development
- Budget
- Learning style preferences

## Step 2 — Core Output

PD plan: 90-day / 6-month / 1-year learning goals → Skill development roadmap (specific to career goal) → Learning resources per skill (courses/books/communities/mentors) → Practice opportunities in current role → Portfolio of evidence for each skill → Progress checkpoints → Investment budget allocation. Connects every development activity to career outcome.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every development activity connected to career goal
- Resources specific — not just 'take an online course'
- Practice in current role identified — not just external learning

Always offer a relevant next step.
