# Informational Interview Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Career goal or target field
- Person being interviewed (role/seniority)
- Relationship (warm intro vs cold)
- Goals for the conversation
- Time available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Informational interview guide: Outreach message (personalized, specific ask, low friction) → 10 best questions in priority order → Questions to avoid → How to take notes without being rude → How to keep the conversation going → Follow-up thank you (within 24h, specific, gives back) → How to turn this into a relationship → What to do with what you learned.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Questions focused on their experience — not job fishing
- Follow-up gives something — not just thanks
- Clear next step for relationship maintenance

Always offer a relevant next step.
