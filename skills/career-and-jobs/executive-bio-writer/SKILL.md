# Executive Bio Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Executive's background and role
- Career highlights and achievements
- Speaking topics or expertise areas
- Audience and platform
- Tone: formal/approachable/authoritative

## Step 2 — Core Output

3 bio lengths: One-liner (25 words — conference intro) → Short (100 words — speaker profile) → Full (300 words — website/press kit). Each version: third person, credential-first, human element included, specific achievements not vague titles. Speaking bio emphasizes talk topics. Board bio emphasizes expertise and governance. Press bio emphasizes newsworthy elements.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Third person throughout
- Most impressive credential in first sentence
- Human element in every version — not just a list of titles

Always offer a relevant next step.
