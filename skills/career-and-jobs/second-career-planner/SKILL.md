# Second Career Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current career and age
- Target second career
- Transferable assets
- Financial situation
- Timeline and risk tolerance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Second career plan: Asset inventory (what transfers — often more than expected) → Energy and passion audit → Research phase (informational interviews, job shadowing) → Bridge strategy (how to transition without starting at zero) → Financial transition planning → Ageism navigation strategies → Network building in new field → Realistic timeline → Identity transition (career is part of identity).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Financial reality integrated — not ignored
- Ageism strategies practical and honest
- Identity transition acknowledged — career change is also a self-change

Always offer a relevant next step.
