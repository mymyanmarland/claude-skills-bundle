# Leadership Style Developer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and team
- Leadership challenges experienced
- Leadership models admired
- Team type and needs
- Feedback received from others

## Step 2 — Core Output

Leadership style development: Style assessment (directive/coaching/supportive/delegating by situation) → Strengths to build on → Blind spots to address → Leadership philosophy statement (what you believe about people and work) → Team-specific approach → Communication style guide → Feedback-giving framework → 90-day leadership development plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Style situational — not one-size-fits-all
- Blind spots named specifically — not avoided
- Leadership philosophy authentic — not corporate language

Always offer a relevant next step.
