# Job Interview Question Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Role being interviewed for
- Industry
- Career level
- Specific concerns about answers
- Interview format: behavioral/technical/case

## Step 2 — Core Output

Answer guide for 20 most common questions: Tell me about yourself → Why this role/company → Greatest strength → Greatest weakness → Biggest achievement → Biggest failure → Where do you see yourself in 5 years → Why are you leaving → Salary expectations → Work style → Management style → Conflict example → Prioritization example → Questions for them. Each: framework + customizable template + what NOT to say.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each answer framework adaptable to their specific story
- 'What not to say' as important as what to say
- Weakness answer genuinely honest — not 'I work too hard'

Always offer a relevant next step.
