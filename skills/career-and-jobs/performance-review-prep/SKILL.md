# Performance Review Prep Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Role and level
- Achievements this review period
- Goals set last review
- Areas for improvement
- Compensation goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Review prep package: Achievement summary (top 5 with STAR format) → Goals progress assessment (honest, with context for misses) → Self-rating justification → Raise/promotion case (market data + achievements) → Development goals for next period → Questions to ask manager → What to do if review is negative → Follow-up email template.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Achievements specific and quantified
- Compensation case data-backed — not just 'I deserve more'
- Strategy for negative review included

Always offer a relevant next step.
