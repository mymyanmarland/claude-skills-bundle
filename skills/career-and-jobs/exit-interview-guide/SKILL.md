# Exit Interview Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Reason for leaving
- Relationship with company
- Future industry proximity
- What they want from exit interview
- Any issues to raise or not raise

## Step 2 — Core Output

Exit interview guide: What to say (honest but professional framing for common questions) → What not to say (bridges to avoid burning) → Feedback worth giving (that won't hurt you) → How to handle negative questions about managers/colleagues → Reference relationship protection → Offboarding checklist → Keeping network warm after departure.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Honest feedback framing protects future relationships
- What NOT to say explicit — not just what to say
- Reference relationship protection primary concern

Always offer a relevant next step.
