# Team Communication Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Team size and distribution
- Tools used
- Communication pain points
- Meeting culture
- Async vs sync balance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team communication guide: Channel norms (what goes where: email/Slack/meeting/document) → Response time expectations by channel → Meeting norms (standing agenda, decisions documented, attendance expectations) → Async communication standards → Escalation protocol → New member onboarding to comms → Quarterly norms review. One-page quick reference included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Channel norms specific — no ambiguity about what goes where
- Response time expectations explicit and realistic
- One-pager genuinely usable as reference

Always offer a relevant next step.
