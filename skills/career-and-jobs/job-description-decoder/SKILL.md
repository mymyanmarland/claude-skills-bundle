# Job Description Decoder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Job description text
- Target role
- Your background
- Industry context
- Concerns about the role

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decoded analysis: Must-haves vs nice-to-haves (many JDs list aspirational requirements) → Culture signals in language (bureaucratic vs fast-paced vs innovative) → Red flags identified (unrealistic expectations, vague compensation, 'wear many hats') → Green flags noted → Real minimum qualifications vs stated ones → Questions to ask in interview → Whether to apply and why.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Must-haves vs nice-to-haves distinction specific and justified
- Red flags explained — not just listed
- Realistic advice on whether to apply given stated background

Always offer a relevant next step.
