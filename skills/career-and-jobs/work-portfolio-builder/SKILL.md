# Work Portfolio Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Field and role
- Available work samples
- Target audience (employers/clients)
- Platform: website/PDF/Behance
- Career stage: entry/mid/senior

## Step 2 — Core Output

Portfolio plan: What to include (work samples, case studies, testimonials, process documentation) → Selection criteria (best over most) → Case study structure for each piece (challenge/approach/result) → Platform recommendation with rationale → Presentation order → What to remove or exclude → About page/bio integration → Ongoing maintenance plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Quality over quantity — 5 strong pieces beat 20 mediocre
- Each piece has context — not just the deliverable
- Case study structure shows thinking — not just output

Always offer a relevant next step.
