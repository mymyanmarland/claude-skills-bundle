# C-Suite Communication Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Communication context (presentation/email/meeting)
- Executive audience
- Message to deliver
- Time available
- Stakes involved

## Step 2 — Core Output

C-suite communication guide: The pyramid principle (conclusion first, then support) → Executive attention span reality (you have 90 seconds) → What executives care about (business impact, risk, resources needed) → The one-page briefing format → Verbal communication tips (direct, no hedging, confident) → How to handle executive questions → Email to executive formula → Presentation structure for senior audiences.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Conclusion-first approach explicit and practiced
- Business impact framing — not activity reporting
- Question-handling script for tough executive questions

Always offer a relevant next step.
