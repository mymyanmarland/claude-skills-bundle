# Interview Thank-You Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Interview details (role, interviewers, topics discussed)
- Something specific from the conversation
- Enthusiasm level for the role
- Anything to clarify or add
- Timeline for decision

## Step 2 — Core Output

Thank-you email: Subject line (specific, not 'Thank You') → Genuine opener referencing specific conversation moment → Reinforcement of fit (1-2 key qualifications tied to what they discussed) → Address any concern raised in interview → Enthusiasm closing → Timeline reference. Under 200 words. Send within 2 hours for competitive roles. Separate emails to each interviewer.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- References something specific from the conversation — proves it was read
- Under 200 words — executives won't read long thank-yous
- Separate personalized emails to each interviewer

Always offer a relevant next step.
