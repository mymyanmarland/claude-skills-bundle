# Job Offer Evaluator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Offer details (salary, benefits, role)
- Current situation
- Career goals
- Personal priorities
- Alternative offers or options

## Step 2 — Core Output

Offer evaluation framework: Total compensation calculation (salary + bonus + equity + benefits value) → Role quality assessment (growth, learning, manager, culture signals) → Company health signals (funding, trajectory, reviews) → Career fit score (does this get you closer to your goal?) → Gut check questions → Negotiation opportunities identified → Decision matrix with your priorities weighted. Go/no-go recommendation with reasoning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total compensation — not just base salary
- Company health assessed — not just the role
- Decision matrix personalised to stated priorities

Always offer a relevant next step.
