# Career Coaching Session Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Career challenge or question
- Life stage
- What they've already tried
- Values and non-negotiables
- Time horizon

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching session: Situation assessment (where are you now — honest) → Desired state (where do you want to be — specific) → Gap analysis (what's between here and there) → Obstacle inventory (internal and external) → Resource audit (what do you have to work with) → Next action identification (smallest step that moves forward) → Accountability commitment. Can be self-facilitated.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Questions open-ended — invite reflection not yes/no
- Internal and external obstacles both explored
- Smallest next action concrete enough to do this week

Always offer a relevant next step.
