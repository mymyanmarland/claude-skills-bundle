# Career Brand Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target role and industry
- Career story and differentiators
- Platforms to develop
- Current online presence
- Time investment available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Career brand package: Brand statement (what you're known for in 10 words) → Consistent value proposition across all platforms → LinkedIn-resume-bio alignment guide → Content strategy (what to share/create/comment on) → Keywords to own → Visual consistency recommendations → 90-day brand building actions → Metrics to track brand growth.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brand statement specific and ownable
- All platform versions tell same story — different formats
- Content strategy sustainable with stated time investment

Always offer a relevant next step.
