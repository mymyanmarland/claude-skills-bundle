# Workplace Visibility Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current visibility level
- Work type (deep work/collaborative)
- Company culture
- Manager relationship
- Career goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Visibility strategy: Work product visibility (how to make great work seen) → Communication cadence (updates that inform without annoying) → Meeting presence (how to contribute meaningfully) → Sponsor vs mentor (finding someone who advocates for you) → Volunteer for visible work → The brag doc (documenting wins for review time) → Cross-functional relationships → External visibility (speaking, writing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Visibility strategies ethical — not credit-stealing
- Manager update cadence appropriate to culture
- Brag doc habit practical — not a big time investment

Always offer a relevant next step.
