# Career Story Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Career history (roles and transitions)
- Any non-linear moves or gaps
- Target role and industry
- What the story needs to explain
- Audience: interviewer/network/LinkedIn

## Step 2 — Core Output

Career story framework: The through-line (what connects all your experience — even if it wasn't planned) → The 3-act structure (early career/growth/now) → How to explain career changes as intentional narrative → The 'tell me about yourself' answer (90-second version) → LinkedIn 'About' version → Networking party version (30-second). Gap and pivot framing included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Through-line is genuine — not invented retrospectively
- Career changes framed as intentional even if they weren't planned that way
- Each version appropriate length for context

Always offer a relevant next step.
