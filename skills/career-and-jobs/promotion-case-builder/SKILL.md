# Promotion Case Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and target promotion
- Achievements this period
- Time in current role
- Market data available
- Manager relationship

## Step 2 — Core Output

Promotion case: Evidence package (top 5 achievements in STAR format, above job description evidence) → Market rate comparison (promoted role salary vs current) → Business case (what the company gains by promoting you now) → Timing strategy (when to have the conversation) → The ask (specific title and salary range) → Handling 'not yet' (what criteria, what timeline) → Conversation script.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Evidence shows operating above current level already
- Business case frames it as company benefit — not entitlement
- 'Not yet' response gets specific criteria — not vague encouragement

Always offer a relevant next step.
