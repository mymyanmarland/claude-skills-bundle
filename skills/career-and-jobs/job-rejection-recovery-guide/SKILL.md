# Job Rejection Recovery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage of rejection (application/interview/final round)
- How invested they were
- Relationship with the company
- Current job search status
- Emotional state

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rejection recovery guide: Immediate response (how to process) → Professional response email (within 24h — stays on good terms, asks for feedback) → Feedback request script → What to do with feedback → Keeping the door open (they might hire again) → Momentum rebuilding plan → What this rejection teaches → Getting back into the search.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Response email gracious and professional — no frustration
- Feedback request specific — increases chance of getting useful feedback
- Keeps door open without being desperate

Always offer a relevant next step.
