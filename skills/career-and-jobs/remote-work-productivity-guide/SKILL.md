# Remote Work Productivity Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Job type (deep work/meetings/collaborative)
- Home environment
- Time zone and team location
- Biggest remote work challenges
- Tools available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Remote productivity system: Physical environment optimization → Schedule architecture (deep work blocks, meeting clustering) → Communication protocols (async vs sync decisions) → Visibility strategy (how to be seen without being in the office) → End-of-day shutdown ritual → Boundary setting with household members → Energy management through the day → Weekly rhythm.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Visibility strategy explicit — remote workers must be intentional
- Deep work blocks protected from meeting creep
- Shutdown ritual separates work from personal time

Always offer a relevant next step.
