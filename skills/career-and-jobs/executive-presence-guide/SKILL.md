# Executive Presence Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and seniority
- Specific presence gaps (communication/gravitas/appearance)
- Audience: peers/senior leaders/direct reports
- Cultural context
- Timeline for development

## Step 2 — Core Output

Executive presence development: The 3 dimensions (communication/gravitas/appearance) → Specific behaviors per dimension → Communication: speaking with authority, handling questions, silence as a tool → Gravitas: being strategic not just tactical, having a point of view → Meeting behavior guide → Written communication elevation → 30-day practice plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Behaviors specific and observable — not vague 'be more confident'
- Cultural context acknowledged — presence looks different across cultures
- Practice plan has concrete daily actions

Always offer a relevant next step.
