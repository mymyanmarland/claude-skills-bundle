# LinkedIn Headline Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and title
- Target role or industry
- Top 3 skills or specializations
- Career goal: job search/thought leadership/clients
- Keywords from target job descriptions

## Step 2 — Core Output

10 headline options across styles: Keyword-rich (for search) → Value proposition (what you deliver) → Title + specialty combo → Aspiration headline (target role) → Social proof headline (numbers/results). Each under 220 characters. Top 3 highlighted with recruiter search reasoning. Preview of how it appears in search results.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Primary keywords appear in first 60 characters
- Each headline genuinely different approach
- Avoids: 'passionate', 'results-driven', 'dynamic professional'

Always offer a relevant next step.
