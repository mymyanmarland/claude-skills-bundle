# Career Gap Explainer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Length and nature of gap
- What happened during gap (real)
- Target role
- How long ago the gap was
- Level of disclosure comfortable with

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gap explanation framework: The framing principle (gaps are common — confidence is key) → What to say for gap type: health/caregiving/layoff/travel/education/intentional break → Resume treatment (how to minimize visual gap) → Cover letter mention (brief and confident) → Interview answer (30 seconds max — pivot to what you bring now) → What not to over-explain → Follow-up evidence (what you did / learned / maintained).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Explanation 30 seconds maximum — then pivot
- Confidence in delivery more important than the content
- What not to over-explain is often the most important guidance

Always offer a relevant next step.
