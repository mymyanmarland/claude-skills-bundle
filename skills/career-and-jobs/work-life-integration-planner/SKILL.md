# Work-Life Integration Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Work demands and hours
- Personal priorities and commitments
- Flexibility available
- Pain points
- What sustainable looks like for them

## Step 2 — Core Output

Integration plan: Values clarification (what actually matters) → Boundary audit (where are limits being violated) → Energy management (not time management) → Weekly template with protected personal time → Work shutdown ritual → Communication to manager/team about boundaries → Recovery practices → Red flag monitoring → Quarterly recalibration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Energy management emphasis — not just time management
- Boundaries communicated professionally — not assumed
- Quarterly recalibration prevents gradual boundary erosion

Always offer a relevant next step.
