# Workplace Boundary Setter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The boundary needed
- Relationship to person
- What has happened so far
- Professional stakes
- Preferred communication style

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boundary-setting toolkit: The boundary stated clearly to yourself first → Email version (professional, non-apologetic) → In-person script → Response to pushback (3 likely responses + replies) → Escalation path if boundary is violated → How to maintain boundary without relationship damage. Focus: professional impact framing, not personal preference.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Boundary stated in professional terms — impact on work, not personal feelings
- No over-explanation or apologizing
- Pushback response calm and repeated — not escalating

Always offer a relevant next step.
