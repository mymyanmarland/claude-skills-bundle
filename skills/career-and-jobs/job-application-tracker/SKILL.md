# Job Application Tracker Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Volume of applications
- Platforms used
- Follow-up preferences
- Spreadsheet or app preference
- Current tracking pain points

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tracking system: Spreadsheet structure (columns: company/role/date applied/status/contact/follow-up date/notes/outcome) → Status codes with definitions → Follow-up schedule (when to follow up after each stage) → Follow-up email templates per stage → Weekly review protocol → Metrics to track (response rate by source) → Red flag indicators (when to move on).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Follow-up schedule specific — not 'follow up after a week'
- Templates ready to personalize — not generic
- Weekly review catches applications falling through cracks

Always offer a relevant next step.
