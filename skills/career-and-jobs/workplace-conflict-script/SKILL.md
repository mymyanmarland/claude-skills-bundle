# Workplace Conflict Script Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The situation or conflict
- Relationship to the other person
- Desired outcome
- What's been tried already
- Stakes involved

## Step 2 — Core Output

Conversation script: Opening line (neutral, non-accusatory) → Your observation (specific behavior, not character) → Impact on work (professional framing) → Your request (specific, reasonable) → How to handle their response (3 likely responses with replies) → Closing and next steps. Script for: in-person conversation, follow-up email. What not to say and why.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Focus on behavior and impact — not character or motives
- Specific request — not 'I need things to be better'
- Handles 3 likely responses — including defensive one

Always offer a relevant next step.
