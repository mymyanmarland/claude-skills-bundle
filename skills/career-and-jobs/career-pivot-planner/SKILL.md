# Career Pivot Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and skills
- Target new field or role
- Timeline for transition
- Financial constraints
- Education/certification options

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pivot plan: Transferable skills audit (what crosses over) → Skills gap analysis (what's missing) → Bridge role identification (intermediate step that moves direction) → Learning pathway (specific courses/certs/projects) → Portfolio/experience building during transition → Network building in new field → Financial runway analysis → 12-month transition roadmap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Transferable skills analysis specific — not generic 'leadership'
- Bridge roles real and achievable
- Financial reality acknowledged — not ignored

Always offer a relevant next step.
