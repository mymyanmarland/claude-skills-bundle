# Job Search Strategy Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current role and experience
- Target role and industry
- Timeline
- Geographic constraints
- Network strength

## Step 2 — Core Output

Job search strategy: Target role definition (3 specific titles to search) → Target company list (20-30 companies by tier: dream/likely/safety) → Channel allocation (referrals 40%/direct apply 30%/recruiter 20%/other 10%) → Weekly activity targets (applications/outreach/networking) → 30/60/90 day milestones → Tracking system setup → Energy management (job searching is a job).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Referral channel prioritized — highest conversion rate
- Target company list tiered honestly
- Weekly targets realistic — not demoralizing

Always offer a relevant next step.
