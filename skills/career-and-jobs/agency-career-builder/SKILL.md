# Agency & Consulting Career Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Agency type and size
- Current level
- Billability pressures
- Client relationship experience
- Advancement goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Agency career guide: The billability trap (how to avoid being too busy to advance) → Client relationship skills that drive career (not just delivery) → Visible work vs invisible work balance → How to move from executor to advisor → Internal vs external reputation building → Specialization vs generalist decision → The jump to client-side: when and how → Senior level expectations by agency type.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Billability trap explicitly addressed — unique to agency life
- Client relationship skills differentiated from delivery skills
- Jump to client-side addressed honestly

Always offer a relevant next step.
