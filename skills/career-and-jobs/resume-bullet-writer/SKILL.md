# Resume Bullet Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Job title and responsibilities
- Specific achievements with any numbers available
- Industry and seniority
- Target role
- ATS keywords needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

10-15 resume bullets per role using CAR formula: Context (brief situation) + Action (what YOU did, strong verb) + Result (quantified impact). Format: '[Strong verb] [what you did] resulting in [measurable outcome]'. Include variety: revenue impact, efficiency gains, team leadership, project delivery. Offer 3 versions of strongest bullet at different lengths.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every bullet starts with a strong past-tense action verb
- At least 60% of bullets include a number or metric
- No 'responsible for' or 'duties included' language

Always offer a relevant next step.
