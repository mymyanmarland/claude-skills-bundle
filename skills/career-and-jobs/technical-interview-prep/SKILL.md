# Technical Interview Prep Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Role type: software/data/analytics/finance
- Interview format: coding/system design/case
- Seniority level
- Time to prepare
- Known weak areas

## Step 2 — Core Output

Technical interview prep plan: Coding interviews (data structures priority list, problem-solving framework, practice schedule) → System design (components to study, design template) → Behavioral components in technical interviews → Company-specific prep signals → Daily practice structure → How to think out loud effectively → What to do when stuck → Post-interview reflection template.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Practice schedule realistic for timeline given
- Problem-solving framework applicable to unseen problems
- Thinking-out-loud guidance explicit — often the difference maker

Always offer a relevant next step.
