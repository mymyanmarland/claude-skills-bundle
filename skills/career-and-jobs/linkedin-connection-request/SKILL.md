# LinkedIn Connection Request Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Who you're connecting with
- How you found them or know them
- Reason for connecting
- Your background
- Goal: network/job/learn/collaborate

## Step 2 — Core Output

5 connection request templates for scenarios: Mutual connection → Admired their content → Same industry/alumni → Cold (saw their profile) → Post-event meeting. Each under 300 characters (LinkedIn limit). Each: specific opener (why THEM), brief who you are (relevant), low-friction reason to connect. No pitch in the connection request. Follow-up message template for after acceptance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specific to the person — proves you looked at their profile
- No pitch in connection request — save for after
- Under 300 characters — LinkedIn limit respected

Always offer a relevant next step.
