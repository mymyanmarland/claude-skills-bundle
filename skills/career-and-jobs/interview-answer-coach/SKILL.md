# Interview Answer Coach Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The interview question
- Relevant experience or story to use
- Role being interviewed for
- Weakness areas in storytelling
- Time limit for answer

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coached answer: Situation (brief context — 1-2 sentences) → Task (your specific responsibility) → Action (what YOU did — detailed, 60% of answer) → Result (quantified outcome + reflection). Plus: what to cut (common over-explaining), what to add (missing specifics), timing check (2-3 min optimal), alternative story if first one is weak. 'Strong' and 'stronger' versions compared.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Action section most detailed — at least 60% of answer
- Result quantified or at minimum qualified specifically
- Answer is 2-3 minutes spoken — no more

Always offer a relevant next step.
