# Job Board Optimization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target roles
- Industry
- Location preferences
- Experience level
- Current profiles if any

## Step 2 — Core Output

Platform-by-platform optimization: LinkedIn (open to work settings, skills section, keyword optimization, activity signals) → Indeed (resume upload, job alerts, salary signal) → Glassdoor (review contribution strategy) → Niche job boards by industry. For each: specific settings to enable, keywords to add, signals that attract recruiters, what to avoid.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Open to work settings specific — broad vs recruiters only decision
- Keywords derived from target job descriptions
- Activity signals (LinkedIn) explicit — what to post/engage with

Always offer a relevant next step.
