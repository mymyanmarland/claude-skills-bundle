# Career Skills Gap Analyzer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current skills and experience
- Target role and job descriptions
- Timeline
- Learning preferences and resources
- Budget for development

## Step 2 — Core Output

Skills gap analysis: Current profile vs target role requirements → Gaps ranked by: critical (must have) / important (should have) / nice-to-have → Learning plan per gap (specific course/project/experience) → Quick wins (skills closable in 30 days) → Medium-term (3-6 months) → Long-term (6-12 months) → Portfolio projects to demonstrate new skills → Timeline to ready.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gaps ranked by hiring impact — not just listed
- Learning resources specific — not 'take a course'
- Portfolio projects bridge gap and demonstrate competence simultaneously

Always offer a relevant next step.
