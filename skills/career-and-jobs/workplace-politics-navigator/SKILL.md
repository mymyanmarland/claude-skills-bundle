# Workplace Politics Navigator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Specific political situation
- Key players and their interests
- Your position and goals
- Organizational culture
- What you're willing and not willing to do

## Step 2 — Core Output

Navigation guide: Stakeholder map (who has power, who has influence, who is affected) → Interest analysis (what does each person actually want?) → Alliance building (who are natural allies?) → Information strategy (what to share, what to protect) → Communication plan per stakeholder → Ethical boundaries (what to never do) → Escalation path if politics become toxic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Interests-based analysis — not personality-based
- Ethical limits explicit — politics ≠ manipulation
- Escalation path for genuinely toxic situations

Always offer a relevant next step.
