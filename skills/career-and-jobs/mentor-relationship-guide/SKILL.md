# Mentor Relationship Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Career stage and goals
- What mentorship is needed: industry/skill/career navigation
- Network access
- What you can offer in return
- Time commitment available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mentor guide: Who to look for (specific characteristics for your goal) → How to identify candidates → Outreach message (specific ask, low commitment first ask) → First meeting agenda → How to come prepared every meeting → What to ask for (specific enough to be useful) → How to be a good mentee → When and how to let a relationship evolve or end.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- First ask is specific and low-commitment
- Mentee responsibilities explicit — not just what mentor does
- 'Good mentee' behaviors detailed — relationship reciprocal

Always offer a relevant next step.
