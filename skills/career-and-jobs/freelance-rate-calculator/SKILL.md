# Freelance Rate Calculator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Specialty and experience level
- Target annual income
- Business expenses
- Hours billable per year
- Market and geography

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rate calculation: Minimum viable rate (income goal + expenses ÷ billable hours + tax buffer) → Market rate research (where to find data for specialty) → Value-based rate potential → Rate by project type (hourly vs project vs retainer) → Rate increase strategy → How to present rate to clients → How to handle 'you're too expensive' → Packaging to make rate feel justified.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Calculation includes taxes and expenses — not just income goal
- Market rate research specific to specialty
- Value-based approach explained — not just cost-plus

Always offer a relevant next step.
