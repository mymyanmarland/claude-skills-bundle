# Paper Marbling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper marbling guide: Size preparation (carrageenan or water) → Paint selection and dilution → Comb and stylus patterns → Stone/feather/chevron designs → Paper preparation (alum sizing) → Laying paper on size → Lifting and drying → Cleaning between prints → Troubleshooting (paint sinking/mixing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Paper alum sizing critical — paint will not transfer without it
- Size viscosity affects pattern spreading
- Cleaning size between prints preserves pattern clarity

Always offer a relevant next step.
