# Sourdough Bread Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sourdough guide: Starter creation and maintenance (feeding schedule) → Autolyse process → Folding vs kneading → Bulk fermentation indicators → Shaping techniques → Cold retard → Scoring patterns → Dutch oven baking → Crust and crumb troubleshooting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Starter health assessment before baking
- Fermentation by feel not just time — the core skill
- Troubleshooting guide for flat or dense loaves

Always offer a relevant next step.
