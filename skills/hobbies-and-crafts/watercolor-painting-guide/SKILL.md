# Watercolor Painting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Watercolor guide: Paper selection (hot vs cold press) → Brush types and care → Pigment characteristics → Wet-on-wet vs wet-on-dry → Color mixing and palette setup → Washes (flat/graduated/variegated) → Masking techniques → Lifting and corrections → Composition basics for watercolor.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Paper quality determines everything — cheap paper frustrates beginners
- Wet-on-wet timing — the watercolor magic
- Color mixing basics — primaries before complex mixes

Always offer a relevant next step.
