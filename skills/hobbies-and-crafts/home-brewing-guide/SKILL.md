# Home Brewing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home brewing guide: Equipment essentials (starter kit) → Sanitization protocols (the most important step) → Extract vs all-grain decision → Yeast selection and pitching → Fermentation monitoring → Dry hopping → Bottling vs kegging → Carbonation → Common off-flavors and causes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sanitization is 80% of home brewing success
- Fermentation temperature control — most common failure point
- Off-flavor diagnosis guides improvement

Always offer a relevant next step.
