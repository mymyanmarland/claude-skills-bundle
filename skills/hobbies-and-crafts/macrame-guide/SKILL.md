# Macrame Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Macrame guide: Cord selection (cotton/jute/nylon) → Cord preparation and cutting → Mounting techniques → Basic knots (square/half hitch/lark's head/spiral) → Pattern reading → Fringe finishing → Color and texture combinations → Project sizing calculations → Troubleshooting uneven knotting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cord preparation length calculation — 4x finished length rule
- Square knot direction consistency — creates pattern or twist
- Fringe finishing options affect the final look

Always offer a relevant next step.
