# Pottery and Ceramics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Ceramics guide: Clay body selection → Hand-building techniques (pinch/coil/slab) → Wheel throwing basics → Trimming and foot ring → Drying stages (leather hard vs bone dry) → Bisque and glaze firing → Glazing techniques → Kiln safety → Troubleshooting cracks and warping.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clay drying stages — rushing creates cracks
- Bisque vs glaze firing temperatures
- Centering on wheel — the foundational challenge

Always offer a relevant next step.
