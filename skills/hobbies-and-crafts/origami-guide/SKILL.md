# Origami Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Origami guide: Paper selection (size and weight) → Valley vs mountain fold vocabulary → Base folds (preliminary/bird/fish/frog) → Crease precision → Reverse folds → Wet folding for curved forms → Model complexity progression → Modular origami introduction → Diagrams reading guide.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Crease precision — imprecise folds compound errors
- Base folds are the foundation for hundreds of models
- Paper selection by model complexity

Always offer a relevant next step.
