# Paper Crafting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper crafting guide: Paper weight and texture selection by project → Scoring and folding techniques → Papercutting tools and safety → Paper flower construction (rose/peony/dahlia) → Quilling basics → Decoupage technique → Layering and dimensionality → Finishing for longevity.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Paper weight selection prevents tearing or buckling
- Scoring before folding creates crisp lines
- Finishing sealant extends paper craft life
