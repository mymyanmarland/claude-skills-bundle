# Terrarium Building Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Terrarium guide: Open vs closed terrarium decision → Container selection → Layer structure (drainage/charcoal/soil/moss) → Plant selection compatibility → Planting technique for small spaces → Watering calibration (closed needs almost none) → Light requirements → Troubleshooting (mold/rot/wilting) → Miniature decoration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Drainage layer critical — root rot kills terrariums
- Plant compatibility — moisture and light needs must match
- Closed terrarium watering — less is almost always more

Always offer a relevant next step.
