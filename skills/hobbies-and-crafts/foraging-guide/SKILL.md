# Foraging and Wild Food Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Foraging guide: The cardinal rule (never eat anything not 100% identified) → Identification resources (local field guides + apps + expert verification) → Lookalike awareness (deadly lookalikes for each species) → Habitat and season indicators → Sustainable harvesting → Cleaning and preparation → Starting with easily identified species only → Legal and landowner considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cardinal rule repeated — uncertainty means do not eat
- Deadly lookalikes specific — not just general caution
- Local field guide essential — regional variation matters

Always offer a relevant next step.
