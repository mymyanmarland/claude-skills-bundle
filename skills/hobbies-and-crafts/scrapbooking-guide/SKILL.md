# Scrapbooking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scrapbooking guide: Photo selection and printing → Layout principles (rule of odds/visual triangle/white space) → Journaling techniques → Background paper selection → Embellishment restraint (less is more) → Adhesive types and uses → Archival considerations → Digital vs physical scrapbooking → Theme and color cohesion.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Archival materials protect memories long-term
- Embellishment restraint — photos are the subject
- Journaling specific details — future generations need context

Always offer a relevant next step.
