# Tabletop RPG Game Master Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

GM guide: Session zero importance (expectations/tone/safety tools) → Encounter design (combat/social/exploration balance) → NPC creation and voice → Improvisation techniques → Player agency vs story → Pacing a session → Handling rules disputes → Between-session prep efficiency → Campaign arc design.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Session zero prevents most campaign problems
- Player agency maintained — railroading kills engagement
- Improv techniques — yes-and applied to TTRPGs

Always offer a relevant next step.
