# Card Making Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Card making guide: Cardstock weights and sizes → Stamping techniques (direct/heat embossing) → Ink types → Die cutting basics → Layering and dimension → Adhesive selection → Sentiment placement → Color theory in card making → Quick card sketch templates → Occasion-specific design guidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ink type matched to paper and embossing needs
- Sentiment placement — often where composition falls apart
- Dimension adds interest — foam adhesive use

Always offer a relevant next step.
