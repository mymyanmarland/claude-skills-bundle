# Home Canning Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Canning guide: Water bath vs pressure canning decision (acidity determines which) → Equipment inspection → Jar preparation and sterilization → Headspace measurement → Processing times by food and altitude → Seal testing → Storage and shelf life → What NOT to adjust in tested recipes → Common spoilage signs.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Water bath vs pressure canning — safety critical distinction
- Altitude adjustment required above 1000 feet
- Never adjust tested recipes — safety testing was specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
