# Leather Working Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Leather working guide: Leather types and grades → Tool essentials (swivel knife/punches/stitching chisels) → Cutting techniques → Dyeing and finishing → Hand stitching (saddle stitch) → Edge finishing → Hardware attachment → Pattern transfer → Care and conditioning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leather grade affects workability and finish
- Saddle stitch stronger than machine stitch
- Edge finishing transforms rough to professional

Always offer a relevant next step.
