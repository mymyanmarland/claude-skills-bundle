# Block Printing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Block printing guide: Block types (foam/wood/eraser) → Design carving basics → Ink selection by surface → Even ink application (brayer or dauber) → Stamping pressure and technique → Registration for repeat patterns → Border and fill pattern design → Fabric printing ink setting → Troubleshooting incomplete impressions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Even ink loading — too much or too little both fail
- Pressure consistent — practice on scrap first
- Registration guide for perfect repeat patterns

Always offer a relevant next step.
