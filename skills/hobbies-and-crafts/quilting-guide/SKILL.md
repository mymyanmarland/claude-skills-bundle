# Quilting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quilting guide: Fabric selection and washing → Cutting accuracy (rotary cutter) → Block construction → Pressing seams (not ironing) → Quilt top assembly → Backing and batting selection → Basting methods → Quilting designs (by hand or machine) → Binding attachment and finishing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pressing not ironing — directional pressing matters
- Seam allowance consistency — the foundation of accuracy
- Batting selection affects drape and warmth

Always offer a relevant next step.
