# Screen Printing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Screen printing guide: Screen preparation (mesh count selection) → Emulsion coating and exposure → Ink selection (fabric vs paper) → Registration marks → Squeegee technique → Multi-color printing → Cleanup protocol → Reclaiming screens → Heat setting fabric prints → Troubleshooting (bleeding/pin holes/coverage).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Emulsion exposure time varies — test strips required
- Squeegee angle and pressure consistent
- Heat setting required — without it fabric prints wash out

Always offer a relevant next step.
