# Furniture Upcycling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Furniture upcycling: Assessment (structural integrity first) → Stripping and sanding preparation → Primer selection (shellac for stains/knots) → Paint techniques (brush/roller/spray) → Chalk paint vs latex → Wax and varnish finishes → Hardware replacement → Decoupage and stenciling → Distressing techniques → Protecting the finish.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Structural repair before aesthetic work
- Shellac primer for stained or knotty wood — prevents bleed-through
- Wax application after chalk paint — protecting the finish

Always offer a relevant next step.
