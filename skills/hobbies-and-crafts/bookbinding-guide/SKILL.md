# Bookbinding Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bookbinding guide: Paper folding and grain direction → Signature creation → Pamphlet stitch (beginner) → Coptic stitch (exposed) → Perfect binding basics → Cover board and paper selection → Adhesive types (PVA vs paste) → Spine finishing → Text block alignment → Journal and notebook applications.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Grain direction prevents warping — test before cutting
- Signature size affects sewability
- PVA dilution ratio affects bond and flexibility

Always offer a relevant next step.
