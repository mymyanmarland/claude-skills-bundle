# Soap Making Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Soap making guide: Method selection (cold process/hot process/melt-and-pour) → Lye safety (cold process only — critical PPE) → Oil selection and properties → Superfat calculation → Fragrance usage rates → Colorant options → Mold selection → Cure time (4-6 weeks for CP) → Labeling requirements for sale.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lye safety non-negotiable — burns and dangerous fumes
- Cure time critical — uncured soap is caustic
- Superfat calculation using soap calculator — never guess

Always offer a relevant next step.
