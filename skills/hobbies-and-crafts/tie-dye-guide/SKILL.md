# Tie-Dye and Fabric Dyeing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tie-dye guide: Fabric preparation (prewash and soda ash soak) → Folding and tying techniques (spiral/accordion/scrunch) → Fiber reactive dye mixing → Application technique → Plastic wrap and cure time → Rinse and wash protocol → Ice dyeing variation → Discharge dyeing → Color mixing for custom colors.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soda ash soak required for fiber reactive dye
- Cure time minimum 8 hours — longer is better
- Rinse until clear before washing

Always offer a relevant next step.
