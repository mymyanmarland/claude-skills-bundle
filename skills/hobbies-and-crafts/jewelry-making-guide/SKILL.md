# Jewelry Making Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Jewelry making guide: Tool essentials by technique → Stringing and beading → Wire wrapping fundamentals → Jump ring techniques → Clasp attachment → Ear wire creation → Chain maille basics → Finishing and polishing → Material selection (metal/bead/gemstone) → Selling handmade jewelry basics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wire gauge selection matched to use
- Jump ring opening/closing technique — wrong way weakens the ring
- Finishing removes burrs that scratch skin

Always offer a relevant next step.
