# Cross Stitch Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cross stitch guide: Fabric selection (Aida count) → Thread preparation (separate strands) → Starting without a knot → Cross direction consistency → Following the chart → Color management → Parking threads for complex patterns → Finishing and framing → Pattern creation basics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cross direction consistent throughout — visual difference
- Starting without knot prevents bumps on back
- Parking threads prevents tangling in complex patterns

Always offer a relevant next step.
