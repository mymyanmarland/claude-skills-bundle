# Food Fermentation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fermentation guide: Fermentation safety basics (salt concentration/pH/anaerobic environment) → Equipment and vessel selection → Lacto-fermentation (vegetables) → Kombucha SCOBY care and brewing → Yogurt making → Troubleshooting (kahm yeast/mold identification) → pH testing → Flavoring and bottling.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Salt concentration for lacto-fermentation — safety critical
- Mold vs kahm yeast identification — important safety distinction
- Anaerobic environment maintenance prevents spoilage

Always offer a relevant next step.
