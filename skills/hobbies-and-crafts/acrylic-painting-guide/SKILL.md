# Acrylic Painting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Acrylic painting guide: Surface preparation (canvas/board/paper) → Brush selection → Palette setup and wet palette → Color mixing (avoid muddy color) → Layering and opaque vs transparent techniques → Dry brushing → Wet blending → Texture with palette knife → Varnishing → Common mistakes and fixes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wet palette keeps acrylics workable longer
- Layering dry before next coat — patience
- Color mixing — analogous mixing before complex mixing

Always offer a relevant next step.
