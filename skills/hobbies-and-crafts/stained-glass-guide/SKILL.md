# Stained Glass Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stained glass guide: Glass selection and color theory → Cutting technique (score and snap) → Pattern transfer → Copper foil vs lead came decision → Copper foil wrapping → Soldering technique → Patina application → Framing and hanging → Safety (glass cuts and lead exposure).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety prominent — glass and lead both require care
- Scoring technique before snapping
- Copper foil vs lead came for project type

Always offer a relevant next step.
