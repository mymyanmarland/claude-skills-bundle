# Wire Art and Sculpture Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wire art guide: Wire gauge selection (jewelry vs sculpture) → Tool essentials (round nose/flat nose/wire cutters) → Basic bending and shaping → Wrapping techniques → Coiling and weaving → Frame construction for sculpture → Finishing (oxidizing/polishing) → 3D form building → Common wire art forms.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wire gauge determines flexibility and strength
- Tool quality matters — cheap tools mark soft wire
- Wrapping direction consistent — aesthetic difference

Always offer a relevant next step.
