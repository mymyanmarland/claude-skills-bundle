# Bird Watching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bird watching guide: Essential equipment (binoculars selection/field guide) → The GISS method (General Impression of Shape and Size) → Field marks identification → Habitat and behavior clues → Life list keeping → Hotspot finding (eBird) → Seasonal and migration patterns → Photography for birding → Ethics and disturbance avoidance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- GISS before field marks — how experts identify birds
- eBird for finding locations and documenting sightings
- Disturbance ethics — bird welfare over the photo

Always offer a relevant next step.
