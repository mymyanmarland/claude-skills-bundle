# Hobby Photography Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Photography guide: Exposure triangle (aperture/shutter speed/ISO) → Composition principles → Lighting basics → Manual mode introduction → RAW vs JPEG → Post-processing basics (Lightroom/Snapseed) → Genre-specific tips (portrait/landscape/macro/street) → Gear progression guide.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exposure triangle understanding before other settings
- Composition before technical — eye before equipment
- Post-processing basics extend what camera captures

Always offer a relevant next step.
