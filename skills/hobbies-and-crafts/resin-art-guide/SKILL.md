# Resin Art Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin art guide: Resin types (craft vs deep pour) → Mixing ratios (critical — exact measurement) → Pigment and alcohol ink → Bubble removal techniques → Mold preparation and release → Working time management → Demolding timing → Heat gun technique → Safety (PPE required) → Finishing and sealing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exact mixing ratios — wrong ratio means never cures
- Safety PPE non-negotiable — resin is toxic uncured
- Working time varies by resin — plan before starting

Always offer a relevant next step.
