# Lino Printing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lino printing guide: Block selection (lino/sofcut/rubber) → Tool safety (always cut away from body) → Design transfer → Cutting depth and technique → Ink rolling → Test prints → Registration for multi-block → Editioning and signing → Cleanup and storage → Reduction printing introduction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tool safety — cutting toward fingers is always dangerous
- Test print reveals what needs adjusting before good paper
- Reduction printing understanding — irreversible process

Always offer a relevant next step.
