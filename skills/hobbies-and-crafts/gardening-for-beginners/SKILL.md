# Gardening for Beginners Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gardening guide: Soil testing and amendment → Sunlight assessment → Raised bed vs in-ground decision → Seed starting vs transplants → Watering principles (deep not frequent) → Fertilization basics → Pest identification and organic management → Companion planting basics → Harvest timing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soil quality determines everything — test before planting
- Deep infrequent watering vs frequent shallow — root development
- Companion planting for pest management

Always offer a relevant next step.
