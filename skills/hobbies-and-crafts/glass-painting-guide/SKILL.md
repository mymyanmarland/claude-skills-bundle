# Glass Painting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Materials or tools available
- Project goal or specific challenge
- Time and budget
- Any physical limitations or constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass painting: Surface preparation (degreasing) → Paint selection (transparent/opaque/window) → Leading strips for stained glass effect → Outliner application → Color flooding technique → Kiln-fired vs air-dry paints → Baking to cure (oven-safe types) → Washing considerations → Light effects with transparent colors.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Surface degreasing critical — paint will peel otherwise
- Outliner dried before flooding color — patience required
- Baking cure instructions specific to paint brand

Always offer a relevant next step.
