# LinkedIn Optimizer Skill

Transform a forgettable LinkedIn profile into one that attracts recruiters, clients, and opportunities.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

Ask in one message if not provided:
- **Current LinkedIn content** (paste headline, About, Experience — or describe what they have)
- **Target role or goal** (job search / personal brand / client attraction / thought leadership)
- **Industry and seniority level**
- **Top 3 skills or strengths** they want to be known for
- **Biggest career achievement** (with numbers)
- **Tone preference** (professional and formal / warm and approachable / bold and direct)

---

## Step 2 — Profile Section by Section

### 🏷️ Headline (220 characters max — most important field)

The headline appears everywhere: search results, connection requests, comments. Most people waste it with just their job title.

**Formula**:
`[Current Role] | [What you help with / key skill] | [Industry/niche or unique value]`

Or for job seekers:
`[Target Role] | [Top skill] | [What you bring]`

❌ Weak: *"Marketing Manager at ABC Corp"*
✅ Strong: *"Marketing Manager | Scaling B2B SaaS brands from 0→1M users | Content & Demand Gen"*

❌ Weak: *"Looking for new opportunities"*
✅ Strong: *"Senior Software Engineer | React & Node.js | Building products that reach millions"*

**Output 3 headline options** and let user choose.

---

### 📸 Profile Photo & Banner (quick advice)
- Photo: Professional, smiling, good lighting, face takes up 60% of frame
- Banner: Use it — most people don't. Add your tagline, website, or a relevant visual.

---

### 📝 About Section (2,600 chars max — first 3 lines must hook)

LinkedIn shows only the first 2–3 lines before "see more" — make them count.

**Structure**:

```
[HOOK LINE — bold claim, surprising stat, or direct value statement]

[WHO YOU ARE — 1–2 sentences: role, industry, years of experience]

[WHAT YOU DO — the specific problems you solve or results you create]

[HOW YOU DO IT — your approach, methodology, or differentiator]

[PROOF — 2–3 quantified achievements]

[HUMAN ELEMENT — brief personal note: what drives you, passion, outside work]

[CALL TO ACTION — what should the reader do next?]
→ Open to [opportunities / connecting / collaborating]
→ DM me about [specific topic]
→ Visit [website] for [resource]
```

**Tone rules**:
- Write in first person ("I built", "I help") — LinkedIn is personal, not corporate
- Short paragraphs — 2–3 lines max, then white space
- No jargon unless your audience expects it
- Keywords woven naturally (recruiters search for these)

---

### 💼 Experience Section

Each role should have:
```
[JOB TITLE] — [Company Name]
[Date – Date] | [Location or Remote]

[2–3 line company description if not well-known]

• [Achievement with metric — what you did and the result]
• [Achievement with metric]
• [Achievement with metric]
• [Key skill or tool used in this role — for keyword visibility]
```

LinkedIn-specific tips:
- Add media (PDFs, links, presentations) to each role — makes profile 10× richer
- Pin your top 3 posts/articles under Featured section
- First role (current) gets most detail; older roles can be 2–3 bullets

---

### 🛠️ Skills Section

LinkedIn lets you list 50 skills — use them strategically:

**Tier 1 (Top 3 — these show prominently)**:
→ Your most marketable, most searched skills

**Tier 2 (Next 7)**:
→ Supporting technical skills relevant to target role

**Tier 3 (Remaining)**:
→ Adjacent skills, tools, soft skills

**Endorsement tip**: Endorse connections in your field — many will reciprocate.

---

### 🎓 Additional Sections to Add (if missing)

- **Certifications**: Every cert adds keyword density and credibility
- **Recommendations**: 3+ is the magic number — request from managers, clients, peers
- **Featured**: Pin your best content, case study, or media mention
- **Volunteer**: Signals character; often a conversation starter
- **Open to Work**: Use "Share with recruiters only" if currently employed

## Step 4 — LinkedIn Algorithm Tips

```
## 📈 Visibility Boosters

**Profile completeness** (aim for "All-Star"):
- [ ] Profile photo ✅
- [ ] Background banner ✅
- [ ] Headline ✅
- [ ] Location ✅
- [ ] Current position with description ✅
- [ ] Education ✅
- [ ] 5+ skills ✅
- [ ] 50+ connections ✅

**Ongoing activity** (signals active user — shown more in search):
- Post or comment 3–5x per week
- Engage with others' posts within first hour of posting
- Use 3–5 relevant hashtags on posts (not 20)
- Long-form posts outperform short ones for reach
- Native video gets 5× more reach than link posts

**Recruiter search optimization**:
- Use exact job title keywords recruiters search for
- Include city name even if remote (recruiters filter by location)
- Turn on "Open to Work" (recruiter-only mode doesn't show green banner)
```

Always offer: "Want me to write a first LinkedIn post to go with this profile refresh?" or "Want recommendations on who to connect with in your industry?"
