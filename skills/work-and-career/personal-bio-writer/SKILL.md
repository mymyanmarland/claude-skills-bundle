# Personal Bio Writer Skill

Write bios that sound genuinely human, memorable, and right for the context.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

Ask in one message if not provided:
- **Context/platform**: LinkedIn / Speaker bio / Portfolio / Instagram / Dating app / Press kit / Conference program / Personal website / Twitter/X
- **Length needed**: One-liner (under 160 chars) / Short (50–100 words) / Medium (100–200 words) / Long (200–400 words)
- **Career/professional background**: Role, industry, years of experience, key achievements
- **Personal highlights**: Unique skills, side projects, passions, life experiences that shaped them
- **Personality**: How do they want to come across? (Authoritative / Warm / Witty / Humble / Bold)
- **Audience**: Who will read this bio?
- **One thing that makes them different**: What sets them apart from everyone else with their job title?

---

## Step 2 — Bio Formulas by Length

### ⚡ One-Liner Bio (under 160 chars — Instagram, Twitter, email signature)

**Formula**: [Role] + [what makes you different or interesting] + [optional: human detail]

```
Examples by tone:

Professional: "Product designer at [Co]. I make complex things feel simple. Previously IDEO + Google."

Warm: "Pediatric nurse & boy mum. Helping families navigate the scary bits of hospitals — one family at a time."

Witty: "Software engineer by day. Sourdough scientist by night. My code compiles. My bread doesn't always."

Personal brand: "Teaching [audience] how to [outcome] without [common pain point]. [X]K students. Start here →"
```

---

### 📝 Short Bio (50–100 words — speaker intro, portfolio header, press kit)

**Formula**: Who you are → What you do → Who you do it for → What makes you credible → Human element

```
[NAME] is a [role/title] who [what they do — specific, not generic].

[One sentence on their background — most relevant credential or achievement, with a number if possible.]

[One sentence on their approach, philosophy, or what makes them distinctive.]

[Optional: one human/personal line — where they're based, what they care about outside work.]
```

**Example**:
*"Maria Chen is a UX researcher who turns user frustration into product breakthroughs. With 12 years at consumer tech companies including Spotify and Airbnb, she's led research that shaped products used by over 200 million people. Maria believes the best insights come from listening to what users don't say. She's based in Amsterdam and is currently writing a book on inclusive design."*

---

### 📄 Medium Bio (100–200 words — conference program, website About page, LinkedIn summary)

**Formula**: Hook → Professional story → Key achievements → Unique angle → Current focus → Personal close

```
[HOOK — a surprising fact, bold claim, or the core of what they do in one sentence]

[Career narrative — 2–3 sentences tracing the journey to where they are now. Not a resume — a story.]

[2–3 specific achievements with numbers or recognizable names.]

[What makes their approach or perspective unique — what do they believe that others don't?]

[Current focus — what are they working on or excited about now?]

[Personal close — 1 line that humanizes: family, passion project, hobby, location.]
```

---

### 📰 Long Bio (200–400 words — press kit, book jacket, keynote speaker page)

**Formula**: Headline → Origin story → Career arc → Major achievements → Philosophy/mission → Recognition → Personal

```
[HEADLINE: One sentence that captures the essence of who they are and why they matter]

[ORIGIN: Where did this journey begin? What's the compelling backstory? 1–2 sentences.]

[CAREER ARC: The progression — not every job, but the narrative thread connecting them. 2–3 sentences.]

[ACHIEVEMENTS: 3–4 specific, quantified, or named accomplishments. Numbers, company names, awards.]

[PHILOSOPHY: What do they believe? What problem are they solving? What's the "why"? 2–3 sentences.]

[RECOGNITION: Awards, media features, speaking stages, publications — 1–2 sentences.]

[PERSONAL: Where they live, family, hobbies — the human element that makes them real. 1–2 sentences.]
```

## Step 3 — Context-Specific Bio Guides

### 💼 LinkedIn Summary
```
**LinkedIn bio rules**:
- First 3 lines must hook (only these show before "see more")
- First person ("I help..." not "Jane helps...")
- Tell a story — not a list of job duties
- End with a CTA or what you're open to
- Keywords matter for search (include your job title, skills)

**Structure**:
What I do + who I help + the result I create (line 1–3 — the hook)

The journey that got me here (2–3 sentences)

What I'm working on / believe in / known for (2–3 sentences)

What I'm looking for / open to (1 sentence)

How to reach me (optional close)
```

### 🎤 Speaker Bio
```
**Speaker bio rules**:
- Always third person
- Establish credibility in first sentence (why should this audience listen?)
- Tie credentials directly to the talk topic
- Include specific numbers (audiences of X, X years, X companies)
- End with 1 human detail — audiences connect with people, not resumes

**Template**:
"[Name] is [title/role] and [what makes them the right person for this topic].

[Key credential 1 with number or name]. [Key credential 2].

[Their core belief or unique perspective on the topic].

[Human close — where they're based or what they do outside work]."
```

### 📱 Dating App Bio
```
**Dating app bio principles**:
- Be specific — "I love hiking" < "I drag people up mountains and convince them it was worth it"
- Show personality, not just resume
- Humor goes a long way if it's genuinely you
- Include one conversation starter (something they can reply to)
- Be honest about what you're looking for

**Structure** (3–4 sentences max):
[Who you are in normal life — job mentioned lightly or not at all]
[One specific interest shown vividly, not listed]
[Something that makes you different or a light joke]
[What you're looking for / a question to invite a reply]
```

## Step 4 — Full Bio Output

Generate 2–3 versions:

```
## 📝 Your Personal Bio: [Context]

---

**Version A — [Tone, e.g., "Professional & Authoritative"]** ([word count]):
[Full bio]

---

**Version B — [Tone, e.g., "Warm & Approachable"]** ([word count]):
[Full bio]

---

**Version C — Short version** (for space-constrained contexts, [word count]):
[Full bio]

---

**Recommended for [context]**: Version [X] — [One sentence explanation]
```

## Step 5 — Bio Quality Checklist

```
## ✅ Bio Review Checklist

- [ ] Doesn't start with "I am" or your name (boring openers)
- [ ] No clichés: "passionate", "dynamic", "results-driven", "thought leader", "guru"
- [ ] At least one specific number or named credential
- [ ] Tone matches the platform (casual for Instagram, formal for press kit)
- [ ] Includes something human and memorable — not just job duties
- [ ] Appropriate length for the context
- [ ] Third person for professional contexts (speaker, press); first person for personal (LinkedIn, social)
- [ ] Has a natural ending — doesn't just stop
- [ ] Read aloud — does it sound like a real person talking?
```

Always offer: "Want a matching headline or tagline to go with this bio?" or "Want versions in both first and third person?"
