# Freelance Proposal Writer Skill

Write proposals that win projects and protect you from scope creep.

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

Ask in one message if not provided:
- **Your service** (web design, copywriting, video editing, consulting, development, etc.)
- **Client name and project description** (what do they want done?)
- **Project scope** (what's IN — and importantly, what's NOT IN)
- **Your pricing model** (fixed price / hourly / retainer / milestone-based)
- **Your rate or target total** (what do you want to charge?)
- **Timeline** (when can you start, when will it be done?)
- **Your name / business name**

---

## Step 2 — Full Proposal Structure

```
## 📄 Project Proposal
**Prepared for**: [Client Name / Company]
**Prepared by**: [Your Name / Business]
**Date**: [Date]
**Valid until**: [Date — 14–30 days from issue]

---

### 1. Understanding Your Needs

[Show the client you understood their problem — paraphrase what they told you in your own words. This builds trust and catches any misunderstandings early.]

[Company/Client] is looking to [core goal]. Specifically, you need [specific deliverable or outcome] in order to [business reason — why this matters to them].

---

### 2. Proposed Solution

[Describe YOUR approach — not just what you'll do, but how and why your approach is the right one.]

I propose to [high-level description of your approach]. This approach is ideal because [reason — your experience, methodology, or why it fits their specific situation].

---

### 3. Scope of Work

**What's included**:
- [ ] [Deliverable 1 — be specific: "5 blog posts, 800–1000 words each, SEO-optimized"]
- [ ] [Deliverable 2 — "3 rounds of revisions per deliverable"]
- [ ] [Deliverable 3]
- [ ] [Deliverable 4]

**What's NOT included** (critical — prevents scope creep):
- [Item out of scope 1 — e.g., "Social media management beyond the content deliverables"]
- [Item out of scope 2 — e.g., "Paid ad campaigns"]
- [Item out of scope 3 — e.g., "Translations into other languages"]

*Any work outside this scope will be quoted separately and agreed upon in writing before proceeding.*

---

### 4. Timeline & Milestones

| Milestone | Deliverable | Due Date |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Project kickoff | Discovery call + brief finalized | [Date] |
| [Milestone 1] | [Deliverable] | [Date] |
| [Milestone 2] | [Deliverable] | [Date] |
| [Milestone 3] | [Deliverable] | [Date] |
| Final delivery | [Final output] | [Date] |

**Note**: Timeline assumes client feedback provided within [X] business days of each delivery. Delays in feedback will shift subsequent deadlines accordingly.

---

### 5. Investment

**Option A — [Recommended]**: [Descriptive name]
[Description of what's included]
**Total**: $[Amount]

**Option B — [Alternative]**: [Name]
[Smaller scope or different structure]
**Total**: $[Amount]

*[Offer 2 options when possible — anchors the client and gives them agency without being confusing]*

**Payment Schedule**:
- [50]% due upon project kickoff: $[Amount]
- [25]% due upon [milestone]: $[Amount]
- [25]% due upon final delivery: $[Amount]

**Payment method**: [Bank transfer / PayPal / Stripe / etc.]

---

### 6. What I Need From You

To get started, I'll need:
- [ ] [Access, asset, or information 1 — e.g., "Brand guidelines and logo files"]
- [ ] [Item 2 — e.g., "Access to your CMS/website"]
- [ ] [Item 3 — e.g., "List of target keywords you're focused on"]
- [ ] Signed agreement and initial payment

---

### 7. About Me

[2–3 sentence bio: your relevant experience, specialization, and why you're the right person for this project. Include 1 quantified result if possible.]

**Relevant work**:
- [Portfolio link or specific example]
- [Testimonial quote if you have one]

---

### 8. Terms & Conditions

**Revisions**: This proposal includes [X] rounds of revisions per deliverable. Additional revisions are billed at $[rate]/hour.

**Intellectual Property**: Upon receipt of full payment, all rights to final deliverables transfer to [Client]. Work-in-progress materials remain the property of [Your Name] until final payment is received.

**Cancellation**: If the client cancels after kickoff, the deposit is non-refundable. Work completed to date will be invoiced at the agreed rate.

**Confidentiality**: Both parties agree to keep project details confidential unless otherwise agreed in writing.

**Governing Law**: This agreement is governed by the laws of [Your State/Country].

---

### 9. Next Steps

If you'd like to move forward:
1. Review and sign this proposal
2. Submit the initial payment of $[amount]
3. We'll schedule a kickoff call for [date range]

I'm available to answer any questions at [email] or [phone].

Looking forward to working together.

[Your Name]
[Business Name]
[Email | Phone | Website]
```

## Step 3 — Pricing Strategy Guide

```
## 💰 Pricing Your Work

### Value-based pricing (recommended)
Price based on the VALUE to the client, not your time.
"This website will generate X leads/month" → charge based on that outcome, not your hours.

### Hourly rate calculation (baseline)
Target annual income ÷ billable hours = minimum hourly rate
Example: $60,000/year ÷ 1,000 billable hours = $60/hr minimum
Add 30% for taxes, benefits, downtime = $78/hr true minimum

### Fixed project pricing
Estimate hours → multiply by hourly rate → add 20% buffer for unknowns
Never quote a fixed price without a clear scope — always define what's NOT included.

### Retainer pricing
For ongoing work. Offer a discount (10–15%) in exchange for commitment.
Monthly retainer = X hours × hourly rate × 0.85

### Red flags to price for (add contingency):
- Vague brief ("we'll figure it out as we go")
- Multiple decision makers / committees
- Client who has already fired another freelancer
- "Just a quick project" (never is)
- Tight deadline (rush fee: +25–50%)
```

## Step 4 — Proposal Best Practices

```
## ✅ Proposal Quality Check

- [ ] Client name spelled correctly throughout
- [ ] Scope is specific — no vague phrases like "as needed" or "miscellaneous"
- [ ] Out-of-scope section is explicit
- [ ] Payment terms include upfront deposit (never start without one)
- [ ] Timeline includes buffer for feedback delays
- [ ] Revision rounds are limited and specified
- [ ] Two pricing options included (anchoring)
- [ ] Valid-until date creates urgency
- [ ] Clear next steps at the end
- [ ] Proofread — typos in a proposal signal sloppiness in the work
```

Always offer: "Want me to write a follow-up email to send with this proposal?" or "Want help creating a shorter 1-page version for smaller projects?"
