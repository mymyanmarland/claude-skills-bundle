# Persuasive Essay Writer Skill
## Step 1 — Gather Inputs
- **Topic and position**: (what are they arguing for/against?)
- **Audience**: Academic / General public / Specific industry / Decision-makers
- **Word count**: 500 / 800 / 1200 / 2000+ words
- **Format**: Academic essay / Op-ed / Opinion column / Blog post
- **Evidence available**: (facts, studies, examples they want to include)
- **Tone**: Formal / Accessible / Passionate / Measured

## Step 2 — Persuasive Essay Structure
```
## Persuasive Essay: [Topic]
Thesis: [The specific, arguable claim — one sentence]

---

### INTRODUCTION (10% of length)
[Hook: a compelling opening — story, statistic, or provocative question]
[Context: brief background the reader needs]
[Thesis statement: the explicit claim, clearly stated]
[Roadmap: preview of the argument's structure]

### BODY PARAGRAPH 1 — STRONGEST ARGUMENT
Topic sentence: [Claim for this paragraph]
Evidence: [Specific support — cite if academic]
Analysis: [Why this evidence proves the claim]
Transition: [Bridge to next argument]

### BODY PARAGRAPH 2 — SECOND ARGUMENT
[Same structure]

### BODY PARAGRAPH 3 — THIRD ARGUMENT
[Same structure]

### COUNTERARGUMENT & REBUTTAL
[Acknowledge the strongest opposing view — fairly]
[Refute it: show why it doesn't undermine the thesis]
[This shows intellectual honesty and strengthens credibility]

### CONCLUSION (10% of length)
[Restate thesis — in new words, not copy-paste]
[Synthesize arguments — don't just summarize, show how they connect]
[Call to action or broader implication: why this matters beyond the essay]
[Memorable closing line]
```

## Step 3 — Persuasion Techniques
- **Ethos**: Establish credibility — cite experts, acknowledge complexity
- **Pathos**: Emotional resonance — a human story that makes abstract stakes concrete
- **Logos**: Logic and evidence — data, cause-effect, historical precedent
- **Concession**: Acknowledge what the opposition gets right — makes you more credible

Always offer: "Want me to find specific statistics and examples to strengthen the weakest argument?"
