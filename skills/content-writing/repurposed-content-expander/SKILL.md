# Repurposed Content Expander Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Source content to expand
- Target length
- Platform/format (blog/LinkedIn/newsletter)
- Tone
- Key point not to lose

## Step 2 — Core Output

Take the provided source and expand each idea with: context setting, a concrete example or anecdote, the implication for the reader, and a transition to the next point. The expansion should feel like the original author wrote more, not like a different voice. Maintain the original's vocabulary and sentence rhythm. Add a proper introduction that hooks the reader and a conclusion that synthesises the expanded points.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Voice must match the source material
- No new claims not implied in the original
- Expansion ratio roughly 4-8× the original length

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
