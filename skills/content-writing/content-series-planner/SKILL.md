# Content Series Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Series theme
- Platform and format
- Frequency and length
- Audience goal
- Total pieces planned

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver: Series concept statement → Naming convention → Individual piece outlines (title, hook, 3 key points, CTA per piece) → How each piece connects to the next → Arc of the series (beginning/middle/end even for ongoing series) → Promotion strategy per piece → Repurposing plan for series end.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each piece works standalone AND as series part
- Arc creates genuine progression — not just topics
- Series has a clear end or renewal point

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
