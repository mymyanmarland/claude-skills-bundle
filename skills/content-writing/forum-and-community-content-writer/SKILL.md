# Forum and Community Content Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Platform and community
- Question or topic
- Brand/person being represented
- Level of self-promotion allowed
- Expertise to demonstrate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Write 3 versions: (1) Direct helpful answer — no mention of brand. (2) Helpful answer with subtle brand context. (3) Full brand story integrated naturally. Each version: community tone matched, expertise demonstrated through specifics, no corporate voice. Include note on appropriate self-promotional level per platform (Reddit is low, Quora medium).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Answer helps even without the brand mention
- Matches community tone exactly — no corporate speak
- Community link or mention natural, not forced

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
