# Thought Leadership Piece Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Author's expertise and credentials
- The core argument or unique perspective
- Target publication or platform
- Industry context / what conventional wisdom says
- Supporting evidence or personal experience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Bold opening claim → Evidence that conventional wisdom is wrong or incomplete → The new framework or perspective the author offers → Real-world proof or case study → Implications for the reader's industry → Call to engage. Avoid hedging language. Use 'I' confidently. Every paragraph should either make a claim, provide evidence, or draw a conclusion. No generic observations — every insight must be specific and earned.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening must stake a clear position in the first sentence
- At least one contrarian or surprising claim
- Ends with a forward-looking implication, not a summary

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
