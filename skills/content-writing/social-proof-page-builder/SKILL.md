# Social Proof Page Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Available testimonials
- Types of proof: reviews/stats/logos/case studies
- Industry
- Page goal: trust/conversion/awareness

## Step 2 — Core Output

Page copy: Intro headline (results-focused) + Stats bar (3 impressive numbers) + Featured testimonial (expanded, 100 words) + Grid testimonials (6-8, 30-50 words each) + Client logos section copy + Results statistics section + Video testimonial placeholder copy. Each testimonial formatted: quote → attribution → result.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every testimonial includes a specific result
- Stats are the most impressive available
- Section headers benefit-focused not descriptive

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
