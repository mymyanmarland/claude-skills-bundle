# Technical Explainer Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Technical content to explain
- Target reader knowledge level
- Analogy preferences
- Length
- Where it will appear

## Step 2 — Core Output

Explanation structure: Real-world analogy (establish before technical) → What it is (plain language definition) → How it works (process in 3-5 steps) → Why it matters (impact on the reader) → Common misconceptions (2-3). Use second person. Avoid jargon or define every term. Analogies specific to the audience context.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every technical term defined or replaced
- Analogy is accurate — not misleading
- Reader doesn't need to Google anything after reading

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
