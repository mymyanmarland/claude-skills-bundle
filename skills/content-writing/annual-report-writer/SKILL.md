# Annual Report Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Key achievements and milestones of the year
- Financial highlights (summary only)
- Challenges faced and how addressed
- Company mission and values
- Message from leadership

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sections: CEO/Executive letter (400 words — personal, reflective, forward-looking) → Year in review summary (bullet highlights with brief context) → Mission impact section (how work connected to mission) → Community/stakeholder section → Looking ahead (priorities for next year). Write in formal but human tone. Avoid jargon. Every section connects activities to mission. Balance accomplishments with honest acknowledgment of challenges.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- CEO letter reads as from a real person, not a committee
- Financial references are summary only — no detailed figures in narrative
- Forward-looking section is specific, not aspirational clichés

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
