# Product Naming Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product description and category
- Target market
- Brand personality (3 adjectives)
- Competitor product names to avoid
- Domain/trademark importance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Generate 20 name options across 5 categories: (1) Descriptive (say what it does), (2) Abstract (feeling or association), (3) Made-up word (portmanteau or invented), (4) Metaphor (comparison to something else), (5) Founder/personal (human-centered). For top 5 candidates: meaning/rationale, memorability score (1-10), pronunciation test, domain note (.com likely available/taken), brand fit assessment. Provide final recommendation with reasoning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- At least one name in each of the 5 categories
- All names under 3 syllables for primary recommendation
- Domain availability flagged for all finalists

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
