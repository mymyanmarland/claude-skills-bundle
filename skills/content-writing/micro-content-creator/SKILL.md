# Micro Content Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic or message
- Platform
- Tone
- CTA if any

## Step 2 — Core Output

Generate 10 micro-content pieces. Each under 280 chars. Formats: standalone tweet, thread hook, SMS blast, push notification, Instagram caption. Each piece optimized for its format. Include word count. Highlight top 3.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every piece works without context
- Hook in first 5 words
- No filler words

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
