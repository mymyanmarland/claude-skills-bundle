# Product Review Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product name and specs
- Personal experience or research
- Pros and cons
- Target buyer
- Affiliate context if any

## Step 2 — Core Output

Review structure: Score box (out of 10, 3 sub-scores) → Who it's for (2 sentences) → Hands-on summary → Pros (5 bullet points with reasoning) → Cons (3 genuine drawbacks) → Comparison to alternative → Verdict. 600-900 words. Genuinely balanced — 1-2 real negatives required.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cons must be real — not trivial
- Recommendation specific to buyer type
- Score justified with evidence

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
