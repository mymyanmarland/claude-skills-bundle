# Long-Form Article Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic and target keyword
- Target audience and knowledge level
- Desired word count
- Tone: educational / authoritative / conversational
- Any sources or data to include

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Compelling headline → Hook intro with stat or question → H2 sections each covering one angle → Data/examples per section → Summary with key takeaways → CTA. Every H2 should address a specific sub-question the reader has. Use short paragraphs (2-3 sentences), transitional phrases between sections, and active voice throughout. Include a meta description and 3 alt headline variations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every claim backed by an example, stat, or analogy
- No section shorter than 150 words or longer than 500
- CTA aligns with the article's goal (subscribe, download, buy, share)

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
