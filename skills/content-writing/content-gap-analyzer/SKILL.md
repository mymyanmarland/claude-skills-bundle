# Content Gap Analyzer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Your content topics (list)
- Competitor URLs or topics
- Target audience and their top questions
- Your content goals
- Industry or niche

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver: Gap matrix (topics competitors cover that you don't) + Audience question analysis (questions your audience asks that aren't answered) + Priority ranking (high/medium/low based on search volume signals and audience relevance) + Quick-win opportunities (gaps you can fill in one piece) + Content type gaps (are you missing video, guides, case studies?). For each gap: suggested title, estimated search intent, content type recommendation, and priority score.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- At least 10 specific gap topics identified
- Each gap includes a concrete content recommendation
- Priority scores justified with reasoning

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
