# Op-Ed Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The argument or position to argue
- Author's credentials on this topic
- Target publication and their guidelines
- Word count (typically 600-1000)
- Supporting evidence available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Op-ed structure: Lede (the specific moment or event that makes this timely) → Nut graph (the argument in 1-2 sentences) → Evidence block 1 (strongest proof) → Complication or counterpoint (show fairness) → Rebuttal → Evidence block 2 → Call to action or implication. 600-800 words. Every paragraph advances the argument — no filler. Active voice throughout. No hedging — take a real position. End with what should happen or what will happen.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Position stated by paragraph 2 at the latest
- Strongest evidence leads — don't build to it
- No 'I believe' or 'In my opinion' — state it as fact then back it up

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
