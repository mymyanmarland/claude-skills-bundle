# Ebook Chapter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Chapter title and goal
- Target reader and their level
- Key points the chapter must cover
- Previous chapter context
- Voice and tone of the ebook

## Step 2 — Core Output

Chapter structure: Opening hook or story → Chapter promise (what the reader will gain) → Main content broken into 3-5 sections → Each section: concept explanation + example + application → Chapter summary (3 bullet takeaways) → Chapter exercise or action step → Bridge sentence to next chapter. 1500-3000 words per chapter. Use second person ('you'). One key idea per section. Include pull quotes (bold, standalone key lines).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening must not start with a definition or overview
- Every concept includes a practical application
- Chapter can be read standalone — context provided where needed

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
