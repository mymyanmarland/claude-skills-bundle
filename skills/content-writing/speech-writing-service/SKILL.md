# Speech Writing Service Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Occasion and audience
- Speaker's relationship to the topic
- Key message to leave with audience
- Length in minutes
- Tone: inspiring / informative / celebratory / somber

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Attention-grabbing opening (story, startling fact, or question) → Speaker establishment (why you) → Core message statement → 3 supporting points each with a story or example → Emotional resonance section → Call to action or aspiration → Memorable closing line. Written for speaking, not reading. Short sentences. Rhythm and pauses marked. Includes delivery notes in [brackets]. Estimated speaking time included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening must not be a thank-you
- Every abstract point illustrated with a specific story
- Closing line is memorable enough to be quoted

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
