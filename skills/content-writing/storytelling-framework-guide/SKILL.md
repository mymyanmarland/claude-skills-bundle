# Storytelling Framework Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Content to transform
- Framework preference or open to suggestion
- Audience
- Desired emotional outcome
- Length of final content

## Step 2 — Core Output

Framework selection with reasoning → Mapped content to the framework stages → Rewritten content following the structure → Explanation of how each stage was applied. Include alternative framework option. Show before/after comparison for the strongest section. Note where the original content was already strong vs where the framework adds most value.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Framework applied fully — no stages skipped
- Emotional arc clear in the rewritten version
- Explanation teaches, not just applies

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
