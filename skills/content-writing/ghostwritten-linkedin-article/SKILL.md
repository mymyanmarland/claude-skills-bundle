# Ghostwritten LinkedIn Article Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Author's role and company
- The topic or experience to write about
- Key insight or lesson to share
- Tone samples (existing posts or preferred style)
- Target audience (peers / prospects / talent)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Provocative or story-opening headline → Personal anecdote or specific situation (200 words) → The insight extracted from that experience → 3 principles or lessons with brief explanation each → The broader implication for the reader's work → Direct question CTA. LinkedIn articles perform best at 800-1200 words. Use first person consistently. Include 2-3 subheadings. Keep paragraphs to 2 lines max. End with a genuine question that invites comments.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Must sound like a specific person, not generic LinkedIn voice
- Opening line cannot start with 'I' or 'Today'
- No bullet point lists — everything in flowing prose

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
