# Repurposed Video Transcript Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Raw transcript text
- Original video length and topic
- Target article length
- Tone adjustment needed
- SEO keyword to optimize for

## Step 2 — Core Output

Transformation process: Remove filler words, false starts, and repetitions → Restructure from spoken to written flow → Add subheadings where topic shifts → Expand key points that were brief verbally → Add transitions between sections → Write a proper introduction (transcripts typically start mid-thought) → Write a conclusion. Maintain the speaker's personality and examples. SEO-optimize the structure without losing the natural voice. Flag any sections that were unclear in the transcript.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Final article should not read like a transcript
- Speaker's key examples and stories preserved
- Reading time should be 1/3 of original video length

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
