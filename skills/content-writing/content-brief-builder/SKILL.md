# Content Brief Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target keyword and search intent
- Competitor URLs to analyze
- Word count target
- Brand voice guidelines
- Key points that must be covered

## Step 2 — Core Output

Brief format: Target keyword + secondary keywords → Search intent classification → Recommended title and H1 → Outline with H2/H3 structure → Word count per section → Tone and voice notes → Key statistics or data to include → Internal and external link suggestions → Meta title and description → Competitor differentiation notes (what to do better) → Images/media suggestions. Make each section directive — tell the writer exactly what to write, not just the topic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every H2 maps to a distinct user question
- Competitor gaps explicitly called out
- Brief is usable without any additional briefing from the client

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
