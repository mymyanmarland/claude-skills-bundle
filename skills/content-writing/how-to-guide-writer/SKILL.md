# How-To Guide Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The process or skill to explain
- Reader experience level
- Format: article/PDF/video script
- Available tools or prerequisites
- Common mistakes to address

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Brief intro (what they'll achieve + time required) → Prerequisites list → Numbered steps (each with: action, why it matters, tip or common mistake) → Troubleshooting section (top 3 problems and solutions) → Next steps or advanced variations. Each step should be a single, discrete action. Use imperative verbs. Include visual cue notes where helpful (e.g., 'You'll see a green checkmark'). Keep each step to 40-80 words.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Steps must be sequential — cannot do step 3 without step 2
- Every step has exactly one primary action
- Troubleshooting addresses the most Googled problems for this topic

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
