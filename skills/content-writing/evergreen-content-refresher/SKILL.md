# Evergreen Content Refresher Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Original article content
- What has changed since publication
- Target keyword to maintain/improve
- What competitors now cover
- Target audience — has it shifted

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver: (1) Audit notes — sections that are outdated, weak, or missing. (2) Updated introduction (hooks are often the weakest part of older content). (3) Refreshed statistics section with current date placeholders. (4) New section suggestions to add. (5) Sections to cut or condense. (6) Updated meta title and description. (7) Suggested internal links to newer content. Flag which changes will most impact rankings vs readability.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Publication date updated clearly
- No outdated statistics or references left in
- Word count maintained or increased — never decreased significantly

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
