# Cornerstone Content Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic to own
- Target keyword cluster
- Current domain authority
- Competitor cornerstone pieces
- Content resources available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Plan: Title (definitive guide format) → Outline (table of contents — 8-12 major sections) → Word count recommendation → Internal linking strategy (what to link to and from) → External link targets (high-authority sources) → Rich content elements (infographics, tables, tools) → Update schedule → Promotional strategy for initial traction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Outline answers every major question on the topic
- Designed to earn featured snippets
- Update schedule prevents content decay

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
