# Native Ad Copy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- Target publication or platform
- Reader profile of that publication
- Click goal vs awareness goal
- Product benefit to feature

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver 5 native ad sets each with: Headline (editorial tone, not ad tone), Subheadline, Image direction, 80-word body copy, CTA. Headlines must feel like editorial content of the target publication. Body copy leads with reader benefit. Brand mention subtle until final sentence. Compliance note: disclosure language suggestion included. A/B test pairs: 2 headlines per ad set with different approaches (curiosity vs benefit vs story).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Headline passes the 'would a journalist write this' test
- Brand name appears no more than twice in body
- Disclosure language included and FTC-compliant

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
