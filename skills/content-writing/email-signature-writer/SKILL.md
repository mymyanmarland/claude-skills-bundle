# Email Signature Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Name and title
- Company
- Top credential
- One CTA goal
- Tone: formal/friendly

## Step 2 — Core Output

3 signature options: (1) Minimal — name, title, one link. (2) Professional — name, title, company, phone, LinkedIn, one CTA. (3) Full — all above plus credential, quote, social links. CTA should be specific and time-sensitive where possible.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- CTA is one action only
- No more than 5 lines in professional version
- Mobile-readable

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
