# White Paper Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic and core argument
- Target reader (role, industry, knowledge level)
- Key research or data available
- Desired length (2000-8000 words)
- Call to action and lead goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Executive summary (250 words) → Problem definition with industry context → Current approaches and their limitations → Your solution or framework → Evidence and case studies → Implementation considerations → Conclusion and next steps. Use professional but readable language. Define technical terms. Include a table of contents. Recommend at least 3 custom graphics/charts. Footnotes for all statistics. End with a specific offer or next step that converts the reader.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Executive summary must stand alone — busy executives read only this
- Every claim cited or attributed
- Avoids promotional language until the final CTA section

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
