# Podcast Script Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Episode topic and key message
- Guest (if any) or solo format
- Episode length target
- Show's tone and audience
- Key questions or points to cover

## Step 2 — Core Output

Script sections: Pre-roll teaser (30 seconds — the most compelling moment from later in the episode) → Intro music cue → Host intro + episode overview → Segment 1 (main topic intro) → Deep dive segments (2-4 topics with transition phrases) → Guest Q&A if applicable (5-7 questions with follow-up prompts) → Listener action item → Outro + subscribe/review CTA. Include stage directions in [brackets]. Mark natural pause points. Provide speaking time estimate per section.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Teaser is the most compelling 30 seconds — not a summary
- Each transition creates anticipation for the next segment
- CTA is specific and single — not three different asks

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
