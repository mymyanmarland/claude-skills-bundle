# Press Kit Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company or personal brand description
- Key news or story hook
- Target media (local news/industry/national)
- Available assets (photos, stats, quotes)
- Spokesperson information

## Step 2 — Core Output

Press kit sections: Company boilerplate (100 words, third person) → Executive/founder bio (200 words) → Story angles (3 unique angles journalists can pitch) → Key statistics and facts (bullet format) → Recent achievements or milestones → Media FAQ (8-10 anticipated questions with answers) → Contact information. Each section written in professional third person. Story angles framed as headlines. FAQs answered as quotes from the spokesperson.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Boilerplate usable verbatim — no editing needed by journalist
- Story angles are genuinely newsworthy, not promotional
- Every stat attributed and date-stamped

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
