# Content Upgrade Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- The blog post or article it accompanies
- Target reader action (what they should do next)
- Format: checklist/template/worksheet/cheat sheet
- Email opt-in headline
- How it delivers immediate value

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver: (1) Opt-in copy: headline (benefit-led), subheadline (what they get), button text (action-oriented). (2) The upgrade itself in formatted structure. Checklist format: title, 15-25 actionable items in logical order, each with a checkbox and brief instruction. Template format: labelled sections with fill-in guidance. Worksheet format: questions + answer space + prompts. Every item must be actionable — not vague guidance. Include a cover page design brief.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Upgrade delivers value without reading the article
- Every checklist item is a specific action not a principle
- Opt-in headline states the specific benefit, not the format

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
