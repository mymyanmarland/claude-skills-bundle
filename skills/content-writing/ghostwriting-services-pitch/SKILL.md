# Ghostwriting Services Pitch Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Writer's background and specialties
- Target client type
- Services offered
- Pricing model
- Portfolio or samples approach

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proposal sections: Opening hook (your positioning statement) → Your approach to capturing client voice → Services menu with scope descriptions → Process overview (discovery → drafts → revisions → delivery) → Pricing presentation (packages or hourly) → Portfolio section brief → Testimonials/social proof section → FAQ (5 common client concerns) → Next steps CTA. Written persuasively but not salesy. Emphasizes confidentiality and the client's end success.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Voice capture process explained specifically — not vaguely
- Pricing presented as investment with ROI framing
- Confidentiality addressed proactively

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
