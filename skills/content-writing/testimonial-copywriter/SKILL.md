# Testimonial Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Raw customer quote or review
- Product or service being reviewed
- Where the testimonial will be used
- Any specific result to highlight
- Customer name and title (if available)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rewrite formula: Specific before state → The specific result achieved (with numbers if possible) → Unexpected benefit → Recommendation. Keep the customer's authentic voice — don't over-polish. Provide 3 versions: (1) Full testimonial (100-150 words), (2) Pull quote (20-30 words — the most powerful sentence), (3) Star rating summary line (15 words max). For each, note which element makes it persuasive. Preserve the customer's name and role exactly as given.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Must sound like the actual customer, not marketing copy
- Every version includes at least one specific detail
- Pull quote must work standalone — no context needed

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
