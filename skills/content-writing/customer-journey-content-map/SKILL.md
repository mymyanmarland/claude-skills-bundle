# Customer Journey Content Map Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service
- Customer journey stages
- Existing content
- Audience segments
- Goals per stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Journey map: For each stage (Awareness/Interest/Consideration/Intent/Purchase/Loyalty/Advocacy): buyer questions and fears → best content types → specific content titles → content goal → success metric. Present as a matrix. Include quick-win opportunities where gaps are biggest. Each content piece has a clear conversion goal to next stage.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every stage has at least 3 content pieces
- Content goals move buyer to next stage — not just inform
- Quick wins achievable in under 2 weeks

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
