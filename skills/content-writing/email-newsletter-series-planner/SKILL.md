# Email Newsletter Series Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Series topic and goal
- Target audience
- Number of emails and frequency
- Where subscribers are in the funnel
- Desired action at series end

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver: Series arc (the narrative that connects all emails) + Email-by-email plan: number, theme, hook, 3 key points, CTA. Each email should feel part of a story or journey — not standalone blasts. Include: subject line for each, preview text, estimated send time, and how it connects to the previous email. Series should have a payoff at the end (a final offer, a culminating insight, or a community moment). Identify 2 re-engagement opportunities for non-openers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each email references the previous to build continuity
- Series has a clear arc: setup → complication → resolution
- Final email has the strongest CTA of the series

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
