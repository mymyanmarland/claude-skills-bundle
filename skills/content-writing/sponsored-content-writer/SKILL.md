# Sponsored Content Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand/sponsor and their product
- Target publication or platform
- Key message the sponsor requires
- Disclosure requirements
- Article topic that naturally integrates the sponsor

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Organic headline (topic-first, not brand-first) → Disclosure placement (per FTC: clear and conspicuous, near the top) → Article content that genuinely serves the reader → Natural brand integration (1-2 mentions, contextual not promotional) → Product mention tied to article's conclusion. Write the article first, add the sponsor second — not the reverse. The reader should find value in the content even if they skip the brand mention. Provide 3 headline options and 2 integration approaches.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Content provides value independently of the sponsor
- Disclosure is visible before reader engages with content
- Sponsor mention feels earned, not inserted

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
