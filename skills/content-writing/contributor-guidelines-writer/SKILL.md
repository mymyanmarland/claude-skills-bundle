# Contributor Guidelines Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Publication type and topic
- Audience
- What you accept and reject
- Style requirements
- Submission process

## Step 2 — Core Output

Guidelines sections: About the publication (who reads it, what you publish) → What we're looking for (topic scope, angles, formats) → What we don't accept (be specific) → Style guide (voice, formatting, length, linking policy) → Submission process (how to pitch, timeline, decision communication) → Rights and compensation. Clear, professional, scannable. Include examples where helpful.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rejection criteria are specific not vague
- Process described step by step — no ambiguity
- Rights and compensation clearly stated

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
