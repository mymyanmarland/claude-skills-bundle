# About Me Page Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Who they are and what they do
- Who they serve
- Their unique story or journey
- Credentials or experience
- Personality and tone preference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Opening hook (surprising fact, bold statement, or vivid scene) → Who you are in one sentence → Your story (the journey that led here — honest and specific) → What you do and for whom → Why you're different or what you believe → Social proof (brief, woven in naturally) → What you want the reader to do next. 300-500 words. Write in first person. Mix professionalism with personality. The reader should feel they know the person and trust them by the end.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opens with something memorable — not 'Hi, I'm [name]'
- Includes at least one vulnerability or honest moment
- Ends with a clear next step for the right visitor

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
