# Product Launch Copy Pack Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product name and description
- Target customer and their pain
- Key benefits (not features)
- Price and offer details
- Launch date and urgency element

## Step 2 — Core Output

Deliver 4 pieces: (1) Sales page outline: Hero section (headline+subhead+CTA), Problem section, Solution introduction, Benefits breakdown, Social proof placeholder, Objection handling, Final CTA. (2) 3-email launch sequence: Teaser, Launch day, Last chance. (3) 5 social media posts (mix of pre-launch hype, launch day, and benefit-focused). (4) SMS/push notification (2 options under 160 chars each). Each piece uses consistent messaging hierarchy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- All copy leads with benefits not features
- Price introduced only after value is established
- Urgency must be genuine — no fake countdown language

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
