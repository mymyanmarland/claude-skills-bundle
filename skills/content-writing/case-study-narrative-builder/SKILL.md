# Case Study Narrative Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Client background (anonymized if needed)
- The challenge or problem before
- The solution implemented
- Specific results with numbers
- Quote from client (if available)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: Results headline (lead with the number — '47% increase in...') → Client background (2 sentences) → The challenge (make the pain real) → The solution (what was done, not just what was delivered) → The results (metrics + qualitative change) → Client quote → Key takeaway for similar prospects. 400-600 words. Third person. Results section uses bold numbers. End with a CTA for similar businesses.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opens with the strongest result — not background
- Specific percentages or numbers required
- Client quote advances the narrative — not just praise

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
