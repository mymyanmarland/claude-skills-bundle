# Newsletter Subject Line Tester Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Newsletter topic and main hook
- Audience type
- Brand tone
- Any current high-performing subject lines
- Desired emotion: curiosity / urgency / value / intrigue

## Step 2 — Core Output

Generate 10 subject line options across 5 styles: (1) Curiosity gap, (2) Numbered benefit, (3) Personal/story, (4) Contrarian take, (5) Direct value. For each provide: the subject line, a preview text (90 chars), why it should work, and an open-rate estimate (relative: low/medium/high). Highlight the top 3 and explain what makes them strongest for this specific audience. Include A/B test pairing suggestions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- No subject line over 50 characters
- At least one emoji-free option
- Each style genuinely different — not variations of the same approach

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
