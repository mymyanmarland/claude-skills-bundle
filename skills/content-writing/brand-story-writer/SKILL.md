# Brand Story Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- How and why the business started
- Founder background and motivation
- The problem being solved
- Key milestones
- Brand values and mission

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structure: The world before (the problem existed) → The founder's encounter with the problem → The moment of decision or creation → The building of the solution → Where we are today → Where we're going and why it matters. Write in present/past blend — conversational but polished. 400-600 words for website use. Avoid: corporate clichés, passive voice, vague mission statements. The reader should feel they understand the WHY of the business by the end.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Must include a specific moment or scene that makes it concrete
- No generic phrases like 'passionate about' or 'world-class'
- Ends with forward vision that connects to the reader

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
