# Landing Page Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What is being sold or offered
- Primary audience and their pain
- Key benefits (top 3)
- Social proof available
- Primary CTA and secondary CTA

## Step 2 — Core Output

Full page copy: Hero (H1 benefit headline + subheadline + primary CTA) → Problem section (agitate the pain) → Solution introduction → Benefits breakdown (3-5 with icon copy) → How it works (3 steps) → Social proof section (testimonial placeholders + stats) → Objection handling (3 common objections answered) → FAQ (5 questions) → Final CTA with urgency element. Every section has a job — cut anything that doesn't move the reader closer to converting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Headline communicates outcome not feature
- CTA button text is action + benefit not just 'Submit'
- Objections handled honestly — not dismissed

Always offer a relevant next step or companion output.
