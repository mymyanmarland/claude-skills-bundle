# Email Win-Back Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Time since last engagement
- Churn reason if known
- What's changed since they left
- Incentive available
- Sunset policy

## Step 2 — Core Output

Win-back strategy: Segment by churn type (price/product/competitor/life change) → Sequence per segment → Subject line formulas that get opens from cold lists → Content that addresses churn reason specifically → Incentive structure → Sunset sequence → List clean protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Segment approach acknowledges why they left
- Subject lines tested for cold-list deliverability
- Sunset sequence honest — only if list will actually be cleaned

Always offer a relevant next step.
