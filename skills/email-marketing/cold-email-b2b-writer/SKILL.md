# Cold Email B2B Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Your product/service
- ICP description
- Pain point to address
- Value proof point
- Follow-up cadence

## Step 2 — Core Output

5-email cold sequence: Email 1 (ultra-short, one insight about their situation, one question) → Email 2 (relevant case study, 3 sentences) → Email 3 (value-add content link + soft offer) → Email 4 (direct ask — meeting) → Email 5 (breakup + final offer). Subject lines. Keep each under 100 words.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Email 1 references something specific about the prospect
- Each email different angle — not repetition
- Breakup email leaves door open professionally

Always offer a relevant next step.
