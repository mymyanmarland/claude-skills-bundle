# Email Upsell Cross-Sell Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current product purchased
- Upsell or cross-sell offer
- Customer relationship age
- Trigger for timing
- Price difference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sequence strategy: Timing trigger definition → 3-email sequence: Email 1 (value delivery + need seeding) → Email 2 (introduce the upgrade with why now) → Email 3 (social proof + offer with deadline). Upsell framing as upgrade/natural progression. Cross-sell as complementary. Subject lines for each.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Upsell framed as progression — not sales push
- Timing trigger specific — not 'after some time'
- Offer appears after value is delivered

Always offer a relevant next step.
