# Email Segmentation Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Data available (behavioral/demographic/purchase)
- Email platform capabilities
- List size
- Business goals
- Current segmentation if any

## Step 2 — Core Output

Segmentation strategy: Data audit (what you have → what you can collect) → Segment definitions (priority order) → Campaign rules per segment → Content differentiation per segment → Suppression rules → Testing methodology → Reporting structure. Implementation roadmap from basic to advanced.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Segments based on available data — not wish list
- Content differentiation meaningful — not just first name
- Implementation roadmap realistic for platform capabilities

Always offer a relevant next step.
