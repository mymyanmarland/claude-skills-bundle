# Email Referral Program Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Referral incentive structure
- Reward for referrer vs referee
- Customer relationship stage
- Brand tone
- Referral tracking method

## Step 2 — Core Output

Referral email kit: Launch announcement (existing customers) → Referral ask (post-purchase trigger) → Referral reminder (30 days later) → Reward confirmation (when referral converts) → Thank you + next milestone email. Subject lines. Referral link/code instructions. Sharing copy for customers to forward.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Incentive explained before ask
- Sharing copy ready to forward without editing
- Reward confirmation immediate — not delayed

Always offer a relevant next step.
