# Post-Webinar Email Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Webinar topic and offer
- Attendee vs no-show distinction
- Offer deadline
- Key objections from Q&A
- Replay availability

## Step 2 — Core Output

Attendee sequence: Email 1 (thank you + replay link + key takeaway) → Email 2 (case study + offer reminder) → Email 3 (FAQ from the webinar + offer) → Email 4 (deadline reminder). No-show sequence: Email 1 (missed you + replay) → Email 2 (key insight from webinar + offer) → Email 3 (deadline). Subject lines for all.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Attendee and no-show sequences genuinely different
- Replay link prominent and early
- Offer deadline consistent across all emails

Always offer a relevant next step.
