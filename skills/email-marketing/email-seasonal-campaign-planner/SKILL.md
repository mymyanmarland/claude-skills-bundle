# Email Seasonal Campaign Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type and products
- Key seasonal opportunities
- Email list size
- Campaign resources
- Brand relationship with holidays

## Step 2 — Core Output

Annual email calendar: Month-by-month key dates → Campaign concept per season → Email count per campaign → Lead time required → Subject line approach per season → Offer strategy per season → Segmentation approach (customers vs prospects). Includes non-holiday campaigns to break up promotional cadence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lead times realistic for production
- Non-promotional campaigns interspersed
- Segmentation applied where it matters most

Always offer a relevant next step.
