# Email Re-Enrollment Campaign Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Original sequence and why they dropped off
- Time since last engagement
- What's new or different
- Re-entry offer
- Sequence length

## Step 2 — Core Output

Re-enrollment campaign: Awareness email (we noticed you've been quiet — value reminder) → New offering email (here's what's changed since you last engaged) → Re-entry CTA email (specific invitation to re-enter sequence). Subject lines designed for cold-list opens. List verification step recommended first.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acknowledges gap without being awkward
- New offering genuinely new — not same pitch
- Re-entry CTA low friction — one click

Always offer a relevant next step.
