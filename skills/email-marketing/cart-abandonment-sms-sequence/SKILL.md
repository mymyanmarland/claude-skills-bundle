# Cart Abandonment SMS Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Average order value
- Brand tone
- Timing preference
- Discount strategy

## Step 2 — Core Output

3-SMS sequence: SMS 1 at 30 min (short reminder — product name + direct link) → SMS 2 at 4 hours (social proof + reminder) → SMS 3 at 24 hours (offer if applicable + final nudge). Each under 160 chars. Opt-out instructions in first SMS. Conversational tone.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each SMS under 160 characters
- Opt-out clearly stated in sequence
- No aggressive urgency language

Always offer a relevant next step.
