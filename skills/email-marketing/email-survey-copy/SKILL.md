# Email Survey Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Survey topic and goal
- Audience
- Time required to complete
- Incentive if any
- Timing in relationship

## Step 2 — Core Output

Survey email kit: Invitation email (hook with why it matters to them + time commitment + incentive) → Reminder email (sent to non-openers at 3 days) → Thank you email (with results teaser if appropriate) → Results email (share key findings — closes the loop). Subject lines. Survey link CTA copy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Time commitment honest and specific
- What they'll get from participating explicit
- Results email closes loop — surveys without follow-up lose trust

Always offer a relevant next step.
