# Email Copywriting Templates Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type
- Common email types needed
- Brand voice
- Audience
- Formality level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Template library: 15 templates covering: prospecting outreach, follow-up, proposal send, meeting request, check-in, value delivery, referral ask, testimonial request, announcement, apology, rejection response, upsell, renewal, offboarding, re-engagement. Each: subject line + body with fill-in sections clearly marked.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Templates feel like starting points — not finished emails
- Fill-in sections specific about what to add
- Every template includes personalization opportunity

Always offer a relevant next step.
