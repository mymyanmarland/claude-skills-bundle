# Email Subject Line Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Email topic and key hook
- Audience
- List type (cold/warm/customers)
- Tone
- Any personalization tokens available

## Step 2 — Core Output

20 subject lines across 5 categories: Curiosity gap (4), Direct benefit (4), Personal/story (4), Urgency/scarcity (4), Question (4). For each: subject line, preview text suggestion, open rate prediction (relative), best list type. A/B test pairs with recommendation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Subject lines varied — not all same formula
- Preview text complements — not repeats subject
- Urgency only when genuine

Always offer a relevant next step.
