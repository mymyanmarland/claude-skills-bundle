# Email Frequency Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- List size and age
- Current open rate
- Unsubscribe rate
- Content production capacity
- Audience relationship (warm/cold/customer)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Frequency recommendation with rationale → Suppression segment strategy (send less to disengaged) → Permission-based preference center concept → Content calendar that justifies frequency → Re-permission strategy for over-emailed lists → A/B test plan for finding optimal frequency.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recommendation specific to stated metrics
- Suppression strategy protects deliverability
- Frequency tied to content quality commitment

Always offer a relevant next step.
