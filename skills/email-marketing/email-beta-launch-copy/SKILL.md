# Email Beta Launch Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What is in beta
- Who qualifies for access
- What beta users get
- Feedback expectation
- Full launch timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beta launch sequence: Teaser email (exclusive hint + waitlist CTA) → Beta invite email (you're in — what to expect) → Welcome to beta (access + what we need from you) → Check-in email (2 weeks — feedback request) → Beta closing + transition email. Makes beta users feel special and invested in success.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Beta access feels genuinely exclusive
- Feedback ask specific — not 'let us know what you think'
- Transition email honors beta users' contribution

Always offer a relevant next step.
