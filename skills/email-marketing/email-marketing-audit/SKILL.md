# Email Marketing Audit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Email metrics provided
- Platform used
- Program age
- Audience type
- Current pain points

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit structure: Deliverability health check (open rates, spam complaints, bounce rates) → List health analysis → Campaign performance review → Automation audit → Compliance check → Competitive benchmarking → Priority recommendations. Red/yellow/green scoring per area.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Benchmarks industry-appropriate
- Recommendations prioritized by impact
- Compliance issues flagged as high priority regardless of other scores

Always offer a relevant next step.
