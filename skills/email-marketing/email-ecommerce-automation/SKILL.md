# Email Ecommerce Automation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Store type and products
- Platform (Shopify/WooCommerce/etc)
- Email platform
- Current automations
- Revenue goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Automation stack: Priority 1 (welcome series, abandoned cart, post-purchase) → Priority 2 (browse abandonment, win-back, VIP) → Priority 3 (replenishment, upsell, birthday). For each: trigger logic, sequence length, copy approach, revenue impact estimate. Implementation order by ROI.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Priority order based on revenue impact
- Each automation has revenue attribution method
- Trigger logic handles edge cases

Always offer a relevant next step.
