# Email Cross-Channel Integration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Channels to integrate
- Customer journey stages
- Tech stack
- Data sharing capabilities
- Goal: awareness/conversion/retention

## Step 2 — Core Output

Integration plan: Channel-by-channel role definition → Handoff triggers (when customer moves from channel to channel) → Audience syncing strategy (email list → ad audiences) → Message coordination rules (don't repeat same offer across channels simultaneously) → Attribution methodology → Unified reporting approach.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each channel has distinct role — not duplication
- Handoff triggers specific and automated where possible
- Attribution acknowledges multi-touch reality

Always offer a relevant next step.
