# Email VIP Segment Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- VIP criteria
- Available exclusive benefits
- Brand relationship
- Revenue concentration
- CRM capabilities

## Step 2 — Core Output

VIP strategy: Segment definition criteria → VIP welcome sequence (3 emails) → Exclusive content calendar (monthly) → Early access protocol → VIP-only offers framework → Annual VIP review communication → Upgrade/downgrade handling. Makes VIPs feel genuinely special — not just marketed to differently.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- VIP benefits genuinely exclusive — not available to all
- Welcome sequence different from standard onboarding
- Annual review acknowledges loyalty specifically

Always offer a relevant next step.
