# Email Conversion Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current email copy
- Current performance metrics
- Goal of the email
- Audience
- Hypothesis about what's wrong

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit: What's reducing conversion (diagnostic) → Rewritten subject line → Rewritten email copy → CTA rewrite → Before/after comparison → Predicted improvement → A/B test recommendation. Specific diagnosis — not generic advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Diagnosis specific to the actual copy, not generic
- Rewrite tests one hypothesis at a time
- Before/after comparison shows clear improvement

Always offer a relevant next step.
