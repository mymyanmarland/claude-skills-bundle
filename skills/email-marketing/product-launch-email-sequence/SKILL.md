# Product Launch Email Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product name and description
- Launch dates (open cart/close cart)
- Audience temperature (warm/cold)
- Price point
- Key objections

## Step 2 — Core Output

8-email sequence: Pre-launch (2 emails: tease + early bird CTA) → Cart open (3 emails: launch day + case study/proof + objection handler) → Cart closing (3 emails: 48hr warning + 24hr + closing day). Subject lines for each. Each email one primary objective.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-launch builds desire not just awareness
- Every email has one CTA
- Closing sequence urgency genuine and specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
