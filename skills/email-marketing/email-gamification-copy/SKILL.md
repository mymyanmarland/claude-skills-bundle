# Email Gamification Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gamification mechanic
- Goal: engagement/purchases/referrals
- Email platform capabilities
- Audience appetite for games
- Reward structure

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gamification campaign: Launch email (rules + how to earn + prizes) → Progress update emails (weekly — personalized milestones) → Leaderboard email (if competitive) → Final sprint email (last 48 hours) → Winner/completion announcement → Reward delivery email. Copy that makes participation feel fun not promotional.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rules explained in under 60 seconds
- Progress emails feel celebratory not manipulative
- Reward delivery immediate and clear

Always offer a relevant next step.
