# Abandoned Cart Email Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Average order value
- Competitor alternatives
- Brand tone
- Discount availability

## Step 2 — Core Output

3-email sequence: Email 1 at 1 hour (reminder — what they left, benefit reminder, simple CTA) → Email 2 at 24 hours (social proof + objection handling) → Email 3 at 72 hours (scarcity or incentive if applicable). Subject lines for each. Each email has different angle — not repetition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Email 1 doesn't offer discount — preserve margin
- Email 2 handles most common objection for this product type
- Urgency in email 3 is genuine if used

Always offer a relevant next step.
