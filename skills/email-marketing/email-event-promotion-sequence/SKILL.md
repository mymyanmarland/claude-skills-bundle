# Email Event Promotion Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Event type and topic
- Registration deadline
- Audience temperature
- Capacity constraints
- Value proposition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Event sequence: Save the date (6 weeks out) → Registration open announcement (4 weeks) → Speaker/content reveal (2 weeks) → Last spots reminder (1 week) → Final 24 hours (urgency) → Pre-event reminder (day before + day of) → Post-event follow-up (replay + offer). Subject lines for all.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Urgency emails only when genuine scarcity exists
- Pre-event reminder includes logistics clearly
- Post-event email sent within 24 hours

Always offer a relevant next step.
