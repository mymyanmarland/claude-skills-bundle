# Email Onboarding Checklist Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Email platform
- Business type
- List size
- Compliance requirements
- Technical setup context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Checklist sections: Technical setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, custom domain) → List hygiene setup → Automation structure → Template creation → Legal compliance (GDPR, CAN-SPAM, CASL) → Deliverability monitoring → Reporting setup. Each item: what to do, why it matters, how to verify.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every item actionable — not just principles
- Legal requirements jurisdiction-flagged
- Technical items have verification steps

Always offer a relevant next step.
