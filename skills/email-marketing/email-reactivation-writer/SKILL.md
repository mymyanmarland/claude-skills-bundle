# Email Reactivation Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Time since last purchase
- Product type
- Likely reason for lapse
- Incentive available
- Brand relationship

## Step 2 — Core Output

3-email reactivation: Email 1 (acknowledge absence + relevant new offering) → Email 2 (personalized recommendation based on last purchase) → Email 3 (time-limited incentive). Subject line for each. Tone acknowledges time passed without being awkward.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Personalization references actual past behavior
- Incentive in email 3 time-bound genuinely
- Tone warm — not desperate or guilt-inducing

Always offer a relevant next step.
