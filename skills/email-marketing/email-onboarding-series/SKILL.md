# Email Onboarding Series Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Key activation milestones
- Onboarding time expected
- Common churn reasons
- User segment if applicable

## Step 2 — Core Output

Onboarding sequence: Day 0 (welcome + first step) → Day 2 (first milestone nudge) → Day 5 (value realization check) → Day 10 (advanced feature spotlight) → Day 14 (social proof + upsell tease) → Day 30 (success check-in + case study). Each email one action requested.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each email tied to specific product milestone
- Action requested achievable in under 5 minutes
- Day 30 email celebrates progress before upselling

Always offer a relevant next step.
