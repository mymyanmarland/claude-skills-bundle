# Email Customer Story Feature Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Customer story to feature
- What made them choose you
- Results achieved
- Similarity to recipient
- CTA goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Story feature email: Subject line (results-first — not 'customer spotlight') → Story hook (start with the before situation) → The journey (what they tried, why it wasn't working) → The result (specific metrics + qualitative change) → Quote → Relevance bridge (why this story matters to the reader) → CTA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Story hook relatable to recipient segment
- Result specific and credible
- Relevance bridge makes reader see themselves in the story

Always offer a relevant next step.
