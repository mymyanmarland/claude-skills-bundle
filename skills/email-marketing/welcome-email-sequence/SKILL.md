# Welcome Email Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Brand and product
- What new subscribers signed up for
- First conversion goal
- Brand voice
- Sending cadence

## Step 2 — Core Output

5-email sequence: Email 1 (deliver the promised lead magnet + what to expect) → Email 2 (your story — build connection) → Email 3 (most valuable content — build trust) → Email 4 (social proof — remove doubt) → Email 5 (soft offer — first conversion). Subject lines for each. Plain text recommended for deliverability.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Email 1 delivers value immediately — no delay
- Sequence builds to offer — doesn't lead with it
- Each email has one job and one CTA

Always offer a relevant next step.
