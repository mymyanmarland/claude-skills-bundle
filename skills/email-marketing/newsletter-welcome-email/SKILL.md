# Newsletter Welcome Email Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Newsletter name and topic
- What subscriber signed up for
- Publishing frequency
- Brand personality
- First content to deliver

## Step 2 — Core Output

Welcome email: Subject (personalized, expectation-setting) → Delivery of promised lead magnet → Who you are (brief, human, relevant) → What to expect (frequency, topics, format) → Best existing content to start → How to whitelist → Personal sign-off. Under 300 words. Feels like a note from a person.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lead magnet delivered immediately
- Expectations specific — not 'great content'
- Reads like a person wrote it — not a system

Always offer a relevant next step.
