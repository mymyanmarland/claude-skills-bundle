# Email Milestone Celebration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Milestone types
- Timing triggers
- What milestone means in their journey
- Reward or recognition offered
- Brand tone

## Step 2 — Core Output

Milestone email kit: 1-year anniversary (relationship reflection + exclusive offer) → Purchase milestone (X orders — thank you + VIP upgrade) → Usage milestone (X sessions/items — what they've achieved) → Referred a friend (thank you + bonus) → Birthday email (if data available). Each: genuine celebration, not marketing in disguise.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Celebration feels genuine — not just a discount trigger
- Milestone acknowledged specifically — not generic 'you're special'
- Offer secondary to recognition

Always offer a relevant next step.
