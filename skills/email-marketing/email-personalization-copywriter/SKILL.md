# Email Personalization Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Personalization data available
- Email type
- Segments
- Platform capabilities
- Fallback content needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Personalization map: Available tokens → Where each adds value in email → Fallback logic for missing data → Dynamic content blocks (by segment) → Behavioral personalization (by past action) → Example email with all personalization marked. Plus: over-personalization warnings (when it feels creepy).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every personalization has a fallback
- Personalization meaningful — not just name insertion
- Creepy personalization examples called out

Always offer a relevant next step.
