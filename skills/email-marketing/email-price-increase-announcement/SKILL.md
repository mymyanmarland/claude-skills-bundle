# Email Price Increase Announcement Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- New price vs old
- Timeline for change
- Grandfathering options
- Reason for increase
- Relationship stage

## Step 2 — Core Output

Price increase communications: Early notice email (30-60 days — honest, value-led, grandfathering offer) → Reminder email (7 days before) → Final day email (lock in current price) → Post-change confirmation. Framing: value delivered → investment change → how to lock in current rate. No apology, no over-explanation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reason stated honestly — not vague
- Grandfathering window genuinely generous if offered
- No apology — confident tone about value

Always offer a relevant next step.
