# Email Deliverability Audit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current open rates
- Spam complaint rate
- Bounce rates
- Email platform
- List age and acquisition method

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit: Technical check (SPF/DKIM/DMARC/custom domain status) → List health assessment → Engagement rate analysis → Spam trigger word scan in templates → IP reputation check guidance → Warming plan if needed → Suppression strategy → 30-day recovery plan. Priority by impact.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Technical items have specific action instructions
- Recovery plan timeline realistic
- Root cause identified — not just symptoms treated

Always offer a relevant next step.
