# Newsletter Monetization Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sponsor and product
- Newsletter audience
- Placement (standalone/integrated)
- Tone match
- CTA goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

3 native ad formats: (1) Integrated mention (50 words — woven naturally into editorial). (2) Sponsored section (100 words — clearly labeled sponsor but native voice). (3) Dedicated send (200 words — full email about sponsor). Each maintains newsletter voice. Subject line option for dedicated send.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Integrated mention passes the 'would I write this without being paid' test
- Sponsorship disclosure clear and early
- All three feel like author wrote them

Always offer a relevant next step.
