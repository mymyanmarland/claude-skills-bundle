# Cold Email Follow-Up Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Initial email context
- Prospect type
- Number of follow-ups
- Tone
- Final offer or ask

## Step 2 — Core Output

4 follow-up variations: Day 3 (direct reference + added value) → Day 7 (different angle — new insight) → Day 14 (social proof + specific question) → Day 21 (breakup — genuine, no gimmick). Each under 75 words. Each adds something new — not just 'bumping this up'.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each follow-up adds new value — not repetition
- Breakup email final — no 'last chance' tricks
- Tone consistent with initial email

Always offer a relevant next step.
