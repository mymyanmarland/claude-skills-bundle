# Email List Growth Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current list size and growth rate
- Traffic sources
- Lead magnet available
- Target subscriber profile
- Growth goal and timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Growth strategy: Lead magnet audit + recommendations → Opt-in form placement strategy → Pop-up strategy + copy → Content upgrade plan (3 highest-traffic pages) → Social media list growth tactics → Partnership and cross-promotion opportunities → Paid list growth options → 90-day growth roadmap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every tactic matched to traffic source
- Lead magnet recommendations specific to audience
- 90-day plan achievable with stated resources

Always offer a relevant next step.
