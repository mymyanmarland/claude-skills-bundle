# Re-Engagement Email Campaign Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- How long subscribers have been inactive
- What they originally signed up for
- Brand tone
- Sunset policy
- Incentive available

## Step 2 — Core Output

3-email win-back sequence: Email 1 (we miss you — value reminder, low ask) → Email 2 (here's what you've missed — content teaser) → Email 3 (last chance before list clean + final offer or honest goodbye). Subject lines. Sunset sequence: Email 4 (we're removing you — change their mind). Each respectful of subscriber's time.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Email 1 reminds of value — doesn't beg
- Last chance email honest — only if you will actually remove them
- Goodbye email leaves positive impression

Always offer a relevant next step.
