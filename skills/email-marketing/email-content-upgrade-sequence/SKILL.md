# Email Content Upgrade Sequence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What was downloaded
- Subscriber intent signal
- Primary conversion goal
- Relationship warmth
- Sequence length preference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Download-triggered sequence: Email 1 at 0 (deliver + quick win from using it) → Email 2 at Day 2 (related tip that extends value) → Email 3 at Day 5 (case study showing transformation) → Email 4 at Day 8 (soft offer — next logical step) → Email 5 at Day 12 (harder offer with deadline). Subject lines.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Email 1 delivers immediately — no delay
- Offer appears only after value is established
- Offer logically connects to what they downloaded

Always offer a relevant next step.
