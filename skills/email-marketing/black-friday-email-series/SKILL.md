# Black Friday Email Series Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Products and offers
- Discount strategy
- Audience (existing customers/new)
- Campaign dates
- Brand tone

## Step 2 — Core Output

10-email series: Pre-BFCM (3 emails: early access VIP, preview, countdown) → BF (3 emails: open, mid-day push, last hours) → Weekend (1 email: extended deal) → CM (3 emails: new offer, mid-day, final hours). Subject lines for all. Urgency that's real. No fake countdown clocks.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Urgency deadlines accurate and consistent across emails
- VIP early access has genuine advantage
- Subject lines varied across the sequence

Always offer a relevant next step.
