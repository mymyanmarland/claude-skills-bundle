# Email Campaign Debrief Template Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Campaign type
- Metrics tracked
- Team size
- Review cadence
- Learning goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Debrief template: Campaign summary (objective, audience, date, offer) → Performance vs benchmark (with traffic light scoring) → What worked (specific, with evidence) → What didn't work (specific, with evidence) → Hypotheses for why → Changes for next campaign → Test recommendations. 1-page format.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Template completable in 30 minutes
- Learning section requires specificity — not 'good open rate'
- Test recommendations carried forward to next campaign

Always offer a relevant next step.
