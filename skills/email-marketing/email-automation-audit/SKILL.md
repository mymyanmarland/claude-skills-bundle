# Email Automation Audit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current automations list
- Performance metrics per flow
- Email platform
- Business goals
- Resources for fixes

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audit structure: Inventory of all active automations → Performance score per automation (open rate/CTR/conversion vs benchmark) → Gaps identified (missing automations for key lifecycle moments) → Top 3 optimization priorities with specific fixes → New automation recommendations → Implementation roadmap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Every automation benchmarked against industry standard
- Gaps identified specifically — not 'could do more'
- Implementation roadmap sequenced by revenue impact

Always offer a relevant next step.
