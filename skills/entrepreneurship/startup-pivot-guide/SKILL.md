# Startup Pivot Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current product and traction
- What's not working
- What IS working (any signal)
- Team conviction
- Runway remaining

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pivot guide: Persevere vs pivot decision framework (pivot on strategy, not vision) → Signal analysis (what's the data saying vs what are you hoping?) → Pivot types (customer segment/problem/solution/revenue model/platform/technology) → The partial pivot (test before committing) → Pivot communication to team and investors → Runway requirement for pivot → The shutdown decision.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pivot on strategy — not on vision
- Partial pivot tested before full commitment
- Shutdown decision addressed honestly — sometimes right answer

Always offer a relevant next step.
