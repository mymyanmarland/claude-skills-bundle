# Startup Sales Playbook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type and price point
- Target customer
- Current sales approach
- Conversion rate problems
- Founder selling capacity

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sales playbook: ICP definition (ideal customer profile — specific) → Lead sources by priority → Discovery call structure (listen more than pitch) → Demo framework (problem → impact → solution) → Objection library (top 5 with responses) → Follow-up cadence → Closing approach → CRM setup → Handoff from founder selling to first salesperson.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- ICP specific — not 'any company that could use this'
- Discovery call prioritizes listening — not pitching
- Handoff criteria defined — when to hire first salesperson

Always offer a relevant next step.
