# Startup Advisor Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage and gaps
- What type of advisor needed
- Equity budget
- Network access
- What you can offer advisors

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advisor guide: Types of advisors (domain/functional/network/strategic) → How to find them → Advisor agreement structure (FAST agreement — 0.1-0.5% with 2-year vesting) → What to ask of advisors (specific, not 'help me grow') → Monthly advisor update template → How to end an advisor relationship that isn't working → Board observer vs advisor distinction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FAST agreement recommended — standard for advisor equity
- Specific asks from advisors — not vague 'guidance'
- Ending non-productive relationships addressed — often avoided

Always offer a relevant next step.
