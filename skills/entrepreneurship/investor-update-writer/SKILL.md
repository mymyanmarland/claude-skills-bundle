# Investor Update Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company stage and metrics
- Key developments this month
- Challenges and asks
- Wins to share
- Investor relationship context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Investor update template: Opening metric (one key number that shows momentum) → Highlights (3 bullets — be specific) → Lowlights (1-2 honest challenges — builds trust) → Key metrics dashboard → Ask (specific — intros/advice/resources) → What's next (top 3 priorities). Under 400 words. Monthly cadence. Honest lowlights distinguish good founders from bad.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lowlights included — honesty builds trust with investors
- Ask is specific — not 'let me know if you can help'
- Under 400 words — investors skim long updates

Always offer a relevant next step.
