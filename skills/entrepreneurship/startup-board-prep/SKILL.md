# Startup Board Meeting Prep Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company stage and board composition
- Key decisions needed
- Current metrics and trajectory
- Challenges to surface
- Time available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Board meeting prep: Pre-read package (circulated 48h before — metrics, key issues) → Meeting agenda (discussion-focused, not presentation-heavy) → What to bring vs what to decide → How to present bad news (directly, with a plan) → Investor relations: who to call before the meeting → Board resolution requirements → Follow-up protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-read circulated before meeting — no surprises
- Meeting discussion-focused — not PowerPoint presentation
- Bad news surfaced directly — never discovered by board

Always offer a relevant next step.
