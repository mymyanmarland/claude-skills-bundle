# Startup Storytelling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Founding story
- Problem and why it matters
- What you've built
- Traction
- Where you're going

## Step 2 — Core Output

Startup story framework: The world as it is (problem exists, it's painful, most people ignore it) → The founder's insight (what you saw that others didn't) → The world as it should be (vision) → The plan to get there → Why now (timing is as important as insight) → Why us (founder-market fit). 3 story lengths: 30-second / 3-minute / full narrative.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Problem made visceral before solution introduced
- 'Why now' included — timing matters enormously
- All 3 lengths consistent — same story, different depth

Always offer a relevant next step.
