# Startup Legal Basics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type and model
- Country/state of incorporation
- Co-founders if any
- Funding plans
- IP concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Legal basics: Entity selection (LLC vs C-Corp — when each is right) → Incorporation basics → Founder agreements and vesting → IP assignment (critical — founders must assign IP to company) → Employment vs contractor classification → Basic customer contracts → Privacy policy basics → When to get a lawyer vs when to DIY. Note: not legal advice — consult an attorney.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- IP assignment flagged as critical — often missed
- Lawyer recommendation for anything complex — not DIY everything
- Disclaimer prominent — not legal advice

Always offer a relevant next step.
