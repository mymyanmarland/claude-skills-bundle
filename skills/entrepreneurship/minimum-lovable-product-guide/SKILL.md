# Minimum Lovable Product Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product concept
- Target user and their emotions
- Core use case
- MVP already built or in progress
- What would make users recommend it

## Step 2 — Core Output

MLP framework: Beyond functionality (what makes users feel something?) → The delight layer (unexpected moments of joy) → Friction removal (what's annoying about similar products?) → Personality (products have personality — what's yours?) → The one thing done exceptionally well → Feedback loops to find what users love → Love metrics (NPS/word of mouth/unsolicited sharing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Delight specific — not 'make it nice'
- One thing done exceptionally — breadth too early kills love
- Love metrics measurable — not just 'users like it'

Always offer a relevant next step.
