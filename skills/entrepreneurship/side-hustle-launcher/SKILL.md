# Side Hustle Launcher Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Side hustle concept
- Full-time job constraints
- Available hours per week
- Financial goal
- Timeline to launch

## Step 2 — Core Output

Side hustle launch plan: Viability test (can this work at part-time hours?) → Concept selection criteria (low capital / leverage existing skills / scalable eventually) → 90-day launch plan → Time allocation (when specifically) → Boundary setting with employer (legal check) → Revenue timeline (realistic: 3-6 months to first dollar) → Transition decision criteria.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Employer conflict check included — often missed
- Revenue timeline realistic — not optimistic
- Transition criteria set before starting — not ad hoc

Always offer a relevant next step.
