# Startup Moat Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model
- Current competitive differentiation
- Stage and resources
- Market dynamics
- What's easiest to copy about you

## Step 2 — Core Output

Moat analysis: Moat types (network effects/switching costs/data/brand/scale/regulatory) → Current moat assessment (honest — most early startups have none) → Moat-building priority by business model → Network effect design (if applicable) → Data moat strategy → Switching cost creation → Brand as moat timeline → What to do before you have a moat.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Honest early-stage moat assessment — most don't have one yet
- Moat-building actions specific — not vague 'build brand'
- Pre-moat strategy for when you're vulnerable

Always offer a relevant next step.
