# Business Model Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business concept
- Customer segments
- Value proposition
- Revenue model ideas
- Cost structure awareness

## Step 2 — Core Output

Business Model Canvas: Customer segments (who exactly?) → Value proposition (what problem solved, what gain created) → Channels (how to reach them) → Customer relationships (how to acquire and retain) → Revenue streams (how money is made + pricing model) → Key resources (what's needed) → Key activities (what must be done) → Key partnerships (who to leverage) → Cost structure. Stress test: what breaks first?

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Customer segment specific — not 'everyone'
- Value proposition from customer perspective — not founder perspective
- Cost structure realistic — no optimistic assumptions

Always offer a relevant next step.
