# Solopreneur System Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business type
- Services or products offered
- Revenue target
- Biggest time sinks
- Delegation or automation appetite

## Step 2 — Core Output

Solopreneur system: Revenue model optimization (leverage, not just hours) → Productized services (package work for repeatability) → Client management system (CRM, contracts, billing) → Content/marketing system (consistent without constant) → Operations automation (what to automate vs outsource) → Capacity management (how to grow revenue without growing hours) → One-person team protocols.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leverage focus — not just efficiency
- Productization creates scalability without hiring
- Automation vs outsourcing decision explicit

Always offer a relevant next step.
