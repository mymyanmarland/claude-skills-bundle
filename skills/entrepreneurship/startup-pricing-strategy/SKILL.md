# Startup Pricing Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type and value delivered
- Customer type (consumer/SMB/enterprise)
- Stage (pre-revenue/early revenue)
- Competitor pricing
- Cost structure

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pricing strategy: Value-based pricing foundation (price to value, not cost) → Pricing model selection (subscription/usage/one-time/freemium/tiered) → Initial price recommendation with reasoning → The danger of underpricing (signals low value) → Price testing methodology → Packaging for different segments → Price increase strategy → Discount policy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value-based pricing — not cost-plus
- Underpricing danger explicit — common startup mistake
- Price testing methodology before committing to structure

Always offer a relevant next step.
