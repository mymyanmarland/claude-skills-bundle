# Startup Operations Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company stage and team size
- Current operational pain points
- Tools budget
- Compliance requirements
- Growth rate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Operations foundation: Tool stack (communication/project management/finance/HR — minimum viable) → Process documentation priority (what breaks most when undocumented) → Meeting cadence design → OKR or goal-setting system → Financial operations (invoicing/payroll/bookkeeping) → Legal and compliance checklist → The first operations hire trigger.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Minimum viable tool stack — not enterprise systems too early
- Documentation priority by what breaks without it
- Operations hire trigger specific — not 'when you feel overwhelmed'

Always offer a relevant next step.
