# Market Sizing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business concept
- Target customer type
- Geography
- Revenue model
- Investor context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Market sizing guide: TAM/SAM/SOM definitions and calculations → Bottom-up vs top-down (always use both — investors know when you fudge it) → Data sources for market sizing → Credibility principles (smaller credible market beats large fantastical one) → The market sizing narrative (framing matters) → Common market sizing mistakes (defining market too broadly) → How to update sizing as you learn.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bottom-up calculation primary — top-down as check
- Data sources cited — not made up
- Common mistakes explicit — founders reliably make the same errors

Always offer a relevant next step.
