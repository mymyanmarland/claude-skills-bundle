# Digital Product Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Expertise to productize
- Target buyer
- Product type
- Pricing range
- Distribution platform

## Step 2 — Core Output

Digital product plan: Idea validation (will people pay for this before building?) → Product scope (minimum viable product first) → Creation workflow → Platform selection (Gumroad/Teachable/Etsy/own website) → Pricing strategy → Launch plan (audience vs no audience approach) → Evergreen vs launch model → Revenue expectations (realistic timeline).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Validation before creation — build only what people will buy
- Minimum viable product first — not comprehensive course
- Revenue timeline realistic — not passive from day 1

Always offer a relevant next step.
