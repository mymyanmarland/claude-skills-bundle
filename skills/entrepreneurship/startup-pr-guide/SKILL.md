# Startup PR Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage and news angle
- Target media
- Story narrative
- Spokesperson
- Budget (likely zero)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Startup PR guide: News angle identification (what's actually newsworthy?) → Media list building (tier 1/2/3 journalists by beat) → Press release vs pitch email decision → Pitch email formula (short, specific, why now, why them) → Follow-up protocol → Embargo strategy → Exclusive offer strategy → What to do with coverage when you get it.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- News angle genuinely newsworthy — editor perspective taken
- Pitch email under 200 words — journalists are busy
- Coverage amplification strategy included — coverage without amplification is wasted

Always offer a relevant next step.
