# Startup Pitch Deck Outline Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Funding stage
- Business model
- Traction metrics
- Team background
- Funding ask

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pitch deck outline: Slide 1 (cover — company name + one-liner) → Slide 2 (problem — make them feel the pain) → Slide 3 (solution — simple, clear) → Slide 4 (market size — TAM/SAM/SOM with methodology) → Slide 5 (business model — how you make money) → Slide 6 (traction — the most important slide) → Slide 7 (team) → Slide 8 (financials — 3-year projection) → Slide 9 (ask — how much, use of funds, milestones). Each slide: what to show, what to say, common mistakes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Traction slide most important — investors fund momentum
- Market size methodology shown — not just a big number
- Ask slide specific: amount + use of funds + 18-month milestones

Always offer a relevant next step.
