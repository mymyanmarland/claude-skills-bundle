# Startup Resilience Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage and current challenges
- Stress sources
- Support system
- Past resilience strategies
- What sustainability looks like

## Step 2 — Core Output

Founder resilience guide: The emotional reality of building (normalize the lows) → Stress-performance relationship (some stress is fuel, too much is toxic) → Decision-making under uncertainty framework → Board of directors for your life (not just business) → Physical foundations (sleep/movement non-negotiable) → When to seek professional support → Identity beyond the startup → Failure reframing practices.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Emotional reality normalized — not hustle culture glorified
- Professional support normalized — not stigmatized
- Identity beyond startup addressed — company failure ≠ personal failure

Always offer a relevant next step.
