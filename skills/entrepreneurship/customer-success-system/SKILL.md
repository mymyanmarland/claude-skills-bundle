# Customer Success System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Customer size and type
- Current churn rate
- Onboarding process
- Team resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Customer success system: Onboarding design (time to first value — minimize it) → Health score definition (what signals a healthy vs at-risk customer) → Engagement triggers (when to proactively reach out) → Renewal and expansion playbook → Churn analysis (why do customers leave?) → QBR framework → Customer success metrics (NRR/GRR/time to value).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Time to first value minimized — most critical success metric
- Health score uses behavioral data — not just survey sentiment
- Churn analysis drives product improvement — not just customer success tactics

Always offer a relevant next step.
