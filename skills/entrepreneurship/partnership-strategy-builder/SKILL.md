# Partnership Strategy Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model
- Target customer
- Potential partner types
- What you can offer partners
- Stage and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Partnership strategy: Partner type analysis (distribution/technology/referral/co-marketing) → Ideal partner profile (who has your customers but doesn't compete?) → Value exchange articulation (what do they get?) → Partnership pitch → Deal structure options → Partner enablement (make it easy for them to sell you) → Partner metrics → Exclusivity considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value exchange explicit — not just 'mutual benefit'
- Partner enablement included — removes friction for partners
- Exclusivity carefully considered — often a trap for startups

Always offer a relevant next step.
