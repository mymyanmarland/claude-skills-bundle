# Freelancer to Agency Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current freelance business
- Revenue and clients
- Service type
- Hiring appetite
- Systems maturity

## Step 2 — Core Output

Agency transition guide: The identity shift (operator not just practitioner) → First hire decision (who removes you from delivery?) → Process documentation before hiring → Pricing model shift (retainer vs project) → Capacity management → Client communication about the change → Profit margin targets for agencies → The growing pains to anticipate.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Identity shift acknowledged — psychological transition as important as operational
- Documentation before hiring — processes in your head don't scale
- Profit margins realistic for agency model

Always offer a relevant next step.
