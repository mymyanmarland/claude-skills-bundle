# Growth Strategy Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model
- Current growth bottleneck
- Stage and budget
- Channels tested so far
- Customer profile

## Step 2 — Core Output

Growth strategy: AARRR audit (where is the biggest leak in the funnel?) → Acquisition channels ranked by fit and cost → Activation optimization (what makes new users successful immediately) → Retention drivers (why do people stay or churn) → Referral mechanics (what makes customers refer) → Revenue expansion (upsell/cross-sell) → 90-day growth experiment plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Biggest funnel leak identified first — not all at once
- Experiments designed with hypotheses and success criteria
- Retention before acquisition — fix leaks before adding water

Always offer a relevant next step.
