# Product Roadmap Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product stage
- Customer feedback and requests
- Business goals
- Technical constraints
- Timeframe

## Step 2 — Core Output

Product roadmap: Vision statement (where the product is going) → Now/Next/Later framework (concrete/directional/aspirational) → Prioritization framework (impact vs effort, user value vs business value) → Customer problems (not features) as roadmap items → Stakeholder communication format → How to say no to feature requests → Roadmap review cadence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Problems on roadmap — not features
- Now/Next/Later prevents false precision in later stages
- 'No' to feature requests includes a reason and alternative

Always offer a relevant next step.
