# Startup Marketing on Budget Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Target audience
- Founder skills
- Time available
- Channels previously tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Zero-budget marketing: Founder audience building (LinkedIn/Twitter/YouTube) → Community infiltration (join and add value before promoting) → SEO long game (write content that ranks) → PR without an agency → Partnership and referral programs → Product-led growth (product markets itself) → Organic social strategy → Content repurposing. Prioritized by time-to-ROI.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tactics prioritized by time-to-ROI — not just by reach potential
- Founder audience building explicit — founders ARE the marketing
- Product-led growth options by product type

Always offer a relevant next step.
