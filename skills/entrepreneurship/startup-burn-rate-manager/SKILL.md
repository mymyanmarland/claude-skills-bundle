# Startup Burn Rate Manager Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current burn rate
- Cash on hand
- Revenue if any
- Cost structure
- Fundraising timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Burn management: Runway calculation (months of cash at current burn) → Default alive/dead analysis (can you reach profitability without more funding?) → Burn breakdown by category → Variable vs fixed costs → Burn reduction lever analysis (what to cut and in what order) → Revenue acceleration options → Fundraising trigger (start raising at 9 months runway) → Monthly burn review protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Default alive/dead analysis is non-negotiable knowledge
- Fundraising trigger at 9 months — not 3 months
- Burn reduction ordered by impact and pain — not arbitrary

Always offer a relevant next step.
