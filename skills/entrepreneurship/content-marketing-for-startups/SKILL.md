# Content Marketing for Startups Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage and audience
- Content creation capacity
- Distribution channels
- SEO vs audience building goal
- Competitors' content approach

## Step 2 — Core Output

Startup content strategy: Content-market fit (what content does your specific audience crave?) → Channel selection (one channel done well before expanding) → Content type selection (written/video/podcast — match to founder strengths) → SEO vs community content balance → Distribution tactics for small audiences → Content-to-pipeline mechanics → 90-day editorial calendar.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One channel before expanding — depth before breadth
- Content-to-pipeline mechanics explicit — content that doesn't convert wastes time
- 90-day calendar achievable for one-person team

Always offer a relevant next step.
