# Startup Hiring Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company stage
- Role to hire
- Founding team gaps
- Budget for hiring
- Culture and values

## Step 2 — Core Output

Startup hiring guide: First hire decision framework (hire to multiply your leverage) → Job description that attracts the right people → Equity vs salary calibration for stage → Interview process (fast but thorough: 3 rounds max) → Culture-add vs culture-fit distinction → Reference check that actually works → Offer letter elements → First 90 days onboarding plan → How to let someone go at a startup.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- First hire multiplies founder leverage — not just task executes
- Equity/salary calibration stage-appropriate
- Reference checks done — not skipped due to excitement

Always offer a relevant next step.
