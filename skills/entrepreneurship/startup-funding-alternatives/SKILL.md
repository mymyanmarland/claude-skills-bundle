# Startup Funding Alternatives Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model
- Revenue stage
- Funding needed
- Control priorities
- Growth timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Funding alternatives: Bootstrapping analysis (can you reach profitability without external capital?) → Revenue-based financing (when it fits) → Small business loans (what types, when applicable) → Government grants by industry and region → Crowdfunding (reward/equity — which fits) → Strategic investors (customers who invest) → Friends and family considerations → Convertible notes from angels. Each: pros/cons/fit/how to access.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each alternative honest about fit — not all work for all businesses
- Bootstrapping financial viability assessed — not assumed possible
- Grant sources specific — not vague 'look for grants'

Always offer a relevant next step.
