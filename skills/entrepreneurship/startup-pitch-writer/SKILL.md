# Startup Pitch Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Startup description and stage
- Problem and solution
- Traction and metrics
- Market size
- Funding ask

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pitch versions: 30-second elevator pitch → 2-minute verbal pitch → Full pitch deck narrative (slide-by-slide). All follow: Problem (make them feel it) → Solution → Market size → Traction → Business model → Team → Ask. Common pitch mistakes called out. Investor-specific language used. Compelling opening stat or story included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Problem made visceral — not just described
- Traction front and center — it's the most important slide
- Ask specific: amount, use of funds, milestones it achieves

Always offer a relevant next step.
