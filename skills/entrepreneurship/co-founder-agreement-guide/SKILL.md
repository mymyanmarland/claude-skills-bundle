# Co-Founder Agreement Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Co-founders involved
- Roles and contributions
- Equity split considerations
- Commitment levels
- Exit and scenario planning

## Step 2 — Core Output

Co-founder guide: The conversations to have before starting (vision/values/roles/commitment/equity/what-if scenarios) → Equity split framework (contribution + risk + opportunity cost) → Vesting schedule recommendation (4 years, 1-year cliff — non-negotiable) → Role clarity document → Disagreement resolution protocol → Founder exit scenarios → What to put in writing now (not later).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Vesting recommended strongly — always
- Equity split formula transparent and agreed
- Uncomfortable scenarios discussed before they happen

Always offer a relevant next step.
