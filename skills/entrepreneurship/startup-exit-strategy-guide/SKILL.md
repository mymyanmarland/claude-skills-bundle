# Startup Exit Strategy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stage and revenue
- Strategic acquirer landscape
- Investor expectations
- Founder goals post-exit
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Exit strategy guide: Exit types (acquisition/merger/IPO/PE/acquihire) → When to start thinking about exit (earlier than you think) → Strategic acquirer mapping → Financial acquirer vs strategic acquirer distinction → Deal readiness checklist (clean cap table, IP assigned, contracts in order) → Banker selection → Term sheet basics → Post-exit considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exit planning starts early — not just when approached
- Deal readiness checklist practical — many startups fail here
- Post-exit considerations include founder role and earnouts

Always offer a relevant next step.
