# Startup Metrics Dashboard Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business model (SaaS/marketplace/e-commerce/service)
- Stage (pre-revenue/early revenue/growth)
- Investor reporting needs
- Team size
- Current metrics tracked

## Step 2 — Core Output

Metrics dashboard: North Star metric (one number that captures value delivered) → Business model-specific metrics (SaaS: MRR growth, churn, CAC, LTV, NRR / marketplace: GMV, take rate, liquidity / e-commerce: revenue, AOV, repeat purchase) → Leading indicators (predict the North Star) → Weekly dashboard (5-7 metrics) → Investor update metrics → What NOT to track.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One North Star metric — not five
- Leading indicators tracked — not just lagging outcomes
- Metrics matched to business model — not generic startup metrics

Always offer a relevant next step.
