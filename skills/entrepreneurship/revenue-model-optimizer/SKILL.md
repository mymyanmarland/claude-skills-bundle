# Revenue Model Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current revenue model
- Revenue metrics
- Customer behavior
- Competition pricing
- Growth goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Revenue model analysis: Model types compared (subscription/usage/transaction/licensing/marketplace) → Predictability score → Scalability score → Margin analysis → Customer preference signals → Model evolution path (how to move from lower to higher quality revenue) → Pricing lever impact → Mixed model opportunities.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Predictability vs scalability trade-offs explicit
- Model evolution realistic — not jump straight to best model
- Mixed model opportunities — not either/or

Always offer a relevant next step.
