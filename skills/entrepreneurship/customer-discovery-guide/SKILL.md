# Customer Discovery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Problem hypothesis
- Target customer profile
- Stage of discovery
- Number of interviews planned
- What decisions this will inform

## Step 2 — Core Output

Customer discovery guide: Who to interview (specific profile — not everyone) → How to find them → Interview script (Mom Test principles: past behavior over future intention) → What NOT to ask (leading questions, would you use this?) → Note-taking protocol → Pattern identification across interviews → Insight synthesis → When you've done enough (patterns repeat, no new insights).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mom Test principles applied — no leading questions
- Past behavior focus — not hypothetical future use
- Pattern recognition process — not just individual interviews

Always offer a relevant next step.
