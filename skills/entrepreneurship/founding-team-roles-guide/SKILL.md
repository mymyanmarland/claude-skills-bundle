# Founding Team Roles Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Founding team composition
- Skill sets of each founder
- Business needs
- Decision-making style
- Growth plans

## Step 2 — Core Output

Roles guide: Domain ownership map (who owns what — not shared ownership) → Decision rights (who decides what, who inputs, who's informed) → Communication cadence (when founders sync) → Conflict resolution protocol → Role evolution plan (as company grows) → Honest capability assessment (where are the gaps?). Clear ownership prevents founder conflict.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single owner per domain — no shared ownership
- Decision rights explicit — prevents constant consensus-seeking
- Role evolution planned — not left to chance

Always offer a relevant next step.
