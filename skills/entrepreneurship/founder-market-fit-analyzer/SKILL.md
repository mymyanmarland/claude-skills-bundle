# Founder-Market Fit Analyzer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business idea
- Founder background and experience
- Domain knowledge
- Network in the space
- Personal connection to the problem

## Step 2 — Core Output

Founder-market fit analysis: Domain expertise (do you know something competitors don't?) → Network advantage (who do you know that creates distribution?) → Personal connection to problem (do you experience this pain?) → Unique insight (what do you believe that most people disagree with?) → Resilience test (will you stay when it's hard because of this?) → Gaps to address (what founder advantages are missing?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Honest assessment — not cheerleading
- Unfair advantages specific — not generic experience
- Gaps identified with paths to address or acknowledge them

Always offer a relevant next step.
