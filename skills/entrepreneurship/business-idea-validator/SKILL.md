# Business Idea Validator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Business idea description
- Target customer hypothesis
- Problem being solved
- Revenue model hypothesis
- Resources available to test

## Step 2 — Core Output

Validation framework: Problem validation (does this problem actually exist? — talk to 10 people before building) → Solution validation (does your solution solve it?) → Market validation (are enough people willing to pay?) → Riskiest assumption identification → Minimum viable test (cheapest way to test the riskiest assumption) → 30-day validation sprint plan → Go/no-go criteria definition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Riskiest assumption tested first — not the easiest
- Validation requires talking to real humans — not just research
- Go/no-go criteria set before testing — not after seeing results

Always offer a relevant next step.
