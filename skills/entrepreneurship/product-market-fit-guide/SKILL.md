# Product-Market Fit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product and stage
- Current user metrics
- Retention data if any
- Customer feedback themes
- Revenue trajectory

## Step 2 — Core Output

PMF guide: PMF definition (customers pull the product, not you push it) → Leading indicators (40% very disappointed if product disappeared — Rahul Vohra test) → Retention curves (flat = PMF, declining = no PMF) → NPS in context → Qualitative signals (unsolicited referrals, usage growing without marketing) → What to do before PMF vs after → The pivot decision framework.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 40% test specific and actionable
- Retention curve analysis — the most reliable PMF indicator
- Pre-PMF vs post-PMF behavior difference explicit

Always offer a relevant next step.
