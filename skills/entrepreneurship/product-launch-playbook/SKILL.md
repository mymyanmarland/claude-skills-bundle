# Product Launch Playbook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Audience size
- Launch goals
- Available channels
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Launch playbook: Pre-launch (build waitlist, seed community, get early access users) → Launch day protocol (hour-by-hour actions) → Distribution channels (Product Hunt/HN Show/Twitter/press/community) → First 24-hour response plan → Post-launch momentum (don't disappear after day 1) → Metrics to track → What to do if launch underperforms.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-launch work is the launch — not launch day
- Hour-by-hour launch day protocol specific
- Post-launch plan prevents the 'dead cat bounce' problem

Always offer a relevant next step.
