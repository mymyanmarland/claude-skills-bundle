# Venture Capital Primer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Funding stage
- Business model
- Previous funding knowledge
- Investor type considering (angel/seed/VC)
- Deal terms received or expected

## Step 2 — Core Output

VC primer: Fund mechanics (why VCs need big outcomes — 1000× return on some) → Stages (pre-seed/seed/Series A/B/C) → Term sheet basics (valuation/dilution/pro-rata/liquidation preference) → What VCs look for (market/team/product/traction) → The fundraising process (from first meeting to close) → Red flag terms to watch for → Bootstrapping vs VC decision framework.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Liquidation preference explained — critical but often misunderstood
- Bootstrapping vs VC decision included — VC not always right
- Red flag terms specific — not just 'read carefully'

Always offer a relevant next step.
