# Startup Community Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product type
- Target community member
- Platform preference
- Community goal
- Resources for moderation

## Step 2 — Core Output

Community strategy: Community purpose (why would people gather here?) → Platform selection → Community flywheel (how community creates product value creates community) → Seed member recruitment → Content and engagement calendar → Moderation guidelines → Community metrics → Monetization without ruining the community → Community-to-product feedback loop.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Purpose beyond product — community serves members first
- Flywheel creates sustainable growth — not just top-of-funnel
- Monetization approaches community health first

Always offer a relevant next step.
