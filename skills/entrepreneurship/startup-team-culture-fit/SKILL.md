# Startup Team Culture Fit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Company values and culture
- Role to hire
- Stage and pace
- Culture challenges already seen
- Culture problems to prevent

## Step 2 — Core Output

Culture fit guide: Values definition (specific behaviors — not words) → Culture-fit vs culture-add distinction (fit = shared values, add = diverse perspectives) → Interview questions that reveal values (behavioral, not hypothetical) → Reference check for culture signals → Onboarding culture transmission → Early culture misfit signals → The difficult decision to exit a culture misfit early.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Culture-add explicitly valued — diversity of thought protected
- Interview questions behavioral — not 'are you a team player'
- Early culture misfit addressed — the difficult conversation

Always offer a relevant next step.
