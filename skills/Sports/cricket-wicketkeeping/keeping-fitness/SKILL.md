# Wicketkeeper Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeping fitness: Squat and leg endurance (keeper squats hundreds of times in a day) → Lateral movement (side-to-side shuffle drills) → Glove reaction training (rebound net, varying feeds) → Core strength (diving, rising, bending all use core) → Concentration endurance (mental fatigue is as real as physical) → Hydration and nutrition for long stints → Recovery between sessions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leg endurance most important — hundreds of squats per day
- Concentration training as important as physical
- Hydration — keepers lose more sweat than they notice

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
