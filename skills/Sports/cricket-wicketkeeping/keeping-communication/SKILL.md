# Keeper Communication and Leadership Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeper communication: Talking to bowlers (encouraging, tactical, specific not vague) → Calling for no-balls → Alerting fielders to batter movement → Run-out communication (calling 'keeper's end' or 'bowler's end') → Talking batter out of concentration (legal gamesmanship — professional and not personal) → Captain communication (fielding suggestions, batter observations) → Keeping energy up in the field.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Communication specific — 'nice ball' less useful than 'keep that line'
- Run-out calls clear and loud — not assumed
- Captain communication — keeper sees more than anyone on the field

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
