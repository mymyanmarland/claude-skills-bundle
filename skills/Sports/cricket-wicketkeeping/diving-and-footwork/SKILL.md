# Keeping Footwork and Diving Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Footwork and diving: Lateral movement step (crossover step for wide takes) → Side step for slight deviation → Diving: push off outside foot, gloves extended, land on forearm not elbow → Getting back up quickly (first priority after a dive) → High catch footwork (push off back foot, get under the ball) → Coming up to stumps from back: moving in as the ball is bowled.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Push off outside foot for dive — inside foot collapses the dive
- Land on forearm — elbow landing causes injury
- Get back up immediately — cannot anticipate next ball on the ground

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
