# Keeping to Spin Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeping to spin: Standing up (close to stumps, gloves just outside off) → Reading the spinner's grip → Left-arm orthodox and off-spin (ball turns away — move to off side) → Leg-spin and chinaman (ball turns other way) → Stumping anticipation (watch feet) → Byes prevention (read the turn) → Communication with spinner (signal for variations).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Read the grip before delivery — not after
- Move early based on spin type — not reactive
- Stumping off the spinner: take then break — same rule applies

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
