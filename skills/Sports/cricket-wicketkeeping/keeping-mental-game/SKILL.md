# Wicketkeeper Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeper mental game: Ball-by-ball reset (between deliveries, mentally prepare) → Handling dropped catches (recover fast — next ball matters) → Concentration techniques (count deliveries, trigger phrases) → Staying engaged in long spells (talk to fielders, move between balls) → Managing dropped chances (team support vs self-criticism) → Pre-match routine (consistent preparation builds confidence).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reset between every ball — a keeper cannot afford lingering on mistakes
- Move physically between deliveries — stillness reduces alertness
- Pre-match routine same every game — reliability trains the mind

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
