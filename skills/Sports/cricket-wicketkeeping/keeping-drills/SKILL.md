# Wicketkeeping Drills Library Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeping drills: Solo (shadow keeping against a wall, reaction bounce drills, glove work in mirror) → Partner fed (short feeds to left and right, full tosses to dive for) → Rebound net (variable height and direction) → Spinner simulation (tennis ball with spin fed by partner) → Stumping practice (partner delivers, batter lifts foot late) → Competition drill (score points: clean take=1, stumping chance=3, dive save=2).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Solo drills build muscle memory before pressure
- Rebound net gives unpredictable angles — realistic preparation
- Competition scoring raises intensity in drills

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
