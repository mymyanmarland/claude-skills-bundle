# Stumping Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stumping: Anticipation (watch the batter's feet, not just the ball) → Take the ball first (stumping after clean take — not simultaneous) → Dislodging the bails (one bail off is enough — clean hit or sweep through) → Wide takes: move early, take ball, pivot, and break stumps → Down leg side stumpings (full dive, extend the glove) → Communication with bowler (slower balls, variations to create chances).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Take the ball first — then break the stumps, not simultaneously
- Anticipate the feet — stumping is anticipation not just reaction
- Down leg side — full commitment dive, not a reach

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
