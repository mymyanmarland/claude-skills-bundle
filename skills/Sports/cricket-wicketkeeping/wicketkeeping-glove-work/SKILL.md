# Wicketkeeping Glove Work Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glove work: Soft hands (give with the ball, cushion the catch) → Taking outside the off-stump (move laterally, gloves together) → Taking down leg side (dive with top hand leading) → High catches (gloves point up, fingers toward ball) → Low takes (scoop down, gloves pointing down) → Variable bounce (be ready to adjust late) → Taking the spinner (read the spin, position early).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soft hands absorb the ball — hard hands cause drops
- Move laterally to take the ball — not reaching across body
- Down the leg side: dive full length — do not reach

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
