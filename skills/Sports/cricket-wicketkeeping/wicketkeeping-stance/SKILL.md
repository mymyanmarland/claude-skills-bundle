# Wicketkeeping Stance and Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeping stance: Pace bowling stance (up to stumps or back — back position: weight on balls of feet, gloves low and wide, eyes level with bail height) → Spin bowling stance (up to stumps: close, gloves just outside off-stump) → Body height (bend from knees and hips, not the back) → Glove position (low and outside, ready to move in any direction) → Head position (eyes level, front shoulder toward bowler).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gloves low and wide — not in the lap
- Bend from knees not back — protects the spine long-term
- Up to stumps for spin — too far back loses stumping chances
