# Keeping to Pace Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Keeping to pace: Standing back distance (right depth — edge carries to gloves) → Ready position timed to bowler's delivery stride → Reading deviations (off the pitch) → Taking the outswinger (outside off, gloves in front of body) → Taking the inswinger (moving to leg side) → Judging the bouncer (watch for the short one) → Calling for no-balls (call audibly if front foot over).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Standing back depth: edge must carry — too close or too far both cause drops
- Ready position precisely timed to delivery stride
- Call no-balls loudly — helps the umpire

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
