# Boxing Recovery Methods Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing recovery: Cool-down (light shadow boxing or skipping, 10 min) → Ice packs for hands and face after sparring → Sleep (7-9 hours — primary recovery tool) → Cold and contrast bathing → Nutrition within 45 minutes → Active recovery sessions (easy jog, swimming) → Not sparring too frequently (hard sparring maximum twice per week) → Mental recovery (boxing is mentally demanding — allow full rest periods).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hard sparring maximum twice per week: more causes cumulative damage
- Sleep: most important recovery variable in boxing
- Never spar when fatigued: most sparring injuries happen when tired

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
