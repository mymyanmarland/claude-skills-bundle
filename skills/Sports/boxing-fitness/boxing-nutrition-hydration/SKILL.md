# Boxing Nutrition and Hydration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing training volume and intensity
- Competition schedule if any
- Current nutrition habits
- Weight class considerations
- Any dietary restrictions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing nutrition: Daily nutrition (carbohydrates for training energy, protein for recovery, fats for hormonal health) → Pre-training meal (carbs 2-3 hours before) → Post-training recovery (protein and carbs within 45 min) → Competition day (familiar foods, no experiments) → Hydration (urine colour target: pale yellow) → Healthy weight management (gradual, not extreme cutting — extreme cutting is dangerous and strongly discouraged). Always consult a registered sports dietitian for personalised advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gradual weight management only: extreme cutting is dangerous and not promoted here
- Competition day: no new foods — familiar and tested only
- Urine colour monitoring: pale yellow is hydrated, dark yellow is dehydrated

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
