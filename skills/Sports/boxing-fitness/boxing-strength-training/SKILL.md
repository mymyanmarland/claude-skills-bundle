# Strength Training for Boxers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxer strength training: Power development (trap bar jumps, medicine ball throws — explosive, not slow lifting) → Rotational strength (cable rotations, landmine rotations — punching power) → Core (anti-rotation, anti-extension — protect and transfer power) → Lower body (trap bar deadlift, split squat — force production) → Upper body push/pull balance (prevent shoulder injuries) → Avoid excessive hypertrophy (bulk slows movement and pushes up weight class).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Explosive power over slow strength: boxing requires speed not just force
- Rotational core: the majority of punch power is generated through rotation
- Avoid bulk: added muscle mass that reduces movement speed is counterproductive

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
