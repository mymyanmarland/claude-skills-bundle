# Core Training for Boxers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing core training: Punching power transfer (core connects lower body power to the punch) → Medicine ball work (rotational throws, wall ball) → Plank variations (standard, side plank, plank with rotation) → Hanging leg raises → Cable rotations → Ab conditioning (body shots in sparring expose core weakness) → Anti-rotation (Pallof press — resisting rotation is as important as creating it).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rotational medicine ball throws: the most boxing-specific core exercise
- Anti-rotation training: protects the spine during punching
- Body shots: a weak core means a body shot stops you — train it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
