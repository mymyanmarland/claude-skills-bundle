# Boxing Warm-Up Protocol Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing warm-up: General warm-up (5 min easy jog or skip) → Dynamic mobility (shoulder circles, hip rotations, neck rolls) → Shadow boxing (2 rounds — technical, not hard — activate the movement patterns) → Hand wrapping (always wrapping before bag or sparring work) → Specific activation (rotational movements, explosive punches at 50%, then 75%) → Total warm-up: 20-25 minutes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shadow boxing in warm-up: activates the specific movement patterns needed
- Hand wrapping: wrap before every contact session — not optional
- 20-25 minute warm-up: boxing training puts high demand on joints

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
