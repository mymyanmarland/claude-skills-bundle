# Boxing for Fitness Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing for fitness: Benefits (cardiovascular, full body strength, coordination, stress relief, confidence) → Fitness boxing vs competitive boxing (no sparring needed for fitness) → What to focus on (bag work, pad work, skipping, circuits) → Class formats (boxercise, boxing fitness classes) → Solo workouts (shadow boxing + bag + skip) → Progression (technique first, then conditioning) → Equipment for home training (bag, gloves, wraps, rope).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- No sparring required: most fitness benefits come without contact
- Technique first: correct form prevents injury and improves results
- Boxing for fitness is safe and accessible: no competitive boxing required

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
