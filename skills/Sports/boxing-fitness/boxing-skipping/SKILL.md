# Boxing Skipping (Jump Rope) Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing skipping: Basic two-footed skip (establish rhythm before advancing) → Alternate foot skip (more boxing-specific footwork pattern) → Boxer step (side-to-side footwork pattern while skipping) → Double under (passes rope under feet twice per jump — conditioning) → Skipping rounds (3-minute rounds with 1-minute rest) → Skipping workout as warm-up (10 min) or conditioning session (30 min) → Rope length and surface.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Alternate foot skip: most boxing-specific — replicates in-ring footwork
- 3-minute rounds: same as boxing rounds — trains specific rhythm
- Rhythm before complexity: consistent basic skip before advanced patterns

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
