# Boxing Speed Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing speed: Relaxation (tense muscles slow punches — stay relaxed until impact) → Speed bag (rhythm, hand-eye coordination, fast repetition) → Double-end bag (react to the bag moving — develops timing and speed) → Speed shadowboxing (fast combinations with full guard maintenance) → Reflex ball (attached to headband — unpredictable movement) → Light resistance training (slight resistance during shadow boxing builds speed endurance) → Reaction drills with a partner.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Relaxation is the source of speed: tension kills punch velocity
- Speed bag: builds rhythm and fast hand movement simultaneously
- Double-end bag: the most reaction-specific boxing training tool

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
