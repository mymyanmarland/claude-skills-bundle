# Boxing Injury Prevention Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing injury prevention: Hand wrapping (correctly wrapping protects knuckles, wrists, and thumb) → Glove selection (correct weight for training vs sparring) → Wrist strength (wrist curls, rice bucket work) → Shoulder health (rotator cuff prehab) → Headguard use (always in sparring) → Mouthguard fit (custom is better than generic) → Not overtaining (fatigue leads to poor form and injury) → Regular medical check-ups for active boxers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hand wrapping: incorrect wrapping causes wrist injuries in heavy bag work
- Headguard in all sparring: no exceptions
- Not overtraining: tired boxers have poor form — poor form causes injury

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
