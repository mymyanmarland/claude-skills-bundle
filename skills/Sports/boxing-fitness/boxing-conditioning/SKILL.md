# Boxing Conditioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing conditioning: Round-based intervals (3 min on / 1 min rest — replicates the sport) → Skipping (continuous and interval skipping — footwork and conditioning combined) → Circuit training (bag / pads / skip / floor work in 3-minute stations) → Hill sprints (short, explosive — replicates burst efforts) → Aerobic base (zone 2 running twice per week — supports recovery between rounds) → Sparring rounds as conditioning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Round-based conditioning: body adapts specifically to 3-1 rhythm
- Skipping: the most boxing-specific conditioning tool
- Aerobic base: without it recovery between rounds is insufficient

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
