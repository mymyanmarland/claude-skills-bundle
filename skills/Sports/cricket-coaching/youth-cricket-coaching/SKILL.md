# Youth Cricket Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Youth coaching: Fun-first philosophy (if it is not fun they will not come back) → Age-appropriate activities (under-10: games and basic skills / under-13: technique and competition / under-16: tactical) → LTAD (Long-Term Athlete Development) principles for cricket → Session structure (warm-up game / skill focus / game situation / cool-down) → Modified cricket formats (Kwik Cricket, Last Man Stands) → Encouraging all abilities → Managing parents on the sideline.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fun-first — bored or scared kids leave the sport
- Age-appropriate activities — 8-year-olds do not need tactical lectures
- Modified formats make cricket accessible to all body types

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
