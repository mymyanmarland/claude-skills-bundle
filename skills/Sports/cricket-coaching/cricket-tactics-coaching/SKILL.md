# Cricket Tactics and Game Management Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tactical coaching: Reading the game (conditions/pitch/weather/opposition) → Batting orders and match situations → Bowling rotations and matchups → Field-setting for different batters and phases → Decision-making frameworks (aggressive vs conservative risk assessment) → Time management in Test cricket → Run rate management in limited-overs → Teaching players to make decisions on the field.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Conditions read before tactics applied — not the other way
- Bowling matchups (left/right batter combinations) → most overlooked tactic
- Teach players to read the game — not just follow instructions

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
