# Cricket Coaching Qualifications Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching qualifications: ECB/Cricket Australia/PCB/BCCI qualification pathways → Level 1 (introductory, working with beginners) → Level 2 (developing players, session planning) → Level 3 (high performance, elite coaching) → Safeguarding and child protection requirements (mandatory in all pathways) → First aid requirements → Continuing professional development → Mentoring from experienced coaches.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safeguarding certification mandatory — not optional for any coach
- Start with Level 1 regardless of playing experience
- CPD ongoing — coaching knowledge evolves constantly

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
