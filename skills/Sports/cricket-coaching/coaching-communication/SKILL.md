# Coaching Communication Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching communication: Questioning over telling (ask what they noticed rather than telling what was wrong) → Positive-constructive-positive feedback structure → Individual communication styles (some players need directness, some need encouragement) → Body language awareness → Dealing with parents → Managing conflict in a team → Giving difficult feedback (dropping a player, addressing poor behaviour) → Active listening.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Questions before instructions — players learn more from discovering
- Match communication style to individual player
- Difficult feedback delivered privately and respectfully

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
