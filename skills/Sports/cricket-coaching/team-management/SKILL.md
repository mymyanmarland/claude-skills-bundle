# Team Management Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team management: Roles and responsibilities (captain vs coach clarity) → Team culture (non-negotiables agreed early) → Selection communication (honest and respectful) → Managing squad dynamics (players in and out of team) → Pre-match preparation (team meetings, tactical briefings) → Half-time and mid-session communication → Post-match debrief (learning not blaming) → Handling poor performances as a group.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Captain vs coach roles clear — overlap creates conflict
- Selection communication honest — being dropped is hard, being lied to is worse
- Post-match debrief within 24 hours — while memory is fresh

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
