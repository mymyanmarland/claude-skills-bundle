# Coaching Batting Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching batting: Fault identification framework (observe stance → grip → backlift → footwork → contact point → follow-through) → Common faults and fixes (falling over: head position / late movement: footwork trigger / bottom hand dominant: grip and top arm drills) → Demonstration vs verbal cue (show first, then cue) → Individualisation (not all batters should look the same) → Video analysis basics → Drills matched to fault.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Observe all phases before diagnosing — one fault often masks another
- Show before telling — visual demonstration faster than description
- Individualise within principles — elite batters all look different

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
