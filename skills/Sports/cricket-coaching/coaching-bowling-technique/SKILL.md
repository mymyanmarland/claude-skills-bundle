# Coaching Bowling Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching bowling: Action assessment (run-up / gather / bound / delivery stride / release / follow-through) → Common faults (no-balls: over the line / wides: release angle / loss of pace: front arm drops) → Reporting and fixing suspect actions (ICC guidelines awareness) → Load management for young bowlers → Bowling workload records → Developing variations after basics are sound.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Action must be sound before working on variations
- Workload records essential for under-18 bowlers — injury prevention
- Suspect action protocol: never coach around it, seek specialist assessment

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
