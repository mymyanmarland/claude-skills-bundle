# Player Development Pathways Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Development pathways: LTAD stages for cricket (FUNdamentals / Learning to Play / Training to Play / Training to Compete / Training to Win) → Skill prioritisation by stage → Competition vs training ratio by age → Identifying talented players → Transition from junior to senior cricket → Managing multi-sport athletes → Parent education on development timelines.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- LTAD principles match physical and cognitive development stages
- Competition ratio low for young players — too much competition too early
- Multi-sport participation up to 16 is advantageous — not a distraction

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
