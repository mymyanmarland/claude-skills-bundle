# Coaching Fielding Sessions Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fielding sessions: Competition-based drills (points system keeps energy high) → Station rotation (every player does every skill) → Position-specific work (slips, close catchers, outfield) → Game simulation (bat and field — real pressure) → Fun warm-up games (reaction games, catching games) → Rewarding effort not just clean execution → Debrief after errors (quick, positive, move on).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Competition in every drill — pure repetition is boring
- Effort rewarded — a bad dive attempt is better than not trying
- Debrief brief and positive — not a lecture after every drop

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
