# Cricket Session Planning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Session planning: Session goal (one main skill per session — not everything) → Warm-up (game-based, 10 min) → Skill station 1 (batting or bowling focus, 20 min) → Skill station 2 (fielding, 15 min) → Game situation (apply the skill in a game context, 15 min) → Cool-down and reflection (5 min) → Equipment prep → Numbers management (station rotation to maximise touches).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One main skill per session — trying to cover everything covers nothing
- Maximise ball touches — players should not stand in lines
- Game context every session — skills applied under pressure

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
