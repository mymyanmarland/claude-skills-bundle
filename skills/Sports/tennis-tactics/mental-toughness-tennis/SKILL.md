# Mental Toughness Development for Tennis Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mental toughness: Pressure handling (breathe, slow down, play simple) → Momentum management (stay even after both good and bad runs) → Tiebreak mindset (every point equal — not more pressure on 6-6) → Injury and adversity resilience → Post-bad-match recovery → Resilience after losing a lead → Building a competitive routine.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tiebreak: treat every point as equal — extra pressure causes errors
- Momentum: same between-point routine regardless of score
- Post-bad-match: analyse then release — do not carry it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
