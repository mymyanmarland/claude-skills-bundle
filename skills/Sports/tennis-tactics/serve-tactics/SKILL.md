# Serve Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Serve tactics: Three targets on the deuce side (wide, body, T) → Three targets on the ad side (wide, body, T) → Serving to the weakness (repeatedly target the backhand) → Pattern serving (serve wide, get a weak return, attack the open court) → Second serve strategy (not just getting it in — place it) → Ace vs forcing error (at what level is the ace realistic?) → Adjusting serve based on opponent's return position.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Body serve: most underused serve target — jams the opponent
- Serve + pattern: serve wide, hit next ball opposite direction
- Second serve: still aim for a target — not just 'in the box'

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
