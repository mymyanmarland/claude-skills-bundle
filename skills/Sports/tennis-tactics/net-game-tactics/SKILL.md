# Net Game Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Net game tactics: When to approach (short ball, opponent out of position, serve-volley) → Approach shot target (down the line to reduce passing angle) → Closing at net (split step close to service line, not service box) → Passing shot defence (cover down the line first, then cross-court) → Lob response (if lobbed, overhead or retreat) → Poaching in doubles vs singles.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Approach down the line: reduces the passing shot angle significantly
- Close after the approach: do not stop at the service line
- Down the line first: the most dangerous passing shot for a net rusher

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
