# Court Surface Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Court surface tactics: Clay (high bounce, slow — longer rallies, topspin, patience, slide into shots) → Grass (low bounce, fast — serve dominant, approach net, shorter rallies, slice) → Hard court (medium pace — balanced game, all tactics apply) → Movement adaptation (sliding on clay is a learned skill) → Serve importance by surface (grass: most important / hard: important / clay: least dominant).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clay: slide into wide balls — standing still costs you time
- Grass: approach net more than any other surface
- Hard court: most balanced — develop all aspects of the game

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
