# Baseline Tactical Patterns Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Baseline patterns: Cross-court to cross-court (build consistency, wait for short ball) → Open-up pattern (hit wide to backhand, attack the open court to the forehand side) → Inside-out forehand (run around the backhand, attack with forehand) → Change-of-direction (wrong-foot the opponent) → High topspin to backhand (push opponent deep and defensive) → Drop shot (when opponent is pushed deep and running hard).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cross-court baseline rally: build pressure before attacking
- Inside-out forehand: the most powerful pattern in modern tennis
- Drop shot timing: only when opponent is on back foot, not as a first option
