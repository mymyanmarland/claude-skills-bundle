# Advanced Tennis Strokes Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced shots: Drop shot (disguise as groundstroke, slow racket at contact, slice spin, land close to net) → Lob (topspin lob for offensive use / defensive lob for depth and time) → Passing shot (down the line or crosscourt — read the net player) → High backhand (for high bouncing balls — contact above shoulder) → Swinging volley (midcourt ball, full swing, put away) → Half-volley (difficult — use as a last resort, guide into court).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Drop shot disguise: the shot must look like a groundstroke until contact
- Topspin lob: most difficult but most effective over the net player
- Passing shot crosscourt: higher percentage than down the line

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
