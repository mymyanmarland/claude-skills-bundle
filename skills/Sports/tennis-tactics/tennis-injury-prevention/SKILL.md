# Tennis Injury Prevention Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tennis injury prevention: Tennis elbow prevention (forearm strengthening, correct grip size, check technique for leading with elbow) → Shoulder health (rotator cuff exercises, scapular stability) → Knee health (eccentric quad and glute work) → Ankle stability (proprioception training on unstable surfaces) → Warm-up protocol (dynamic, specific) → Recovery between sessions → Load management (not increasing volume more than 10% per week).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tennis elbow: grip size check first — incorrect grip is a major cause
- Rotator cuff prehab: 10 minutes per week prevents months off
- 10% volume rule: applies to match play and training combined

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
