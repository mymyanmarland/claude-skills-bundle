# Tennis Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tennis coaching: Lesson structure (warm-up / technical focus / point play / cool-down) → Feed types (hand feed / racket feed for different purposes) → Competitive drills (score rallies — competition raises quality) → Technical correction (change one thing at a time) → LTAD in tennis (10 and under with modified equipment, building adult game) → Group lessons vs individual lessons.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Modified equipment for juniors: low compression balls make learning faster
- Technical correction: one change at a time — multiple changes cause regression
- Competitive feeding: scoring even practice drills improves quality

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
