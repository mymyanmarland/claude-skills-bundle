# Serve Receive Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Serve receive: W formation (five-player, balanced) → U formation (four-player, libero centre) → Three-player (two passers and libero) → Two-player (elite — specialists pass everything) → Read the server before moving → Adjust position after serve contact.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Read before moving: moving before contact gets caught by a different serve
- W formation is safest default for developing teams
- Protect weak passers: design system around best passers
