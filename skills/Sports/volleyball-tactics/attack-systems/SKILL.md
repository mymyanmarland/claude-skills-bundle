# Volleyball Attack Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Attack systems: 5-1 (one setter, five potential attackers) → 6-2 (two setters, always three front-row attackers) → Quick attacks (A/B/C ball) → Combination plays (crossing attackers confuse blockers) → Pipe (back row from position 6) → Attack selection from pass quality.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 5-1 vs 6-2: choose based on setter quality and team composition
- Quick sets require precise passing — poor pass eliminates options
- Combination plays require drilling — poorly timed is worse than simple

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
