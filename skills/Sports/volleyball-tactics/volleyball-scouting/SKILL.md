# Volleyball Scouting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scouting: Serve tendencies (where each server targets) → Attack preferences → Passing weaknesses → Setter tendencies → Blocking patterns → Statistical tracking during match → Adjustments from findings.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Setter tendencies: knowing first-ball preference limits attacker's options
- Serve the weak passer: forces setter into poor positions
- In-match adjustments: scouting only useful if the team responds

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
