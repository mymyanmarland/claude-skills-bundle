# Volleyball Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volleyball coaching: Session structure (warm-up/technique/tactical/competition/debrief) → Position-specific development → Maximise ball contacts → Game-based learning → Video analysis → Competition within training → Youth: emphasise passing and setting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maximise ball contacts: lines are the enemy of development
- Game-based: most volleyball skills develop best in game-like situations
- Youth foundation: passing and setting ability determines the ceiling

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
