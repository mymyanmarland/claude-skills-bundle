# Beach Volleyball Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beach volleyball: Two-player system → Wind awareness (serve and attack with wind) → Sun awareness → Line vs angle shots → Pokey and cobra (knuckle and open-hand shots — legal in beach) → Communication essential across the whole court.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wind: serve downwind — ball movement adds difficulty
- Sun: position so it is not in your eyes during serve receive
- Line vs angle: read the blocker's position

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
