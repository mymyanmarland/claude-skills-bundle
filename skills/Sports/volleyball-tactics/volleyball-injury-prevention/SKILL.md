# Volleyball Injury Prevention Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volleyball injury prevention: Jump-landing mechanics (soft, knee over toe — ACL prevention) → Shoulder prehab (rotator cuff: bands, external rotation) → Ankle stability (previous sprain increases re-sprain — proprioception) → Knee health (quad and glute strength) → Finger buddy taping → Attack volume load management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Landing mechanics: ACL injury is the most devastating volleyball injury
- Shoulder prehab weekly: every hitting session should include it
- Ankle proprioception: a sprained ankle not fully rehabilitated will be resprained

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
