# Volleyball Transition Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Transition: Coverage after attack (surround hitter, ready for block touch) → Dig-set-attack (three-touch objective) → Setter transition (call early, run to target) → First tempo transition attack (quick middle when perfect set unavailable).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Coverage: return to defensive position — do not watch your attack
- Setter call: must call for ball or dig goes nowhere
- Three-touch discipline: do not take two when three is better

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
