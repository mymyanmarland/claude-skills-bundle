# Volleyball Blocking Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Blocking: Read blocking (watch setter, move to attack — safer) → Commit blocking (choose attack before set — riskier but effective against quick) → Middle blocker moves after setter contacts ball → Two-person block coordination → Block touch mentality (every touch counts).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Read vs commit: read blocking appropriate for most non-elite teams
- Middle blocker timing: must move immediately after setter contacts
- Block touch: redirected ball handled by defence equals a kill-block
