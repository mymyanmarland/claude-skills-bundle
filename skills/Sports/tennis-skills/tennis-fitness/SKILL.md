# Tennis Fitness Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tennis fitness: Movement speed (lateral shuffle, split-step reaction, diagonal movements) → Strength (rotational core for groundstrokes, legs for movement, shoulder stability) → Aerobic base (matches can last 3+ hours) → Anaerobic conditioning (5-10 second points with 20-25 second recovery) → Flexibility (shoulder, hip, thoracic spine) → Injury prevention (rotator cuff, wrist, knee).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lateral movement drills: more time-effective than straight-line running
- Rotational core: the source of groundstroke power
- Shoulder prehab: rotator cuff injury ends seasons

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
