# Tennis Volley Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volley: Continental grip (same for forehand and backhand volley) → Ready position at net (split step, racket in front, high) → Punch not swing (short compact motion, block the ball) → Forehand volley (step across, punch forward) → Backhand volley (step across, firm wrist) → High volley vs low volley (different contact points) → Overhead smash (similar to serve, turn sideways, point at ball) → Drop volley (soft hands, absorb pace).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Punch not swing: big swing at net causes errors
- Continental grip: allows both forehand and backhand from same grip
- Split step timing: just before opponent's contact — not after

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
