# Tennis Return of Serve Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Return of serve: Position (stand where comfortable — adjust based on opponent's serve) → Split step timing (as opponent tosses, split step) → Short backswing (use the opponent's pace on fast first serves) → Aggressive return of second serve (step in, attack) → Return targets (crosscourt is the percentage return) → Chip and charge → Lob return (when pulled wide) → Blocking back fast serves.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short backswing on first serve: use the pace already in the ball
- Step in on second serve: go on offence immediately
- Crosscourt return: the highest percentage target in tennis

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
