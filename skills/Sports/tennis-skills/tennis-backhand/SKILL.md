# Tennis Backhand Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Backhand: Two-handed (non-dominant hand does the work, dominant guides — unit turn, contact in front, follow-through high across body) → One-handed (Eastern backhand grip, full shoulder turn, high takeback, swing out and up, finish extended) → Slice backhand (high-to-low swing, open racket face, controlled depth) → When to use slice vs topspin → Backhand return of serve.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Non-dominant hand drives 2HBH: it is not just a support hand
- One-handed: full shoulder turn is non-negotiable
- Slice backhand: keep it low and deep — not short and sittable

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
