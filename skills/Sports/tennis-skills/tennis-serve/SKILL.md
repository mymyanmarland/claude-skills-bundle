# Tennis Serve Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Serve technique: Grip (Continental — the foundation for all serve variations) → Toss (in front and slightly to the right for right-hander, consistent height) → Trophy position (weight on back foot, racket back, toss up) → Leg drive (push up with legs — power comes from legs not arm) → Contact point (highest reach, slightly in front) → Pronation (racket face rotates through the ball) → Flat vs slice vs kick (contact point and swing path variations).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Continental grip: only grip that allows all serve variations
- Toss consistency: practice toss without hitting 100 times
- Leg drive: players who skip the legs lose 30% of serve power

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
