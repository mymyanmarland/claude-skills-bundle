# Tennis Singles Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Singles tactics: Rally cross-court (safest shot — lowest net, longest court) → Attack short balls (approach down the line) → Opening the court (move opponent wide, hit behind them) → Serve + 1 (serve to a target, hit the next ball to the open court) → Return + 1 (return crosscourt, attack the next ball) → Exploiting weaknesses (target the backhand, the short ball, the serve) → Patterns: serve wide-forehand, serve body-backhand.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Crosscourt rally: 20% more court than down the line
- Attack short balls: step in, hit with pace and direction
- Serve + 1: the most practised tactic by top players

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
