# Tennis Forehand Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Forehand technique: Grip (Eastern or Semi-Western — Semi-Western for topspin) → Ready position (split step as opponent strikes) → Unit turn (shoulder and hip rotation together, early preparation) → Low-to-high swing path (brushing up the back of the ball = topspin) → Contact point (out in front, at comfortable hitting zone height) → Follow-through (over the non-dominant shoulder) → Recovery (back to ready position immediately).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Unit turn early: preparation before the ball reaches you
- Low-to-high swing path: the source of topspin
- Recovery immediately: never watch your shot — get back to position

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
