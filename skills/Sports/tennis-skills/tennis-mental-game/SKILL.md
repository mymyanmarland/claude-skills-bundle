# Tennis Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tennis mental game: Between-point routine (consistent — walk to the baseline, reorganise, focus word) → Handling unforced errors (acknowledge, reset, move on — 20 seconds max) → Playing the big points (breathe, slow down, play percentage) → Managing nerves (normal physiological response — channel it, not suppress it) → Win the next point mindset (not the next game or set) → Body language (strong body language independent of results).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Between-point routine same every time: trains the nervous system
- 20-second reset: longer dwelling keeps the negative alive
- Win the next point: the only point you can control

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
