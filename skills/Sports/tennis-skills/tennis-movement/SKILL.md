# Tennis Movement and Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tennis movement: Split step (every time opponent strikes the ball — the foundation of all movement) → Crossover step (for balls wide and far) → Shuffle step (for small adjustments and recovery) → Recovery steps (back to base position after every shot) → Approach shot footwork (approach and split step at net) → Anticipation (read the opponent's body, not just the ball) → Spider drill and cone work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Split step: the single most important movement habit in tennis
- Recovery: never watch your shot — move immediately
- Anticipation: read the opponent's shoulder and hip rotation

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
