# Tennis Doubles Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Doubles tactics: Positioning (server's partner at net, returner's partner at baseline) → Poaching (net player crosses to cut off the return) → I-formation (both players on same side before serve) → Australian formation → Communication (poach signals before serve) → Return tactics (return at the net player's feet) → Lob defence (both players retreat or one covers).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Poaching: communicate with serve partner before every point
- I-formation: varies the angle, confuses the returner
- Return low at net player: keeps them from attacking
