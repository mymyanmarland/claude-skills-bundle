# Referee Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Referee fitness: Sport-specific demands (football referee: 10-12km per match / rugby referee: 8-10km with frequent direction changes) → Aerobic base (sustain activity for full match duration — 90 minutes football / 80 minutes rugby) → Sprint ability (referee must be at key moments — get there before play) → Agility (repeated direction changes) → Fitness tests (RIFAL test for football / Yo-Yo test — know your sport's standard) → In-season maintenance (referee 2-3 times per week plus structured fitness training).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Get there before play: a referee who arrives late cannot see the incident
- Fitness tests: most governing bodies require minimum fitness standards — know yours
- In-season maintenance: match officiating alone does not maintain fitness

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
