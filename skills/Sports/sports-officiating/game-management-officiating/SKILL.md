# Game Management for Officials Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game management: Preventive officiating (anticipate and prevent flashpoints before they ignite) → Communication (calm, clear, brief — 'my decision is X because Y' once, not repeatedly) → Consistency (same decision for the same offence throughout the match) → Managing dissent (address it early, calmly, firmly) → Player management (know the name or number, speak privately) → Carding decisions (yellow card is a management tool — use it before it escalates to red) → Post-match.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Preventive officiating: address situations before they become incidents
- Consistency: inconsistency erodes respect faster than an incorrect call
- Yellow card is a management tool: use it before situations escalate

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
