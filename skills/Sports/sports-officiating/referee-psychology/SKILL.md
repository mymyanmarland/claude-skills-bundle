# Referee Psychology Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Referee psychology: Dealing with dissent (do not take it personally — player frustration is usually not about you) → Mistake management (every official makes errors — short memory is essential) → Pre-match preparation (consistent routine — mental readiness) → Concentration maintenance (the focus required for 90+ minutes) → Post-match review (objective analysis — what was good, what would I change) → Seeking feedback (grow from assessors and experienced mentors) → Managing bias awareness (recognising and correcting for unconscious patterns).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Do not take dissent personally: players react to the decision not to you as a person
- Short memory for mistakes: carrying the last error leads to over-compensation
- Assessor feedback: the fastest development tool for officials

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
