# Officiating Signals and Communication Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Officiating signals: Sport-specific signal library (each sport has standardised signals — learn them all) → Signal clarity (large, confident, held until acknowledged) → Verbal communication alongside signals (clear, brief, factual: 'number 7, holding') → Advantage signal (arm forward — intention clear before the play develops) → The score gesture (goal/try/point — clear and visible to all) → Communication hierarchy (on-field referee is primary — all others defer) → Technology communication (earpiece protocol — when to speak and when to listen).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clear confident signals: tentative signals create confusion
- Brief factual verbal: player name or number plus offence — not a lecture
- Advantage signal before calling: communicate intention early

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
