# Officiating Youth Sport Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Youth sport officiating: Different priorities (development not performance — the official supports learning) → Teaching moments (explain decisions calmly to young players — they are learning the laws) → Managing parents (often harder than managing the players) → Tone of voice (encouraging and calm — not authoritarian) → Fewer strict law applications (the spirit of the game matters more at youth level) → Safeguarding awareness (officials are also responsible for welfare) → Parent management protocols.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Youth officiating is developmental: explaining decisions is part of the role
- Parent management: a calm word early prevents sideline problems later
- Safeguarding awareness: officials are part of the welfare network — know the reporting procedure

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
