# Video Technology in Officiating Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video technology: VAR principles (review only: clear and obvious errors — not re-officiating the game) → Reviewable categories (goal/offside/penalty/red card — sport-specific) → On-field review (when the referee goes to the monitor — their final decision) → Communication with VAR team (protocols for ear contact and recommendations) → Time management (long reviews disrupt flow — protocols for efficiency) → Communication with players (brief explanation where protocols allow) → Limitations (VAR does not remove all controversy).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- VAR reviews clear and obvious errors: it is not a second officiating of every decision
- On-field review: the referee who goes to the monitor makes the final call
- Communicate calmly: players want to understand — brief explanation reduces frustration

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
