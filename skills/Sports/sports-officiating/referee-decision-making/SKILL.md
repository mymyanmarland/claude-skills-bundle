# Referee Decision-Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Referee decision-making: Positioning (the right angle and distance from play — most missed decisions are positional errors) → Pre-decision preparation (anticipate where play is going — be there before it arrives) → Decision confidence (decide and communicate — hesitation invites dissent) → The 80% decision (most decisions are clear — focus cognitive energy on the 20% that are not) → Advantage (applying advantage correctly — only when the disadvantaged team benefits) → Communication with other officials → Not chasing the game (stay composed after a mistake).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Positioning: the most important skill in officiating — most errors are positional
- Decide and communicate: hesitation invites dissent more than the decision itself
- Advantage: apply it when the team clearly benefits — not automatically
