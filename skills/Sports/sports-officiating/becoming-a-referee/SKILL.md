# Becoming a Sports Official Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Becoming an official: Entry pathway (most sports have a beginner course — typically a weekend) → Mentoring (most governing bodies pair new officials with experienced mentors) → Development through officiating (quantity and variety of games builds experience) → Assessment (regular assessments by senior officials — use them as development tools) → Progressing the grades (each level requires assessments and minimum game counts) → The community (officiating has a strong community — engage with it) → Why officials are vital (without officials there is no sport).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Start early in your career: many ex-players become excellent officials
- Mentoring: learn from watching experienced officials — not just from being on the pitch
- Assessment: treat assessors as development allies not judges

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
