# Assistant Referee and Linesperson Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport being officiated
- Current officiating level
- Specific officiating challenges
- Training and development access
- Competition level being officiated

## Step 2 — Core Output

Assistant referee: Role clarity (the AR supports the referee — final decisions rest with the referee) → Flag technique (clear, decisive, held up until the referee acknowledges) → Offside positioning (in line with the second-to-last defender — always) → Communication with the lead official (eye contact, signals, earpiece if available) → Staying in line (the AR must track the defensive line — anticipation not reaction) → When to flag vs not (flag for clear infringements — doubt stays with the referee).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stay in line with the second-to-last defender: the most common AR positioning error
- Flag decisively and hold: a tentative flag is ignored
- The lead referee decides: the AR signals, the referee decides

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
