# Volleyball Defence and Digging Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Defence: Low ready position, weight forward, eyes on hitter → Read hitter (shoulder angle) → Dig platform (absorb ball, control direction) → Diving (forearm slide — court must be safe) → Roll technique → Court positioning by system.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Read the hitter: shoulder angle reveals 80% of attack direction
- Diving: forearm slide — absorb on arms not face
- Low position: upright defender cannot get low fast enough

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
