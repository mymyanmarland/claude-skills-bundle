# The Libero Role Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Libero: Rules (different colour jersey / cannot serve, set front row, spike above net / free substitution) → Responsibilities (passing specialist / digging leader / communication hub) → Back row attack zone 6 (legal if below net) → Setting from behind attack line → Communication (calling for balls, directing blockers).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Libero rules: no spike above net and no front-row substitution
- Most serves target the libero: elite passing is required
- Communication: the libero directs traffic from the back row
