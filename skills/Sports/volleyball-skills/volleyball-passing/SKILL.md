# Volleyball Passing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Forearm passing: Platform (flat forearms, wrists together, elbows extended) → Body position (low, feet shoulder-width, arms in front) → Move to ball (position behind it — do not reach) → Contact on forearms above wrist → Angle platform to setter → Shuffle step to ball.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Platform angle directs ball: flat platform sends it straight up
- Move to ball: reaching causes erratic passes
- Body low before contact: upright means no control

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
