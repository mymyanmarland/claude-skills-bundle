# Volleyball Blocking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Blocking: Hands up at net, close to tape → Lateral movement (step-close or shuffle) → Jump timing (slightly after attacker) → Hands angle inward toward the court → Penetrate over the net → Read setter (shoulder angle reveals set direction).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Jump slightly after: jumping with the toss means you are down too early
- Reach over: not penetrating the plane is a net ball
- Read the setter: shoulder direction reveals the set
