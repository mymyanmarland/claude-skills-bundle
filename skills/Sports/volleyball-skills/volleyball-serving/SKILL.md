# Volleyball Serving Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volleyball serving: Underhand serve (pendulum swing from below) → Float serve (no spin, stiff wrist, ball floats unpredictably) → Topspin serve (snap at contact, ball drops quickly) → Jump serve → Serve placement (corners and seams between passers) → Six rotation serving zones.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Float serve: no wrist snap — spinning removes the float effect
- Serve placement: seam between passers or deep corners
- Jump serve: only when overhead serve is consistently in
