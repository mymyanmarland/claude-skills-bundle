# Volleyball Spiking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Spiking: 4-step approach for right-handers (left-right-left-right) → Arms back on final step, swing up → Contact top of ball — arm fully extended, snap wrist → Line vs cross-court (angle shoulders at contact) → Tip shot → Roll shot → Approach timing matched to set.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Approach timing: too early or late ruins the attack
- Contact top of ball: below the equator goes into the net
- Wrist snap: adds speed and topspin

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
