# Volleyball Fitness Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volleyball fitness: Vertical jump (plyometrics: box jumps, depth jumps) → Lateral agility (shuffle drills, reaction cones) → Leg strength (squats — drives jump height) → Core stability → Shoulder stability (rotator cuff prehab) → Anaerobic conditioning (short burst intervals).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Vertical jump: leg strength and plyometrics are the two pillars
- Shoulder prehab: hitting injuries end seasons — prevent them proactively
- Anaerobic conditioning: short bursts replicate volleyball demands

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
