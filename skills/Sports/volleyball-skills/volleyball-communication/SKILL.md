# Volleyball Communication Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Communication: Call for the ball (mine!) → Ball in or out calls (immediate) → Setter calls options ('go!') → Blocker calls (line! or angle!) → Positive only between points → Pre-game communication (lineup, rotation, serve receive positions).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Call every ball: 'mine' prevents two players watching it drop
- Positive only: negativity lowers team performance
- Blocker calls: communication determines coverage

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
