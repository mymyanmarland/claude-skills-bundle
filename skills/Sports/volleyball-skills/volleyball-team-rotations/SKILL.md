# Volleyball Rotation and Positioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rotations: Rotate clockwise after every side-out → Six rotations (each player serves from position 1) → Front row vs back row (back row cannot attack above net from in front of attack line) → Overlap rule (maintain rotation order when ball is served) → 5-1 vs 6-2 vs 4-2 systems.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Overlap: incorrect position before serve loses the point
- 6 rotations: every player serves once — strategic implications
- 5-1 vs 6-2: understand which system your team runs

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
