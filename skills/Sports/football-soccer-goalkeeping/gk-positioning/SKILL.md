# Goalkeeper Positioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK positioning: Angle reduction (cut down the angle, come off the line) → Line positioning (distance from goal based on defensive line) → Set position (before every shot — balanced, on toes) → Near and far post positioning → Positioning for crosses (judge early — come or stay?) → Set piece positioning (free kicks, corners).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Angle reduction: come off line and narrow the angle on every shot
- Set position before every shot — never flat-footed
- Crosses: decide early and commit — indecision causes errors

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
