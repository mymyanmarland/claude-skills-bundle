# Shot Stopping Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shot stopping: Basic save (set position, hands behind ball, W shape with thumbs) → Low saves (collapse dive, get body behind ball) → High saves (reach and parry or catch) → Side saves (step and dive, push to safety) → Reaction saves (close range — reflexes) → Parrying safely (to the side, away from danger) → Catching vs parrying decision.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- W shape hands — thumbs together behind ball, fingers spread
- Dive to push wide — not into the path of a follow-up
- Catch when confident, parry wide when not — no central parries

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
