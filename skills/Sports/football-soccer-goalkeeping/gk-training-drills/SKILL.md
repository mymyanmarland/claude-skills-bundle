# Goalkeeper Training Drills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK training drills: Warm-up (handling rolls, low shots to sides, gentle catches) → Shot-stopping (driven shots from edge — both feet) → Reflex saves (close-range volleys, rebounds) → Cross claiming (served crosses from both sides) → Distribution (throwing accuracy, kick from hand) → 1v1 simulation → Competition drill (points for clean catches, saves, distribution accuracy).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Warm-up starts with handling — not diving immediately
- Distribution practice every session — it starts attacks
- Competition in every session — raises goalkeeper intensity

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
