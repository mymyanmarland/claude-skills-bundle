# Goalkeeper Handling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK handling: Calling ('keeper!') → Approach (decisive run to ball) → Take-off (one-footed jump for height) → Handling position (arms extended, catch at highest point) → Body protection (knee up, protect the ball) → Claiming vs punching decision (punching only when cannot catch safely) → Through ball sweeping (rush off line, slide to collect).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Call early and loudly — 'keeper!' means everyone clears the way
- Catch at highest point — not at chest level after coming down
- Punch only with two fists — one-fist punching is inaccurate

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
