# Goalkeeper Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK mental game: Mistake recovery (conceding a goal: reset within 60 seconds, cannot change what happened) → Confidence after poor performance → Concentration over 90 minutes → Pre-match routine (visualise key saves) → Handling pressure moments (penalty situations) → Long periods without action (staying alert).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 60-second reset after conceding — nothing can be done about it now
- Pre-match visualisation: see yourself making saves confidently
- Long inaction = concentration danger — stay mentally busy

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
