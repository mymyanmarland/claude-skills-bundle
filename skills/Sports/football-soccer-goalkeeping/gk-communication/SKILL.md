# Goalkeeper Communication Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK communication: Organising the defensive line (push up/hold/drop) → Set piece organisation (wall setup, marking assignments) → General defensive calls ('squeeze!', 'step!', 'away!') → Communication after saves (quickly reorganise) → Positive communication (encourage, do not blame) → Pre-match defensive briefing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clear concise calls — long sentences in a match are useless
- Positive calls — goalkeeper sets the defensive mood
- Pre-match briefing: agree calls and responsibilities before kickoff

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
