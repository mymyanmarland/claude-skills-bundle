# Goalkeeper 1v1 Situations Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK 1v1: When to come out vs stay (come when you will win the ball, stay if unsure) → Rush out: get big, spread body wide → Narrow the angle quickly → Smother (dive at feet, head away from ball) → Hold position (if attacker too close — stay on feet) → Recovery position (if attacker gets round you).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Come out decisively — half-measures lose the 1v1
- Get big: spread arms and legs wide — increase the target
- Smother with head away from ball — safety first

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
