# Goalkeeper-Specific Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK fitness: Explosive power (jumping, diving — plyometrics: box jumps, explosive push-ups) → Lateral speed (short lateral bounds, shuffle drills) → Reaction speed (reflex ball, reaction light training) → Core stability (every save, throw and kick uses the core) → Flexibility (splits saves, extreme reaches) → Game fitness (concentration endurance — a different kind of tiring).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Explosive power over endurance — goalkeepers sprint and dive, not run
- Reaction training specific — goalkeeper reaction differs from outfield
- Flexibility maintained — inflexible goalkeeper cannot make extreme saves

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
