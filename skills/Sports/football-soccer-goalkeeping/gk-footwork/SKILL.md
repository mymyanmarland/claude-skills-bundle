# Goalkeeper Footwork and Agility Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK footwork: Shuffle step (lateral movement without crossing feet) → Drop step (back and across for wide shots) → Crossover (for balls to far post) → Recovery step (after dive, get up quickly) → Agility drills: ladder patterns, reaction cones → Spring exercises (explosive push-off) → Set position reset after every movement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shuffle step for lateral movement — crossing feet slows movement
- Recovery speed after diving — cannot leave the goal unguarded
- Explosive push-off: power in the legs drives the dive

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
