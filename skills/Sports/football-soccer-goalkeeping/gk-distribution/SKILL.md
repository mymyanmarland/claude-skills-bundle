# Goalkeeper Distribution Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK distribution: Short distribution (roll to feet, underarm throw — accuracy) → Overarm throw (javelin style for distance) → Bowling to fullback → Goal kick: short option vs long → Driven kicks (goal kick, from hands) → Half-volley from hands → Tactical distribution (when to go short, when to go long) → Counter-attack trigger.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short distribution first — goalkeeper starts build-up play
- Count the press: if 3+ opponents press, kick long
- Accurate over distance — distribution starts attacks

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
