# Outfield Fielding Excellence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Outfield excellence: Positioning (read batter's shots, move early) → Boundary riding (judge trajectory, timing the dive or slide) → Saving boundaries (slide along rope, foot on rope warning) → Long throw (full crow-hop, high arc for distance) → Relay throw system (cut-off man, relay partner) → Covering ground (sprint lines, direct angle to ball).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Move before the ball is hit — read the batter
- Slide along rope not into it — foot on rope is a boundary
- Relay throw used over 60m — single throw accuracy drops

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
