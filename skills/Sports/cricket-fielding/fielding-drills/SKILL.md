# Fielding Drills Library Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fielding drills: Individual (cone circuits, stump-throwing stations, rebound net catches) → Pairs (throw-and-catch, ground fielding and return) → Small group (circle relay, 3-2-1 outfield drill) → Full team (fielding simulation with batter) → Game-based (fielding challenge — points for direct hits and clean stops) → Session structure (10 min individual / 10 min pairs / 10 min team game).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Game-based drills simulate pressure — pure technique drills do not
- Score keeping in team drills — competition raises intensity
- 10-minute cap per drill type — concentration drops after

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
