# Slip Catching Mastery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Slip catching: Stance (low, weight on balls of feet, hands relaxed below waist) → Depth and angle → Reaction training (rebound net, slip cradle, fed edges) → Communication ('mine!') → Left and right catches → Late-swinging edges → Concentration (stay present on every ball — long wait) → Cordon setup (coverage and angles).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Low stance — standing upright means diving equals drops
- Hands below waist until ball passes the batter
- Communicate every edge — avoid collision with keeper

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
