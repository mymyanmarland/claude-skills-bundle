# Ground Fielding Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Ground fielding: Ready position (hands low, weight forward, on toes) → Long barrier (back knee down, body behind ball) → Aggressive attack (charge the ball, field on the move) → Pick up and throw in one motion → Two-handed technique (always when possible) → Sliding stop (run past, slide, body behind) → Communication (calling for the ball).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ready position every ball — not standing upright
- Long barrier for skiddy ground — saves the boundary
- Never give up on the chase — effort creates pressure

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
