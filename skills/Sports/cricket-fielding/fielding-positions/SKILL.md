# Fielding Positions Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fielding positions: Slip cordon (angles, depth, communication) → Gully (cut and edge) → Point and cover (cut off boundaries) → Mid-wicket and mid-on/off (cutting off drives) → Fine leg and third man (boundary protection) → Sweeper positions (T20) → Saving one vs saving four (field logic) → Adjust position based on batter's preferred zones.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slip cordon communication — agree who takes what
- Cover and point depth depends on batter's foot movement
- Fine leg angle changes with bowling — move between deliveries

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
