# Throwing Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Throwing: Crow-hop (gather step for momentum) → Grip (across seam for flat throw) → High elbow, hip rotation, wrist snap → Flat throw vs lob (when to use each) → Backing up at the stumps (always have a backer) → Direct hit technique (aim early, call for it) → Practice: stump throws from various distances and on the run.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Crow-hop generates power — standing throw loses pace
- Elbow high at release — low elbow means low throw
- Back up at both ends — one missed throw equals four overthrows

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
