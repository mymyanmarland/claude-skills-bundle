# Captain's Fielding Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Captain's fielding: Reading wagon wheels (where the batter scores) → Attacking vs defensive fields → New batter tactics (more slips, attacking) → Settled batter tactics (dry up scoring, frustrate) → T20 fielding restrictions (powerplay vs death) → Rotating fielders (keep energy high) → Best catchers in slips → Over rate management (quick field changes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wagon wheel reading before setting field — not guessing
- New batter equals attacking field — do not back off immediately
- Over rate management — slow field changes cost penalties

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
