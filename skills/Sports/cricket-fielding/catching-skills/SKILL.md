# Catching Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Catching: Basic mechanics (fingers up above waist, down below) → Soft hands (cushion the ball, do not snatch) → Watch ball into hands → Outfield: judge trajectory early, move early, set yourself → Running catches (head still, eyes on ball) → Reaction catches (close catching — anticipation, relaxed hands) → Sun and bright light handling (cap and sunglasses) → Slip stance (low, relaxed, wide base).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soft hands — hard hands cause drops
- Watch into the hands — most drops happen from looking away
- Set yourself early for outfield — last-second adjustments cause drops

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
