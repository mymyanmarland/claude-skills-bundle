# Fielding to Support the Wicketkeeper Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Supporting the keeper: Short leg positioning (angle, depth — helmet mandatory) → Silly positions (only when batter is playing defensively) → Communication (keeper calls for ball) → Backing up at bowler's end (mid-off must always back up) → Returning ball to keeper vs bowler → Short fine leg (leg-side nudges) → Stumping awareness.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short leg helmet mandatory — no exceptions
- Backing up bowler's end every single ball
- Silly positions only when batter is playing defensively

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
