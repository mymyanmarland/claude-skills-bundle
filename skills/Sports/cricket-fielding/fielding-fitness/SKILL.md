# Fielding Fitness and Agility Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fielding position or role
- Playing level
- Specific weakness
- Training environment
- Match format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fielding fitness: Lateral speed (shuffle drills, lateral bounds) → Acceleration (5m and 10m sprint drills — fielding is short bursts) → Agility (T-drill, change of direction, reaction to signal) → Diving and getting up (practise safely on grass) → Reaction training (tennis ball drops, rebound board) → Endurance (50+ overs of concentration — aerobic base needed).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short burst speed — not endurance running alone
- Diving practice on soft surface first — technique before intensity
- Reaction training separate to fielding practice

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
