# Hockey Shooting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey shooting: Position (shooting space before receiving) → Hit (powerful, full backswing) → Push shot (accurate, no backswing, used in the D) → Deflection (redirect a cross into goal) → Reverse stick shot → Drag flick (penalty corners) → Shot placement → Only goals within the D count.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Power with direction: wide is as costly as wide and hard
- Deflection: simplest goal is often most effective
- Shooting from outside the D: only goals within the D count

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
