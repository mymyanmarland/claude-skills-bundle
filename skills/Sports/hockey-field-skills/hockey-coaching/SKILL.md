# Hockey Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey coaching: Session structure (warm-up stick work/skill/conditioned game/cool-down) → Small-sided games (3v3, 4v4 for maximum touches) → Goalkeeper inclusion → Penalty corner practice → Video analysis (aerial view reveals positioning) → Youth: Indian dribble and basic passes before tactical work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Small-sided games: maximum ball contacts essential for development
- Goalkeeper in all sessions: part of the team not a separate entity
- Penalty corners: 20-30% of goals — practise proportionally

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
