# Hockey Passing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey passing: Push pass (stick on ball, push forward, no backswing — illegal above waist) → Flat hit (backswing allowed, hard contact) → Slap pass (quick, minimal backswing) → Receiving (cushion with stick) → Early pass → Weight matched to receiver's movement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Push pass: no backswing — illegal if stick rises above waist
- Cushion receive: snatching causes bounce
- Early pass: before pressure arrives keeps possession flowing

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
