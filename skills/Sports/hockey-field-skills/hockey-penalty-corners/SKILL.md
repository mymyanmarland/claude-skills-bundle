# Penalty Corner Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Penalty corners: Attack (runner/stopper/striker/drag flicker/back runners) → Stopper technique (stop ball dead) → Striker (deflect or push into goal) → Drag flick (advanced — requires training) → Defensive rush (four charge out, blocker, goalkeeper) → Set plays → Safety equipment mandatory for blockers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Defensive blockers: shin guards and gloves mandatory
- Stop quality: imperfect stop significantly reduces scoring chances
- Practise at least 3 different penalty corner options

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
