# Hockey Goalkeeper Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey goalkeeping: Full kit mandatory (helmet, pads, kickers, gloves, body protection) → Angle reduction (come off line for 1v1s) → Kicker clearance → Glove save → Stick save → Shuffle step (stay on feet) → Penalty stroke (set, watch ball, dive).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Full equipment mandatory: no training without it
- Angle reduction: coming off the line is most important goalkeeping skill
- Do not dive early: read the striker and wait

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
