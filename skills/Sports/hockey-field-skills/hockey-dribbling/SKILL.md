# Hockey Dribbling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey dribbling: Straight dribble (close, small touches, head up) → Speed dribble (push slightly ahead, run to it) → Indian dribble → 3D skills (eliminate with ball over or around stick) → Change of pace → Body feints → Left hand strong: enables full 360-degree control.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Head up: if you cannot see you cannot make good decisions
- Left hand dominant: enables full 360-degree stick face control
- 3D skills: legal and effective — practise lifting safely

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
