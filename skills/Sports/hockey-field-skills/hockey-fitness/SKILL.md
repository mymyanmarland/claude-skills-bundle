# Hockey-Specific Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey fitness: Aerobic base (70-minute matches on synthetic pitch) → Repeated sprint ability → Agility (change of direction — low body, quick push-off) → Core stability → Shoulder stability → Hip flexibility (low body position demands it) → Pre-season conditioning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Repeated sprint ability: many fast sprints more important than one long one
- Low body position: hip flexibility and quad strength determine how low you play
- Pre-season: build aerobic base before adding intensity

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
