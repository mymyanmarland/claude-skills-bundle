# Hockey Grip and Ball Control Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grip and control: Left hand at the top (controls stick face) / right hand lower for support → Open face for Indian dribbling / flat face for straight → Indian dribble (ball moved left to right alternately) → Ball close → 3D skills introduction (lift ball over defender's stick).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Left hand dominant: controls the stick face — right is secondary
- Ball close: wide ball takes time to control
- Indian dribble: the foundation of creative field hockey play

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
