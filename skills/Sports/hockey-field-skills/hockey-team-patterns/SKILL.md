# Hockey Team Movement Patterns Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team patterns: Triangle support (two options always available) → Lead runs (move before ball arrives) → Width and depth (stretch defence) → Switching play (ball across pitch faster than running) → Overload (2v1 situations) → Press as a team after losing possession.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Triangle support: foundation of all passing in hockey
- Lead runs: support players must move before receiving
- Switch play: moving ball across is faster than running across

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
