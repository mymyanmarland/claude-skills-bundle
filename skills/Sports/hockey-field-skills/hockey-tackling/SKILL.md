# Hockey Tackling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey tackling: Channel before tackling (guide attacker — do not commit early) → Block tackle (stick flat, intercept the ball) → Jab tackle (quick jab at ball) → Reverse stick tackle (tackling on the left side) → Legal tackle (stick contacts ball only — not opponent) → No slide tackles (must be standing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Channel first: commit too early and you are beaten easily
- Legal tackle: contact stick before body — foul otherwise
- No slide tackles: field hockey tackles while standing only

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
