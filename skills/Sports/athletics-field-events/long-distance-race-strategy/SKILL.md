# Long Distance Race Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Long distance strategy: Negative split (second half slightly faster) → Pack running (drafting, positioning) → Surge management (go with critical surges, let others go) → Fuelling (marathon: gel every 45 min) → Kick timing (last 400m for most distances) → Weather adaptation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Negative split: almost every world record is negative split
- Pack running conserves energy significantly
- Surge management: not every surge needs following

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
