# Hammer Throw Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hammer throw: Grip (both hands on handle, interlocked) → Pendulum swings (build rhythm before rotation) → Entry into rotation (smooth, not jerky) → Rotational mechanics (low and sweeping) → Release (high and forward) → Safety: the most dangerous track event — only practise in a proper cage with qualified supervision.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cage training mandatory: hammer without a cage is extremely dangerous
- Pendulum swings first: build rhythm before rotation
- Supervised only: hammer requires expert coaching at all stages

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
