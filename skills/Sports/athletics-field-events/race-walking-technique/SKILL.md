# Race Walking Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Race walking: Rule 1 — continuous contact (one foot always on ground) → Rule 2 — straight leg (advancing leg straight when foot contacts ground until vertical) → Hip rotation (exaggerated hip drive — generates speed) → Arm action (high and compact) → Judging awareness (disqualification for violation).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Continuous contact: any loss is a rule violation
- Straight leg at contact: bent knee is automatic violation
- Hip rotation is the key to speed — without it you shuffle

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
