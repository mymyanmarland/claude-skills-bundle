# Discus Throwing Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Discus: Grip (fingers spread across rim, thumb underneath) → Standing throw first (master balance before rotation) → Wind-up (swing back, discus trails) → Rotation (hips lead, shoulders follow) → Release (roll off index finger, slight upward angle) → Safety: cleared sector mandatory before every throw.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Standing throw mastery before rotation: balance first
- Hips before shoulders: the rotational sequence that generates power
- Safety sector: no one retrieves until clear signal from coach

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
