# Pole Vault Introduction Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pole vault: Pole matches body weight → Grip (top hand as high as safely possible) → Run-up (consistent, accelerating) → Plant (pole into box as final foot plants) → Swing (hang and swing) → Pull and turn → Bar clearance → Safety: mats must meet standards, qualified coaching essential, beginners start without crossbar.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pole weight rating must match athlete: wrong pole is dangerous
- Standards-compliant mats: non-negotiable
- Qualified coaching: pole vault must not be self-taught

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
