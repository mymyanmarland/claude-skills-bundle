# Steeplechase Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Steeplechase: 3000m — 28 hurdles and 7 water jumps → Step on top and off (more efficient than clearing) → Water jump (step on barrier, land on shallow end, sprint out) → Pack positioning → Wet feet management → Conditioning (aerobic base + hurdles + strength).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Step on and off the barrier: more efficient than clearing
- Water jump: land on the shallow end — the far end is deep
- Wet feet: accept it immediately — anxiety about water slows you down

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
