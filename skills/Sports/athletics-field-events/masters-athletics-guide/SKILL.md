# Masters Athletics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Masters athletics: Age-related changes (speed events decline faster than endurance) → Recovery requirements (20-30% more recovery time) → Injury prevention priority → Masters competitions (World Masters Athletics by 5-year age bracket) → Training adjustments (reduce high-intensity frequency) → Strength training importance increases with age.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recovery: most important training variable for masters
- Strength training: becomes more important after 40
- Masters competitions: age-graded performances often better than open-age PBs
