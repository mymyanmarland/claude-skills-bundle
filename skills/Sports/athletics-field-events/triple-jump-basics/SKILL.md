# Triple Jump Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Triple jump: Three phases (hop on take-off foot / step on other foot / jump to pit) → Consistent approach run → Hop mechanics (drive free leg) → Step mechanics (active landing, drive through) → Jump mechanics (tall take-off, forward landing) → Phase ratio approximately 35/30/35 → Safety matting essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Land correctly: hop same foot, step opposite, jump to pit
- Phase ratio: unequal phases indicate a weakness
- Approach consistency: same as long jump — variable approach kills performance

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
