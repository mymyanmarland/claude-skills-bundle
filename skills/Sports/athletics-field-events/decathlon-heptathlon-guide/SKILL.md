- Athletics event and distance

- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Multi-event: Event orders (decathlon/heptathlon) → Scoring tables (points from performance) → Prioritise weakest scoring events → Training balance across events → Competition energy management → Mental resilience across two days.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Energy management: day 2 events suffer if day 1 too aggressive
