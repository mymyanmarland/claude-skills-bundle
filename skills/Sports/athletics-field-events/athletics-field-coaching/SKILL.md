# Athletics Field Events Coaching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Field events coaching: Safety first — protocols taught before technique → Technical progression (standing throws before rotation) → Individual differences (technique adapts to body type) → Equipment inspection before each session → LTAD for field events (younger athletes: bounding and throwing without heavy implements) → Video analysis for technical feedback.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety protocols before technique: non-negotiable in throws
- Standing throw before rotation: saves years of wasted training
- Video analysis: most field event errors invisible without it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
