# Flexibility and Mobility Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Flexibility and mobility: Flexibility vs mobility (flexibility: passive range / mobility: active control through range — mobility is more relevant to sport) → Dynamic warm-up (movement through range before training — not static stretching) → Static stretching (after training — improves flexibility / before training reduces power output) → Foam rolling (self-myofascial release — reduces muscle tension before stretching) → Key areas for athletes (hip flexors / thoracic spine / ankle dorsiflexion / shoulder) → Yoga as mobility training.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Static stretching before training reduces power: use dynamic instead
- Mobility over flexibility: active control through range is more sport-relevant
- Hip flexors and thoracic spine: the most limited areas in most athletes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
