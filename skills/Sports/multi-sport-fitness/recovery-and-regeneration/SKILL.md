# Recovery and Regeneration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Recovery and regeneration: Sleep (7-9 hours — no recovery tool is more effective — non-negotiable) → Nutrition timing (protein and carbs within 45 minutes of training — the most controllable recovery variable) → Active recovery (easy movement — 20-30 min — promotes blood flow without adding fatigue) → Cold water immersion (10-15°C, 10-15 min — reduces muscle soreness) → Massage and foam rolling (reduces muscle tension, promotes parasympathetic recovery) → Mental recovery (psychological rest from sport — as real as physical) → Monitoring (HRV, perceived fatigue, motivation — subjective measures are valid).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sleep: no tool replaces it — optimise sleep hygiene before buying recovery gadgets
- Nutrition window: 45 minutes — the most impactful single recovery action
- Mental recovery: an athlete who cannot switch off from sport is not fully recovered

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
