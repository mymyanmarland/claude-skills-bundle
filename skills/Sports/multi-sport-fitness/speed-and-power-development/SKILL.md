# Speed and Power Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Speed and power: Sprint mechanics (acceleration and top speed — separate training demands) → Plyometrics for power (bounding / box jumps / depth jumps — develop explosive force) → Strength as the foundation (you cannot express power you have not built as strength) → Training sequencing (strength before power — always / power before speed — always / speed last in the session) → Rest between efforts (speed and power require full recovery — 3-5 minutes between sprint efforts).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Strength before power: the most important sequencing rule
- Full recovery between sprints: insufficient rest trains endurance not speed
- Plyometrics progress slowly: tendons adapt 10x slower than muscles

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
