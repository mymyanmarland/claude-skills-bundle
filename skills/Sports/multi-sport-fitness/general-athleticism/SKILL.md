# General Athleticism Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

General athleticism: Movement literacy (run / jump / throw / catch / change direction — competency in all) → Speed (acceleration and top-end — improves performance in any sport) → Agility (reactive change of direction — decelerate, redirect, accelerate) → Power (explosive force production — transfers to throws, jumps, starts) → Endurance (aerobic base — sustains all activity) → Mobility (full range of motion — prerequisite for all movement) → The athlete first, sport second.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Movement literacy: the foundation of all sport-specific skill
- The athlete first: broad athletic development before sport-specific detail
- Reactive agility transfers better than pre-planned cone drills

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
