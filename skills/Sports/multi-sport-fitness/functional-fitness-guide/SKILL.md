# Functional Fitness for Sport Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Functional fitness: What functional means (movements that replicate or support sport demands — not just aesthetics) → Movement patterns (push/pull/hinge/squat/rotate/carry — train all six) → Unilateral training (single-leg and single-arm — mirrors sport demands) → Rotational power (core rotation is central to most sport) → Loaded carries (farmer carries / suitcase carries — builds functional stability) → Ground-based movements (most sport happens standing — train standing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Unilateral training: sport happens on one leg — train it
- Rotation: the most undertraining movement pattern in most gym programmes
- Ground-based: bench press has less sport transfer than standing cable press

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
