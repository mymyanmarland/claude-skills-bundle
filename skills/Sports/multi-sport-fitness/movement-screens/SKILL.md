# Movement Screening and Assessment Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Movement screening: Functional Movement Screen (FMS) overview (7 fundamental movements scored 0-3) → Squat assessment (depth / symmetry / foot position) → Single-leg squat (hip stability — the single most revealing test) → Hip hinge (touch toes / hamstring flexibility) → Shoulder mobility (reach behind back / overhead) → Core stability (plank / side plank) → Key principle: address the lowest-scoring movement first → Referral (below a certain score — refer to physiotherapist).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single-leg squat is the most revealing screen: reveals hip stability and asymmetry
- Address the lowest score first: programming to strengths ignores the limiter
- Physiotherapist referral: screening identifies — it does not diagnose or treat

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
