# Injury Prevention for Multi-Sport Athletes Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Multi-sport injury prevention: Load monitoring (total training hours across all sports — not per sport) → Overuse injury recognition (gradual onset pain in the same area — stop and assess) → Movement asymmetry correction (the most reliable injury predictor) → Concurrent load management (strength training volume when in-season in multiple sports) → Communication between coaches (coaches of multiple sports the athlete plays must communicate load) → Rest weeks (periodic planned unloading — every 4-6 weeks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total load across all sports: the sum is what causes injury — not any single sport
- Coach communication: the most common multi-sport injury cause is unknown combined load
- Rest weeks: mandatory — body does not distinguish between sports when accumulating fatigue

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
