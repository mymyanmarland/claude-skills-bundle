# Aerobic Base Building Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sports participated in
- Current fitness level
- Training hours available per week
- Primary performance goal
- Any injury history or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Aerobic base: Why it matters (aerobic base supports recovery between high-intensity efforts — all sport depends on it) → Zone 2 training (60-70% max HR, conversational — 80% of base training should be here) → Minimum effective dose (3-4 sessions per week, 30-60 minutes each) → Building volume (10% per week maximum) → Aerobic base vs cardio (base is low intensity and long — not HIIT) → Cross-sport aerobic training (running / cycling / swimming / rowing all build the base).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zone 2 is harder than it sounds: most athletes go too hard
- 80% of training should be easy: polarised training produces better results than mostly moderate
- 10% per week: increase volume slowly — consistent low-intensity beats aggressive loading

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
