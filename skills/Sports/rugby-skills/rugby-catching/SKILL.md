# Rugby Catching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby catching: Watch ball into hands, fingers spread → High ball (call mark, position under, jump) → Mark call (free kick rule — use it) → Under pressure (shield with body) → Wet conditions (squeeze tighter, softer hands).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Watch into the hands: the most fundamental catching principle
- High ball mark call: protection call — use it in your own half
- Wet conditions: adjust grip pressure — not the technique

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
