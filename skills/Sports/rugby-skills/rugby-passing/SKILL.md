# Rugby Passing Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby passing: Spin pass (hold at both ends, swing from hip, flick top wrist, follow through) → Pop pass (short offload, push directly to target) → Pass before the tackle → Equal left and right development.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Follow-through toward target: the spin pass is swing and follow
- Pass before contact: passing in the tackle usually results in a turnover
- Equal left and right: one-sided passers are predictable

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
