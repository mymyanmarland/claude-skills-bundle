# Rugby Fitness Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby fitness: Aerobic base (80 minutes at high intensity requires a foundation) → Repeated sprint ability → Contact strength (tackle and be tackled) → Power (scrummaging and lineout) → Position-specific (props strength-dominant / backs speed-dominant) → Pre-season vs in-season adjustments.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Position-specific: prop fitness needs differ completely from winger
- Repeated sprint ability: many fast sprints more important than one
- Pre-season: build the foundation — in-season maintains it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
