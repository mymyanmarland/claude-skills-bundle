# Rugby Offloading in Contact Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Offloading: When to offload (support player in position — not desperation) → Arm free (keep one arm free as you contact) → Popped ball → One-handed flick → Decision (go to ground cleanly if no support) → Support running is essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Arm free before contact: once both arms trapped no offload possible
- Support running: offload only as good as the support player's timing
- Not in desperation: bad offload becomes a turnover

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
