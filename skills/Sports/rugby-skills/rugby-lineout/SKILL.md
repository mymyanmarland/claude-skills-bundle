# Rugby Lineout Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lineout: Straight throw mandatory → Jumper technique (driven off feet, reach at peak) → Lifting (wait until jumper airborne — safety rule) → Coded calls → Defending (offside line 5m away) → Lineout as set-piece platform → Lifters control descent carefully.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Straight throw mandatory: crooked throw concedes possession
- Lifting only after airborne: legal and safer
- Calls coded: opposition must not decipher signals

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
