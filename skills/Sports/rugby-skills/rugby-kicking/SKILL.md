# Rugby Kicking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby kicking: Punt (drop ball onto boot, swing through) → Spiral punt (spirals for distance) → Grubber (along ground, chase into space) → Chip kick (over defence) → Box kick (high, contested) → Place kick (goalkicking — approach angle, contact through ball) → Decision-making (when to kick vs run vs pass).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Decision first: the wrong kick well-executed is still wrong
- Spiral punt: contact point is the key — practise ball-to-boot
- Grubber: bounce is unpredictable — kick into space not to a point

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
