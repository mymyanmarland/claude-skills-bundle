# Rugby Tackling Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby tackling: Below the armpits (World Rugby law) → Approach (bend knees, get low, eyes open) → Contact (cheek to cheek — your cheek to outer thigh) → Both arms wrap → Drive through → Stay on feet → Tackle height protocol (high tackle — mandatory reporting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cheek to cheek contact: reduces injury risk significantly
- Both arms wrap: one-armed tackle far less secure
- High tackle protocol: any contact above armpit must be assessed

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Contact Sport Disclaimer: Rugby is a full-contact sport with inherent risk of injury. Always play under qualified coaching and officiating, wear appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance. Follow all World Rugby laws and concussion protocols.
