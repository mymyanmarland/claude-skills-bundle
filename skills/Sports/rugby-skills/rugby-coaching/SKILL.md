# Rugby Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby coaching: Session structure (warm-up/skill/contact-game situation/cool-down) → Contact coaching safety (tackle technique before contact — non-negotiable) → Under-12s (modified non-contact formats only) → Concussion protocol (remove if in doubt, do not return same day) → STEP model planning (Space/Task/Equipment/People).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Concussion protocol: remove if in doubt — no exceptions
- Under-12s: non-contact modified formats only
- Contact coaching: tackle technique must precede all contact sessions

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Contact Sport Disclaimer: Rugby is a full-contact sport with inherent risk of injury. Always play under qualified coaching and officiating, wear appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance. Follow all World Rugby laws and concussion protocols.
