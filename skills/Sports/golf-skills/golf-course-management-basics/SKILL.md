# Golf Course Management Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Course management: Know your distances (carry distances for each club — accurate, not aspirational) → Play to the fat side of the fairway (avoid trouble on one side) → Miss in the right place (miss long rather than short / miss to the side with easier chip) → Tee shot strategy (driver is not always the right club) → Par 3 strategy (aim for the middle of the green — flag-hunting on par 3s costs strokes) → Score management (bogey is fine — double bogey hurts / avoid the big number).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Accurate distances: most amateurs overestimate — measure on a calm day
- Bogey recovery: a bogey is one over par — a double is two — stop the bleeding at bogey
- Par 3 strategy: centre of green always unless the flag is in a friendly position

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
