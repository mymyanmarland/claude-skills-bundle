# Golf Short Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Short game: Chip shot (narrow stance, ball back, weight forward, rocking motion — no wrist flip) → Pitch shot (fuller swing, use loft of the club, ball position centre) → Bunker shot (open stance and clubface, aim left of target, hit the sand 5cm behind the ball) → Reading the chip (upslope adds height / downslope adds roll — play accordingly) → Club selection for chipping (low loft for more roll / high loft for more carry).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chip: no wrist flip — the hands lead through impact
- Bunker: hit the sand not the ball — the sand propels the ball
- Short game practice: 60% of strokes are within 100 yards — practise proportionally

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
