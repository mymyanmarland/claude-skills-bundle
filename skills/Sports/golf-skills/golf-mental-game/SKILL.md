# Golf Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf mental game: Pre-shot routine (same every shot — builds consistency under pressure) → Between-shot management (walk slowly, think slowly — rushing creates tension) → Error recovery (one shot is gone — focus only on the next) → Score awareness (know where you are but do not obsess — play shots not scorecard) → Course management decisions (realistic shot selection — play to strengths) → Pressure situations (breathe, slow down, routine — then commit).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-shot routine: the anchor that holds the round together under pressure
- One shot gone: 5-second emotional window, then move on
- Course management: most amateurs are too aggressive — play the percentage shot
