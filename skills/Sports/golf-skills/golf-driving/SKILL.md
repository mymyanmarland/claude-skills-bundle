# Golf Driving Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Driving: Setup (ball off lead heel, tee high, weight favouring trail foot) → Sweep not hit (driver hits slightly on the upswing — ascending angle of attack adds distance) → Wide arc (driver needs width in the backswing — not tight) → Shoulder turn (full 90-degree turn) → Downswing (hips clear first, then everything follows) → Finish (full finish, weight on lead foot, facing target) → Accuracy vs distance (fairway is the priority — long and crooked costs strokes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ascending angle of attack: 3-4 degrees up adds significant carry distance
- Full shoulder turn: restricted turn is the most common distance leak
- Fairway first: a 200m straight drive beats a 260m drive in the rough

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
