# Golf Fitness Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf fitness: Rotational power (the golf swing is a rotation — cable rotations, medicine ball throws) → Hip mobility (the source of shoulder-hip separation that generates power) → Core stability (controls the rotation and protects the lower back) → Shoulder mobility and stability (overhead mobility for backswing) → Lower back health (most common golf injury — glute and core strength prevent it) → Balance (single-leg work) → Flexibility (hip flexors and thoracic spine most critical).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hip mobility: restricted hips are the most common cause of compensations in the golf swing
- Lower back health: most golf injuries are lower back — strengthen glutes and core
- Rotation training: golf power comes from separation of hips and shoulders

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
