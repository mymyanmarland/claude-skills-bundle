# Golf Stance and Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf setup: Posture (hinge forward from the hips — not bending the back / slight knee bend / arms hang naturally) → Ball position (driver: off the lead heel / irons: centre to slightly forward) → Stance width (driver: shoulder-width / irons: slightly narrower) → Alignment (feet, hips, and shoulders parallel to the target line — use a club on the ground to check) → Weight distribution (driver: 60% on trail foot / irons: 50/50) → Distance from the ball (arms hang naturally from the shoulders — not reaching or crowding).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hinge from hips not back: back bend causes a steep swing plane
- Alignment: the most common problem in recreational golf — use alignment sticks when practising
- Ball position changes with club: driver forward, wedge centre — check it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
