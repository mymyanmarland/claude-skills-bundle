# Golf Swing Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf swing: Takeaway (club low along the ground, shoulder turn initiates — not just the arms) → Backswing (shoulder turn 90 degrees, hip turn 45 degrees, weight shifts to trail foot) → Top of backswing (lead arm relatively straight, wrists hinged) → Downswing (hips lead, then shoulders, then arms — not the other way) → Impact (weight on lead foot, hands ahead of the ball at impact) → Follow-through (complete rotation, face the target at finish).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hips lead the downswing: arms leading causes the slice
- Shoulder turn 90 degrees: restricted shoulder turn loses power
- Impact: hands ahead of the ball — this delofts the club and compresses the ball

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
