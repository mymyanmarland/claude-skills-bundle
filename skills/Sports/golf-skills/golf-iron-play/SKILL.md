# Iron Play and Ball Striking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Iron play: Ball-first contact (irons are struck ball-then-divot — not scooping) → Shaft lean at impact (hands ahead = ball-first contact) → Descending blow (swing down and through, not scooping up) → Distance control (consistent swing speed and contact — not harder swings) → Lie reading (different lies require adjustments: plugged lies, tight lies, fluffy lies) → Yardage gapping (know how far each iron goes — on a calm day with clean contact).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ball-first contact: the fundamental iron-play skill that separates ball strikers
- Divot after the ball: a divot in front of the ball means ball-first contact
- Consistent distance: same swing, not harder swings — effort variance kills consistency

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
