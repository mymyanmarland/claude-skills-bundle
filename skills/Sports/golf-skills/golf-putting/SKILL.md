# Golf Putting Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Putting: Setup (eyes over the ball, slightly inside target line / putter face square to target / light grip pressure) → Stroke (pendulum motion — shoulders rock, not wrists / even back and through) → Green reading (read from behind the hole and behind the ball / slope affects pace and direction) → Pace control (most 3-putts are from poor pace, not poor line) → Routine (same pre-putt routine every time — builds confidence and consistency) → Short putts (target a spot an inch in front of the ball — not the hole).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pendulum stroke: wristy putters are inconsistent — shoulders not wrists
- Pace control: line matters more on short putts / pace matters more on long putts
- Routine: same every time — comfort on the greens comes from ritual

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
