# Golf Grip Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf grip: Grip styles (Vardon overlapping / interlocking / ten-finger — choose based on hand size and preference) → Lead hand position (club runs diagonally across the fingers, not the palm — V points toward the trail shoulder) → Trail hand (covers the lead thumb, V also toward trail shoulder) → Grip pressure (1-10 scale: 4-5 — firm enough to control but not tension-inducing) → Neutral vs strong vs weak grip (affects shot shape) → Check grip every session — it drifts.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Grip runs across the fingers: palm grip causes loss of wrist hinge and power
- Grip pressure 4-5: white knuckles kill clubhead speed
- Check grip first: 90% of swing problems originate at address

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
