# Esports Mental Performance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports mental performance: Focus management (staying in the present moment — not tilting on the last mistake) → Tilt management (recognising emotional state change and resets) → Confidence building (from preparation and practice, not outcomes) → Pre-match routine (consistent mental and physical warm-up) → Pressure performance (breathing techniques to lower arousal) → Post-match review (structured, not emotional) → Sleep and rest as performance tools.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tilt recognition: identifying the mental state change early allows recovery before performance collapses
- Pre-match routine: same every time — consistency builds readiness
- Post-match review: structured analysis within 30 minutes — emotional distance before criticism

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
