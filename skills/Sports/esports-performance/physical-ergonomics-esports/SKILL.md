# Esports Physical Ergonomics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports ergonomics: Monitor height (top of monitor at or slightly below eye level) → Chair and desk height (elbows at 90 degrees, feet flat on floor) → Mouse and keyboard position (arms relaxed, not reaching forward) → Wrist position (neutral — not bent up or down) → Screen distance (arm's length from the monitor) → Lighting (no glare, room not darker than the screen) → Break schedule (20-20-20 rule: every 20 min look 20m away for 20 sec) → Warm-up exercises for hands and wrists.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Neutral wrist position: gaming wrist injuries are common — prevention is critical
- 20-20-20 rule: eye strain affects focus and reaction time
- Break every 60-90 minutes: standing and moving prevents musculoskeletal problems
