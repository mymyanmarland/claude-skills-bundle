# Game Sense Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game sense: Map awareness (always knowing where enemies might be based on available information) → Resource management (economy in strategy games / ability cooldowns in MOBAs) → Win condition identification (what needs to happen for your team to win) → Rotation decisions (when to rotate in FPS and battle royale games) → Information processing (using the minimap, kill feeds, and callouts) → Watching high-level play (VOD review of professional players in your role) → Play fewer games, learn more per game.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Minimap habits: check the minimap every 5-10 seconds — information changes the decision
- Win condition awareness: most amateur losses are from not having a clear objective
- VOD review: watching pro players in your role is the fastest learning tool

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
