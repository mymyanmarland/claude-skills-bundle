# Esports Team Communication Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team communication: Callout vocabulary (agreed team language for locations and events — same term every time) → Positive communication culture (negativity causes tilt for the whole team — positive callouts only) → Information sharing (share what you see, not what you think — 'two pushing A' not 'they might come A') → Shot-calling (who calls the strategy — one primary caller reduces confusion) → Silence discipline (unnecessary talk clutters the channel) → Debrief after the game (not during).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Positive comms: a team that tilts together loses together — culture starts with you
- Information not opinion: share what you see, not predictions
- One shot-caller: multiple conflicting strategies cause hesitation at crucial moments

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
