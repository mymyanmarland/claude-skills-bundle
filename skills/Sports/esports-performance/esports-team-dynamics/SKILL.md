# Esports Team Dynamics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports team dynamics: Role clarity (each player's responsibilities in and out of game) → Shot-calling structure (one primary IGL — in-game leader — reduces chaos) → Practice culture (regular, structured scrimmages with debrief) → Conflict resolution (in-game conflict protocol: resolve after the session, not during) → Team communication standards (positive, information-rich, non-blaming) → Player-to-player accountability (team-set standards, peer-held) → Off-game relationship (teams that communicate off-game perform better under pressure).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- IGL clarity: one leader for in-game strategy — clear before the match
- In-game conflict protocol: address after the game, never during — it costs focus
- Off-game relationships: trust built outside the game performs under pressure during it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
