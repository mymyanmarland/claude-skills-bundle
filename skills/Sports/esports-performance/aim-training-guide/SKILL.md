# Aim Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Aim training: Sensitivity settings (find the right mouse sensitivity for your play style — lower is more precise) → Aim training software (Aim Lab, KovaaK's — specific mechanics drills) → Flicking vs tracking (different scenarios train different aim mechanics) → Warm-up routine (15-20 minutes of aim training before ranked play) → Muscle memory development (consistent sensitivity and settings — never change settings frequently) → Crosshair placement (the most impactful non-mechanical aim improvement — always aim at head height).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Crosshair placement: the largest aim improvement available that is not mechanical
- Consistent settings: changing sensitivity resets muscle memory
- Warm-up: aim training before ranked play improves early game performance

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
