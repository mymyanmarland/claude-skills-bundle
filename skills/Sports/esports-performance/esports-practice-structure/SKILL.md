# Esports Practice Structure Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports practice structure: Deliberate practice (specific, focused, feedback-oriented — not just playing games) → Skill isolation (aim training / mechanics practice / specific scenarios) → Solo queue practice vs team practice (different objectives) → VOD review (review your own gameplay — identify decision errors, not just aim errors) → Weekly structure (aim training / ranked play / review / team scrimmage) → Volume vs quality (fewer hours of focused practice beats many hours of passive play).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- VOD review: the most valuable practice tool — watching yourself reveals invisible errors
- Deliberate practice: identify a specific thing to improve in each session
- Quality over volume: 2 hours of focused practice beats 8 hours of unfocused grinding

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
