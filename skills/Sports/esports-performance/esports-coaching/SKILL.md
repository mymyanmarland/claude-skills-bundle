# Esports Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports coaching: Performance areas (mechanics / game sense / mental / physical / team dynamics) → VOD analysis (systematic review of replays — pattern identification) → Individual vs team development (both needed — individual skills enable team execution) → Communication culture building (coach sets the standard for team comms) → Goal setting with players (specific, measurable, time-bound improvement targets) → Mental health awareness (gaming culture has specific mental health challenges — coaching includes wellbeing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- VOD analysis is the core coaching tool in esports — without it coaching is guesswork
- Individual and team: individual skill gaps limit team performance — address both
- Mental health: coaching in esports includes recognising burnout and toxic habits

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
