# Esports Nutrition and Sleep Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Esports health: Sleep (7-9 hours — the most important performance variable: cognitive function, reaction time, and decision-making all decline significantly with less than 7 hours) → Nutrition (regular meals: blood sugar stability affects focus / avoid high-sugar snacks during competition) → Hydration (dehydration impairs cognitive function — keep water at the desk) → Exercise (regular physical activity improves cognitive function and reduces RSI risk) → Screen time before sleep (blue light suppresses melatonin — stop screens 60 min before bed or use blue light glasses). Note: consult a healthcare professional for personalised health advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sleep is the number one performance tool in esports: no supplement replaces 8 hours
- Regular exercise: improves reaction time, focus, and mental health for all gamers
- Blood sugar stability: eat regularly — a hungry gamer makes worse decisions
