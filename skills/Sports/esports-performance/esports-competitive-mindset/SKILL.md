# Competitive Esports Mindset Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Game genre: FPS/MOBA/RTS/fighting/BR
- Current rank or skill level
- Practice hours per week
- Performance goals
- Any physical or mental performance concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Competitive mindset: Outcome vs process (focus on execution, not rank) → Warm-up before competition (aim training, review game plan) → Tournament format understanding (double elimination, Swiss, round-robin — how each affects strategy) → Pressure inoculation (practise in high-stakes conditions to build tolerance) → Reset after a loss (review quickly, move to the next match mentally) → Team preparation (pre-match team meeting, confirm strategy and communication).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Process focus: rank improves when execution improves — not by wanting it more
- Warm-up: performance peaks after a structured warm-up, not from cold
- Reset: 10-minute review then full focus on the next match — not dwelling

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
