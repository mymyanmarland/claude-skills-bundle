# 5K and 10K Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

5K/10K training: Weekly structure (easy/interval/easy/tempo/long) → 5K intervals (10x400m at 5K pace) → 10K sessions (4x2000m at 10K pace) → Negative split race strategy → Taper (1 week for 5K, 2 weeks for 10K) → Start at target pace — not faster.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Negative split: the most evidence-based race strategy
- Start at target pace: going out too fast causes massive blow-up
- Final kilometre: planned surge, not desperation

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
