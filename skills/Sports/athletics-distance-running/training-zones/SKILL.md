# Distance Running Training Zones Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Training zones: Zone 1 — Easy (60-70% max HR, conversational — most weekly mileage) → Zone 2 — Aerobic (70-80%, sentences) → Zone 3 — Tempo (80-87%, comfortably hard) → Zone 4 — Threshold (87-93%, hard sustained) → Zone 5 — VO2max (93-100%, very short intervals) → 80% of training in Zone 1-2.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 80% Zone 1-2: most runners go too hard on easy days
- Zone 2 is the most underdeveloped zone in amateur runners
- Tempo and threshold: different paces, different adaptations

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
