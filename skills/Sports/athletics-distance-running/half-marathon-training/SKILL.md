# Half Marathon Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Half marathon training: 12-16 week plan → Key sessions (long run/tempo/interval) → Long run peak 18-21km, 3 weeks before race → Tempo development (20 to 40 minutes at threshold) → Half marathon intervals (3x3000m at race pace) → Even effort race strategy → Gel at 8km for most runners.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Peak long run 18-21km: no need to run the full distance in training
- Even effort not even pace: hills change pace — effort is the constant
- Fuelling: practise race-day gel strategy in training

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
