# Interval Training for Distance Runners Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Interval training: VO2max (4-6x1000m at 5km pace, 2-3 min rest) → Lactate threshold (3-4x10-15 min at threshold, 3 min rest) → Short intervals (10-20x200-400m faster than race pace, full recovery) → Easy day after intervals always → Warm-up and cool-down mandatory.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Full recovery for speed work: insufficient rest trains the wrong system
- Easy day after intervals: non-negotiable for injury prevention
- Warm-up 15 minutes minimum before any interval session

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
