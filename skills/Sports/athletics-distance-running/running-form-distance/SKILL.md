# Distance Running Form Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Running form: Posture (upright, slight forward lean from the ankles) → Head position (eyes 20-30m ahead, relaxed jaw) → Arm swing (forward-back, 90-degree bend, hands relaxed, not crossing the midline) → Cadence (170-180 steps per minute) → Foot strike (midfoot preferred for distance) → Hip extension (push off fully, do not shuffle).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Forward lean from ankles not waist — the most common posture error
- Arm swing: crossing the midline wastes energy
- Cadence 170-180: count for 30 seconds x4 — increase gradually

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
