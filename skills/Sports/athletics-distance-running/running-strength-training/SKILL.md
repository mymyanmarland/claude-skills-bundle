# Strength Training for Distance Runners Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strength for runners: Single-leg squat (mimics running demands) → Romanian deadlift (hamstring and glute) → Calf raises eccentric (Achilles and shin prevention) → Hip abductor work (IT band and runner's knee prevention) → Plyometrics (bounding, hops — running economy) → Twice per week on easy days.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single-leg exercises: most running-specific
- Eccentric calf raises: most evidence-based Achilles injury prevention
- Twice per week maximum: recovery for running is the priority

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
