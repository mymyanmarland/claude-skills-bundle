# Running Injury Prevention Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event and distance
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Running injury prevention: 10% rule for weekly mileage → Strength training (single-leg squats, calf raises, hip abductors) → Hip weakness as root cause of most injuries → Shin splints (gradual surface changes, calf strength) → IT band (hip abductor strength) → Runner's knee (quad and glute strength) → Rest when pain signals an issue.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hip strength is the root of most running injuries
- 10% mileage rule: non-negotiable for injury-free training
- Rest when pain appears: running through it worsens injuries

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
