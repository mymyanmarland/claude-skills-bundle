# First Touch and Ball Control Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

First touch: Surface selection (instep/outside/sole/chest/thigh — match surface to ball trajectory) → Cushioning (give with the ball, absorb the pace) → Directional control (touch toward your next action — not just stopping the ball) → Under pressure (protect with body, shield while controlling) → Aerial control (chest and thigh cushioning, head control) → Solo practice: throw against a wall, vary the angle and height.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Directional first touch — toward your next action, not just stopping
- Cushion the ball — snatching causes the ball to bounce away
- Wall practice simulates variable angles cheaply

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
