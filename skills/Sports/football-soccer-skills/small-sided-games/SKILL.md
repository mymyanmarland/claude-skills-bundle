# Small-Sided Games for Skill Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Small-sided games: Rondo (possession circle: keep ball away from defender — develops quick passing and movement) → 3v3 and 4v4 (more touches per player than 11v11) → Directional possession games → 1v1 and 2v2 (decision making under pressure) → Positional games (overloads: 5v3) → Constraints (two-touch max, must combine before shooting) → Debrief questions after games.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- More touches per player in small-sided — more skill development
- Constraints force specific behaviours
- Rondo is the best passing drill ever invented — use it every session

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
