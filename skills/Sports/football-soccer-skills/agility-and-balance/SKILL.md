# Agility and Balance Training for Football Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Agility for football: Ladder drills (quick feet, varied patterns) → Cone agility (T-drill, 5-10-5, Illinois) → Balance training (single-leg work, unstable surfaces for proprioception) → Change of direction with ball → Reactive agility (coach signals direction) → Coordination (ball juggling builds touch and coordination simultaneously).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reactive agility with ball — static cone drills have limited transfer
- Single-leg balance — prevents ankle and knee injuries
- Juggling counts as agility training — coordination and touch together

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
