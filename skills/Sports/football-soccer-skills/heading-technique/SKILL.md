# Heading Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Heading: Attack the ball (do not wait for it — head the ball, do not let it head you) → Contact point (use the forehead — the flat area above the eyes) → Directional heading (neck strength drives direction) → Defensive headers (height and distance — get it out of danger) → Attacking headers (downward header = corners) → Glancing header (redirect, not power) → Timing the jump (meet at highest point).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Attack the ball — waiting means losing to the opponent
- Forehead contact — temples and top of head cause injury
- Head down through the ball for downward, goal-bound headers

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
