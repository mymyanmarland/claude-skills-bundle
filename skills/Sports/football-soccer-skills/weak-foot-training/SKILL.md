# Weak Foot Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Weak foot training: Acceptance (feel of the ball at first is normal — persist) → Passing (start short and accurate before long) → Receiving and controlling → Shooting (placement before power) → Dribbling (small touches, gradual increase) → Progression: 100 weak foot touches per session minimum → Game situations (set challenge: pass or shoot with weak foot X times per match).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 100 touches per session minimum — consistency builds the skill
- Short before long — accuracy before distance
- Game challenge — transfer practice to match situations

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
