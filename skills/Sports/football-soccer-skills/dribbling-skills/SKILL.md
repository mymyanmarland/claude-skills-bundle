# Dribbling Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dribbling: Close control (small touches, ball close to feet) → Speed dribbling (fewer touches, push ball ahead) → Change of direction moves (Cruyff turn, step-over, scissors) → Body feints (sell the fake with shoulder and hips) → 1v1 decision (when to take on vs pass) → Dribbling with head up (see the field while controlling) → Weak foot dribbling.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Head up while dribbling — cannot play if you cannot see
- Change of pace — the actual skill that beats defenders, not just the move
- Weak foot drills — at least 40% of practice on weaker foot

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
