# Passing Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Passing: Short pass (instep, head over ball, non-kicking foot beside ball, lock ankle, follow through to target) → Medium pass (same mechanics, more hip rotation) → Long pass (laces, lean back slightly, strike through centre of ball) → Bent pass (inside of foot, cut across ball for curve) → Weight of pass (pace matched to recipient's movement) → No-look pass introduction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Non-kicking foot placement — beside the ball, not behind it
- Ankle locked at impact — loose ankle kills accuracy
- Weight of pass — too hard is as bad as inaccurate

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
