# Crossing Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crossing: Run-up angle (approach from slightly behind the ball for better contact) → Early cross (whip it in before the defender recovers) → Driven cross (low, fast, across goal) → Lofted cross (higher, delivery for aerial attackers) → Cut-back (pull back to edge of area for late runners) → Near-post cross → Far-post cross → Target zone awareness (where are your attackers?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Approach angle creates the right contact point
- Early cross catches defence before they recover
- Target zone awareness — cross to where your striker is

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
