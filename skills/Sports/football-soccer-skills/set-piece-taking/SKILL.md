# Set-Piece Taking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Set pieces: Direct free kick (wall, placement, dipping technique — strike down through ball) → Indirect free kick (delivery into the box, timed runs) → Corner technique (outswinger vs inswinger, delivery zones) → Short corners → Long throw technique (run-up, two-handed overhead throw) → Penalty technique (pick spot early, commit, do not change mind).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Penalty: decide early, commit — changing mind causes the miss
- Corner delivery zone: near post, far post, or penalty spot
- Long throw as an attacking weapon — underused at amateur level

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
