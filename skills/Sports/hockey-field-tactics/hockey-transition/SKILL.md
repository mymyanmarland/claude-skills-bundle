# Hockey Transition Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey transition: Attack-to-defence (immediate press after losing possession) → Defence-to-attack (quick vertical pass into attacker's run) → Counter-attack patterns (2-3 attackers against retreating defence) → Goalkeeper distribution starts the counter.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Goalkeeper distribution: first pass of counter is most important
- Immediate press: 5 seconds after losing ball are critical
- Counter-attack pattern: agree before the game

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
