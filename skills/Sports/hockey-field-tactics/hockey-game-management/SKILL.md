# Hockey Game Management Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game management: Protecting a lead (defend deep, use possession wisely) → Chasing a game (attack wide, increase tempo, more risk) → Managing possession (circulate in safe areas) → Formation changes by game state → Card management (10-player reorganisation immediate) → End of quarter management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Score and time: two most important variables
- Lead protection: depth without being passive — still engage when opportunity arises
- 10-player management: reorganise immediately

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
