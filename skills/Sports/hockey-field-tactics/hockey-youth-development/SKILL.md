# Youth Hockey Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Youth hockey: Under-10 (soft ball, modified rules, fun and basic stick work) → Under-12 (Indian dribble, push pass, small-sided games) → Under-14 (full field play, penalty corners, team concepts) → Under-16 (full game, tactical introduction) → Goalkeeper from under-14 (earlier damages development) → Safeguarding certification mandatory for all youth coaches.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safeguarding mandatory for all youth coaches
- Fun first: young player who does not enjoy it will not continue
- Goalkeeper specialisation from 14: younger is too early

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
