# Hockey Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey mental game: Between-passage routine (breathe, one positive word, refocus) → Error management (high error rate — accept and reset) → Penalty corner pressure (same routine every time) → Big moment execution (simplest reliable option beats creative risky one) → Momentum management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Routine: same between every restart — builds resilience
- Error acceptance: a player who fears errors will not take risks — risks score goals
- Big moment: simple reliable beats creative risky

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
