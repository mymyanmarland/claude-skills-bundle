# Hockey Pressing Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pressing: Press trigger (opponent receives with back to goal — press immediately) → Coordinated (first presser + cover + compact rest) → Half-court press (from centreline — less risky) → Full press (aggressive — very fit team required) → Transition after press fails (compact and retreat).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Coordinated press: one player pressing alone is ineffective
- Half-court press: safer default — full press only if elite fitness
- Transition after press fails: immediate compactness not individual chasing

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
