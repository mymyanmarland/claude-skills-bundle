# Hockey Attacking Third Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Attacking third: Penetrating the D (movement and 1v1 elimination) → Centre passes (crosses from baseline) → Overloads → One-touch play (prevents defenders recovering) → Set plays from sidelines → Switching play for shooting space → Penalty corner development.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One-touch in the D: two touches allows defenders to recover
- Switching play: move ball across face of D to find gaps
- Creating overloads: move quickly before defence reshapes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
