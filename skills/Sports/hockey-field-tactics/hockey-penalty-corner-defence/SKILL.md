# Penalty Corner Defence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Penalty corner defence: Rush (3-runner: blocker first, two beside / 4-runner) → Blocker stands on drag flicker line → Goalkeeper: decide rush or stay → After rush: defend from position → Safety equipment mandatory for all rush runners.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rush blocker stands directly on drag flicker line — not to the side
- Runners and goalkeeper must agree before the corner
- Protective equipment: shin guards and gloves mandatory for all rush runners

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
