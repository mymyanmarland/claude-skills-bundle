# Hockey Defensive Structure Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Defensive structure: Low block (compact, 11 behind ball when defending deep) → Zonal defence → Man-to-man in dangerous positions → Channels (force opponent wide) → Transition defence (immediate pressure when possession lost) → Set-piece defence → Goalkeeper communication (organises the line).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compact block: space between defensive lines is dangerous
- Transition defence: immediate pressure — not watching
- Goalkeeper communication: the organiser who sees the whole picture

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
