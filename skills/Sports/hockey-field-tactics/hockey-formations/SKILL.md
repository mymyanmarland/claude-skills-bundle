# Hockey Formations Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hockey formations: 4-3-3 (offensive, three attackers, midfield control) → 4-4-2 (balanced) → 5-3-2 (defensive) → 3-3-4 (very attacking — chasing a game) → Positional flexibility (modern hockey — versatile players) → Formation must match personnel.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Formation must match personnel: best system for your players
- Versatility: modern hockey players operate across multiple positions
- Adjust in game: trailing by a goal? move to 3-3-4 in the final quarter

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Sports Disclaimer: Field hockey involves physical contact and fast-moving equipment. Always wear appropriate protective equipment, play under qualified coaching and officiating, and seek medical clearance. Goalkeepers must wear full protective equipment at all times.
