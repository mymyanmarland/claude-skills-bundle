# Cycling Descending Safely Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Descending: Body position (low, weight back, arms slightly bent) → Braking (both brakes evenly, braking before corners — not in them) → Cornering at speed (look through the corner, inside pedal up, lean the bike, weight on outside pedal) → Wet descending (brake earlier and more gently — reduced traction) → Speed management (maintain a speed you can stop from safely) → Surface awareness (gravel, white paint, drain covers — all are slippery when wet).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brake before the corner: braking in a corner causes the bike to wash out
- Inside pedal up in corners: pedal strike is dangerous on steep corners
- Wet surfaces need 40-50% more braking distance — plan ahead

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
