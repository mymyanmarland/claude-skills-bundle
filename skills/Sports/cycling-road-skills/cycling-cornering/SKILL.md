# Cycling Cornering Mastery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cornering mastery: Entry speed (brake before the corner, carry speed through — not brake in the corner) → Vision (look through the exit of the corner, not at the apex) → Body position (outside pedal down, weight on outside foot, inside knee slightly out) → Lean angle (lean the bike more than the body) → Wet cornering (take wider lines, reduce lean angle, brake earlier) → Confidence building (practise at increasing speeds on safe quiet roads).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Look through the exit: where you look is where you go
- Outside pedal down: prevents pedal strike and lowers centre of gravity
- Wet weather: wider lines and earlier braking — not just slower entry

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
