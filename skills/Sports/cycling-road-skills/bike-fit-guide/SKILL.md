# Bike Fit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bike fit: Saddle height (heel on pedal, leg almost straight — pedalling position: 25-35 degree knee bend at the bottom of the stroke) → Saddle fore-aft (knee over pedal spindle when cranks are horizontal) → Handlebar height (beginners: higher for comfort / experienced: lower for aerodynamics) → Reach (slight bend in the elbows when hands are on the hoods) → Cleat position (ball of foot over pedal spindle) → Professional fit for serious riders (recommended once for a baseline).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Saddle height is the most important fit variable — too low or too high causes knee pain
- Cleat position affects knee tracking — misaligned cleats cause long-term injury
- Professional fit recommended: a £100 fit saves thousands in physio bills

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
