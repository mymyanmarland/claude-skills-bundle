# Road Cycling Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Road cycling technique: Pedalling efficiency (smooth circles, not just pushing down — pull up through the bottom of the stroke) → Cadence (80-100 rpm is efficient for most riders) → Body position (relaxed upper body, most weight on the saddle) → Climbing (seated: high cadence, low gear / standing: lower cadence, more power) → Descending (low body position, weight back and low, hands on the drops) → Cornering (look through the corner, lean the bike not just the body).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Smooth pedalling circles: stomping reduces efficiency significantly
- Descending hands on drops: lower centre of gravity and more control
- Cornering: look through the corner — the bike follows the eyes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
