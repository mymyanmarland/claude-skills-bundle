# Cycling Endurance Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling endurance: Building volume (weekly hours or kilometres increase no more than 10% per week) → Long ride (the centrepiece of the training week — 60-80% of weekly volume in one ride) → Zone 2 training (most rides at conversational pace — builds aerobic base) → Sweet spot training (88-93% FTP — efficient for time-limited riders) → Taper before an event (reduce volume 2 weeks before, maintain intensity) → Recovery week (every 3-4 weeks: reduce volume 30-40%).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Long ride is the most important training session of the week
- Zone 2: most cycling training should be done here — not tempo
- Taper: trust the reduction — fresh legs on event day beat fatigued legs

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
