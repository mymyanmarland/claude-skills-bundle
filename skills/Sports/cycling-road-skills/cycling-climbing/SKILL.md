# Cycling Climbing Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Climbing technique: Gear selection (stay in a gear that maintains cadence — shift before the gradient increases) → Seated climbing (relaxed upper body, smooth pedalling, pace yourself) → Standing climbing (grip the bars, rock the bike gently side to side, use for short steep sections) → Pacing a climb (start slower than comfortable — not faster) → Breathing rhythm (steady, controlled — not gasping at the bottom) → Mental approach (break the climb into sections).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Start slower than feels right: burning matches at the bottom kills the summit
- Stay seated: standing costs 10-15% more energy — use it for short bursts only
- Break climbs into sections: the next lamppost, tree, or bend

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
