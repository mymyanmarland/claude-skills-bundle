# Cycling Nutrition and Hydration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling nutrition: Before (carbohydrate-based meal 2-3 hours before, top up 60 min before) → During rides over 60 minutes (30-60g of carbohydrate per hour — gels, bars, or real food) → Hydration (500ml per hour — more in heat) → Electrolytes (sodium loss in sweat — replace with electrolyte drinks or food in long rides) → Recovery (protein and carbs within 45 minutes of finishing) → Bonking (hypoglycaemia from under-fuelling — eat before you are hungry). Note: consult a sports dietitian for personalised advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Eat before hunger: by the time you feel hungry, blood sugar is already low
- 60g carbs per hour for rides over 90 minutes
- Recovery window 45 minutes: the most important post-ride nutrition habit

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
