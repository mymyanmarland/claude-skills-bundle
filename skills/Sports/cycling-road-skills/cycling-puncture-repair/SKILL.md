# Cycling Puncture Repair and Maintenance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Puncture repair: Tools needed (tyre levers, spare inner tube, pump or CO2 — always carry) → Removing the wheel (quick release or thru-axle depending on the bike) → Removing the tyre (tyre levers — start opposite the valve) → Finding the puncture cause (check the tyre for embedded object) → Fitting the new tube (valve first, tuck tube in before seating bead) → Inflation (inflate gradually, check tyre is seated evenly) → Basic maintenance (chain lubrication, brake checks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Always carry two tubes, two levers, and a pump: one puncture is manageable, two is not
- Find the cause before fitting: a glass shard will puncture the new tube immediately
- Inflate to correct pressure: check the tyre sidewall for the recommended range

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
