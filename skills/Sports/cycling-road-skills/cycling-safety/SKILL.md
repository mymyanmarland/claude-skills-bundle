# Cycling Road Safety Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling safety: Helmet (mandatory personal choice — wear one) → Visibility (bright clothing, lights front and rear — even in daylight) → Road position (ride 1-1.5m from the kerb — not in the gutter) → Anticipation (read the traffic ahead, anticipate doors, junctions, and merging vehicles) → Eye contact with drivers (make eye contact before assuming they have seen you) → Signalling (clear hand signals before turning) → Riding with traffic (never against it).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Road position: riding in the gutter invites being squeezed — primary position is safer
- Lights in daylight: the single most effective visibility measure
- Eye contact: a driver who has not seen you cannot give way to you

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
