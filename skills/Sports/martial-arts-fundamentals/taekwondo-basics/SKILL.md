# Taekwondo Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Taekwondo basics: Stance (junbi sogi — ready) → Front kick (ap chagi — chamber, extend, snap back) → Roundhouse (dollyo chagi — chamber, pivot, rotate hip, extend) → Side kick (yop chagi — chamber sideways, extend heel-first) → Back kick (dwi chagi — turn, chamber, thrust) → Poomsae introduction → Protective equipment for all contact training.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Kicking chamber: all kicks chamber first
- Pivot foot: roundhouse power from hip rotation not just the leg
- Protective equipment mandatory for any partner contact

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
