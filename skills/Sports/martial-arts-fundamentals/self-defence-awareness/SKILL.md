# Self-Defence Awareness and Avoidance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Self-defence awareness: Situational awareness (best self-defence is avoiding dangerous situations) → Colour code (white unaware / yellow relaxed alert / orange specific threat / red action) → De-escalation (voice, body language, distance) → Verbal boundary setting → Escape and avoidance over confrontation → Physical confrontation: primary goal is to escape safely → Physical techniques under qualified instruction only.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Avoidance and de-escalation are always better than confrontation
- Situational awareness: the most effective self-defence skill
- Physical techniques only discussed as last resort — escape is the goal

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
