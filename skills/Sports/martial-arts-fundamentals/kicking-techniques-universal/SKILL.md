# Universal Kicking Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Universal kicks: Front kick (chamber, extend, snap back) → Roundhouse (chamber, pivot, rotate hip, strike) → Side kick (chamber laterally, extend heel) → Back kick (turn, chamber, thrust) → Spinning back kick → Kick chamber as universal principle → Flexibility requirements.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Kick chamber: every kick starts with the knee coming up
- Pivot foot: without the pivot the roundhouse has no power
- Flexibility: hip flexor and hamstring limit kick height and efficiency

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
