# Universal Striking Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Universal striking: Guard (hands protect face in all striking arts) → Relaxed until impact → Power from ground up (floor, hips, shoulder, fist) → Jab (lead, quick) → Cross (rear, full rotation) → Hook (circular, elbow 90 degrees) → Uppercut (upward, bend knees, drive up) → Elbow strikes (short range, powerful) → Return every strike to guard.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Power from ground up: legs and hips generate most power
- Relaxation: tense muscles slow the strike
- Return to guard: every strike not returned leaves you open

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
