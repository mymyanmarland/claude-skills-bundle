# Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

BJJ basics: Positional hierarchy (guard/half guard/side control/mount/back) → Guard closed (legs around opponent) → Mount escapes (trap and roll / elbow-knee) → Side control escapes (frame and recover) → Basic submissions (rear naked choke / triangle / armbar) → Tap early — the most important safety habit → Hygiene (wash gi after every session).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tap early: late tapping causes injuries and ends training careers
- Positional hierarchy: understand position before attempting submissions
- Hygiene: wash gi every session — skin infections are serious

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
