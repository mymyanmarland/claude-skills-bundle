# Martial Arts for Children Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Martial arts for children: Benefits (discipline, focus, fitness, confidence, respect) → Age-appropriate starting points (5+) → What to look for (qualified instructor / child safeguarding / non-aggressive / fun) → Red flags (aggressive culture, contact too early, pressure) → Suitable styles (karate, judo, taekwondo — structured, clear progressions) → No full-contact under-16s → Safeguarding requirements for instructors.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Child safeguarding policies: mandatory — ask to see them
- No full-contact under 16: light technical sparring with equipment only
- Fun first: a child who is not enjoying it will not develop

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
