# Martial Arts Dojo Etiquette Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dojo etiquette: Bowing (upon entering and leaving, to instructor and partners) → Addressing the instructor → Uniform care (clean gi every session) → Punctuality → Senior-junior relationships → Respect for training partners → Leaving ego at the door → Purpose of martial arts (discipline, character, physical skills — not aggression).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bowing: mutual respect not submission — everyone bows
- Clean uniform: respect for the art and your training partners
- Ego at the door: the dojo is for growth not proving superiority

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
