# Karate Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Karate basics: Stances (zenkutsu-dachi front / kiba-dachi horse / kokutsu-dachi back) → Punches (choku-zuki — drive from hip rotation) → Blocks (age-uke rising / soto-uke outside / uchi-uke inside / gedan-barai downward) → Kicks (mae-geri front kick — chamber before extending) → Kiai (exhalation at point of technique) → Bowing etiquette.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stances: every technique starts from a correct stance
- Hip rotation: karate power comes from the hips not the arms
- Dojo etiquette: bowing is mutual respect — taught from lesson one

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
