# Judo Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Judo basics: Ukemi (breakfalls) first — fall safely before being thrown → Basic throws (o-soto-gari outer reaping / ippon-seoi-nage shoulder throw / o-goshi hip throw) → Grip fighting (kumi-kata) → Ground work basics (osaekomi hold-downs) → Randori after basics are established → Bowing and rei etiquette.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ukemi first: a student who cannot fall safely cannot be thrown safely
- Breakfalls must be automatic before randori begins
- Bowing: judo tradition and respect are inseparable from technique

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
