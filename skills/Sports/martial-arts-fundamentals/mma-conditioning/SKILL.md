# MMA and Combat Sports Conditioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Martial art style
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Training goals: fitness/self-defence/competition
- Gym or club access
- Age and physical condition

## Step 2 — Core Output

MMA conditioning: Energy systems (striking explosive / grappling lactic / pacing aerobic) → 5-minute rounds with 1-minute rest → Circuit training (striking/wrestling/ground in rounds) → Strength (takedowns, punching, clinch) → Aerobic base (supports recovery) → Hip flexibility (kicks and takedowns) → Mental conditioning under fatigue.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 5-minute rounds: train in the format you compete in
- All three energy systems: MMA demands all — train all three
- Mental conditioning: performing technical skills while exhausted is learnable

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Martial arts and combat sports carry inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified instructor, use appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance before starting any combat sport or martial art.
