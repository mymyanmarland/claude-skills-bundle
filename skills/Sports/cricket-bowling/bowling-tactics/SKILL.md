# Bowling Tactics and Field Setting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bowling tactics: Reading the batter (watch feet movement, grip changes, wagon wheel) → Setting a trap (set field for shot they will play) → Over plans (varied ball selection, not random) → Dot ball pressure (before the wicket ball) → Death bowling T20 (yorker + wide yorker + bouncer combination) → New ball vs old ball strategy → Communicating with captain and keeper.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Set the trap before bowling the wicket ball — not first ball
- Read feet movement — it reveals the batter's plan
- Over plan agreed with captain before each over

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
