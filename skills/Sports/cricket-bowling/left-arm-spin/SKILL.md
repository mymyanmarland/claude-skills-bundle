# Left-Arm Spin Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Left-arm orthodox: Grip (mirrored off-spin grip) → Turn: going away from right-handed batter → Flight and drift (ball drifts into right-hander then turns away) → Arm ball (stays straight, variation) → Over the wicket: angling into right-hander → Around the wicket: angling away → Chinaman (left-arm wrist spin): advanced variation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Drift then turn — the left-arm spinner's greatest weapon
- Over vs around the wicket changes the angle completely
- Arm ball same action — deception requires identical release
