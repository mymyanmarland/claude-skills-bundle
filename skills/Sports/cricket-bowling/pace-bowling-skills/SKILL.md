# Pace Bowling Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pace bowling: Generating pace (hip drive, shoulder rotation, wrist behind ball) → Line and length (good length on off-stump corridor — 60% of deliveries) → Outswing setup (seam upright, angled toward fine leg, shiny side off) → Inswing setup (seam upright, shiny side on leg) → Bouncer (targeting shoulder/helmet zone) → Yorker (targeting base of stumps) → Slower ball (deeper grip, same action).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Good length corridor — not half-volleys or long-hops
- Seam position specific for swing — general tips do not work
- Practise the yorker daily in nets — it is a wicket-taking delivery

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
