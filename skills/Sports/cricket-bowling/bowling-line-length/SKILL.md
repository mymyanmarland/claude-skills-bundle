# Bowling Line and Length Mastery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Line and length mastery: Good length definition by format (Test: 6-8m / ODI: slightly fuller / T20: full) → Off-stump corridor (between off and fourth stump) → Pitch maps in practice (mark zones, record deliveries) → Targets in nets (cone on good length off-stump) → Feedback loop (video or partner calls) → Over plans (4 good lengths + 1 bouncer + 1 yorker per over) → Concentration drills (6 perfect balls in a row before moving on).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pitch maps in practice — what gets measured improves
- Off-stump corridor — 60% of balls here minimum
- Plan each over before bowling it — no reactive short-pitched balls

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
