# Off-Spin Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Off-spin technique: Grip (index and middle finger across seam) → Side-on delivery stride → Finger-spin imparted (roll fingers over the top at release) → Flight and loop (toss it up — spin maximised in the air) → Arm ball (no finger spin — straight through) → Line: over or around the wicket into the rough → Length: flight to tempt drive, shorter to defend.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Flight is the off-spinner's wicket-taking tool — do not bowl flat
- Arm ball concealed — same action as off-spinner
- Bowl into footmarks around the wicket — angles create problems

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
