# Leg-Spin Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Leg-spin: Grip (ball in fingers, third finger across seam) → Wrist position (back of hand faces batter = leg-break) → Loop and dip (high arc, late dip) → Googly (wrist rotated further, ball goes other way) → Flipper (squeezed from front of hand, low trajectory, skids) → Top-spinner (extra bounce) → 7 out of 10 leg-breaks on target before learning variations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Control before variation — erratic leg-spin is ineffective
- Wrist position is everything — not arm or shoulder
- Googly only when leg-break is consistent — not a crutch
