# Bowling Fitness and Load Management Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bowling fitness: Core strength (anti-rotation exercises, side planks) → Hip mobility (pace bowling force through hips — tight hips cause injury) → Shoulder health (rotator cuff prehab: band work, external rotation) → Load management (under-16s: 36 overs/week maximum) → Recovery between spells (bowling is explosive — treat like sprint intervals) → Warning signs of overload.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Load management under-16s strict — growth plate injury risk
- Core before shoulder — power chain starts at centre
- Warm up bowling minimum 10 minutes before full effort

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
