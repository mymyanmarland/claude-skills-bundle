# Bowling Action Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bowling action: Run-up length and consistency (mark and repeat) → Load-up position (side-on or front-on — commit to one) → Bound (gather phase — coil for energy) → Front arm pull-down (high front arm drives pace and accuracy) → Hip rotation (hips through first, then shoulder) → Release point (consistent high release) → Follow-through (complete it — injury prevention). Checklist for each phase.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Run-up same every ball — inconsistency kills rhythm
- High front arm — the single most impactful pace cue
- Complete follow-through — stopping causes back injuries

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
