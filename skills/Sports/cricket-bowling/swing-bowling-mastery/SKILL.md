# Swing Bowling Mastery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Swing bowling: Conventional outswing (seam upright, angled, shiny side off, fingers behind ball) → Conventional inswing (shiny side on leg) → Ball maintenance (keep one side shiny — legal polishing only) → Conditions for swing (overcast, humidity, new ball) → Reverse swing (old ball 35+ overs, rough side out) → Contrast swing (from 20 overs).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Seam position specific and consistent — not guessed
- Reverse swing needs old ball — not a new ball technique
- Wrist position behind ball at release — key to late swing

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
