# Age Grade Rugby Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Age-grade rugby: Under-8 (non-contact tag rugby) → Under-10 (tag, very light contact introduction) → Under-12 (modified contact — World Rugby Rookie Rugby) → Under-14 (fuller contact, lineouts, scrums) → Under-16 (full game with adjusted laws) → Concussion protocol mandatory at all ages → Multi-sport encouraged.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Non-contact under-10: World Rugby guidance — protect young players
- Concussion protocol mandatory: applies to all ages
- Multi-sport: young rugby players who play other sports are better athletes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Contact Sport Disclaimer: Rugby is a full-contact sport with inherent risk of injury. Always play under qualified coaching and officiating, wear appropriate protective equipment, and seek medical clearance. Follow all World Rugby laws and concussion protocols.
