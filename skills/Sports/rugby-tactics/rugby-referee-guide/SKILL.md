# Rugby Laws Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby laws: Offside (open play: behind the ball / ruck: back foot of rearmost player) → Knock-on (ball forward off hands — scrum) → Forward pass (scrum) → Maul and ruck (formation and what is legal) → Penalty vs free kick (free kick cannot be kicked for goal) → Yellow and red cards → Tackling height laws → Advantage.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Offside: most frequently penalised law in rugby
- Forward pass: judgment call — straight across is not forward
- Tackling height laws: evolving — always check current World Rugby regulations

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
