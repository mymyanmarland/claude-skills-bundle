# Kicking Game Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Kicking game: Territorial (kick into opposition 22) → Exit strategy (relieve pressure from own half) → Box kick (contested) → Kick chase (team chases together) → Kick to space (behind the winger) → Counter-attacking → Wind assessment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Territory: kicking deep is winning strategy in bad conditions
- Box kick: requires both good kick and fast chase
- Wind: always kick with it in the first half if possible

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
