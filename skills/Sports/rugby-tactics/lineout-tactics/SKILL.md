# Lineout Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lineout tactics: Own lineout (rehearsed calls — attacking platform) → Throw variation (front/middle/back confuses defence) → Short lineout → Maul from lineout → Defending (offside line 5m from touchline) → Complex coded calls.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Vary the throw: predictable lineouts are stolen
- Short lineout: the element of surprise is valuable
- Maul from lineout: one of the most effective close-range try-scoring options

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
