# Rugby Game Management Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game management: Score management (ahead: territory / behind: attack wide) → Time management (use all 80 minutes) → Weather adaptation (wet: tight and forward / dry: wide game) → Penalty decisions (points/touch/quick tap) → Yellow card management (14 players for 10 minutes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Score and time: the two most important variables in any decision
- Weather adaptation: game plan must adjust to conditions
- 14-player management: reorganise defence immediately after yellow card

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
