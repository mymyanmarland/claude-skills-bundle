# Scrum Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scrum tactics: Number 8 pick-up → Box kick from 9 → Attack right → Attack left → Reading scrum pressure → Penalty decision (kick/touch/re-scrum) → Defensive scrum (stay until ball is out).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scrum dominance creates choices: losing scrums create few options
- Number 8 pick-up: effective near the tryline when scrum dominant
- Penalty choice: consider score and time before deciding

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
