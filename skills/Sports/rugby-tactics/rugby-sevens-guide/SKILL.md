# Rugby Sevens Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby sevens: 7 players / 7-minute halves → Fitness demands (explosive, repeated, very high intensity) → Wider spaces (speed and decision-making critical) → Defence (fewer defenders — line speed essential) → Ruck quick ball → Sevens fitness (sprinting with ball, repeated short bursts).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sevens fitness: among the most demanding in all rugby
- Wide spaces: every player needs genuine pace
- Decision-making speed: space appears and closes faster with fewer players

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
