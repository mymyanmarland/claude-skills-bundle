# Rugby Defensive Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby defence: Line speed (flat line moves forward together) → Rush defence (attack ball carrier — high risk high reward) → Drift defence (slide along line, push play into touch) → Communication (blue/red — common defensive calls) → Covering the kick → Ruck offside management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Line speed together: one late defender creates a gap
- Rush defence: if it fails space behind is exposed
- Drift defence: safer against attacking width

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
