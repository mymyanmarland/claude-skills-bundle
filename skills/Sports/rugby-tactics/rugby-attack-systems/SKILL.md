# Rugby Attack Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rugby attack: Phase play (keep ball through multiple phases) → Width and depth (stretch defence) → Quick ball (fast recycling pressures defensive line) → Set-piece attack (lineout and scrum as platforms) → Gainline (crossing it puts defence under pressure) → Pick and go → Overload.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gainline crossing: attack not crossing loses momentum
- Quick ball: slow ball gives defence time to reset
- Width: narrow attack is easy to defend — stretch before going through the middle

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
