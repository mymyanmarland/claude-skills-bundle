# GPS and Wearable Data Analysis Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and competitive level
- Current data collection methods
- Technical capability of coaching staff
- Budget for analytics tools
- Specific performance questions to answer

## Step 2 — Core Output

GPS and wearables: Key GPS metrics (total distance / high-speed running distance / sprint count / max velocity / accelerations and decelerations) → Heart rate monitoring (training load / recovery / intensity distribution) → HRV (Heart Rate Variability — readiness indicator for training load adjustment) → Data interpretation (compare to baseline — not absolute values) → Load monitoring (training load + recovery = adaptation) → Practical thresholds (flag athletes who are 15% above their typical weekly load).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compare to baseline: individual norms matter more than population norms
- HRV for readiness: morning HRV consistently low signals accumulated fatigue
- 15% above typical load: the threshold where injury risk increases significantly

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
