# Athlete Performance Profiling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and competitive level
- Current data collection methods
- Technical capability of coaching staff
- Budget for analytics tools
- Specific performance questions to answer

## Step 2 — Core Output

Performance profiling: What it measures (the athlete's perception of what qualities matter and where they stand) → The process (list key qualities with the athlete / athlete self-rates 1-10 / compare to ideal / identify gaps) → Physical profile (speed/strength/endurance/power) → Technical profile (sport-specific skills) → Tactical profile (decision-making, game reading) → Psychological profile (confidence/focus/resilience) → Goal setting from the profile.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Athlete-led: the athlete identifies qualities — more ownership than coach-imposed
- Self-rating: perception gaps (athlete vs coach ratings) are often the most useful finding
- Goal setting from the profile: 1-2 gaps to focus on — not everything at once

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
