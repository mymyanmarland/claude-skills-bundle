# Sports Statistics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and competitive level
- Current data collection methods
- Technical capability of coaching staff
- Budget for analytics tools
- Specific performance questions to answer

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sports statistics: Descriptive statistics (mean / median / mode — understanding central tendency) → Variance and range (how consistent is the performance?) → Percentile ranking (where does this athlete sit relative to peers?) → Rate statistics (per 90 minutes / per game — normalise for playing time) → Correlation vs causation (two things happening together does not mean one causes the other) → Sample size (small samples are unreliable — 5 games is not enough to draw conclusions) → Practical significance vs statistical significance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Correlation vs causation: the most important statistical concept for coaches
- Sample size: 5 games is not enough — resist over-interpretation of small samples
- Rate statistics: normalise for playing time before comparing players
