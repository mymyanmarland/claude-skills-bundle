# Archery Aiming Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Archery aiming: Sight picture (ring of the aperture surrounds the target face or gold) → Aiming process (settle on the gold, maintain anchor, squeeze through the shot) → Aiming time (short — 3-6 seconds is optimal for most archers) → Breathing (exhale partially, hold, aim and shoot — not holding a full breath) → Mental focus during aiming (focus on the process, not the outcome) → Bow movement during aiming (some movement is normal — aim at the centre on average).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short aiming time: more than 6-8 seconds causes fatigue and poor release
- Partial exhale: too much breath held causes trembling in the bow arm
- Aim at centre: perfect stillness is impossible — aim in the gold zone and shoot
