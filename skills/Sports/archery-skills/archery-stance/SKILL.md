# Archery Stance and Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Archery stance: Square stance (feet shoulder-width perpendicular to the target — beginner default) → Open stance (lead foot turned slightly toward target — used by many recurve archers) → Weight distribution (even on both feet) → Body alignment (hips and shoulders square to the target) → Head position (upright, turned to face the target) → Relaxed shoulders (tension in the upper body transfers to the shot) → Consistent foot position (mark it or practise from the same point every shot).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shoulder relaxation: tension in the bow shoulder causes the arrow to fly right for right-handers
- Head position: tucking the chin or raising it changes the anchor point
- Consistent foot placement: foot position determines body alignment — same every shot

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
