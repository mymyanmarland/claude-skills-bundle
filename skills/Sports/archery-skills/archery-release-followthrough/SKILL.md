# Archery Release and Follow-Through Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Release and follow-through: Release mechanics (the draw hand relaxes — the bow pushes the string away / not a deliberate open of the fingers) → Surprise release (the click of the clicker or the natural end of the aiming process triggers the shot — not a planned deliberate action) → Follow-through (bow arm stays up and aimed at the target after release) → Draw hand (moves backward along the face naturally after release) → Not watching the arrow (maintain the follow-through, not peeking).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Surprise release: a deliberate release causes a flinch that moves the bow
- Bow arm stays up: dropping the arm before the arrow clears the bow affects the shot
- Follow-through holds for 2-3 seconds: this is where aiming discipline is trained

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
