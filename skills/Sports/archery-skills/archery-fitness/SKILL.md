# Archery-Specific Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Archery fitness: Back strength (the draw is powered by the rhomboids and rotator cuff — row variations) → Rotator cuff health (archery places repeated stress on the shoulder — prehab essential) → Core stability (stable platform for every shot) → Bow arm stability (shoulder stability — external rotation strength) → Endurance (tournament archery involves 70-144 arrows over many hours — aerobic base) → Grip strength (not crushing the bow — support grip endurance) → Stretch cord training (bow-specific resistance exercise).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rotator cuff prehab: archery is repetitive shoulder use — prehab prevents long-term injury
- Back strength: rhomboids and traps are the primary drawing muscles
- Stretch cord: the most archery-specific warm-up and training tool

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
