# Archery Equipment Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Equipment setup: Arrow selection (spine must match bow draw weight and draw length — consult a chart or pro shop) → Arrow rest positioning (centred, perpendicular to the string) → Nocking point (where the arrow sits on the string — consistent height) → Brace height (distance from grip to string — affects performance and hand clearance) → Paper tuning (shoot through paper — tear tells you how to adjust the rest) → Sight setup (start at 18m for indoor) → Stabiliser weight and position.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Arrow spine: wrong spine is the number one cause of poor groups — get it right first
- Paper tuning: the most reliable way to confirm bow tune
- Brace height: outside the manufacturer's range causes consistency and safety issues

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
