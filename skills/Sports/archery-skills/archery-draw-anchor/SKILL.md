# Archery Draw and Anchor Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Draw and anchor: Grip (bow hand relaxed — push the bow with the thumb pad, not a tight fist) → Draw hand (hooks fingers around the string — one or three fingers depending on style) → Drawing motion (use the back muscles to draw, not just the arm — elbow should travel behind the body) → Anchor point (consistent contact point on the face — corner of mouth or chin for recurve) → Full draw position (bow arm extended, draw elbow behind the line of the arrow) → Aiming before release.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Back muscles draw: arm-only drawing causes fatigue and inconsistency
- Bow hand relaxed: gripping the bow rotates the face at release
- Anchor point the same every shot: variation here changes every arrow
