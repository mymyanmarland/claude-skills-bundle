# Archery Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Archery mental game: Pre-shot routine (same every arrow — stance, grip, draw, aim, release) → Process focus (focus on what you control — the process, not the score) → Error management (one bad arrow is one arrow — reset and execute the next) → Competition nerves (normal physiological response — use breathing to regulate) → Score detachment (shoot the arrow, not the scorecard) → Visualisation (see the perfect execution before drawing the bow).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-shot routine: every arrow the same — consistency in the process creates consistency in the result
- One bad arrow: let it go completely — it cannot be changed, the next can be executed well
- Process focus: the score takes care of itself when the process is right
