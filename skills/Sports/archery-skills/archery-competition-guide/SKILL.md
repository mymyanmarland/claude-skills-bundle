# Archery Competition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Archery style: recurve/compound/barebow
- Experience level
- Training environment: indoor/outdoor/range
- Equipment available
- Goals: recreational/club/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Archery competition: Round formats (indoor: 18m / outdoor: 70m recurve / 50m compound) → Equipment checks (everything legal and functioning before the day) → Practice day (use it — arrive early, settle in) → Warm-up routine (same as in training) → Arrow collection and scoring etiquette (wait until the end of each end to walk to the target) → Calling arrows (archer calls their score, the recorder confirms) → Dealing with equipment issues (carry spare strings and arrows) → Post-competition review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Equipment check the day before: discovering a broken nocking point at the range costs concentration
- Arrow collection etiquette: never retrieve before the signal
- Post-competition review: identify patterns, not individual bad arrows

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
