# Back Foot Play Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Back foot technique: Back and across movement (trigger move, weight on back foot, head still) → Back foot defence (high elbow, soft hands, angled bat face) → Cut shot (back foot to off side, horizontal bat, hit through point) → Pull shot (back foot to leg side, swivel hips, hit in front of body) → Hook shot (high back lift, top-edging awareness) → Reaction drills (short-pitch throwdowns building pace gradually).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Back and across completed before ball arrives
- Head still during back foot shots — no swaying
- Pull in front of body — reduces top edge risk
