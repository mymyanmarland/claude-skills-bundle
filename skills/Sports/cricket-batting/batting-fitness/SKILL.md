# Batting-Specific Fitness Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Batting fitness: Reaction time training (reflex ball, reaction lights, partner drills) → Footwork speed (ladder drills, side-to-side shuffle) → Core stability (bat speed and balance both depend on core) → Running fitness (between wickets — short sprint intervals, 17m turns) → Hand-eye coordination drills → Grip strength (forearm work for long innings).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reaction training separate from skill work — specific adaptation
- Short sprint intervals — batting runs are 17m not 100m
- Core stability before arm strength — power comes from the middle

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
