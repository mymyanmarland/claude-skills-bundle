# Power Hitting Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Power hitting: Foundation (bat speed over brute force) → Slog sweep mechanics (hip rotation, horizontal bat, hit through ball) → Straight slog (clear front leg, swing from shoulders) → Scoop/ramp (get low, open bat face, use pace of ball) → Batting in the arc (target your strong zones) → Pre-planned shots for specific deliveries (full toss, short ball, wide yorker).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bat speed not arm strength — rotate through the ball
- Pre-planned shots — reacting to a yorker is too slow
- Hit in your strong zones more than 60% of the time

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
