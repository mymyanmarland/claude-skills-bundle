# Batting Stance Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stance setup: Foot alignment (parallel to crease, shoulder-width apart) → Weight distribution (50/50 on balls of feet, slight knee bend) → Bat position (grounded near back toe, face slightly open) → Head position (level, eyes horizontal, front shoulder pointing toward bowler) → Grip check (top hand dominant, V's pointing toward off-stump) → Guard selection (middle, leg, or off). Deliver a step-by-step checklist the batter can self-check in a mirror or with a partner.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Weight on balls of feet — flat-footed stance kills footwork
- Head level — tilted eyes create misjudgement of line
- Top hand dominant — bottom hand provides power only

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
