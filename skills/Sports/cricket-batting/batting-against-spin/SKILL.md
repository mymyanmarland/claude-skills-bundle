# Batting Against Spin Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Playing spin: Reading the spinner (wrist position, seam, flight) → Use of crease (back foot deep to create time, front foot full to kill spin) → The big stride (get to the pitch of the ball to drive) → Sweep shot mechanics (front knee down, bat horizontal, hit toward square leg) → Defensive play against turn (soft hands, angled bat) → Attacking: slog sweep, dance down the track.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Use of crease — standing in crease gives spin time to bite
- Big stride kills spin — half-stride is half-measure
- Sweep is a percentage shot against spin — learn it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
