# Batting Drills and Practice Plans Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Batting drills: Solo (shadow batting — footwork patterns, stance checks, mirror work) → Throwdown drills (front foot drives to specific zones, back foot progressions) → Net session structure (first 10 balls: leave and defend / next 20: specific shot practice / final 10: free hit) → Machine drills (line and length variation) → Weak-area drill focus (if cutting poorly: 15 cuts per session minimum).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Quality over quantity — 50 focused balls beats 200 mindless ones
- Structure every session — random practice loses improvement
- Track what you practise — measure improvement over weeks

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
