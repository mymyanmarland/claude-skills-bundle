# Playing Swing and Seam Bowling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Playing swing and seam: Watch the seam position → Leave policy (widen the off-stump leave early innings) → Play late (wait longer before committing) → Head position (stay behind the line) → Soft hands on defensive shots → Inswing: front pad and bat together → Outswing: bat close to pad, no gap → Build the innings: leave more in first 30 balls.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Playing late — the most important skill against swing
- Leave outside off — every ball left is a dot ball, not a risk
- Soft hands — edges go to ground not slips
