# Front Foot Drive Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Front foot drives: Weight transfer mechanics (front foot to pitch of ball, head over knee) → Cover drive (open face, swing through ball, follow-through toward mid-off) → Straight drive (bat face straight, through the line) → On drive (rotate hips, bat face toward mid-on) → Off drive (full swing, bat face open toward cover) → Timing drill: shadow counting 'step-swing-follow' → Common faults: reaching, falling over, bottom hand taking over.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Head over front knee — not reaching with arms
- Full follow-through — stopping the bat kills timing
- Practise each drive separately before combining

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
