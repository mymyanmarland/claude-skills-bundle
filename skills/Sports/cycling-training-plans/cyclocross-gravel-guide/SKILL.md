# Cyclocross and Gravel Cycling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cyclocross and gravel: Tyre selection (wider tyres, lower pressure for grip off-road) → Dismounting and remounting (cyclocross: dismount before obstacles, shoulder the bike, run, remount) → Sand and mud technique (momentum over technique — keep power on) → Gravel descending (wider line, lower speed, braking earlier than tarmac) → Gravel nutrition (longer rides — carry more food and water) → Gravel community (typically more relaxed racing culture than road).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lower tyre pressure: more grip on loose surfaces — experiment with pressure
- Momentum in mud: stopping means sinking — keep pedalling
- Gravel nutrition: carry more than you think you need — no feed stations on a gravel course

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
