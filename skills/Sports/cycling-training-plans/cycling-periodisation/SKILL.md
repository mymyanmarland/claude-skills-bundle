# Cycling Periodisation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling periodisation: Base phase (high volume, low intensity — build aerobic foundation) → Build phase (add structure: sweet spot, threshold, VO2max work) → Peak phase (reduce volume, increase intensity, race-specific work) → Taper (2-3 weeks before target event — reduce volume 40-50%, maintain intensity) → Transition (post-season recovery — unstructured riding or rest) → Annual training plan (identify 1-3 A-events, B-events around them).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Base phase must not be rushed: 8-12 weeks minimum for meaningful base
- Taper trust: performance anxiety during taper is normal — it is working
- Annual plan: choose A-events first, then build the rest around them

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
