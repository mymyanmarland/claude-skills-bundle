# Strength Training for Cyclists Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling strength training: Single-leg squat (most cycling-specific — replicates pedalling mechanics) → Romanian deadlift (hamstring and glute — power through the pedal stroke) → Hip hinge (powerful glutes generate more watts) → Core stability (transfers power from legs through the core) → Upper body (cyclists neglect it — shoulder and back health for riding position) → Plyometrics (box jumps — develops explosive pedalling power) → Twice weekly in off-season, once in season.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single-leg squat: most cycling-specific gym exercise — bilateral misses the asymmetry
- Core stability: a weak core leaks power that strong legs generate
- Off-season emphasis: reduce gym work in-season — recovery is priority

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
