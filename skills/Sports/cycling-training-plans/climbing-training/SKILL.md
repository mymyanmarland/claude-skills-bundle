# Cycling Climbing Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Climbing training: Watts per kilo (power-to-weight ratio is the key climbing metric) → Improving w/kg (raise FTP or reduce body weight — ideally both) → Seated climbing intervals (long threshold efforts on the bike — 2×20 min at threshold) → Standing climbing (shorter, higher power — 3×10 min with standing sections) → Specific climb repeats (if a target climb exists, train on it) → Hill reps (short, steep repetitions — anaerobic capacity) → Weight: approach weight management sensibly and with professional support.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- W/kg is the climbing metric: power and weight are both levers
- Threshold intervals on climbs: the most specific climbing training
- Weight management: only with professional nutritional support — health first

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
