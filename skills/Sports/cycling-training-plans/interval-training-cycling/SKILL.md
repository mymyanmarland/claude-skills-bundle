# Cycling Interval Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling intervals: VO2max intervals (5×5 min at 106-120% FTP, 5 min rest — raises aerobic ceiling) → Threshold intervals (2-3×20 min at 95-105% FTP, 5 min rest — raises sustainable power) → Sweet spot (2-3×20 min at 88-93% FTP — high benefit, manageable fatigue) → Anaerobic intervals (10-20×30 sec all-out, 30 sec rest) → Sprints (6-10×10 sec maximum, full recovery — neuromuscular power) → Workout structure (warm-up / intervals / cool-down).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sweet spot: the best effort-to-gain ratio for time-limited cyclists
- VO2max intervals: hard but the fastest route to a higher aerobic ceiling
- Full recovery for sprints: insufficient rest trains a different system

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
