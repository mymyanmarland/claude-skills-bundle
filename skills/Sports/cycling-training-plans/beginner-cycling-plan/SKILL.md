# Beginner Cycling Training Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beginner plan: Weeks 1-3 (base building — 3 rides per week, all easy, 45-60 min) → Weeks 4-6 (add one longer ride — 90 min / keep two rides short and easy) → Weeks 7-9 (introduce one 20-min moderate effort per week) → Weeks 10-12 (add one interval session per week — short sweet spot efforts) → Consistency over intensity → Rest days (at least 2 per week) → Progression markers (able to hold a conversation throughout all rides in weeks 1-3).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Consistency is more important than any single ride
- All easy in the first 4 weeks: building the aerobic base that everything else rests on
- Conversational pace test: if you cannot speak in sentences, it is too hard

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
