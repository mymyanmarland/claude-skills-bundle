# Cycling Recovery Methods Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling recovery: Active recovery ride (very easy, Zone 1, 30-45 min — promotes blood flow without adding fatigue) → Nutrition recovery (protein and carbs within 45 min of finishing) → Sleep (the primary recovery tool — 7-9 hours for training cyclists) → Foam rolling (legs: quads, hamstrings, calves — not the IT band directly) → Cold water therapy (cold bath or cold shower for legs after very hard efforts) → Days off (mandatory — fatigue accumulates without rest).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Active recovery ride: easier than you think — if in doubt, go slower
- Sleep: no training stress reliever is more effective
- Mandatory rest days: at least one full day off per week

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
