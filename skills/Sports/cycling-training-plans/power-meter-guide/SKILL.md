# Power Meter Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Power meter training: What a power meter measures (watts — objective, not affected by heat, wind, or fatigue like heart rate is) → Setting up power zones (from FTP test) → Training with power (stay in the zone — not drifting above it on climbs) → TSS (Training Stress Score — quantifies workout load) → CTL, ATL, TSB (fitness, fatigue, form — training load metrics) → Pacing with power (hold target watts, not target speed) → Software options (TrainingPeaks, Garmin Connect, Zwift).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Power over speed: speed is affected by wind and gradient — power is not
- Stay in zone: drifting above target zone on climbs is a very common error
- TSS tracking: ensures progressive overload and adequate recovery

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
