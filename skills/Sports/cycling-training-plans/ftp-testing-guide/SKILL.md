# FTP Testing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

FTP testing: What FTP is (the maximum power a cyclist can sustain for approximately 60 minutes — the anchor of all training zones) → 20-minute FTP test protocol (warm-up 20 min / all-out 20 min effort / FTP = 95% of 20-min average power) → Ramp test alternative (easier to execute, slightly different result) → Training zones from FTP (Zone 1: recovery / Zone 2: endurance / Zone 3: tempo / Zone 4: threshold / Zone 5: VO2max / Zone 6: anaerobic) → Retesting (every 6-8 weeks or after a training block).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 95% of 20-minute power: not 100% — accounts for pacing difference
- Retest every 6-8 weeks: training raises FTP — zones must be updated
- Warm-up properly: a cold FTP test underestimates real FTP

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
