# Indoor Cycling and Zwift Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Indoor cycling guide: Smart trainer setup (resistance unit + Zwift or similar app) → Benefits (no traffic, no weather, structured workouts, racing) → Limitations (heat build-up — fan essential / boredom — music or entertainment / sweat management — towel and mat) → Zwift basics (ride, race, or workout modes) → Structured workouts (follow a plan within the app) → Zwift racing (real competition — treat like outdoor racing) → Alternative platforms (TrainerRoad, Rouvy, Kinomap).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fan essential: core temperature rises much faster indoors without airflow
- Sweat management: a puddle under the trainer corrodes the unit — use a mat
- Zwift racing: riders who sit in and sprint at the end always win — same tactics as outdoor

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
