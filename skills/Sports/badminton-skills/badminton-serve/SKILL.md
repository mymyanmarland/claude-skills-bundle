# Badminton Serve Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Badminton serve: Short serve (low over the net, land in the front service line area — brush the shuttle) → Flick serve (disguise as short serve, suddenly flick past the net player to the rear) → Drive serve (flat and fast, middle of the court) → Service rules (shuttle below the waist, both feet stationary) → Service position (close to the centre line for best angle coverage).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short serve low: if it passes high, it invites an attack
- Flick serve disguise: preparation identical to short serve
- Both feet stationary: service fault is a free point conceded
