# Badminton Doubles Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Doubles tactics: Attack formation (one up, one back — attacker smashes from rear, net player intercepts) → Defence formation (both players side by side) → Transition (from defence to attack — clear the shuttle, reorganise, go back to attack) → Communication ('yours' / 'mine') → Service tactics (short to body, flick to rear) → Rotation (rotate between attack and defence smoothly).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Attack formation: net player intercepts lifts — not a spectator
- Transition: clear to rear court, then reorganise attack formation
- Communication: call early — silence in doubles causes collisions

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
