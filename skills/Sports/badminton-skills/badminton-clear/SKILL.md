# Badminton Clear Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Badminton clear: Grip (forehand grip for overhead shots) → Footwork to rear court (get behind the shuttle) → Throwing action (reach up and hit through the shuttle) → High clear (deep to rear court, maximum height — defensive) → Attacking clear (flatter, faster, lands near rear boundary) → Contact point (in front of and above the hitting shoulder) → Follow-through (continue the swing, recover immediately).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Get behind the shuttle: contact in front of the shoulder
- High clear height: opponent cannot intercept en route
- Attacking clear flatter: lower trajectory means opponent has less time
