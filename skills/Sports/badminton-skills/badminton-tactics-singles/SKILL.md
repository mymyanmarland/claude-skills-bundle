# Badminton Singles Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Singles tactics: Width and depth (move opponent to all four corners) → Smash-drop combination (smash to rear, drop to front, mix it up) → Target the backhand rear corner (most players weakest area) → Serve tactics (vary short and flick) → Change of pace (mix fast and slow shots to disrupt rhythm) → Reading the opponent (anticipate from their preparation) → Point construction (do not go for winners too early — build the rally).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Backhand rear corner: the hardest area of the court to cover
- Smash-drop: the classic singles combination — relentless
- Build the rally: going for winners too early leads to easy errors

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
