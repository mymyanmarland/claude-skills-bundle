# Badminton Defensive Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Badminton defence: Defensive stance (wider base, lower, racket up) → Block return (absorb the pace, redirect softly to net) → Lift return (clear over the smasher, buy time) → Drive return (counter-attack, flat return) → Anticipation (read the opponent's preparation) → Defensive formation in doubles (both players near the baseline for a smash) → Footwork for wide smashes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Low stance: better angle to receive low smashes
- Block return: soft hands, let the shuttle do the work
- Anticipation: watch the opponent's racket preparation, not just the shuttle
