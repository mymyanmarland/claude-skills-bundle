# Badminton Grip and Ready Position Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grip and ready: Basic grip (shake hands grip — racket as an extension of the arm) → Forehand vs backhand grip change (subtle rotation, not a full re-grip) → Finger placement (pads of fingers, not the palm) → Grip pressure (loose until impact, then firm) → Ready position (knees slightly bent, racket up in front at chest height, weight on balls of feet, feet shoulder-width apart) → Recovery to ready position after every shot.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Loose grip until impact — tight grip kills wrist snap
- Ready position racket up: not at the side or down
- Recover to ready position — never watch your shot

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
