# Badminton Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Footwork: Base position (centre of court) → Six-point movement (front left, front right, mid left, mid right, rear left, rear right) → Split step (before every return) → Lunge step (front court — right foot lunge for forehand) → Chassé steps (rear court movement) → Recovery (push back to base after every shot) → Shadow footwork (practice movement without shuttle — builds pattern and fitness).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shadow footwork: most underused training tool in badminton
- Recover to base: a player who does not recover cannot cover the next shot
- Six-point system: cover all six zones — practise each daily

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
