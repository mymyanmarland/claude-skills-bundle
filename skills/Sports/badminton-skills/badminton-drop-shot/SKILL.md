# Badminton Drop Shot Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Drop shot: Preparation same as clear or smash (deception is essential) → Slow the racket at contact (touch the shuttle rather than hitting it) → Fast drop (near the net, steep angle) → Slow drop (higher arc, lands just over the net) → Cross-court drop (changes angle) → Backhand drop (from backhand rear corner) → When to use: opponent retreating, short lift from opponent.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Same preparation as smash: deception is 50% of the drop shot
- Touch not hit: slow the swing at the last moment
- Cross-court drop: catches the opponent moving the wrong way
