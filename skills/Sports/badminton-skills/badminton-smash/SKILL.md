# Badminton Smash Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Badminton smash: Set up position (behind the shuttle, sideways to net) → High contact point (as high as comfortably reachable) → Steep downward angle (aim at the opponent's body or mid-court) → Wrist snap at contact (generates power) → Jump smash (even higher contact point, extra power) → Body smash (aim at opponent's torso — difficult to return) → Accuracy over power: a placed smash beats a powerful missed one.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- High contact point: height determines the downward angle
- Body smash: most effective at amateur level — very difficult to deal with
- Accuracy over power: a placed smash beats a missed thunderbolt

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
