# Badminton Net Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Net play: Net shot (gently push shuttle over the net, tumbling or spinning) → Straight net shot (parallel to the side line) → Cross-court net shot (angle to opposite side) → Net kill (when shuttle is high — punch it down) → Lift from the net (when pushed low — use a clear to recover) → Net position (low, close to net, racket up, ready to pounce) → Communication in doubles.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tumbling net shot: just touch the shuttle — it creates spin the opponent struggles to lift
- Net kill: only play it when shuttle is clearly above the net tape
- Racket up at net: fastest reaction time possible
