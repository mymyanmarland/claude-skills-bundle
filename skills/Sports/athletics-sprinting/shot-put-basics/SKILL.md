# Shot Put Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shot put: Grip (three fingers behind the shot, thumb to the side, shot resting on the base of the fingers — not the palm) → Chin rest (shot rests against the neck under the chin, elbow high) → Stance and start (back of circle, weight on right foot for right-handers) → Glide technique (slide across circle, rotate, release) → Rotational technique (advanced — similar to discus rotation) → Release angle (approximately 42 degrees) → Safety: shot put safety rules are mandatory.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chin rest position: shot must not fall away from the neck
- Release angle: 42 degrees — too steep loses distance
- Safety rules: the circle and landing sector must be clear before throwing
