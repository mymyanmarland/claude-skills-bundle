# Javelin Throwing Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Javelin basics: Grip (Finnish grip or American grip — index finger behind the cord) → Run-up (controlled acceleration, last 5 strides are the throwing run) → Withdrawal (javelin drawn back, shoulder turns) → Power position (javelin behind, hips turned, shoulder square) → Release (pull through from behind, release over the right shoulder for right-handers, tall final stance) → Safety: clear sector, no retrieving until instructor calls → Release angle (30-35 degrees).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety first: all athletes must understand sector rules before throwing
- Withdrawal complete before power position: sequence is critical
- Release over the shoulder: not around the side — elbow injury risk

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
