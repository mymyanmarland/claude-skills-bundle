# High Jump Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

High jump: Approach run (curved run — 3-5 stride curve from the left for right-footed jumpers) → Take-off (inside foot for curved approach, lean into the curve) → Back arch (back arches over the bar, hips rise) → Bar clearance (chin to chest = hips rise / legs drop after hips clear) → Landing (on the back and shoulders — safety mat essential) → Bar height progression.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Curved approach: generates centrifugal force that aids rotation over bar
- Chin to chest: the key to arching and clearing the bar
- Safety mat essential: do not practise Fosbury Flop without proper landing mat

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
