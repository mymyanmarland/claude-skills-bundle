# Top Speed Mechanics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Top speed mechanics: Upright position (slight forward lean, not bent at the waist) → High knee lift (thigh parallel to ground or higher) → Foot strike (under the centre of mass — not in front) → Full hip extension (behind the body — generates propulsive force) → Arm action (90 degree elbow angle, drive back not forward) → Relaxation (tense muscles slow you down — relax the face, hands, shoulders).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Foot strike under centre of mass: in front creates a braking force
- Full hip extension: most amateur sprinters do not get full extension
- Relax: tension is the enemy of maximum speed
