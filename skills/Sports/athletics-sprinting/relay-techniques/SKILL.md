# Relay Exchange Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Relay exchange: Exchange zone (baton must be passed within the 20m exchange zone) → Blind exchange (receiver runs, does not look back — most common for 4×100m) → Visual exchange (receiver watches the baton — used in 4×400m) → Outgoing runner timing (leave on a check mark, not on sight of incoming runner) → Pass-and-receive technique (incoming: push forward / outgoing: hand back, palm up) → Practice from speed, not standing still.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Check mark: leave too early or late both cause disqualification
- Blind exchange: practise the timing until it is automatic
- Practice at race speed: walking baton exchanges do not transfer

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
