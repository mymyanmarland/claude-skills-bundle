# Sprint Conditioning and Speed Endurance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sprint conditioning: Alactic speed (10-30m sprints, full recovery — pure speed) → Lactic threshold (150m-300m efforts, 3-5 min recovery — builds tolerance) → Special endurance (250-350m runs, long recovery — race-specific) → Speed endurance (80-120m, 4-6 reps, 10 min recovery) → Taper protocol (reduce volume 10 days before major race) → Warm-up for sprint training (minimum 20 minutes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Full recovery for speed work: insufficient recovery trains the wrong system
- Warm-up 20 minutes minimum: sprint training on cold muscles causes injury
- Taper: 10 days before major race — reduce volume, maintain quality

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
