# Long Jump Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Long jump: Approach run (consistent stride pattern, measured and practised) → Penultimate stride (longer stride, lowers centre of mass) → Take-off (full hip extension, tall body, drive the free leg knee up) → Flight phase (hang technique or hitch-kick) → Landing (feet forward, hands drive back, fall into the pit not back) → Approach consistency (same number of strides, same speed, same take-off foot every jump).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Approach consistency: a variable approach makes every jump a guessing game
- Take-off tall: do not bend forward — loss of height = loss of distance
- Landing: drive hands back so body falls forward into the pit

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
