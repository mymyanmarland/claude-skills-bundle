# Hurdles Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Athletics event
- Current performance level
- Training environment
- Age and physical development
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hurdles: Approach (7-8 strides to first hurdle — consistent) → Lead leg (straight kick over the hurdle, toe up) → Trail leg (hip flexor drives through, bent knee clears the hurdle) → Upper body (forward lean, opposite arm to lead leg) → Landing (on the ball of the foot, immediately accelerate) → 3-stride pattern between hurdles → Hurdle height awareness by age and gender.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 7-8 strides to first hurdle: find your number and keep it consistent
- Trail leg hip flexor: the most technically demanding part
- 3-stride pattern: losing it means 4-stride recovery is needed

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
