# Inclusive Football Coaching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Inclusive coaching: Universal design (activities accessible to all by default) → Differentiation (adapt tasks for different ability levels) → Communication (consider language barriers, visual communication) → Physical inclusion (adapt to physical limitations) → Psychological safety (every player feels welcome) → Adapted football formats (walking football, wheelchair football, blind football awareness) → Anti-discrimination responsibilities.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Universal design first — adapt for specific needs second
- Physical inclusion: small adaptations often make full participation possible
- Anti-discrimination training required for all coaches

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
