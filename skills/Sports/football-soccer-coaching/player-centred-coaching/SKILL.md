# Player-Centred Coaching Approach Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Player-centred coaching: Guided discovery (ask 'what did you notice?' not 'you did that wrong') → Questions that develop thinking (what / why / how / when) → Player ownership of solutions → Positive + instructional + positive feedback structure → Observation before intervention → Giving players choices → Debrief questions at end of session.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ask before telling — players who discover retain better
- Observe fully before intervening — not every error needs correction
- Player solutions — they will use what they created

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
