# Coaching Young Footballers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coaching young footballers: Fun-first (children play sport for enjoyment — when it is not fun they stop) → Age-appropriate demands (under-8: no tactical talk, just play / under-12: basic roles / under-16: tactics) → Positive reinforcement over correction → Small-sided formats (5v5 and 7v7 more touches than 11v11) → Playing time equality at younger ages → Parent management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fun first always — motivation determines long-term participation
- Age-appropriate: 8-year-olds cannot process tactical lectures
- Playing time equality under-12: development not winning

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
