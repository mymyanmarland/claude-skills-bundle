# Football Session Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Session design: Session goal (one clear topic — not everything at once) → Warm-up game (topic-related, low intensity) → Main activity 1 (practice the skill in isolation or small groups) → Main activity 2 (apply the skill in a game-like situation with defenders) → Conditioned game (full game with a constraint that emphasises the topic) → Cool-down and debrief → 70/30 rule (70% with ball, 30% without).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One topic per session — covering everything covers nothing
- 70% ball contact: players improve by touching the ball
- Conditioned game makes the topic realistic under pressure

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
