# Teaching Tactical Concepts in Football Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Teaching tactics: Simple before complex (1v1 → 2v1 → 3v2 → small team → full game) → Freeze and show (stop the game, show what happened, ask the questions) → Shadow play (no opposition, execute the pattern) → Opposed practice (same pattern with defenders) → Conditioned game (rule reinforces the tactic) → Review with video (if available).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Simple to complex: players must understand basic before team tactics
- Freeze and ask: 'what could you do here?' before telling
- Conditioned game: rules reinforce the tactic without instructions

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
