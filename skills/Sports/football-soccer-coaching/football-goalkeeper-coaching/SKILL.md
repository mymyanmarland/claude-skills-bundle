# Coaching Goalkeepers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

GK coaching: Specialist warm-up (handlers, reactions, footwork before team joins) → Technical work (shot-stopping, handling, distribution) → Integration with team (defending crosses with defenders, distribution into build-up) → Psychological support (goalkeepers are isolated — specific attention needed) → Competition in GK training (GK vs GK, saves scored) → Individual feedback (separate from team debrief when needed).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Specialist warm-up before team session — goalkeeper needs more preparation
- GK integrated with defenders: crossing, set pieces, pressing triggers
- Individual feedback separately — group debrief embarrasses goalkeepers

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
