# Football Training Periodisation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football periodisation: Pre-season (build fitness base, introduce tactics) → Early season (maintain fitness, competition focus) → Mid-season (maintenance, avoid fatigue accumulation) → Competition congestion (minimal training load between matches) → End of season (taper or rest) → Weekly microcycle (match day -4, -3, -2, -1, MD, MD+1) → Load monitoring tools.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Microcycle: hardest session on MD-3, technical work MD-2, activation MD-1
- Congested fixture: reduce training load — recovery is the priority
- End of season rest: mandatory — overuse injuries occur without a break

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
