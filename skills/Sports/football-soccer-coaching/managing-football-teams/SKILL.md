# Managing a Football Team Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team management: Culture and values (agreed at the start of the season) → Squad management (playing time, rotation, squad harmony) → Selection communication (honest, clear, respectful) → Pre-match preparation (logistics, team talk, warm-up) → Half-time management (short, clear, no more than 3 points) → Post-match debrief (process and learning, not just results) → Handling parents.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Culture: set it at the start — retrofitting culture is hard
- Half-time: maximum 3 points — players cannot process more
- Parent management: clear communication policy from day one

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
