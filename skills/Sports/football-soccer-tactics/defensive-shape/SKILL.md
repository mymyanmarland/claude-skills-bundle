# Defensive Shape and Organisation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Defensive shape: Defensive block (low vs mid vs high — choose based on game state) → Compactness (horizontal and vertical lines close together) → Covering shadows (cut passing lines as you press) → Shifting as a unit (when ball moves, team moves) → Defending set pieces (zonal vs man-marking) → Last line of defence (offside trap timing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compact block — space between lines kills the defence
- Shift as a unit — one player out of position creates a gap
- Offside trap requires perfect communication and timing

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
