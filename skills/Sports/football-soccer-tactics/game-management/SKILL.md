# Game Management and Tactical Adjustments Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game management: Reading the game (score, time, momentum) → Protecting a lead (lower block, maintain possession, time management) → Chasing a game (risk vs reward decisions) → Formation changes in-game → Substitution strategy (impact substitutions) → Slowing the tempo (back to goalkeeper, long throw-ins) → Accelerating the tempo.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Protecting a lead: drop slightly but do not invite pressure
- Substitution timing: change before the opponent does
- Time management: waste time where legal but maintain professionalism

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
