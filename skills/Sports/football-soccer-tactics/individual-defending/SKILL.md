# Individual Defending Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Individual defending: Defensive stance (sideways, low, on toes) → Delay (slow the attacker, give team time to recover) → Jockeying (stay on feet, do not dive in) → The tackle (wait for the right moment — body shape, attacker off-balance) → Covering position (when not the first defender) → Pressing the ball (when to press, when to hold) → Defending on the back foot (turning and recovering).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stay on feet — diving in leaves a gap
- Delay as a defensive skill — buying time is valuable
- Cover position: second defender covers for the first

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
