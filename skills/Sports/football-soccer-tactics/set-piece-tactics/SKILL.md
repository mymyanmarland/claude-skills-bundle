# Set-Piece Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Set-piece tactics: Attacking corners (near post flick-on, far post runner, penalty spot runner, short corner option) → Defensive corners (zonal vs man-marking pros and cons, goalkeeper command) → Attacking free kicks (wall runs, dummy runs, direct vs indirect) → Defensive free kicks (wall setup, covering the side of the wall) → Throw-ins as set pieces.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Practise corners both attacking and defensive every session
- Free kick wall: one extra man on side of the wall always
- Short corner option forces defensive adjustment

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
