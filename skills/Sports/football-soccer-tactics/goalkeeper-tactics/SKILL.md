# Goalkeeper Tactical Role Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Goalkeeper tactics: Positioning (line position based on defensive line height) → Sweeping behind the defence (reading through-balls) → Distribution (short distribution to build-up, long distribution to switch play) → Communication (organise the defence at set pieces and open play) → Starting positions for different game states → Command of the penalty area.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Line position: goalkeeper must be up when defensive line is high
- Distribution: short option first, long only when blocked
- Penalty area command: goalkeeper must be dominant, not passive

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
