# Positional Play and Space Creation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Positional play: Width and depth (stretch the defence) → 3rd man movement (create for a team-mate) → Body orientation (open up to receive, see the field) → Positioning in and out of possession → Half-spaces (most dangerous attacking areas) → Pin the defenders (make them stay so team-mates can run) → Spacing (not too close, not too far — triangle shapes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Half-spaces: the most dangerous areas in football, often underused
- Width stretches the defence — create space centrally
- Body orientation: be open to the field before receiving

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
