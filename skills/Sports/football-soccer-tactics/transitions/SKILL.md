# Transition Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Transition play: Attack-to-defence transition (immediate press after loss or compact block) → Defence-to-attack transition (quick vertical pass, support runners) → Counter-attack triggers (ball won in specific zones) → Counter-attack structure (1-2 passes maximum, run in behind) → Counter-pressing after transition → When to slow down vs go direct.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Attack-to-defence: first 5 seconds most dangerous — immediate response
- Counter-attack: maximum 2 passes before shooting
- Counter-pressing wins more second balls

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
