# Attacking Patterns of Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Attacking patterns: Third-man combinations (A to B to C — bypassing pressure) → Overlapping runs (fullback or winger overlaps) → Underlapping runs (inside cut) → Switching play (move ball quickly to the other flank) → Forward rotations (striker dropping, midfielder running) → Playing through the press (goalkeeper and centre-backs as first line) → Set-piece attacking patterns (corners, free kicks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Third-man combinations break compact defences
- Switching play: move the ball faster than legs
- Set-piece patterns practised — they represent 30% of goals

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
