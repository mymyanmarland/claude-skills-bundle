# Formations Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Formations: 4-4-2 (balanced, easy to organise, clear roles) → 4-3-3 (width in attack, high pressing shape) → 4-2-3-1 (double pivot protects, number 10 creates) → 3-5-2 (wing-backs key, numerical midfield advantage) → 5-3-2 (defensive solidity, quick transition) → Formation vs opponent (matching up or creating mismatches) → Formation changes in-game.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Formation is a guide not a straitjacket — players move
- Match formation to personnel — not to trends
- Changing formation in-game is a tactical weapon

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
