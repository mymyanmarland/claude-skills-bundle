# Pressing and High Press Tactics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pressing tactics: Press triggers (bad touch, back pass, specific player) → Coordinated press (first presser + second presser + rest of team compact) → Pressing traps (channel the opponent into specific areas) → Gegenpressing (win the ball back immediately after losing it) → When not to press (opposition has escaped the press — regroup) → Fitness requirement for pressing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Press triggers defined — random pressing is exhausting
- Compact shape: rest of team must compress when pressing starts
- Pressing requires high fitness — cannot press for 90 minutes without preparation

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
