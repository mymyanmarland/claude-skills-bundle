# Basic Gymnastics Floor Skills Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basic floor skills: Forward roll (chin to chest, push off feet, roll onto curved back, stand) → Backward roll (sit, roll back, hands push at ears, feet over head, stand) → Cartwheel (step, kick up, quarter turn, land side-on) → Handstand progression (kick up to wall, hold, balance away from wall) → Bridge (from the floor: lie down, push up with arms and feet) → Safety: mats must be appropriate — never practise on hard surfaces.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chin to chest in forward roll: rolling onto the head causes neck injury
- Cartwheel: step and kick — not jump
- Handstand wall practice: build strength and balance before freestanding

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
