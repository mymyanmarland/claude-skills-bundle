# Gymnastics Beam Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Balance beam basics: Start on the floor beam (low, wide — 10cm high) → Foot placement (heel-toe walk, focus point straight ahead) → Turns (pivot on the ball of the foot) → Basic jumps (two feet to two feet, land softly) → Sitting and kneeling positions → Dismounts (step off the side — before anything involving height) → Progression from floor to low beam to full height (gradual — no rushing) → Mats beside full-height beam at all times.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Floor beam first: full height beam without progression is dangerous
- Focus point: looking down at the beam causes loss of balance
- Never rush beam progression: fear is a signal — not a weakness

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
