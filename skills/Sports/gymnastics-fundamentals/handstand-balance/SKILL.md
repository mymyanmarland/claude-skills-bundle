# Handstand Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Handstand development: Wrist warm-up (mandatory before any handstand work) → Wall handstand (kick up, find the balance point, hold 10 seconds before progressing) → Hollow body shape (straight line from wrists to heels — no banana back) → Finger pressure (fingers spread, press to control balance) → Bail-out technique (cartwheel out, not falling on the back) → Freestanding progression (gradually move away from the wall, 2-5-10 second holds) → Shoulder strength prerequisite.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wrist warm-up mandatory: every session, before the first handstand
- Hollow body shape: banana back collapses — active shoulder press is the fix
- Bail-out technique: cartwheel or pirouette — never fall backwards

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
