# Gymnastics Safety Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gymnastics safety: Qualified coaching (gymnastics must be coached by qualified, safeguarding-certified coaches) → Progression principle (never attempt a skill without prerequisite skills being solid) → Matting standards (FIG or national federation standards — not just any mat) → Spotting (coach manually assists the athlete through skills during learning) → Load management (growth plates in young gymnasts are vulnerable — limit repetitions) → Injury reporting (never train through pain without medical clearance) → Child welfare (gymnastics has specific welfare codes — parents should know them).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Progression principle: every skill has prerequisites — skipping them causes injury
- Qualified coaching: gymnastics should not be self-taught
- Child welfare: parents and athletes should know the welfare code of their federation

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
