# Gymnastics Vault Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Vault basics: Springboard use (two-footed hurdle onto the board — do not step) → Hand position on vault (hands on the horse/table, fingers forward) → Basic vault (straight body over vault with push from the arms — do not collapse at the shoulders) → Run-up (accelerating, controlled, consistent) → Safety matting (landing mats must meet gymnastics standards) → Supervised only: vault must not be attempted without a qualified coach spotting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Supervised only: vault without a coach is extremely dangerous
- Springboard hurdle: two feet land simultaneously — step approach loses power
- Arms push through: collapsing at the shoulder means landing on the vault

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
