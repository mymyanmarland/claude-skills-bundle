# Gymnastics Bars Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bars basics: Grip types (overgrip / undergrip / mixed) → Active hang (engage the shoulders — do not dangle passively) → Swinging (generate swing from the core and shoulder engagement) → Glide swing (introductory swing skill) → Cast (push away from the bar to horizontal) → Back hip circle (introduction to circling skills) → Safety: bars must be at appropriate height, mats positioned correctly, coach spotting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Active hang: passive hanging puts stress on passive structures
- Supervised only: bar release skills and circles require a spotter
- Grip strength: prerequisite for all bar work — build it before attempting skills

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
