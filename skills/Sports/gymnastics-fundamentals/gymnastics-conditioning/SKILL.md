# Gymnastics Conditioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gymnastics conditioning: Core strength (hollow body / arch body / L-sit progression — the foundation of all gymnastics positions) → Push and pull strength (straight arm strength: planche progressions / pull strength: skin the cat) → Leg strength and flexibility (splits, pancake stretch — patience required) → Shoulder flexibility (overhead mobility) → Hip flexor strength (critical for pike positions) → Wrist conditioning (gradual loading — gymnasts have high wrist injury rates) → Conditioning before skills: always.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hollow body: the most fundamental gymnastics position — master it before everything
- Wrist conditioning: progressive loading over weeks — not sudden
- Conditioning before skills: a weak body cannot execute skills safely

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
