# Rhythmic Gymnastics Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Gymnastics experience level
- Apparatus or discipline focus
- Training environment: gym/club/home
- Age and physical development
- Competition or recreational goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rhythmic gymnastics: Apparatus overview (rope / ball / hoop / clubs / ribbon — different FIG apparatus) → Body movement foundations (flexibility, expression, dance) → Ball skills (rolling on body, throwing and catching, bouncing) → Rope skills (skipping, rotation) → Hoop skills (rolling, rotation around the body) → Ribbon skills (spirals and snakes) → Competition structure (individual and group competitions) → Expression: rhythmic gymnastics combines sport with artistic performance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Expression is scored: technical skill alone is insufficient in rhythmic gymnastics
- Apparatus mastery: one at a time — not all simultaneously
- Flexibility foundation: without it, the technical skills cannot be performed at the required quality

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.

---
📌 Sports Disclaimer: Gymnastics carries inherent risk of injury. Always train under a qualified gymnastics coach on appropriate equipment with proper matting. Never attempt new skills without qualified supervision and appropriate progressions.
