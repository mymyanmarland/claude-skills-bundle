# Golf Nutrition and Hydration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf nutrition: Before the round (moderate meal 2 hours before — not too heavy) → During the round (small snack every 4-5 holes to maintain blood sugar) → Hydration (sip water constantly — 500ml per hour minimum in warm conditions) → Avoid large meals mid-round (blood flow to digestion affects focus and energy) → Caffeine (moderate caffeine improves focus — excessive causes anxiety and shakiness) → Alcohol (impairs decision-making and motor control — avoid during competitive rounds). Note: consult a registered dietitian for personalised advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Snack every 4-5 holes: blood sugar dips cause poor decision-making
- Hydration: even mild dehydration affects concentration
- Alcohol during competition: impairs the decision-making that course management requires
