# Pre-Round Warm-Up Routine Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pre-round warm-up: Physical (dynamic mobility: hip circles, torso rotations, leg swings — 5 min) → Putting green (start with 1-2m putts for confidence / then work on pace with long putts — 10 min) → Chipping area (chip to a specific target, vary distances — 5 min) → Range (start with wedges / build through the bag to driver — 15 min) → Return to putting green (3-4 final short putts — finish confident) → Total: 35-40 min.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Start with short putts: confidence early in the warm-up carries into the round
- Build through the bag: starting with the driver is how you hit it poorly all day
- Return to putting: last thing before you tee off is holing putts — confidence builder

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
