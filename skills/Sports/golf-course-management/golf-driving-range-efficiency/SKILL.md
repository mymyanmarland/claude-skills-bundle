# Driving Range Efficiency Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Driving range efficiency: Set a target (every shot to a specific target — not just hitting balls) → Club rotation (do not just hit driver — simulate a round by rotating clubs) → Pre-shot routine (same routine every shot as you would on the course) → Quality over quantity (50 purposeful shots beats 150 mindless ones) → Feedback (observe ball flight — where is it going and why?) → Finish with driver (then go back to a comfortable club to finish confident).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Target for every shot: hitting without a target teaches nothing
- Pre-shot routine: practise your process, not just your swing
- Quality over quantity: tired, bored practice produces poor muscle memory

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
