# Playing Different Golf Courses Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Course adaptation: Links golf (firm, fast, running approach shots / wind dominant / bump and run is a valid approach shot) → Parkland golf (softer, aerial approach shots / more predictable / course management more detailed) → Tree-lined courses (accuracy over distance) → Desert and dry courses (hard fairways, carry is less important than roll) → First time on a course (ask the pro for local knowledge / walk the course if possible) → Reading yardage books.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Links golf: the ground is part of the game — running shots are valid
- Links wind: learn to flight the ball low or you will never score well links
- Local knowledge: always ask about local rules and unusual features

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
