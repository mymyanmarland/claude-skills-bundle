# Golf Mental Resilience Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf mental resilience: The 10-second rule (10 seconds of emotion after a bad shot — then move on completely) → Shot-by-shot thinking (the only shot that matters is the next one) → Routine as anchor (same pre-shot routine under pressure — automation reduces anxiety) → Score detachment (play shots not scorecard — the numbers happen if the shots are right) → Bad round recovery (most rounds have a bad patch — how you respond defines the round) → Post-round review (learn from bad shots without dwelling).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 10-second rule: longer emotional reaction bleeds into the next shot
- Next shot only: the last shot cannot be changed — the next can be managed
- Bad patch response: every round has 3-4 bad holes — how you respond determines the final score

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
