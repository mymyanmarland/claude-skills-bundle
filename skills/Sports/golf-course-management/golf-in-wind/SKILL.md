# Playing Golf in the Wind Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf in wind: Into the wind (take more club, swing easier — do not hit harder / keep the ball lower by playing it slightly back and delofting) → Downwind (take less club, ball flies further / easier to control distance with a shorter club) → Crosswind (aim into the wind and let it work the ball back / or curve the ball into the wind) → Putting in wind (wider stance, grip tighter, lower ball flight from putter face) → Mental approach (wind affects everyone — equanimity is a competitive advantage).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Swing easier into wind: harder swing creates more spin and more curve — loses control
- Downwind: landing angle changes — ball rolls more / choose the right club
- Equanimity: wind affects your opponent equally — composure is the advantage

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
