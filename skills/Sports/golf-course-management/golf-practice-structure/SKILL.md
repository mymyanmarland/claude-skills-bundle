# Golf Practice Structure Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf practice: Block practice vs random practice (block: drill a skill in repetition / random: mix skills as they appear in a round) → Range practice vs course practice (range: technical work / course: decision-making and scoring) → Short game priority (chip to a target, not just away / vary the lie, distance, and target) → Putting practice (from 1-3m for confidence / from 6-9m for pace control) → Playing games on the range (adds pressure and simulates decisions).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Random practice transfers better to the course than block practice
- Short game target: always practise to a specific target — not just direction
- Putting 1-3m: confidence zone — make 10 in a row before moving back

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
