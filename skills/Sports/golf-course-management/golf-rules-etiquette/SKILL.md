# Golf Rules and Etiquette Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Golf rules and etiquette: Pace of play (be ready when it is your turn — slow play is the biggest frustration in golf) → Marking the ball on the green (mark with a coin behind the ball) → Lost ball (3-minute search then stroke and distance) → Unplayable lie (options: stroke and distance / drop within 2 club lengths / keep it on a line between where it lies and the hole going back) → Repair pitch marks on the green (repair your pitch mark and one other) → Bunker raking → Out of bounds (stroke and distance).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pace of play: ready golf — whoever is ready plays, not strictly honour
- Pitch mark repair: repair yours and one more — look after the course
- Lost ball: 3 minutes maximum — then stroke and distance

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
