# Advanced Course Management Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Handicap or experience level
- Specific weaknesses: driving/iron/short game/putting
- Course type and typical playing conditions
- Training time and equipment available
- Goals: enjoyment/handicap reduction/competition

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced course management: Hole-by-hole strategy (play each hole as designed — not the way you want to play it) → Wind management (adjust for wind direction and strength at each shot) → Pin position reading (back pin: easier approach / front pin: more dangerous) → Scoring holes vs defence holes (attack the birdie holes, par the hard ones) → Competitive round management (protect the good round — do not gamble late) → Playing for position (lay up to a comfortable yardage not a perfect number).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pin position: always know where the pin is before selecting the club and target
- Scoring holes: identify the birdie opportunities pre-round and plan to attack them
- Good round protection: most amateur rounds collapse in the final 3 holes through unnecessary risk

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
