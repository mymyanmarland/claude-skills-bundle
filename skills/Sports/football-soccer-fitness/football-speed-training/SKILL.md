# Football Speed Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football speed: Acceleration mechanics (drive phase — push into ground, lean forward) → Top speed mechanics (upright, high knees, full hip extension) → Short sprint intervals (10m, 20m, 30m — football sprints) → Sprint recovery (repeated sprint ability) → Sprint drills: resisted sprints, flying sprints, band-assisted → Ball-integrated sprints (sprint then receive).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Football sprints are short: 0-30m acceleration most important
- Sprint recovery as important as sprint speed — repeated ability
- Ball-integrated sprints: speed plus skill under fatigue

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
