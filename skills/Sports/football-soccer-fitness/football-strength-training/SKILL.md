# Strength Training for Footballers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football strength: Lower body (squats, split squats, Romanian deadlifts — build force production) → Upper body (push-pull balance, core anti-rotation) → Power development (trap bar deadlift, loaded jumps) → Single-leg work (most football force produced on one leg) → In-season frequency (2x per week, lower volume) → Youth players (bodyweight before loaded work).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single-leg exercises most football-specific — bilateral has limits
- Power after strength base — do not skip the foundation
- In-season: 2 sessions maximum — recovery is priority

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
