# Youth Football Fitness Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Youth football fitness: Under-12 (game-based activities — no structured fitness training → coordination, agility through fun) → Under-14 (introduce structured agility and speed — still game-based mostly) → Under-16 (aerobic base, introduce strength with bodyweight) → Under-18 (begin structured strength training, sports-specific conditioning) → Avoid early specialisation — multi-sport activity beneficial.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Under-12: only game-based fitness — structured conditioning is inappropriate
- Bodyweight before loaded strength — under-16 minimum for weights
- Multi-sport participation: reduces injury risk and improves athleticism

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
