# Football Pre-Season Training Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pre-season plan: Phase 1 (weeks 1-2: aerobic base, mobility, low intensity) → Phase 2 (weeks 3-4: intensity increase, small-sided games, strength) → Phase 3 (weeks 5-6: high intensity, match simulation, tactical) → Friendly matches integration → Testing (fitness benchmarks pre and post) → Individual fitness gap addressing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Aerobic base first — cannot rush the foundation
- Friendly matches in phase 3 only — not when not yet fit
- Individual testing reveals gaps in group training

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
