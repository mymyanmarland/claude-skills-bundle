# Football Recovery Methods Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football recovery: Active cool-down (10 min light jog and stretch immediately after) → Cold water immersion (10-15°C, 10-15 min) or contrast bathing → Nutrition (within 45 min — protein and carbs) → Sleep (7-9 hours — primary recovery tool) → Next-day recovery session (light movement, swimming, cycling) → Heart rate variability monitoring (optional).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cool-down immediately after match — not next day
- Sleep is the most important recovery tool — no gadget replaces it
- Next-day recovery session: 20-30 min light movement only

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
