# Injury Prevention for Footballers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Injury prevention: FIFA 11+ warm-up (evidence-based: reduces injuries 30-50%) → Hamstring eccentric work (Nordic curl — most evidence for hamstring injury prevention) → ACL prevention (landing mechanics, hip strength) → Ankle stability (balance board, proprioception work) → Groin strength (adductor squeeze, Copenhagen plank) → Load management (training load monitoring).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FIFA 11+ warm-up: use it every session — evidence is very strong
- Nordic curl: the most evidence-based hamstring injury prevention exercise
- Landing mechanics: knee over toe, soft landing — prevents ACL injury

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
