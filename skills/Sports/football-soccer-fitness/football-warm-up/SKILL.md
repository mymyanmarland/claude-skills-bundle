# Football Warm-Up Protocols Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football warm-up: Dynamic mobility (leg swings, hip circles, ankle rotations — 5 min) → Progressive jogging (easy to moderate — 5 min) → FIFA 11+ activation (specific exercises — 15 min) → Ball work (passing, dribbling at moderate intensity — 5 min) → Speed activation (3-4 short sprints building to max) → Team set-piece practice (2-3 min) → Total: 30-35 min.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dynamic not static warm-up — static stretching reduces power output
- FIFA 11+ reduces injuries — use it every session
- Speed activation: must sprint before the match — not the first sprint in the game

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
