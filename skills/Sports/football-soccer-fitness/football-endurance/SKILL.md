# Football Endurance Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football endurance: Aerobic base (Zone 2 running 20-30 min, twice weekly) → High-intensity intervals (4x4 minutes at 90-95% max HR, 3 min rest) → Small-sided games as fitness (constant movement) → Repeated sprint ability (10 sprints of 30m, 30 sec rest) → Pre-season conditioning vs in-season maintenance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Aerobic base supports recovery between high-intensity moments
- 4x4 interval protocol: proven for football fitness
- In-season maintenance: 1-2 fitness sessions per week maximum

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
