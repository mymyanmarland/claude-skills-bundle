# Football Nutrition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football nutrition: Pre-match meal (3-4 hours before: carbohydrates + moderate protein) → Pre-match snack (60-90 min before: easily digestible carbs) → Half-time nutrition (carbohydrate gel or banana if match is intense) → Post-match recovery (protein + carbs within 45 min) → Daily nutrition (carbohydrate periodisation — more on training days) → Hydration (500ml before, 200ml per 20 min during). Note: consult a registered dietitian for personalised plans.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Carbohydrate loading on training and match days — not rest days
- Post-match 45-minute window: protein + carbs = faster recovery
- Hydration: dehydration impairs speed and decision-making

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
