# Football Agility Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Football agility: Change of direction mechanics (decelerate, plant, push off) → Reactive agility (respond to visual cue vs pre-planned) → Football-specific patterns (cut, spin, drop step) → Ball-integrated agility (receive, turn, accelerate) → Agility drills: T-drill, 5-10-5, reactive cone drill → Deceleration strength (eccentric knee and hip work).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reactive agility transfers better than pre-planned drills
- Deceleration is where injuries happen — eccentric strength prevents this
- Ball-integrated: agility with the ball is game-realistic

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
