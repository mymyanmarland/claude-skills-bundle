# Shadow Boxing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shadow boxing: Purpose (visualise an opponent, practise technique, warm up or cool down) → Guard maintained at all times → Every punch fully extended and returned → Footwork incorporated (not standing still) → Head movement between combinations → Varying pace (slow for technique, fast for conditioning) → Mirror feedback (shadow box in front of a mirror — visual self-correction) → Use for mental rehearsal (visualise an opponent's moves and respond).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Guard maintained: shadow boxing with dropped hands practises bad habits
- Move throughout: shadow boxing in one spot is wasted training
- Mirror shadow boxing: the most honest feedback available

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
