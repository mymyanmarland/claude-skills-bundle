# Boxing Combinations Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing combinations: Jab-cross (1-2): the foundation → Jab-cross-hook (1-2-3): the most fundamental combination → Jab-cross-hook-cross (1-2-3-2): power finisher → Body-head combinations (jab to body, cross to head) → Combination principles (set up with the jab / mix levels / return to guard between combos / finish with your strongest shot) → Combination variety (never be predictable).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Jab sets up everything: do not throw combinations without the jab
- Mix levels: body-head combinations are much harder to defend
- Return to guard: every combination ends with hands up

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
