# Heavy Bag Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Heavy bag work: Warm-up rounds (light, focus on technique and movement) → Combination rounds (practise specific combinations each round) → Power rounds (full power combinations, short bursts with rest) → Conditioning rounds (continuous output, work rate) → Round structure (3-minute rounds with 1-minute rest — standard) → Distance from the bag (punching range: not too close, not too far) → Bag does not hit back — add head movement to practise defence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Add head movement to bag work: the bag does not punch back — simulate defence
- Distance: too close means elbow punches, too far means reaching
- Round structure: 3-minute rounds build fight-specific conditioning

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
