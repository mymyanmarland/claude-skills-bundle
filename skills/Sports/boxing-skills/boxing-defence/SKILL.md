# Boxing Defence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing defence: Guard (high hands protect chin and cheeks) → Block (absorb a punch with the gloves or forearms) → Slip (move head to the outside of a straight punch) → Roll (duck under a hook) → Parry (deflect the punch with an open glove) → Pivot (rotate around the opponent) → Step and angle (move off the straight line after a combination) → Defence first mindset (never get hit by what you can avoid).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slip outside: puts you in a position to counter
- Roll under hooks: stay bent knees — do not stand up into the punch
- Pivot: changes the angle, disrupts the opponent's balance

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
