# Boxing Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing mental game: Controlled aggression (focused and purposeful, not wild) → Pre-bout routine (consistent warm-up, visualisation, focus) → Round-by-round focus (each round starts fresh) → Handling getting hit (a punch landed is information, not a disaster) → Fatigue management (conserve energy mentally, not just physically) → Referee instructions and ring discipline → Handling a tough opponent (stay disciplined, execute the game plan).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Controlled aggression: wild punching wastes energy and leaves you open
- Each round fresh: do not carry the previous round forward
- Handling getting hit: breathe out, reset, stay disciplined

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
