# Boxing Punches Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing punches: Jab (lead hand, straight, fast, rotate shoulder, snap back immediately) → Cross (rear hand, drive from rear foot through hip and shoulder, rotate fully) → Hook (lead hand, horizontal arc, pivot on lead foot, elbow at 90 degrees) → Uppercut (rear or lead hand, bend knees, drive upward through hip rotation) → Punch return (every punch returns to guard immediately — the most important habit) → Punch mechanics: generate power from the legs and hips, not just the arms.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Return every punch: a punch that does not return leaves you open
- Power from legs and hips: arm punches are weak
- Jab is the most important punch: sets up everything else

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
