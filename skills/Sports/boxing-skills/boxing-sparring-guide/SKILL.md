# Boxing Sparring Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing sparring: Equipment requirements (headguard, gumshield, groin guard, gloves — all mandatory) → Sparring purposes (technical: light contact, focus on skill / hard: match simulation) → Beginner sparring (light contact, slow speed — learn without fear of getting hurt) → Choosing a sparring partner (similar level, trustworthy partner) → Coach supervision (sparring without a coach present is not recommended) → Tap and stop (verbal signal to pause if it gets too hard) → Post-sparring review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- All equipment mandatory: no exceptions for any sparring
- Tap and stop: every boxer must know and use it
- Supervised sparring only: a responsible coach present is non-negotiable

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
