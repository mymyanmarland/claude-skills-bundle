# Boxing Stance and Guard Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing stance and guard: Orthodox vs southpaw (lead hand and foot forward) → Foot position (shoulder-width, lead foot at 45 degrees, rear foot perpendicular) → Weight distribution (60/40 — slightly more weight on the back foot) → Guard (hands protecting the chin — lead hand in front, rear hand by the cheek) → Chin tucked behind the shoulder → Eyes above the fists → Knees slightly bent, weight on balls of feet.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chin tucked: the most important safety position in boxing
- 60/40 weight distribution: enables movement in all directions
- Eyes above the fists: if you cannot see the punch you cannot avoid it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
