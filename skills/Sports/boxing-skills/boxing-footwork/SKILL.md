# Boxing Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Boxing experience level
- Training goals: fitness/technique/competition
- Gym access and equipment
- Coach availability
- Physical fitness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boxing footwork: Push step (push off the back foot to move forward — do not cross feet) → Step-drag (step with the foot closest to the direction, drag the other) → Pivot (on the ball of the lead foot, rotate to change angle) → Ring generalship (control the centre of the ring) → Cutting off the ring (angle to corner the opponent) → Lateral movement (avoid straight lines) → The circle (move clockwise to a right-handed opponent's right hand — away from power).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Push step: never cross the feet — leaves you off-balance
- Circle away from the power hand: move away from the cross
- Centre of the ring: control it, force the opponent to the ropes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
