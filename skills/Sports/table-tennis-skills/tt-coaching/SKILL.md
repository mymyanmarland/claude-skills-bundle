# Table Tennis Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT coaching: Practice types (regular (fixed) / irregular (varied) / competitive) → Multi-ball training (coach feeds many balls rapidly — highest repetitions) → Technical vs tactical progression (technique first, then tactical application) → Serve and return practice (most important part of every session) → Match analysis (identify patterns, exploit weaknesses) → Physical and mental development alongside technical.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Multi-ball: most efficient training method in table tennis — high repetitions
- Regular to irregular: learn pattern first, then apply it variably
- Serve and receive: 50% of every session should involve it

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
