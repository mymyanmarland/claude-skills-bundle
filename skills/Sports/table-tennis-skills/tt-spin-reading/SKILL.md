# Reading Spin in Table Tennis Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Spin reading: Identifying topspin (ball will jump forward — angle bat down slightly) → Backspin (ball will drop — angle bat up slightly, brush forward) → Sidespin (ball will curve — compensate by aiming opposite direction) → No-spin (looks like spin but has none — adjust angle accordingly) → Reading from the bat contact (observe the direction of the opponent's swing) → Practice: serve boxes of each spin, partner reads and returns.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Watch the bat contact: direction of swing = direction of spin
- Topspin response: close the bat angle — or it flies off the end
- No-spin: most beginners over-adjust for spin that is not there

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
