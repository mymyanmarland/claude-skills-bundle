# Table Tennis Backhand Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT backhand: Compact backswing (elbow stays in front of body) → Forearm rotation at contact (wrist snap for topspin) → Block (minimal backswing, absorb incoming spin, redirect) → Topspin backhand (brush up, follow through across body) → Flick (quick wrist snap for short balls) → Banana flick (cross-over flick against short backspin) → Transition from backhand to forehand footwork.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Elbow in front: compact backhand is faster and more consistent
- Block technique: absorb the spin — do not fight it
- Flick: the most attacking return of a short serve
