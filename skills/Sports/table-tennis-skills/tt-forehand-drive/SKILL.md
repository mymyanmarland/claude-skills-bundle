# Table Tennis Forehand Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT forehand: Stance (right foot slightly back for right-handers) → Backswing (bat back and low) → Contact (brush up the back of the ball = topspin) → Follow-through (towards the target, then recover) → Loop (heavy topspin — slower swing but greater brush angle) → Drive (more direct contact, less spin, more speed) → Footwork for forehand (step right to cover wider balls).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Low backswing: allows upward brushing motion for topspin
- Follow-through toward target: guides the direction
- Loop vs drive: loop for heavy spin, drive for speed — know when to use each

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
