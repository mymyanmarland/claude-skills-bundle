# Table Tennis Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT footwork: 2-step shuffle (lateral movement for wide balls) → 3-step adjustment (step in for short balls, step back for wide) → Crossover step (for balls very wide to the backhand) → In-out movement (step in for short ball, step back after) → Shadow footwork (practise footwork patterns without a ball) → Footwork drills: cone targets, partner calling direction → Recovery to ready position after every stroke.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shadow footwork: builds muscle memory cheaply — no equipment needed
- 2-step shuffle: the most common movement in table tennis
- Recovery: cannot cover the next ball if you are still moving sideways

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
