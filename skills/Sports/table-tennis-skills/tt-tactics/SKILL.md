# Table Tennis Tactical Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT tactics: Serve and third ball attack (serve short backspin, get a lift, attack the third ball) → Serve and loop (serve long fast, force a weak return, loop) → Placement tactics (wide forehand, wide backhand, body — three targets) → Transition from backhand to forehand (crossover step to use powerful forehand) → Changing pace and spin (variation disrupts opponent's rhythm) → Reading the opponent (anticipate from preparation).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Third ball attack: the most important tactical pattern in table tennis
- Body shot: most players leave their body open — target it
- Variation: consistent same-type play is easy to read and return

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
