# Table Tennis Serve Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT serve: Legal serve basics (ball on open palm, tossed 16cm, struck behind the end line, visible to opponent) → Forehand backspin serve (brush under the ball = backspin) → Sidespin serve (brush across the ball) → Short serve (lands twice on receiver's side before the end) → Long serve (fast, deep, catches receiver off guard) → Disguise (same motion for different spins — the key to effective serving).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Legal serve: rules must be followed — illegal serves are penalised
- Short serve: forces the receive close to the table
- Disguise: same toss, same preparation, different contact point

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
