# Table Tennis Physical Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT physical training: Reaction time (reflex ball, reaction light drills) → Lateral speed (short shuffle drills) → Rotational core (forehand and backhand power comes from hip and core rotation) → Leg endurance (table tennis players squat for the entire match) → Wrist and forearm strength (spin and speed) → Agility (multi-direction, first-step quickness) → Aerobic base (long matches — best of 7 games).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leg endurance: the most overlooked physical demand in table tennis
- Rotational core: brushing the ball requires whole-body rotation
- Reaction training: 0.1 seconds difference at elite level matters

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
