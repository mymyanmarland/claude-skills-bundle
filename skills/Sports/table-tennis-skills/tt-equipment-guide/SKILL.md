# Table Tennis Equipment Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

TT equipment: Blade (all-wood for beginners, carbon for speed) → Rubber type (inverted = most versatile, short pips = speed, long pips = disruption) → Rubber thickness (max 4mm total, thicker = more spin and speed) → Rubber hardness (softer for beginners — easier to control) → ITTF approval (for competition use) → Ball standards (40+ plastic ball) → Table selection (outdoor vs indoor, regulated dimensions).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- All-wood blade for beginners: more control, teaches feel first
- Inverted rubber: most versatile — learn on it before specialising
- ITTF approved equipment required for official competition

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
