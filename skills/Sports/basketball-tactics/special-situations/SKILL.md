# Basketball Special Situations Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Special situations: Down 3 with 10 seconds (quick 3, foul if missed, 3 again) → Up 1 with 30 seconds (inbound, foul to give, time management) → Intentional fouling (on poor free-throw shooters — Hack-a-player) → Jump ball situations → Technical foul situations → Overtime preparation → One-possession game execution.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Practise special situations weekly — they happen every close game
- Down-3 scenario: specific set play, not improvised
- Foul to give: understand the rules before attempting

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
