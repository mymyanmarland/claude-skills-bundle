# Basketball Set Plays Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Set plays: SLOB (sideline out of bounds): Spain pick-and-roll, flat play → BLOB (baseline out of bounds): box play, double screen → Half-court sets: horns, floppy, zipper, chin → Plays off timeouts → Plays for specific players (design around your best scorer) → Simple over complex (a simple play run well beats a complex play run poorly).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- BLOB plays: highest percentage scoring opportunities in basketball
- Plays for your best player: design around talent
- Simple and well-executed: 3 set plays run perfectly beats 10 run poorly

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
