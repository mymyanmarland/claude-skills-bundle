# Basketball On-Court Communication Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

On-court communication: Screen calls ('screen left!') → Defensive switches ('switch!' or 'no switch!') → Loose ball calls ('mine!' or 'ball!') → Out-of-bounds calls (who has possession) → Play calls (point guard calls plays loudly) → Encouragement (positive only on the court — negativity is contagious) → Timeout communication (listen, ask questions, confirm understanding).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Screen calls: everyone must hear — communicate loudly
- Switches: decide and communicate before the screen comes
- Encouragement only: negativity on the court lowers performance

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
