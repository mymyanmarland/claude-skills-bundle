# Basketball Defensive Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Defensive systems: Man-to-man principles (ball pressure, help side, communicate) → 2-3 zone (clog the lane, force perimeter shots, rebound by position) → 1-3-1 zone (active, trapping corners) → Match-up zone (hybrid — man principles in a zone shape) → Full-court press (man press, zone press, when to use each) → Switching (when it helps and its risks) → Defensive rotations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Man-to-man first: teaches fundamentals before zone
- Zone: must communicate — silence kills zone defence
- Press: only if fit enough — gassed defenders give easy layups

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
