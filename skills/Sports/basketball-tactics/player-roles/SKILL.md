# Basketball Player Roles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Player roles: Point guard (floor general — facilitates, makes decisions, controls pace) → Shooting guard (scoring, off-ball movement, perimeter defence) → Small forward (versatile — score, defend multiple positions, rebound) → Power forward (post and perimeter, screen, rebound) → Centre (rim protection, post scoring, screen and roll) → Positionless basketball (modern: players fulfil multiple roles) → Role player vs star player mindset.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Point guard: decision-making is the primary skill — not just speed
- Centre: rim protection changes the entire defence
- Positionless: versatility is the future — develop multiple skills

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
