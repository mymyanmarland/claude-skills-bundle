# Basketball Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basketball coaching: Practice structure (warm-up / skill work / competitive drills / scrimmage / cool-down) → Maximise repetitions (players learn by doing, not watching) → Competition in every drill (score it — intensity rises) → Teaching by position (guards, wings, bigs separate and together) → Film as teaching tool (show, ask, show correction) → Player development tracking → Parent communication.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maximise repetitions: lines are the enemy of development
- Competition in every drill: scores create game intensity
- Teach by position and together: specialist work then combine

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
