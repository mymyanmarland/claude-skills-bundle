# Team Rebounding Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team rebounding: Defensive rebound assignments (guard-your-man plus one crashes) → Outlet pass lanes (rebounders know where the guards are) → Offensive rebound assignments (one or two crash, others protect) → Long rebound reads (3-point miss kicks out further) → Zone rebounding (by area, not man) → Rebounding mentality (every possession ends with a rebound — offence or defence).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Guard-your-man: most missed assignments lead to offensive rebounds
- Long rebound awareness: 3-point misses rebound further
- Zone rebounding: area responsibility must be clear

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
