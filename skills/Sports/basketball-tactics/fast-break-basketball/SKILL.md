# Fast Break Execution Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fast break: Outlet pass (rebounder to guard immediately) → Floor filling (wings sprint wide, trailer follows) → 3v2 advantage (attack quickly, do not slow down) → 2v1 advantage (drive and kick or drive and score) → Secondary break (if primary is stopped, set quickly into half-court) → Defence to offence mindset (every rebound is a fast break opportunity).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Outlet pass immediately: a slow outlet kills the break
- Fill all three lanes: not two players down one side
- Secondary break: do not give up when primary is stopped

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
