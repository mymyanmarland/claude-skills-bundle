# Basketball Game Strategy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Game strategy: Scouting the opponent (their best players, their sets, their tendencies) → Adjusting to what the game presents → Managing foul trouble (key players rest with 2 fouls in first half) → Clock management (when to push, when to hold) → Strategic fouling (foul to stop clock, foul poor free-throw shooters) → Timeout usage (stop momentum, set up plays) → End-of-game situations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Foul trouble: 2 fouls in first half = rest the player
- Timeout: stop momentum immediately — do not wait
- End-of-game: practise specific scenarios weekly

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
