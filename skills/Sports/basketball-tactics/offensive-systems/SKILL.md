# Basketball Offensive Systems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Offensive systems: Motion offence (read and react, 5-out spacing) → Horns set (two elbow screeners, options off each) → Baseline out-of-bounds plays → Transition offence (push pace after every rebound or made basket) → Half-court sets (specific play calls) → Last-second plays → Offence vs zone (gaps, corner 3, high post).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Motion offence: spacing is the foundation — without it plays break down
- Horns: versatile — run from it every half-court set
- Last-second plays: practise specific clock-and-score scenarios

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
