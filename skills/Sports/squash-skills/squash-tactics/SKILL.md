# Squash Tactical Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash tactics: Length game (build pressure with deep drives — tire the opponent and wait for the short ball) → Moving the opponent (front-back movement is more tiring than left-right) → Attacking the short ball (any ball above the tin and short — attack immediately) → Pattern play (drive-boast-drive: tire with drives, throw in a boast, attack the drop) → Exploiting weaknesses (target the weaker side — backhand for most players) → Court positioning (play from behind the opponent to have more time and options).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Front-back movement: the most tiring pattern in squash
- Attack short balls immediately: hesitation gives the opponent recovery time
- Pattern play: the drive-boast-drop combination is the most effective pattern
