# Squash Coaching Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash coaching: Session structure (movement / solo practice / paired feeds / conditioned games / match play) → Solo practice (ghosting, hitting to length without a partner) → Feeds (coach or partner feeds from a fixed point / player plays the shot) → Conditioned games (rule constraint emphasises a tactic: drives only / back half only) → Match play (apply in a game context) → Video analysis (squash is fast — video reveals what is invisible in real time).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Solo practice: the most underused training tool in squash — can be done alone
- Conditioned games: constraints force specific behaviours without instruction
- Video analysis: squash moves too fast to correct in real time — video reveals the patterns

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
