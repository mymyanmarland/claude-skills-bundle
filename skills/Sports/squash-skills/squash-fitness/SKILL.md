# Squash Fitness Training Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash fitness: Aerobic base (squash matches can last over an hour — aerobic foundation essential) → Repeated sprint ability (squash points are very short bursts) → Lateral agility (quick change of direction) → Lunging strength (front corners — quads and hip flexors) → Core stability (all squash movements originate at the core) → Ghost movement as conditioning (very effective squash-specific fitness) → Court sprints (baseline to front wall and back — repeat for conditioning).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ghost movement: fitness and skill development simultaneously
- Lateral agility: quick feet to the T and back is the squash fitness foundation
- Lunging strength: front corner lunges are the most demanding body position in squash

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
