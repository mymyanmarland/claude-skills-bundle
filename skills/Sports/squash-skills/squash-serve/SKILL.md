# Squash Serve Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash serve: Service box rules (at least one foot in the box when serving) → High lob serve (hit high on the front wall, ball arcs to the back corner — ideal target: dies in the back corner nick) → Hard drive serve (low and fast, body serve) → Service targets (hit the opponent's backhand when possible — weaker for most players) → Service variations (vary pace and target to keep the opponent uncomfortable) → Return of serve position (stand halfway between the side wall and centre, slightly back).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lob serve target: die in the back corner — anywhere shorter sits up
- Backhand target: most players are weaker on the backhand — serve there by default
- Return position: do not stand too close to the side wall — cut off the angle

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
