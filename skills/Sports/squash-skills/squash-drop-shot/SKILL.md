# Squash Drop Shot Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash drop shot: Preparation (same as drive — deception is essential) → Slow the racket at contact (touch the ball, do not hit it) → Target (nick or tight to the front wall, dying before the opponent arrives) → When to use (opponent is behind you / after a boast from the opponent / when you are in front of the court) → Cross-court drop (nick in the opposite front corner) → Backhand drop → Touch: the drop shot requires the softest of hands.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Same preparation as drive: deception is 50% of the drop shot's effectiveness
- Touch not power: the drop shot requires restraint — practise softening the hands
- Use when in front: a drop from the back of the court allows the opponent time to get there

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
