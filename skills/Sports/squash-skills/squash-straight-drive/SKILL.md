# Squash Straight Drive Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Straight drive: Preparation (shoulder turn early, racket back) → Swing path (horizontal swing, contact point level with the front foot) → Contact (firm wrist, hit through the ball) → Target (aim at the side wall nick or a length that dies in the back corner) → Backhand drive (same mechanics — non-dominant shoulder turn) → Length (a perfect length die in the back corners) → Cross-court alternative (change direction of drive to wrong-foot the opponent).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Length is everything: a short drive sits up for the opponent to attack
- Shoulder turn early: late preparation produces a cramped shot
- Back corner die: a ball that bounces back off the back wall sits up — length must prevent this
