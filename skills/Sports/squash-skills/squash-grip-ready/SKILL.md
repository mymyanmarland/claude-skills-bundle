# Squash Grip and Ready Position Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash grip and ready: Grip (Continental — similar to badminton, allows both forehand and backhand without re-gripping) → Grip pressure (loose — firm only at contact) → Ready position (centre of the court near the T, racket up in front, weight on balls of feet) → T-position (the T of the court markings — the most advantageous defensive position) → Recovering to the T after every shot → Anticipation from the T (read the opponent's body and racket position).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Continental grip: allows both forehand and backhand without re-gripping
- Recovering to the T: whoever controls the T wins most rallies
- Loose grip until contact: tension kills racket head speed
