# Squash Boast and Angle Shots Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash boast: Forehand boast (hit the side wall at 45 degrees so the ball travels to the front corner) → Backhand boast → Angle of contact on the side wall (too flat = ball hits the front wall too high / too steep = ball dies on the back wall) → When to use a boast (opponent at the front, you at the back — change the direction) → Three-wall boast (hits two side walls before the front wall — trickier to control) → Boast as a tactical weapon not a default shot.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Boast angle: practise the 45-degree contact point on the side wall
- Boast as weapon: overuse gives the opponent easy front-court opportunities
- Three-wall boast: only attempt when confident with the standard boast

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
