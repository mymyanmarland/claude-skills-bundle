# Squash Movement and Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash movement: Ghost movement (simulate court movement without the ball — 6 positions, move from T to each position and back) → Lunge (front corners — low, controlled landing) → Side steps (back corners — reach with the racket foot) → Recovering to the T (immediate recovery after every shot) → Not obstructing the opponent (squash requires giving the opponent a clear path to the ball — interference is a let or stroke) → Fitness foundation (squash movement is very demanding physically).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ghost movement: the single most effective squash practice tool — no ball needed
- T recovery: the player who recovers the T faster wins more points
- Obstruction awareness: blocking the opponent is a let or stroke — know the rules

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
