# Squash Mental Game Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness to address
- Court access and training frequency
- Physical fitness level
- Competition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Squash mental game: Between-point routine (towel off, breathe, refocus) → Let and stroke decisions (accept the referee's decision — do not react negatively) → Momentum management (squash has momentum swings — stay even) → Serving from 0-8 down (the game is not over — the first to 9 wins a game to 11) → Physical composure (slumped shoulders communicate defeat to your body — stand tall) → Opponent management (do not give your opponent information about your fatigue or frustration).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Never show fatigue: the opponent plays better when they know they are winning the fitness battle
- Let decisions: one negative reaction to a let decision can derail a match
- 0-8 down: squash scoring makes comebacks possible — keep competing

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
