# Basketball Footwork Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basketball footwork: Pivot (establish pivot foot, protect the ball, open passing angles) → Triple threat position (ready to pass, shoot, or drive) → Jab step (test the defender, commit to decision) → Drop step (post move, quick spin away) → Jump stop (land on both feet to establish either pivot) → V-cut and L-cut (create separation from defender) → Box-out footwork (seal the defender on every rebound).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pivot foot: once established never lift it — travel violation
- Triple threat: always be a threat before deciding to dribble
- Box-out: establish position before the ball arrives

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
