# Basketball-Specific Conditioning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basketball conditioning: Aerobic base (interval running — basketball is stop-start) → Sprint and recover (17m sprints: width of court, 10 seconds rest) → Lateral conditioning (slide drill suicides) → Jump training (plyometrics: box jumps, depth jumps for rim-attacking) → Core strength (every move in basketball uses the core) → Agility ladder (foot speed) → Game-specific conditioning: 3-on-3 intensity sets.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Court sprints: 17m is the basketball distance — not 100m
- Jump training progresses slowly — tendons adapt slower than muscles
- 3-on-3 sets: best game-specific conditioning

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
