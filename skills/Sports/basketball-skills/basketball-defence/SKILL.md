# Basketball Defence Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Defence: Defensive stance (low, wide, arm up, slide step — never cross feet) → Ball pressure (contest every shot, no free looks) → Ball-you-man triangle (stay between your man and the ball) → Help defence (on the weak side, help stop the drive) → Close-out (sprint then chop steps, hand up — no fly-bys) → Deny the wing (front foot in passing lane) → Post defence (front or side — team call).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slide step: crossing feet loses lateral speed
- Ball-you-man triangle: know where both the ball and your player are
- Close-out chop steps: flying past the shooter gives a free shot

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
