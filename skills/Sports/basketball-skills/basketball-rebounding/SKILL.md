# Basketball Rebounding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rebounding: Box-out (find your man, seal with back, create space) → Reading the shot (anticipate miss direction based on shot angle) → Defensive rebound (secure with two hands, chin the ball, outlet quickly) → Offensive rebound (crash the boards, hustle, put-back or kick out) → Timing the jump (explode at peak of the ball's descent) → Rebounding positioning (non-shooter on strong side crashes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Box-out first, then go get the ball — sequence matters
- Chin the ball after catching: protect from strips
- Offensive board: crash immediately after every shot — not sometimes

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
