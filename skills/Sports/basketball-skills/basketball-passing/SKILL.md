# Basketball Passing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Passing: Chest pass (step into pass, hands behind ball, thumbs down on release) → Bounce pass (aim two-thirds of the way to receiver, arrives at hip height) → Overhead pass (skip defence, two hands, strong release) → No-look pass (sell the fake with eyes, pass opposite) → Outlet pass (quick, accurate after rebound) → Pocket pass (feed into the post) → Passing under pressure (pivot to create passing angle).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Step into every pass — arm pass loses speed and accuracy
- Bounce pass timing — most effective when defender is upright
- No-look pass: sell the fake convincingly or it telegraphs

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
