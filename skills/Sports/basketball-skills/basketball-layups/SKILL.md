# Basketball Layups Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Layups: Right-hand layup (right foot takeoff, left knee up, right hand extends up) → Left-hand layup (left foot takeoff, right knee up) → Power layup (two-foot takeoff, absorb contact) → Reverse layup (use the rim as protection) → Euro step (gather, step one way, step other, layup) → Floater introduction (for smaller players over shot blockers) → Both hands equally: 50/50 practice split.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opposite foot takeoff: right hand = left foot off
- Chin the ball on the way up — protects from blocks
- Both hands: weak hand layup is a game-changer

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
