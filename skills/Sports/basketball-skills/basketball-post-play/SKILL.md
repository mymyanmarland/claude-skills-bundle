# Basketball Post Play Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Post play: Post entry (seal the defender, call for the ball) → Drop step (quick spin to the baseline side) → Jump hook (high release, difficult to block) → Up-and-under (pump fake, step through, layup) → Face-up game (step out, triple threat from post) → Post defence (prevent easy catches, position) → Screen setting (solid base, arms in, no moving screens).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Seal the defender before calling for entry pass
- Pump fake: make it convincing — sell it with the eyes too
- Hook shot: high release makes it near-unblockable

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
