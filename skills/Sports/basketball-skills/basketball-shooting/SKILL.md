# Basketball Shooting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shooting: BEEF acronym (Balance/Eyes/Elbow/Follow-through) → Stance (shoulder-width, slight forward lean, weight on balls of feet) → Grip (ball on fingertips, guide hand on side) → Elbow under ball (not flared) → Release (flick of the wrist, fingers point to floor after) → Follow-through (hold it until the ball hits the rim) → Catch-and-shoot → Off the dribble → Free throw routine.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Elbow under the ball — flared elbow causes left/right misses
- Follow-through held — releases tension and builds consistency
- Catch-and-shoot: footwork into the shot, not just the shot itself

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
