# Basketball Dribbling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dribbling: Fingertip control (pads of fingers, not the palm) → Low dribble (protect the ball under pressure) → High dribble (speed in open court) → Crossover (low, quick, protect with body) → Between the legs → Behind the back → Hesitation dribble (change of pace) → Weak hand development (40% of all dribbling practice) → Solo cone drills and partner pressure drills.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fingertips not palm — palm dribble kills control
- Low dribble under pressure — high dribble is vulnerable to steals
- 40% weak hand — most players neglect it entirely

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
