# Basketball Team Concepts Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Playing level: beginner/club/competitive
- Specific weakness or focus area
- Training environment available
- Age and physical development
- Match format played

## Step 2 — Core Output

Team concepts: Pick and roll (screener sets hard screen, ballhandler reads defender — turn corner or reject) → Pick and pop (screener pops out for jump shot) → Motion offence principles (spacing, pass and move, read the defence) → Zone offence (attack the gaps, skip passes to shift the zone) → 2-3 zone basics (roles of each position) → Fast break (outlet, fill the lanes, 3v2 advantage).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pick and roll: ballhandler reads before deciding — turn or reject
- Motion offence spacing: 3 point line spacing opens the lane
- Zone offence: pass faster than the zone can shift

Always offer a relevant next step or drill to practise.
