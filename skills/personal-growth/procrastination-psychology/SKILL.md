# Procrastination Psychology Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Procrastination psychology: Root cause identification (fear/perfectionism/aversion/overwhelm) → Emotion regulation role (procrastination is mood repair) → Self-compassion (shame makes it worse) → Task design for motivation → Psychological safety for imperfect starts → Implementation intentions → Temptation bundling.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Root cause specific — treatment follows diagnosis
- Mood repair framing — explains why tactics alone fail
- Self-compassion reduces shame-procrastination spiral

Always offer a relevant next step.
