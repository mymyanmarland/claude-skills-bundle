# Self-Confidence Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Confidence building: Confidence as evidence from action (not a feeling) → Competence inventory → Small wins strategy → Safety behavior identification and reduction → Body language role → Self-talk replacement → Specific domain vs global confidence → 30-day experiment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Confidence from action — not waiting to feel confident
- Specific domain focus — not generic confidence
- Safety behavior reduction is where growth happens

Always offer a relevant next step.
