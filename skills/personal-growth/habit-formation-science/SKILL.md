# Habit Formation Science Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Habit formation: Cue-Routine-Reward loop → Implementation intentions → Habit stacking → Environment design → Minimum viable habit → Two-day rule (never miss twice) → Tracking and streaks → Immediate rewards → Identity statement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Minimum viable habit — consistency beats intensity
- Two-day rule — recovery matters more than perfection
- Identity framing — behavior follows identity

Always offer a relevant next step.
