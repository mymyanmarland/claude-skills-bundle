# Self-Awareness Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Self-awareness: Internal vs external self-awareness (both needed) → Johari Window application → Feedback-seeking practice (how to get honest feedback) → Behavioral pattern journal → 360-degree reflection → Blind spot investigation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Internal vs external self-awareness distinction
- Johari Window applied practically
- Feedback-seeking designed for honest responses

Always offer a relevant next step.
