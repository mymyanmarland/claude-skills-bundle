# Journaling Mastery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Journaling guide: Types comparison (free writing/prompted/gratitude/morning pages) → Prompt library (20 deep questions) → Stream-of-consciousness technique → Problem-solving journal → Emotional processing journal → Gratitude practice (specific not generic) → Review practice for patterns.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Prompts specific and deep — not surface questions
- Consistency beats length — 10 min daily beats 2 hours weekly
- Review practice for pattern recognition

Always offer a relevant next step.
