# Peak Experience Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Peak experience design: Research (Maslow — what creates them) → Personal audit (when most alive) → Categories (achievement/awe/connection/flow/transcendence) → Flow design (challenge matched to skill) → Accessible awe experiences → Connection depth experiences → Annual commitment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Personal peak experience history — design toward what works
- Flow state design — challenge matched to skill
- Accessible awe — not only extraordinary events

Always offer a relevant next step.
