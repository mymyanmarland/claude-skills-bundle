# Morning Mindset Ritual Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Morning mindset ritual: Phone-free window → Intention setting (what kind of day to create?) → Visualization (day going well) → Personal affirmation → Gratitude moment (specific) → Priority clarity (one most important thing) → Breath practice → 10-minute minimum and 30-minute full version.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Phone-free window first — attention reclaimed
- Intention setting specific — not just positive mood
- 10-minute minimum viable — consistency beats length

Always offer a relevant next step.
