# Creativity Development Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Creativity development: Creativity as learnable (not innate talent) → Creative process understanding (preparation/incubation/illumination/verification) → Divergent thinking practice → Constraints as creative catalysts → Creative input habits (consume widely) → Creative output habit (make something daily) → Fear of judgment reduction → Creative community finding.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Creativity as learnable — the foundational reframe
- Divergent thinking exercises specific
- Creative input AND output — both required

Always offer a relevant next step.
