# Fear of Failure Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fear of failure: Identity threat (the real reason — not the outcome) → Worst-case analysis (how bad is it really?) → Definition of failure examination → Failure resume (reframe past failures) → Growth mindset for failure → Minimum viable action → Post-failure protocol → Success redefinition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Identity threat — the real reason failure is feared
- Worst-case analysis done thoroughly
- Failure resume creates evidence of survival

Always offer a relevant next step.
