# Personal Growth Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Personal growth plan: Life wheel assessment → Growth priority (1-2 areas max) → SMART goal per area → Skills and knowledge needed → Learning resources → Practice opportunities → Accountability structure → Milestones → Monthly review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maximum 2 areas — spreading thin prevents depth
- Milestones specific enough to know when reached
- Monthly review builds course correction

Always offer a relevant next step.
