# Boundary Psychology Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boundary psychology: Boundary as self-respect (not selfishness) → Guilt normalization (limits feel bad before they feel right) → Boundary types → Resentment as boundary signal → Fear of rejection examination → Boundary communication scripts → Holding boundaries when pushed → Self-compassion when crossed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Resentment as boundary signal — powerful diagnostic
- Guilt normalization — it always feels bad at first
- Holding when pushed — often missing from boundary advice

Always offer a relevant next step.
