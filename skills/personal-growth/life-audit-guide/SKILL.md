# Life Audit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Life audit: Life wheel (rate and reflect on 8 areas) → Energy audit (gives vs drains per area) → Authenticity check (living own life or someone elses) → Achievement vs meaning balance → Relationships audit (energizes vs drains) → Regret pre-mortem (what will I regret at 80) → Priority reset → 90-day commitments.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Energy audit beyond satisfaction rating
- Authenticity check — living own life or someone elses
- Regret pre-mortem grounds priorities in what matters

Always offer a relevant next step.
