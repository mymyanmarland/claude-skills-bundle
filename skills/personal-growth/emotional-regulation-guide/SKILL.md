# Emotional Regulation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Emotional regulation: Emotions as information and energy → Window of tolerance (optimal arousal zone) → Physiological regulation techniques (breath/movement/cold water) → TIPP skills (Temperature/Intense exercise/Paced breathing/Progressive muscle) → Ride the wave (surfing emotions) → Opposite action → Emotional log for pattern recognition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Window of tolerance — explains why regulation fails in crisis
- Physiological first — body regulates before mind
- Ride the wave — emotions pass if not fought

Always offer a relevant next step.
