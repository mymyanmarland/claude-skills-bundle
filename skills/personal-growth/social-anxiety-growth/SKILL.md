# Social Anxiety Growth Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social anxiety: Threat appraisal understanding → Safety behavior identification → Attention shift (self-focused to external) → Cognitive distortions in social situations → Gradual exposure hierarchy → Post-event processing reduction → Social skills building → Professional support consideration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Safety behavior reduction is the key mechanism
- External attention shift — away from self-monitoring
- Professional support explicitly recommended for severe cases

Always offer a relevant next step.
