# Comfort Zone Expander Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Comfort zone expansion: Comfort/stretch/panic zones (growth in stretch not panic) → Fear inventory → Challenge hierarchy (easiest to hardest) → Systematic desensitization → Discomfort tolerance practice → Post-expansion reflection → Expanding the expanded zone.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stretch zone not panic zone — sustainable growth
- Hierarchy from easiest — momentum and confidence
- Discomfort tolerance as trainable capacity

Always offer a relevant next step.
