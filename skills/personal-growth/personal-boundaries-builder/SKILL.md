# Personal Boundaries Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Boundaries building: Limit inventory → Resentment audit → Internal permission (I am allowed to have limits) → No as full sentence (no explanation required) → Boundary scripts → Anticipated pushback and responses → Graduated practice (easiest first) → Limit maintenance when tested.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Resentment audit identifies where limits most needed
- Internal permission work — external limits start internally
- No as full sentence — over-explanation undermines limits

Always offer a relevant next step.
