# Difficult Conversations Growth Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Difficult conversations: Three conversations model (what happened/feelings/identity) → Contribution not blame framing → Starting from curiosity → The and stance (both can be right) → Feelings as data not drama → Identity conversations (who am I in this?) → Purpose clarity before starting → Script for common difficult conversations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Three conversations model — comprehensive
- And stance vs but stance — key reframe
- Purpose clarity before starting — prevents derailing

Always offer a relevant next step.
