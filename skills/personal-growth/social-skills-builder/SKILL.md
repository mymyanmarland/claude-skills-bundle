# Social Skills Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social skills: Conversation fundamentals (ask/listen/share) → FORD framework (Family/Occupation/Recreation/Dreams) → Active listening (most underrated skill) → Storytelling in conversation → Awkward silence management → Entry and exit scripts → Body language basics → Name-remembering technique → Follow-up after meeting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Listening emphasized — underrated and learnable
- Practical scripts included — not just concepts
- Following up — often missing from social skills advice

Always offer a relevant next step.
