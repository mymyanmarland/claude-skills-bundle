# Personal Legacy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Legacy guide: Legacy as present-tense construction (not just after death) → Legacy inventory (already contributing) → Ripple effect (how actions affect others) → Mentorship as legacy → Story and wisdom transmission → Financial or creative legacy → Relationship legacy (how people feel after time with you) → Daily legacy question.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Legacy as present-tense construction
- Daily legacy question — makes abstract practical
- Relationship legacy — often overlooked

Always offer a relevant next step.
