# Personality-Based Growth Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Personality-based growth: Type identification or confirmation → Core motivation and fear → Signature strength overuse (when strength becomes weakness) → Growth direction for type → Integration patterns → Type-specific blind spot awareness → Daily practice for growth edge.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Core motivation and fear — deeper than behavior
- Strength overuse — growth edge hiding in strength
- Growth direction specific to type

Always offer a relevant next step.
