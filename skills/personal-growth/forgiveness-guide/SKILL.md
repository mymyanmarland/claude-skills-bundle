# Forgiveness Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Who or what needs forgiveness
- Depth of hurt and how long ago
- Current emotional state around it
- What forgiveness would mean
- Misconceptions about forgiveness

## Step 2 — Core Output

Forgiveness guide: Forgiveness misconception (not condoning or forgetting — releasing the grip on you) → REACH model (Recall/Empathize/Altruistic gift/Commit/Hold) → Self-forgiveness protocol (often harder than forgiving others) → Anger and grief processing before forgiveness → Letter writing (sent or unsent) → The ongoing nature of forgiveness (decision made, feelings take time) → What forgiveness frees you to do.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Forgiveness as self-release — not excusing behavior
- Self-forgiveness addressed separately — often harder
- Ongoing nature — decision then process

Always offer a relevant next step.
