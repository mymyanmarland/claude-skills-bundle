# Personal Strengths Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strengths development: Strengths identification (VIA survey, Gallup StrengthsFinder, or reflection) → Signature strengths (top 5) → Strengths in use audit (where used vs underused) → Strengths at work application → Strengths in relationships → Strength overuse awareness (too much of a strength) → Strengths-based goals.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Identification before application — know them first
- Overuse awareness — strength can become weakness
- Strengths-based goals replace deficit-focused goals

Always offer a relevant next step.
