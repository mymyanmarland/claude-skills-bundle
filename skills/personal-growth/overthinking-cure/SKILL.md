# Overthinking Cure Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Overthinking: Productive vs unproductive thinking distinction → Worry window (scheduled worry time) → Cognitive defusion (thoughts are not facts) → Mindfulness for observation → Action as antidote → Writing to externalize → Two-minute rule → Body activation as mental reset.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Productive vs unproductive thinking distinction first
- Worry window — counterintuitive but effective
- Action as antidote — behavioral interruption

Always offer a relevant next step.
