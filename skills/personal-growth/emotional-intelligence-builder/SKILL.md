# Emotional Intelligence Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

EQ development: Emotion labeling (name it to tame it) → Vocabulary expansion beyond happy/sad/angry → Trigger mapping → Self-regulation toolkit → Empathy building (curiosity before judgment) → Social awareness → Assertiveness (not aggression or passivity).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Emotion labeling specific — vocabulary expansion practical
- Trigger mapping behavioral — specific situations
- Assertiveness distinguished from aggressiveness

Always offer a relevant next step.
