# Imposter Syndrome Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Imposter syndrome: Normalization (70% of high achievers experience this) → Competence evidence audit → Attribution error (success to luck, failure to self) → Double standard test → Reframe to beginner → Share with trusted others → Contribution focus (value delivered not credentials).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Normalization first — reduces shame isolation
- Evidence audit creates concrete counter-narrative
- Contribution focus shifts from self-evaluation to impact

Always offer a relevant next step.
