# Decision-Making Psychology Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decision psychology: Cognitive biases (loss aversion/status quo/FOMO/sunk cost) → Emotional vs rational balance → Satisficing vs maximizing → 10-10-10 rule → Regret minimization framework → Pre-mortem → Making peace with uncertainty → Commitment and implementation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cognitive biases named — recognition enables correction
- Satisficing vs maximizing — most decisions need satisficing
- Pre-mortem surfaces hidden concerns before deciding

Always offer a relevant next step.
