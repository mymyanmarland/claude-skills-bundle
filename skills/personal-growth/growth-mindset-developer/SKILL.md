# Growth Mindset Developer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Growth mindset: Fixed vs growth triggers (specific not generic) → The yet practice → Failure as information → Process praise self-application → Neuroplasticity basics → Challenge-seeking practice → Role model selection.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trigger-specific — not generic mindset lecture
- Neuroplasticity science grounds the concept
- Challenge-seeking behavioral experiment

Always offer a relevant next step.
