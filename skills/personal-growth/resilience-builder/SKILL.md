# Resilience Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resilience development: Resilience as bounce-back capacity (not toughness) → Post-traumatic growth research → ABCDE model → Protective factors audit → Social support as foundation → Meaning-making from difficulty → Resilience practice (voluntary small discomforts) → Recovery ritual.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Resilience as bounce-back — important reframe
- Protective factors acknowledged — build on existing
- Voluntary discomfort builds capacity

Always offer a relevant next step.
