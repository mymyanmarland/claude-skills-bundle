# Mindfulness Integration Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mindfulness integration: Formal vs informal practice (meditation is training, life is the field) → Mindful moments in routine activities → STOP technique (Stop/Take a breath/Observe/Proceed) → Mindful transitions → Single-tasking as mindfulness → Phone mindfulness → One-minute practices for busy days.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Informal practice emphasis — life is the field
- STOP technique specific and memorizable
- One-minute practices — no excuse for no time

Always offer a relevant next step.
