# Failure Recovery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Failure recovery: Grief permission (failure deserves mourning) → The failure narrative (what story are you telling?) → Responsibility without self-destruction (control vs not) → Lesson extraction → Identity separation (failing is not being a failure) → Recovery timeline → Comeback planning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Grief permission — processed before lessons
- Responsibility distinction — neither self-blame nor excuses
- Identity separation — the most critical reframe

Always offer a relevant next step.
