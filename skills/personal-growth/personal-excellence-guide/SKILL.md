# Personal Excellence Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Excellence framework: Excellence vs perfectionism distinction → Deliberate practice vs repetition → Feedback-seeking as excellence tool → The plateau (part of mastery) → Standards (high but flexible) → Recovery and rest as excellence components → Long-game patience.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Excellence vs perfectionism distinction throughout
- Deliberate practice — not just experience accumulation
- Rest as excellence component — often omitted

Always offer a relevant next step.
