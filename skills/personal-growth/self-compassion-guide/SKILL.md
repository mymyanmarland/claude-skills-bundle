# Self-Compassion Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Self-compassion: Three components (mindfulness/common humanity/self-kindness) → Self-compassion vs self-pity distinction → Common humanity (not alone in suffering) → Friend test → Self-compassion break (3-step practice) → Research on performance (improves not reduces) → Self-compassion letter → Fierce self-compassion.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Self-compassion vs self-indulgence myth addressed with evidence
- Common humanity reduces isolation
- Fierce self-compassion — not just soft kindness

Always offer a relevant next step.
