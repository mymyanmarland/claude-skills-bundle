# Mindset Shift Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mindset shift: Current mindset articulated exactly → Origin story → Cost analysis → Evidence examination → Alternative mindset construction (specific, believable) → Behavioral experiment → Reinforcement practice until automatic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Current mindset written exactly — vague work fails
- Cost analysis creates motivation for change
- New mindset must be believable — not aspiration leap

Always offer a relevant next step.
