# Authenticity Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Authenticity: Authentic vs performed self mapping → People-pleasing as survival mechanism (origin) → Cost of inauthenticity (energy, resentment) → Authentic expression practice in safe relationships → Boundaries as authenticity → Disagreement tolerance → Approval need reduction → Authentic communication scripts.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Survival mechanism origin — compassion before change
- Cost of inauthenticity made concrete
- Authentic expression starts in safe context

Always offer a relevant next step.
