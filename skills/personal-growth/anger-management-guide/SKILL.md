# Anger Management and Expression Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Anger guide: Anger as information (unmet need) → Anger physiology → Activation curve (catch escalation early) → 20-minute de-escalation rule → Anger journal → Assertive expression script (I feel angry when... because... I need...) → Underlying emotions → Root cause patterns.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Anger as information — purpose before management
- 20-minute physiological rule — cannot rationalize when activated
- Assertive expression framework — anger without aggression

Always offer a relevant next step.
