# Life Vision Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Life vision: Eulogy exercise (what do you want said about you?) → Ideal day visualization (specific, sensory) → Life areas future-state → Contribution vision → Vision statement draft → First small step identified immediately → Annual vision review ritual.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Eulogy exercise grounds vision in what matters
- Specific sensory detail — not vague aspiration
- First step identified immediately — vision without action is fantasy

Always offer a relevant next step.
