# Positive Body Image Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current situation and specific challenge
- Previous attempts and what happened
- Goals and desired outcome
- Relevant life context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Body image guide: Body image vs body reality distinction → Media literacy (filter awareness) → Body neutrality vs body positivity (which is accessible) → Body functionality focus (what does your body do?) → Movement for joy not punishment → Clothes and mirror practices → Social media audit → Self-compassion for body → Professional support consideration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Body neutrality as accessible alternative to positivity
- Body functionality focus — powerful perspective shift
- Social media audit — often overlooked as driver

Always offer a relevant next step.
