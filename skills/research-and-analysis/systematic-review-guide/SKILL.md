# Systematic Review Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Systematic review: Protocol development and pre-registration → PRISMA flow diagram → Search strategy across multiple databases → Duplicate removal → Title and abstract screening → Full-text screening → Data extraction form → Quality assessment tool selection → Narrative synthesis → Meta-analysis decision → GRADE evidence rating → Reporting according to PRISMA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Protocol pre-registration — prevents bias
- PRISMA flow diagram — the transparency mechanism
- GRADE rating — quality of evidence not just quantity

Always offer a relevant next step.
