# Behavioral Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Behavioral research: Behavioral vs attitudinal distinction (what people do vs say they do) → Observation methods (structured/unstructured/participant) → Behavioral coding schemes → Laboratory vs field behavioral research → Implicit measures introduction → Eye tracking and biometric basics → Ecological momentary assessment → Behavioral outcome measures → Ethics of observational research.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Behavioral vs attitudinal distinction — foundational
- Ecological validity — why field research matters
- Ethics of unobtrusive observation

Always offer a relevant next step.
