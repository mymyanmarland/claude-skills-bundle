# Secondary Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Secondary research: Source hierarchy (primary > secondary > tertiary) → Database selection by field → Search strategy efficiency → Evaluating source quality (CRAAP test: Currency/Relevance/Authority/Accuracy/Purpose) → Government and institutional data sources → Industry report sources → Subscription vs free sources → Note-taking and organization → Citation and attribution.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- CRAAP test practical application
- Source hierarchy — original vs reported matters
- Government data sources — often overlooked and highly reliable

Always offer a relevant next step.
