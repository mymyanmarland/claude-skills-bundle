# Competitive Intelligence Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Competitive intelligence: Intelligence question definition (what decision does this inform?) → Source classification (public/paid/primary) → Company analysis framework (products/pricing/positioning/culture/financials) → Digital footprint analysis (website/social/job postings/patents) → Primary research (customer interviews/mystery shopping) → SWOT and competitive matrix → Ongoing monitoring setup → Ethical intelligence gathering.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Intelligence question first — avoids data collection for its own sake
- Job postings as intelligence source — often overlooked
- Ethical boundaries explicit — legal and reputational protection

Always offer a relevant next step.
