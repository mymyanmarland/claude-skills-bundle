# Meta-Analysis Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Meta-analysis: PRISMA checklist for reporting → Search and selection strategy → Data extraction form → Effect size calculation (Cohen d/odds ratio/correlation) → Heterogeneity assessment (I squared) → Fixed vs random effects models → Funnel plot for publication bias → Forest plot interpretation and creation → Subgroup and moderator analysis → Limitations of meta-analytic evidence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- PRISMA checklist — the reporting standard
- Heterogeneity assessment — often ignored in poor meta-analyses
- Publication bias detection — funnel plot and trim-and-fill

Always offer a relevant next step.
