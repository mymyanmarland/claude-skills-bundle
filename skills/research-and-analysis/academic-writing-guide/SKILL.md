# Academic Writing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Academic writing: Academic voice (precise, objective, hedged where appropriate) → Argument structure (claim/evidence/reasoning) → Paragraph construction (topic sentence/development/transition) → Citation integration (not just dropping quotes) → Academic vocabulary building → Avoiding common academic writing errors → Abstract writing → Literature positioning (situating your work) → Revision strategy for academic work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Citation integration — synthesis not just quotation
- Hedging language — appropriate academic uncertainty
- Argument structure explicit — claim then evidence then reasoning

Always offer a relevant next step.
