# Statistical Literacy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Statistical literacy: Descriptive vs inferential statistics → p-values and what they actually mean (and do not mean) → Effect size (statistical vs practical significance) → Confidence intervals → Sample size and power → Common statistical errors in media → How to read a study (abstract/methods/results/discussion) → Replication crisis context → Evaluating statistical claims critically.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- p-value misconceptions addressed directly
- Effect size vs statistical significance distinction — critical
- Replication crisis context — healthy skepticism

Always offer a relevant next step.
