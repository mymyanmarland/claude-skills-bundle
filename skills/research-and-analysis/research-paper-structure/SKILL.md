# Research Paper Structure Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research paper structure: IMRaD format (Introduction/Methods/Results/Discussion) → Introduction components (context/gap/research question/overview) → Methods section completeness (replicability standard) → Results presentation (neutral reporting) → Discussion (interpretation/implications/limitations/future) → Abstract writing (last, from the finished paper) → Title crafting → Acknowledgments and declarations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- IMRaD as the standard — why structure matters
- Results neutral — interpretation reserved for discussion
- Abstract written last — from the actual paper

Always offer a relevant next step.
