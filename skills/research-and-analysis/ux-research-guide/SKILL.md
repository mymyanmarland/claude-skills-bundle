# UX Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

UX research: Research question to method matching → Generative vs evaluative research → User interviews for discovery → Usability testing (moderated vs unmoderated) → Card sorting and tree testing → Surveys for UX → Eye tracking basics → Affinity mapping for synthesis → Journey mapping from research → Research repository building.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Generative vs evaluative — research type determines method
- Usability testing moderation technique
- Affinity mapping — synthesis tool after data collection

Always offer a relevant next step.
