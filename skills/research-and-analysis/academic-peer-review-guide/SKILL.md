# Peer Review Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Peer review guide: Reviewer responsibilities → Structure of a good review (summary/major/minor/recommendation) → Constructive critique language → Identifying fatal vs fixable flaws → Ethical reviewer conduct (confidentiality/conflicts) → Author response letter writing → Responding to reviewer 1 (the difficult reviewer) → Appeals process → Open peer review considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Constructive critique language — examples of how to phrase
- Fatal vs fixable flaw distinction — saves time for all
- Author response letter — the most stressful academic writing

Always offer a relevant next step.
