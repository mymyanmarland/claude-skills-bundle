# Grant Writing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grant writing: Funder alignment (your mission to their priorities) → Specific aims or executive summary → Needs statement (the problem evidence) → Project description (clear, logical, innovative) → Methodology rigor → Evaluation plan → Budget and budget justification → Organizational capacity section → Letter of support strategy → Common grant mistakes → Review process understanding.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Funder alignment first — most grants fail here
- Specific aims — the most important page
- Budget justification specific — reviewers read this carefully

Always offer a relevant next step.
