# Grounded Theory Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grounded theory: Inductive vs deductive approach distinction → Theoretical sampling (data collection and analysis simultaneous) → Open/axial/selective coding → Constant comparative method → Theoretical saturation → Memo writing → Core category identification → Theory development → Constructivist vs objectivist GT → Writing grounded theory.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Theoretical sampling — unique to grounded theory
- Simultaneous collection and analysis — the key distinction
- Memo writing — the analytical backbone

Always offer a relevant next step.
