# Quantitative Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quantitative research: Research question and hypotheses → Study design selection (experimental/quasi-experimental/correlational/descriptive) → Sample size calculation (power analysis) → Measurement validity and reliability → Survey instrument design → Data collection protocol → Statistical analysis plan → Common tests and when to use them (t-test/ANOVA/regression/chi-square) → Reporting standards.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hypothesis formation precedes design
- Power analysis for sample size — not arbitrary
- Statistical test selection matched to question and data type

Always offer a relevant next step.
