# Literature Review Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Literature review: Search strategy (databases/keywords/boolean operators) → Inclusion and exclusion criteria → Citation management (Zotero/Mendeley/EndNote) → Reading and extraction protocol → Synthesis vs summary distinction → Thematic organization → Gap identification → Writing the review (logical flow not annotation) → Citation style application.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Synthesis vs summary — the core skill in literature reviews
- Gap identification — the scholarly contribution
- Boolean search operators specific — AND/OR/NOT

Always offer a relevant next step.
