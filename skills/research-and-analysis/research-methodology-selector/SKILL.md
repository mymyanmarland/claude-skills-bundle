# Research Methodology Selector Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Methodology selection: Research question analysis (descriptive/exploratory/explanatory/evaluative) → Ontological and epistemological considerations simplified → Qualitative vs quantitative vs mixed methods decision tree → Within-method options → Paradigm alignment → Practical constraints (time/budget/access) → Mixed methods designs → Justifying methodology choice in writing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Decision tree practical — not purely philosophical
- Practical constraints included — theory meets reality
- Justification language for methods section

Always offer a relevant next step.
