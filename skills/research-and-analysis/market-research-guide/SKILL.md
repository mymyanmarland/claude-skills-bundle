# Market Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Market research: Research objective definition (what decision does this inform?) → Secondary research first (existing data) → Primary research design (surveys/interviews/focus groups) → Market sizing approaches (top-down/bottom-up) → Segmentation research → Customer persona development from data → Competitive landscape research → Synthesis into actionable insights → Research report structure.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Decision-informing objective first
- Secondary before primary — efficient sequencing
- Bottom-up sizing — more credible than top-down

Always offer a relevant next step.
