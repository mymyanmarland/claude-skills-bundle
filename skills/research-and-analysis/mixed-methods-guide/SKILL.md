# Mixed Methods Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mixed methods: Rationale for mixing (triangulation/complementarity/expansion/development) → Design types (convergent/sequential explanatory/sequential exploratory) → Integration at design/collection/analysis/interpretation levels → Priority and timing decisions → Reconciling conflicting findings → Sample and participant considerations → Notation system (QUAL+quan) → Quality criteria for mixed methods → Writing mixed methods papers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rationale for mixing — not just 'I did both'
- Integration level decision — the key design choice
- Conflicting findings — the most interesting mixed methods outcome

Always offer a relevant next step.
