# Critical Thinking Framework Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Critical thinking: Argument mapping (premises/conclusions/assumptions) → Logical fallacy identification (20 most common with examples) → Evidence evaluation criteria (relevance/sufficiency/accuracy) → Source credibility assessment → Distinguishing fact from opinion from inference → Steelmanning (argue the best version of opposing view) → Intellectual humility practices → Applying to real-world claims.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fallacy identification with real examples — not just naming
- Steelmanning — the most useful critical thinking skill
- Evidence criteria specific — not vague 'check sources'

Always offer a relevant next step.
