# Research Gap Identifier Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research gap identification: Gap types (empirical/methodological/theoretical/contextual/population) → Systematic literature mapping → Citation network analysis → Author claims of limitation and future research → Contradiction and inconsistency → Understudied populations or contexts → Methodological improvements needed → Temporal gaps → Cross-disciplinary opportunities → Framing your gap compellingly.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gap types taxonomy — not just 'nobody has studied this'
- Citation network analysis — finds clusters and missing links
- Framing language for gap identification

Always offer a relevant next step.
