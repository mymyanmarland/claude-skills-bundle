# Environmental Scanning Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Environmental scanning: PESTLE framework (Political/Economic/Social/Technological/Legal/Environmental) → Stakeholder analysis integration → Weak signals and emerging trends → Opportunity and threat identification → Priority assessment (likelihood × impact) → Synthesis into strategic implications → Monitoring system setup → Scan update frequency → Communication to leadership.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- PESTLE with specific application not just listing
- Weak signals — distinguishing scanning from surface analysis
- Synthesis into strategic implications — the output that matters

Always offer a relevant next step.
