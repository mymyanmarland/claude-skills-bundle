# Survey Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Survey design: Research objective → Question types (closed/open/Likert/ranking/matrix) → Question wording pitfalls (leading/double-barreled/loaded) → Response scale design → Survey length and completion rate trade-off → Logical flow and branching → Pilot testing → Distribution strategy → Response rate optimization → Data analysis plan before deployment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Question wording pitfalls with examples — before-and-after
- Pilot testing required — surprises always found
- Analysis plan before deployment — reverse design

Always offer a relevant next step.
