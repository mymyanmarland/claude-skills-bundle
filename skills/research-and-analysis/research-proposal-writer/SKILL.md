# Research Proposal Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research proposal: Title and abstract → Background and rationale → Research questions and objectives → Literature review summary → Methodology with justification → Ethical considerations → Timeline and feasibility → Budget (if required) → Expected outcomes and significance → Dissemination plan → Reference list. Common proposal mistakes and how to avoid them.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rationale connects to gap in knowledge
- Methodology justified — why this method for this question
- Timeline realistic — proposals often overpromise

Always offer a relevant next step.
