# Data Ethics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Data ethics: Data collection ethics (consent/transparency/purpose limitation) → Privacy principles (minimize collection, secure storage, controlled access) → Algorithmic fairness and bias → Data quality as ethics (bad data leads to bad decisions affecting people) → Data sharing and open science → Third-party data ethics → Surveillance and tracking concerns → Anonymization and re-identification risks → GDPR and CCPA basics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Purpose limitation — collect only what you need
- Re-identification risk — anonymization not always sufficient
- Algorithmic bias — data ethics extends to analysis

Always offer a relevant next step.
