# Trend Analysis Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Trend analysis: Trend types (short-term/cyclical/secular) → Quantitative trend detection (moving averages/regression) → Qualitative trend signals (weak signals framework) → Environmental scanning (PESTLE application) → Distinguishing trends from noise → Horizon scanning → Scenario development from trends → Trend report structure → Confidence levels for trend claims.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trend vs noise distinction — the hardest part
- Confidence levels required — honest about uncertainty
- Weak signals framework — early trend detection

Always offer a relevant next step.
