# Focus Group Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Focus group guide: Focus group vs individual interview trade-offs → Participant recruitment and screening → Group composition (6-10 participants) → Discussion guide development → Facilitation techniques (depth not breadth) → Managing dominant participants → Probing group dynamics → Note-taking while facilitating → Analysis approach → Report writing for focus groups.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trade-offs vs interviews — when focus groups add value
- Dominant participant management — common facilitation challenge
- Group interaction as data — unique to focus groups

Always offer a relevant next step.
