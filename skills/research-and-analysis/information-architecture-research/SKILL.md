# Information Architecture Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

IA research: Card sorting (open/closed/hybrid) → Tree testing methodology → Navigation analysis → Mental model research → Content audit and inventory → Labeling and nomenclature research → Taxonomy development → IA documentation (site maps/navigation flows) → Iterative testing → IA for search optimization.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Card sorting types — open reveals mental models
- Tree testing validates proposed IA
- Mental model research — understanding user expectations

Always offer a relevant next step.
