# Experiment Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Experiment design: Causal vs correlational distinction → Randomization and control → Treatment and control group design → Blinding protocols → Confound identification and control → Sample size for experiments → Internal vs external validity → Field vs lab experiment trade-offs → A/B testing as experimentation → Pre-registration to prevent p-hacking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Causal vs correlational — the fundamental distinction
- Pre-registration prevents p-hacking — explains why it matters
- Internal vs external validity tension

Always offer a relevant next step.
