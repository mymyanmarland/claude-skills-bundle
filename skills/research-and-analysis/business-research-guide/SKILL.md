# Business Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Business research: Business question to research question translation → Secondary research efficiency (industry reports/databases/public filings) → Primary research decision → Customer research methods → Competitor research framework → Financial and market data analysis → Research synthesis for decision-making → Business research report structure → ROI of research vs cost of bad decisions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Question translation — business to research language
- Secondary first — most business questions answerable from existing data
- Decision-oriented synthesis — not just findings

Always offer a relevant next step.
