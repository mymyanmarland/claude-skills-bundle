# Business Report Writing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Business report writing: Report types (analytical/informational/progress/recommendation) → Executive summary (most read section) → Structure for complex vs simple reports → Recommendations before evidence (for decision-makers) → Evidence presentation (tables/charts/appendices) → Professional writing style (active/concise/precise) → Visual design for reports → Executive vs technical audience versions → Review protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recommendations first for decision-maker audience
- Executive summary — complete standalone document
- Active voice and concision — professional standard

Always offer a relevant next step.
