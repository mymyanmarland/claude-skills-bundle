# Causal Inference Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Causal inference: Correlation vs causation with clear examples → Counterfactual framework → Confounding and how to address it → Randomization as gold standard → Natural experiments → Instrumental variables (simple explanation) → Difference-in-differences → Regression discontinuity → Directed acyclic graphs (DAGs) → When to claim causation vs association.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Counterfactual framework — the heart of causal thinking
- Confounding specific examples — makes abstract concrete
- DAGs — the most useful visual tool for causal reasoning

Always offer a relevant next step.
