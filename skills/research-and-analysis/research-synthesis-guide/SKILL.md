# Research Synthesis Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research synthesis: Synthesis vs summary distinction (the most important concept) → Organizing frameworks (thematic/chronological/theoretical) → Handling conflicting evidence → Weight of evidence approach → Identifying consensus and controversy → Narrative synthesis methodology → Presenting synthesized findings → Visual synthesis tools (concept maps/matrices) → Limitations of synthesis.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Synthesis vs summary — the core skill
- Handling conflicting evidence — the hardest part
- Weight of evidence — not all studies equal

Always offer a relevant next step.
