# Think Tank and Policy Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Policy research: Policy brief structure (executive summary/problem/evidence/recommendations) → Evidence standards for policy (accessible but rigorous) → Stakeholder-appropriate framing → Recommendation specificity (who does what by when) → Political feasibility acknowledgment → Dissemination strategy → Media engagement basics → Testimonial and expert quotes → Visual policy communication.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Executive summary as standalone document
- Recommendation specificity — who does what by when
- Political feasibility acknowledged — not just ideal policy

Always offer a relevant next step.
