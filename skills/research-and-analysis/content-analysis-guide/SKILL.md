# Content Analysis Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Content analysis: Manifest vs latent content → Sampling strategy for content → Unit of analysis selection → Codebook development (inductive vs deductive) → Coding protocol and training → Intercoder reliability calculation → Quantitative vs qualitative content analysis → Software options (ATLAS.ti/NVivo/manual) → Reporting content analysis findings.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Manifest vs latent distinction — the key methodological choice
- Intercoder reliability — rigor demonstration
- Codebook development — the core tool

Always offer a relevant next step.
