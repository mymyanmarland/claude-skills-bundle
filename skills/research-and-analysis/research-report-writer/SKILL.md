# Research Report Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research report writing: Audience analysis (who reads this and what do they need?) → Executive summary (the complete paper in miniature) → Introduction and problem framing → Methods transparency → Findings presentation (logical flow) → Conclusions and recommendations → Appendices and technical detail placement → Visual display of data → Plain language for non-expert audiences → Review and revision process.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audience analysis determines depth and language
- Executive summary complete standalone
- Plain language version — research should be accessible

Always offer a relevant next step.
