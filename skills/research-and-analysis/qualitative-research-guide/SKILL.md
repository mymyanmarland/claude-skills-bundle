# Qualitative Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Qualitative research: Research question formation (what/why/how — not how many) → Methodology selection (phenomenology/grounded theory/ethnography/case study) → Sampling strategy (purposive/snowball/theoretical) → Interview guide development → Focus group facilitation → Data collection protocols → Thematic analysis steps (familiarization/codes/themes/review/naming/writing) → Trustworthiness and rigor.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Research question determines methodology — not vice versa
- Purposive sampling — qualitative logic differs from quantitative
- Thematic analysis steps specific and sequential

Always offer a relevant next step.
