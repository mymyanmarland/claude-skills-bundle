# Primary Research Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Primary research: Primary vs secondary decision (when original research is needed) → Method selection (interviews/surveys/observation/experiments) → Research participant recruitment → Informed consent and ethics → Data collection instruments → Field notes and documentation → Data management → Analysis approach → Validity and reliability → Reporting primary research.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Primary vs secondary decision framework first
- Informed consent and ethics — non-negotiable
- Validity and reliability specific to method type

Always offer a relevant next step.
