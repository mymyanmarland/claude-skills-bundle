# Research Ethics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research ethics: Core principles (autonomy/beneficence/non-maleficence/justice) → IRB/ethics committee process → Informed consent design → Vulnerable populations protections → Confidentiality and anonymization → Data management and security → Conflict of interest disclosure → Deception in research (when permissible) → Research integrity and misconduct → Reporting ethical violations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Core principles applied practically — not just listed
- Vulnerable populations — specific heightened protections
- Deception permissibility — nuanced honest treatment

Always offer a relevant next step.
