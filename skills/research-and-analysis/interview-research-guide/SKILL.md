# Research Interview Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Research interview guide: Interview types (structured/semi-structured/unstructured) → Interview guide development (topics not scripts) → Probing techniques (elaboration/clarification/contrast) → Rapport building → Recording and transcription → Sensitive topic navigation → Reflexivity during interview → Analysis preparation → Transcription conventions → Number of interviews needed (saturation).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Semi-structured as default — flexibility with focus
- Probing techniques specific — follow-up without leading
- Saturation concept — when you have enough

Always offer a relevant next step.
