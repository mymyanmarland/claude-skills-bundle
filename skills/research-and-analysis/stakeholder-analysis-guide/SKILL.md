# Stakeholder Analysis Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Research question or objective
- Context and audience
- Existing knowledge and data available
- Methodology experience level
- Timeline and resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stakeholder analysis: Stakeholder identification (who is affected/who affects outcomes) → Interest and influence matrix (power/interest grid) → Stakeholder types (primary/secondary/key) → Interest and position analysis → Relationship mapping → Engagement strategy by quadrant → Monitoring stakeholder shifts → Conflict identification between stakeholders → Communication plan per group.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Power/interest grid application specific
- Interest analysis — what they actually want (not what they say)
- Conflict between stakeholders — often the most important finding

Always offer a relevant next step.
