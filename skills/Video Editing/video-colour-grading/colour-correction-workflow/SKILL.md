# Colour Correction Workflow Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour correction workflow: Step 1 — Exposure (use the Waveform / set black point and white point) → Step 2 — White balance (use the Vectorscope / neutral grey or skin tones should be on the skin tone line) → Step 3 — Contrast (S-curve in the tone curve) → Step 4 — Saturation (Vectorscope shows saturation radius — adjust to taste) → Step 5 — Creative grade (apply the look / LUT / style) → Always use scopes (never trust your monitor for colour decisions) → Grade order (correction before creative — never grade before correcting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Correction before creative: fixing technical issues first prevents the grade fighting the problems
- Waveform for exposure: the only reliable exposure tool — monitors lie
- Vectorscope skin tone line: the most reliable white balance reference for footage with people
