# Black and White Video Grading Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Black and white grading: Not just desaturation (desaturating to grey is flat — a real B&W grade shapes the tones) → Luminance controls (bright reds in colour = bright in B&W / blue skies = darker) → Luminosity LUT approach (apply a creative B&W look via a LUT) → Contrast and grain (B&W benefits from high contrast and film grain) → Skin tones in B&W (yellow and orange channel boosted to brighten skin) → HSL Luminance sliders (control the brightness of specific original colours in the B&W conversion) → Film noir style (deep shadows / bright highlights / punchy contrast / grain).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- HSL Luminance controls: the key to expressive B&W — not just desaturation
- Boost orange for skin: brightens skin tones in the monochrome conversion
- Film grain on B&W: almost every classic B&W grade includes grain — it adds authenticity
