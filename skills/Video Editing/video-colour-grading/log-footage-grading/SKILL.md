# Grading Log and Raw Footage Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Log footage grading: Identify the log profile (S-Log2/S-Log3/Log C/V-Log/C-Log — each camera has its own) → Apply the correct technical LUT (camera manufacturer provides these free — the starting point for the grade) → Colour Managed workflow (Resolve: use Colour Space Transform nodes to manage the conversion) → Expand the dynamic range (log retains highlight and shadow detail — the grade reveals it) → Matching clips from different cameras (match exposure and colour to the reference camera) → Noise in log footage (log footage in the shadows is very noisy — grade brightens noise).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Correct technical LUT for the log profile: wrong LUT produces wrong colour
- Log retains dynamic range: this is why it looks flat — all the information is there
- Shadow noise in log: lifted shadows reveal noise — clean up with noise reduction before grading
