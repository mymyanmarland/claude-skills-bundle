# Colour Grading for Social Media Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour grading for social media: Mobile viewing considerations (small screens / often viewed outdoors / high ambient light) → Higher contrast for mobile (shadows are crushed on phone screens — lift them slightly for mobile delivery) → Saturation (slightly more saturated than broadcast — pops on phone screens) → Colour cast check (phones have blue-shifted displays — check your grade on a phone) → Instagram colour shift (Instagram slightly desaturates and compresses uploaded video — grade accordingly) → Platform delivery specs (Instagram: sRGB colour space / TikTok: sRGB / YouTube: Rec.709) → Thumbnail colour (the thumbnail drives clicks — make the hero colour pop).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Check on a phone: desktop monitors and phone screens look very different
- Slightly more saturated for mobile: subtle grades disappear on phone screens
- Instagram desaturates on upload: grade slightly more saturated than you think is needed
