# Colour Grading Styles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour grading styles: Cinematic teal and orange (desaturate green from the shadows / push shadows toward teal / push skin tones toward orange) → Film emulation (grain / slight desaturation / lifted blacks / halation) → Bleach bypass (desaturated / high contrast / silver retention look) → Day for night (grade day footage to look like night: cool blue shift / darken / reduce saturation) → Vintage (faded blacks / warm shift / slight colour crossover) → Modern clean (true blacks / white whites / natural saturation) → Choosing a style (the grade should serve the story — not be applied regardless of content).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Teal and orange: the signature Hollywood blockbuster look — complementary colours on skin and shadows
- Lifted blacks for vintage: the most immediate vintage-look indicator
- Grade serves the story: the most beautiful grade that fights the story is a bad grade
