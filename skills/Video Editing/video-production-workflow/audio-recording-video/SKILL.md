# Audio Recording for Video Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software and platform
- Experience level
- Project type
- Specific task
- Output format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audio recording for video: Microphone types (shotgun: directional / on-camera / boom use / lavalier: clip-on / worn / interview use / cardioid: desktop / podcast) → Signal chain (microphone → preamp → recorder / or camera) → Gain staging (input level peaking between -18 and -12dBFS — never reaching 0) → Monitoring (always use headphones to monitor — you cannot hear problems otherwise) → Wind protection (dead cat / blimp for outdoor shooting) → Room acoustics (soft furnishings absorb reflections / hard surfaces cause reverb) → Dual system audio (dedicated recorder + scratch track on camera) → Timecode or clapper board for sync.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Monitor with headphones always: you cannot fix audio that was not monitored during recording
- Gain at -18 to -12dBFS: leaves headroom for unexpected peaks
- Wind protection outdoors: wind on a microphone is the most common and most fixable audio disaster
