# Interview Production Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software and platform
- Experience level
- Project type
- Specific task
- Output format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Interview production: Setup (camera on a tripod / slightly off-axis / subject looks toward the space in front of them / never directly at camera) → Background (depth behind the subject / slightly out of focus / not a plain wall) → Lighting (key from the side of the interview / back light separates from background) → Audio (lavalier clipped to the chest / or boom just above the frame) → Interview technique (ask open questions / allow silence — subjects fill silence with their best answers) → B-roll list (make a list of all b-roll needed during the interview) → Cutaways (nod shots and listening shots from a second angle).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slightly off-axis camera: direct-to-camera is powerful but unusual — offset feels more natural for most interviews
- Allow silence: the most valuable interview directing technique — silence produces the best answers
- B-roll list during the interview: capture what to shoot while the subject is still there
