# Camera Settings for Video Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software and platform
- Experience level
- Project type
- Specific task
- Output format

## Step 2 — Core Output

Camera settings for video: Frame rate (24fps: cinematic / 25fps: PAL broadcast / 30fps: NTSC broadcast / 60fps: slow motion) → Shutter speed (180-degree rule: shutter speed = 2× frame rate / 1/50 for 25fps / 1/60 for 30fps) → ISO (as low as possible — ISO 100-800 for most conditions / higher ISO = more noise) → Aperture (controls depth of field and exposure / wide = shallow DOF / narrow = more in focus) → White balance (manual for consistency — auto shifts throughout the shoot) → Picture profile (LOG for maximum dynamic range / Standard for quick delivery) → ND filter (reduces light without changing aperture or shutter — essential outdoors).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 180-degree shutter rule: correct motion blur — doubling the frame rate gives natural motion
- Manual white balance: auto white balance shifts colour between shots — a nightmare to match in post
- ND filter for outdoor shooting: allows wide aperture in bright sunlight
