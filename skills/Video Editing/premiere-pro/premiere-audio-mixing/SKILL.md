# Audio Mixing in Premiere Pro Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audio mixing in Premiere: Gain vs Volume (Gain: clip-level adjustment before fader / Volume: track-level fader) → Normalise audio (right-click clip / Audio Gain / Normalise to -12dB for dialogue) → Essential Sound panel (tag clips: Dialogue/Music/SFX/Ambience — applies appropriate processing) → Dialogue cleanup (DeNoise / DeReverb / DeEss in Essential Sound) → Keyframing volume (create volume automation in the timeline: Ctrl/Cmd click) → Audio track mixer (monitor levels in real time) → Target levels (-12 to -6dBFS peak for dialogue / music under the dialogue).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Essential Sound panel: the fastest route to broadcast-quality audio in Premiere
- Tag clips before processing: the panel applies appropriate presets by clip type
- Normalise to -12dB: leaves headroom for mixing — not to 0dBFS
