# Final Cut Pro Magnetic Timeline Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Magnetic Timeline: What it is (FCP's timeline automatically closes gaps and avoids collisions) → Primary storyline (the main video track — everything attaches to or is part of it) → Connected clips (attach to the primary storyline / move with their attachment point) → Storylines (create a secondary storyline from connected clips for more complex editing) → Disable Magnetic Timeline (hold Backquote to temporarily use position tool) → Ripple Delete (Delete key removes clip and closes gap) → Skimming (hover over footage in the timeline / press S to toggle).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Connected clips move with attachment: clips attached to the primary storyline follow it
- Storylines for complex connected editing: secondary storylines give more control than individual connected clips
- Skimming: the most efficient FCP navigation method — always enable it
