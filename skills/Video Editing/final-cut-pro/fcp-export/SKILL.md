# Final Cut Pro Export and Delivery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

FCP export: Share destinations (File / Share / Add Destination — save custom presets) → Master File (File / Share / Master File — uncompressed ProRes export — the archive master) → YouTube and Vimeo (built-in presets / select quality level / automatic upload option) → Compressor (send to Apple Compressor for advanced encoding options) → Export by Role (export separate audio and video stems) → Batch Share (select multiple projects / share all at once — a unique FCP advantage) → Frame.io integration (FCP 10.6+ / review and approve direct from FCP).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Master File first: always export a ProRes master before the compressed delivery
- Batch Share: export multiple sequences at once — a significant FCP advantage
- Frame.io integration: client review built into the application — saves round-trips

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
