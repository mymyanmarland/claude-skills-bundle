# Final Cut Pro Multicam Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

FCP multicam: Create Multicam Clip (select clips / right-click / New Multicam Clip / sync by audio waveform) → Angle Editor (double-click multicam clip in timeline — shows all angles) → Switch angles (Command-click an angle in the Angle Viewer) → Cut and switch simultaneously (live cutting in the Angle Viewer: video cut with angle click / audio cut with Ctrl-click) → Override audio angle (Multicam Inspector / Audio Component — choose a specific angle for audio) → Expand Multicam Clip (Control-Shift-Y — shows all angles in the main timeline) → Smooth Cut (FCP's content-aware cut — reduces jump cuts on talking heads).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sync by audio waveform: the most reliable sync method for any multicam setup
- Override audio angle: always use the dedicated audio track not the switching video audio
- Smooth Cut: a genuine time-saver for interview editing — AI removes jump cuts
