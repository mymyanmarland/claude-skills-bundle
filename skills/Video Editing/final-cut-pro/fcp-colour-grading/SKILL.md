# Final Cut Pro Colour Grading Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

FCP colour grading: Colour inspector (open with Cmd-6) → Colour board vs Colour curves vs Colour wheels (three interfaces for the same corrections) → Colour curves (the most powerful — HSL curves / individual YRGB curves) → Colour wheels (traditional Lift/Gamma/Gain interface) → Video Scopes (Window / Show in Workspace) → Colour mask (select a colour range for secondary corrections) → Shape mask (spatial mask for local corrections) → Match Colour (FCP analyses a reference frame and applies its grade to another clip automatically) → Broadcast Safe effect (limits the signal to broadcast legal levels).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Match Colour: the fastest route to consistency across multiple clips
- Colour curves for power: the most precise interface within FCP's native tools
- Video scopes mandatory: colour grade without scopes is guesswork
