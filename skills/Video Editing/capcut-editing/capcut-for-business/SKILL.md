# CapCut for Business Content Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

CapCut for business: Brand consistency (save a colour palette and font choice / apply consistently) → Product showcase template (search Product / Showcase in templates) → Corporate templates (search Corporate / Professional in templates) → Logo overlay (import logo as PNG / position and resize as an overlay) → Screen recording integration (import screen recordings for tutorials and demos) → Text lower thirds (name / title bars for interviews) → Export for LinkedIn (1080x1080 or 1920x1080 / MP4 / H.264 / under 200MB) → CapCut for Teams (business accounts with shared assets).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Logo as PNG with transparent background: white background logos look unprofessional
- Brand colour palette saved: consistency across videos builds brand recognition
- LinkedIn format: 1:1 or 16:9 / under 10 minutes / MP4 under 200MB
