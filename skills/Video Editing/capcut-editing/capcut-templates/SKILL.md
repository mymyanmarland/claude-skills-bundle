# CapCut Templates Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

CapCut templates: Finding templates (Home / Templates tab / search by style or trending) → Using a template (tap Use Template / add your photos or videos in the prompted slots) → Customising (once added / edit text / change filters / adjust timing) → Creating a template (complete a project / Share / Save as Template — share with others) → Business templates (search for professional styles: product showcase / corporate) → Music replacement (not all templates allow music changes — depends on the creator's settings).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Add clips in the correct order: templates are designed for specific clip placements
- Customise after applying: templates are a starting point not a final product
- Save as Template: share your own reusable edits with your audience

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
