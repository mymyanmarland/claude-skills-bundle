# CapCut Effects and Filters Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

CapCut effects and filters: Filters (select clip / Filters tab / choose a filter / adjust intensity) → Effects (Effects tab / Video Effects: clip-specific / Overlay Effects: on top of everything) → Transitions (tap the white square between clips / select a transition / adjust duration) → Stickers and overlays (Stickers tab / search for animations / position and resize) → Trending effects (New / Popular — effects used in viral videos) → Remove background (Auto Removal or Custom removal) → Beauty and face effects (Beauty tab for skin smoothing / reshape).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Effects intensity slider: always reduce from 100% — most effects look better at 50-70%
- Transitions sparingly: overused transitions are the hallmark of amateur editing
- Auto background removal: works best on clean single-person shots
