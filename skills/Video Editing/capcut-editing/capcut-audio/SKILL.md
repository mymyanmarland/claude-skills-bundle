# CapCut Audio Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

CapCut audio: Add music (Audio tab / Music / search or browse / trim to fit) → Sound effects (Audio / Sound Effects / search) → Extract audio from video (select clip / Audio / Extract Audio — separates the audio) → Voice changer (Audio / Voice Effects) → Auto beat sync (Audio / Auto Beat Sync — automatically cuts to the music beat) → Reduce noise (select audio / Noise Reduction toggle) → Volume (select clip / Volume / adjust main vs original audio balance) → Fade in/out (Audio / Fade — add natural entry and exit).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Auto Beat Sync for music videos: the fastest route to a beat-matched edit
- Extract audio to re-sync: useful when the original audio needs to be separated
- Fade in and out on music: abrupt music starts and stops always sound unprofessional
