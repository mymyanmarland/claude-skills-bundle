# CapCut Green Screen and Chroma Key Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

CapCut chroma key: Overlay method (import background clip / add person clip as an overlay) → Remove background (select overlay clip / Remove BG / Auto Removal) → Chroma Key (Remove BG / Chroma Key / tap the colour to remove) → Edge refinement (Strength slider / Feather for soft edges) → Shadow (add a shadow layer for realism) → Backdrop (the background clip plays behind) → AI background (select from CapCut's AI background options) → Green screen filming (consistent even lighting on the background for clean removal).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Even lighting on the green screen: uneven lighting is the most common removal failure
- Feather the edge: hard edges look composited — feathering blends naturally
- Add a shadow for realism: the most effective single improvement to a composite
