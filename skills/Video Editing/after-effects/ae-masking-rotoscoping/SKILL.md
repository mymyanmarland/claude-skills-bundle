# After Effects Masking and Rotoscoping Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Masking and rotoscoping: Mask types (Bezier / RotoBezier / Ellipse / Rectangle — draw on a layer) → Mask Feather (softens the edge) → Track Matte (uses a layer as a mask for another: Luma / Alpha Matte) → Rotobrush (the automated rotoscoping tool: draw on the subject / it tracks frame by frame) → Refine Edge tool (after Rotobrush — smooths the edge particularly on hair) → Content-Aware Fill (fill a masked area using surrounding pixels across the timeline) → Motion tracking (track a point on the footage / attach a mask or layer to the track).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rotobrush: AE's automated rotoscoping — dramatically faster than manual masking
- Track Matte: use one layer as the shape for another — a non-destructive masking approach
- Refine Edge on hair: the most difficult rotoscoping challenge — Refine Edge handles it
