## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

## Step 2 — Core Output

AE Expressions: What expressions are (small pieces of JavaScript that drive properties dynamically) → Enable expression (Alt/Option click the stopwatch) → Wiggle expression (wiggle(frequency, amplitude) — random movement: wiggle(3,20) = 3 times per second, 20 pixels) → Loop expression (loopOut('cycle') — loops the keyframed animation indefinitely) → Value expression (value + [0, -5] — offsets a property) → Time expression (time * 360 — continuously rotates at 1 revolution per second) → Link expressions (pickwhip from one layer's property to another — they follow each other).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wiggle expression: the single most used expression in motion graphics
- loopOut cycle: the fastest way to loop an animation indefinitely
- Pickwhip linking: link properties between layers — changes one and both update
