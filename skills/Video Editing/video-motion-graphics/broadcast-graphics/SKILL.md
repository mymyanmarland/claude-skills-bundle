# Broadcast Graphics Production Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Broadcast graphics: Safe areas (action safe 90% / title safe 80% — critical for broadcast) → Broadcast legal levels (IRE levels: black at 16 / white at 235 in 8-bit / use Broadcast Safe effect to check) → Drop shadows for legibility (text on video must always be readable — subtle shadow or background box) → Lower third standards (name / title / 5-second minimum duration) → Bug placement (station logo: top right or bottom right / opacity 60-80%) → Font licensing (broadcast-safe font licence required for fonts used in broadcast deliverables) → Graphics rendered as an alpha channel (PNG sequence or MOV with transparency for flexible playout).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Title safe zone mandatory for broadcast: text outside the title safe zone is cut off on older TVs
- Broadcast legal levels: footage outside the legal range causes distortion on broadcast equipment
- Font licence for broadcast: retail font licences do not cover broadcast — check terms
