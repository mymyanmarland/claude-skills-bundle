# Title Sequence Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Title sequence design: Purpose (set the tone / introduce the world / establish the style before the content begins) → Concept development (what visual world does this title sequence inhabit?) → Typography as the hero (many great title sequences are typographic / font choice is the primary decision) → Reveal mechanics (position / rotation / scale / path / particle) → Music and timing (the music drives the edit — choose it first) → Colour palette (reflects the content's emotional world) → Duration (30-90 seconds for a feature / 5-15 seconds for a YouTube video) → Reference viewing (watch The Art of the Title website for inspiration).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Choose the music first: the title sequence is timed to the music — not the other way around
- Font choice is the primary decision: the typeface communicates the genre and tone immediately
- The Art of the Title: the definitive reference for title sequence design
