# Lower Thirds Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lower thirds design: Anatomy (name on the first line / title on the second / optional logo) → Safe area placement (bottom third of the frame — not touching the bottom edge) → Animation (slide in from the left / or fade up / 1-2 second duration) → Typography (one typeface / two weights: bold for name / regular for title) → Colour (brand colour as accent / high contrast with the frame) → Duration (on screen for 3-5 seconds minimum — long enough to read twice) → Lower third templates (save as a Premiere .mogrt or After Effects template for reuse).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 2-second animation: longer animations feel slow / shorter feel rushed
- Read it twice rule: the lower third must be on screen long enough to read the text twice comfortably
- One typeface two weights: more than one typeface looks amateur
