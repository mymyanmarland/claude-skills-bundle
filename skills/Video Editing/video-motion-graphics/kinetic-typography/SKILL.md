# Kinetic Typography Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Kinetic typography: What it is (text that animates in sync with speech or music) → Planning (transcribe the audio / mark every word / plan the animation timing) → Word-by-word animation (Premiere clip per word / or AE text animator) → Reveal animations (masked wipe / scale up / slide in) → Colour emphasis (highlight the key words — lighter and brighter) → Camera moves (zoom into key words / pull back for context) → Lyric video approach (music drives the timing / beat drives the cut) → Font selection (bold weight for emphasis / display fonts for personality).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Plan the timing first: transcribe and mark every word before animating anything
- Highlight key words: not every word needs equal emphasis — colour and scale distinguish
- Beat-driven timing: words that hit on the beat feel satisfying — off-beat feels wrong

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
