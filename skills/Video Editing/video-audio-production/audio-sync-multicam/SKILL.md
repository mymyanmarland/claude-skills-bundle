# Audio Syncing and Multicam Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audio syncing: Clapper board (the visual and audio sync reference — clap at the start and end of each take) → Manual sync (find the clap in the video waveform / match to the clap in the audio recorder) → Software sync (PluralEyes / DaVinci Resolve sync by waveform / Premiere Merge Clips by audio) → Dual system audio (camera records scratch track / external recorder records clean audio / sync in post) → Timecode sync (professional workflow: jam-sync timecodes between camera and audio recorder) → Post-sync verification (play back the synced audio / check for drift over time).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scratch track on the camera: always record audio on the camera for the sync reference
- Waveform sync in Resolve or Premiere: eliminates manual sync for most projects
- Check for drift: timecode drift over long takes requires re-syncing at intervals
