# Sound Design for Video Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sound design basics: Why sound design matters (70% of film is sound / audiences forgive bad visuals before bad audio) → Sound effects libraries (Freesound.org / Zapsplat / SoundSnap / Universal SoundFX) → Foley (record your own sound effects: footsteps / cloth / objects — a microphone and creativity) → Layers (every sound is made up of layers: a punch sound = impact + body + whoosh + reverb) → Perspective (close sounds are louder / distant sounds are quieter — match the visual) → Room ambience (add background ambience to every scene — silence feels dead) → Reverb and delay (add space and depth — match the physical space shown on screen).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Silence feels dead: add ambience to every scene — even a quiet room has a sound
- Layer sound effects: one sound effect is thin — three to five layers are convincing
- Perspective matching: a close-up shot needs close-sounding audio — wide shot needs wider audio
