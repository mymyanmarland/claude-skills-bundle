# Dialogue Editing for Video Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dialogue editing: Room tone (fill gaps with room tone to avoid dead silence) → Noise reduction (iZotope RX / Resolve Fairlight / Adobe Audition — reduce background noise first) → EQ for dialogue (high-pass at 80-100Hz / boost 2-5kHz presence range / cut 300Hz for less boxiness) → De-essing (reduce sibilance / harsh S sounds / typically 5-8kHz) → Compression (gentle compression: 4:1 ratio / -6dB threshold / 30ms attack / 100ms release) → Mouth noise removal (RX Mouth De-click / iZotope) → Normalise to -12dBFS before mixing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- High-pass filter at 80-100Hz: removes rumble and HVAC noise from every dialogue track
- Room tone fills silence: dead cuts between dialogue are jarring — fill with room tone
- iZotope RX: the industry-standard dialogue repair tool for professional results
