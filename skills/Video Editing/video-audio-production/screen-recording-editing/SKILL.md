# Screen Recording and Tutorial Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Screen recording editing: Remove mouse drift (move the cursor purposefully / edit out aimless mouse movement) → Zoom and highlight (zoom into the important area / highlight with a colour rectangle) → Chapter structure (break tutorials into logical chapters with titles) → Cut dead time (remove waiting for programs to load / unnecessary pauses) → Caption all instructions (screen recordings often have small text — captions make them accessible) → Demo then explain (show the action / then explain what happened) → Screen recording tools (OBS for free / Camtasia for pro tutorial editing / Loom for quick capture and share).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zoom to the relevant area: viewers cannot see small UI elements on a full-screen recording
- Cut all dead time: loading screens and pauses test viewer patience
- Chapter structure with titles: tutorial viewers navigate to specific sections — make it easy

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
