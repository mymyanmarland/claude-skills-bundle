# Podcast and Talking Head Video Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Podcast and talking head editing: Remove filler words (um / uh / like — selectively: too many removals sound unnatural) → Jump cut coverage (B-roll / cutaways / graphics hide the visible jump cuts) → Multicam podcast (switch between angles on key moments / not randomly) → Noise floor (consistent background noise between cuts — room tone fills the gaps) → Breath removal (reduce loud breaths before dialogue lines — do not remove all breathing) → AI dialogue removal tools (Descript / Adobe Podcast / auto-remove filler words) → Pacing (podcasts and interviews need rhythm — vary the cut rate).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- B-roll covers jump cuts: the reason B-roll is gathered in the first place
- AI filler word removal: Descript and Adobe Podcast automate this for long-form content
- Consistent noise floor: the audible cut between different audio environments is very jarring
