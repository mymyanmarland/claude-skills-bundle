# Vertical Video Production Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Vertical video production: Aspect ratio (9:16 / 1080x1920 pixels) → Shooting vertically (rotate the camera / or crop from 16:9 — cropping loses quality) → Safe zone (1080x1350 is the visible area when swiping on TikTok / action must stay within it) → Text placement (subtitles and graphics in the lower third / top third is safe from UI overlays) → Composition reframe (when converting 16:9 to 9:16: centre the subject / zoom in / reframe per scene) → Auto Reframe (Premiere and Resolve both have AI auto-reframe tools) → Dual delivery (shoot natively for 9:16 while planning 16:9 coverage simultaneously).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shoot vertically if you can: cropping 16:9 to 9:16 always sacrifices quality
- Safe zone for UI: avoid the bottom 30% and top 10% for any critical information on TikTok
- Auto Reframe for conversions: AI tools handle the reframe faster and more accurately than manual
