# Video Delivery Specifications Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video delivery specs: YouTube (H.264 or H.265 / 1920x1080 or 3840x2160 / 25/30/50/60fps / AAC 320kbps / MP4 container) → Instagram Reels (MP4 / H.264 / 1080x1920 / 30fps / AAC / under 90 seconds) → Broadcast (MXF or MOV / DNxHD or ProRes / 1920x1080 / 25fps PAL or 29.97fps NTSC / PCM audio / -23 LUFS audio) → Cinema DCP (JPEG2000 / 2048x1080 or 4096x2160 / 24fps / up to 250Mbps) → Client delivery (ask before starting — most clients do not know what they want until you ask) → Master file (always export an uncompressed master before the delivery encode).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Always export a master first: the compressed delivery file should never be the only copy
- Ask the client before starting: discovering wrong specs after finishing is very costly
- Broadcast specs are strict: broadcast delivery requires exact compliance — check with the broadcaster

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
