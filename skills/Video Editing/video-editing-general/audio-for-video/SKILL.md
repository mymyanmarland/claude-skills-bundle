# Audio Fundamentals for Video Editors Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Audio fundamentals: Levels (dialogue: -12dBFS peak / music: 6dB below dialogue / sound effects: match the energy of the scene) → LUFS (integrated loudness: YouTube -14 LUFS / Spotify -14 LUFS / broadcast -23 LUFS / streaming -16 LUFS) → Sample rate and bit depth (48kHz / 24-bit for video production — not 44.1kHz which is music) → Phase cancellation (when two identical signals cancel each other — flip phase on one track if audio sounds thin) → Room tone (record 30 seconds of silence on every location shoot — fills gaps in the edit) → Peak vs integrated loudness (peak clips badly / integrated measures perceived loudness).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Music 6dB below dialogue: the most commonly broken audio mixing rule
- Record room tone on every shoot: fills edit gaps with natural silence instead of dead air
- LUFS not dBFS for delivery: streaming platforms normalise to LUFS — target the platform's spec
