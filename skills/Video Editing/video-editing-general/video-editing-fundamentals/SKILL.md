# Video Editing Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video editing fundamentals: The cut (the basic editorial unit — every cut should have a reason) → Continuity editing (match cuts / eyeline matching / 180-degree rule) → The 180-degree rule (camera must stay on one side of an imaginary line — crossing it disorients the viewer) → Pacing (cut rhythm matches the emotional tone — faster cuts = more energy) → J-cut and L-cut (audio from the next clip starts before the video / audio from the previous clip continues — the most cinematic transitions) → Cutaways (B-roll covers cuts and provides context) → The 6 rules of film editing (Walter Murch: emotion / story / rhythm / eye trace / 2D plane / 3D space).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- J-cuts and L-cuts: the most cinematic audio transitions — use them instead of straight cuts
- Every cut needs a reason: if you cannot explain why you cut, do not cut
- B-roll covers everything: cutaways hide mistakes, hold attention, and add context
