# Storytelling Through Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Storytelling through editing: The three-act structure (set up / confrontation / resolution — apply to any length video) → Character introduction (establish who / where / why before the action) → Tension and release (build tension through pacing and music / release through a cut or resolution) → Show don't tell (let the footage tell the story / minimise narration) → Interview structure (shape the narrative from the answers — the story emerges from the material) → Music and emotion (music drives the emotional response — choose deliberately) → The power of silence (pause and silence are as powerful as cuts and music).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Music drives emotion before the cut does: choose the music before you start editing
- Show don't tell: a close-up of hands or a look says more than ten words of narration
- The power of silence: a pause before the most important moment amplifies it
