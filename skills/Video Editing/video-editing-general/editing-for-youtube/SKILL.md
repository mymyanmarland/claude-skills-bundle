# Editing for YouTube Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

YouTube editing: Retention editing (remove anything that does not serve the viewer — every second should earn its place) → Pattern interrupts (change the frame / add a graphic / cut every 20-30 seconds to maintain attention) → Chapter markers (add timestamps in the description for YouTube chapters) → End screen (last 20 seconds / add subscribe / next video elements) → Intro length (under 30 seconds — audiences leave if the video takes too long to start) → B-roll quantity (60%+ of a talking-head YouTube video should be covered with B-roll) → Thumbnail and title (optimise for click-through rate — the most important metric).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Remove everything that does not serve the viewer: the most important YouTube editing rule
- Pattern interrupt every 20-30 seconds: sustained attention requires regular stimulation
- Intro under 30 seconds: tell viewers what they will get then get to it
