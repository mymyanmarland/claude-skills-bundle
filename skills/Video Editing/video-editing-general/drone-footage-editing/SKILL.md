# Editing Drone Footage Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Drone footage editing: D-Log and D-Log M (DJI's log format — apply a LUT before grading) → Wind noise removal (EQ: high-pass filter at 200Hz removes propeller hum) → Reveal shots (drone reveals a location as it rises or turns — a classic edit) → POV matching (cut from ground to drone at a similar angle for continuity) → Transition cuts (drone swoops or pulls back as a natural transition) → Stabilisation (drone footage often needs additional stabilisation despite gimbals) → DJI LUTs (DJI provides free LUTs for each camera model on their website).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Apply the DJI LUT before grading: D-Log without a LUT looks flat and grey
- High-pass filter for wind noise: remove the rumble below 200Hz — most wind is low frequency
- DJI LUTs are free: download from the DJI website for the exact camera model
