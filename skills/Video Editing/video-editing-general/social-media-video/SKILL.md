# Social Media Video Editing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social media video editing: Aspect ratios (TikTok/Reels/Shorts: 9:16 vertical / YouTube: 16:9 horizontal / Instagram Feed: 1:1 or 4:5 / LinkedIn: 16:9 or 1:1) → Duration (TikTok: 15-60 seconds optimal / Reels: 15-90 seconds / YouTube Shorts: under 60 seconds / YouTube: 7-15 minutes) → Captions (85% of social media video is watched without sound — captions are essential) → Hook (the first 3 seconds must stop the scroll) → Pacing (social media edits are faster than broadcast) → Thumbnail (create a custom thumbnail for YouTube — the most important click driver).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- First 3 seconds are the hook: if the viewer is not engaged by then they have already scrolled
- Captions on all social video: 85% watched without sound — captions are not optional
- 9:16 for vertical: always re-edit for the correct aspect ratio — do not just crop 16:9
