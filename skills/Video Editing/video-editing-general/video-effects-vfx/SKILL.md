# Video Effects and Basic VFX Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Software version and platform (Windows/Mac)
- Experience level: beginner/intermediate/advanced
- Type of project: social media/film/documentary/YouTube/corporate
- Specific task or technique
- Output format or delivery requirement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video effects and VFX: Motion tracking (track a point in footage / attach graphics or effects) → Stabilisation (warp stabiliser / remove camera shake) → Screen replacement (track the screen / replace with graphic) → Speed ramping (slow down the peak moment / speed up the approach) → Glitch effects (RGB split / distortion / static — deliberately broken aesthetic) → Light leaks and flares (overlay blend mode / add atmosphere) → Ken Burns effect (animated zoom and pan on still images — gives life to photos) → Compositing basics (layering elements / blend modes / masking).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Motion tracking before screen replacement: accurate tracking makes compositing convincing
- Speed ramp at the peak: the best slow motion moments are action peaks not random moments
- Ken Burns: pan and zoom together — not just zoom or just pan
