# Science Lab Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Science concept to investigate
- Grade level
- Safety considerations
- Available equipment
- Inquiry level: structured/guided/open

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lab design: Phenomenon or question → Materials list → Safety notes → Procedure (appropriate to inquiry level) → Data collection table → Analysis questions (from data to claim) → Conclusion protocol (claim + evidence + reasoning) → Error analysis → Extension questions. CER framework embedded throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Investigation starts with a question — not a procedure
- CER applied to actual data collected — not given
- Safety addressed specifically — not just 'be careful'

Always offer a relevant next step.
