# Classroom Management Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- School context (urban/suburban/Title I)
- Class size
- Known behavioral challenges
- Teacher's philosophy: authoritative/restorative/structured

## Step 2 — Core Output

Management plan: Physical environment setup → Procedures for routines (entering, transitions, materials, dismissal) → Expectations: 3-5 positively stated → Acknowledgment system → Consequence hierarchy (natural → logical → escalating) → Relationship-building strategies → Parent communication plan → Restorative practices integration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Procedures specific enough to teach — not just listed
- Expectations positively stated — what TO do not what not to do
- Consequence system proportionate and consistent

Always offer a relevant next step.
