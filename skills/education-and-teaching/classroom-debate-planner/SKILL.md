# Classroom Debate Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Debate topic and grade level
- Format: Oxford/Lincoln-Douglas/Parliamentary/informal
- Time available
- Research access
- Assessment focus

## Step 2 — Core Output

Debate plan: Topic framing (arguable, age-appropriate, evidence-available) → Research guide (sources, evidence types) → Argument structure template (claim + evidence + reasoning) → Rebuttal practice → Format explanation → Speaking roles → Evaluation rubric → Debrief questions. Both sides of the argument developed.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Topic genuinely arguable — two defensible positions
- Research guide leads to appropriate sources for grade level
- Evaluation assesses argumentation quality — not win/loss

Always offer a relevant next step.
