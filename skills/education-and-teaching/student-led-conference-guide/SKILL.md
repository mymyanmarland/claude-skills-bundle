# Student-Led Conference Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- Portfolio or work evidence available
- Conference time allotment
- Student communication skills
- Parent audience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Student-led conference guide: Student preparation process (portfolio curation, reflection writing, practice) → Conference script template (introduction, portfolio walk, goals, closing) → Parent preparation materials (what to ask, what to look for) → Teacher role during conference → Follow-up protocol. Makes students agents in their own learning story.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Student presents own work — teacher doesn't take over
- Script template gives structure without scripting every word
- Reflection genuine — not just listing what they did

Always offer a relevant next step.
