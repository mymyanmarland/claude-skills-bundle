# Student-Centered Learning Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current lesson description
- Objectives to maintain
- Student readiness for autonomy
- Available resources for choice
- Time constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Redesigned lesson: Voice (students contribute to the question or direction) → Choice (menu of products or paths) → Reflection (metacognitive components) → Agency (students drive at least one segment) + teacher's shifted role. Each shift explained: why this matters for learning. Gradual release model applied.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Student choice is genuine — different paths to same objective
- Teacher role described specifically — what they do differently
- Agency level appropriate to student readiness

Always offer a relevant next step.
