# Restorative Practices Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Incident type or general practice
- Grade level
- School culture context
- Community readiness
- Time and space available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Restorative practices guide: Circle types (proactive/responsive) → Circle facilitation script → Affective statements (expressing impact) → Restorative questions (what happened / who was affected / how to repair) → Agreement protocol → Follow-up check-in → Re-entry plan for students returning from suspension. Harm-focused not rule-focused.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Process centers the harm — not the rule violation
- All affected parties have voice
- Agreement is specific and agreed to — not imposed

Always offer a relevant next step.
