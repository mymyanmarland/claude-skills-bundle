# Growth Mindset Curriculum Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- Subject context
- Current fixed mindset patterns observed
- Time allocation
- School culture support

## Step 2 — Core Output

Growth mindset curriculum: Core concepts (brain science + effort + strategies + help-seeking) → Academic integration points (where to embed in content naturally) → Language shifts (fixed vs growth self-talk) → Mistake-celebration protocols → Challenge culture builders → Progress tracking over time (not performance tracking). Dweck's research applied practically.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Growth mindset integrated with academics — not separate from content
- 'Yet' language embedded throughout
- Effort praised for strategy — not just working hard

Always offer a relevant next step.
