# Reading Comprehension Strategy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Text type: narrative/informational/argumentative
- Grade level
- Reading level of students
- Strategy focus: inferencing/main idea/text structure
- Assessment goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strategy instruction sequence: Explicit modeling (I do — teacher thinks aloud) → Guided practice (We do — shared text) → Collaborative practice (You do together) → Independent practice. For each strategy: what it is, when to use it, think-aloud script, practice materials, transfer opportunities. Metacognitive language throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gradual release evident — doesn't skip 'I do'
- Think-aloud script models actual strategic thinking — not just the answer
- Strategy transfer to new texts explicit

Always offer a relevant next step.
