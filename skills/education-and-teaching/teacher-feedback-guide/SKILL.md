# Teacher Feedback Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Assignment type
- Student readiness for feedback
- Feedback channel: written/verbal/peer
- Time available
- Growth area to focus on

## Step 2 — Core Output

Feedback framework: What is praise vs feedback (praise evaluates, feedback describes next steps) → Feed-up (where are we going?) + feedback (where are we now?) + feed-forward (how do we get there?) → Written feedback template → Verbal feedback script → Student response protocol (what do they DO with feedback?) → Closing the feedback loop.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Feedback describes next steps — not just what's wrong
- Feed-forward is specific and actionable
- Student responds to feedback — it's not a monologue

Always offer a relevant next step.
