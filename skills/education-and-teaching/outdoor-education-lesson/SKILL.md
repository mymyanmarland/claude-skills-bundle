# Outdoor Education Lesson Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Subject and grade level
- Outdoor space available
- Weather/safety considerations
- Academic standards to address
- Learning goals beyond academics

## Step 2 — Core Output

Outdoor lesson: Academic integration (how content connects to outdoor context) → Safety briefing → Observation and inquiry activities → Data collection in the field → Indoor connection and synthesis → Debrief questions → Weather adaptations → Assessment of both academic and environmental learning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Academic content genuine — not just 'we went outside'
- Safety briefing age-appropriate and specific
- Outdoor experience cannot be replicated indoors — that's the point

Always offer a relevant next step.
