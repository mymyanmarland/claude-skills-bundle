# Teacher Self-Care & Sustainability Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Teaching context
- Specific stressors
- Current self-care practices
- Time constraints
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sustainability plan: Energy audit (what drains vs restores) → Boundary setting in the classroom and beyond → Efficiency systems for grading/planning/communication → Community building for teacher support → Professional joy practices → Non-negotiables list → Red flag monitoring (when to seek support). Teaching is a marathon — protect the runner.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Strategies practical for actual teacher schedule
- Boundary setting validated — not just productivity hacks
- Professional joy center — not just stress management

Always offer a relevant next step.
