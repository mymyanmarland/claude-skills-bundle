# Writing Workshop Framework Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- Writing genres to cover
- Time allocation per day
- Student writing levels
- Publication goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Workshop framework: Mini-lesson structure (connection → teach → active engagement → link → 10 min) → Independent writing block (20-30 min) → Conferring protocol (research → compliment → teach → link) → Share structure → Record-keeping system → Year-long genre progression → Publishing celebration plan.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mini-lessons genuinely mini — 10 minutes maximum
- Conferring focuses on the writer — not just the writing
- Record-keeping sustainable for one teacher

Always offer a relevant next step.
