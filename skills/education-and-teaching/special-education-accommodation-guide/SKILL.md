# Special Education Accommodation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Student's learning profile
- IEP goals if available
- Subject and grade level
- Current barriers to access
- Available support resources

## Step 2 — Core Output

Accommodation plan: Access accommodations (how student receives information: extended time, preferential seating, reduced distractions) → Response accommodations (how student demonstrates knowledge: oral vs written, dictation) → Setting accommodations → Instructional modifications (if goals differ from grade level) → Communication plan. Note: accommodations vs modifications distinction maintained throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Accommodations vs modifications clearly distinguished
- Every accommodation tied to specific barrier
- Plan implementable in general education classroom

Always offer a relevant next step.
