# Gifted Learner Extension Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Core curriculum content
- Gifted learner profile
- Extension goals: depth/complexity/independence/creativity
- Time available
- Compacting opportunities

## Step 2 — Core Output

Extension plan: Curriculum compacting assessment → Enrichment menu (not just harder versions of same work) → Depth and complexity prompts applied to content → Independent study pathway → Product options requiring creative synthesis → Mentorship or expert connection opportunity. Focus: more depth and complexity — not more work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Extensions require thinking at higher Bloom's levels — not just more practice
- Curriculum compacting eliminates mastered content — not added to it
- Student voice in extension path where possible

Always offer a relevant next step.
