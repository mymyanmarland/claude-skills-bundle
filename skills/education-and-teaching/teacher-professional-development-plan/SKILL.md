# Teacher Professional Development Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Teaching area and grade level
- Current evaluation rating or self-assessment
- Specific growth area
- Available PD resources
- Timeline: semester/year

## Step 2 — Core Output

PD plan: Current state assessment (evidence-based) → SMART growth goal → Learning pathway (readings, observations, courses, coaching) → Implementation milestones → Evidence of impact on student learning → Reflection protocol → Supervisor conference talking points. Connected to classroom practice — not theoretical.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Goal SMART and tied to student learning
- Learning pathway specific resources — not vague categories
- Evidence of impact requires student data — not just teacher actions

Always offer a relevant next step.
