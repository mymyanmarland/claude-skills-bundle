# Academic Vocabulary Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Words to teach (or topic to identify them)
- Grade level
- Subject area
- Prior vocabulary knowledge
- Time for instruction

## Step 2 — Core Output

Vocabulary sequence: Tier 2 and 3 word identification → 6-step vocabulary instruction (Marzano): describe with explanation + student in own words + picture/symbol + compare/classify + metaphor/analogy + game. Plus: word wall design, context sentence examples, cognate connections for ELL students, assessment strategy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tier 2 (academic) and Tier 3 (domain-specific) distinguished
- 6-step process builds deep knowledge — not just definition
- Assessment checks use — not just definition recall

Always offer a relevant next step.
