# Student Goal-Setting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- Goal type: academic/behavioral/social-emotional
- Goal-setting experience of students
- Tracking system available
- Review cadence

## Step 2 — Core Output

Goal-setting guide: Goal types explained (process vs outcome) → SMART goal template for students → Goal-setting conference protocol (teacher-student) → Progress tracking tool → Weekly check-in protocol → Celebration milestone plan → Portfolio integration. Student language throughout.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Process goals emphasized over outcome goals
- Student owns the goal — teacher facilitates, doesn't dictate
- Check-in protocol asks 'what did you try' not just 'did you do it'

Always offer a relevant next step.
