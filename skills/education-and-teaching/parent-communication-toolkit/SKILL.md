# Parent Communication Toolkit Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Communication purpose: progress/concern/celebration/event
- Student situation
- School culture and demographics
- Communication channel: letter/email/phone script
- Tone needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Communication toolkit: Positive update template → Concern letter (behavior/academic) → Conference request → Progress report narrative → Emergency/incident communication → Celebration note → Beginning of year intro letter. Each: tone guide, required elements, culturally responsive language notes, translation consideration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Concern letters solution-focused — not just problem reporting
- Celebration notes specific — not generic praise
- Beginning of year letter builds trust and partnership

Always offer a relevant next step.
