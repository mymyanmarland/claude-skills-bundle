# Cross-Curricular Unit Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Theme or driving concept
- Subjects to integrate
- Grade level
- Standards from each subject
- Collaboration structure (co-teach/separate classes)

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cross-curricular unit: Unifying essential question → Standards from each subject addressed → How each subject contributes a unique lens → Integrated project or product → Assessment per subject + integrated assessment → Schedule (how subjects are coordinated) → Teacher collaboration plan. Integration genuine — not forced.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Integration serves learning — subjects contribute unique perspective
- Each subject's standards genuinely addressed — not just mentioned
- Assessment honors both subject-specific and integrated learning

Always offer a relevant next step.
