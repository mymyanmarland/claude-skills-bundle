# Classroom Culture Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level
- Previous class culture challenges
- Community-building priorities
- School culture context
- Time investment possible

## Step 2 — Core Output

Culture-building plan: First week activities (community circle, norms co-creation, identity affirmation) → Ongoing rituals and routines (morning meeting, celebrations, check-ins) → Equity practices (turn-taking, amplification, history representation) → Repair protocols (when community ruptures) → Student leadership structures → Year-long relationship maintenance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Community built through genuine connection — not icebreakers
- Student voice in norms creation genuine
- Repair protocols specific — not just 'talk it out'

Always offer a relevant next step.
