# Curriculum Mapping Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Subject and grade level
- Standards document
- School calendar
- Assessment calendar
- Cross-curricular connections

## Step 2 — Core Output

Curriculum map: Month-by-month or unit-by-unit overview → Standards addressed per unit → Essential questions per unit → Assessments → Skills progression (how skills build on each other) → Cross-curricular connections → Differentiation notes. Format: calendar view + standards matrix.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Standards distributed logically — not crammed at end
- Skills progression shows genuine building
- Assessment calendar integrated — not separate

Always offer a relevant next step.
