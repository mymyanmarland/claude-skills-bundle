# Learning Station Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Topic and grade level
- Number of stations
- Differentiation levels needed
- Station management system
- Technology availability

## Step 2 — Core Output

Station design: Station quantity and rotation schedule → Each station: objective, materials, task, differentiation option, self-check → Management procedures → Group assignment strategy → Teacher station (small group instruction) → Transition protocol → Assessment of station work → Student accountability system.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each station has clear objective and self-check
- Teacher station focused on highest-need students
- Transitions efficient — procedures practiced before station use

Always offer a relevant next step.
