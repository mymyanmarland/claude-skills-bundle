# ELL Support Lesson Modifier Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Original lesson content
- ELL proficiency levels in class: entering/emerging/developing/expanding/bridging
- Language demands of the lesson
- Visual resources available
- Home languages if relevant

## Step 2 — Core Output

ELL modifications per proficiency level: Entering/Emerging (visual support, home language glossary, sentence frames for all activities) → Developing (modified text complexity, structured sentence stems, vocabulary preview) → Expanding (partial scaffolds, extended discussion prompts) → Bridging (near-grade modifications). For each level: specific modification to each lesson component.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Modifications specific to proficiency level — not one-size approach
- Language scaffolds removed gradually as proficiency grows
- Content rigor maintained — language scaffolded not content simplified

Always offer a relevant next step.
