# Critical Thinking Activity Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Subject and grade level
- Critical thinking skill focus: analysis/evaluation/synthesis/argumentation
- Content being taught
- Complexity appropriate for grade
- Assessment approach

## Step 2 — Core Output

Critical thinking activity design: Skill definition (what does good thinking look like here?) → Anchor task (complex problem requiring the skill) → Scaffolding sequence → Discussion protocol → Self-assessment tool → Transfer practice. For analysis: compare/contrast, cause/effect, perspective-taking. For evaluation: criteria development, evidence weighing, claim assessment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Activity requires genuine critical thinking — not factual recall
- Scaffolding removed gradually as skill develops
- Students articulate their thinking — not just produce an answer

Always offer a relevant next step.
