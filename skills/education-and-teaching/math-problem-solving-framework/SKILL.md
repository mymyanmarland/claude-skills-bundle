# Math Problem Solving Framework Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grade level and content area
- Problem type: open/closed/real-world
- Current instructional approach
- Conceptual understanding goals
- Assessment of reasoning

## Step 2 — Core Output

Problem-solving framework: Problem selection criteria (multiple entry points, multiple strategies, real context) → Launch protocol (notice/wonder, estimate first) → Explore phase (student strategies, teacher circulation) → Discussion structure (sequence student work from concrete to abstract) → Connect to grade-level goal. Assessment: 4-point reasoning rubric.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Problems have multiple valid strategies — not one right procedure
- Launch creates curiosity before students see the numbers
- Discussion sequences from concrete to abstract deliberately

Always offer a relevant next step.
