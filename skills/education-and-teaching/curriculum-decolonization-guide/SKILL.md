# Curriculum Decolonization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Subject and grade level
- Current curriculum audit
- Historical content areas
- Student demographics
- School community context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decolonization guide: Curriculum audit tool (whose perspectives are centered/marginalized) → Key questions for each unit → Resource expansion strategy → Alternative and additional texts → Student opportunity to critique the curriculum → Community knowledge integration → Revised unit description examples. Process: examine → question → expand → include.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Audit honest about current gaps
- Additions are authentic sources — not just mentioning marginalized groups
- Student critical analysis of curriculum itself included

Always offer a relevant next step.
