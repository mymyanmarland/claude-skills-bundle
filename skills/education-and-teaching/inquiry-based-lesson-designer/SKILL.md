# Inquiry-Based Lesson Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Science or social studies topic
- Grade level
- Prior student knowledge
- Laboratory/investigation resources
- Standards addressed

## Step 2 — Core Output

5E lesson: Engage (hook — creates curiosity, reveals misconceptions) → Explore (student investigation — hands-on, minimal teacher direction) → Explain (students share findings, teacher formalizes vocabulary) → Elaborate (apply to new situation) → Evaluate (assessment — how will you know they got it?). Teacher role: facilitator in Explore, not explainer.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Engage creates genuine curiosity — not just tells what's coming
- Explore is student-led — teacher resists explaining too early
- Explain comes AFTER explore — not before

Always offer a relevant next step.
