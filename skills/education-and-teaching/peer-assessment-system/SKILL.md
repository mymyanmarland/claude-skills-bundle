# Peer Assessment System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Assignment type
- Grade level
- Peer assessment goals: feedback/editing/evaluation
- Time available
- Student experience with peer assessment

## Step 2 — Core Output

Peer assessment system: Training protocol (how to give quality feedback — practice with samples) → Feedback form (structured prompts — not 'good job') → Criteria students assess against → Anonymity decision and rationale → Response protocol (how students use feedback) → Teacher review checkpoint. Sentence starters for constructive criticism included.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Training required before first use — not assumed
- Feedback form requires specific evidence — not general praise/criticism
- Revision required after receiving feedback — assessment informs action

Always offer a relevant next step.
