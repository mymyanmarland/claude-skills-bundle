# Family Screen Time Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Children ages
- Current screen time patterns
- Device types
- Biggest concerns
- Family values around technology

## Step 2 — Core Output

Screen time guide: Age-appropriate guidelines (AAP guidance) → Quality vs quantity distinction → Screen-free zones and times (meals/bedrooms) → Content evaluation framework → The 'off switch' challenge and solutions → Tech-free alternatives → Co-viewing and co-playing → Family media plan creation → Transition warnings reduce conflict.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Quality vs quantity distinction — not all screen time equal
- Transition warnings reduce conflict — practical
- Family media plan co-created — kids buy in more

Always offer a relevant next step.
