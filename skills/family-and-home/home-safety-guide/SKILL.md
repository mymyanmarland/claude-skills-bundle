# Home Safety Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Children ages
- Home type
- Specific hazards known
- Medical needs
- Emergency preparedness level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home safety guide: Age-specific hazard checklist (by stage: crawling/walking/climbing/curious) → Room-by-room safety audit → Medication and chemical storage → Pool and water safety → Stranger and online safety age-appropriate introduction → Emergency contact accessible to children → Fire safety and escape plan → Kitchen safety for cooking with children.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age-specific hazards — not one generic list
- Room-by-room comprehensive audit
- Fire escape plan practiced — not just written

Always offer a relevant next step.
