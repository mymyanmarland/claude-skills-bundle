# Family Health Management Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family size and ages
- Chronic conditions if any
- Insurance situation
- Current health management approach
- Biggest health admin challenges

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family health system: Medical record organization (digital vs physical) → Vaccination and screening schedule tracker → Medication management (storage/refills/administration) → Insurance card and EOB organization → Provider relationship management → Annual preventive care calendar → Urgent vs emergency care decision guide → Health history document for new providers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Record organization specific — what to keep and where
- Annual preventive care calendar prevents missed screenings
- Urgent vs emergency decision guide reduces ER overuse

Always offer a relevant next step.
