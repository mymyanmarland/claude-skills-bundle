# Home Cleaning System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Home size
- Household composition
- Work schedules
- Current cleaning approach
- Problem areas

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cleaning system: Daily reset (15 min) → Weekly deep areas (one room/zone per day) → Monthly maintenance tasks → Seasonal deep clean → Division by household member → Cleaning product minimalism (3-5 products for everything) → Speed cleaning protocol for unexpected guests → Cleaning task cards for children.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Daily reset 15 minutes — sustainable
- Task division explicit — not assumed
- Cleaning product minimalism — less is more

Always offer a relevant next step.
