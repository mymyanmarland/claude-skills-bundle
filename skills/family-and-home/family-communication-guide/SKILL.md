# Family Communication Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family composition and ages
- Biggest communication breakdowns
- Current communication attempts
- Conflict patterns
- Goal: connection/conflict resolution/co-parenting

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family communication guide: Family meeting structure (weekly, 30 min, all voices heard) → Active listening for parents → Age-appropriate conflict resolution → Teenager communication (autonomy while connected) → Screen-free connection time → Repair conversations after family conflict → Co-parenting communication (if applicable) → Family values clarification.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Family meetings structured — not free-for-all
- Teenager communication specifically addressed
- Repair conversations normalized

Always offer a relevant next step.
