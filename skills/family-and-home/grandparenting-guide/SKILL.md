# Grandparenting Connection Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Grandchildren ages
- Geographic distance
- Relationship with adult children
- Time available
- Interests to share

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grandparenting guide: Age-appropriate connection activities → Long-distance relationship maintenance (video calls/letters/care packages) → Role clarity (grandparent vs parent) → Conflict navigation with adult children → Special traditions for grandparent-grandchild only → Legacy sharing (stories/skills/history) → Technology for long-distance connection → Grandparent self-care.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Role clarity explicit — grandparent vs parent boundary
- Long-distance specific strategies not just in-person
- Legacy sharing — the unique grandparent gift

Always offer a relevant next step.
