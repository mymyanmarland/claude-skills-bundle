# Home Declutter Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Home size
- Biggest clutter areas
- Emotional attachment to items
- Time available
- Declutter goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Declutter guide: Mindset shift (things are for using, not keeping) → Room-by-room sequence (start easiest) → Keep/donate/trash decision framework → Category method vs room method comparison → Sentimental items protocol (last, not first) → Donation logistics → Selling strategy for valuable items → Preventing future accumulation → One in, one out rule.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Easiest rooms first — builds momentum
- Sentimental items last — not starting point
- Future accumulation prevention included

Always offer a relevant next step.
