# Family Pet Care Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Pet type and age
- Children ages
- Work schedule
- Budget for pet care
- Biggest pet challenges

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pet care plan: Age-appropriate child responsibilities → Adult oversight requirements → Veterinary care calendar (vaccinations/check-ups/preventives) → Feeding and exercise schedule → Emergency vet preparation → Pet budget (monthly and annual) → Pet sitter and boarding plan → Introducing pet to children protocol → When pet behavior needs professional help.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Child responsibilities age-appropriate
- Emergency vet preparation before needed
- Pet budget realistic — often underestimated

Always offer a relevant next step.
