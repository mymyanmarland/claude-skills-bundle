# Home Maintenance Calendar Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Home type: owned/rented, house/apartment/condo
- Climate zone
- Home age
- Current maintenance approach
- Budget for maintenance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home maintenance calendar: Monthly task list for all 12 months → Seasonal deep maintenance (spring/fall priority) → Priority tasks by urgency and consequence of neglect → Annual professional services (HVAC/chimney/inspection) → DIY vs professional decision guide → Emergency shut-off locations → Maintenance log template.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Monthly tasks achievable — not overwhelming
- Priority by consequence of neglect
- Emergency shut-off locations included — safety

Always offer a relevant next step.
