- Children ages

WFH with kids guide: Schedule architecture (non-overlapping work blocks with independent play) → Independent play development for younger children → Boundary-setting rituals (work mode signal) → Age-specific keeping-busy strategies → Meeting survival kit → Partner schedule coordination → Nap-time maximization → Grace for imperfect days — the camera-off permission.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Independent play development — investment that pays off
- Meeting survival kit specific and tested
