# School-Age Children Sleep Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Child ages
- Current sleep struggles
- School start time
- Screen time before bed
- Sleep duration concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Children sleep guide: Age-appropriate sleep needs (7-11: 9-11 hours / 12-18: 8-10 hours) → Bedtime routine design (consistent, calming, 30-45 min) → Sleep environment optimization → Screen-free period before bed → Sleep anxiety support → School-night vs weekend schedule → Tired cues for different ages → When sleep problems need professional assessment.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sleep needs specific by age — not one-size
- Screen-free period non-negotiable guidance
- Sleep anxiety support specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
