# Family Meal Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family size and ages
- Dietary restrictions or preferences
- Budget per week
- Cooking time available on weekdays
- Picky eater issues

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family meal plan: Weekly dinner schedule (5 nights planned, 2 flexible) → Batch cooking opportunities → Kid-friendly versions of adult meals → Pantry staple system (always able to make 5 meals from pantry) → Weekly grocery list organized by store section → Meal prep Sunday protocol → Leftover reinvention guide.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Picky eater strategy — exposure not force
- Pantry staple system for emergency nights
- Grocery list organized by store section — saves time

Always offer a relevant next step.
