# Kids Activity Schedule Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Child ages and interests
- School schedule
- Parent availability for transportation
- Budget for activities
- Overscheduled concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Activity schedule: Developmental priorities by age → Structured vs unstructured time ratio (unstructured play essential) → Activity selection framework (child-led vs parent-led) → Transportation reality check → Financial cost audit → Overscheduling warning signs → Dropping activities gracefully → Summer schedule vs school year.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Unstructured play protected — not all time scheduled
- Child-led choices emphasized
- Transportation and cost included — often overlooked

Always offer a relevant next step.
