# Family Meeting Facilitator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family composition and ages
- Meeting history
- Key issues to address
- Decision-making style
- Time available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family meeting guide: Meeting structure (appreciation → agenda items → problem-solving → fun planning) → Rotating roles (facilitator/recorder/timekeeper) → Age-appropriate participation → Decision-making methods (consensus vs voting vs parent final say) → Agenda building (anyone can add) → Meeting length by family composition → Between-meeting communication.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Appreciation first — sets collaborative tone
- Age-appropriate roles for children
- Decision-making method transparent

Always offer a relevant next step.
