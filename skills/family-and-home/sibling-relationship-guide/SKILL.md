# Sibling Relationship Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sibling ages and gap
- Conflict frequency and intensity
- Family dynamics
- Birth order considerations
- Specific conflict patterns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sibling guide: Conflict normalization (siblings fight — it is how they learn negotiation) → Parental intervention decision (when to step in vs let them work it out) → One-on-one time with each child → Sibling-only connection time → Fairness vs sameness distinction → Birth order awareness → Comparison avoidance → Sibling conflict mediation protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Conflict normalization first — reduces parent anxiety
- Parental intervention threshold specific
- Comparison avoidance — the rivalry driver

Always offer a relevant next step.
