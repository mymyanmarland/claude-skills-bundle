# Family Travel Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Destination or type of trip
- Ages of children
- Budget
- Duration
- Kids special needs or interests

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family travel plan: Destination selection for ages → Accommodation strategy (kitchen access value) → Packing list by age → Kid-friendly activity balance → Daily pace (family travel slower than adult travel) → Meal strategy (snack prep reduces meltdowns) → Budget breakdown → Backup plan for bad days → Managing screen time on travel.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pace calibrated to children's stamina — not adult pace
- Snack strategy explicit — prevents hangry meltdowns
- Backup plan for difficult days — essential with kids

Always offer a relevant next step.
