# Homework Help Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family context and composition
- Specific situation or challenge
- Ages involved
- Current approach
- Goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Complete, practical guide for homework help guide addressing the specific family context. Deliver structured, actionable advice organized by priority with specific scripts, templates, or plans as appropriate. Include common mistakes to avoid and realistic expectations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Advice age-appropriate and specific to stated context
- Realistic expectations set — no toxic positivity
- Actionable next steps clearly identified

Always offer a relevant next step.
