# Co-Parenting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Conflict level with co-parent
- Children ages
- Current co-parenting challenges
- Legal arrangement
- Communication method preference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Co-parenting guide: Business partner mindset (separate personal feelings from parenting) → Communication protocols (text/email for low conflict, app for high) → Transition routine for children → Consistency across households (what matters most) → Handling different parenting styles → Children's loyalty conflict prevention → High-conflict parallel parenting approach → Children's therapist consideration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Business partner mindset — removes personal from parenting
- Transition routines reduce children's stress
- Loyalty conflict prevention — children's primary need

Always offer a relevant next step.
