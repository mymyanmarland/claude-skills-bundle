# Family Grief Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Type of loss
- Children ages
- Family composition
- Cultural or religious context
- Support available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family grief guide: Age-appropriate explanations of death (concrete language for young children) → Honest answers to hard questions → Grief expression varieties (not linear) → Maintaining routines during grief → Memorialization ideas (keeping memory alive) → School communication → When professional grief support is needed → Adult grief and parenting simultaneously → Anniversary and trigger dates.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Concrete language for young children — 'passed away' confuses them
- Professional support triggers specific
- Anniversary dates planning prevents being caught off-guard

Always offer a relevant next step.
