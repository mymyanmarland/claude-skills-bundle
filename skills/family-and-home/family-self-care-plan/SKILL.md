# Family Self-Care Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Children ages and independence level
- Work situation
- Support system
- Time available
- Self-care history

## Step 2 — Core Output

Parent self-care plan: Reframe (self-care is parenting quality maintenance, not indulgence) → Micro self-care (5-min practices that actually restore) → Non-negotiable minimum (what cannot be eliminated) → Partner negotiation for individual time → Community building for support → Modeling self-care for children → Burnout warning signs → Permission to not be fine.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Micro self-care specific — realistic for parents
- Permission framing — removes guilt barrier
- Burnout warning signs help prevent crisis

Always offer a relevant next step.
