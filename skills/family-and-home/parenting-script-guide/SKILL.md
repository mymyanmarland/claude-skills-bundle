# Parenting Script Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Child age
- Specific behavior or situation
- Parenting style: authoritative/gentle/structured
- What has been tried
- Goal: correction/connection/explanation

## Step 2 — Core Output

Parenting scripts: Age-appropriate language calibration → The script for specific situation → What to do if child escalates → Follow-up conversation → Natural consequences framework → Repair conversation after losing patience. Scripts for: tantrums/lying/hitting/refusal/screens/sibling conflict/big feelings.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age-appropriate language — scripts differ for 4 vs 14
- Repair conversation included — parents lose it sometimes
- Natural consequences over punishment

Always offer a relevant next step.
