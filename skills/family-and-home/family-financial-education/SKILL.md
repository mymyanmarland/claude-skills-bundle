# Family Financial Education Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Children ages
- Current money exposure
- Family financial approach
- Allowance budget
- Learning goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family financial education: Age-appropriate money concepts (4-6/7-10/11-14/15-18) → Allowance structure and purpose → The three-jar system (spend/save/give) → First bank account timing → Earning beyond allowance → Teaching opportunity moments → Teen financial independence steps → Avoiding both extremes (too sheltered or too much too soon).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age-appropriate concepts specific — not one approach for all ages
- Three-jar system simple and effective
- Teen financial independence structured progressively

Always offer a relevant next step.
