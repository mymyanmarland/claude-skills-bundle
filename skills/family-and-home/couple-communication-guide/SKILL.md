# Couple Communication Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Relationship length and status
- Biggest communication breakdowns
- Conflict patterns
- Communication goal
- Children or co-parenting context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Couple communication guide: Gottman's four horsemen (criticism/contempt/defensiveness/stonewalling) and antidotes → Softened startup → The speaker-listener technique → Repair attempts during conflict → Bids for connection and turning toward → Weekly state-of-union meeting → Flooding recognition and break protocol → Appreciation ritual.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Four horsemen specific — most common communication destroyers
- Repair attempts normalized — imperfect communication is okay
- Weekly connection meeting protects relationship

Always offer a relevant next step.
