# Family Traditions Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Family composition and ages
- Cultural or religious background
- Current traditions
- Time and budget
- Values to reinforce

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family traditions guide: Daily connection rituals (short, consistent) → Weekly family traditions (family night/Sunday meals) → Annual celebrations (holidays + personal milestones) → Seasonal traditions → Spontaneous adventure rituals → Legacy traditions (connecting to family history) → New tradition creation process → Letting go of traditions that no longer fit.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Daily rituals short — consistency beats elaborateness
- Child input in tradition creation — increases meaning
- Letting go of outdated traditions normalized

Always offer a relevant next step.
