# Special Needs Parenting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Child diagnosis or challenges
- Child age
- School situation
- Support system
- Parent wellbeing

## Step 2 — Core Output

Special needs parenting guide: IEP/504 advocacy basics → School rights overview → Therapy coordination → Sibling communication and support → Extended family education → Parent self-care (caregiver fatigue is real) → Community and support resources → Transition planning (school to adult life) → Celebrating different milestones. Note: professional guidance essential.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Parent self-care explicitly included — not an afterthought
- IEP rights concisely stated
- Sibling support addressed — often overlooked

Always offer a relevant next step.
