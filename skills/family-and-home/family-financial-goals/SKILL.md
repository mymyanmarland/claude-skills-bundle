# Family Financial Goals Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Household income
- Current savings rate
- Key goals: home/college/retirement/vacation
- Financial conflict areas
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family financial goals: Goal inventory (individual and shared) → Priority alignment conversation (what matters most to each person) → Goal timeline and funding requirements → Savings automation setup → College savings options → Emergency fund as foundation → Investment basics for family goals → Annual financial review → Trade-off conversations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Goal priority alignment conversation — avoids resentment
- Automation removes willpower from saving
- Annual review keeps goals current

Always offer a relevant next step.
