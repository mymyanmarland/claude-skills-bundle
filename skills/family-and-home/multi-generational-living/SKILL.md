# Multi-Generational Living Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Generations involved
- Living arrangement type
- Relationship quality
- Space configuration
- Specific tensions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Multi-generational guide: Shared vs private space design → Financial contribution structure → Household responsibility division → Parenting authority clarity (grandparents as support, parents as authority) → Privacy and autonomy protocols → Conflict escalation procedure → Cultural and value difference navigation → When the arrangement is not working.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Parenting authority explicitly parents — common conflict source
- Financial contribution structure upfront
- Exit plan when arrangement not working

Always offer a relevant next step.
