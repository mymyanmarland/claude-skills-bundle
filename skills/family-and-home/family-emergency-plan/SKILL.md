# Family Emergency Preparedness Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Geographic location and risks
- Family composition including pets
- Medical needs
- Home type
- Communication plan needs

## Step 2 — Core Output

Emergency plan: Risk assessment (local hazards) → Evacuation routes (2+ options) → Meeting point protocol → Emergency contact list → Go-bag contents → 72-hour supply checklist → Communication plan (if separated) → Children's school emergency procedures → Medical emergency protocol → Pet plan → Practice drills schedule.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Location-specific risks addressed
- Children know the plan — practiced not just written
- Pet plan included — often forgotten

Always offer a relevant next step.
