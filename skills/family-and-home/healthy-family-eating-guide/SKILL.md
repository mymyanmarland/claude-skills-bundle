# Healthy Family Eating Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Children ages
- Current food battles
- Dietary restrictions
- Cooking skill and time
- Nutrition goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family eating guide: Division of responsibility (parent provides, child decides how much) → Family meal structure (sit together, connection not performance) → Vegetable exposure (not forcing, but regular offering) → Kitchen involvement for kids (increases willingness) → Snack timing (hunger drives better eating at meals) → Food label literacy for older kids → Treat approach (restriction increases desire).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Division of responsibility reduces battles
- Restriction increases desire — permission approach
- Kitchen involvement evidence-based for food acceptance

Always offer a relevant next step.
