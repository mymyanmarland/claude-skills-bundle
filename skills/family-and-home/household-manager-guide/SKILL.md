# Household Manager Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Household composition
- Whether planning to return to work
- Partner work situation
- Biggest household management challenges
- Personal fulfillment needs

## Step 2 — Core Output

Household manager guide: Household CEO mindset (professional skills applied) → System building for efficiency → Career continuity planning (skills maintenance, network) → Mental load visibility (making invisible work visible) → Partner equity conversations → Adult connection and identity beyond household role → Financial independence awareness → Transition planning if returning to work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mental load visibility tool — often the core issue
- Career continuity planning — practical protection
- Identity beyond role addressed — wellbeing essential

Always offer a relevant next step.
