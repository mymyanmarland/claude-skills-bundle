# Toddler Routine Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Toddler age
- Work schedule of parents
- Nap status
- Current routine pain points
- Daycare or home

## Step 2 — Core Output

Toddler routine: Wake to sleep schedule with windows → Meal and snack timing → Nap transition guidance → Activity types by time of day → Warning time strategy (5-minute warnings reduce tantrums) → Transition rituals (makes change predictable) → Weekend vs weekday adjustment → When routines need updating.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Warning times built in — reduces transition tantrums
- Activity types matched to energy windows
- Routine adjustment triggers identified

Always offer a relevant next step.
