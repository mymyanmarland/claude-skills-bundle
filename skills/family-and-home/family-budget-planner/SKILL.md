# Family Budget Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Household income (combined)
- Family size and ages
- Fixed expenses
- Current savings rate
- Financial goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Family budget: Income breakdown → Fixed expenses → Variable spending categories with targets → Child-specific costs (activities/school/childcare) → Emergency fund status → Savings goals (college/retirement/vacation) → Budget review process → Where most families overspend → Zero-based vs envelope approach options.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Child-specific costs explicitly included
- Emergency fund as non-negotiable line item
- Budget review process weekly or monthly — consistency matters

Always offer a relevant next step.
