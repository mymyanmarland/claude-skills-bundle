# Newborn and Baby Sleep Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Baby age
- Current sleep situation
- Parent sleep deprivation level
- Parenting philosophy
- Sleep training openness

## Step 2 — Core Output

Baby sleep guide: Sleep science basics (what is developmentally normal by age) → Safe sleep environment (AAP guidelines) → Wake window guide by age → Drowsy but awake foundation → Nap schedule evolution → Sleep association awareness → Sleep training options overview (no-cry to cry-it-out — honest pros and cons) → Realistic expectations → Prioritizing parental sleep.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Developmentally normal framing — reduces parent anxiety
- Safe sleep guidelines prominent
- Sleep training options presented without judgment

Always offer a relevant next step.
