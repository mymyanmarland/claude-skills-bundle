# Cause Marketing Campaign Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cause marketing guide: Partner selection (mission alignment check) → Value exchange articulation (what each party gains) → Campaign mechanics (purchase/donation/awareness) → Legal structure (commercial co-venture requirements) → Communication guidelines → Impact measurement → Consumer communication transparency → Long-term partnership vs one-off.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mission alignment genuine — transactional partnerships damage reputation
- Commercial co-venture legal requirements — jurisdiction specific
- Consumer transparency — FTC requires clear disclosure

Always offer a relevant next step.
