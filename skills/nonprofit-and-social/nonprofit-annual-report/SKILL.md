# Nonprofit Annual Report Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Annual report: Opening letter (personal, reflective, forward) → Year in numbers (headline metrics) → Impact stories (3-5 beneficiary profiles) → Program highlights → Financial summary (accessible language) → Donor recognition → Volunteer and partner thank you → Looking ahead section → Call to continued partnership.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Opening letter personal — not committee-written
- Impact stories show transformation not just service delivery
- Financial summary accessible — not just the 990

Always offer a relevant next step.
