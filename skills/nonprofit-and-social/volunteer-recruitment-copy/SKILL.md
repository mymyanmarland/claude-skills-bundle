# Volunteer Recruitment Copy Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Volunteer recruitment: Mission connection (why this cause needs them specifically) → Role descriptions with meaningful titles → Time commitment honesty → Skills needed vs skills gained → Community and belonging appeal → Impact statement (what their time accomplishes) → Onboarding preview (what getting involved looks like) → Application or next step CTA.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Time commitment honest — over-promising creates attrition
- Skills gained — volunteers want to develop too
- Impact statement specific — their hours equal what outcome

Always offer a relevant next step.
