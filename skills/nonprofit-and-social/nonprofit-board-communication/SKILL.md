# Nonprofit Board Communication Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Board communication: Board packet structure (consent agenda/discussion items/information) → Executive director report template → Financial report narrative → Board meeting agenda design → Board recruitment materials → Board orientation guide → Annual board assessment process → Crisis communication to board.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Consent agenda — routine items approved without discussion
- Executive director report: wins/challenges/asks — not just activity
- Financial narrative — translates numbers to story for board

Always offer a relevant next step.
