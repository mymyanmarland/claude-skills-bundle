# Donor Stewardship Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Donor stewardship: Thank you within 48 hours (the most important rule) → Multi-touch stewardship plan (12 months) → Impact reporting back to donors → Personal touches for major donors → Upgrade asks timing → Lapsed donor reactivation → Donor recognition program → Major gift cultivation pathway.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 48-hour thank you — the standard that retains donors
- Impact report back — close the loop on their gift
- Lapsed donor reactivation — often easier than acquiring new

Always offer a relevant next step.
