# Social Impact Storytelling Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Impact storytelling: Beneficiary-centered narrative (their voice, not the organization's) → Before state (specific, humanizing) → The intervention (what the organization did) → Transformation (specific, measurable, honest) → Their words (direct quote) → Universal theme (what this story says about the larger issue) → Ethical storytelling principles (dignity, consent, complexity).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Beneficiary-centered — they are the hero, not the organization
- Ethical storytelling — dignity preserved, no poverty porn
- Consent and complexity honored — not oversimplified

Always offer a relevant next step.
