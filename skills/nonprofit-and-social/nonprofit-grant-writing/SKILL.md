# Nonprofit Grant Writing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grant writing: Funder research and alignment → LOI (Letter of Inquiry) formula → Needs statement with data → Project description (SMART objectives) → Evaluation plan → Organizational capacity section → Budget narrative → Sustainability plan → Common grant mistakes → Relationship cultivation alongside applications.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Funder alignment research before writing — most rejections are misalignment
- Budget narrative justifies every line — reviewers read this
- Sustainability plan addresses the most common funder concern

Always offer a relevant next step.
