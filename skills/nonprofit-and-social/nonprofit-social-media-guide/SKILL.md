# Nonprofit Social Media Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Nonprofit social media: Content mix (70% education/community / 20% impact stories / 10% appeals) → Platform selection by audience → Volunteer and beneficiary voices → Behind-the-scenes content → Awareness date calendar → Emergency and crisis communication → Donor thank you content → Peer-to-peer fundraising amplification.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 70/20/10 content mix — not mostly appeals
- Volunteer voices — authentic third-party perspective
- Emergency communication protocol — crises happen

Always offer a relevant next step.
