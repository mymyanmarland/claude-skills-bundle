# Nonprofit Fundraising Letter Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fundraising letter: One specific beneficiary story (not statistics first — one person) → The need statement (without the donor, what happens?) → The impact statement (with the donor, what changes?) → The specific ask (amount suggestion with context) → Gift impact ladder ($25/$50/$100 — what each does) → Urgency element → P.S. (repeat the ask) → Reply device copy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One person story first — statistics follow emotion
- Gift impact ladder specific — donor sees their exact impact
- P.S. repeats the ask — often the first thing read

Always offer a relevant next step.
