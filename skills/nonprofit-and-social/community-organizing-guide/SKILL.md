# Community Organizing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Organization type and mission
- Target audience for this communication
- Current challenge or goal
- Resources and constraints
- Impact to communicate

## Step 2 — Core Output

Community organizing: Power analysis (who has what power, who is affected) → Relationship building before asking → One-to-one conversation structure → Issue identification with the community → Campaign goal setting (winnable, specific, timely) → Escalation ladder → Coalition building → Leadership development → Narrative and messaging for the campaign.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Relationship building before any ask — organizing 101
- Power analysis before strategy — understand the landscape
- Leadership development — organizing builds people not just campaigns

Always offer a relevant next step.
