# Stress Management Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Stress sources
- Stress symptoms
- Time for management techniques
- Previous approaches tried
- Lifestyle context

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stress plan: Stress audit → Physiological regulation (box breathing/4-7-8) → Cognitive reframing → Physical stress relief → Recovery activities → Stress inoculation for resilience → Professional support guidance → Daily routine.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Breathwork technique specific
- Recovery activities validated
- Professional support normalized for chronic stress

Always offer a relevant next step.
