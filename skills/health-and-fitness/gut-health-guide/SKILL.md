# Gut Health Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Symptoms if any
- Current diet
- Stress level
- Medication use
- Goal: symptom management or optimization

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gut health plan: Fiber diversity (30 plant foods per week) → Probiotic foods vs supplements → Prebiotic foods → Fermented foods introduction → Foods that harm gut → Stress-gut connection → Sleep role → Antibiotic recovery → Medical referral triggers → Symptom and food tracking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 30 plants per week — specific and evidence-based
- Probiotic supplement vs food distinction evidence-based
- Medical referral trigger points included

Always offer a relevant next step.
