# Gym Anxiety Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Specific anxiety triggers
- Experience level
- Type of gym
- Social anxiety level
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gym anxiety guide: Normalize (most people focused on themselves) → Strategic timing → Gym navigation map → Headphones as social signal → Pre-planned workout → Buddy system option → Start with machines → Confidence-building progression.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Normalization first — reduce catastrophizing
- Practical strategies not just 'be confident'
- Progressive exposure approach

Always offer a relevant next step.
