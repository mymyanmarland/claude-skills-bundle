# Yoga Sequence Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Yoga experience level
- Goal: flexibility/strength/stress/recovery
- Duration
- Physical limitations
- Style: Hatha/Vinyasa/Yin/Restorative

## Step 2 — Core Output

Yoga sequence: Opening (centering, breath) → Warm-up postures → Main sequence (peak poses or theme) → Cool-down → Savasana. Each pose: name, alignment cues, modifications, duration. Lengths at 20/45/60 minutes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Modifications for every pose — accessibility essential
- Peak pose built toward — not random
- Savasana non-negotiable

Always offer a relevant next step.
