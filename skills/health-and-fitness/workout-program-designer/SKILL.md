# Workout Program Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Primary goal: strength/muscle/fat loss/endurance
- Training days per week
- Equipment available
- Current fitness level
- Any injuries or limitations

## Step 2 — Core Output

12-week program: Phase 1 (weeks 1-4: foundation) → Phase 2 (weeks 5-8: build) → Phase 3 (weeks 9-12: peak). Each week: day-by-day sessions with exercises, sets, reps, rest periods. Progressive overload built in. Warm-up and cool-down protocols. Deload week at week 4 and 8. Nutrition timing notes. Progress tracking method.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Progressive overload explicit week-to-week
- Deload weeks prevent overtraining
- Warm-up specific to session type

Always offer a relevant next step.
