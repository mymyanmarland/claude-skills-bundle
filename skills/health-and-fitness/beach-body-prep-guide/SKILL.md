# Physique Preparation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Timeline to event
- Current starting point
- Goal physique changes
- Training history
- Dietary flexibility

## Step 2 — Core Output

Physique prep: Realistic timeline expectations → Training (muscle retention during cut) → Moderate deficit high protein approach → Water and sodium management (final days if timeline appropriate) → Presentation tips for photoshoots → Week-of protocol → Sustainable vs temporary changes → Body image considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Realistic expectations for timeline
- Sustainable vs temporary changes distinguished
- Body image considerations included

Always offer a relevant next step.
