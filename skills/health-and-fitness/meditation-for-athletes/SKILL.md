# Meditation for Athletes and Performers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport or performance context
- Meditation experience
- Time available
- Specific mental challenges
- Training schedule integration

## Step 2 — Core Output

Athlete meditation guide: Why meditation improves performance (focus/recovery/pain tolerance/pressure handling) → Breath awareness practice (foundation) → Body scan for recovery → Visualization meditation (sport-specific) → Pre-competition meditation routine (5-10 min) → Sleep meditation → Integration into training schedule → Apps and guided options.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sport-specific visualization — not generic mindfulness
- Pre-competition routine short and repeatable
- Integration into existing schedule — not added burden

Always offer a relevant next step.
