# Sleep Optimization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current sleep issues
- Sleep schedule
- Environmental factors
- Lifestyle factors
- Goal: more sleep/better quality/earlier wake

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sleep plan: Circadian rhythm alignment → Sleep hygiene protocol (consistent schedule/dark/cool/quiet) → Pre-sleep 90-min routine → Caffeine cutoff timing → Evening light management → Morning light protocol → Nap guidelines → Quality tracking → Issue troubleshooting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Circadian alignment first — timing matters most
- Caffeine cutoff specific to individual
- Morning light protocol included — often missed

Always offer a relevant next step.
