# Senior Fitness Program Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Age and current fitness level
- Health conditions and medications
- Exercise history
- Equipment available
- Doctor clearance status

## Step 2 — Core Output

Senior program: Functional movements (get up from floor, carry, climb) → Balance training (fall prevention primary) → Resistance training (sarcopenia prevention) → Moderate cardiovascular → Flexibility for daily function → Longer recovery time → Common condition modifications → Social exercise options.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fall prevention explicitly primary
- Longer recovery built into programming
- Functional movement focus — fitness for daily life

Always offer a relevant next step.
