# Sports Nutrition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and position
- Training volume and intensity
- Competition schedule
- Current nutrition approach
- Weight class considerations

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sports nutrition: Energy periodization → Carbohydrate timing for performance → Protein for recovery → Hydration strategy → Pre-competition protocol → During-competition fueling → Post-exercise recovery nutrition → Travel and away nutrition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Energy periodization not same calories daily
- Hydration specific to event type
- Competition nutrition practiced in training first

Always offer a relevant next step.
