# Marathon and Endurance Nutrition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Race distance
- Training volume
- Current fueling strategy
- GI issues history
- Race date

## Step 2 — Core Output

Endurance nutrition: Training nutrition → Carbohydrate loading protocol (3-day) → Race day morning nutrition → On-course fueling (timing/amounts/products) → Hydration with electrolytes → GI management → Post-race recovery → Training cycle nutrition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- On-course fueling practiced in training first
- GI issues addressed specifically
- Carbohydrate loading protocol specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
