# Injury Prevention Program Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport or activity
- Previous injuries or problem areas
- Training volume
- Age and training age
- Current prehab

## Step 2 — Core Output

Injury prevention: Risk assessment for sport → Targeted prehab exercises → Warm-up activating vulnerable areas → Training load management → Recovery protocols → Form priorities → Pain guidance → Return-to-sport progressions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Training load management primary — most injuries overuse
- Prehab targeted to risk areas
- Pain guidance specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
