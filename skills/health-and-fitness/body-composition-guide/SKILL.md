# Body Composition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current body composition
- Goal timeline
- Training experience
- Nutrition history
- Measurement method

## Step 2 — Core Output

Body composition plan: Recomposition vs bulk/cut decision → Caloric strategy per goal → Protein as constant (high regardless) → Training to preserve muscle in deficit → Progress via multiple measures (scale/photos/measurements/strength) → Timeline expectations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Recomp vs bulk/cut based on experience level
- Multiple measurement methods — scale alone misleading
- Timeline expectations realistic — recomp is slow

Always offer a relevant next step.
