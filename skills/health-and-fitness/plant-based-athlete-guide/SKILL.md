# Plant-Based Athlete Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and training volume
- Plant-based experience
- Nutrient concerns
- Performance goals
- Supplement budget

## Step 2 — Core Output

Plant-based athlete guide: Critical nutrients (protein/B12/iron/zinc/omega-3/D/creatine) → Protein strategy (quantity and variety) → Iron optimization → Supplement non-negotiables (B12/D/creatine) → Meal timing → High-volume food choices → Deficiency symptoms and monitoring.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- B12 supplement non-negotiable — prominently stated
- Iron absorption strategy specific
- Creatine recommended — evidence strong, dietary source absent

Always offer a relevant next step.
