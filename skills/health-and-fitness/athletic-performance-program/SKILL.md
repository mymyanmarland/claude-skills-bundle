# Athletic Performance Program Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport and position
- Performance goals: speed/power/agility/endurance
- Training age
- Season timing
- Facility access

## Step 2 — Core Output

Performance program: Physical demands analysis → Off-season strength foundation → Pre-season power → In-season maintenance → Speed development (sprint mechanics, acceleration, max velocity) → Plyometric progressions → Agility training → Energy system development for sport.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Season periodization changes training
- Sprint mechanics first — rarely coached
- Energy system matched to sport demands

Always offer a relevant next step.
