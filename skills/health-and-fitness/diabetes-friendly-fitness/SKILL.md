# Diabetes-Friendly Fitness Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Diabetes type
- Current medications
- Blood glucose patterns
- Exercise history
- Doctor involvement

## Step 2 — Core Output

Diabetes fitness guide: Exercise and glucose relationship → Monitoring around exercise → Hypoglycemia prevention and protocol → Exercise timing → Resistance training for insulin sensitivity → Carbohydrate adjustments → Signs to stop → Long-term management benefits. Work with healthcare team.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Healthcare team collaboration emphasized
- Hypoglycemia protocol specific — safety critical
- Aerobic vs anaerobic glucose response explained

Always offer a relevant next step.
