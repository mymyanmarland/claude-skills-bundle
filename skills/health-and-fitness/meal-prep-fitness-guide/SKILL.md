# Fitness Meal Prep Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fitness goal
- Calorie and macro targets
- Cooking skill and time
- Food preferences
- Equipment available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fitness meal prep: Weekly prep schedule (1-2 sessions) → Macro-friendly food list → Batch cooking guide → Portioning system by macro → Container system → 5 meal templates (high protein, balanced) → Snack prep for protein targets → Travel and office prep.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Protein-first meal design
- Portioning by macro — not by eye
- Travel meal prep included — often where diets break

Always offer a relevant next step.
