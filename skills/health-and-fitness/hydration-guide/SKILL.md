# Hydration and Electrolyte Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Activity level and sweat rate
- Climate and environment
- Sport if applicable
- Current habits
- Health conditions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hydration guide: Daily water target (calculated by weight and activity) → Urine color monitoring → Electrolyte needs (when water is not enough) → Sports hydration (before/during/after) → Electrolyte sources → Signs of dehydration and overhydration → Caffeine and alcohol adjustments.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Urine color monitoring — accessible to anyone
- Electrolytes included for long activity
- Overhydration included — hyponatremia risk real

Always offer a relevant next step.
