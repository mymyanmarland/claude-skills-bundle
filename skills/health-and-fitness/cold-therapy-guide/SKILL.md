# Cold Therapy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goal: recovery/mental health/fat loss/performance
- Current cold exposure experience
- Health conditions
- Timing around training
- Access to tools

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cold therapy: Evidence review (what it does vs hype) → Protocol by goal → Temperature and duration guidelines → Safety contraindications → Timing relative to strength training (avoid immediately post — blunts hypertrophy) → Gradual progression → Heat contrast protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Evidence honest — benefits real but limited for some claims
- Timing relative to strength training critical
- Safety contraindications listed

Always offer a relevant next step.
