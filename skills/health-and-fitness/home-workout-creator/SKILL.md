# Home Workout Creator Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Equipment: none/dumbbells/bands/pull-up bar
- Goal: fat loss/strength/cardio/flexibility
- Time per session
- Fitness level
- Space available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home workout program: Bodyweight progression system (easier to harder variations) → Workout templates by duration (20/30/45 min) → HIIT protocols → Strength progressions without adding weight (tempo/pause/unilateral) → Weekly schedule → Superset and circuit designs.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Progressions provided so workouts evolve
- Time-efficient designs using circuits
- Form cues for injury prevention

Always offer a relevant next step.
