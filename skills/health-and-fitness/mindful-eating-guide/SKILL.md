# Mindful Eating Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current eating challenges
- Emotional eating triggers
- Eating environment
- Restrictive diet history
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mindful eating: Hunger and fullness scale → Eating without distraction → Pause before eating → Emotional eating pattern recognition → Non-food coping strategies → Permission to eat all foods → Satisfaction vs fullness distinction → Social eating navigation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Permission to eat all foods — restriction drives bingeing
- Emotional eating triggers identified specifically
- Non-food coping alternatives specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
