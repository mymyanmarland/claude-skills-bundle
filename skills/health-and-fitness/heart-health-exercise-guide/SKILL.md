# Heart Health Exercise Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current cardiovascular health
- Medical history and clearance
- Fitness level
- Medications
- Exercise history

## Step 2 — Core Output

Heart health plan: 150 min moderate or 75 min vigorous per week → Zone 2 training (primary tool) → Heart rate monitoring → Resistance training for heart health → Gradual intensity progression → Warning signs to stop → Long-term adherence → Complementary lifestyle factors. Medical clearance for high-risk.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Medical clearance for high-risk individuals prominently stated
- Zone 2 training emphasis — evidence-based
- Warning signs specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
