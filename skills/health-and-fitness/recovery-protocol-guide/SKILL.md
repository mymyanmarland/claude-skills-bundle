# Recovery Protocol Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Training volume and intensity
- Current recovery practices
- Sleep quality
- Nutrition status
- Available recovery tools

## Step 2 — Core Output

Recovery protocol: Sleep optimization (primary tool) → Nutrition timing post-workout → Active recovery sessions → Modalities with evidence ratings → HRV monitoring → Deload planning → Stress management role → Overtraining recognition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sleep listed as primary — more important than any gadget
- Evidence ratings for modalities — honest
- Overtraining recognition prevents pushing through

Always offer a relevant next step.
