# Pain Management Exercise Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Pain location and type
- Pain severity 1-10
- Medical diagnosis if any
- Current activity level
- Medical clearance

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pain exercise guide: Pain neuroscience basics (pain does not equal damage) → Graded exposure approach → Movement variety → Specific exercises for common pain areas → Pacing strategy (boom-bust cycle prevention) → Flare management → Progress monitoring → When to stop → Multidisciplinary recommendation. Work with healthcare provider.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pain neuroscience framing reduces fear of movement
- Graded exposure — evidence-based for chronic pain
- Healthcare provider collaboration explicit

Always offer a relevant next step.
