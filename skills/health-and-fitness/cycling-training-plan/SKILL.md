# Cycling Training Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Cycling experience and current fitness
- Target event or goal
- Weekly hours available
- Indoor vs outdoor split
- Power meter or heart rate monitor availability

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cycling plan: Training zones (power or HR based) → Weekly structure (base/build/peak phases) → Key workouts per phase (Z2 base, threshold, VO2max) → Long ride protocol → Recovery ride use → On-bike nutrition → Race preparation → Rest week cadence → Cross-training for cyclists.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Training zones established before zone-specific work
- Z2 base dominates — most cyclists go too hard
- On-bike nutrition matched to duration

Always offer a relevant next step.
