# Weight Loss Strategy Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current weight and goal
- Previous diet attempts
- Activity level
- Lifestyle constraints
- Timeline

## Step 2 — Core Output

Weight loss strategy: Moderate caloric deficit (300-500 cal/day) → Protein prioritization for satiety and muscle → Food environment design → Hunger management tactics → Exercise selection → Sleep and stress impact → Plateau protocol → Maintenance plan — often ignored, causes rebound.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Moderate deficit not crash diet
- Food environment design behavioral not willpower-based
- Maintenance plan included

Always offer a relevant next step.
