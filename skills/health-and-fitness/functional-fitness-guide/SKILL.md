# Functional Fitness Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Age and current functional ability
- Daily life activities to improve
- Limitations or injuries
- Equipment access
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Functional fitness: Movement pattern approach (squat/hinge/push/pull/carry/rotate/locomotion) → Daily life movement mapping → Functional movement tests → Exercise selection by pattern → Carry variations (farmer's carry) → Ground-to-stand practice → Balance and stability → Work capacity.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Daily life movement mapped first
- Carry variations included — highly functional, often omitted
- Ground-to-stand critical for older adults

Always offer a relevant next step.
