# Protein Optimization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goal: muscle gain/fat loss/maintenance
- Body weight
- Training type and volume
- Dietary preferences
- Current protein intake

## Step 2 — Core Output

Protein guide: Individual target (0.7-1g per lb for active individuals) → Distribution (4-5 meals with 30-40g each) → Leucine threshold explanation → Best sources ranked → Timing around training → Protein before bed → Plant protein strategy → Protein during fat loss.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Evidence-based range
- Leucine threshold explained — why distribution matters
- Plant protein strategy specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
