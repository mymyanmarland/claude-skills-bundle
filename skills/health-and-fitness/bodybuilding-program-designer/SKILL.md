# Bodybuilding Program Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Training experience
- Current physique and goal
- Days per week
- Equipment access
- Priority muscle groups

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hypertrophy program: Volume landmarks (MEV/MAV/MRV) → Split design matched to experience and days → Exercise selection (best builders per muscle) → Rep ranges 6-20 mostly 8-15 → Intensity techniques for advanced → Progressive overload for hypertrophy → Diet fundamentals for muscle gain.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Volume landmarks not arbitrary sets
- Rep ranges evidence-based
- Progressive overload covers weight and volume

Always offer a relevant next step.
