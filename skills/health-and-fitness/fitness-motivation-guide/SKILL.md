# Fitness Motivation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Previous motivation failures
- What has briefly worked
- Intrinsic vs extrinsic motivation style
- Lifestyle context
- Fitness goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Motivation system: Motivation vs discipline reframe (motivation unreliable, build systems) → Identity-based approach → Intrinsic motivation development → Social accountability → Progress visibility → Minimum viable training → Reward systems → Long-term vision → When to change approach.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Motivation unreliable — systems focus
- Identity-based approach research-backed
- Minimum viable training defined

Always offer a relevant next step.
