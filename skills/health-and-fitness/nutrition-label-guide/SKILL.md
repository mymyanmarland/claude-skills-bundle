# Nutrition Label and Food Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goal: weight loss/muscle/health/allergy
- Knowledge level
- Specific food categories
- Shopping context
- Dietary restrictions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Nutrition literacy: Label anatomy (serving size manipulation, daily values) → Ingredient list reading → Macronutrient guide → Micronutrient priorities → Food quality signals → Marketing claim decoder → Shopping framework → Restaurant navigation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Serving size manipulation highlighted
- Marketing claim decoder specific
- Shopping framework practical

Always offer a relevant next step.
