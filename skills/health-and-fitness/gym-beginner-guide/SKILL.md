# Gym Beginner Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goal
- Age and health status
- Gym access
- Days per week
- Fears or concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beginner guide: Gym etiquette → Equipment familiarity → First 4 weeks (3 days full body, machines then free weights) → Form priorities → Progressive overload concept → Tracking → When to move to intermediate → Common mistakes → Nutrition basics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gym etiquette reduces anxiety
- Machine-first then free weights — safer
- Common mistakes prevent wasted early months

Always offer a relevant next step.
