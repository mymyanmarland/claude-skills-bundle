# Posture Improvement Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Specific posture issues
- Hours sitting
- Pain associated
- Previous attempts
- Exercise experience

## Step 2 — Core Output

Posture program: Postural assessment → Daily corrective routine (10 min) → Targeted exercises by pattern (forward head/rounded shoulders/anterior pelvic tilt/tech neck) → Ergonomic setup → Movement variety (posture is position, rest is variety).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Posture as position — movement variety framing
- Exercises targeted to assessed pattern
- Ergonomic setup fixes cause not just symptoms

Always offer a relevant next step.
