# Strength Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current strength levels or beginner
- Training days available
- Equipment: barbell/dumbbells/machines
- Primary lifts to improve
- Experience level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Strength program: Big compound lifts prioritized (squat/deadlift/bench/press/row) → Linear progression for beginners → Intermediate periodization → Form cues for main lifts → Accessory work → Warm-up set protocol → Deload approach → When to switch programs → Nutrition for strength.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compound lifts first, accessory work secondary
- Progressive overload on specific schedule
- Form cues for injury prevention on main lifts

Always offer a relevant next step.
