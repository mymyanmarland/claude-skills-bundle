# Triathlon Training Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Target distance
- Current fitness in each discipline
- Time available
- Weakest discipline
- Race date

## Step 2 — Core Output

Triathlon program: Discipline balance (train weakness, not neglect strengths) → Weekly structure with brick workouts → Swim progression → Bike zones → Run alongside bike → Brick workout design → Transitions practice → Race nutrition per discipline → Taper → Race day logistics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brick workouts included — race-specific adaptation
- Weakest discipline gets extra focus
- Race day logistics specific

Always offer a relevant next step.
