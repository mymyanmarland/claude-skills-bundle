# Intermittent Fasting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goal: weight loss/metabolic health/simplicity
- Current eating schedule
- Lifestyle constraints
- Activity level and training timing
- Medical considerations

## Step 2 — Core Output

IF guide: Protocol comparison (16:8/18:6/5:2/OMAD pros and cons) → Recommended starting protocol → Science (what IF does and does not do) → Hunger management → Training timing around fasts → Breaking the fast correctly → Common mistakes → Who should not do IF → Exit strategy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Honest about what IF does and does not do
- Who should not do IF listed
- Sustainability question asked

Always offer a relevant next step.
