# Habit-Based Health Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Health behaviors to establish
- Current habits and routine
- Past habit failure patterns
- Motivation style
- Lifestyle constraints

## Step 2 — Core Output

Habit design: Habit audit (existing as anchors) → Tiny habit design → Habit stacking → Environment design → Identity framing → Tracking system → Recovery protocol (missing once vs twice) → Long-term maintenance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tiny habits — consistency beats intensity
- Environment design removes willpower
- Missing once vs twice distinction critical

Always offer a relevant next step.
