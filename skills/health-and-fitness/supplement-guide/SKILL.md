# Evidence-Based Supplement Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Health and fitness goal
- Current diet
- Budget
- Health conditions
- Current supplements

## Step 2 — Core Output

Supplement guide: Tier 1 (well-evidenced: creatine/vitamin D/omega-3/magnesium) → Tier 2 (goal-specific: caffeine/beta-alanine) → Tier 3 (weak evidence, not worth money) → Avoid list → Quality considerations (third-party testing) → Timing for effectiveness → Food-first principle.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Evidence tiers honest — most supplements Tier 3
- Food-first principle stated
- Third-party testing for safety

Always offer a relevant next step.
