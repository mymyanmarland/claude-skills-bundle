# Desk Worker Exercise Plan Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Hours sitting per day
- Work schedule flexibility
- Equipment at or near work
- Commute type
- Specific pain points

## Step 2 — Core Output

Desk worker plan: Movement breaks every 45-60 min → Desk stretches (2-min routines) → Corrective exercises for posture → After-work training (counteracts sitting) → Ergonomic setup basics → Walking incorporation → Stand-up desk guidance → Weekly schedule around work.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Movement breaks timer-based — not aspirational
- Corrective exercises address sitting posture specifically
- Ergonomic setup prevents desk damage undoing exercise gains

Always offer a relevant next step.
