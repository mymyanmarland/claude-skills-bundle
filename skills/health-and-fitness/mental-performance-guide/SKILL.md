# Mental Performance Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sport or performance context
- Specific mental challenges
- Competition level
- Mental skills history
- Goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mental performance plan: Arousal regulation → Pre-performance routine → Visualization protocol → Self-talk strategy → Focus strategies → Resilience building → Process vs outcome focus → Post-performance review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-performance routine specific and timed
- Visualization structured — not just imagining winning
- Refocus routine for errors — the critical skill

Always offer a relevant next step.
