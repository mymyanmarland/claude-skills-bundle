# HIIT Workout Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fitness level
- Equipment available
- Session length
- Frequency per week
- Goal: fat loss/conditioning/athletic performance

## Step 2 — Core Output

HIIT program: Work-to-rest ratio by level (beginner 1:4 / intermediate 1:2 / advanced 1:1) → Protocol types (Tabata/AMRAP/EMOM/sprint intervals) → Exercise selection → Weekly structure (not every day) → Intensity via RPE → Progression model → Combination with strength training.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Work-to-rest matched to fitness level
- Not every day — recovery essential
- RPE monitoring prevents wrong intensity

Always offer a relevant next step.
