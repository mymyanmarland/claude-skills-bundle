# Fitness Tracking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Fitness goal
- Most important metrics
- Tools available
- Current tracking approach
- Data use for decisions

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tracking system: What to track (strength/body composition/cardiovascular/recovery) → How and frequency → Weekly check-in → Monthly review → Plateau protocol → Tools comparison → Photo and measurement protocol → Warning about obsessive tracking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Multiple metrics — not just weight
- Monthly review drives adjustments
- Warning about obsessive tracking — data should serve you

Always offer a relevant next step.
