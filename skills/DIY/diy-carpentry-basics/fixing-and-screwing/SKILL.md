# Fixing and Fastening in Carpentry Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fixing and fastening: Pilot holes (always pre-drill near the end of timber — prevents splitting) → Countersinking (countersink bit creates a neat recess for the screw head) → Screw length (minimum two-thirds of the screw should be in the receiving piece) → Screw types (wood screws / multi-purpose / structural screws — different uses and strengths) → Nailing (punch nail heads below the surface / fill over) → Coach bolts (through-fixing for structural connections — washer and nut) → Wood glue (PVA for most woodwork joints — clamp while curing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pilot holes near ends: splitting is avoidable — pre-drill always
- Two-thirds into receiving piece: screw length is a common mistake
- PVA and clamp: glue adds enormous strength — clamp until cured
