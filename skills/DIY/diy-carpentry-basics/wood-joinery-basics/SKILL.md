# Basic Wood Joinery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basic joinery: Butt joint (simple end-to-face — glue and screws / weakest joint but easiest) → Mitred joint (45-degree cuts at corners — neater appearance / use a mitre box for accuracy) → Rebate joint (a step cut into one piece / stronger than a butt joint) → Dowel joint (round wooden dowels align and reinforce butt joints) → Pocket screw joint (Kreg-style jig — fast and strong for cabinet and furniture making) → Choosing the right joint (strength required vs complexity vs tools available).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Butt joint reinforced: glue alone is weak — add screws or dowels
- Pocket screw joints: the fastest strong joint for DIY furniture — worth buying the jig
- Mitre box for 45 degrees: freehand mitres are never accurate

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
