# Measuring and Marking for Carpentry Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Measuring and marking: Tape measure technique (hook the end on a corner, read the measurement at eye level — not at an angle) → Marking (sharp pencil not a thick marker — precision matters) → Square (check your square is square — mark a line, flip the square, if the line continues straight it is accurate) → Mark the waste side (mark which side of the line the cut will remove) → Datum lines (mark a reference line from which all other measurements are taken) → Transfer dimensions (step off, do not re-measure from scratch every time — errors compound).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mark the waste side: cutting the wrong side of the line wastes the piece
- Sharp pencil: a thick line is 1mm of error — use a marking knife for finest work
- Check your square: a bad square makes every joint inaccurate
