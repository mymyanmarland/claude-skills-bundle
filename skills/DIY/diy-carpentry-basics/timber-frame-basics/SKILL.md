# Timber Frame Construction Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Timber framing basics: Stud wall components (sole plate / head plate / studs at 400 or 600mm centres / noggings for stiffness and fixings) → Sole plate fixing (to floor with appropriate fixings) → Head plate (to ceiling joists — locate joists before fixing) → Stud positions (400mm centres for plasterboard — allows boards to span three studs) → Noggings (horizontal timbers between studs at 1200mm height for board joints) → Door opening (doubled studs at jambs / lintel over the opening) → Structural walls (do not remove walls without checking whether they are structural — consult a structural engineer).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Never remove a wall without checking if it is structural: consult an engineer
- 400mm stud centres: 600mm centres are too wide for standard plasterboard spans
- Locate floor and ceiling joists: fix plates to structure not just the surface

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
