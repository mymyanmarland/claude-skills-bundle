# Door Hanging and Fitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Door hanging: Size and trim (mark the door, plane to width — 2mm gap each side / hinge side first, latch side second) → Hinge positions (100mm from top / 150mm from bottom / third hinge midway for heavy doors) → Marking recesses (score around the hinge / chisel recess to exact depth — hinge face flush with the timber surface) → Fitting hinges (pilot holes prevent splitting) → Hanging and adjusting (pack under the bottom, check the swing / adjust until the gap is even all around) → Latching (mark the strike plate from the latch bolt impression, chop the recess).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Even gaps all around: the mark of a well-hung door
- Hinge recess depth exact: too deep binds the hinge / too shallow makes the door proud
- Score around the hinge: chiselling without scoring tears the grain

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
