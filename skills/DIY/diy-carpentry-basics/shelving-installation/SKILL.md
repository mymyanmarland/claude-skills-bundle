# Shelving Installation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shelving installation: Wall type and fixing (solid wall / stud wall / plasterboard — choose correct fixing for the load) → Shelf brackets (standard / floating — choose based on aesthetics and load) → Level (use a spirit level — one end up or down is very noticeable) → Spacing (loaded shelves: support every 600mm for timber / every 400mm for particleboard) → Adjustable shelving systems (Ikea-style uprights — flexible and strong for heavy loads) → Floating shelves (concealed brackets — precise installation needed for strong result).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Level check always: tilted shelves are the most noticeable DIY mistake
- Support every 600mm: sagging shelves are a fixings and spacing failure
- Stud wall fixings: always try to hit the studs for heavy shelving

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
