# Basic Sawing Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sawing technique: Hand saw (start cut with pull strokes / guide the blade with the thumb of the non-cutting hand / let the saw do the work — no forcing) → Setting up (secure the workpiece in a vice or with clamps — never hold with the hand) → Cutting on the waste side (the saw kerf removes material) → Staying on the line (use a bench hook or saw guide for accurate cuts) → Circular saw (fence guide or straight-edge clamped to the board for straight cuts / blade depth set just below the material thickness) → Crosscut vs rip cuts (across and along the grain).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Let the saw do the work: forcing causes binding and inaccuracy
- Secure the workpiece: cutting into something held by hand is dangerous
- Blade depth just below material: reduces binding and kickback risk on circular saw
