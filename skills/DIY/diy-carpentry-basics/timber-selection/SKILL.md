# Timber Selection and Preparation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Timber selection: Softwood vs hardwood (softwood: pine/spruce — easier to work, cheaper / hardwood: oak/ash — more durable, harder to work, more expensive) → Sheet materials (plywood: strong and stable / MDF: smooth surface, easy to work but heavy and not water resistant / OSB: structural use) → Timber grades (structural grades for load-bearing / appearance grades for visible surfaces) → Moisture content (dry timber is essential — check for warping, cupping, and bowing before buying) → Sustainable sourcing (FSC-certified timber).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dry timber before using: wet timber shrinks and warps as it dries — this destroys joints
- MDF not in wet areas: moisture causes it to swell and crumble
- Check for warp in the shop: sight down the length — warped timber causes problems on every project
