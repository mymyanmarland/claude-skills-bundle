# Three-Dimensional Mosaic Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

3D mosaic: Base preparation (exterior of a pot / sculpture / architectural feature / must be sealed and keyed) → Adhesive for 3D (cement tile adhesive for rigid bases / silicone for flexible bases) → Working on curves (smaller tesserae fit curves better / cut to triangular wedge shapes for tight curves) → Top vs underside (the underside of projections: tesserae may fall before the adhesive cures / working in sections) → Temporary support (tape or foam supports underside tesserae while curing) → Outdoor 3D mosaic (all the outdoor considerations plus the complexity of 3D application).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Smaller tesserae on curves: large flat pieces cannot follow curved surfaces
- Work in sections: underside tesserae fall before adhesive cures — temporary support needed
- Key and seal the base: adhesive does not bond to dusty or smooth unprepared surfaces

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
