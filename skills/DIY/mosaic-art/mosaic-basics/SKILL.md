# Mosaic Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic basics: Materials overview (tesserae: the individual pieces / grout: the filler between / adhesive: what holds them down / base: what they are attached to) → Tesserae types (ceramic tiles / vitreous glass / smalti / found objects / pebbles) → Adhesive selection (PVA for indoor/flat surface / cement adhesive for outdoor/wet areas / silicone for glass-to-glass) → Cutting (tile nippers: score and snap / wheeled nippers for precise cuts) → Andamento (the direction of the lines of tesserae / affects the visual flow of the mosaic) → Grouting (fill the gaps / clean off the surface / polish).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Andamento is the mosaic's handwriting: the direction of the lines is a design choice not an afterthought
- Adhesive for the context: indoor adhesive fails outdoors / outdoor adhesive works everywhere
- Safety glasses for cutting: tesserae shards travel unpredictably when cut

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
