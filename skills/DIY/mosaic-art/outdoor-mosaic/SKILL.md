# Outdoor Mosaic Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Outdoor mosaic: Frost-resistant tesserae (vitreous glass / porcelain / natural stone — not all ceramics are frost-resistant) → Substrate (concrete / exterior board / sealed terracotta) → Cement-based adhesive (flexible / waterproof / suitable for freeze-thaw cycles) → Epoxy or exterior grout → Sealing (all grout lines sealed with a penetrating sealer) → Water drainage (any horizontal mosaic must have a slope for water to run off) → Frost-cycle testing (test materials together in freeze-thaw conditions before large projects).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Frost-resistant tesserae only: standard ceramic tiles crack in freeze-thaw cycles
- Flexible cement adhesive: rigid adhesive cracks as the substrate expands and contracts
- Water drainage slope: horizontal outdoor mosaics must drain — standing water causes failure

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
