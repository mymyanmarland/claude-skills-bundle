# Mosaic on Glass Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic on glass: What the reverse method is (tesserae placed face-down on a template / grouted / turned over to reveal the face) → Glass base (clear or coloured glass / allows light through) → Direct method on glass (place tesserae face up / use silicone adhesive / no grout or minimal grout) → Reverse method (arrange face-down on paper template / adhere temporarily / grout / turn over / remove paper) → Window mosaic (light transforms the piece — plan which tesserae will be used for their translucency) → Outdoor glass mosaic (fully waterproof silicone and grout required).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Silicone adhesive for glass-to-glass: only flexible silicone allows the glass to expand and contract
- Reverse method for complex designs: the face is always perfect when turned over
- Plan for translucency: which tesserae will glow when lit from behind is a key design decision

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
