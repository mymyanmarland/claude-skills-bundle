# Selling Mosaic Art Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Selling mosaic art: Pricing (materials + time at fair hourly rate + overheads — mosaic is labour intensive / most artists underprice) → Commission work (discuss design / substrate / location / budget / timeline / get a deposit) → Portfolio (photograph work with good lighting / show scale) → Platforms (local art fairs / Etsy / commission via Instagram / local interior designers) → Installation (outdoor and large indoor mosaics require professional installation — factor into the price) → Shipping (mosaic is heavy and fragile — local sales or freight shipping only for large pieces).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mosaic is labour intensive: the hourly rate must be realistic or the business is unsustainable
- Deposit for commissions: materials cost is significant — never start without a deposit
- Local sales for large pieces: heavy fragile art is expensive and risky to ship

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
