# Mosaic Restoration and Repair Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic restoration: Assessment (document the damage / photograph before starting) → Removing damaged tesserae (chisel carefully to avoid disturbing surrounding pieces) → Matching materials (find or cut matching tesserae / colour and texture must match) → Re-bedding (clean the surface / apply fresh adhesive / set the new piece level with the surface) → Regrouting (remove old grout / apply new grout to match / colour matching is rarely perfect) → Professional restoration (significant historical mosaics should be assessed by a professional conservator) → Preventive maintenance (seal grout annually in outdoor mosaics).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Document before starting: records of the original guide the restoration
- Colour match is rarely perfect: accept slight variation in repaired areas
- Professional conservator for historic mosaics: DIY restoration of significant mosaics risks further damage

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
