# Mosaic Design Principles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic design: Scale (scale the tesserae to the project size / large pieces on large work / small on small) → Value contrast (mosaic reads across a room — strong value contrast is more important than subtle colour) → Andamento planning (sketch the flow of lines before starting) → Background (the background tesserae flow behind the subject / their direction contrasts with the subject) → Opus regulatum (grid pattern / calm) → Opus vermiculatum (worm line following the subject outline) → Cartoon (draw or transfer the design to the base before cutting any tesserae).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value contrast above all: a mosaic that reads at distance needs strong contrast
- Opus vermiculatum outline: the classic mosaic technique of a line following the subject
- Background direction contrasts subject: the difference in direction separates subject from ground

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
