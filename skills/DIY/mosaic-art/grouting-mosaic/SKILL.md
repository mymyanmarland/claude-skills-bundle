# Mosaic Grouting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grouting mosaic: Grout selection (unsanded: joints under 3mm / sanded: joints over 3mm / epoxy grout: waterproof and chemical resistant) → Grout colour (dark grout: dramatic / unifies / lighter coloured tesserae pop / light grout: airy / colours are more distinct) → Mixing (thick peanut butter consistency) → Application (spread over the whole surface / work into all joints) → Clean-up (damp sponge / wring out thoroughly / circular motions) → Haze removal (dry cloth once the haze sets / polish) → Sealing (porous tesserae benefit from sealing before and after grouting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Grout colour is a design decision: test the effect before committing to a colour
- Thoroughly rung-out sponge: too much water weakens the grout and pulls it from joints
- Seal porous materials before grouting: unsealed stone and terracotta absorb grout

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
