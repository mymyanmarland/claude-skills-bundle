# Mosaic Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic community: Society of American Mosaic Artists SAMA (international membership / conferences) → British Association for Modern Mosaic BAMM → Mosaic Art Source (online tutorials and resources) → Books (The Mosaic Artist's Companion / Making Mosaics) → YouTube (Gold Leaf Mosaics / mosaic tutorials) → Online suppliers (Mosaic Art Supply / Mosaic Tile Mania / Jacksons Art) → Workshop directories → Instagram mosaic community (#mosaicart #mosaicartist) → Local art centres (often offer mosaic workshops).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- SAMA membership: connects to the international mosaic community and educational resources
- Workshops for technique: mosaic is best learned hands-on — attend a workshop
- Instagram mosaic community: one of the most supportive craft communities online

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
