# Mosaic Materials Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic materials: Vitreous glass (the most versatile / uniform thickness / wide colour range / suitable for indoor and outdoor) → Smalti (traditional Italian glass / irregular / rich colour / expensive / for serious work) → Ceramic tiles (affordable / wide availability / not all frost-resistant) → Porcelain (very hard / frost-resistant / requires special cutters) → Natural stone (marble / granite / slate / beautiful but heavy) → Recycled materials (broken pottery / china / tiles — distinctive and sustainable) → Grout (cement-based / epoxy / polyurethane) → Adhesive (PVA / cement / silicone — match to the application).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Vitreous glass for most projects: the best combination of quality, versatility, and price
- Smalti for serious work: expensive but the traditional finest mosaic material
- Match adhesive to context: indoor PVA / outdoor cement / glass silicone

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
