# Fibre Preparation for Spinning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fibre preparation: Raw fleece scouring (wash in hot soapy water / do not agitate — causes felting) → Picking (opening the fleece by hand after washing) → Hand carding (two hand cards / transfer fibre back and forth / peel off a rolag) → Drum carding (faster for large quantities / makes a batt) → Hand combing (aligns fibres parallel / produces worsted preparation) → Buying prepared fibre (combed top / roving / batts — skips the preparation entirely).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- No agitation when scouring: agitation felts raw fleece — ruin before it starts
- Carding vs combing: carding makes a lofty woollen yarn / combing makes a smooth worsted
- Buying prepared fibre: perfectly valid for beginners — raw fleece processing is a separate skill
