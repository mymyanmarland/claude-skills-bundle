# Spinning Wheel Maintenance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wheel maintenance: Oiling (oil all moving parts: flyer bearings / treadle pivot / maiden bearings — avoid getting oil on the drive band) → Drive band (leather or nylon cord / adjust tension with the scotch tension spring / replace when worn) → Brake band (controls the bobbin draw-in speed / adjust for the fibre being spun) → Flyer hooks (fibres catch on rough hooks — polish or replace) → Wheel alignment (drive wheel must spin without wobble — spoke tightening) → Bobbin storage (store with a little twist in the yarn to prevent the twist getting out).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Oil moving parts regularly: a dry wheel is inefficient and eventually damaged
- Avoid oiling the drive band: oil causes slipping
- Brake band adjustment: the draw-in should feel like a gentle tug — not a yank
