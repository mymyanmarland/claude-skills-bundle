# Art Yarn Spinning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Art yarn spinning: What art yarns are (highly textured decorative yarns — prioritise appearance over function) → Coils (thick singles wrapped tightly around a thinner core) → Beehive yarn (sections of thick low-twist fibre) → Nepps and locks (adding raw fibre pieces during spinning) → Plying with ribbon or thread (sparkle and texture) → Core spinning (fibre wrapped around a stabilising core yarn) → Uses (wall hangings / framing / woven art — not for functional knitting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Art yarn prioritises appearance: not designed for functional garments — tension and consistency not relevant
- Core spinning for stability: wrapping fibre around a core creates a wearable art yarn
- Plying with ribbon: sparkle and textural contrast add visual interest

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
