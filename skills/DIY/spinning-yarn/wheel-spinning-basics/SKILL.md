# Wheel Spinning Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wheel spinning basics: Wheel parts (drive wheel / treadles / flyer / bobbin / orifice / hooks) → Treadling (steady rhythm / both feet / drive wheel turns the flyer) → Drafting zone (the space between the orifice and the drafting hand — where twist enters the fibre) → Woollen vs worsted preparation (woollen: lofty / worsted: smooth and dense) → Singles and plying → Navajo plying (three-ply from a single bobbin) → Wheel maintenance (oil the bearings / check the drive band tension).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Steady treadling rhythm: inconsistent treadling creates inconsistent yarn
- Keep fibre out of the orifice: twist entering the fibre supply creates a knot
- Oil the wheel regularly: a dry wheel is a squeaky and inefficient wheel
