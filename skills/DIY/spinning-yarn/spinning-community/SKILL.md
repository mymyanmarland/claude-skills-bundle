# Spinning Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Spinning community: Ravelry spinning groups (project records / forum / fibre reviews) → Local spinning guilds → Spin-offs (magazines: Spin Off / PLY — dedicated to handspinners) → YouTube channels (The Woolery / KCatC) → Fibre festivals (Rhinebeck / SOAR / Black Sheep) → Online fibre shops (The Woolery / Paradise Fibres / Gist Yarn) → Study groups (spin-alongs focused on a single technique).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ravelry project records for spinning: tracking what you spun and how is invaluable
- Fibre festivals: the best place to touch fibre before buying and meet the community
- Spin-alongs: focused community learning is more motivating than solo practice

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children.
