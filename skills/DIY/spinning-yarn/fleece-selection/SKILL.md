# Fleece and Fibre Selection Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fleece and fibre selection: Breed characteristics (Merino: soft fine / BFL: lustrous medium / Corriedale: beginner-friendly / Corriedale: slightly coarser / Shetland: elastic and warm) → Micron count (lower micron = softer — under 25 microns is next-to-skin soft) → Staple length (longer staple = worsted preparation / shorter = woollen) → Prepared forms (combed top / roving / batts / raw fleece — ordered by preparation level) → Plant fibres (cotton / linen / bamboo — require different spinning technique from wool) → Blends (wool-silk / wool-alpaca — luxury for fine garments).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Corriedale for beginners: forgiving staple length and micron count
- Under 25 microns next-to-skin: coarser wools itch against bare skin
- Combed top for beginners: raw fleece preparation is a separate skill — buy prepared
