# Polymer Clay Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Polymer clay basics: What polymer clay is (PVC-based / remains soft until oven-baked / does not dry out in the air) → Conditioning (knead the clay before use — makes it pliable / cold clay crumbles) → Brands (Fimo / Sculpey / Premo / Cernit — different firmness and finish) → Baking (domestic oven / manufacturer temperature — typically 110-130°C / do not exceed or the clay burns) → Tools (smooth tools / ball stylus / silicone shaping tools / cookie cutters) → Finishing (sand with wet/dry sandpaper / buff to a shine / varnish for protection).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Condition thoroughly before sculpting: cold stiff clay is hard to work and cracks
- Do not exceed baking temperature: over-baked polymer clay burns and releases fumes
- Wet-sand then buff for a smooth finish: the most satisfying polymer clay surface

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
