# Air Dry Clay Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Air-dry clay: What it is (water-based clay / dries in air without firing / not as durable as fired ceramic) → Types (Das / Crayola / cold porcelain — different weight and finish) → Working time (keep covered when not working — wraps in plastic or damp cloth) → Joins (score and slip or use water to join pieces / dry joins crack) → Shrinkage (air-dry clay shrinks as it dries — small cracks are normal / fill with more clay while still damp) → Painting (prime with white gesso / paint with acrylics / seal with varnish) → Not food safe (do not use for functional food or drink items).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Keep clay covered: air-dry clay begins drying immediately — wrap when not working
- Score and water for joins: dry joins crack when the clay dries
- Gesso before painting: raw air-dry clay soaks paint unevenly

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
