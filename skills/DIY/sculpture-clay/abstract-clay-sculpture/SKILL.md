# Abstract Clay Sculpture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Abstract clay sculpture: Freeing from representation (abstract sculpture explores form, texture, space, and material — not subject) → Exploring the material (what does clay want to do? — slump / stack / fold / tear / press) → Texture vocabulary (coarse / smooth / rough / faceted / organic) → Form relationships (how do two forms sit together?) → Scale consideration (the same abstract form reads very differently at 10cm vs 1m) → Finishing (fire / paint / leave natural) → Concept-led work (start with an idea / let material and process shape the result).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Let the material lead: some of the best abstract work comes from following the clay's behaviour
- Texture contrast: surfaces that contrast in texture create visual interest
- Scale changes the reading: test your form at different scales

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
