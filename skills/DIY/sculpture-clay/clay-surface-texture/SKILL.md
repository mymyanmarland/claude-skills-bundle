# Clay Surface Texture and Pattern Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Clay surface texture: Impressed texture (stamps / rollers / found objects / lace / leaves — pressed into wet clay) → Carved texture (leather-hard clay carved with loop and ribbon tools) → Sprigging (small clay relief motifs made in a mould / applied with slip to the main piece) → Applied coils and ropes (coils of clay applied in patterns) → Combing and raking (toothed tools dragged across wet clay) → Inlay (carve lines / fill with contrasting clay / scrape back when dry) → Texture consistency (consistent texture over a whole surface reads better than random application).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Impressed texture in wet clay: leather-hard clay resists and cracks under impression
- Consistent application: random texture looks accidental — intentional coverage is more effective
- Sprigging for fine relief: the most controlled way to apply raised surface decoration
