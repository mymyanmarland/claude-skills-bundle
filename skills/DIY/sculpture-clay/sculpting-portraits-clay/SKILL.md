# Sculpting Portraits in Clay Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Portrait sculpting: Armature (aluminium wire skull shape / polymer clay over the armature) → Proportions (eyes: halfway down the head / nose: halfway between eyes and chin / mouth: one-third up from chin — same as drawing) → Working in stages (rough forms first / refine in later stages) → Eye sculpting (the iris and pupil are concave spheres / lids lay over the eyeball) → Nose (simple triangular wedge at first / refine with nostrils) → Mouth (the lips wrap around the teeth below them / philtrum above) → Skin texture (fine texturing tool / light pressing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Same proportions as drawing: the landmarks transfer directly from 2D to 3D
- Eyes are spheres under the lids: sculpt the eyeball then lay the lid over
- Work rough to refined: every stage should look complete at its own level of detail
