# Mixed Media Sculpture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mixed media sculpture: Clay with wood (fired ceramic attached to wood with adhesive or mechanical fixing) → Clay with metal (wire / found metal objects embedded before firing / or attached after) → Clay with fabric (fibre fired away leaving the clay form / or fabric attached after firing) → Found object integration (everyday objects embedded in clay / become part of the surface) → Adhesives for combining (epoxy for fired ceramic / PVA for unfired / contact adhesive for ceramics to other materials) → Concept first (what does the combination mean? / mixed media should serve the concept not merely decorate).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Concept first: mixed media that lacks intention looks like decoration not art
- Fabric burns away in the kiln: the impression remains in the fired clay — a deliberate technique
- Epoxy for post-firing joins: the strongest adhesive for ceramic to other materials

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
