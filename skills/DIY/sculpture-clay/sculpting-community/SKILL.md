# Sculpture Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sculpture community: Local sculpture classes (clay centres / community arts / art colleges) → Online sculpture courses (Proko / Gnomon / dedicated instructors) → Books (The Complete Guide to Sculpting the Human Form / Sculpture for Beginners) → YouTube (Chavant / sculpture tutorial channels) → Material suppliers (Tiranti / Miller's for UK / Sculpture House for US) → Open studios and exhibitions → Instagram sculpting community (#sculpture #clayart #ceramicsculpture) → Critique groups (feedback from other sculptors accelerates development).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Local classes: hands-on instruction from a teacher is irreplaceable for sculpture
- Proko for figure sculpting: the most comprehensive online figure sculpture resource
- Critique groups: the most accelerating learning environment for sculptors

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
