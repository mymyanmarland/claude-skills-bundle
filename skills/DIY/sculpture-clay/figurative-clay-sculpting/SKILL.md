# Figurative Clay Sculpting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Figurative sculpting: Gesture first (capture the action / rhythm before anatomy) → Blocking in the form (large masses: head / torso / pelvis / limbs — rough spheres and cylinders) → Proportion check (7-8 heads for realistic adult) → Adding muscle forms (secondary forms over the primary masses) → Surface refinement (smooth / texture / detail) → Supporting the figure during work (armature / support stands) → Reference (anatomy books / gesture drawing apps / life drawing experience translates directly to sculpting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gesture before anatomy: a figure with energy but imperfect anatomy reads better than a stiff accurate one
- Large masses first: working detail on incorrect proportions is wasted effort
- Life drawing translates directly: observational skills from drawing apply to sculpture

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
