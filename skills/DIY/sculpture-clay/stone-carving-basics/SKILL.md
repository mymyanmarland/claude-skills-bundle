# Stone Carving Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stone carving basics: Suitable stones for beginners (soapstone / alabaster / limestone — soft enough to carve by hand) → Tools (rasps / rifflers / chisels / sandpaper — begin with rasps for shaping) → Safety (always wear a dust mask / eye protection / gloves / work in a well-ventilated space or outside) → Technique (carve from the large form inward / remove material gradually / you cannot add back what you remove) → Wet carving (wet the stone — reduces dust / easier to work) → Finishing (sand through grits / oil or wax for protection and sheen).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dust mask mandatory: stone dust is a serious health hazard — wear protection always
- Large to small: remove bulk first / detail last
- Wet carving: reduces dust dramatically — work with water whenever possible

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
