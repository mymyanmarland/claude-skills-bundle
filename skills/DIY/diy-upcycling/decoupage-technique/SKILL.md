# Decoupage Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decoupage: Materials (PVA or dedicated decoupage medium / tissue paper / napkins / printed images / scissors) → Surface preparation (clean and sand lightly — decoupage needs a key to adhere) → Application (apply medium to surface / lay paper / apply medium over the top / smooth out bubbles with fingers or a soft brush) → Thin layers (tissue paper and napkins are translucent — build up layers for opacity) → Drying (each layer must dry fully before the next) → Sealing (3-5 coats of varnish or decoupage medium for durability) → Furniture application (larger images tiled or overlapped to cover a surface).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Thin papers tear when wet: wet paper is fragile — move it carefully
- Smooth bubbles before it dries: dried bubbles are very difficult to fix
- Seal thoroughly: an unsealed decoupage surface is not durable — 4+ topcoats

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
