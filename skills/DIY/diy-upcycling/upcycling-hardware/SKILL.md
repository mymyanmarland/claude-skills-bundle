# Hardware Restoration and Replacement Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hardware guide: Assessment (original hardware — keep if appropriate / replace if broken or stylistically wrong) → Cleaning original hardware (soak in white vinegar for tarnished brass / wire brush for rusty iron / polish appropriate to metal) → Spray painting hardware (removes and degreases / self-etching primer / metallic spray paint — transforms cheap hardware) → Sourcing replacements (architectural salvage for period-appropriate / online for contemporary / measure hole centres before ordering) → Fitting (pilot holes / correct screw length — too long splits thin panels).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Measure hole centres before ordering: most handles have a specific centre-to-centre dimension
- Spray paint transforms hardware: primer first or paint chips within weeks
- Period hardware from salvage: the right handle makes or breaks a period piece

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
