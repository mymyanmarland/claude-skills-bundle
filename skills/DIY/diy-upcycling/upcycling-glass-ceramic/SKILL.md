# Glass and Ceramic Upcycling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass and ceramic upcycling: Cleaning (methylated spirits removes grease — essential before painting) → Glass paint (enamel-based glass paint / some require oven-curing for durability) → Spray painting ceramics (self-etching primer / ceramic-compatible spray paint) → Frosted glass effect (spray frosting film / etching cream — follow manufacturer safety instructions carefully) → Gold leaf and gilding (adhesive size / loose leaf gold / seal over the top) → Mosaic from broken ceramics → Decoupage on ceramics (same technique as other surfaces / varnish to seal).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Methylated spirits before painting: grease prevents adhesion
- Oven-cure glass paint: non-cured glass paint peels with washing
- Etching cream safety: highly caustic — gloves and eye protection always

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
