# Fabric and Textile Upcycling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Textile upcycling: Fabric assessment (condition / fibre content / quantity) → Washing before use (upcycled fabrics must be washed — avoid surprises when the finished item is first washed) → Pattern cutting from irregular pieces (work around stains and damage) → Cushion covers (the simplest textile project — envelope back requires no zip) → Curtain-to-cushion (typically enough fabric in one curtain for 2-4 cushions) → Patchwork from small pieces → Clothing alterations (taking in / shortening / adding details) → Sewing basics (straight stitch for most upcycling / zigzag for stretch fabric).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wash before cutting: upcycled fabric may shrink — wash before making the item
- Envelope back cushions: the simplest closing method — no zip required for beginners
- Work around damage: cut patterns to avoid stains and holes

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Always follow safety guidelines when undertaking DIY projects. Wear appropriate protective equipment at all times. For structural work, consult a licensed professional. When in doubt, seek professional advice before proceeding.
