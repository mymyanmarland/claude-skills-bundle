# Upcycling Project Assessment Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Assessment process: Structural check (joints solid? / legs stable? / any woodworm or rot?) → Surface condition (veneer lifting / deep gouges / water damage) → Hardware quality (hinges and handles — replace or restore?) → Style potential (proportions attractive? / period piece worth preserving?) → Transformation options (paint / stain / reupholster / decoupage / new hardware) → Cost vs buy-new analysis (time + materials vs replacement cost) → Sustainability argument (upcycling extends product life — environmentally positive).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Structural repair before cosmetic work: a wobbly piece painted beautifully is still wobbly
- Hardware replacement transforms a piece: new handles cost little and change everything
- Period pieces: research before stripping — original patina may be valuable

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Always follow safety guidelines when undertaking DIY projects. Wear appropriate protective equipment at all times. For structural work, consult a licensed professional. When in doubt, seek professional advice before proceeding.
