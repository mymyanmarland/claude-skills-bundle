# Chalk Paint Furniture Upcycling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Chalk paint upcycling: Why chalk paint (adheres to most surfaces without sanding or priming — the key advantage) → Surface preparation (clean with sugar soap — the only prep usually needed) → Application (thick, slightly dry brush for a textured look / wet brush for smoother finish) → Two coats minimum → Distressing (optional — sand lightly on edges and raised details while paint is dry) → Waxing (clear wax seals and protects — apply thinly, buff when set / dark wax for aged effect) → Durability (waxed chalk paint is less durable than varnish — reapply wax annually on high-use pieces).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clean thoroughly before painting: chalk paint adheres to surfaces not grease
- Wax thinly: thick wax never dries properly — thin coats build to a durable finish
- Distress while dry: sanding wet chalk paint tears it — wait until fully dry
