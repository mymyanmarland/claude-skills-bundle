# Pallet Wood Projects Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pallet projects: Safe pallet selection (look for HT stamp — heat treated / avoid MB — methyl bromide treated pallets — toxic / avoid stained or chemical-smelling pallets) → Disassembly (circular saw along the blocks / pry bar and patience / reciprocating saw for nails) → Timber preparation (denail — metal detector is useful / plane or sand the face) → Suitable projects (garden furniture / shelving / wall art / raised beds) → Joining methods (screws into the blocks / standard carpentry joinery) → Finishing (treat outdoor pallets with exterior decking oil or paint).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- HT only: never use MB-marked pallets — methyl bromide is a serious health hazard
- Remove all nails before machining: a nail in a planer or saw is extremely dangerous
- Exterior finish for outdoor use: untreated pallet wood rots quickly outside

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Always follow safety guidelines when undertaking DIY projects. Wear appropriate protective equipment at all times. For structural work, consult a licensed professional. When in doubt, seek professional advice before proceeding.
