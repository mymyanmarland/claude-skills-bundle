# Selling Upcycled Items Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Selling upcycled items: Pricing formula (materials + time at fair hourly rate + 20% overhead — do not undercharge) → Photography (natural light / clean background / before and after shots tell the story) → Platforms (Facebook Marketplace for furniture / Etsy for smaller crafts / Instagram for building an audience / local markets for direct sales) → Shipping considerations (furniture: local collection only or specialist couriers / small items: standard shipping) → Descriptions (materials used / dimensions / condition notes / care instructions) → Building a reputation (consistent quality / good communication / honest descriptions).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pricing: include your time — undercharging is the most common upcycling business mistake
- Before and after photos: the most compelling upcycling marketing
- Facebook Marketplace for furniture: the most effective free platform for large items

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Always follow safety guidelines when undertaking DIY projects. Wear appropriate protective equipment at all times. For structural work, consult a licensed professional. When in doubt, seek professional advice before proceeding.
