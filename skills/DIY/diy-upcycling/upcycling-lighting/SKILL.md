# Upcycling Lighting and Lampshades Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lighting upcycling: Safety first (only work on the shade and base — do not modify electrical wiring unless qualified / use a qualified electrician for rewiring) → Lampshade recovering (remove old fabric / measure and cut new fabric / spray adhesive and wrap tightly / trim excess) → Lamp base transformation (chalk paint / spray paint / gilding / decoupage) → Drum shade fabric (measure circumference and height / add seam allowance / iron-on adhesive tape) → New shade on old base (measure fitting — European or Duplex fittings) → Energy-efficient bulbs (LED appropriate to the shade size and heat dissipation).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Electrical wiring: do not modify — use a qualified electrician for any wiring work
- Iron-on adhesive tape for drum shades: no-sew method for tight clean finish
- Fitting compatibility: measure before buying a new shade

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Always follow safety guidelines when undertaking DIY projects. Wear appropriate protective equipment at all times. For structural work, consult a licensed professional. When in doubt, seek professional advice before proceeding.
