# Colour Theory for Digital Illustration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour theory digital: HSB model (Hue/Saturation/Brightness) → Colour harmony (complementary/analogous/triadic) → Temperature and mood → Value hierarchy (value contrast carries more weight than colour) → Saturation control → Colour scripts (plan the story before painting) → Adjustment layers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value hierarchy: desaturate to greyscale — if it reads, colour will enhance it
- Colour scripts for multi-page work: plan the emotional arc through colour
- De-saturate supporting areas: guides the eye as effectively as value contrast

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
