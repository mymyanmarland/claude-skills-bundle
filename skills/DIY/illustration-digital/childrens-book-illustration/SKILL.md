# Children's Book Illustration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Children's book illustration: Character consistency (design sheets keep you on model) → Age of audience (younger: bolder simpler / older: more complexity) → Narrative illustration (extends the text — does not just repeat it) → Visual language (thought bubbles/action lines/expression emphasis) → Page turn as suspense → Dummy book before any finished art.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Design sheet for consistency: impossible to maintain across 32 pages without reference
- Image extends the text: illustration that repeats words adds nothing
- Dummy book before finished art: sequence and pacing are the picture book craft

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
