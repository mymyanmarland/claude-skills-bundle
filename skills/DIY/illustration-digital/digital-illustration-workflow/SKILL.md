# Digital Illustration Workflow Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Digital workflow: Thumbnail stage → Line art stage → Flat colour stage (base colours / no shading yet) → Shading stage (clipping mask / multiply for shadows / screen for highlights) → Detail stage → Final adjustments (colour grading/contrast/saturation) → Export (keep the layered file).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Flat colour before shading: establish the palette before adding dimension
- Clipping masks for shading: colour stays within the shape automatically
- Keep the layered file: flat exports cannot be edited

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
