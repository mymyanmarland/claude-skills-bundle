# Texture in Digital Illustration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Digital texture: Pure digital illustration can feel cold — texture adds warmth → Scanned texture overlays (scan paper / import / multiply or overlay blend mode) → Built-in textured brushes → Noise and grain adjustment layers → Texture masking → Combining textures for richness → Test print before finalising.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scanned paper overlay: the fastest route from digital to hand-made look
- Multiply blend mode: darkens and allows colour underneath to show
- Test print textured work: screen textures do not always translate to print

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
