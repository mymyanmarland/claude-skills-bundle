# Vector Illustration Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Vector illustration: Vectors (mathematical paths — scalable without quality loss) → Pen tool (clicks place anchors / drag creates curves) → Bezier curves (handles control curve direction) → Shape builder (combine and subtract) → Pathfinder (boolean operations) → Colour fills and strokes → Export (SVG/PDF/PNG).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pen tool mastery: the most important and most avoided vector skill
- Bezier handles: the direction of the handle determines curve direction
- SVG for web: scales without quality loss on any screen

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
