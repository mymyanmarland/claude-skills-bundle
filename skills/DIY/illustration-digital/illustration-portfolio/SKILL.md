# Illustration Portfolio Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Illustration portfolio: Specialisation (decide your niche) → Quantity (8-12 strong pieces / quality over quantity) → Case study format (show process) → Personal projects (fill gaps with self-directed work) → Platforms (own website/Behance/Instagram) → Approaching clients (research before approaching) → Rates (Graphic Artists Guild Handbook).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Remove weak work: judged by the weakest piece not the strongest
- Personal projects fill gaps: show the work you want to get
- Research before approaching: targeted outreach outperforms mass emails

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
