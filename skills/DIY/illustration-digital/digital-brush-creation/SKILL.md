# Digital Brush Creation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Custom brush creation: Brush anatomy (shape/grain/dynamics/properties) → Shape source (scan a physical mark / draw digitally) → Grain source (repeating texture) → Brush dynamics (pressure controls size/opacity/flow) → Testing and refining → Organising custom brushes → Importing .brushset files.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Physical mark scanning: a real brush or pencil mark becomes the brush shape
- Test on a sample canvas: save testing on a real artwork
- Name and organise: a brush library becomes useless without organisation

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
