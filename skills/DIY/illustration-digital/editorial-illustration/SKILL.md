# Editorial Illustration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Editorial illustration: Brief understanding (what is the article saying / what is the tone) → Concept development (brainstorm 10+ ideas before drawing) → Visual metaphor → Composition for the page → Rough approved before finished art → Style appropriateness → Rights and licensing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brainstorm 10 before drawing: the first idea is usually the most obvious
- Visual metaphor: abstract ideas made concrete — the mark of editorial illustration
- Small scale test: editorial illustrations are often used small — test readability

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
