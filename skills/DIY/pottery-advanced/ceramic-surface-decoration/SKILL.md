# Ceramic Surface Decoration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Surface decoration: Slip (liquid clay / applied to leather-hard greenware / coloured with oxides) → Sgraffito (scratch through a slip layer into the clay beneath / reveals contrasting clay colour) → Underglaze (colour applied to bisqueware / covered with a clear glaze / fires bright) → Wax resist decoration (wax patterns on bisqueware / glaze applied over / wax burns off in firing leaving the pattern) → Carving (leather-hard clay carved with loop tools / reveals pattern through texture) → Impressed texture (stamps / rollers / found objects pressed into wet clay) → Oxide washes (metal oxides brushed on / wiped back / highlights texture).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slip on leather-hard: wet clay cannot receive slip — timing is critical
- Underglaze covered with clear: underglaze alone has no shine — clear glaze seals and brightens
- Sgraffito depth: scratch through the slip but not too deep into the clay body

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
