# Clay Bodies and Materials Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Clay bodies: Earthenware (fires at low temperature / porous unless glazed / terracotta / warm tones / good for hand building and wheel throwing) → Stoneware (mid to high fire / vitrified and strong when fired / most versatile / grey or brown) → Porcelain (fine / translucent when thin / challenging to work / beautiful results / fires white) → Firing temperature matching (clay body and glaze must be compatible — same temperature range) → Commercial clay (pre-mixed and tested / recommended for beginners) → Recycled clay (reclaim trimmings and failed pots / wedge thoroughly before reuse).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clay and glaze must be compatible: mismatched temperatures cause crazing or crawling
- Porcelain is difficult: beautiful but less forgiving than stoneware — master stoneware first
- Recycle clay trimmings: clay is expensive — reclaim and recycle all scraps

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
