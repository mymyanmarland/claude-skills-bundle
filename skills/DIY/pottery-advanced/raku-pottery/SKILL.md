# Raku Pottery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Raku pottery: What raku is (Japanese-inspired firing method / pots removed from kiln while glowing red-hot / dramatic reduction effects) → Appropriate clay (raku clay: grog-filled / withstands the thermal shock) → Raku glazes (metallic lusters / crackle effects / unique to the reduction process) → The firing process (small outdoor kiln / fast firing to approximately 1000°C / remove with tongs) → Reduction chamber (combustible material: newspaper / wood shavings / sealed container / oxygen reduction creates metallic effects) → Safety (very high temperatures / heat-resistant gloves / tongs / appropriate footwear / fire safety) → Pit firing alternative (no kiln / fire in a pit / organic unpredictable results).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Raku clay essential: standard clay cracks from the thermal shock of removal
- Full safety equipment for raku: this is one of the most hazardous pottery techniques
- The reduction chamber creates the magic: the sealed combustion is where the surface effects happen
