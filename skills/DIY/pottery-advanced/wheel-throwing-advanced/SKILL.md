# Advanced Wheel Throwing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced wheel throwing: Centring large amounts (more clay requires more body weight over the clay) → Pulling walls taller (less pressure / more pulls / keep the base thick until the final pull) → Consistent wall thickness (pinch test: finger and thumb through the wall / feel for even thickness) → Collaring (narrow the neck by pressing inward from both sides simultaneously) → Compression of the base (compress with a rib / prevents S-cracks in drying) → Trimming (leather-hard clay / foot ring definition) → Handles (pulled handles attached at leather-hard stage).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compress the base: the most common cause of S-cracks in wheel-thrown pots
- Fewer pulls with less pressure: more pulls with light pressure builds taller walls
- Pinch test for even walls: feel the thickness at regular intervals as you pull

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
