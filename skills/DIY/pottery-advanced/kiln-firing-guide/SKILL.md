# Kiln Firing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Kiln firing: Bisque firing (first firing / converts greenware to ceramic / typically cone 06 / slow preheat removes moisture) → Glaze firing (second firing / glazes melt and bond / temperature depends on clay body) → Cone system (cone 06 is low fire / cone 6 is mid-fire / cone 10 is high fire stoneware) → Loading the kiln (pots must not touch / bisqueware can be stacked / glazeware must have space) → Raku (fast reduction firing / removed from kiln while glowing / smothered in combustibles / dramatic metallic and carbon effects) → Kiln safety (hot kilns and hot pots — appropriate protection always).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pots must not touch in glaze firing: touching causes the glaze to fuse them together
- Slow preheat for bisque: rapid heating can crack pots that still contain moisture
- Never touch a hot kiln or pot: ceramic holds heat for hours after firing
