# Making Functional Pottery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Functional pottery: Food safety (all commercial pottery glazes are food safe when fired to the correct temperature / studio glazes: research lead-free and food-safe formulations) → Microwave safety (some glazes and clays are not microwave-safe — test or research) → Dishwasher safety (many handmade pots are not dishwasher-safe — inform recipients) → Functional requirements (mug handles: comfortable to hold / bowl feet: stable on a surface / lids: well-fitted) → Consistency (making a matched set requires measuring tools and consistent technique) → User testing (make a prototype / use it for a week / adjust before making the set).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Research glaze food safety: not all studio-mixed glazes are food safe — verify
- Inform recipients about care: handmade pots may need hand washing
- Prototype before a set: a week of use reveals practical problems

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
