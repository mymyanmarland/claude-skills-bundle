# Pottery Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pottery community: Local pottery studios (classes / open studio time / firing access / the most important resource) → Ceramics courses (community colleges / art schools / dedicated pottery schools) → Books (The Potter's Complete Book of Clay and Glazes / Mastering Cone 6 Glazes) → YouTube (Simon Leach / Florian Gadsby / LiamFarrelCreations) → Clay magazines (Ceramics Monthly / Pottery Making Illustrated) → Craft fairs (sell your work and meet the community) → Instagram ceramics community (very active — share work / get feedback).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Local studio access: wheel and kiln access is the barrier — studios solve both
- Ceramics Monthly: the leading journal — technique articles and community news
- Instagram ceramics: one of the most supportive craft communities online

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools, hot equipment, or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
