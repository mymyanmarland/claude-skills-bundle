# Glazing Techniques for Pottery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glazing techniques: Bisqueware preparation (clean / dust-free / dry) → Dipping (hold the piece / dip fully / withdraw smoothly / even coating) → Pouring (pour inside first / then pour over exterior / rotate as you pour) → Brushing (multiple even coats / cross-hatch between coats) → Wax resist (wax the foot ring / protects the kiln shelf / wax patterns on the surface) → Layering glazes (some combinations create beautiful effects / sample first — some combinations are unpredictable) → Glaze thickness (too thin: bare / too thick: crawls and runs).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wax the foot ring: glaze on the foot sticks to the kiln shelf and ruins the piece
- Sample glaze combinations: layered glazes are unpredictable — test tiles before a full piece
- Glaze thickness: dip for the manufacturer's recommended time — thickness matters

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
