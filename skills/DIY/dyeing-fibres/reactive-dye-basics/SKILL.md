# Reactive Dyeing for Plant Fibres Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Reactive dyeing: Why reactive dyes (bond chemically with plant fibres: cotton/linen/bamboo/rayon) → Soda ash (the fixative — not an acid / raises the pH to bond the dye) → Process (soda ash soak / remove / dye application: immersion or direct) → Immersion dyeing (dye bath / add soda ash / stir constantly for 45-60 min / rinse thoroughly) → Colour permanence (reactive dyes are very washfast when applied correctly) → Safety (soda ash irritates skin and eyes — gloves and eye protection always).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soda ash is the fixative: without it reactive dyes wash out completely
- Stir constantly in immersion dyeing: uneven stirring causes patchy colour
- Rinse thoroughly: residual soda ash weakens fibre over time

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
