# Ice Dyeing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Ice dyeing: What it creates (unique crystalline patterns as ice melts slowly through dry dye powder) → Process (soda ash soak and dry / or wet with soda ash / scrunch fabric into a container / heap ice on top / sprinkle dry dye powder over the ice / allow to melt slowly over 8-24 hours) → Colour mixing (different powders blend as they melt / complementary colours create muddy areas — plan) → Setting (seal in plastic / leave 24 hours / rinse and wash) → Unpredictability (ice dyeing results cannot be fully controlled — the magic is in the surprise).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Soda ash first: the fixative must be in the fabric before the dye is applied
- Dry dye powder on ice: powder on wet fabric creates a different effect
- Allow slow melt: fast melting reduces the crystalline pattern

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
