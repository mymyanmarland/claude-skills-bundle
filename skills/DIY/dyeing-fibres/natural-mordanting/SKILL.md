# Natural Mordanting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Natural mordanting: Why mordant (mordant bonds the dye to the fibre — without it most natural dyes fade) → Alum (the safest and most common mordant / potassium aluminium sulphate / 15-20% of the fibre weight / dissolve in hot water / add wetted fibre / heat 45 min) → Iron mordant (saddens and deepens colour / used as a modifier after dyeing / small amounts only — excess damages fibre) → Tannin mordanting for cotton (cotton needs tannin as a pre-mordant before alum) → Mordant ordering (mordant → dye / or dye → mordant for different effects).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Alum is the starting point: the safest most reliable mordant for all natural dyeing
- Iron in small amounts: too much iron saddens the colour and damages protein fibre
- Tannin for cotton: plant fibres need tannin pre-treatment before alum mordanting

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
