# Kettle Dyeing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Kettle dyeing: What kettle dyeing creates (tonal variation within one colour — semi-solid rather than uniform solid) → Process (add dye to the pot / add wetted fibre / heat gradually / do not stir — allow the dye to strike naturally) → Colour variation (different areas of the yarn take up more or less dye depending on heat distribution) → Multiple colours (add different colours to different areas of the pot) → Exhaust the dye bath → Cool in the pot for deeper tonal variation → Exhaust indicator (water runs clear).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Do not stir: stirring creates a solid — movement creates a tonal
- Cool in the pot: longer contact time deepens the variation
- Controlled chaos: the dye distributes in unpredictable ways — embrace the result

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
