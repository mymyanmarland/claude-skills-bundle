# Hand Painting Yarn and Fibre Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hand painting: Setup (squeeze bottles / protect surface / gloves) → Yarn preparation (skeins soaked / placed on plastic wrap / or laid in a tray) → Dye application (squeeze or brush colour sections onto the yarn) → Colour placement (leave small gaps between colours — they blend at the edges during setting) → Setting (steam over boiling water for protein fibres / or wrap in plastic and microwave in bursts) → Rinsing (when cool / gentle / until water runs clear) → Hanging to dry.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leave gaps between colours: touching colours bleed into each other during setting
- Steam setting for protein fibres: microwave method works but is less even
- Cool completely before rinsing: rinsing hot wool causes felting

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
