# Colour Mixing for Dyers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour mixing for dyers: Primary dyes (use the manufacturer's stated primaries — not red/yellow/blue exactly) → Colour wheel for dyers (mixing opposites creates browns and greys) → Colour recipes (record the exact recipe by weight: grams of dye per litre of water) → Depth of shade DOS% (the ratio of dye to fibre weight — 1% DOS is light / 4% is medium / 8% is deep) → Exhaust rate (different dyes exhaust at different rates — affects the final colour) → Sampling (small samples before a large batch — always).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Record by weight: volume measurements are inaccurate for dye mixing
- DOS% for consistency: the same DOS% on different quantities gives the same colour
- Sample before the full batch: large quantities behave differently from small samples

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Dyeing involves hot liquids, chemicals, and dyes that can stain and may be harmful if misused. Always work in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves and eye protection, use dedicated equipment not used for food, keep children away from the dye area, and follow all product safety instructions. Dispose of dye chemicals responsibly in accordance with local regulations.
