# Simple Home Sewing Projects Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Beginner home sewing: Tea towels (hemmed rectangle / the fastest project) → Pillowcase (envelope style / no zip) → Simple tote bag → Hot water bottle cover → Draught excluder → Skill progression (tea towel → pillowcase → tote → cushion).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tea towel first: the simplest possible project — straight lines only
- Skill progression: each project should introduce one new technique
- Envelope pillowcase: teaches the envelope back used in cushion covers too

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Sewing machines and needles are sharp — always follow safe operating procedures. Keep pins and needles stored safely, especially if children are present. Follow all manufacturer instructions for your sewing machine.
