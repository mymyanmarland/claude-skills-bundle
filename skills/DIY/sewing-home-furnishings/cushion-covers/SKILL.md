# Sewing Cushion Covers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cushion covers: Sizing (pad + 1cm all sides for plump cover) → Envelope back (two overlapping pieces / no zip) → Zip closure → Piping (pre-made or bias-cut strips) → Applying piping (baste to front before assembly / clip curves) → Mitre corners → Contrast piping is most impactful single addition.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cushion plus 1cm: undersized covers look deflated — slightly tight is correct
- Envelope back for beginners: no zip needed — fastest construction
- Clip curves at corners: allows piping to turn without puckering

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
