# Loose Covers for Furniture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Loose covers: Dining chair drop-in seat (staple gun method) → Chair back and seat cover (pattern from the chair itself) → Draft patterns from the actual furniture → Tie closures or Velcro → Washable fabric choice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Draft patterns from the actual piece: commercial patterns rarely fit real furniture
- Washability: loose covers exist to be washed — choose appropriate fabric
- Velcro for easy removal: practical for regular washing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
