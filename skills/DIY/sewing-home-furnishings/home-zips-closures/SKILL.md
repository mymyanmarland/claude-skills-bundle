# Zips and Closures for Home Furnishings Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home furnishing closures: Cushion zip (centre back) → Exposed zip (decorative) → Velcro for loose covers (allows washing) → Heavy duty zips for large cushions and outdoor → Zip length (longer than opening for home furnishings — easier daily use).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zip longer than the opening: makes the closure easy to use daily
- Velcro for loose covers: allows removal and washing
- Upholstery zips for outdoor cushions: lighter zips corrode outdoors

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
