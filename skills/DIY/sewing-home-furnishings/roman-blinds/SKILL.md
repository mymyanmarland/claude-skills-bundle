# Roman Blinds Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Roman blinds: Face fabric / lining / dowels / rings / cord / cleat → Face and lining assembled (right sides together / stitch sides and bottom / turn) → Ring placement (every 25-30cm) → Dowel pockets → Cording → Wooden lath in bottom casing keeps hem straight.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Even ring spacing: uneven spacing causes the blind to stack unevenly
- Wooden lath in the bottom: keeps the hem straight and weighted
- Cleat for cord: safe and standard cord management system

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
