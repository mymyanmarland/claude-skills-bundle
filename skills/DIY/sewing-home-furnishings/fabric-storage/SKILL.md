# Fabric Storage and Organisation Projects Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Storage organisers: Tote bags (box bottom corner / interfaced handles) → Box bottom technique (stitch across corner at right angle for gusset) → Fabric baskets (heavyweight interfacing) → Drawer dividers → Bread bags (cotton drawstring) → Drawstring bags (two cords from opposite sides).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Box bottom for bags: transforms flat tote into useful structured bag
- Heavyweight interfacing for baskets: the structure comes from interfacing not fabric
- Two cords from opposite sides: each cord pulls the drawstring closed

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
