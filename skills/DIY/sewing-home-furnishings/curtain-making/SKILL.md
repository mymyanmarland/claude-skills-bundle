# Curtain Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Curtain making: Width (2-2.5x the track width) → Drop (track to sill or floor) → Unlined (side hems/bottom hem/heading tape) → Lining (lock stitches) → Heading tape (pin pleat/pencil pleat/eyelet) → Pattern matching (add fabric for repeat) → Weighted hem tape keeps curtains hanging straight.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fullness factor: flat curtains look cheap — minimum 2x track width
- Weighted hem tape: keeps curtains that swing outward hanging straight
- Pattern matching at seams: the most visible sewing error in curtains

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
