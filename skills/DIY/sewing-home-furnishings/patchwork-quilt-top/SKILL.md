# Patchwork Quilt Top Construction Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Patchwork quilt top: Design → Rotary cutter for cutting accuracy → Pressing (consistent direction throughout) → Consistent 6mm seam allowance → Block construction (sub-units first / then blocks / then join) → Sashing → Measure through the centre for border length.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rotary cutter accuracy: imprecise cutting creates problems that compound
- Consistent 6mm seam: every 1mm error multiplies across multiple seams
- Measure through centre for borders: quilt edges are always stretched

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
