# Coptic Stitch Bookbinding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Coptic stitch: Materials (signatures / waxed linen thread / two curved needles / bone folder / awl) → Signature preparation (fold and press 4-6 sheets per signature / 4-6 signatures per book / pierce holes with awl through a template) → Covers (heavy board / leather / paste paper — pierce to match) → Sewing sequence (first signature to back cover / kettle stitch link at each end / chain link between signatures) → The Coptic stitch pattern (link through the previous signature's corresponding hole) → Waxing thread (reduces tangling and drag through the holes) → Finishing (trim fore-edge / paste paper endpapers).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wax the thread: unwaxed thread tangles and frays through the holes
- Consistent hole spacing: a template ensures holes align across all signatures
- Coptic lays perfectly flat: its great advantage — no spine glue
