# Paste Paper Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paste paper: Paste preparation (rice starch or methylcellulose / consistency like single cream / no lumps) → Paper selection (dampened first / cartridge or Japanese tissue — smooth surface) → Colour (gouache or acrylic mixed into paste / consistent mixing important) → Application (apply paste to the paper with a wide brush or brayer) → Pattern tools (comb / finger / crumpled paper / notched card / print with found objects) → Drying (flat / press between boards when dry to prevent buckling) → Uses (book covers / endpapers / decorative paper for boxes and frames).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dampen paper before applying paste: dry paper absorbs the paste unevenly
- No lumps in the paste: strain through a sieve if needed
- Press when dry: paste paper buckles as it dries — press flat under weight
