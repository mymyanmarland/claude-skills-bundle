# Leporello and Accordion Books Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Accordion books: Basic accordion (score and fold paper into equal sections / concertina fold) → Leporello with covers (glue covers to the first and last panel) → Panoramic book (photos span across multiple panels when open / great for landscapes) → Double-sided accordion (recto and verso both used) → Nested accordion (accordion inside a case — protects the pages) → Attachment of multiple strips (join at the last panel of each section — overlap by one panel and glue) → Pop-up accordion (pop-up structures on each panel).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Equal sections: mark and score all folds before starting — uneven panels cannot be corrected
- Panoramic images: the accordion format is perfect for wide panoramic photographs
- Extend length by joining strips: plan the joins to fall on valley folds

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
