# Experimental Book Structures Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Experimental book structures: Flag book (panels that fan out / images on alternate panels create a sequence) → Tunnel book (accordion structure viewed end-on / creates depth and perspective) → Secret Belgian binding (exposed sewing on the spine / open flat / complex but beautiful) → Drum leaf binding (individual sheets attached at one edge to a spine / lies completely flat) → Star book (interlocking diagonal folded pages / opens in a star shape) → Portfolio structures (folded enclosures for loose sheets) → Concept-led design (the structure chosen should serve the content — not be arbitrary).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Structure serves content: choose the form that expresses the meaning of the work
- Star book: a striking format for exhibition — impact is immediate
- Tunnel book perspective: consider the layers as foreground mid-ground background

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
