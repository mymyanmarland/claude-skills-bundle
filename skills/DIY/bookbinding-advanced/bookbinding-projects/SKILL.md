# Bookbinding Project Library Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bookbinding projects: Beginner (pamphlet stitch notebook / accordion travel journal / simple sketchbook) → Intermediate (Coptic stitch journal / Japanese stab bound book / longstitch notebook) → Advanced (case-bound hardcover sketchbook / leather-covered journal / paste paper covered book) → Gift projects (personalised journals / recipe books / wedding guest books) → Commercial potential (handmade notebooks sell well — Etsy / craft markets / commissions) → Portfolio pieces (showcase different bindings to demonstrate skills).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pamphlet stitch for beginners: three holes and a simple sewing pattern — perfect first book
- Gift potential: personalised handmade books are among the most appreciated gifts
- Portfolio shows range: include different bindings and cover treatments

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
