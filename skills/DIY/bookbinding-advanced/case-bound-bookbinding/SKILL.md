# Case Bound Bookbinding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Case binding: Text block (signatures sewn on tapes / rounded and backed / lined with mull) → Case construction (bookboard covers / spine material / covering material — cloth / leather / paper) → Casing in (attach the text block to the case via the mull and endpapers) → Materials (PVA adhesive / mull / headbands / book cloth) → Rounding and backing (gives the book a professional curved spine) → Pressing (book press or heavy weights — essential for flat final result) → Endpapers (the visible connection between text block and cover — choose carefully).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mull connects text block to case: inadequate mull results in a cover that separates
- Rounding and backing: creates the professional shoulder that hardback books have
- Press under weight while drying: unpressed case bindings dry with bumps

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
