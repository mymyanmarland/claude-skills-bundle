# Longstitch Bookbinding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Longstitch binding: Overview (sewing directly through the cover — the stitches show on the spine as a decorative element) → Cover preparation (scored / pierced holes / soft cover card or leather) → Signature preparation (4-8 sheets folded / pierced through a template) → Sewing pattern (standard longstitch / herringbone / linked stitch variations) → Thread selection (waxed linen / contrasting colour for decorative effect) → Cover materials (leather is ideal — flexible and durable / paste paper / lokta) → No adhesive required (the sewing structure holds the book together).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Contrasting thread colour: the exposed stitching is a design feature — use it
- Leather covers ideal: flexible enough to open easily / durable for daily use
- Consistent tension throughout: loose sections of sewing look uneven on the spine

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
