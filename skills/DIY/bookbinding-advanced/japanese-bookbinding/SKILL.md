# Japanese Stab Binding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Japanese stab binding: Overview (sheets stacked — not folded into signatures / bound through holes along the spine edge) → Four-hole binding (the simplest pattern — practical and attractive) → Hemp leaf binding (more complex 8-hole pattern — traditional) → Tortoiseshell binding → Paper selection (any weight — this is the most flexible binding for mixed papers) → Cover options (boards / wrap-around soft cover / window cut for a decorative element) → Hole spacing template (measure and mark all holes before piercing) → Finishing knot (concealed between the boards on the back).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hole spacing template: uneven holes look amateurish — always use a template
- Mixed papers work in stab binding: unlike signatures — any paper can be included
- Concealed knot: the ending knot sits between boards — invisible from the outside

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
