# Book Repair Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Book repair basics: Diagnosis (loose pages / broken hinge / damaged spine / torn cover) → Re-hinging (open the book / apply thin PVA with a brush / press / dry under weight) → Loose page reattachment (narrow strip of Japanese tissue as a hinge / PVA / fold over the page edge) → Spine repair (remove loose spine material / reline with fresh mull / re-apply covering material) → Paper repair (Japanese tissue and paste — traditional conservation method) → Guiding principle (less is more — do not over-repair / reversible methods preferred for books of value).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Japanese tissue for paper repair: the conservation standard — strong but thin and reversible
- Re-hinge before the spine fails: a loose hinge repaired early is much simpler
- Reversible methods for valuable books: consult a conservator for anything of monetary or sentimental value

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
