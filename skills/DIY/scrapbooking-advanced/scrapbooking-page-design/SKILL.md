# Advanced Scrapbook Page Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Page design: Focal photo selection (choose one hero image / support with 2-3 secondaries) → Colour palette extraction (pull 3-4 colours directly from your photos) → The triangle principle (arrange photos and embellishments in a triangular flow) → Cluster embellishment (group embellishments in one area — not scattered) → Breathing space (leave white or plain areas — the eye needs rest) → Rule of odds (odd numbers of embellishments look more natural than even) → Journalling placement (integrate the writing / not as an afterthought) → Two-page spreads (one continuous design across both pages).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Extract colours from the photos: a palette that echoes the images creates unity
- Cluster embellishments: scattered elements look random — cluster creates intention
- Journalling integrated: blocked-off journalling feels separate — weave it into the design

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
