# Digital Scrapbooking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Digital scrapbooking: Software options (Photoshop / Photoshop Elements / Canva / Affinity Photo / free: GIMP) → Digital kits (coordinated digital papers / elements / fonts — available free and paid) → Resolution (300dpi for printing / 72dpi for digital sharing only) → Layer-based workflow (each element on its own layer for editability) → Blending modes (multiply / screen / overlay — create depth and texture effects) → Printing (home printing / online print services for albums) → Hybrid scrapbooking (print digital pages and add physical embellishments).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 300dpi for printing: 72dpi pages look pixelated when printed
- Layer everything: flat digital files cannot be edited — keep everything in layers
- Hybrid scrapbooking: the best of both worlds — digital base with physical texture

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
