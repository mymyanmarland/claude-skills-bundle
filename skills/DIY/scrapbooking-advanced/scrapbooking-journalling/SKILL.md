# Scrapbook Journalling and Storytelling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scrapbook journalling: The five Ws (who / what / when / where / why — the essential record) → Sensory detail (what did it smell like / feel like / sound like) → Emotions (how did you feel — not just what happened) → Quotations (speech bubbles for direct quotes / your own words in someone else's voice) → Lists (numbered lists / love-lists / things I want to remember) → Hidden journalling (folded journalling / pocket cards / journalling hidden under photos) → Font and handwriting (your handwriting is personal — do not always use printed type).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- The five Ws: who what when where why — the minimum a scrapbook page should record
- Sensory detail: takes the reader into the moment — beyond just recording facts
- Hidden journalling: allows longer personal writing without dominating the design

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
