# Scrapbooking Theme Development Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Theme development: Album themes (baby's first year / holiday / school years / family / garden / pet) → Visual theme (consistent colour palette / repeated motif / consistent style across all pages) → Photographic consistency (plan the photography with the album in mind / similar angles and lighting) → Narrative arc (beginning / middle / end — albums tell a story) → Titles and headers (consistent title style across pages — same font family and treatment) → Endings (the closing page is as important as the opening — choose a strong closing image).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Consistent colour palette across all pages: variation within a theme not between themes
- Album narrative arc: a scrapbook tells a story — plan the story before starting
- Close with a strong image: the last page is remembered

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
