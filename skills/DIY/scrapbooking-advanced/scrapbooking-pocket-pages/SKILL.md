# Pocket Page Scrapbooking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pocket page scrapbooking: System overview (pre-designed page protectors with multiple pockets / photos and cards slot in) → Card types (photo cards / journal cards / title cards / filler cards) → Digital card sources (free printables / Project Life app / Canva templates) → Speed advantages (fastest scrapbooking method — no cutting or gluing) → Photo sizing (4x6 / 3x4 / 2x2 — pre-sized or cropped) → Adding dimension (some pockets can hold 3D embellishments) → Album selection (D-ring albums allow adding and reordering pages easily).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-sized photos: crop or print to pocket size — standard prints often too large
- Mixed card sizes in one pocket page: visual variety without complexity
- D-ring albums: allow rearranging pages — more flexible than ring-bound

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
