# Photo Preservation and Archival Standards Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Photo preservation: Acid-free materials (acid migrates and yellows photos over decades — use acid-free paper, card, and adhesive) → Lignin-free (lignin degrades and causes yellowing — check products) → Photo-safe adhesives (double-sided archival tape / photo corners / never use glue sticks on original photos) → Printing considerations (laser prints are more archival than inkjet / pigment ink more stable than dye ink) → Storage (acid-free boxes / upright storage / cool dry environment / away from light) → Digital backup (every physical photo should have a digital backup).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acid-free everything: acid migration destroys photos within decades
- Photo corners: allow photos to be removed without damage
- Digital backup: physical albums are irreplaceable — back up all photos digitally
