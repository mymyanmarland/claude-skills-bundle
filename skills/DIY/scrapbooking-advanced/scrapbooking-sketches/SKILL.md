# Using Scrapbooking Sketches Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Scrapbooking sketches: What they are (simplified diagrams showing photo placement / titles / embellishment areas — not exact templates) → Sources (free sketches online / sketch books / published collections) → Adapting a sketch (mirror / rotate / change photo sizes / add or remove elements) → Sketch to page (identify photos that fit the layout / gather supplies in the palette) → Building your own sketches (sketch your favourite pages as templates for future use) → Challenge sketches (online sketch challenges — creative constraints produce better designs).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Adapt not copy: use sketches as a starting point — not a rigid template
- Sketch your best pages: your successful layouts become your best templates
- Sketch challenges: constraints overcome blank-page paralysis

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
