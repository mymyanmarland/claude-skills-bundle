# Home Maintenance Schedule Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home maintenance schedule: Spring (gutters after winter debris / check roof from ground / test smoke and CO alarms / service boiler before switch-off) → Summer (exterior paint touch-ups / driveway sealing / outdoor taps and hoses / window sills and seals) → Autumn (clean gutters before leaf fall / check loft insulation / prepare heating system / weatherstrip and draught proof) → Winter (lag pipes in unheated spaces / check loft for frost damage / service boiler / clear paths) → Monthly (test smoke alarms / check under sinks for leaks) → Annual (professional boiler service / check roof / test RCD safety switches).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Boiler service annually: the most important maintenance task — safety and efficiency
- Gutters twice yearly: spring and autumn — blocked gutters cause damp and structural damage
- Smoke alarm monthly: the test button is there — use it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
