# Fixing a Dripping Tap Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dripping tap repair: Turn off the water supply (under the sink isolating valve or at the mains) → Release pressure (open the tap until water stops) → Remove the tap head (decorative cap covers the screw) → Remove the valve body (headgear nut with spanner — note how it comes apart) → Identify the problem (old washer / O-ring / ceramic cartridge) → Tap washer (remove and replace — take old one to match) → Ceramic disc cartridge (replace the whole cartridge — note orientation) → Reassemble and test. Note: if unsure, call a plumber.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Turn off water before starting: always isolate before any plumbing work
- Take the old washer to match: washers come in many sizes
- Ceramic cartridges: replace the whole unit — do not try to resurface ceramic discs

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
