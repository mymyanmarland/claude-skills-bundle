# Fixing Sticking and Squeaking Doors Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Door fixes: Diagnose the cause (hinge sag / swelling / paint build-up / structural movement) → Hinge sag (tighten hinge screws — use longer screws if stripped / cardboard shim behind hinge) → Swollen door (plane the sticking edge — mark where it drags first) → Paint build-up (strip and repaint hinges or edges) → Squeaking hinge (petroleum jelly or hinge oil — WD-40 is a short-term fix) → Latch not catching (adjust strike plate — move it with a file or chisel) → Seasonal movement (many sticking doors are seasonal — do not over-plane in summer).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tighten hinges first: most sticking doors are a loose hinge problem
- Mark the drag: plane only where marked — not the whole edge
- Seasonal doors: do not over-plane in summer — winter contraction may make it rattle

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
