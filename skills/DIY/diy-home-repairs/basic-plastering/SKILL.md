# Basic Plastering and Skimming Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Basic plastering: Tools (float / hawk / mixing bucket / water) → Mix (consistency of peanut butter — not too wet / mix small batches for practice) → First coat (scratch coat — flick on, spread with float, cross-scratch before it sets) → Second coat (thinner, flatten with float, use arcing strokes to level) → Polishing (damp float and tight strokes as plaster stiffens — pulls the cream to the surface) → Practice essential (plastering is a skill — budget extra mix for the learning curve) → Patching existing plaster (PVA bond the area first — prevents suction pulling moisture out of the new plaster).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- PVA bonding: essential on existing plaster — dry plaster pulls moisture out too fast
- Mix in small batches: plaster sets quickly — do not mix more than you can apply
- Practice on a board: develop the feel before plastering the actual wall
