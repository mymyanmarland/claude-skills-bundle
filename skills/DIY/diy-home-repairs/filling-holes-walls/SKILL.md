# Filling Holes in Walls Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wall hole filling: Small nail holes (ready-mixed filler, finger or putty knife, overfill slightly, sand when dry) → Medium holes 10-50mm (clean edges, apply mesh patch or backing strip for support, filler in layers — do not apply too thick in one pass) → Large holes (cut to a square shape, fit a timber backing piece, board patch, skim coat, sand and prime) → Drying time (must be fully dry before sanding — white filler turns uniform colour when dry) → Primer before painting (bare filler is porous — always prime).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Apply in layers: one thick application shrinks and cracks — thin layers build up
- Prime before painting: unpainted filler absorbs paint unevenly
- Sand between coats: smooth each layer before applying the next
