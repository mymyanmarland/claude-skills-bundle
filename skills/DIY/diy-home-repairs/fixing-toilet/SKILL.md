# Fixing Common Toilet Problems Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Toilet repairs: Running toilet (water constantly running into bowl — flap valve worn or float set too high) → Flap valve replacement (turn off inlet valve, flush to empty, lift flap, unhook, replace, reconnect) → Float adjustment (adjust the float arm or ball — lower the float if water too high) → Weak flush (check water level in cistern / check flap valve seating) → Leaking base (wax ring / toilet floor bolts — more complex, may need plumber) → Leaking inlet pipe (inlet valve washer replacement or isolating valve). Note: for complex toilet problems consult a plumber.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Running toilet: wastes hundreds of litres per day — fix promptly
- Float adjustment: lower the float to stop overfilling
- Leaking base: usually a wax ring — if unsure, consult a plumber

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
