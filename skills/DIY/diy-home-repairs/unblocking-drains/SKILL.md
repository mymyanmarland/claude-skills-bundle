# Unblocking Drains Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Unblocking drains: Start without chemicals (plunger first — create a seal and pump vigorously) → Remove and clean the trap (the U-bend under the sink — bucket underneath, unscrew, clear the blockage) → Drain snake / auger (for blockages further down the pipe) → Baking soda and vinegar (pour down then follow with boiling water — good for organic build-up) → Chemical unblockers (last resort — strong alkalis or acids / follow instructions exactly / gloves and eye protection) → Prevention (hair trap / no grease down the drain).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Plunger first: the simplest and safest method — try before chemicals
- Remove the trap: the blockage is usually in the trap — accessible with a bucket
- Chemical unblockers: wear gloves and eye protection — corrosive chemicals

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
