# Window and Door Draught Proofing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Draught proofing: Diagnose draughts (candle or incense stick held near edges — smoke moves toward draughts) → Door draught excluders (brush seal for the bottom / foam or V-strip for the sides) → Window draught proofing (self-adhesive foam strip for casement windows — where sash meets frame) → Sash windows (specialist pile seals for the sashes / V-strip for parting beads) → Letterbox insulation → Keyhole covers → Loft hatch seal → Cost vs benefit (draught proofing has one of the best payback periods of any home improvement).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Diagnose first: identify every draught before buying products
- Letterbox and keyhole: small gaps with surprisingly large heat loss
- Sash windows: foam strips do not work — use pile seal or V-strip for sash

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
