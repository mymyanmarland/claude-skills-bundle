# Fixing a Leaking Pipe Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Leaking pipe repair: Isolation first (close the isolating valve or mains stopcock) → Temporary fixes (pipe repair putty / rubber repair clamp / self-amalgamating tape — temporary only) → Permanent repair options (compression fitting / push-fit fitting / solder — skill dependent) → Push-fit repair (cut out the damaged section / insert a push-fit coupler / push pipe in firmly) → Compression fitting (cut pipe squarely / slide nut and olive over pipe / insert fitting / tighten nut 1.5 turns past hand-tight) → Pipe material identification (copper / plastic / chrome — different solutions for each) → Test slowly (turn water back on slowly and check for weeping before relying on the repair).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Isolation before any pipe work: water damage from an uncontrolled leak is severe
- Push-fit couplers: the easiest pipe repair for beginners — no tools or soldering needed
- Test slowly: pressure test before relying on the repair — turn on gradually

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Plumbing Safety Disclaimer: Always turn off the water supply before starting any plumbing work. For gas pipes, boilers, or any work you are unsure about, hire a qualified plumber or gas engineer. Poor plumbing can cause flooding, structural damage, or health hazards — seek professional advice when in doubt.
