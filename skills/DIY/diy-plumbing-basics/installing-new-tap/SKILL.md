# Installing a New Tap Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Installing a tap: Isolation (close the isolating valves under the sink or at the mains) → Drain down (open the old tap to relieve pressure) → Disconnect supply pipes (flexible hoses usually / adjustable spanner or basin wrench for restricted access) → Remove old tap (backnut under the basin — basin wrench for inaccessible positions) → Fit new tap (follow manufacturer instructions / rubber seal and top hat washer on basin) → Connect supply (flexible connectors allow slight misalignment / PTFE tape on compression joints) → Test (turn on slowly / check all connections / run hot and cold to purge air).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Basin wrench: essential for removing tap backnuts in confined spaces under sinks
- Flexible connectors allow misalignment: rigid pipes must be perfectly aligned — flexible avoids this
- PTFE tape on threaded connections: 3-4 wraps clockwise — prevents weeping joints

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
