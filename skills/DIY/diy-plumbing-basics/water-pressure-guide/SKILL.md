# Water Pressure Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Water pressure guide: Normal pressure (1-3 bar for a gravity system / 1.5-4 bar for mains pressure) → Diagnosing low pressure (check one tap vs all / check neighbours / check the stopcock is fully open / check for a pressure reducing valve) → Gravity system low pressure (cold tank empty or feed restricted / tank needs a ball valve service) → Mains pressure (contact the water company if all outlets have low pressure simultaneously) → Improving pressure (mains: pressure boost pump / gravity: electric pump) → Boiler pressure (different from water pressure — check the filling loop if boiler pressure is low).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Check all outlets first: single tap low pressure is different from whole-house low pressure
- Stopcock fully open: a partially closed stopcock reduces pressure across the whole house
- Water company responsibility: mains pressure below 1 bar is a water company issue

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Plumbing Safety Disclaimer: Always turn off the water supply before starting any plumbing work. For gas pipes, boilers, or any work you are unsure about, hire a qualified plumber or gas engineer. Poor plumbing can cause flooding, structural damage, or health hazards — seek professional advice when in doubt.
