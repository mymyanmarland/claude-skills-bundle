# Cold Weather Plumbing Protection Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cold weather plumbing: Prevention (lag vulnerable pipes — foam lagging on pipes in unheated spaces / loft / garage) → Lag outdoor taps (turn off supply inside / drain the tap / foam cover on the tap) → If pipes freeze (do not use a naked flame / hot water bottle or hair dryer on low from the nearest unfrozen section working toward the frozen area) → If pipes burst (close the mains immediately / open taps to drain / assess damage before turning water on) → Going away in winter (leave heating on low / turn off water if very cold period expected).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lag before the cold arrives: fitting lagging during a freeze is too late
- Never use a naked flame on frozen pipes: explodes and causes fire
- Going away: turn off and drain if extended cold period — most burst pipes happen in empty properties

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Plumbing Safety Disclaimer: Always turn off the water supply before starting any plumbing work. For gas pipes, boilers, or any work you are unsure about, hire a qualified plumber or gas engineer. Poor plumbing can cause flooding, structural damage, or health hazards — seek professional advice when in doubt.
