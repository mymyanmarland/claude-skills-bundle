# Toilet Cistern Repair Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cistern repair: Turn off the water (isolation valve on the supply pipe) → Flush to empty the cistern → Common problems: running toilet (flapper valve / siphon worn — replace like-for-like) → Slow fill (float valve limescale — clean or replace the float valve) → Not flushing properly (check flush volume — adjust if adjustable / replace siphon) → Leaking from cistern to pan (siphon seal — siphon replacement) → Universal replacement parts (many components are now universal fit — take the old part to the merchant) → Reassemble and test (fill rate / flush volume / no leaks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Take the old part to the merchant: cistern internals vary — exact match prevents return trips
- Universal fit components: modern replacements fit most cisterns — check before buying bespoke
- Slow fill is often limescale: cleaning or replacing the float valve solves most slow-fill problems

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
