# Radiator Bleeding and Balancing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Radiator bleeding and balancing: Bleeding (turn off heating / let cool / radiator key on bleed valve / open slowly until water dribbles / close immediately / repressurise boiler if pressure drops) → When to bleed (cold spots at the top of radiators / gurgling sounds) → Balancing (if some radiators heat faster than others — adjust lockshield valves) → Balancing process (open all lockshield valves fully / turn on heating / identify first and last to heat up / restrict first-to-heat radiators gradually until system heats evenly) → Power flushing (if multiple cold radiators — indicates sludge / professional power flush recommended).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Repressurise boiler after bleeding: check pressure gauge — should be 1-1.5 bar cold
- Balancing: patience required — small adjustments and waiting for the system to respond
- Power flush for sludge: multiple cold radiators despite bleeding indicates professional power flush

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
