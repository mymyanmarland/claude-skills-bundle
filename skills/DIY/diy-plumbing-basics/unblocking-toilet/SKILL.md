# Unblocking a Toilet Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Toilet unblocking: Do not flush repeatedly (overflows the bowl — stop after first failed flush) → Toilet plunger (cup or accordion plunger / create a seal / pump vigorously 10-15 times / release) → Hot water and washing-up liquid (pour hot but not boiling water with a squirt of washing-up liquid — softens organic blockages) → Drain snake or auger (for deeper blockages / push and rotate into the U-bend) → Chemical unblockers (enzyme-based preferred over caustic for regular toilet use) → What not to flush (wipes even labelled flushable / cotton wool / dental floss — common causes of blockage) → Call a plumber (if none of the above works — deeper blockage possible).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Do not keep flushing: overflow cleaning is much worse than the original blockage
- Toilet plunger: different from a sink plunger — the flange cup seals better in a toilet pan
- What not to flush: prevention is far easier than unblocking

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
