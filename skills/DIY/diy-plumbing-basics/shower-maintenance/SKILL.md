# Shower Maintenance and Descaling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shower maintenance: Descaling showerhead (soak in white vinegar overnight / rinse / repeat for heavy scale) → Cleaning rubber nozzles (pinch or rub to dislodge scale) → Flow restrictor check (some showerheads have a restrictor — remove to improve flow) → Thermostat cartridge (for thermostatic showers with inconsistent temperature — cartridge replacement) → Shower hose replacement (check for kinks and leaks / standard BSPT thread in most UK showers / PTFE tape on threads) → Shower tray and seal (reseal with silicone if leaking from the tray edges) → Electric shower servicing (professional only — mains electricity and water).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Overnight vinegar soak: the most effective descaling method for mineral deposits
- Flow restrictor removal: often responsible for disappointing shower pressure
- Electric shower: any electrical fault in an electric shower requires a qualified person

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
