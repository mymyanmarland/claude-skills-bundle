# Silicone Sealing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Silicone sealing: Preparation (remove old silicone completely — score with a blade / peel / residue with silicone remover) → Surface dry and clean (silicone does not adhere to damp or contaminated surfaces — use methylated spirits) → Tape masking (apply masking tape either side of the joint for a clean line — press firmly) → Cutting the nozzle (45 degrees / small hole for fine beads) → Application (steady continuous bead / do not stop mid-joint) → Smoothing (wet finger or smoothing tool / one continuous stroke before the silicone skins) → Remove tape (while silicone is still wet / pull back at 45 degrees) → Cure time (24 hours minimum before exposing to water).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Remove tape while wet: tape removed after curing tears the edge of the bead
- Surface must be dry: silicone on damp surfaces fails within months
- One continuous smoothing stroke: stopping mid-joint leaves a mark
