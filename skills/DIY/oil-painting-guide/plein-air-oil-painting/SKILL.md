# Plein Air Oil Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Plein air painting: Small portable kit (pochade box / limited palette / small panels) → Work small (30cm maximum to start) → Value over colour → 20-minute alla prima → Decide the light at the start — do not chase it → Thumbnail before setting up → Plein air as study not always a finished painting.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Work small: a 20x25cm panel forces economy of decision
- Decide the light at the start: chasing moving light produces inconsistent paintings
- Plein air as study: outdoor paintings feed studio work

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
