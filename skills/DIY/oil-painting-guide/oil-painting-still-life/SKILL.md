# Oil Painting Still Life Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Oil still life: Setup (3-5 objects / height variation / one light source) → Background (neutral mid-tone not white) → Thumbnail composition → Block-in (lean burnt sienna wash) → Work lean to fat → Surface variety develops rendering skills → Include reflections between objects.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Background first: white canvas distorts colour judgement
- Thumbnail before setup: test composition before spending time painting
- Include reflections: reflected light between objects unifies the scene

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
