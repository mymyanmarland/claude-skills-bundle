# Oil Painting Landscape Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Oil landscape: Sky first (establishes the light key) → Aerial perspective (distance: cooler/lighter/less saturated) → Three zones (sky/middle distance/foreground) → Clouds (paint sky colour first / warm grey shadows / cooler light) → Trees (masses not individual leaves) → Water (reflects the sky) → Keep horizon level.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sky first: everything else mixed relative to the sky
- Aerial perspective: distance is cooler lighter and less saturated — creates depth
- Trees as masses: individual leaves always look wrong at scale

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
