# Colour Mixing in Oil Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour mixing oils: Mud prevention (mix similar temperature colours) → Work from the larger quantity → Temperature (warm light / cool shadow) → Neutralising with complementary (not adding grey) → Mixing darks (ultramarine + burnt sienna — not black alone) → Mix the light first / compare and mix the shadow → Organised palette.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Warm with warm cool with cool: the foundational mud-prevention rule
- Ultramarine plus burnt sienna for darks: richer than black
- Mix the light first then the shadow: value comparison becomes easier
