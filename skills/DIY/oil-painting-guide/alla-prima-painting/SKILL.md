# Alla Prima Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Alla prima: All at once — wet into wet in one session → Mix plenty of paint → Work background to foreground / dark to light → Blending while wet → Lean first even in alla prima → Work quickly → Stop before overworking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mix plenty of paint: running out causes inconsistency
- Dark to light: lights go on last and stay cleaner
- Stop before overworking: freshness lost with repeated passages

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
