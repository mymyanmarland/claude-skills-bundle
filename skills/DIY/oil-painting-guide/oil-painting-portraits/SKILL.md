# Oil Painting Portraits Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Oil portrait painting: Composition → Block-in (lean wash of major shadow shapes) → Value structure (3-4 zones) → Mixing flesh (yellow ochre/white/cadmium red/touch of blue) → Edge control (hard edges forward / soft edges shadow) → Eyes last (over-detailed eyes are the most common portrait mistake) → Hair as a shape.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shadow shapes first: the block-in establishes the structure
- Flesh mixing: yellow ochre white cadmium red as the base
- Hair as a shape: individual hairs in the lights / shadow as a single tone

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
