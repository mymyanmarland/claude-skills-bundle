# Oil Painting Materials Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Oil painting materials: Artist vs student quality → Starter palette (titanium white/yellow ochre/burnt sienna/ultramarine blue/viridian/burnt umber) → Mediums (linseed oil/liquin/stand oil) → Brushes (hog bristle/sable/fan) → Supports (linen/cotton canvas/board) → Solvents (odourless mineral spirits) → Glass or stay-wet palette.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Artist quality paint: more pigment means less paint needed — not more expensive long-term
- Starter palette of 6: learn to mix before expanding
- Odourless mineral spirits: standard turpentine has strong fumes

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
