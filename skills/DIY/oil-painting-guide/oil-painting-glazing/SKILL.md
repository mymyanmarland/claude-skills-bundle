# Glazing in Oil Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glazing: Thin transparent layer over dry underpainting → Luminosity impossible with opaque colour alone → Glaze medium (linseed/liquin) → Apply with soft brush / spread evenly → Multiple thin glazes (each must dry before next) → Translucent pigments only (alizarin crimson/phthalocyanine/viridian) → Glaze vs scumble (dark over light vs light over dark).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Translucent pigments only: opaque pigments block the underpainting
- Each glaze fully dry: wet on wet creates mud not depth
- Scumble vs glaze: both create depth — in opposite directions
