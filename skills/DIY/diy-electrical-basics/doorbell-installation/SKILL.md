# Wired and Wireless Doorbell Installation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Doorbell installation: Wireless doorbell (battery-powered push / plug-in chime — no wiring / straightforward DIY) → Smart video doorbell (wireless version: as above / wired version: low-voltage existing doorbell wiring or new low-voltage cable) → Wired doorbell (transformer in airing cupboard or consumer unit area — low voltage cable to push and chime) → Transformer location (small transformer reduces mains to 8-12V — low voltage side is safe to handle) → Drilling and cable routing → Positioning (eye level / sheltered from rain).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wireless is the simplest option: no wiring, no regulations, fully DIY
- Smart doorbell wired version: uses existing low-voltage doorbell cable if present
- Transformer secondary side is low voltage: safe to handle once transformer is installed

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
