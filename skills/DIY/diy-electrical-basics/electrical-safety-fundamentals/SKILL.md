# Electrical Safety Fundamentals Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Electrical safety: Consumer unit (locate yours before any electrical work / identify each circuit) → Isolation (switch off the circuit breaker / lock off if possible / test with a non-contact voltage tester) → Test before touch (non-contact voltage tester on every cable before handling — never assume) → Regulations (Part P in England and Wales — some electrical work is notifiable and requires a qualified electrician or inspection) → What DIY is and is not permitted (replacing like-for-like fittings / adding sockets with an RCBO in a garage / full rewires and consumer unit work — professional only) → RCD protection (residual current device — the life-saving device in your consumer unit).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Test before touch: always verify a circuit is dead with a voltage tester — do not assume
- Know your local regulations: Part P and equivalent regulations — some work requires notification
- Consumer unit work is professional only: never DIY a consumer unit or main fuse

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Electrical Safety Disclaimer: Electrical work carries serious risk of injury or death if performed incorrectly. Always isolate the power supply and verify it is dead before working. Consult or hire a licensed electrician for any work beyond the most basic tasks. In many jurisdictions certain electrical work must be performed or certified by a licensed electrician — check your local regulations.
