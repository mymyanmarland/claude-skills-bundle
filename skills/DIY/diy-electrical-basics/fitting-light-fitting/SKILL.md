# Fitting a Ceiling Light Fitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fitting a light: Isolation (switch off the lighting circuit / test dead) → Remove existing fitting (unscrew rose or fitting / note wire connections) → Junction box vs loop wiring (older homes may have junction boxes / modern homes use loop-in roses — different wiring arrangements) → Pendant replacement (loop-in rose: connect to the same terminals as before / use the photograph) → Heavier fittings (require a ceiling hook or fitting backplate into a joist — not just plasterboard) → Maximum wattage (follow the fitting's specification — exceed it and the fitting overheats).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Support for heavy fittings: plasterboard alone cannot support a chandelier — fix to a joist
- Maximum wattage compliance: overfitting causes heat buildup and fire risk
- Loop-in vs junction box: different systems — identify yours before purchasing a replacement

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
