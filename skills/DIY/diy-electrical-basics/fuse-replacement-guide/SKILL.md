# Fuse Replacement Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fuse replacement: Plug fuses (13A for most appliances over 700W / 3A for lamps and small appliances under 700W — use the correct value) → Cartridge fuses in older consumer units (identify the blown fuse — no continuity on a meter / replace with identical rating only) → Modern circuit breakers (press to reset after fault is corrected — if it trips again there is a fault — do not tape it up) → Spur fused connection units (the fuse in a fused spur — replace like a plug fuse) → Never overrate (a larger fuse defeats the protection — replace like-for-like only) → If repeatedly blowing (investigate the cause — do not just keep replacing).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Never overrate a fuse: a larger fuse removes the protection — it does not fix the problem
- Correct fuse value: 3A for under 700W appliances / 13A for over 700W
- Repeatedly tripping: investigate the fault — do not reset without understanding why
