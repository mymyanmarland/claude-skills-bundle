# Replacing a Socket Outlet Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Replacing a socket: Isolation (switch off circuit / test dead with voltage tester) → Remove old socket (unscrew faceplate / pull gently — cable length is limited) → Photograph wiring → Single socket wiring (L/N/E — brown/blue/green-yellow) → Double socket or spur (may have two sets of wires — L/N/E for each) → Terminal connections (L to L / N to N / E to E — check tightness / each wire secure) → Backbox check (metal box: earth the box / plastic box: no earthing of box required) → Test with a socket tester after restoring power.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Test dead before touching: non-contact tester on every cable
- Socket tester after completion: cheap device verifies correct wiring
- Metal backbox earth: metal boxes must have an earth connection

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

📌 Electrical Safety Disclaimer: Electrical work carries serious risk of injury or death if performed incorrectly. Always isolate the power supply and verify it is dead before working. Consult or hire a licensed electrician for any work beyond the most basic tasks.
