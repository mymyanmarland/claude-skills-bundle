# Flat-Pack Furniture Assembly Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Flat-pack assembly: Preparation (read the instructions before opening bags / lay out all pieces and hardware / check parts list) → Sequence (follow the numbered steps — assembling out of sequence causes having to disassemble) → Cam locks and dowels (dowels align / cam locks fix — tighten fully) → Do not overtighten (cam locks and thread-in hardware strip easily — firm hand tightening only) → Back panel (adds rigidity — do not skip it / ensure square before fixing) → Level and adjust (adjustable feet / shim if on an uneven floor) → Wall fixing (supplied wall brackets — use them, especially in children's rooms).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Follow the sequence: skipping steps means disassembling later
- Cam locks: firm hand tight only — overtightening strips the fitting
- Wall bracket: essential in children's rooms — tip-overs kill children

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
