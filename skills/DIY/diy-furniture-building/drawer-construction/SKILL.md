# Drawer Construction and Fitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Drawer construction: Sizing (drawer width = opening minus slide clearance / height = whatever fits) → Materials (solid timber for hand-cut / 12mm plywood for manufactured) → Construction (sides / front and back / plywood bottom in groove) → Dovetail joints (traditional — beautiful but advanced) → Simple butt or rebate construction (faster — reinforce with glue and nails) → Slide selection (side-mount cheap and easy / undermount concealed and quality) → Fitting (adjust clearance / soft-close mechanisms).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Drawer sizing: check the slide manufacturer's clearance requirements
- Solid timber sides: more durable than MDF for drawers under regular use
- Undermount slides: the best quality drawer experience — worth the extra cost

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
