# Build a Simple Workbench Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Workbench build: Design (height: elbow height minus 50mm for bench work / depth: 600mm comfortable) → Frame (4x4 or 100x100mm legs / 2x4 rails and stretchers) → Joints (mortise and tenon for maximum strength / or heavy structural screws with bracing) → Top (two layers of 18mm OSB or MDF / glued and screwed) → Vise (face vise is the most useful — mount at the left end for right-handers) → Tool tray at the back (keeps tools from falling off) → Shelf below (storage and additional stiffness).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Height critical: wrong height causes back pain on every project
- Top in layers: two thinner layers glued together are stiffer than one thick layer
- Vise position: face vise at the left end for right-handers allows long board work

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
