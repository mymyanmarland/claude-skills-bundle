# Simple Bookcase Build Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Simple bookcase: Materials (18mm plywood or MDF for strength / pine boards for lighter loads) → Cut list (two sides / top and bottom / back panel / shelves) → Construction sequence (sides first / join top and bottom / then shelves / back panel squares the unit) → Shelf spacing (measure what it will hold / adjustable shelving allows flexibility) → Back panel (6mm ply / MDF / or timber back / essential for rigidity — a bookcase without a back racks) → Finishing (sand / paint or varnish / fill screw holes) → Wall fixing (secure to wall — tall bookcases are a tip hazard).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Back panel is essential: a bookcase without a back is unstable
- Wall fix: bookcases over 1.2m tall must be fixed to the wall — tip hazard, especially for children
- Adjustable shelf holes: drill a row of 5mm holes at 32mm centres before assembly
