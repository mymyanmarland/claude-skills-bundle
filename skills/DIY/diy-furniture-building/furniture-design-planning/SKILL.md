# Furniture Design and Planning Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Furniture design: Purpose and dimensions (what will it hold / where will it go / clearances for doors and drawers) → Sketch and dimension (hand sketch with all dimensions labelled — not to scale is fine for planning) → Cut list (every piece of timber or sheet: length / width / thickness / quantity) → Materials calculation (cut list + 10-15% waste) → Joint selection (butt/pocket screw/dowel/mortise and tenon — match complexity to skill level) → Test with cardboard (mock up complex shapes before cutting expensive material).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cut list first: every piece listed before buying anything
- 10-15% waste allowance: grain matching and errors require extra material
- Cardboard mockup: for drawers and cabinet doors — verify dimensions before cutting

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
