# Fitted Furniture Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fitted furniture: Measure the space (measure in three places — rooms are rarely square / use the smallest dimension) → Design (sketch with dimensions / mark the walls and floor) → Framework (build in situ — not on the bench / fix framework to walls, floor, and ceiling) → Scribing (cut timber or MDF to follow the wall contour — uses a compass to trace the wall profile onto the panel) → Doors and face frame → Finishing (paint before fitting doors and drawers — easier) → Services (ensure access to any pipes or cables before closing them in).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scribe to walls: pre-built units never fit perfectly — scribe panels to follow the contour
- Measure three times: rooms are not square — use the smallest dimension
- Paint before fitting doors: much easier than painting around installed hardware

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
