# Upcycling Old Furniture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Upcycling furniture: Assessment (solid joints / missing pieces / surface only or structural repair needed) → Stripping (chemical stripper or heat gun for old paint / avoid heat guns near veneer) → Repair (re-glue joints with PVA / clamp while curing / fill holes and chips with wood filler) → Surface preparation (sand with the grain / 80-120-180 progression for bare wood) → Chalk paint (minimal prep needed — adheres to most surfaces without sanding) → Waxing chalk paint (finishing layer — protects the paint) → Restoring character (preserve original features — over-sanding destroys patina).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Chalk paint: the easiest furniture transformation — minimal prep
- Re-glue before painting: a structurally wobbly piece is not improved by paint
- Preserve original features: patina and wear can be beautiful — not everything needs sanding smooth

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
