# Cabinet Making Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cabinet making: Box construction (sides / top and bottom / back / face frame or frameless) → Sheet materials (18mm plywood or MDF for carcase / veneered sheet for visible surfaces) → Joinery (pocket screws or biscuits for carcase / avoid butt joints on cabinets — too weak for the loads) → Squaring (diagonal measurement equal = square / brace and clamp until adhesive sets) → Door fitting (overlay or inset — different hinge types) → Drawer slides (full extension is worth the extra cost — undermount for best quality) → Finishing (veneer edging tape on exposed sheet edges).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Square before the glue sets: diagonal check every box
- Edging tape on all exposed sheet edges: raw MDF edges look unprofessional
- Pocket screws for carcase: fast, strong, and accurate for box construction
