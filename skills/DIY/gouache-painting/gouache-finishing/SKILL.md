# Finishing and Protecting Gouache Paintings Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Finishing gouache: Gouache is vulnerable to water and abrasion when dry → Fixative spray (light coats from 30cm) → Framing (behind glass — most reliable long-term protection) → Mounting (archival mat board / float mount) → Storage (flat / acid-free tissue between paintings) → Scan before fixing (colour may shift slightly).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Frame behind glass: the most reliable long-term protection
- Test fixative on practice painting: can alter the surface slightly
- Scan before fixing: colour may shift — record it first

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
