# Gouache Opacity and Consistency Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Opacity and consistency: Thick (full opacity / chalky) → Diluted (transparent / like watercolour) → Milk consistency (most useful — flows but remains opaque) → Avoid cracking (do not apply too thickly especially white) → Test on scrap paper → Traditional gouache can be reactivated with water.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Milk consistency: the most versatile gouache consistency
- Thick white cracks: thick gouache especially white cracks when dry
- Test on scrap paper: first brush load is always different from the palette
