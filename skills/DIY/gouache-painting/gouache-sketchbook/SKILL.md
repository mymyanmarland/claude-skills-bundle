# Gouache in a Sketchbook Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gouache sketchbook: Watercolour sketchbook minimum 200gsm → Gesso priming for different surface → Postcard-size studies (fast / develop intuition) → Colour notes from life → Combining with other media → Date and reference notes → 15 minutes daily beats 3 hours weekly.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Watercolour sketchbook: regular paper buckles immediately with gouache
- Small scale studies: postcard size develops intuition faster
- 15 minutes daily: consistency is the key to rapid improvement
