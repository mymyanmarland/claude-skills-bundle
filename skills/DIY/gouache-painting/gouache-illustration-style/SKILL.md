# Gouache for Illustration Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gouache illustration: Mid-century illustration heritage → Flat graphic approach (limited palette / clear shapes / controlled edges) → Naturalistic approach (looser / more tonal / painterly) → Gouache and watercolour combined → Ink line with gouache (classic illustration combination) → Digitising (scan at 600dpi / adjust levels) → Reproducibility (gouache scans and prints beautifully).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gouache scans beautifully: the flat opaque colour holds well in reproduction
- Watercolour and gouache combined: complementary — use both in one painting
- Ink line with gouache: the classic illustration combination

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
