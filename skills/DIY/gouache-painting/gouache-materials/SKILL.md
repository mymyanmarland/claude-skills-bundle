# Gouache Materials Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gouache materials: Opaque water-based paint → Traditional (re-wettable) vs acrylic gouache (dries permanent) → Artist quality (Holbein/Turner/Winsor Newton Designer) → Paper (watercolour 300gsm minimum / hot press for smooth / cold press for texture) → Brushes (soft synthetic) → Stay-wet palette. Starter palette: titanium white / ivory black / yellow ochre / cadmium red / ultramarine / viridian.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stay-wet palette: gouache dries fast on a standard palette
- 300gsm minimum paper: lighter buckles with water-heavy gouache
- Artist quality: student gouache lacks opacity — the defining quality

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
