## Step 2 — Core Output

Flat colour technique: Mix enough paint before starting → Wet brush / work quickly / pull from wet areas → Work wet into wet (finish before edge dries) → Tape masking for crisp edges → Frisket film for complex shapes → The graphic style is the trademark of gouache illustration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mix enough paint: remixing mid-area never matches perfectly
- Maintain a wet edge: tide marks form where wet meets dry
- Masking tape for crisp edges: the foundation of graphic gouache style
