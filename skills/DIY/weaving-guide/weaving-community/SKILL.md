# Weaving Community and Learning Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Weaving community: Handweavers Guild of America / local guilds → Ravelry weaving groups → The Woolery / Gist Yarn (resources and yarn) → Books (The Weaver's Idea Book / Colour and Weave / Interlacements) → YouTube (GIST Yarn / weaving tutorials) → Workshops (weekend workshops at weaving studios) → Pattern databases (Handweaving.net — free pattern archive) → Loom reviews and community advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Local guild: the fastest way to access looms and expertise without buying everything
- Handweaving.net: free archive of thousands of weaving drafts and patterns
- Weekend workshops: hands-on time with different looms before purchasing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools, hot equipment, or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
