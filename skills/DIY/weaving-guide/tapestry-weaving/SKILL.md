# Tapestry Weaving Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tapestry weaving: Cartoon (the design drawn or traced onto the warp as a guide) → Colour joining techniques (interlocking wefts / shared warp / slit tapestry) → Hatching (diagonal blending of two colours) → Eccentric wefts (curved weft lines for organic shapes) → Cartoon transfer (draw on paper / tape behind warp / trace with marker) → Building in sections (work different colour areas simultaneously) → Finishing (hemstitch / pull warp ends through / wet blocking).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Interlocking vs slit: slits create defined edges / interlocking creates a softer join
- Work sections simultaneously: lets you check colour balance across the whole tapestry
- Wet blocking tapestry: sets the shape and evens the tension

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
