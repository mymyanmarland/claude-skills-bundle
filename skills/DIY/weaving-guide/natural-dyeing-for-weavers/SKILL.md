# Natural Dyeing for Weavers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Natural dyeing for weavers: Mordanting (mordant fixes the dye to the fibre — alum is the safest and most common) → Plant dye sources (onion skins: yellow-gold / weld: clear yellow / madder: red / woad or indigo: blue) → Dyeing process (wet fibre / mordant bath / dye bath / rinse gently) → Colour modifiers (iron saddens / copper greens / acid brightens) → Colour planning for weaving (dye samples first / build a colour story) → Safety (some natural dyes use toxic mordants — research before using chrome or tin).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mordant first: unmordanted fibre takes little dye and the colour fades rapidly
- Sample before committing: natural dye colours vary by batch — sample first
- Research mordant safety: alum is safe / chrome and tin are toxic — avoid them

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
