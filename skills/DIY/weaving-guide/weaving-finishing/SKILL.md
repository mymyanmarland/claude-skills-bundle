# Weaving Finishing Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Weaving finishing: Hemstitch (lock the weave at both ends before cutting from the loom — prevents unravelling) → Removing from the loom (cut close to the hemstitch / leave fringe length if desired) → Twisting fringe (twist two groups together / tie an overhand knot) → Knotted fringe patterns → Wet finishing (hand wash gently / roll in a towel / lay flat to dry) → Pressing (press woven cloth while slightly damp — transforms it) → Shrinkage (wool shrinks significantly — account for this in calculations).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hemstitch before cutting: removing from the loom without hemstitch unravels the weave
- Wet finishing transforms cloth: dry cloth off the loom feels nothing like the finished fabric
- Account for shrinkage: wool can shrink 10-20% — sample to determine actual shrinkage
