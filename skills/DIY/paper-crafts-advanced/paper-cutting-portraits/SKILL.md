# Silhouette and Portrait Papercutting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Silhouette papercutting: Tracing (project a profile shadow onto paper / or print from a photograph) → Simplification (reduce the profile to clean lines — remove hair detail for a classic silhouette) → Cutting technique (small sharp scissors for curves / scalpel for detail) → Framing (mount onto contrasting colour / oval or rectangular mat) → Detailed portrait cutting (more complex — cut facial features / hair detail / clothing) → Symmetrical silhouettes (fold paper / cut one half / unfold for a perfect mirror image) → Negative space cutting (cut out the figure / the hole is the artwork).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Profile simplification: the classic silhouette style removes distracting detail
- Sharp scissors for curves: scalpels struggle with curves — use small curved scissors
- Negative space cutting: the absence of paper becomes the artwork — a different mindset

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
