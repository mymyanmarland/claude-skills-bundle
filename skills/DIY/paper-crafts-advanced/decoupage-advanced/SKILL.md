# Advanced Decoupage Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced decoupage: Napkin technique (separate the layers of a printed napkin — use only the top printed layer) → Glass decoupage (apply to the inside of a glass bowl — reverse decoupage / image faces outward through the glass) → 3D decoupage (cut the same image from multiple copies / layer with foam pads to create depth) → Shabby chic finish (crinkle the paper before applying / sand the dried surface) → Image transfer (gel medium transfer onto wood or fabric) → Sealing (epoxy resin for a completely flat surface — professional result).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Separate napkin layers: using all layers makes the print look thick and heavy
- Reverse decoupage on glass: apply image face-down inside the glass so it shows through
- 3D decoupage: the same image cut multiple times — 3-5 layers for good depth

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
