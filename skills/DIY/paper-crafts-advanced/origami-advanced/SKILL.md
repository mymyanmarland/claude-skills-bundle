# Advanced Origami Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced origami: Crimp and sink folds (inside reverse sink fold — collapses a point inward) → Wet folding (dampen with a fine mist / allows curves and sculptural shapes / dry in position under weight) → Modular origami (individual units / assembled into geometric solids — sonobe unit / kusudama) → Diagramming conventions (symbols for mountain / valley / squash / petal folds) → Paper selection (washi / tissue foil / tant — different properties for different models) → Design (create original models — start by modifying existing models before designing from scratch).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wet folding allows curves: impossible with dry paper — opens up sculptural forms
- Modular: all units must be identical — precise folding is essential
- Paper selection affects the model: tissue foil for complex insects / tant for geometric models

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
