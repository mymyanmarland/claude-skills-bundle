# Paper Sculpture and 3D Paper Art Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper sculpture: Paper types (cartridge paper / watercolour paper / cover stock — heavier for structural pieces) → Scoring (bone folder on the back of the paper for mountain folds / on the front for valley folds) → Curling (roll over a pencil or skewer / curl against a curved surface) → Armature (wire skeleton for larger sculptures / balsa wood supports) → Assembly (PVA for large areas / double-sided tape for quick assembly / bone folder to press seams) → Surface interest (scoring parallel lines creates a textured surface / cut fringe for petals and texture) → Finishing (paints / inks / metallic leaf on paper sculptures).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Score on the correct side: scoring on the wrong side creates the wrong fold direction
- Armature for large pieces: paper alone cannot support sculptures over 30cm without support
- Bone folder for clean scores: fingernail scoring leaves a rough edge

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
