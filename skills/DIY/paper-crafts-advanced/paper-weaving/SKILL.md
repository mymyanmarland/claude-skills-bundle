# Paper Weaving and Interlacing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper weaving: Strip preparation (cut precise widths with a ruler and craft knife / consistent width is essential) → Basic plain weave (warp strips vertical / weft strips woven over-under alternating) → Twill weave (over 2 under 2 / creates a diagonal pattern) → Colour patterns (plan the colour sequence before starting) → Finishing edges (fold and glue or tape the last weft strips / trim ends flush) → Diagonal weaving (warp at 45 degrees / creates a diamond pattern) → Paper basket construction (stiffer card strips / weave a flat base then bend the uprights to form sides).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Consistent strip width: uneven strips create uneven weave
- Plan colour sequence: random colour choices rarely achieve the intended pattern
- Stiffer card for baskets: regular paper strips are too flexible to hold a basket shape
