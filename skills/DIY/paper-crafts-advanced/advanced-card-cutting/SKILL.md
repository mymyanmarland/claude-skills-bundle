# Advanced Paper Cutting and Kirigami Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced papercutting: Tools (sharp scalpel or craft knife / self-healing cutting mat / light box for tracing) → Design principles (continuous cuts — the whole design must remain connected) → Blade maintenance (change the blade every 30-60 minutes of cutting — dull blades tear) → Layered papercuts (multiple layers of different colours assembled) → Kirigami pop-ups (cuts and folds combined to create 3D structures from flat sheets) → Symmetrical cutting (fold paper before cutting for perfect symmetry) → Framing (float mount to show shadows — the shadow is part of the art).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Connectivity: every element must connect to the rest — plan before cutting
- Change blades frequently: dull blades tear the paper instead of cutting
- Float mounting: the shadow depth created by float mounting is part of the finished effect

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
