# Advanced Paper Marbling Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper marbling: Materials (carrageenan size / ox gall dispersant / marbling paints) → Size preparation (carrageenan dissolved in water / rest 12-24 hours to degas / right consistency: paint drops should float and spread to 5cm) → Alum-treating paper (alum solution brushed on / dry before using — the paper must be treated to accept the colour) → Colour application (pipette or brush / ox gall determines spread) → Pattern making (comb / stylus / feather — draw through the floating paint) → Laying paper (lower onto the surface smoothly / lift and rinse immediately) → Drying flat (press between blotting paper if curling).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Degas the size: bubbles in the size cause holes in the pattern
- Alum treatment is essential: untreated paper will not accept the colour
- Lay the paper smoothly: lifting and re-lowering causes smearing
