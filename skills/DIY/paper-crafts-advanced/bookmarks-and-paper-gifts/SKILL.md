# Paper Craft Gifts and Bookmarks Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paper craft gifts: Bookmarks (corner bookmark — square of paper folded into an envelope / long strip bookmarks with decorative ends) → Gift boxes (score and fold from a single sheet / interlocking lid and base) → Origami gift wrapping (furoshiki-inspired paper wrapping without tape) → Paper flowers (5-petal flower from circle / rose from a spiral / dahlia from squares) → Paper stars (lucky stars from strips / moravian star folded) → Advent calendar base (numbered envelopes / pockets on a card) → Paper lanterns (accordion fold cut base).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Corner bookmarks: the most satisfying paper gift — simple but thoughtful
- Score before folding: crisp folds make all the difference in gift boxes
- Paper flowers: vary the paper size to vary the flower size proportionally
