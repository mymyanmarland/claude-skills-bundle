# Printmaking Inks and Paper Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Printmaking inks and paper: Ink types (oil-based: traditional / water-based: safer / water-soluble oil-based: compromise / fabric inks: specific to textile printing) → Ink consistency (relief printing: stiff ink / intaglio: slightly looser) → Tack (high tack picks up fine detail / low tack for soft papers and layering) → Paper types (dampened for intaglio / dry for relief / Japanese for both / heavyweight for watercolour / newsprint for proofing) → Paper absorbency (absorbent = softer impression / sized = crisper impression) → Paper grain (tear test reveals grain direction / grain parallel to spine for bookwork).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Water-based for home printing: less ventilation required and easier cleanup
- Newsprint for proofing: cheap way to check the print before using good paper
- Paper dampening for intaglio: evenly dampened paper gives consistent results
