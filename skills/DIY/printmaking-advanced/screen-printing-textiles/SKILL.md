# Screen Printing on Textiles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Textile screen printing: Fabric selection (natural fibres take ink better than synthetics / pre-wash before printing) → Ink selection (water-based fabric ink / plastisol for professional use) → Fabric support (insert a board inside a t-shirt / tape flat fabric to a printing board) → Platen adhesive (spray adhesive on the printing board — holds fabric flat without adhesive on the garment) → Print technique (firm even squeegee stroke / flood coat then print stroke) → Heat setting (heat gun or iron over parchment / or a heat press / 150-160°C) → Washing (heat-set prints are washable — test wash first).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pre-wash fabric: remove sizing from new fabric — improves ink adhesion
- Board inside t-shirts: prevents bleed-through and gives a firm printing surface
- Heat setting is mandatory: unheat-set fabric ink washes out immediately

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
