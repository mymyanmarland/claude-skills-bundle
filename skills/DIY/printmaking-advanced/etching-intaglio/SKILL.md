# Etching and Intaglio Printmaking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Etching and intaglio: Safety first (acid and mordants require ventilation / gloves and eye protection / ferric chloride is safer than nitric acid for beginners) → Plate preparation (zinc or copper / degrease / apply hard ground — wax resist) → Drawing (scratch through the ground with an etching needle / expose the metal) → Acid bath (ferric chloride / time in acid determines line depth / longer = deeper / darker) → Stopping out (stop out varnish over areas etched enough) → Inking (oil-based intaglio ink pushed into lines / surface wiped clean) → Printing (dampened paper / etching press / high pressure pushes paper into the lines).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ferric chloride for beginners: safer than nitric acid / predictable results
- Ventilation mandatory: acid fumes are harmful
- Dampened paper for intaglio: dry paper does not pick up ink from the recessed lines

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
