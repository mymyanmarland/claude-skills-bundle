# Advanced Screen Printing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced screen printing: Multi-colour registration (registration marks on every screen / align to the marks on a fixed table) → Four-colour process (halftone separations / CMYK screen printing — advanced) → Specialist inks (discharge ink: removes dye from garment rather than printing over / puff ink: raises when heated / water-based vs plastisol) → Emulsion reclaiming (remove emulsion with reclaiming fluid for screen reuse) → Photo emulsion types (dual-cure / diazo / SBQ — different exposure times and detail resolution) → Printing on dark fabrics (white underbase print first / then colour on top).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- White underbase on dark fabrics: without it colours appear muddy
- Discharge ink: prints into the fabric not on top — softer hand feel
- Registration marks on every screen: multi-colour printing cannot be registered without them

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
