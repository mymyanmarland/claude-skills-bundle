# Colour in Urban Sketching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour for urban sketching: Three-colour palette (warm yellow/cool red/blue) → Mix on the page → Local vs atmospheric colour → Large flat wash first → Unified shadow colour → Temperature contrast (warm light = cool shadows).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Three colour palette: limits force beautiful mixes — more colours create mud
- Unified shadow colour: mixing different greys looks fragmented
- Warm and cool contrast: makes a sketch feel lit

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
