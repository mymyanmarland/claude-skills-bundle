# Urban Sketching Community and Practice Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Urban sketching community: Urban Sketchers organisation (local chapters / free to join) → Sketchcrawls (group sketching events) → Instagram #urbansketchers → Schedule regular outings → Personal challenges → Sketch journal as visual diary → Teaching others accelerates improvement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Schedule regular outings: irregular practice produces irregular improvement
- Sketchcrawls accelerate learning: sketching with others is more motivating
- Sketch your neighbourhood: most accessible and personally meaningful

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
