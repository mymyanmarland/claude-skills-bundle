# Sketching at Night and in Bad Weather Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Challenging conditions: Night (high contrast / rich darks with bright focal lights) → Rain (embrace blooms from rain on the page) → Bright sun (strong shadows / work in short sessions) → Moving subjects in poor conditions (more abstraction) → Limitations force interesting solutions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rain blooms: embrace them — fight them and you lose
- High contrast for night: dark darks and bright focal lights — limited midtones
- Short sessions in bright sun: colours shift as the sun moves

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
