# Ink and Wash Sketching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Ink and wash: Waterproof ink essential → Sequence options (ink first/wash first/simultaneous) → Water brush advantages (no separate water pot) → Colour palette (3-6 colours maximum) → Lost and found edges (some sharp from ink/some soft from wash — creates depth).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Waterproof ink: non-waterproof ink bleeds into wash — most beginners' first mistake
- Water brush for outdoor work: eliminates water pot spillage
- Lost and found edges: prevents the stiff look of all-ink drawing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
