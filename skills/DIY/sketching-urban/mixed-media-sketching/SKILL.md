# Mixed Media in Urban Sketching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mixed media sketching: Pencil under ink (structure/ink on top) → Collage elements (tickets/labels/maps into wet wash) → Gouache accents (opaque whites over watercolour) → Coloured pencil over dried wash → Restraint (know when to stop).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pencil structure under ink: the pencil is a scaffold not a commitment
- Collage on location: tickets and receipts tell the story
- Gouache for white highlights: recovering whites lost in watercolour

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
