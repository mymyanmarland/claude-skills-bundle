# Composition for Urban Sketching Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Composition on location: Viewfinder (frame with fingers) → Rule of thirds → Focal point (what made you stop?) → Simplification (leave things out) → Three thumbnails before committing → Crop tighter than feels comfortable → Leading lines.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Thumbnail first: three small tests before committing
- Zoom in more: artists consistently crop too wide
- Focal point is what made you stop: trust the initial instinct

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
