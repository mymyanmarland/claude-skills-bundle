# Urban Sketching Mindset and Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Urban sketching mindset: Draw on location from direct observation → Small light kit (pen/watercolour/sketchbook) → Working in public (self-consciousness fades fast) → Work quickly — scenes change → The imperfect record is more alive than a photo.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Work quickly and accept imperfection: presence not perfection
- Pen before pencil: committing immediately prevents tentative overworked drawings
- People are curious: most passersby are interested — lean into it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
