# Drawing Buildings and Architecture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Drawing buildings: Horizon line first → Simplify then detail → Perspective (one or two vanishing points) → Texture and material (minimal marks suggest a lot) → Windows (proportions and spacing more important than detail) → People for scale → Suggest brickwork selectively.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Horizon line first: every building decision depends on established eye level
- Suggest brickwork selectively: drawing every brick loses the building
- People for scale: a building without figures often looks wrong

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
