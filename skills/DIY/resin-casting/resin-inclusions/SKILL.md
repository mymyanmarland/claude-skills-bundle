# Resin Inclusions and Embedments Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin inclusions: Dried flowers (must be completely dry — moisture causes cloudiness / press for 2-4 weeks minimum) → Pigments and dyes (resin-specific pigments / alcohol inks / mica powder — start with small amounts) → Glitter and metallic effects (add to mixed resin / distribute evenly) → Natural materials (shells / stones / botanicals — ensure fully dry) → Layering (pour a base layer / cure / add inclusion / pour over) → Photos and prints (seal with resin-compatible sealer / or laminate / paper absorbs and distorts in resin) → Avoiding sinking (thick inclusions sink / pour a thin base layer and set inclusions before topping).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Completely dry flowers: moisture in botanicals causes white cloudiness in the resin
- Seal paper before embedding: unsealed paper goes transparent and distorts
- Layer to prevent sinking: a gel base layer holds inclusions in position

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
