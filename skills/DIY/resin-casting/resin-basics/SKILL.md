# Resin Casting Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin basics: Resin types (epoxy resin: most common for casting and art / UV resin: cures under UV light — fast / polyurethane resin: fast cure for moulds) → Mixing ratio (epoxy resin must be mixed in exactly the right ratio by volume or weight — follow the manufacturer precisely) → Mixing technique (mix thoroughly / scrape the sides and bottom of the cup / 3-5 minutes of stirring) → Pot life (working time before resin begins to gel — typically 20-45 minutes for standard epoxy) → Curing time (resin takes 24-72 hours to fully cure) → Temperature effects (warmer = faster cure / too hot = exothermic reaction — overheating).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mixing ratio is critical: wrong ratio results in sticky uncured resin that cannot be fixed
- Scrape sides and bottom while mixing: unmixed resin at the edges causes sticky spots
- Do not rush the cure: handling before full cure can distort the piece

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
