# Functional Resin Items Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Functional resin: Coasters (thick pour moulds / heat-resistant resin for hot drinks coasters / cork backing) → Serving trays (mould or frame on a wood base / decorative resin inlay) → Handles and knobs (resin cast into the shape / insert metal screw thread before curing) → Heat resistance (standard epoxy is not heat resistant — use heat-resistant resin for anything near hot items) → Food safety (most epoxy resin is NOT food safe / do not use for cutting boards or food contact) → UV resistance (outdoor resin items require UV-resistant resin or they yellow).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Heat-resistant resin for coasters: standard epoxy softens with heat from hot cups
- Not food safe: most epoxy resin is not approved for food contact — research before making food items
- UV-resistant resin for outdoor use: standard epoxy yellows in sunlight

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
