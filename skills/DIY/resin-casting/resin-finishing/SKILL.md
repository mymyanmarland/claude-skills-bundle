# Resin Finishing and Polishing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin finishing: Wet sanding (use wet/dry sandpaper with water / progress through grits: 400-600-800-1000-1500-2000) → Sanding removes surface scratches (each grit removes the scratches of the previous) → Polishing compounds (automotive polish / resin-specific polish / apply with a soft cloth or polishing machine) → Buffing (soft microfibre cloth / circular motions) → Top coat (another thin layer of clear resin over sanded resin — restores full gloss without polishing) → Drilling (drill at slow speed / water coolant / prevent cracking).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Progress through grits: skipping grits leaves deeper scratches visible under the polish
- Top coat over sanding: the fastest route to a perfect finish is a flood coat not hand polishing
- Wet sanding only: dry sanding creates dust and heat that mars the resin

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
