# Advanced Resin Safety Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin safety: Sensitisation risk (repeated exposure to uncured resin can cause a permanent allergy — always use protection) → Nitrile gloves (not latex — resin permeates latex) → Respirator (organic vapour respirator — not a dust mask — for enclosed spaces) → Eye protection → Ventilation (windows open / fan exhausting air outside / not recirculating) → Skin contact (wash immediately with soap and water — not solvents) → Waste disposal (cure all resin waste before disposal / never pour uncured resin down the drain) → Take breaks (fresh air regularly / rest the hands and eyes).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sensitisation is permanent: once allergic to resin you cannot work with it — prevention is the only cure
- Nitrile not latex gloves: resin permeates latex gloves
- Cure all waste before disposal: uncured resin is a chemical hazard

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
