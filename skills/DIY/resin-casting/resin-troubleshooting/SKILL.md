# Resin Troubleshooting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin troubleshooting: Sticky resin (incorrect mixing ratio / insufficient mixing / too cold — remeasure / remix a new batch) → Bubbles (pour slowly / warm the resin slightly / use a heat gun after pouring) → Cloudiness (moisture contamination / cold curing conditions / resin too cold when mixed) → Fish eyes (contamination on the surface / silicone residue) → Yellowing (insufficient UV protection / heat / use UV-resistant resin) → Warping (thin pieces warp as they cure — support flat until fully cured) → Crazing (too thick pour / exothermic heat build-up — pour in layers for deep pours).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sticky resin: almost always a mixing error — never shortcut the mixing time or ratio
- Deep pours in layers: thick single pours overheat and craze
- Cloudiness from cold: warm both parts to room temperature before mixing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
