# Resin Jewellery Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin jewellery: Jewellery-specific resin (crystal clear / UV-resistant) → Small moulds (silicone jewellery moulds / UV resin in small amounts) → UV resin for jewellery (thin layers cured under a UV lamp / fast / ideal for small pieces) → Inclusions (tiny dried flowers / glitter / gold leaf / mica) → Finding attachment (resin-specific glue / or embed the bail before curing) → Sanding and polishing (wet sand 400-800-1500-2000 / polish with resin polishing compound) → Settings (open bezel settings filled with resin / elegant and professional look).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- UV resin for jewellery: faster and cleaner than epoxy for small pieces
- Embed the bail before curing: gluing after is less secure
- Wet sand then polish: sanded resin is cloudy — polishing restores the clarity

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
