# Resin Pigments and Colour Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin pigments: Mica powder (pearl and metallic effects / mix into Part A before combining) → Alcohol inks (vibrant transparent colour / beautiful for petri art and cells) → Resin pigment pastes (fully opaque / highly concentrated) → Acrylic paint (use sparingly — too much interferes with curing) → Glow-in-the-dark powder (charged by UV light) → Colour mixing (test in small cups before committing) → Cells and lacing (alcohol ink on the resin surface / heat gun creates cell effects) → Marble effect (two colours swirled with a cocktail stick).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mica into Part A first: mixing mica after combining causes streaks
- Alcohol inks for transparent colour: the most beautiful resin colouring medium for petri art
- Small test mixes: colour looks different in bulk resin — always test

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
