# Resin Moulds and Casting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin moulds: Silicone moulds (flexible / resin releases easily / reusable) → Making your own silicone moulds (two-part silicone / pour over the original) → Mould release (spray or wax on non-silicone moulds / prevents sticking) → Filling (pour slowly to reduce bubbles / fill in layers for deep moulds) → Bubble removal (heat gun or torch passed quickly over the surface / or pressure pot for bubble-free casting) → Demolding (wait for full cure / flex the mould / do not rush) → Mould lifespan (silicone moulds last 20-50 pours depending on the resin).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Heat gun for bubbles: passes quickly over the surface — do not hold in one spot
- Pour slowly to reduce bubbles: pouring height affects aeration
- Full cure before demolding: early demolding distorts the piece

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
