# Resin Art Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Resin art: Resin pour painting (pigmented resin poured on a canvas / tilt to spread / cells form with silicone or torch) → Petri art (alcohol inks dropped on resin surface / heat gun creates cells and movement) → Geode art (deep pour layers / mica and crystal inclusions / mimics natural geodes) → Ocean art (blue and white layers / cells suggest waves) → Canvas preparation (level surface is essential — resin flows to low points) → Top coat (a flood coat of clear resin over artwork seals and deepens the colours).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Level surface mandatory: resin flows away from high points — level is the most important setup step
- Silicone drops create cells: a drop of silicone oil in the resin before pouring creates cells when torched
- Flood coat seals artwork: UV-resistant top coat protects and deepens the finished art

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Resin contains chemicals that can cause skin sensitisation, respiratory irritation, and eye damage. Always work in a well-ventilated space, wear nitrile gloves, eye protection, and a respirator rated for organic vapours when working with uncured resin. Keep resin away from children and follow all manufacturer safety instructions.
