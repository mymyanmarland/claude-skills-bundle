# Wet Felting Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wet felting basics: How felting works (wool fibres have microscopic scales / hot water and agitation cause scales to interlock permanently) → Materials (wool fibres: carded batting or fleece / hot water / dish soap / bubble wrap or reed mat) → Laying the fibres (thin layers at right angles to each other / build up gradually) → Wetting (pour hot soapy water / press gently to saturate) → Felting (rub in circular motions / then in all directions / then roll in bubble wrap and roll vigorously) → Fulling (throw the felt against the table / shrinks and densifies the fabric) → Finishing (rinse in cold water / press / dry flat).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Thin layers at right angles: thick layers and parallel fibres prevent even felting
- Hot water is essential: cold water slows the felting process dramatically
- Fulling after initial felting: throwing firms and shrinks the felt significantly
