# Felting Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Felting community: International Feltmakers Association → Ravelry felting groups → YouTube (World of Wool tutorials / Sarafina Fibre Art) → Books (Uniquely Felt by Christine White / The Art of Felt) → Local workshops (wet felting workshops are widely available) → Fibre festivals (touch and buy fibre) → Instagram felting community (#feltmaking #needlefelting) → Online courses (many felters offer online video courses).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Local workshops: hands-on learning with a teacher is irreplaceable for felting
- YouTube for technique: visual medium suits felting — many excellent free tutorials
- International Feltmakers Association: the professional community for serious felters

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools, hot equipment, or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
