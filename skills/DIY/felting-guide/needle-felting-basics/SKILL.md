# Needle Felting Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Needle felting basics: How needle felting works (barbed needle / tangles and matts fibres with each poke / no water or heat) → Equipment (felting needles: barbed in different gauges / foam pad or brush mat) → Adding colour to existing felt (place fibre on surface / poke repeatedly to embed) → 2D picture making (layer colours onto a background felt) → 3D sculpting (ball of fibre poked and shaped into a 3D form) → Needle safety (needle felting needles break easily / work on foam not hard surfaces / keep fingers away from the needle path).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Keep fingers away from the needle path: the barbs are sharp and the needle breaks if it hits anything hard
- Foam pad under the work: hard surfaces break needles instantly
- Multiple pokes embed the fibre: one or two pokes sits on the surface — many pokes embeds

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
