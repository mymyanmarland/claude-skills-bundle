# Surface Design on Felt Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Surface design on felt: Inlaid felt (coloured prefelt or wool shapes inlaid before felting — becomes part of the fabric) → Embroidery on felt (wool or cotton thread / felt does not fray — no hoop needed) → Needle felted surface design (add colour detail after wet felting) → Printing on felt (block printing / screen printing / stamp printing) → Stencilling (acrylic paint through a stencil / seal with textile medium) → Resist printing (wax resist / shibori fold and dye) → Cutting and layering (cut felt shapes and layer for a collage effect).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Inlaid felt becomes permanent: the most integrated surface design method
- Embroidery without a hoop: the stable felt surface holds itself
- Needle felt detail after wet felting: adds fine surface colour without disturbing the structure

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
