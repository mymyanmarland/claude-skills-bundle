# Nuno Felting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Nuno felting: What nuno felting is (wool fibres felted through an open weave fabric base — silk chiffon / gauze / linen scrim) → Why it works (wool fibres migrate through open weave and felt with fibres on the other side) → Fibre placement (thin layers of wool on the fabric surface) → Wetting and felting (same as wet felting — hot soapy water / rub gently then more firmly) → Shrinkage (nuno felted fabric shrinks significantly — the more wool the more shrinkage) → Texture (the shrinkage creates a characteristic crinkled textured surface) → Applications (scarves / wall hangings / wearable art).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Open weave fabric only: tight weaves do not allow the fibres to migrate through
- Thin wool layers: too much wool creates a thick felted surface not a nuno textile
- Plan for significant shrinkage: nuno felt typically shrinks 30-50%
