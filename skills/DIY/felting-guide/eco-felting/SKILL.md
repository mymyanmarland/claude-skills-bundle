# Eco-Friendly Felting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Eco-friendly felting: Natural fibre sourcing (local farm fleece / organic certified wool / fleece from rare breeds) → Water temperature (use the hottest your tap produces — heating water uses less energy than boiling) → Soap choice (olive oil soap / eco dish soap / avoid petroleum-derived surfactants) → Water reuse (the soapy felting water can be reused for the next project) → Natural dyes (plant dyes before synthetic) → End of life (wool felt is compostable — unlike synthetic felt) → Ethical sourcing (look for mulesing-free certification for Australian Merino).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tap hot water is sufficient: water does not need to be boiling for successful wet felting
- Reuse felting water: the soapy water works just as well for the next project
- Compostable wool felt: at end of life, wool felt returns to the soil — a genuine sustainability advantage

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
