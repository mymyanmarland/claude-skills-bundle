# Felt Sculpting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Felt sculpting: Starting form (compressed ball of wool / or prefelt base form) → Building up (add layers of wool and poke to attach) → Shaping (poke more in areas you want to compress / less in areas you want fuller) → Fine detail (finer gauge needles for detail work) → Eyes (glass eyes / felted dots / beads) → Wire armatures (for figures with jointed limbs — insert wire before felting the exterior) → Wet finishing (a surface wet felt over a needle-felted core creates a harder exterior).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wire armature before exterior: inserting wire after exterior is felted is very difficult
- Finer needles for detail: coarser needles leave visible holes in detail areas
- Wet surface over needle core: creates a harder more durable exterior

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
