# Prefelt and Resist Felting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Prefelt and resist felting: Prefelt (partially felted sheet — stable enough to cut but not fully felted) → Cutting prefelt shapes (cut like fabric / felts further when added to a project) → Resist felting (a foam or plastic resist defines the interior shape — used for bowls / hats / bags / slippers) → The process (wrap wool around the resist / felt from both sides / remove the resist / full the hollow form) → Shrinkage around the resist (the felt shrinks to form the interior shape) → Resist removal (cut or stretch the opening / remove the resist when felt is firm).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Remove resist before final fulling: final fulling without the resist allows the opening to shrink closed
- Prefelt cuts like fabric: use it to create precise shapes that felt further when combined
- Felt from both sides equally: uneven felting creates uneven thickness
