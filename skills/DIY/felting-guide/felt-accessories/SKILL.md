# Felted Accessories Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Felted accessories: Felted slippers (resist felting around a foot-shaped resist / rubber soles added with contact adhesive) → Felted bags (resist defines the interior / opening cut and hemmed after felting) → Felted jewellery (needle or wet felt components / assembled with wire or jump rings) → Corsages and brooches (wet felted flowers / needle felted embellishments) → Sizing (felt shrinks significantly — work to a template 30-40% larger than the desired final size) → Durability (felt does not fray / very durable / water resistant when fully fulled).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 30-40% larger template: felting shrinkage must be built into every accessory project
- Rubber soles on slippers: felt alone is very slippery on hard floors
- Fully full for durability: lightly felted accessories distort with use

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
