# Fused Glass Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fused glass design: Colour interaction (some colour combinations react in the kiln — test before a full piece) → Colour change in firing (some colours shift significantly at fusing temperatures — especially copper-based reds and oranges) → Design for the process (think about what happens at fusing temperature / flat designs / simple geometry for beginners) → Paper template (design on paper first / cut to size / arrange the glass to the template) → Colour testing (fire a test tile of all colour combinations you plan to use) → Negative space (open areas are part of the design — plan them).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fire test tiles of all colour combinations: unexpected colour reactions in a finished piece are common
- Some colours shift at temperature: reds and oranges especially — test first
- Paper template first: the design locked in on paper before cutting any glass

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
