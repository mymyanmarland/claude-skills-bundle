# Mosaic Techniques in Fused Glass Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mosaic glass fusing: Design approach (small pieces of glass arranged in patterns / similar to traditional mosaic but fused permanently) → Cutting small pieces (score and break into small regular shapes / or use nippers for irregular tessera) → Colour planning (the mosaic pattern relies on colour contrast / value contrast as important as hue) → Arrangement on kiln paper or shelf (arrange carefully / glass moves slightly when fusing) → Grout (glass mosaic can be grouted after fusing like traditional mosaic / or fused together without grout) → Borders and frames (contain the composition / unify the design).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Value contrast is more important than hue: a mosaic where all values are the same reads as flat
- Glass moves during fusing: leave slight gaps — they will close during full fuse
- Grout option: fused glass mosaic can also be grouted for a traditional mosaic look

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
