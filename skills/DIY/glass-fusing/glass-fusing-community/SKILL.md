# Glass Fusing Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass fusing community: Bullseye Glass educational resources (comprehensive free tutorials) → Glass Fusing Made Easy (book / comprehensive beginner guide) → YouTube (The Vitrigraph / fusing tutorials) → Local stained glass studios (often do fusing classes) → Glass suppliers (Bullseye / Wissmach / Oceanside) → Online forums (Warmglass.com — the most comprehensive glass fusing forum) → Kiln ownership group (share a kiln with a neighbour to split the cost) → Regional glass guilds.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Warmglass.com: the most comprehensive and knowledgeable glass fusing community online
- Bullseye educational resources: free and comprehensive — start here
- Local stained glass studio: access to a kiln and expertise without the investment

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
