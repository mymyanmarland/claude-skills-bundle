# Introduction to Flameworking Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Flameworking basics: What flameworking is (shaping soft glass rods in a flame / also called lampworking) → Equipment (torch / borosilicate or soft glass rods / mandrels for beads / annealing kiln) → Safety (protective goggles rated for working with flame and glass — standard glasses are not safe / ventilation / fire safety) → Basic bead making (wind soft glass onto a mandrel in the flame / add colour and shape) → Annealing (glass must be annealed — flameworked glass is under stress without it) → Borosilicate vs soft glass (boro: higher temperature / more complex / soft glass: lower temperature / more accessible for beginners).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Flameworking goggles mandatory: the flame emits IR and UV radiation — standard glasses are not protective
- Annealing after flameworking: unannealed flameworked glass is fragile and may crack spontaneously
- Soft glass for beginners: lower working temperature / more forgiving

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
