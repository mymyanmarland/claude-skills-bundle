# Glass Fusing Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass fusing basics: What glass fusing is (glass pieces heated in a kiln until they bond together) → Compatible glass (all glass in one piece must be the same COE — coefficient of expansion — 90 or 96 are common) → Glass types (fusible glass: Bullseye / Spectrum / Uroboros — not window glass) → Basic fusing (cut glass pieces / clean thoroughly / arrange on kiln shelf with kiln wash / fire according to a schedule) → Tack fuse vs full fuse (tack fuse: pieces bond but retain their shape / full fuse: pieces melt together and flatten) → Kiln wash (separates the glass from the shelf — essential).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Compatible glass only: mixing COE numbers causes the piece to crack as it cools
- Kiln wash on every shelf firing: without it glass fuses to the shelf permanently
- Tack vs full fuse: different temperatures produce different bonding levels

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
