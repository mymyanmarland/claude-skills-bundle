# Glass Fusing Tools and Equipment Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass fusing tools: Kiln (the essential investment / small square kilns start around affordable prices / digital controller required) → Glass cutter (pistol grip / pen style — personal preference / keep oiled) → Running pliers → Grozing pliers → Kiln wash (apply fresh before every firing) → Kiln furniture (posts and shelves / allow kiln loading at multiple heights) → Mosaic nippers (for small irregular pieces) → Glass grinder (wet wheel grinder / smooths edges and allows accurate shaping) → Safety glasses → Work surface (cut on a self-healing mat or glass-specific surface).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Digital controller kiln: manual switching is too imprecise for glass fusing schedules
- Glass grinder for accuracy: cutting alone cannot achieve tight fitting curved pieces
- Fresh kiln wash before every firing: contaminated kiln wash causes glass to stick

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
