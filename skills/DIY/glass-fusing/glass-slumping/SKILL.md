# Glass Slumping Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass slumping: What slumping is (a fused glass blank heated over or into a mould — the glass softens and takes the mould shape) → Mould materials (ceramic bisque / stainless steel / kiln-formed moulds) → Mould preparation (kiln wash on all surfaces that contact glass) → Slumping temperature (lower than fusing — approximately 650-700°C — the glass should only soften enough to drape) → Drape slumping (glass draped over a raised mould) → Drop slumping (glass drops into a recessed mould) → Post-slump (additional annealing schedule).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Kiln wash on all mould surfaces: glass fusing to the mould destroys both
- Lower temperature than fusing: slumping only requires softening not melting
- Anneal after slumping: the second firing requires the same annealing care as the first

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
