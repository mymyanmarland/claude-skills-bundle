# Glass Cutting for Fusing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass cutting: Tools (glass cutter / cutting oil / running pliers / grozing pliers / safety glasses mandatory) → Scoring (single firm stroke from edge to edge / do not repeat the score / score does not cut — it creates a stress line) → Breaking (running pliers on the score line / or flex the glass with hands) → Curves (score the curve / make relief cuts to the edge / break out small sections) → Curves with pliers (groziers nibble away glass toward the scored line) → Safety (glass edges are very sharp — handle with care / brush glass chips away from the work surface with a brush not hands).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single firm score: repeating the score dulls the cutter and creates a bad break
- Safety glasses always: glass shards from breaking travel unpredictably
- Relief cuts for curves: curved breaks without relief cuts crack the whole piece

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
