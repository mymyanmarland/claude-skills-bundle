# Glass Inclusions and Special Effects Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Glass inclusions: Metal foils (gold / silver / copper foil sandwiched between glass layers — fires to a permanent metallic inclusion) → Mica and lustre powders (applied to the glass surface before firing / fuses into the glass) → Enamels (powdered glass in colour / applied to the surface / melts in firing) → Dichroic glass (glass with metallic coating / reflects multiple colours / spectacular) → Stringers (thin rods of glass / arrange in patterns on the surface) → Frit (crushed glass powder and granules / fills areas with texture and colour) → Confetti glass (very thin sheets of colour / layered for effect).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Metal foils between layers: exposed foil oxidises and changes colour
- Test dichroic combinations: the reflective coating interacts with adjacent colours
- Frit sizes: finer frit creates a smoother surface / coarser creates texture

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Safety Disclaimer: Glass fusing involves high temperatures and sharp glass. Always wear safety glasses when cutting glass, use appropriate gloves, follow all kiln manufacturer safety instructions, and ensure adequate ventilation. Never leave a kiln unattended during firing.
