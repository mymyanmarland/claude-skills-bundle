# Outdoor Low-Voltage Lighting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Low-voltage garden lighting: System overview (transformer plugs into indoor socket / low-voltage cable runs to lights / safe to handle at 12V or 24V) → Transformer selection (total wattage of all lights + 20% headroom) → Cable laying (bury minimum 300mm deep / use armoured cable even at low voltage — protection from spades) → Connections (weatherproof connectors / spike lights simply push in) → Solar alternative (no cabling — effective for accent lighting) → Mains voltage garden lights (these need a qualified electrician — do not DIY mains outdoor wiring) → Timer and sensor control.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Bury cable: even low-voltage cable needs protection from garden tools
- Mains outdoor wiring: requires a qualified electrician — not a DIY task
- Transformer headroom: undersized transformer dims all lights

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
