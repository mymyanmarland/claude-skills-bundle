# Compost Bin Construction Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Compost bin build: Bin types (simple pallet bin / slatted timber / wire cage / three-bay system) → Three-bay rationale (fresh / active / ready — allows continuous composting) → Dimensions (minimum 1m x 1m x 1m — smaller bins do not generate enough heat) → Timber slat bin (treated posts / removable slats at front for access) → Pallet bin (four pallets wired together — very fast and free) → Base (no base — ground contact allows worms to enter) → Aeration (turn the pile / ventilation gaps in the sides) → Cover (lid or cardboard — keeps rain out and heat in).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Minimum 1m cube: smaller bins do not reach the temperature needed for active composting
- No base: worm access from below is essential for good composting
- Removable front slats: access for turning and removing finished compost

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
