# Outdoor Furniture Building Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Outdoor furniture: Timber selection (hardwood: oak/teak/iroko — durable / treated softwood: cheaper / composite: maintenance-free) → Joinery (mortise and tenon for strength / or structural screws with plug covers) → Fasteners (stainless steel screws and bolts — standard steel rusts outdoors) → Finishing (outdoor wood oil / decking stain / leave hardwood to silver naturally) → Angled seats (5-degree seat angle for comfort) → Ground contact avoidance (end grain sealed / raise slightly if possible) → Annual maintenance (oil before winter / store cushions).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stainless steel fixings: standard steel leaves rust streaks within one season
- Ground contact: where timber meets ground is where it rots — minimise contact
- Annual oil treatment: extends timber life dramatically

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
