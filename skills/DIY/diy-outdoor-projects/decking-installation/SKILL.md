# Decking Installation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decking installation: Planning (level / sub-frame direction perpendicular to boards / drainage slope 1:100 minimum) → Subframe (joists at 400mm centres for timber boards / treated softwood or steel frame) → Joist fixing (to post anchors or on concrete pads — not in direct soil contact) → Board laying (5mm gaps for drainage and expansion / hidden fixings for clean look) → Cutting around posts and obstacles → End treatment (end grain sealed with decking end grain sealer) → Finishing (decking oil annually for timber / composite: low maintenance but still needs cleaning).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Slope for drainage: flat decking retains water and rots — 1:100 fall minimum
- 5mm gaps: boards swell and close the gaps — leave them
- Treat end grain: the most vulnerable part of timber decking — seal it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
