# Garden Wall Laying Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Garden wall laying: Planning (low decorative wall vs structural retaining wall — structural requires engineering) → Foundation (footing: twice the width of the wall / 150mm concrete minimum) → Mortar mix (1 part cement : 4 parts sharp sand for general brickwork) → Laying technique (bed of mortar / brick pressed down and tapped level / 10mm perpendicular joints staggered) → String line (kept taut as a guide for each course) → Plumb check (every few courses / use a spirit level) → Coping or cap (weather protection on top — essential) → Low walls only: walls over 600mm need professional design.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Walls over 600mm: need professional structural advice — especially if retaining soil
- Staggered joints: continuous vertical joints create a weak plane
- Coping on top: unprotected wall tops erode quickly in rain

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
