# Raised Garden Bed Construction Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Raised bed construction: Materials (untreated hardwood / railway sleepers / brick / galvanised steel — avoid treated timber next to food crops) → Size (maximum 1.2m wide — accessible from both sides without stepping in) → Height (300mm minimum — 600mm for easier access) → Corner joints (half-lap or timber corner posts) → Sleeper stacking (stagger the joints like brickwork / pin with rebar through pre-drilled holes) → Drainage (no base needed — the ground acts as drainage / line only the sides if sleepers are borderline treated) → Fill mix (topsoil 60% / compost 30% / grit 10%).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maximum 1.2m wide: wider means stepping in — compacts the soil and defeats the purpose
- Untreated timber next to food: creosote and some preservatives leach into soil
- Fill mix ratio: good drainage and fertility balance from the start

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
