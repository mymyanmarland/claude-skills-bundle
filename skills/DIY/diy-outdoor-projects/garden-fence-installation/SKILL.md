# Garden Fence Installation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fence installation: Planning (check property boundaries / neighbour consent / local regulations / check for buried services — call before you dig) → Post spacing (for standard 1.8m panels: 1.83m centre to centre) → Post depth (minimum one-third in ground: 1.8m post = 600mm below ground) → Setting posts (concrete — mix stiff / overfill and slope away for drainage / check plumb from two directions) → Panel fitting (arris rails or slot into post channels / 50mm above ground — prevent rot) → Gate hanging (gate posts need extra concrete — they carry dynamic load) → Preservative treatment on any new cuts.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Check buried services before digging: hitting a cable or pipe is dangerous and expensive
- One-third post depth: shallow posts blow over in wind — common mistake
- Slope concrete away from post: water pooling at the post base causes rot
