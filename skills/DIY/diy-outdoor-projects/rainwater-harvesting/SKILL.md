# Rainwater Harvesting Setup Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Rainwater harvesting: Water butt options (standard plastic / slimline / raised / underground tank) → Downpipe diverter (install in the downpipe — diverts overflow back to drains when butt is full) → Siting (as close to the downpipe as possible / raised on a stand for gravity-fed hose connection) → Overflow (connect to another butt or back to the drain) → Mosquito prevention (tight-fitting lid — prevents breeding) → First flush diverter (optional but useful — diverts the initial dirty flush away from the tank) → Winterising (partially empty before frost to prevent cracking).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tight-fitting lid: standing water breeds mosquitoes — lid is essential
- First flush diverter: removes the debris-laden first rainfall from entering the tank
- Raise on a stand: gravity-fed hose requires the outlet above the plant level
