# Free Motion Quilting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Free motion quilting: Preparation (drop feed dogs / attach darning or free motion foot) → Machine settings (slightly longer stitch length / slow machine speed / moderate hand speed) → The balance (machine speed and hand speed must be balanced — both too fast creates tiny stitches / both too slow creates long ones) → Starting and stopping (bury thread tails / tiny stitches to lock) → Basic designs (stippling / meandering / loops / feathers) → Gloves (quilting gloves with rubber fingertips give grip on the quilt) → Practice on a quilt sandwich first.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Balance machine speed and hand speed: the most important FMQ skill
- Drop the feed dogs: raised feed dogs fight the hand-guided movement
- Gloves with rubber fingertips: grip the quilt and reduce fatigue

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
