# Quilt Binding Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quilt binding: Cutting binding strips (2.5 inch wide / bias cut for curved edges / straight cut for straight edges) → Joining strips (diagonal seam to reduce bulk) → Applying to front (raw edges aligned / 6mm seam / mitre the corners: fold and align) → Mitred corners (fold binding up and away then back down — forms a mitre) → Turning to the back (fold over / pin) → Hand stitching the back (slip stitch / or machine stitch in the ditch from the front) → Starting and stopping (diagonal join of the two binding ends).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mitre the corners: square folded corners look bulky — mitres look professional
- Diagonal join of binding ends: the flattest and neatest way to join the two ends
- Hand stitch the back: machine in the ditch from the front is faster / hand stitching is more invisible
