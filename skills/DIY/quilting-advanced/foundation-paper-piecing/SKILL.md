# Foundation Paper Piecing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Foundation paper piecing: What FPP is (fabric sewn to the back of a printed paper foundation — precise results from complex shapes) → Paper foundation (printed with the block in reverse) → Sewing sequence (numbered sections sewn in order / always sewing on the line) → Fabric placement (slightly larger than the section / placed on the back of the paper) → Reducing stitch length (shorter stitch makes paper removal easier) → Paper removal (tear away after the block is fully assembled) → Precision (FPP is the most accurate piecing method for complex shapes like flying geese or curved blocks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Reduce stitch length for FPP: perforated seams tear away cleanly
- Fabric slightly larger than the section: the seam allowance must extend beyond the line
- Sewing on the line: you are sewing on the paper side not the fabric side
