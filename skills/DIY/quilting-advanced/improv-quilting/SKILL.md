# Improvisational Quilting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Improvisational quilting: What improv quilting is (making design decisions at the cutting table — not from a pattern) → Slash and insert (cut a sewn piece / insert a strip / re-sew) → Off-centre seams (join fabrics with a non-straight seam) → Stacking and slicing (stack fabrics / slice / rearrange and sew) → Working with a limited palette (2-3 fabrics create cohesion in a patternless quilt) → Press first quilt later (sew large amounts of fabric / then cut into quilt top pieces) → Embracing imperfection (improv quilting celebrates the handmade — perfect matching is not the goal).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Limited palette for improv: fewer fabrics create more coherence in a free-form quilt
- Embracing imperfection: the language of improv quilting — intentional not accidental
- Slash and insert: the most accessible improv technique — transform any sewn piece

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
