# Quilt Sandwich and Basting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quilt sandwich: Three layers (quilt top / wadding / backing) → Backing (10cm larger than the top on all sides / seamed if needed) → Wadding selection (cotton: shrinks slightly and drapey / polyester: lofty and stable / wool: warm and luxurious / bamboo: lightweight) → Layering order (backing face down / wadding / top face up — all wrinkle-free) → Basting methods (safety pins: every 10cm / spray basting: temporary adhesive / tacking stitches by hand) → Flat surface is essential (use a table not the floor for larger quilts).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Backing 10cm larger all round: quilts shift during quilting — the extra prevents running out of backing
- Wadding choice changes everything: cotton shrinks slightly and creates that antique crinkle look
- Spray basting for large quilts: safer than crawling on the floor with safety pins
