# Hand Quilting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Hand quilting: Thread (dedicated hand quilting thread: waxed and stronger than regular thread) → Needle (between needle: short and fine / size 10-12) → Thimble (essential for pushing the needle through three layers) → The rocking stitch (push needle down through all layers / rock back up / load 2-4 stitches on the needle) → Hoop or frame (maintains consistent tension in the quilt) → Marking quilting designs (washable marker / tailor's chalk / masking tape as a straight line guide) → Stitch length (smaller stitches require practice — aim for consistent before small).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Waxed quilting thread: standard thread tangles and breaks in hand quilting
- Rocking stitch: the efficient hand quilting technique — not a stab stitch
- Consistent before small: even large stitches done consistently look better than inconsistent small ones

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
