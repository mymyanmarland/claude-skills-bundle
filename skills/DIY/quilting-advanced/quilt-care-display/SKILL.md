# Quilt Care and Display Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quilt care: Washing (gentle cycle / cold water / gentle detergent / mesh laundry bag for antique quilts) → Drying (air dry flat / or low heat in the dryer with clean dry towels) → First wash (expect slight shrinkage / test for colour fastness before washing a new quilt) → Storage (acid-free tissue or unbleached muslin / fold with padding / never in plastic / refold periodically to prevent permanent crease lines) → Display (away from direct sunlight — fades fabric / hanging sleeve and rod for wall quilts / rotate if displayed long-term).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fold with acid-free tissue inside: prevents permanent crease lines at fold points
- Never plastic storage: plastic traps moisture and causes mildew
- Direct sunlight fades quilts: even a few months of sun exposure is visible damage
