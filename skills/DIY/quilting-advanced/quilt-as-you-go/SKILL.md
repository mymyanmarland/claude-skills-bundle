# Quilt-As-You-Go Method Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quilt-As-You-Go: What QAYG is (each block individually layered and quilted before assembly) → Block preparation (top block / wadding / backing cut to the same size) → Quilting each block (through all three layers while small and manageable) → Joining blocks (sew top layers together / join wadding edges / fold backing over the seam / slip stitch) → Advantages (no managing a large quilt under the machine / portable / each block is a small manageable project) → Wadding overlap (leave a small seam allowance of wadding to butt together).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wadding butt-join: overlapping wadding creates a ridge — butt the edges together
- Quilt while small: the most accessible domestic machine quilting method
- Backing folds over the seam: creates a neat finished seam on both sides
