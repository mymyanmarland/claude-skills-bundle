# Sewing Trousers Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sewing trousers: Crotch curve fitting (most challenging / make a toile first) → Construction sequence (front pockets/fly zip/crotch seam/back pieces/inner leg/outer leg/waistband) → Fly zip (mark carefully / interface the extension) → Waistband (interface/stitch/fold/understitch) → Hem last.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Toile for trousers first: crotch curve fitting without a toile almost always fails
- Hem last: trouser length confirmed only when all other fitting is complete
- Interface the fly extension: un-interfaced flies do not hold their shape

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
