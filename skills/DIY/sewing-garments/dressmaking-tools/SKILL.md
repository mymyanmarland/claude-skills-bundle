# Dressmaking Tools and Equipment Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dressmaking tools: Cutting (dressmaking shears/rotary cutter/pinking shears/thread snippers) → Marking (chalk/chalk wheel/disappearing ink pen) → Pressing (iron/sleeve board/tailor's ham/seam roller) → Machine feet (zipper/invisible zip/walking/buttonhole) → Measuring (tape/seam gauge/clear ruler) → Dress form for regular sewers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tailor's ham: irreplaceable for pressing curved seams and darts
- Invisible zip foot: cannot install invisible zips without it
- Dress form for regular sewers: the most valuable investment for garment sewing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
