# Garment Fitting and Alterations Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Garment fitting: Muslin toile first → Common problems (swayback/broad shoulders/full bust/square shoulders) → Swayback (horizontal fold in back at waist) → Full bust adjustment FBA (add fabric to bodice front) → Shoulder adjustment → Basted seams allow adjustment → RTW adjustments using same techniques.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Muslin toile first: fitting adjustments in final fabric are costly
- FBA for full bust: the most important adjustment for many sewers
- Basted seams allow adjustment: do not sew permanently until fit is confirmed

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
