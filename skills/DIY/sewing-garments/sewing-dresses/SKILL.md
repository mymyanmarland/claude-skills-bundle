# Sewing Dresses Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sewing dresses: Construction sequence (darts/side seams/bodice-skirt join/zip/facings/hem) → Darts (wide end to point / tie off at point — do not backstitch) → Understitch facings (prevents rolling outward) → Hems (hand/machine topstitch/blind hem) → Lining (face down construction).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dart tip finishing: backstitching at the point causes a dimple — tie off instead
- Understitch facings: the single stitch that prevents facings rolling outside
- Follow construction sequence: logical order prevents having to undo completed work

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
