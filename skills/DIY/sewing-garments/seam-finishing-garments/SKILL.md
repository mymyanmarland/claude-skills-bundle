# Seam Finishing Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Seam finishing: Zigzag stitch (fastest / most fabrics) → Overlocker/serger (professional / trims and overlocks) → French seam (encloses raw edge — ideal for sheers) → Flat felled seam (strong / visible outside — jeans) → Hong Kong finish (bias-bound — beautiful in unlined jackets) → Pinking shears (reduces fraying without stitching).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Overlocker: the professional garment-sewing tool — worth investing in
- French seams for sheers: encloses all raw edges
- Hong Kong finish for unlined jackets: the mark of careful tailoring

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
