# Sewing Knit Fabrics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sewing knit fabrics: Stretch stitch (zigzag/overlock/twin needle — not straight stitch) → Ballpoint or stretch needle (pierces between fibres) → Cutting (lay flat without stretching) → Twin needle for professional hem → Lightweight interfacing on shoulder seams prevents stretching.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stretch stitch always: straight stitch on knit breaks on the first wearing
- Ballpoint needle: regular needle damages knit fibres and causes skipped stitches
- Twin needle hem: closest to bought-looking hem on a domestic machine

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
