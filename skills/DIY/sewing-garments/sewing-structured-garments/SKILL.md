# Structured Garment Sewing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Structured garments: Interfacing (fusible/sew-in/hair canvas for tailored jackets) → Where to interface (fronts/collar/cuffs/pocket flaps) → Pad stitching (creates the lapel roll) → Welt pockets (practise on scrap first) → Lining (cut larger / ease allows movement) → Pressing is 50% of tailoring.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pressing is 50% of tailoring: poorly pressed jacket looks amateur regardless of sewing
- Interface all structural edges: un-interfaced fronts and collars collapse
- Welt pocket on scrap first: the most punishing pocket to unpick

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
