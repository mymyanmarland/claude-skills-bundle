# Inserting Zips Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Zip insertion: Centred zip (seam opened / zip centred / topstitched both sides) → Lapped zip (overlaps / one side only) → Invisible zip (invisible zip foot essential / appears as a seam) → Fly zip (jeans — more complex) → Press zips before sewing → Buy longer and trim after installation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Invisible zip foot: essential — standard foot cannot get close enough to the coil
- Buy longer and trim: gives flexibility during installation
- Press the invisible zip before sewing: unrolling the coil improves the sewing position

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
