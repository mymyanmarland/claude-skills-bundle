# Fabric Cutting and Layout Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Sewing experience level
- Specific project or garment
- Fabric type and quantity available
- Sewing machine or hand sewing
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Fabric cutting: Pre-washing (prevents shrinkage in finished garment) → Grain line (parallel to selvedge / measure both ends) → Pinning or pattern weights → Dressmaking shears only → Notches outward not inward → Marking (tailor's chalk/disappearing marker/tailor's tacks).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Grain line accuracy: off-grain cutting causes garments to twist and hang crookedly
- Pre-wash fabric: skipping causes the finished garment to shrink
- Notches outward: cutting inward into the seam allowance weakens the seam

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
