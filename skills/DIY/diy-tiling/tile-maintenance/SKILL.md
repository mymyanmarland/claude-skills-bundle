# Tile Cleaning and Maintenance Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tile maintenance: Regular cleaning (appropriate cleaner for tile type — acid cleaners damage marble and natural stone) → Grout cleaning (bicarbonate of soda paste and old toothbrush / commercial grout cleaner) → Re-grouting (remove old grout with oscillating tool / apply new grout / seal when dry) → Sealing natural stone tiles (impregnating sealer annually) → Preventing mould (ventilate after showering / anti-mould grout in wet rooms) → Addressing silicone (remove with a tool and silicone remover spray / re-apply fresh silicone) → Re-grouting entire floor (grout rake, regrout, reseal).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acid cleaners damage natural stone: check tile type before applying any cleaner
- Ventilation prevents mould: the most effective and cheapest mould prevention
- Silicone replacement: black mouldy silicone cannot be cleaned — replace it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
