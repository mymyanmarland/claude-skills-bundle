# Wall Tiling Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wall tiling: Find the centre line (vertical and horizontal — start from the middle outward for symmetrical layouts) → Battens (fix a horizontal batten one tile height above the floor — ensures straight first course) → Mix adhesive (creamy, not too wet) → Notched trowel (comb adhesive onto the wall in one direction) → Back butter large tiles (adhesive on the tile back as well) → Spacers (consistent grout joints) → Check level every row → Cut tiles last (measure each cut individually — walls are rarely perfectly square).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Horizontal batten: the key to straight tiling — do not skip it
- Level checks every row: one unlevel row multiplies through the whole job
- Measure each cut individually: walls are not square — do not measure once and cut many
