# Tile Cutting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tile cutting: Score and snap (tile cutter — score firmly in one pass / snap over the break edge — works for straight cuts on ceramic) → Angle grinder with diamond blade (curved cuts / L-shapes / porcelain) → Wet tile saw / disc cutter (accurate straight cuts on hard tiles — water coolant prevents dust and overheating) → Tile nippers (nibbling curves — slow but works for small curved cuts) → Eye protection and dust mask mandatory → Porcelain (very hard — score-and-snap often does not work — wet saw or angle grinder usually required).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Single firm score: multiple passes do not improve the cut — one firm score then snap
- Angle grinder for curves: the only practical tool for curved ceramic cuts
- Eye protection mandatory: tile fragments travel at speed

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
