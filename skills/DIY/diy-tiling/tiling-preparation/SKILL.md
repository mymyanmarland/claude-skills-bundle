# Tiling Surface Preparation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tiling preparation: Surface check (solid / flat / clean / dry — tiles need a stable base) → Plasterboard vs plaster (standard plasterboard is fine for light tiles / heavy tiles need tile backer board) → Waterproofing for wet areas (tanking slurry or waterproof membrane behind tiles in showers and wet rooms) → Removing old tiles (chisel and bolster or electric chisel — protect eyes and wear gloves) → Flattening the surface (high spots — grind down / low spots — skim with tile adhesive) → Priming (prime porous surfaces — plasterboard and bare plaster before tiling) → Planning the layout (find the centre, plan the tile positions before mixing any adhesive).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Waterproofing in wet rooms: non-negotiable — tiles alone are not waterproof
- Surface must be flat: lippage (tiles at different heights) is a preparation failure
- Plan before mixing: dry-lay tiles to check the layout before committing

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
