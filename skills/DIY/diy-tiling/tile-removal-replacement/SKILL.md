# Tile Removal and Replacement Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tile removal and replacement: Grout removal (oscillating tool or grout saw — remove the grout from all four edges first) → Tile removal (cold chisel or drill multiple holes / work from the centre outward / do not lever against adjacent tiles) → Surface preparation (remove old adhesive / flatten if needed) → Replacement (match the adhesive type / back-butter the new tile / use spacers to match joint width) → Regrout (match the grout colour — buy the same brand if possible) → Limitation (some patterns and colours are discontinued — plan for this).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Remove grout before the tile: chiselling without removing grout cracks adjacent tiles
- Work from centre outward: pressure at the edges cracks neighbours
- Grout colour matching: buy extra of the original batch — colour varies between batches

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
