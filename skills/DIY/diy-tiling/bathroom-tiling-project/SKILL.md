# Bathroom Tiling Project Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Bathroom tiling project: Order of work (walls before floor — floor tiles tuck under wall tiles) → Waterproofing (tanking the shower area — pipe seals and corner tape) → Layout planning (set out all walls before starting — find the dominant wall) → Starting point (horizontal batten at correct height / vertical batten at centre line) → Working sequence (back wall / side walls / floor) → Sealing (silicone at all internal corners / around bath / shower tray / window sills) → Sealant colour (grout colour or white) → Grouting and polishing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Walls before floor: floor tiles go in last and tuck under the wall tiles
- Tanking the shower: absolutely essential — water gets behind tiles regardless of grout quality
- Sealing all internal corners: grout cracks here — silicone is essential
