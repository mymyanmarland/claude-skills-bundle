# Tiling Around Obstacles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tiling around obstacles: Pipes (measure from the last tile to the pipe centre / mark on the tile / drill a hole slightly larger than the pipe with a tile drill bit or hole saw) → Sockets and switches (isolate power / tile up to the box / cut the tile with a grinder or wet saw to fit tightly) → Internal corners (one wall overlaps the other — cut tiles meet in the corner) → External corners (round-edge tiles or corner trim profiles — adhesive over the trim) → Odd shapes (template from card, trace and cut) → Safety: always isolate electrical supplies before tiling near sockets.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Always isolate power before tiling near sockets: live electrical work is dangerous
- Corner profiles: external corners need finishing — raw tile edges chip
- Card template for odd shapes: paper template first then transfer to tile

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
