# Grouting Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Grouting: Timing (adhesive must be fully cured — 24 hours minimum) → Mix consistency (thick toothpaste) → Application (grout float at 45 degrees, push into joints from multiple directions) → Remove excess (damp sponge in circular motion — not too wet / ring out thoroughly) → Final polish (dry cloth once the haze has set) → Joints (seal silicone at all changes of plane — wall to floor / wall to bath — not grout) → Sealant (porous grout benefits from grout sealer — especially floors).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Silicone at changes of plane: grout cracks at these points — always silicone
- Damp not wet sponge: too much water weakens the grout and pulls it from the joints
- Seal porous grout: especially on floors subject to water — sealer extends the life
