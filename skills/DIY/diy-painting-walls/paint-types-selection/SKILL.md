# Paint Types and Selection Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paint selection: Matt emulsion (most wall and ceiling applications — hides imperfections / not washable) → Silk or eggshell (slight sheen / more washable — kitchens, bathrooms, children's rooms) → Satinwood or satin (woodwork and trim — durable, wipeable) → Gloss (high durability woodwork / traditional doors and skirting) → Masonry paint (exterior walls — flexible and weather resistant) → One coat paints (useful but not always as claimed — heavily pigmented colours often still need two coats) → Water vs oil-based (water dries faster and cleans up easier / oil harder wearing but slower and solvent cleanup).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Matt for walls: hides imperfections / not washable — choose accordingly
- Satin or eggshell in wet rooms: more moisture resistant than matt
- One coat paints: useful for recoats over similar colour — rarely true one coat on fresh plaster
