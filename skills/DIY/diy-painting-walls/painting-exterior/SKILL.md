# Exterior House Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Exterior painting: Timing (dry weather forecast for 24+ hours / above 10°C / not direct hot sun) → Preparation (power wash / allow to dry fully / repair cracks with exterior filler) → Masonry paint application (brush or thick-pile roller / two coats minimum) → Exterior woodwork (scrape loose paint / sand / prime bare wood / undercoat / gloss or satinwood) → Working order (top to bottom / windows from top) → Masking (protect glass and surrounding surfaces) → Scaffold and access (safety first — never overreach from a ladder).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Dry weather only: painting in damp conditions causes peeling
- Top to bottom: drips fall on unworked surface
- Never overreach from a ladder: reposition the ladder — overreaching causes falls

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
