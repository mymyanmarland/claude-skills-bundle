# Professional Painting Tips Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Professional tips: Never skip preparation (professionals spend 60-70% of their time preparing) → Quality tools (a cheap brush marks itself on every surface — buy one good brush) → Two thin coats over one thick (thinner coats dry flatter and adhere better) → The order (ceiling first / walls second / woodwork last — always) → Consistent direction (lay off in one direction only — never back and forth in the final strokes) → Clean equipment (brushes cleaned immediately after use last for years) → Lighting (a bright light held at an angle reveals every imperfection before it dries).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Preparation is 70% of the job: professionals know this, beginners skip it
- Quality brushes: one good brush outlasts ten cheap ones
- Angled light reveals everything: check your work before it dries
