# Cutting In and Edging Technique Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cutting in: Brush selection (angled 50mm brush — the single most important tool for cutting in) → Loading the brush (one-third of bristle length — not soaked) → Technique (draw a bead of paint along the edge, then smooth it away from the edge with a single stroke) → Steady hand development (use the tip of the brush, rest the hand against the wall for stability) → Straight lines (masking tape for true beginners — remove while paint is still wet) → Work in sections (cut in a metre at a time then fill in with roller while paint is wet — wet into wet).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Angled brush: the correct tool makes cutting in achievable for beginners
- Remove masking tape wet: tape left to dry pulls paint off the edge
- Cut in then roll while wet: dry edges leave a visible line
