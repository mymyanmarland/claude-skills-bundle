# Paint Finish Troubleshooting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Paint troubleshooting: Runs and drips (overloaded brush or roller / sand smooth when dry, repaint) → Streaks and roller marks (visible roller texture — wrong pile for the surface / lay off gently while wet) → Brush marks in gloss (pressing too hard / too much paint / lay off lightly along the grain) → Peeling (poor preparation / painting over contamination — sand back to bare, prime, repaint) → Lap marks (dry edge painted over — maintain wet edge) → Grinning (old colour showing through — more coats needed or better primer required) → Bleed-through (resin or stains — shellac or stain-blocking primer).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Runs: overloading is the cause — load less, apply more coats
- Peeling: always a preparation failure — no shortcut except stripping and starting again
- Lap marks: entirely preventable by maintaining a wet edge

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
