# Colour Selection for Home Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Colour selection: Undertones (every colour has an undertone — a grey may be blue, green, or pink / hold it against a true neutral to see the undertone) → Light testing (paint a large sample (A3 or bigger) on the actual wall — view in natural and artificial light) → Colour flow (rooms that connect should have related colours — not jarring contrast) → Small room colours (light colours open a room / dark colours can make a small room dramatic rather than oppressive) → Ceiling colour (bright white adds height / tone down with a pale version of the wall colour for cosiness) → Test sample before committing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Large sample test: a 10cm test pot square tells you very little — paint a large sample
- Undertone testing: the cause of most colour disappointments — check undertones
- Natural and artificial light: colours look completely different under different lighting
