# Wall Preparation for Painting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: complete beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wall preparation: Fill holes (filler, sand flush when dry) → Sand rough areas (key the surface — paint adheres to a scuffed surface) → Clean the walls (sugar soap removes grease and nicotine — essential for paint adhesion) → Repair cracks (hairline cracks: flexible filler / larger cracks: raking out and filling) → Prime bare plaster/filler (PVA or mist coat — seals the porous surface) → Masking tape (apply to skirting, architrave, and ceiling line if needed) → Remove light switch plates and outlet covers.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sugar soap cleaning: skip it and paint peels within months on greasy walls
- Prime bare areas: unpainted filler and plaster drink in paint — primer prevents this
- Fill and sand before masking: masking before filling wastes tape and time
