# Knitting Garments Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Knitting garments: Gauge swatching (wash and block before measuring) → Construction methods (top-down raglan / set-in sleeve / yoke / seamless vs seamed) → Top-down (try on as you go) → Seamed (more structure and shaping) → Sizing (ease: positive for relaxed / negative for fitted) → Reading pattern schematic.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wash and block the swatch: unwashed gauge is not garment gauge
- Top-down try-on: the key advantage of seamless construction
- Check ease in the pattern: ease is difference between body and garment measurement

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
