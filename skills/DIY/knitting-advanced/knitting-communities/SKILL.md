# Knitting Community and Resources Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Knitting community: Ravelry (patterns/yarns/forums/project records) → Local knitting groups → YouTube tutorials → Test knitting → Knit-alongs → Local yarn shops (hub of local knitting knowledge).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ravelry project pages: the most useful record of previous projects
- YouTube for techniques: visual techniques are much clearer in video
- Local yarn shop: often the best local knitting knowledge resource

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
