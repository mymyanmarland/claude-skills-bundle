# Lace Knitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Lace knitting: Foundation (knit/purl/yarn over/k2tog/ssk) → Reading lace charts → Yarn over as decorative increase (creates a hole deliberately) → Paired decreases (k2tog leans right / ssk leans left) → Lifelines (waste yarn through live stitches every 10 rows) → Blocking (must be blocked to open the pattern — pins on foam mats) → Smooth yarn shows the pattern / mohair obscures it.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lifelines are non-negotiable: lace is difficult to rip back without one
- Blocking reveals the lace: unblocked lace looks nothing like the finished fabric
- Smooth yarn for lace: fuzzy yarn obscures the pattern entirely

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
