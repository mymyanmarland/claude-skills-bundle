# Cable Knitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Cable knitting: Cables (stitches swapped in position by holding on a cable needle) → Cable needle technique → C4F (hold front) vs C4B (hold back) → No cable needle method for experienced knitters → Twist stitch (2-stitch cable without needle) → Reading cable charts → Gauge warning (cables pull in dramatically — swatch over the cable pattern).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Cable swatch over the cable pattern: cables dramatically change gauge
- C4F twists left / C4B twists right: front vs back determines direction
- No cable needle method: faster once confident — practise on swatches first

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
