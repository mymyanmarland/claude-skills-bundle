# Sock Knitting Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sock knitting: 4-ply sock yarn (nylon content adds durability) → Needles (DPNs/two circulars/magic loop) → Cuff / leg / heel flap and gusset / foot / toe decreases → Kitchener stitch (graft toe closed — practise on waste yarn first) → Second sock syndrome (cast on immediately after completing the first).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Kitchener stitch: practise on waste yarn before the actual sock
- Magic loop or two circulars: more manageable than DPNs for many knitters
- Cast on second sock immediately: second sock syndrome is real

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
