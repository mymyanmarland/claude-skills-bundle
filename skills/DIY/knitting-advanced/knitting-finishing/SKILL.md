# Knitting Finishing Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Knitting finishing: Weaving in ends (diagonally / purl bumps on WS / both directions) → Mattress stitch (invisible seaming for stockinette) → Three-needle bind off (seaming while binding off) → Kitchener stitch → Wet blocking (soak/pin to measurements/dry fully) → Steam blocking → Button bands (even pick-up rate).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mattress stitch for side seams: the invisible seam that transforms finished knitwear
- Wet block to measurements: blocking changes the fabric completely
- Button band pick-up rate: too many or few stitches cause ruffling or puckering

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
