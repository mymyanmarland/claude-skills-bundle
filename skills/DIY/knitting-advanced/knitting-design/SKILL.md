# Designing Your Own Knitwear Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Knitwear design: Gauge as foundation → Sketch the design → Stitch counts from measurements (width × stitches per cm) → Shaping calculations → Sample knitting (note everything) → Test knitting (someone else tests) → Pattern writing conventions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gauge as foundation: every stitch count comes from the gauge swatch
- Note everything while knitting: memory of the process fades quickly
- Test knitting by someone else: the designer cannot find their own errors

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
