# Watercolour Card Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Watercolour card making: Paper selection (watercolour cardstock 300gsm minimum — regular cardstock buckles) → Masking (mask off areas with masking fluid / frisket film for crisp edges) → Wet on wet (soft blooms / background washes) → Wet on dry (crisper edges / detail work) → Ink sprays (spritz water over dried ink — creates bloom effects) → Stamping on watercolour (stamp first with waterproof ink / then paint over without disturbing the line) → Distress inks (water-reactive dye inks / blend and spritz for backgrounds).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Watercolour cardstock: regular cardstock buckles and pills — use watercolour specific
- Waterproof ink for stamping: water-based inks bleed when you paint over them
- Masking fluid: test on scrap — some masking fluid pulls the paper surface on removal

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
