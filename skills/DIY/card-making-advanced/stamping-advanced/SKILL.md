# Advanced Rubber and Clear Stamping Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced stamping: Resist technique (stamp with embossing ink / emboss / paint over — embossed area resists the ink) → Stamp masking (stamp an image / cover with a copy cut out / stamp overlapping image / remove mask — creates a behind-and-in-front effect) → Ghost stamping (stamp with barely any ink for a faded background effect) → Direct to paper (rub ink pad directly on the paper for backgrounds) → Stamp positioner (guarantees perfect placement for stamping in a window) → Two-step stamping (shadow stamp first / then image stamp on top / creates depth).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ghost stamping creates depth: a full-strength background competes with the focal image
- Stamp masking: creates perspective — one element appears behind another
- Direct to paper inking: creates soft textured backgrounds faster than painting

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
