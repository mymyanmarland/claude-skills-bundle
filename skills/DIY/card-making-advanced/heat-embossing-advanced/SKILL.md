# Advanced Heat Embossing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Heat embossing: Materials (embossing ink / embossing powders / heat tool) → Stamp application (press stamp into embossing ink / stamp onto card) → Powder application (pour embossing powder over wet ink / tap off excess / return excess to pot) → Heat application (heat tool held 5-10cm away / move continuously until powder melts and becomes glossy) → Powder varieties (clear / white / gold / silver / chunky glitter / detail) → No-stamp embossing (write with an embossing pen directly / pour powder / heat) → Embossing folders (a different technique — texture plate pressed in a die-cutting machine).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Move the heat tool: holding still burns the powder and the card
- Tap off excess thoroughly: stray powder grains melt as unintended dots
- Embossing pen for lettering: write directly then emboss — clean and crisp results

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
