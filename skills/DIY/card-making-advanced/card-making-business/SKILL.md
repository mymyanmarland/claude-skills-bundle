# Selling Handmade Cards Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Card making business: Pricing (materials + packaging + time at fair rate + platform fees / most handmade card makers underprice) → Streamlining production (design-led batches: make 12 of the same design more efficiently than 12 different cards) → Photography (natural light / clean background / show the inside of the card too) → Platforms (Etsy / local markets / Instagram for marketing / not-for-profit craft fairs for community) → Packaging (cello bags / backing card / simple branding) → Licensing designs (bought stamps — check licence for commercial use).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stamp licensing: most stamp manufacturers permit commercial use — verify yours
- Batch production: making 12 of one design is far more efficient than 12 different ones
- Show the inside: buyers want to see the complete product

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
