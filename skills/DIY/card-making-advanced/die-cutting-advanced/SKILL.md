# Advanced Die Cutting Techniques Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced die cutting: Sandwich construction (cutting plates / die / paper / shimming for different paper weights) → Intricate dies (thicker cutting plate / slower pass / slight shimming) → Wafer thin dies (embossing folder plate on top) → Layered dies (cut multiple times in different papers / assemble in layers) → Negative die (the cut-out frame is as useful as the die-cut shape — use both) → Stencils from dies (apply ink through the negative) → Debossing (die on top of the card / emboss folder underneath — creates an impression without cutting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Shimming for intricate dies: an extra cardstock shim improves cut quality dramatically
- Use the negative: the frame leftover from die cutting is a useful mask or stencil
- Debossing: die on top creates an impressed design without cutting through

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
