# Mixed Media Card Making Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Mixed media cards: Gesso (prime the base card for mixed media / allows paint to adhere to slick surfaces) → Texture paste (spread through a stencil / add texture to the background / dry before painting) → Alcohol inks (dropped onto slick surfaces / spread with a straw / unpredictable and beautiful) → Distress oxides (water-reactive / blend with water / blend colours directly on the card) → Fabric and ribbon (adhere with gel medium / complement without overwhelming) → Embellishments (sequins / buttons / wire / dried flowers) → Restraint (mixed media can become too busy — one or two special mediums per card).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gesso first: mixed media over bare card produces uneven and muddy results
- Restraint: the temptation is to use everything — one or two mediums is more elegant
- Alcohol inks on slick surfaces only: they pool on absorbent surfaces
