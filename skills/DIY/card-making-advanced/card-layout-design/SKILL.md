# Card Layout and Composition Design Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Card layout design: Rule of thirds (divide the card into thirds / place the focal element at an intersection) → Focal point (one main element — the eye needs somewhere to rest) → White space (resist filling every space — white space creates elegance) → Colour rule of three (3-colour cards are harmonious / more creates visual noise) → The triangle (arrange elements in a triangle for visual stability) → Layering (build up layers for depth / shadow stamps / foam pads) → Repetition (repeating a small element ties the design together) → Clean lines vs shabby (decide on a style and commit).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- One focal point: two competing focal points create tension not interest
- White space is not wasted space: restraint is harder than filling — and usually more elegant
- Rule of three colours: a starter rule for harmonious cards

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
