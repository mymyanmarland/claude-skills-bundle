# Card Making Tools Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Card making tools: Essential starter tools (craft knife and cutting mat / metal ruler / bone folder / corner rounder / tweezers / adhesive range: tape runner / foam pads / liquid glue) → Stamping tools (clear acrylic blocks for clear stamps / rubber block for rubber stamps) → Cutting machines (manual: Sizzix Big Shot / electronic: Cricut / Silhouette — range of price and capability) → Organisation (dies in magnetic sheets / inks in stackable trays / stamps in binders) → Maintenance (clean stamps after every use / replace cutting mats when grooves cause miscuts / store inks flat to prevent drying).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Clean stamps after every use: dried ink is difficult to remove and damages the stamp
- Metal ruler for scoring: plastic rulers compress rather than score cleanly
- Store inks flat: upright storage causes ink to settle away from the pad

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
