# Card Making for Children Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal / gift / sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Children's card making: Safety first (age-appropriate tools / rounded scissors for under 7 / no heat tools for under 10 / adult supervision for any sharp tools) → Age-appropriate techniques (3-5: finger printing / sponging / hand cutting simple shapes / 6-9: simple stamping / tearing paper / collage / 10+: basic die cutting / simple stamping with embossing) → Process over product (the making matters more than the result — celebrate what they make) → Accessible materials (thick card / bold stamps / simple die cuts / no fiddly small pieces) → Display the work (children's cards should be received and displayed with enthusiasm).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age-appropriate tools: safety scissors and rounded tools for young children
- Process over product: the making is the point — resist correcting
- No heat tools under 10: heat embossing tools reach dangerous temperatures

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.

---
📌 Note: This content is for educational and creative purposes. Always follow product safety instructions, work in a well-ventilated area, and keep sharp tools and chemicals away from children. Some techniques involve sharp tools or strong adhesives — use appropriate protective equipment.
