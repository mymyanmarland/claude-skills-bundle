# Embroidery on Clothing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Embroidery on clothing: Stabiliser (tear-away for woven / cut-away for knits) → Hoop the stabiliser and float the garment on top → Iron-on or traced placement → Flat stitches lie better on worn items → Colourfast thread → Wash instructions considered before selecting thread.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stabiliser always: without it fabric puckers under the stitching
- Flat stitches for worn clothing: raised embroidery catches and pulls in daily wear
- Colourfastness: test thread before using on something to be washed

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
