# Contemporary Embroidery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Contemporary embroidery: Any subject is valid → Photo-realistic (tiny stitches / long and short shading) → Abstract (non-representational / texture and mark-making) → Text in embroidery → Mixed media (fabric/embroidery/photography combined) → Alternative materials → Instagram embroidery community.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Any subject is valid: contemporary embroidery has no restrictions
- Photo-realistic: treated like painting in thread — stitch direction follows form
- Instagram embroidery community: the most active contemporary embroidery community

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
