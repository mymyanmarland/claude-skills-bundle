# Crewelwork Embroidery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crewelwork: Crewel wool / linen twill / crewel needles → Long and short stitch shading (alternating in first row / long only in subsequent) → Laid fillings (diagonal laid with couching at intersections) → Trellis couching → Bullion knots → Scale (bold and large suits the wool thread) → Nature-inspired patterns.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Long and short stitch for shading: the most important crewelwork stitch to master
- Linen twill: the traditional base that holds the wool stitches
- Bold designs suit crewelwork: the scale of wool thread demands bold imagery

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
