# Silk Ribbon Embroidery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Silk ribbon embroidery: 4mm and 7mm silk ribbon / chenille needles → Threading (pierce ribbon end with the needle to lock) → Ribbon stitch (insert needle through the ribbon for a curled petal) → Japanese ribbon stitch → French knots with ribbon → Keep ribbon untwisted → Applications (floral / textile jewellery / crazy quilting).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lock thread through the needle: the standard ribbon threading technique
- Keep the ribbon untwisted: twisted ribbon loses its dimensional quality
- Ribbon stitch petal curl: entering through the ribbon creates the characteristic curl
