# Stumpwork Embroidery Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stumpwork: Wire detached elements (stitch over wire / cut from main fabric / attach at one end) → Buttonhole stitch (foundation for most detached elements) → Slip (worked flat / cut out / reattached) → Felt and string padding → Wired petals → Detached needlelace → Build from background to foreground.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Background to foreground: detached elements placed last cover the background
- Wire gauge for elements: too heavy and petals cannot curl / too fine and they collapse
- Buttonhole stitch is the foundation: most detached elements use it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
