# Embroidery Design Transfer Methods Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Design transfer: Light box tracing (water-soluble pen on light fabric) → Carbon transfer paper → Iron-on transfer pencil → Printed soluble stabiliser (print and dissolve) → Prick and pounce (traditional / for dark fabrics) → Test water-soluble pen on scrap first.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Test the pen on scrap: some pens are difficult to remove from certain fabrics
- Water-soluble stabiliser for complex designs: precise printing of the design
- Prick and pounce for dark fabrics: traditional method when fabric cannot be seen through

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
