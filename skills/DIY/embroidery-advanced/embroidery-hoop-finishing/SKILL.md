# Embroidery Hoop Finishing and Framing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Embroidery finishing: Hoop display (tighten outer / trim to 2cm / fold and glue or lace back) → Lacing for framing (lace fabric over mount board / tension evenly) → Washing (hand wash gently / block flat to dry) → Conservation glass (prevents fading / embroidery should not touch glass) → Mounting on card.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tension even across the whole piece: uneven lacing distorts the design
- Conservation glass: prevents UV fading
- Wash before mounting: sizing and handling marks removed before final presentation
