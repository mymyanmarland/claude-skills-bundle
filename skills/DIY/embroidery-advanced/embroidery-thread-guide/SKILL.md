# Embroidery Thread Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Embroidery thread: Stranded cotton (6 strands / separate for fine / 1-2 for fine work / 4-6 for bold) → Perle (twisted / not separable / bold texture) → Silk (lustrous / finer / more expensive) → Wool (crewel / tapestry) → Metallic (short lengths / mellowed with other threads) → Specialty (variegated/overdyed) → Store out of light.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Short lengths of metallic: longer lengths tangle and fray
- Separate strands before threading: do not just pull from the skein
- Store out of light: exposure fades threads over time
