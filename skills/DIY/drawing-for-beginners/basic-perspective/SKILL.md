# Basic Perspective Drawing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Perspective drawing: Horizon line (eye level) → Vanishing point (where parallel lines converge) → One-point perspective (looking straight at a wall) → Two-point perspective (seeing a corner) → Vertical lines stay vertical → Ellipses in perspective (circles become ellipses) → Common mistakes (vanishing points too close / ignoring the horizon).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Horizon line first: always establish eye level before drawing anything
- Vertical lines stay vertical: the most common beginner perspective error
- Wide vanishing point spacing: points too close create distortion

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
