# Gesture Drawing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Gesture drawing: Purpose (capture action and energy — not anatomy) → Time limits (30s/1min/2min — forces decisions) → Line of action (one flowing line through the whole figure) → Rhythm lines (sweeping curves through body) → Online resources (Line of Action / SenshiStock / pose apps) → 100 gestures in a session trains the eye.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Line of action first: the flowing line that defines pose energy
- Time limits force priorities: choose what matters most
- 100 gestures per session: volume is the gesture training method

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
