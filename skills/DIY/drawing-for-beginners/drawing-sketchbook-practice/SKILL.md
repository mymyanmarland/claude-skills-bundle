# Sketchbook Practice Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Sketchbook practice: Remove pressure (sketchbook is for practice not finished work) → Daily minimum (one page / 15 minutes) → Subject variety → Studies vs exploration → Date every entry → Different sketchbooks for different purposes → Review old sketchbooks (shows real progress).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sketchbook is for practice: this removes the paralysis that ruins progress
- Date every entry: progress visible over months is the best motivation
- Review old sketchbooks: the most honest measure of improvement

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
