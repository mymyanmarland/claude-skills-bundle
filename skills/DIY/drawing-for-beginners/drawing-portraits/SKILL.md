# Drawing Portraits Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Portrait drawing: Oval head shape → Placement (eyes: halfway down / nose: halfway between eyes and chin / mouth: one-third up from chin) → Eye structure (almond / iris partly hidden by lid) → Nose (nostrils and tip — avoid outlining the bridge) → Mouth (upper lip darker / corners slightly down at rest) → Build from light to dark.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Eyes halfway down the head: the single most corrected error in portrait drawing
- Avoid outlining the nose bridge: draw shadow shapes instead
- Upper lip darker: the lighting difference makes the mouth readable

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
