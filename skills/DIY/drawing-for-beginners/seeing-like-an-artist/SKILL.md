# Seeing Like an Artist Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Observational drawing: Symbolic drawing problem (beginners draw what they think they see — symbols not actual shapes) → Negative space drawing (draw the spaces between objects) → Contour drawing (slow deliberate line-following without looking at the paper) → Upside-down drawing (turns image into abstract shapes — disables the symbolic brain) → Five skills of drawing (edges/spaces/relationships/light and shadow/the gestalt) → Daily 10-minute practice beats one hour weekly.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Negative space: immediately improves accuracy by shifting focus from symbols to shapes
- Upside-down drawing: the most immediate proof that drawing is about looking not skill
- Draw daily for 10 minutes: consistency matters more than duration

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
