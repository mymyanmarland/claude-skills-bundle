# Proportion and Measurement in Drawing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Proportion and measurement: Pencil measuring (arm's length / close one eye / use as a ruler) → Sighting angles (pencil checks angles against vertical) → Head as a unit (standard adult 7-8 heads tall) → Block-in method (large proportions first / details last) → Checking relationships (compare elements to each other) → Grid method (divide reference and paper equally).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pencil measuring at arm's length: the most transferable measuring technique
- Block-in first: committing to detail before proportions always causes errors
- Grid method for accuracy: use to learn — then try without it

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
