# Drawing Texture Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Art experience level
- Specific technique or project
- Materials and tools available
- Intended use: personal/exhibition/sale
- Any particular style or reference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Drawing texture: Texture is light and shadow at small scale → Wood grain (parallel curved lines / knots as ellipses) → Fabric (soft curves / wrinkles radiate from tension points) → Metal (high contrast / sharp highlights / reflections) → Glass (light and dark simultaneously) → Fur (draw clumps not individual hairs) → Stone (shadows in mortar lines).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Texture is small-scale light and shadow: understanding this simplifies every texture
- Fabric wrinkles radiate from tension points: gravity and pulling create patterns
- Fur in clumps not individual hairs: individual lines never look like fur
