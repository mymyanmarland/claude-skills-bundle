# Advanced Granny Squares Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Advanced granny squares: Variations (traditional/solid/sunburst/lace) → Colour planning before crocheting a single square → Join as you go JAYG (no sewing required) → Garments from granny squares → Tension (all squares must be same size — swatch each colour combination).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Join as you go: eliminates sewing hundreds of seams
- Colour plan before crocheting: randomising after rarely achieves the intended layout
- Tension per colour: different yarns crochet differently — swatch each

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
