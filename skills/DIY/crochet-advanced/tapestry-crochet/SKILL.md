# Tapestry Crochet Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tapestry crochet: Carry unused colours along the row / crochet over them → Reading tapestry charts → Single crochet tapestry → Consistent tension for carried colours → Change colour on last pull-through of previous stitch → Larger hook than usual (carrying tightens fabric).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Change colour on last pull-through: clean colour changes require this timing
- Larger hook for tapestry: carrying colours tightens the fabric — compensate
- Tension consistent for carried colours: tight carries distort the pattern
