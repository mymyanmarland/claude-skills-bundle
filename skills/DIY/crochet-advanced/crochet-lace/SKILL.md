# Crochet Lace Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crochet lace: Filet crochet (grid of open and filled squares / chart-based) → Irish crochet (3D motifs joined with net background) → Brugge lace → Thread crochet (fine cotton / steel hooks) → Block all lace crochet → Filet charts: read bottom right to left on RS.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Block all lace crochet: unblocked is unrecognisable
- Steel hooks for thread crochet: regular hooks too large
- Filet chart reading: bottom right corner is the start

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
