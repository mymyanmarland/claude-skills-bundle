# Crocheting Garments Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crochet garments: Gauge (changes with every hook size and stitch — swatch every project) → Construction methods (top-down/bottom-up/seamless/seamed) → Seaming (slip stitch/single crochet/mattress stitch) → Sizing (positive ease for most) → Neckline pick-up → Armhole finishing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gauge for every project: varies more than knitting — never skip the swatch
- Seaming is a learnable skill: poorly seamed garments look handmade
- Armhole finish: unfinished armholes stretch out of shape

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
