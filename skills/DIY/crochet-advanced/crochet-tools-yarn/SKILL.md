# Advanced Crochet Tools and Yarn Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crochet tools and yarn: Hook materials (aluminium/bamboo/ergonomic) → Inline vs tapered (affects stitch definition and tension) → Yarn fibre (cotton/linen: structured / wool: springy / acrylic: budget and washable) → Locking stitch markers for crochet → Row counter apps.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ergonomic hooks for long sessions: repetitive strain is a real risk
- Locking stitch markers: open ring markers fall out of crochet stitches
- Inline vs tapered: try both and choose — both have strong advocates

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
