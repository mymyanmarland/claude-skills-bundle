# Amigurumi Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Amigurumi: Magic ring → Working in continuous rounds → Invisible decrease (neater than sc2tog) → Stitch marker every round → Stuff as you go → Safety eyes (insert before closing / washer secures) → Joining parts with ladder stitch → Embroidered eyes for children under 3 — not safety eyes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Stitch marker every round: continuous rounds lose the start easily
- Invisible decrease: neater than sc2tog — the amigurumi standard
- Embroidered eyes for under-3 toys: safety eyes are a choking hazard

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
