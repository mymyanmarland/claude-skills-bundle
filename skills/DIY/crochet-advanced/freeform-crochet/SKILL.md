# Freeform Crochet Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Craft experience level
- Specific project or technique
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Intended use: personal/gift/sale

## Step 2 — Core Output

Freeform crochet: No fixed pattern — intuitive and exploratory → Scrumbling (small sections of different stitches joined) → Texture variety (bobbles/popcorns/puff/shells/clusters) → Building fabric (patches joined / fill gaps) → 3D sculptural applications → Wearable art.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scrumbling approach: small patches — more manageable than working freely
- Texture variety: the interest comes from extreme stitch contrast
- No wrong approach: freeform is intentionally free from rules

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
