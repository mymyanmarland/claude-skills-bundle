# Luxury Vinyl Tile and Plank Installation Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

LVT/LVP installation: Type selection (click LVT: floating floor — no adhesive / glue-down LVT: adhered directly — flatter result / peel-and-stick: simplest) → Subfloor requirement (LVT is thinner than laminate — requires a flatter subfloor: 2mm tolerance over 1800mm) → Click installation (same method as laminate / smaller expansion gap 5-8mm) → Glue-down (pressure-sensitive adhesive spread with a notched trowel / allow to tack before laying) → Stair nosings and transitions (purpose-made profiles) → Around doors (undercut door frame liners — flush fitting looks professional).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Flatter subfloor than laminate: LVT is thin and unforgiving of lumps and hollows
- Click vs glue-down: glue-down is flatter and more stable in temperature extremes
- Undercut door frames: a professional finish that beginners often miss
