# Floor Maintenance and Care Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Floor maintenance: Laminate (no wet mopping / damp mop only / protect legs with felt pads / clean spills immediately) → LVT/LVP (sweep or vacuum / damp mop / avoid abrasive cleaners / no steam mop on click LVT) → Solid wood (sweep and dry buff / oil-finished: re-oil annually / varnished: damp mop only / no steam) → Tile (regular cleaning with appropriate cleaner for tile type / re-grout if grout is damaged / reseal natural stone annually) → Carpet (hoover twice weekly / professional clean every 1-2 years / treat stains immediately with cold water and mild detergent).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- No steam mop on click flooring: steam forces moisture into the join and causes swelling
- Felt pads on furniture legs: scratches from furniture are the most common floor damage
- Reseal natural stone annually: unsealed stone stains permanently
