# Floor Transition Strips and Profiles Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Transition strips: Profile types (T-bar: two floors at same height / reducer: different height floors / carpet edge: carpet meets hard floor / end cap: floor to wall or step edge) → Material match (same brand and finish as the flooring where possible) → Installation methods (track-and-snap: insert track / cover with the profile / click in / or direct screw) → Door thresholds (the most common transition location — measure the door frame gap accurately) → Expansion coverage (T-bar profiles often cover the expansion gap between two floating floors) → Cutting (hacksaw or mitre saw at 45 degrees for corners).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Profile type must match the height difference: wrong profile looks wrong and catches underfoot
- Expansion gap coverage: ensure the T-bar covers both floors' expansion gaps
- 45-degree corners: mitre the profile for internal corners — butt joints look amateurish

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
