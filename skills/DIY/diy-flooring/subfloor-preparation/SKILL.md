# Subfloor Preparation for Flooring Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Subfloor prep: Level check (lay a straight edge / acceptable tolerance: 3mm over 1800mm for most click flooring) → Levelling compound (self-levelling compound poured into low areas / prime first if concrete subfloor) → High spots (grind or sand down — screed high spots) → Squeaks (secure loose floorboards from above with screws) → Moisture (damp proof membrane for concrete subfloors / check MC below 75% RH before laying engineered or solid wood) → Old floor removal (ceramic tiles: floor scraper / carpet: cut into strips / pull away from gripper rods) → Clean (vacuum and damp mop — dust prevents adhesive bonding).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Level is critical: humps and hollows cause click flooring to flex and unlock
- Moisture check on concrete: engineered wood over damp concrete will cup and buckle
- High spots cause more problems than low spots: grind them down before levelling

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
