# Solid Hardwood Flooring Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Solid hardwood flooring: Acclimatisation (2-4 weeks in the room — critical for solid wood) → Moisture management (solid wood should not go over underfloor heating without specific products / DPM on concrete essential) → Direction (lay parallel to the longest wall / perpendicular to joists on timber subfloor) → Secret nailing (nail gun through the tongue at 45 degrees into the joist / every joist / first and last rows face-nailed and filled) → Glue-down method (on concrete / solid wood adhesive applied with a trowel) → Sanding and finishing (new flooring often needs sanding after installation — belt sander).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Acclimatisation is essential for solid wood: skip it and the floor will gap in winter
- DPM on concrete: moisture destroys solid wood flooring — this is non-negotiable
- Secret nail into every joist: missing joists causes squeaking
