# Carpet Laying Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Carpet laying basics: Gripper rods (pin-faced / nail to floor 10mm from walls / cut with a hacksaw / teeth pointing toward the wall) → Underlay (cover the whole floor / cut to the inside edge of the gripper / tape joins) → Cutting carpet (roll out / rough cut 100mm oversize all around) → Fitting one wall (hook onto gripper rod / cut into corners) → Stretching (knee kicker across the room / hook onto opposite gripper / work systematically) → Trimming (bolster or wide chisel to push into the gap between gripper and wall / trim with carpet knife) → Doorways (smooth bar or gripper rod at the threshold).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Gripper teeth toward wall: pointing away allows the carpet to hook over
- Knee kicker for stretching: unstretched carpet ripples within months
- Trim into the gap: pushing the edge between gripper and wall creates a neat finish

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
