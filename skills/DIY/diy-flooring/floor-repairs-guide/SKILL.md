# Floor Repairs Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Floor repairs: Laminate (chips: colour-matched repair paste / individual board replacement — remove from edge to the damaged board) → LVT (chips: repair with adhesive and colour filler / board replacement: click boards replace easily from an edge) → Solid wood (deep scratches: fill with coloured wax filler / surface scratches: sand and refinish locally or refinish the whole floor) → Tile (replace a cracked tile: remove grout / chisel out tile / replace / regrout) → Carpet (patch repair: cut a square over the damage / cut a matching patch / secure with double-sided tape).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Laminate board replacement from the edge: laminate cannot be repaired in the middle
- Colour match for fillers: test before applying to a visible area
- Carpet patch from a hidden area: cut the replacement from inside a wardrobe for a colour match

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
