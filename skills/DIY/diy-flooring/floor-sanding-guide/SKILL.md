# Floor Sanding and Finishing Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Skill level: beginner / some experience / confident DIYer
- Specific task or project
- Materials and tools available
- Budget
- Any safety or structural concerns

## Step 2 — Core Output

Floor sanding: Preparation (check for protruding nails — punch all nail heads below the surface / fill large gaps with flexible wood filler) → Belt sander (hire a drum sander / 40 grit for initial cut / 60 / 80 / 120 grit progression) → Direction (diagonally first on very rough floors / then along the grain) → Edge sander (sands the perimeter the drum cannot reach) → Detail work (hand sanding in corners / profile sand on mouldings) → Dust management (seal gaps under doors / wear mask) → Finishing (oil / hard wax oil / varnish — oil is most forgiving for DIY) → Between coats (fine sanding with 240 or white scotch pad).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Punch all nails first: a nail tears the sanding belt instantly
- Progress through grits: skipping grits leaves scratches visible through the finish
- Oil is most forgiving for DIY: varnish shows every imperfection

Always offer a relevant next step or tip to try.
