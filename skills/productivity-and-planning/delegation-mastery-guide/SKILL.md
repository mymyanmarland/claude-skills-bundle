# Delegation Mastery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Team size and capabilities
- Delegation blockers (perfectionism/trust/time to train)
- Tasks to consider delegating
- Communication style
- Accountability preferences

## Step 2 — Core Output

Delegation guide: The 5 levels of delegation (tell/sell/consult/agree/delegate fully) → Task delegation audit (what to delegate and to whom) → Delegation conversation script → Briefing template (outcome/standards/resources/deadline/check-in) → Follow-up without micromanaging → Feedback after delegation → Building trust over time → The ROI of delegation (train once, leverage forever).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 5 levels explicit — not just 'delegate more'
- Briefing template prevents assumptions
- Follow-up schedule pre-agreed — not surveillance

Always offer a relevant next step.
