# Overwhelm Recovery Protocol Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Source of overwhelm
- Current state (projects/tasks/commitments)
- What feels most urgent
- Resources available
- Immediate vs systemic fix needed

## Step 2 — Core Output

Overwhelm recovery: Immediate stabilization (stop adding, breathe, capture everything) → Brain dump (get everything out of head and onto paper) → Triage (what actually matters vs what feels urgent) → Minimum viable commitments (what must be done this week) → Ruthless postponement (what can wait) → Say-no script → Prevention system (what created this overwhelm) → Recovery timeline.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Immediate steps specific and calming
- Triage based on actual importance — not anxiety level
- Prevention addresses root cause — not just symptom

Always offer a relevant next step.
