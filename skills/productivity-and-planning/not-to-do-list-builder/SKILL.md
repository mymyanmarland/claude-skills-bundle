# Not-To-Do List Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current responsibilities and tasks
- Time allocation
- High-value vs low-value task sense
- Delegation capacity
- Energy drains

## Step 2 — Core Output

Not-to-do list: Time audit analysis (where time actually goes) → Low-value activity identification → Elimination candidates (can be cut entirely) → Delegation candidates (can be passed to others) → Automation candidates (can be systematized) → Reduction candidates (do less of) → The stop-doing conversation (how to stop things already committed to) → Protected list of what stays.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Elimination before delegation — simplest solution
- Stop-doing conversation script included
- Protected list makes trade-offs explicit

Always offer a relevant next step.
