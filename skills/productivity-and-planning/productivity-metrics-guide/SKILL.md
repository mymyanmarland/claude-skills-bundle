# Productivity Metrics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Role and work type
- Goals being measured toward
- Current measurement approach
- Vanity metric habits
- Decision frequency from metrics

## Step 2 — Core Output

Productivity metrics: Leading vs lagging indicators (what predicts output vs what results from it) → Output metrics by work type (creative: pieces completed / knowledge: decisions made / operational: throughput) → Depth metric (% time in deep work) → Weekly scorecard template → What NOT to measure (busyness metrics) → Monthly metric review → Adjusting metrics as goals change.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Output metrics — not activity metrics
- Leading indicators tracked — not just outcomes
- What NOT to measure explicit — busyness metrics removed

Always offer a relevant next step.
