# One Thing Method Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goals in work and life
- Current activities
- Time allocation
- Biggest results to achieve
- Distraction patterns

## Step 2 — Core Output

ONE Thing application: Focusing question ('What is the ONE Thing I can do such that by doing it everything else will be easier or unnecessary?') → Applied to different time horizons (today/week/year/career/life) → Domino identification (what knocks others down) → Time blocking for ONE Thing first → Saying no to protect the ONE Thing → Goal-setting to the now (from purpose to priority to today).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Focusing question applied at multiple time horizons
- ONE Thing always scheduled first — not fit in around other work
- Goal-setting to the now connects big goal to today's action

Always offer a relevant next step.
