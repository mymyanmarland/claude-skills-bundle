# Energy Management Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Natural energy pattern (morning/afternoon/evening)
- Work type (creative/analytical/administrative)
- Sleep schedule
- Physical activity habits
- Current energy problems

## Step 2 — Core Output

Energy management plan: Chronotype assessment → Energy mapping (high/medium/low throughout the day) → Work type matching (high energy = deep creative/analytical, medium = meetings/collaboration, low = admin/email) → Recovery protocols (micro-breaks, movement, nutrition timing) → Energy drains audit → Weekly energy investment (what restores vs depletes) → Monthly energy review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Work matched to energy — not just time availability
- Recovery protocols specific and brief
- Energy drains audit honest — includes pleasant but depleting activities

Always offer a relevant next step.
