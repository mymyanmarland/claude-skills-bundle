# Slow Productivity Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current workload and pace
- Sustainability concerns
- Quality vs quantity trade-offs
- Professional constraints
- Long-term output goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Slow productivity application: Do fewer things (active commitment reduction) → Work at a natural pace (remove artificial urgency) → Obsess over quality (fewer outputs, much better) → Workload audit and reduction plan → Pseudo-productivity trap identification → Inbox management to reduce commitments → Saying no system → Long game metrics (year-scale output, not daily output).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Commitment reduction concrete — specific things to exit
- Pseudo-productivity activities named specifically
- Long game metrics replace daily busyness metrics

Always offer a relevant next step.
