# Creative Block Buster Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Creative discipline
- Block duration and severity
- Last time flow worked
- Deadline pressure
- Suspected cause

## Step 2 — Core Output

Creative block toolkit: Block diagnosis (fear/perfectionism/boredom/resource depletion/unclear brief) → Intervention per type → Quantity over quality practice (draft without judgment) → Constraint exercise (creative freedom through limits) → Input renewal (consume before creating) → Physical movement as creative catalyst → Lower the stakes (play, not produce) → Re-entry ritual after block.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Diagnosis before intervention — causes differ
- Constraints as creativity tools — not just restrictions
- Re-entry ritual after block prevents re-blocking

Always offer a relevant next step.
