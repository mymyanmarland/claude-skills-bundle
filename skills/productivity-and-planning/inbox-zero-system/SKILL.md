# Inbox Zero System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Email volume
- Tools available (Gmail/Outlook/other)
- Role and communication demands
- Current inbox state
- Time willing to spend on email

## Step 2 — Core Output

Inbox zero system: Processing protocol (do/delegate/defer/delete/file) → Folder/label structure (minimal — 5 folders max) → Processing frequency (2-3 times daily — not constant) → Email writing that reduces replies (complete emails) → Unsubscribe sprint → Filter automation → Search-first approach (don't over-organize) → Weekly review of deferred items.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Processing frequency specific — not 'check less often'
- Folder structure minimal — search is more efficient
- Deferred items have a review cadence — not a black hole

Always offer a relevant next step.
