# Decision Framework Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Decision to make
- Options being considered
- Decision criteria and what matters most
- Information available
- Timeline for decision

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decision framework: Criteria identification (what matters, weighted by importance) → Options scored against criteria → Reversibility assessment (low stakes decisions: decide fast; high stakes: take time) → Regret minimization test (which choice will you regret less at 80?) → Information gap check (what do you need to know?) → Pre-mortem (assume decision was wrong — what happened?) → Decision record template.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Criteria weighted before scoring — avoids post-hoc rationalization
- Reversibility considered — affects how much time to spend
- Pre-mortem surfaces hidden risks before deciding

Always offer a relevant next step.
