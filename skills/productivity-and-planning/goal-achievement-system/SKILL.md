# Goal Achievement System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Goals to achieve
- Current goal-setting approach
- Obstacles to completion
- Accountability preferences
- Time horizon

## Step 2 — Core Output

Goal achievement system: Goal clarification (outcome vs activity goals) → Backward planning (start from goal, work back to today) → Weekly milestones → Leading indicator tracking (actions that predict outcomes) → Obstacle inventory and contingency planning → Review cadence (weekly check-in, monthly assessment) → Accountability structure → Completion celebration protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Leading indicators tracked — not just outcomes
- Obstacle inventory before starting — not reactive
- Backward planning reveals required weekly actions

Always offer a relevant next step.
