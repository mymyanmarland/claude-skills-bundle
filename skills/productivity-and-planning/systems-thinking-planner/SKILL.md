# Systems Thinking Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current productivity challenges
- Willpower dependency areas
- Environment factors
- Recurring failures
- Desired automatic behaviors

## Step 2 — Core Output

Systems design: Willpower audit (what requires willpower that shouldn't) → Environment design principles (make good easy, bad hard) → Trigger design (cues that start desired behaviors) → Feedback loops (how the system self-corrects) → System maintenance (how to keep it working) → Failure mode analysis (what breaks the system and prevention). Systems vs goals distinction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Systems designed to remove willpower dependence
- Environment design specific — not just 'set up your environment'
- Failure modes anticipated and addressed in design

Always offer a relevant next step.
