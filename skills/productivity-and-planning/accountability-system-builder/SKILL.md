# Accountability System Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- What needs accountability
- Self vs external accountability preference
- Reporting cadence
- Consequence system
- Current follow-through problems

## Step 2 — Core Output

Accountability system: Self-accountability (tracking + review + consequence) → Peer accountability (partner selection + check-in structure + what to share) → Public commitment (when and how to use) → Accountability app options → The commitment contract (written, specific, consequence agreed) → Streak maintenance vs reset recovery → Weekly accountability review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- System matched to accountability preference — not one-size
- Consequence for non-completion agreed upfront
- Recovery from broken streak protocol — failure isn't fatal

Always offer a relevant next step.
