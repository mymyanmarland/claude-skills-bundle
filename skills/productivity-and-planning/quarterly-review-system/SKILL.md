# Quarterly Review System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Areas to review: work/personal/health/relationships
- Goals set last quarter
- Tools used
- Time available for review
- Planning horizon

## Step 2 — Core Output

Quarterly review system: Review session structure (2-3 hours) → Past quarter analysis (wins/lessons/incomplete/abandon) → Life areas assessment (wheel of life or custom) → Theme for next quarter (one word or phrase) → Quarterly goals (3 max) → Monthly breakdown → Key projects and commitments → What to protect (non-negotiables next quarter) → Schedule the session now.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Session scheduled before the quarter ends — not on the last day
- Goals 3 maximum — more dilutes focus
- What to protect explicit — prevents default yes to everything

Always offer a relevant next step.
