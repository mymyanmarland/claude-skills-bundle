# Single Tasking Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Multitasking triggers
- Work environment demands
- Current single-task ability
- Technology habits
- Performance concerns about single-tasking

## Step 2 — Core Output

Single-tasking guide: Multitasking myth (it's sequential rapid switching — expensive) → Single-task commitment protocol → Physical environment for single focus → Browser tab discipline → Phone removal practice → The one-tab rule → Meeting single-tasking (close laptop, be present) → Single-task practice exercises → Weekly single-task improvement tracking.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Multitasking cost quantified — motivates the change
- Physical commitments specific — not just intentions
- Practice exercises build the habit gradually

Always offer a relevant next step.
