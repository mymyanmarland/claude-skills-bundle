# Creative Productivity System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Creative discipline (writing/design/music/etc)
- Project types and timelines
- Creative energy patterns
- Administrative burden
- Creative blocks experienced

## Step 2 — Core Output

Creative productivity system: Maker schedule vs manager schedule (no meetings in creative blocks) → Creative session start ritual → Warm-up practice → Protection of generative vs refinement phases → Administrative batching (away from creative time) → Creative constraint use (limits force creativity) → Fallow periods (rest is part of the system) → Project cycle management.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maker vs manager schedule distinction explicit
- Administrative tasks batched — not interspersed
- Rest and fallow periods legitimate — not guilt-inducing

Always offer a relevant next step.
