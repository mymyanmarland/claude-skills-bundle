# Shutdown Ritual Designer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Work end time
- Work type
- Biggest work-after-hours problems
- Home environment and responsibilities
- Current transition difficulty

## Step 2 — Core Output

Shutdown ritual: Open loops capture (tasks not completed → captured and scheduled) → Tomorrow's top 3 (decision made now, not in the morning) → Communication closure (email response / Slack status) → Physical signal (desk cleared or specific action) → Verbal declaration ('shutdown complete') → Transition activity → What counts as 'unavailable' tonight. Total time: 10-15 minutes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Ritual under 15 minutes — sustainable
- Verbal declaration trains brain to release work
- Tomorrow's top 3 decided now — morning brain not taxed

Always offer a relevant next step.
