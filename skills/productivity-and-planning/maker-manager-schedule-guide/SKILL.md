# Maker-Manager Schedule Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Role balance (maker/manager/hybrid)
- Current schedule
- Meeting load
- Deep work requirements
- Team coordination needs

## Step 2 — Core Output

Schedule design: Maker vs manager role identification → Current schedule audit (which type dominates) → Ideal allocation for role → Maker half-days (minimum units of creative work) → Manager time batching → Transition costs between types → Team norms for reaching you → Weekly schedule template → Calendar blocking strategy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Maker blocks minimum half-day — shorter blocks ineffective
- Manager time batched to preserve maker blocks
- Team norms for interruption established proactively

Always offer a relevant next step.
