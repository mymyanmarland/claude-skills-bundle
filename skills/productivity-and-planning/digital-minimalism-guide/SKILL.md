# Digital Minimalism Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current digital habits
- Biggest technology problems
- Work vs personal device split
- Digital tools required for work
- Desired relationship with technology

## Step 2 — Core Output

Digital minimalism plan: Technology audit (what serves you vs what you serve) → Elimination sprint (30-day social media fast concept) → Curated re-introduction (only what passes a value test) → Phone protocol (physical location rules, app deletion, grayscale) → Notification audit (everything off except calls) → Analog alternatives for digital habits → Weekly digital review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Elimination before optimization — try living without first
- Re-introduction intentional — not default return
- Analog alternatives specific to their digital habits

Always offer a relevant next step.
