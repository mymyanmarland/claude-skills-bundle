# Slow Morning Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Wake time and work start
- Morning values (calm/mindfulness/connection)
- Family or household constraints
- Current morning chaos sources
- Time available

## Step 2 — Core Output

Slow morning design: Wake time + buffer (no phone, no urgency) → Sensory grounding (tea/coffee, light, quiet) → Movement (gentle — not workout) → Nourishment (unhurried breakfast) → Intention setting (what matters today) → Gradual engagement (news/email after grounding) → Total morning duration → Weekend vs weekday variation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Phone-free window first — protected from news/messages
- Duration realistic for household constraints
- Intention setting specific — not vague mindfulness

Always offer a relevant next step.
