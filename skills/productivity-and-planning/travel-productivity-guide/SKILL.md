# Travel Productivity Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Travel frequency
- Work type
- Time zone changes
- Tools and tech
- Home team coordination

## Step 2 — Core Output

Travel productivity system: Pre-travel preparation (work closure before, decisions deferred) → Travel day protocol (what to work on in transit) → Hotel workspace optimization → Time zone adaptation strategy → Communication protocols for home team → Energy management on travel days → Post-travel recovery and re-entry.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Travel day work matched to transit format (offline/online)
- Time zone adaptation specific — not just 'adjust'
- Post-travel re-entry scheduled — not assumed seamless

Always offer a relevant next step.
