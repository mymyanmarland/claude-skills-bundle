# Attention Management Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Biggest distraction sources
- Work environment
- Technology habits
- Focus goals
- What 'focused work' looks like for this person

## Step 2 — Core Output

Attention management system: Distraction audit (what steals attention and when) → Phone protocol (settings, physical location, check schedule) → Digital environment design (website blockers, notification settings) → Physical environment optimization → Focus session structure (start ritual, duration, break) → Recovery from distraction (how to re-enter focus state) → Weekly attention review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Phone settings specific — not 'put your phone away'
- Distraction sources specific to this person's audit
- Re-entry protocol for after distraction — often forgotten

Always offer a relevant next step.
