# Morning Routine Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Wake time and work start time
- Morning window available
- Goals for the morning
- Current morning friction points
- Energy needs for the day

## Step 2 — Core Output

Morning routine design: No-phone protocol (length and why) → Movement (duration and type matched to available time) → Nutrition window → Planning moment (day preview, top 3 priorities) → Mindset practice (optional — journaling/meditation/reading) → Work startup ritual → Total time estimation. Minimum viable morning (15 min) + full morning (60 min) versions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Phone-free window justified and specific
- Minimum viable version for difficult days
- Routine serves the person — not a performance

Always offer a relevant next step.
