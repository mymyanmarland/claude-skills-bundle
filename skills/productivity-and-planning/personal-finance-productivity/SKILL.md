# Personal Finance Productivity Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Financial accounts and tools
- Income structure
- Bill types and timing
- Current financial friction
- Goals for automation level

## Step 2 — Core Output

Finance system: Automation setup (bills, savings transfers, investments) → Account architecture (checking/savings/sinking funds) → Monthly financial review (30 min) → Budget automation vs conscious spending decision → Bill calendar → Net worth tracking (quarterly) → Tax preparation system (year-round) → Financial dashboard setup.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Automation reduces required attention — not eliminates
- Monthly review only — not obsessive checking
- Tax preparation year-round — not February panic

Always offer a relevant next step.
