# Meeting Audit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current meeting load
- Meeting types
- Decision-making authority
- Team culture around meetings
- Ideal meeting hours per week

## Step 2 — Core Output

Meeting audit: Inventory (list every recurring meeting) → Necessity test per meeting (decision/alignment/brainstorm/status update — status = async) → Meeting diet (which to cancel/reduce frequency/shorten/delegate) → Async alternatives for eliminated meetings → Meeting-free block protection → How to say no to meetings professionally → Redesigned meeting cadence.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Status update meetings identified as async candidates
- 'How to say no' script practical and professional
- Meeting-free blocks specifically protected in calendar

Always offer a relevant next step.
