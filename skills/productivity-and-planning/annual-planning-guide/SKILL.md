# Annual Planning Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Areas to plan: work/personal/financial/health
- Previous year data
- Long-term vision
- Key themes or intentions
- Planning depth preference

## Step 2 — Core Output

Annual planning guide: Year in review (wins/disappointments/surprises/lessons) → Life areas assessment → Long-term vision check (3-5 year picture) → Annual theme → 3 annual goals (one per key life area) → Quarterly breakdown → Key experiences to protect (non-negotiable commitments) → Word of the year → Planning session structure (3-4 hours).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Review honest — disappointments included, not just wins
- Goals connect to long-term vision
- Annual theme guides decisions throughout the year

Always offer a relevant next step.
