# Review and Reflect System Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Review areas (work/personal/health/relationships)
- Time available per review type
- Review tools preference
- Current reflection practice
- Growth goals

## Step 2 — Core Output

Multi-cadence review: Daily (10 min — what happened, what's tomorrow) → Weekly (30 min — wins, lessons, priorities) → Monthly (60 min — progress on goals, life areas) → Quarterly (2-3 hours — big picture, goals review) → Annual (half day — year review, year design). Each: specific prompts, format, output. Prompts that generate insight — not just recap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Prompts generate insight — not just summarize
- Review cadence builds on previous level
- Each review has a specific output — not just reflection

Always offer a relevant next step.
