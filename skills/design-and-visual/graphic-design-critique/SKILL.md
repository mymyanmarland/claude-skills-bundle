# Graphic Design Critique Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Design critique: Critique structure (describe/analyze/interpret/judge) → Separating preference from principle → Objective criteria for good design → How to give actionable feedback (not 'I do not like it') → How to receive critique productively → Design critique language (specific, kind, useful) → Self-critique checklist before sharing work → Red lines (design principles vs stylistic preference).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Describe before judge — analysis before evaluation
- Objective criteria vs preference distinction
- Actionable feedback — what to change and why

Always offer a relevant next step.
