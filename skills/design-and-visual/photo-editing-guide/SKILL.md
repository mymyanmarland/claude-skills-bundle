# Photo Editing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Photo editing: Exposure correction (the foundation of good editing) → White balance and color temperature → Contrast and clarity → Highlight and shadow recovery → Color grading for brand consistency → The preset approach (one preset applied consistently) → Skin tones and portraits → Cropping and composition correction → Batch editing workflow → When to reshoot vs edit.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Exposure correction first — the foundation
- Preset for consistency — one style across all images
- Batch editing workflow — efficiency for volume content

Always offer a relevant next step.
