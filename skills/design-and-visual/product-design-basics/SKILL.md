# Product Design Basics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Product design basics: Human-centered design process (research/ideate/prototype/test) → Ergonomics fundamentals → Materials and manufacturing constraints → Prototype types (low-fidelity to high-fidelity) → Design for manufacturing (DFM) → Packaging integration → IP and design patent basics → Working with industrial designers → CAD and 3D printing for prototyping.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Human-centered design process — start with user
- DFM basics — beautiful products that cannot be manufactured
- IP protection basics — often ignored until too late

Always offer a relevant next step.
