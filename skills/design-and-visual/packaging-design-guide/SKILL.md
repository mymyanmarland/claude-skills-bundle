# Packaging Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Packaging design: Structural design basics (dielines) → Billboard effect (how it looks on shelf vs in hand) → Hierarchy (brand/product/flavor/claim) → Typography for packaging (legible at distance and up close) → Color system for product lines → Legal requirements (ingredients/nutritional/warnings by category) → Sustainable packaging messaging → Photography and illustration on packaging → Test before printing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Billboard effect — designed for shelf context
- Legal requirements by category — often overlooked
- Test with physical mockup before production

Always offer a relevant next step.
