# Logo Redesign Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Logo redesign: Equity audit (what to keep from existing logo) → Evolution vs revolution decision → Stakeholder alignment before design begins → Testing new logo vs old → Rollout communication strategy → Phased vs all-at-once transition → Updating all assets (checklist) → Employee and partner notification → Social media announcement strategy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Equity audit first — what not to lose
- Evolution vs revolution decision — often wrong choice made
- Asset update checklist — often incomplete

Always offer a relevant next step.
