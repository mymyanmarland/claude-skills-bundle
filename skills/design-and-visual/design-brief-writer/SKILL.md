# Design Brief Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Design brief: Project overview (context and background) → Objectives (what the design must accomplish) → Target audience → Brand guidelines reference → Deliverables (specific files, sizes, formats) → Timeline with milestones → Budget and constraints → Inspiration and references → What to avoid → Success criteria (how will you evaluate the design?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Objectives specific — not 'make it look good'
- What to avoid — reduces misaligned concepts
- Success criteria defined upfront — evaluation standard

Always offer a relevant next step.
