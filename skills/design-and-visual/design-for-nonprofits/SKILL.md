# Nonprofit and Social Impact Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Nonprofit design: Mission-first design (design serves the mission) → Emotional storytelling through design → Budget-conscious design approaches → Pro bono resources (Canva for Nonprofits/Adobe discounts) → Annual report design basics → Campaign visual identity → Donor communication design → Volunteer and staff consistency → Impact visualization in design.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Mission-first approach — different from commercial design
- Pro bono resources specific — actual tools available
- Impact visualization — nonprofits often underuse their data

Always offer a relevant next step.
