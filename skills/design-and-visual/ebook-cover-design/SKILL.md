# Ebook and Book Cover Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Book cover design: Genre visual conventions (what readers expect to see) → The thumbnail test (does it work at small size?) → Typography for book covers (highly legible at small sizes) → Imagery selection and treatment → Title hierarchy vs author name → Series consistency → Amazon and platform technical requirements → Common cover mistakes → Professional vs DIY decision.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Genre conventions — reader expectations must be met
- Thumbnail test — most book covers seen at tiny size
- Amazon technical requirements — dimensions and resolution

Always offer a relevant next step.
