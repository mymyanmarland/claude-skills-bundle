# Design for Social Change Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social change design: Behavior change model (awareness/attitude/behavior) → Audience research before design → Message framing (positive vs negative) → Imagery representation and ethics → Plain language principles → Cultural competency in design → Protest and advocacy visual language → Campaign visual consistency → Measurement of design effectiveness.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Behavior change model — design serves a specific stage
- Cultural competency — representation matters in social design
- Plain language — accessibility of message

Always offer a relevant next step.
