# Event Branding and Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Event design: Event identity creation (separate from company brand or extension?) → Environmental design principles (signage/wayfinding/stage) → Digital vs print asset list → Attendee touchpoints (badge/program/stage graphics/social) → Presentation template for speakers → Pre-event social graphics → Post-event content templates → Vendor and print specifications.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Environmental design principles — physical space design
- Touchpoint complete list prevents gaps
- Speaker presentation template — often forgotten

Always offer a relevant next step.
