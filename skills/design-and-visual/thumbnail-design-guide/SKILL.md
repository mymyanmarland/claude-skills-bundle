# Thumbnail Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Thumbnail design: The curiosity-clarity balance (intriguing but not confusing) → Faces and emotion (highest click-through) → Text on thumbnails (maximum 3-5 words, large) → Color contrast for visibility in feed → Consistent style across channel → A/B testing thumbnails → Platform-specific sizes → Competitor thumbnail analysis → The scroll test (does it stand out in the feed?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Curiosity-clarity balance — both needed
- Faces and emotion — evidence-based highest performance
- Scroll test — view in context, not isolation

Always offer a relevant next step.
