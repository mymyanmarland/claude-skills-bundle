# Print Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Print design: CMYK vs RGB distinction (print vs screen) → Resolution requirements (300 DPI minimum) → Bleed and safe zone setup → Color proofing basics → Paper stock selection impact on design → Die-cut and specialty printing considerations → File format for print (PDF/print-ready) → Proofing checklist before submission → Common print design mistakes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- CMYK vs RGB — foundational print knowledge
- Bleed and safe zone setup specific
- Proofing checklist prevents expensive reprints

Always offer a relevant next step.
