# Design Trends Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Tools and skill level
- Specific challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Design trends guide: Trend lifecycle (emerging/peak/mainstream/dated) → Current macro trends (minimalism/maximalism/retro revival/AI aesthetics) → Trend relevance to specific brand → Timeless vs trend-driven design balance → When to adopt vs resist → Trend implementation without full rebrand → Monitoring sources for trends → Trend fatigue and distinctiveness.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Trend lifecycle — adopt at right stage not too early or late
- Brand relevance test — not all trends fit all brands
- Timeless vs trend balance — most brands need both

Always offer a relevant next step.
