# Menu and Print Collateral Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Menu and collateral design: Menu design psychology (eye tracking and placement) → Typography for menus (readable in low light) → The anchor and decoy effect in menu pricing → Brochure fold types and design flow → Trifold layout principles → Paper stock impact on brand perception → Photography and illustration in menus → Legal and regulatory required information → Print specifications.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Menu psychology — design affects what people order
- Typography for low light — restaurant specific
- Print specifications prevent costly mistakes

Always offer a relevant next step.
