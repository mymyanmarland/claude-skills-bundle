# Before and After Design Improvement Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Design improvement: Design audit (what is and is not working) → Typography improvements first (highest impact) → Color refinement → Spacing and layout corrections → Hierarchy clarification → Photography and imagery upgrade → Removing clutter → Alignment and grid application → The final test (does it solve the original communication goal?).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Typography first — highest visible impact
- Spacing correction — often transforms amateur to professional
- Final communication test — does it do its job?

Always offer a relevant next step.
