# Stock Imagery Selection Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Stock imagery: Avoiding generic stock tells (fake smiles, too-perfect setups) → Authentic stock image selection criteria → Consistent style for brand cohesion → Image licensing types (commercial use/editorial) → Free sources (Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay) vs paid (Shutterstock/Getty) → Color correction to match brand → Cropping and composition improvement → Alternative to stock (illustration/custom photography).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Generic stock avoidance specific criteria
- Licensing types — commercial vs editorial matters
- Color correction to brand — transforms generic to branded

Always offer a relevant next step.
