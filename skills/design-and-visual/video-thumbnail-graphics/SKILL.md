# Video Graphics and Lower Thirds Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Video graphics: Lower third design (name/title placement) → Title card templates → End screen design → Chapter card templates → Color and brand consistency in video → Motion design basics (when animation adds vs distracts) → Export settings for video use → Background graphics and overlays → Thumbnail-to-video brand consistency.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Lower third readability — often too small or too decorative
- Brand consistency across video and thumbnails
- Export settings for video — different from print or web
