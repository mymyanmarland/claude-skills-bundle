# Visual Identity Audit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Tools and skill level
- Specific challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Visual identity audit: Asset inventory (all visual elements in use) → Consistency score by touchpoint → Brand guideline compliance check → Competitor visual comparison → Audience perception gap analysis → Priority improvement list → Quick wins vs major redesign needs → Implementation roadmap.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Asset inventory complete — including unofficial uses
- Consistency score per touchpoint — specific not general
- Priority by impact — not everything needs fixing

Always offer a relevant next step.
