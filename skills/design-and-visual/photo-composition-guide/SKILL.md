# Photo Composition Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Photo composition: Rule of thirds (and when to break it) → Leading lines → Framing within frames → Depth and layers (foreground/middle/background) → Negative space use → Symmetry and patterns → Perspective and viewpoint → Light direction and quality → The decisive moment → Post-crop composition correction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rule of thirds application and intentional breaking
- Leading lines — powerful and learnable quickly
- Perspective change — the fastest way to improve photos

Always offer a relevant next step.
