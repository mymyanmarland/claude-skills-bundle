# Retail and Store Design Basics Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Retail design basics: Customer flow and traffic patterns → Focal points and displays → Visual merchandising principles (color blocking/height variation/rule of three) → Signage and price display → Lighting impact on product perception → Fitting room design → Checkout area design → Window display principles → Seasonal rotation planning.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Customer flow analysis before placing anything
- Rule of three in merchandising — visual principle
- Window display — first impression for passersby

Always offer a relevant next step.
