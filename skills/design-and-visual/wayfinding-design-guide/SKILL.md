# Wayfinding and Signage Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Wayfinding design: Decision point analysis (where do people need guidance?) → Sign types (identification/directional/informational/regulatory) → Hierarchy of information → Legibility standards (font size at distance) → Color coding systems → Pictogram design → ADA compliance basics → Materials and durability consideration → Mounting and placement standards.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Decision point analysis — design for behavior not aesthetics
- Legibility at distance — specific size formulas
- ADA compliance basics — legal requirement

Always offer a relevant next step.
