# Brand Identity Design Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Design context and purpose
- Target audience and platform
- Brand guidelines or style preferences
- Available tools and skill level
- Specific design challenge or goal

## Step 2 — Core Output

Brand identity: Brand strategy before design (who, what, why, for whom) → Visual identity components (logo/colors/typography/imagery/patterns) → Brand style guide creation → Application across touchpoints (business cards/website/social/packaging) → Primary and secondary color palette → Image style guidelines (photography direction) → Icon set and illustration style → Do and do not examples → Brand evolution vs rebrand.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Brand strategy before visual design — sequence critical
- Touchpoint application — theory into practice
- Do and do not examples most valuable part

Always offer a relevant next step.
