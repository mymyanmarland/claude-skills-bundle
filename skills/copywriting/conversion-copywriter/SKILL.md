# Conversion Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Conversion copy: Voice of customer research (exact words customers use) → Jobs-to-be-done framing → Feature vs benefit vs outcome hierarchy → Objection mapping and pre-emptive handling → Social proof placement by funnel stage → Micro-commitments (small yeses leading to big yes) → CTA copy beyond 'Buy Now' → Heatmap-informed copy placement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Voice of customer research before writing — use their words
- Objections pre-empted not ignored
- Micro-commitments — each yes makes the next easier

Always offer a relevant next step.
