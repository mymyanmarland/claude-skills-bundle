# Facebook and Instagram Ad Copy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Social ad copy: Pattern interrupt opening (first line stops the scroll) → Pain or desire hook → Solution bridging → Social proof element → Risk removal → Specific CTA → Copy length by objective (awareness longer/conversion shorter) → Emoji use for mobile → Comment-bait question variation → Video ad script adaptation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pattern interrupt first line — the scroll stopper
- Specific CTA — not 'learn more' when 'get 50% off today' is available
- Comment-bait variation drives algorithmic reach

Always offer a relevant next step.
