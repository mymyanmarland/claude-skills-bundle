# Long-Form Sales Page Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Long-form sales page: The promise (biggest transformation) → The story (why you, why this solution) → Proof stack (multiple forms of proof) → The offer breakdown → Value vs price comparison → Objection crusher section → Guarantee presentation → Bonus stack → Urgency mechanism → FAQ → Final CTA. Every section has one job.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Promise statement the transformation not the features
- Proof stack varies types — testimonials plus stats plus case studies
- Value vs price comparison done before price reveal

Always offer a relevant next step.
