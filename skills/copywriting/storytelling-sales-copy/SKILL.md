# Story-Based Sales Copy Writer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Story-based copy: The hero (your customer before) → The struggle (the problem they faced) → The catalyst (what forced change) → The discovery (your solution found) → The transformation (life after) → The invitation (reader can have this too) → Story authenticity markers → Transition from story to offer → Story length by medium.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Hero is the customer — not the founder
- Transformation specific and measurable
- Transition from story to offer must feel earned

Always offer a relevant next step.
