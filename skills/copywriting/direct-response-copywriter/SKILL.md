# Direct Response Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Direct response copy: AIDA framework (Attention/Interest/Desire/Action) → Headline hierarchy (outer envelope/headline/subhead) → The big idea (one compelling concept) → Proof elements (testimonials/statistics/demonstrations) → Risk reversal (guarantee language) → Urgency and scarcity mechanics → P.S. line (second most-read element) → Response mechanism clarity.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Big idea identification before any writing
- P.S. line the second most-read element — use it
- Guarantee language specific — reduces perceived risk

Always offer a relevant next step.
