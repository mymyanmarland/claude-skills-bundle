# Brand Voice Copywriter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Brand voice copy: Voice audit (existing copy analyzed) → Personality dimensions (sophisticated/playful/authoritative/friendly) → Vocabulary signature (words we use and avoid) → Sentence structure pattern → Cultural references and humor calibration → Platform voice adaptation (same voice, different tone) → Before/after examples → Voice consistency checklist.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Voice audit before writing — understand before mimicking
- Platform adaptation — same voice, appropriate register
- Consistency checklist — catches drift over time

Always offer a relevant next step.
