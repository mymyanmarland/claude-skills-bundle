# Headline Writing Master Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Headline mastery: The 4 U's framework (Urgent/Unique/Ultra-specific/Useful) → Formula library (How to/Number list/Question/Warning/Secret) → Emotional trigger identification → Curiosity gap construction → Power words by emotion (fear/greed/curiosity/approval) → A/B testing pairs → Platform-specific headline adaptation → Headline scoring before choosing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 4 U's applied to every headline before publishing
- Curiosity gap — the reader must want to know more
- Emotional trigger matched to audience motivation

Always offer a relevant next step.
