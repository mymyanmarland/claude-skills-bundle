# Email Subject Line Master Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Subject line mastery: Open rate psychology (self-interest/curiosity/social proof/urgency) → Character limit by device (40 chars mobile) → Preview text as second subject line → Personalization tokens effectively → Spam trigger word avoidance → Split test pairs (curiosity vs direct benefit) → Re-engagement subject lines → Subject line swipe file by category.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Preview text as second headline — often wasted
- Spam trigger words specific list — avoid deliverability damage
- Split test pairs genuinely different — not slight variations

Always offer a relevant next step.
