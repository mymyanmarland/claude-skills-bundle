# Copywriting Audit Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Product or service being sold
- Target audience and their primary pain
- Current copy if any to improve
- Tone and brand voice
- Conversion goal and traffic source

## Step 2 — Core Output

Copy audit: Above the fold assessment (value prop clear?) → Headline strength score → Clarity test (5-second test) → Benefit vs feature ratio → Proof element inventory → Objection coverage → CTA strength → Reading level check → Mobile experience → Competitor comparison. Priority fixes ranked by conversion impact.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Above the fold first — most visitors never scroll
- 5-second clarity test specific process
- Priority ranking by conversion impact — not all fixes equal

Always offer a relevant next step.
