# Socially Responsible Investing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

SRI guide: ESG criteria explanation (Environmental/Social/Governance) → Investment options (ESG funds/impact funds/negative screening/positive screening) → Performance comparison (evidence on ESG returns) → Cost considerations (ESG funds often higher expense ratios) → Greenwashing awareness → Divestment vs engagement approach → Values-aligned portfolio construction → How to research specific funds.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- ESG performance evidence honest — mixed results
- Greenwashing awareness — not all ESG funds are equal
- Cost comparison — expense ratio difference quantified

Always offer a relevant next step.
