# Financial Crisis Recovery Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crisis recovery: Immediate triage (what must be paid to prevent worst outcomes — shelter first) → Income emergency options → Creditor negotiation (most are willing to work with you) → Government assistance programs overview → Medical debt negotiation (negotiable more than most debt) → Bankruptcy basics (when it is the right tool) → Credit recovery after crisis → Emotional support alongside practical steps.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Triage prioritization — shelter before everything
- Creditor negotiation possible — people do not know this
- Emotional component — financial crisis is a trauma

Always offer a relevant next step.
