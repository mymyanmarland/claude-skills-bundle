# Real Estate Investment Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Real estate investing: Investment types (primary residence/rental/REITs/syndications) → Rental property math (cap rate/cash-on-cash return/1% rule) → Financing strategies → Landlord responsibilities and risks → Property management options → REIT alternative (liquidity, diversification, no landlord hassle) → Market selection factors → When real estate makes sense in portfolio.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rental math formulas specific — 1% rule, cap rate
- REIT as alternative — often overlooked
- Landlord responsibilities honest — not passive income

Always offer a relevant next step.
