# Investment Portfolio Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Portfolio building: Risk tolerance assessment (not just age — actual loss tolerance) → Time horizon impact on allocation → Asset allocation options (aggressive/moderate/conservative) → Three-fund portfolio approach (US stocks/international/bonds) → Rebalancing strategy (when and how) → Tax-efficient fund placement → Portfolio review cadence → Behavioral investing pitfalls → Low-cost fund selection.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Risk tolerance assessment honest — beyond age-based
- Three-fund portfolio simple and effective
- Behavioral pitfalls — where most investors lose money

Always offer a relevant next step.
