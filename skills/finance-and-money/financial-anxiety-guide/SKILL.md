# Financial Anxiety Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial anxiety: Normalization (money is the top stressor for most people) → Avoidance patterns and costs → Financial anxiety vs financial reality distinction → Knowledge as anxiety reduction (the unknown is worse) → Gradual exposure (open the account, look at the number) → Control focus (what can you actually affect?) → Financial progress reframing → Professional support consideration.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Avoidance costs made explicit — avoidance makes things worse
- Gradual exposure — start small, open one account
- Knowledge reduces anxiety — the unknown is worse

Always offer a relevant next step.
