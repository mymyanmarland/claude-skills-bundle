# Student Loan Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Student loan guide: Loan inventory (all loans, rates, servicers) → Federal vs private loan distinction → Repayment plan options (standard/income-driven/graduated) → Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) eligibility → Income-driven repayment forgiveness → Refinancing (when it helps vs costs PSLF) → Accelerated payoff strategy → Employer student loan benefit check.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Federal vs private distinction — different rules and options
- PSLF eligibility check — many qualify without knowing
- Refinancing warning for PSLF seekers — common costly mistake

Always offer a relevant next step.
