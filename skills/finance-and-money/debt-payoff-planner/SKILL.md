# Debt Payoff Planner Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Debt payoff plan: Complete debt inventory (all debts, rates, minimums, balances) → Method comparison (snowball: smallest balance first for motivation / avalanche: highest rate first for math / hybrid) → Extra payment allocation → Payoff timeline per method → Interest saved calculation → Motivation milestones → What to do after each debt is paid → Avoiding new debt during payoff.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Complete debt inventory before strategy selection
- Method choice based on personality — both work if sustained
- Interest saved calculation creates motivation

Always offer a relevant next step.
