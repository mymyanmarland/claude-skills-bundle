# Passive Income Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Passive income guide: Passive income reality check (most requires significant upfront work) → Categories (dividend investing/rental income/digital products/licensing/affiliate marketing) → Effort-to-return analysis per category → Starting capital or skill requirements → Timeline to meaningful income → Tax implications of each stream → Diversification across income types → The passive income stack.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Upfront work reality acknowledged — not get-rich-quick
- Effort-to-return honest per category
- Timeline to meaningful income realistic

Always offer a relevant next step.
