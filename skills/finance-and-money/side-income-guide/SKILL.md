# Side Income Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Side income guide: Skills inventory (what can you sell?) → Time audit (when is the time?) → Income goal clarity ($200/month vs $2000/month very different strategies) → Platform selection by skill type → Pricing for side income → Tax implications of side income (quarterly estimated taxes) → When to formalize → Scaling decisions → Side income vs main income balance.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Skills-based matching — not generic side hustle list
- Quarterly tax reminder — common side income mistake
- Scaling decisions addressed — not just getting started

Always offer a relevant next step.
