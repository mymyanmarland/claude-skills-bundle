# Pension vs 401k Decision Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Pension vs 401k: Defined benefit vs defined contribution mechanics → Pension calculation (years of service x salary x multiplier) → Break-even age analysis → Portability comparison → Survivor benefit options → Pension lump sum vs annuity decision → Job mobility implications → Hybrid approach when available → Risk distribution (employer vs employee).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Break-even age calculation specific
- Survivor benefit options — often overlooked
- Portability — critical for career changers

Always offer a relevant next step.
