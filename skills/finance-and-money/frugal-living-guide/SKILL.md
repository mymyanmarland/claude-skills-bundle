# Frugal Living Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Frugal living: Spending audit (where does money actually go?) → Value-based spending (spend on what matters, cut what does not) → Big three optimization first (housing/transportation/food) → Frugality quick wins (subscriptions/insurance/utilities) → Mindful consumption habits → The frugality spectrum (extreme vs sustainable) → Social pressure management → Frugality as freedom framing.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Big three housing/transport/food — 70% of most budgets
- Value-based frugality — not equal cuts everywhere
- Social pressure management — often overlooked

Always offer a relevant next step.
