# Housing Cost Reduction Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Housing cost reduction: Refinancing analysis (break-even calculation) → Mortgage payment reduction strategies → Property tax appeal process → Insurance shopping → HOA fee negotiation (yes it is possible) → Renting a room or ADU → House hacking overview → Rent negotiation → Downsize analysis → Relocation for cost reduction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Break-even calculation for refinancing — specific formula
- Property tax appeal — most homeowners never try this
- House hacking — offset housing cost entirely for some

Always offer a relevant next step.
