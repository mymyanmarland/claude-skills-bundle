# Tax-Loss Harvesting Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tax-loss harvesting: What it is (selling losers to offset gains) → Wash sale rule (critical — 30 days before/after) → Short-term vs long-term gains distinction → When it makes sense (taxable accounts only) → How to do it manually vs automated → Net benefit calculation → Harvesting vs holding decision → Tax-gain harvesting in low-income years.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Wash sale rule prominently explained — most common mistake
- Taxable accounts only — not in 401k/IRA
- Net benefit calculation — not always worth the effort

Always offer a relevant next step.
