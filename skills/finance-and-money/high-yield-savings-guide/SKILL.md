# High-Yield Savings Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

HY savings guide: HYSA vs traditional savings (rate comparison) → Online banks vs traditional (why online pays more) → FDIC insurance and limits → Account selection criteria (rate/features/minimums) → Ladder strategy for CDs → Money market accounts comparison → I-bonds consideration → Treasury bills for larger cash holdings → When to move money out of savings into investments.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Rate comparison quantified — the opportunity cost is real
- FDIC limit awareness for large balances
- Treasury bills — higher yield than most HYSAs

Always offer a relevant next step.
