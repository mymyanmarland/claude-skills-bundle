# Charitable Giving Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Charitable giving: Values-based cause selection → Charity evaluation (effective altruism principles vs personal connection) → Giving percentage goal → Tax efficiency (timing/DAF/appreciated stock) → Donor-Advised Fund for bunching strategy → Recurring vs lump sum → Volunteering as giving → Impact measurement → Giving record keeping for taxes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Values-based cause selection — personal connection matters
- DAF bunching strategy — significant tax benefit
- Impact measurement — giving more intentionally

Always offer a relevant next step.
