# Healthcare Finance Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Healthcare finance: Health insurance plan comparison (premium vs deductible trade-off) → HSA eligibility and benefits (triple tax advantage) → HSA as stealth retirement account → HDHP vs traditional plan break-even analysis → Negotiating medical bills → GoodRx for prescriptions → Free preventive care maximization → FSA vs HSA decision → COBRA vs marketplace comparison.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Triple tax advantage of HSA explained
- HSA as retirement account — often not used this way
- Medical bill negotiation — 30-40% reduction possible

Always offer a relevant next step.
