# Investing for Beginners Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Investing guide: Why invest (compound growth, inflation protection) → Investment account types (401k/IRA/brokerage — tax implications) → Asset classes overview (stocks/bonds/real estate/cash) → Index funds vs active management (evidence for index) → Asset allocation basics (by age/risk tolerance) → Dollar-cost averaging → First investment steps → Common beginner mistakes.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Index funds evidence explained — not just asserted
- Account type tax implications practical
- Common beginner mistakes prevent costly errors

Always offer a relevant next step.
