# Annual Financial Checkup Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Annual financial checkup: Net worth calculation (year-over-year) → Budget vs actual spending → Investment performance and rebalancing → Insurance coverage review → Tax planning (October-December optimal window) → Estate documents check → Beneficiary designations review → Goal progress assessment → Next year financial goals → One financial improvement to implement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Beneficiary designations — commonly outdated, high stakes
- October-December tax planning window specific
- One improvement focus — not overwhelming overhaul

Always offer a relevant next step.
