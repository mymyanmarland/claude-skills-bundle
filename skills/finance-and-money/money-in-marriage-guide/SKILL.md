# Money in Marriage and Partnership Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Couples money guide: Money conversation starter (values, history, goals) → Financial system options (fully joint/fully separate/hybrid) → Each option pros and cons → Income disparity handling → Spending autonomy preservation → Bill splitting methods → Financial meeting cadence → Differing risk tolerances → What to do when you financially disagree.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Money conversation before system decision
- Spending autonomy preservation — eliminates allowance feeling
- Financial disagreement protocol — not just preventing arguments

Always offer a relevant next step.
