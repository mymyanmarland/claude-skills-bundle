# Common Financial Mistakes Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial mistakes guide: 20s mistakes (lifestyle inflation/no emergency fund/ignoring retirement) → 30s mistakes (over-housing/under-insuring/neglecting estate planning) → 40s mistakes (helping kids vs securing retirement/peak spending) → 50s mistakes (too conservative/Social Security early claiming) → Universal mistakes (timing the market/emotional investing/no estate documents/ignoring tax-advantaged accounts).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age-specific mistakes — different for different stages
- Quantified cost of each mistake where possible
- Universal mistakes relevant to everyone

Always offer a relevant next step.
