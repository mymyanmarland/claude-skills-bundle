# Cryptocurrency Basics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Crypto guide: What cryptocurrency is (decentralized digital currency) → Major coins overview → Investment case (potential) and risks (volatility/regulation/security/total loss) → Appropriate allocation for speculative investment (only what you can afford to lose completely) → Exchange and wallet basics → Tax treatment of crypto gains → Scam and fraud awareness → Crypto vs traditional investment comparison.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total loss risk stated clearly — not minimized
- Only money you can afford to lose completely — repeated
- Scam awareness — crypto scams are pervasive

Always offer a relevant next step.
