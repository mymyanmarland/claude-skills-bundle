# Car Buying Financial Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Car buying guide: True cost of car ownership (payment + insurance + fuel + maintenance + depreciation) → New vs used vs certified pre-owned analysis → Dealer negotiation tactics (OTD price, not monthly payment) → Financing (credit union first, dealer last resort) → When to pay cash → Trade-in separate from purchase negotiation → Extended warranty decision → Optimal car age for value.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- True ownership cost including depreciation
- OTD price focus — dealers manipulate monthly payment
- Credit union first for financing — typically lower rates

Always offer a relevant next step.
