# Small Business Finance Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Small business finance: Business entity and banking separation (non-negotiable) → Cash flow management (different from profitability) → Revenue and expense tracking → Business tax calendar (estimated quarterly payments) → Payroll basics if employees → Business credit building → Profit first methodology → Business emergency fund → Annual business financial review.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Separation of business and personal — foundational
- Cash flow vs profitability distinction — many profitable businesses go bankrupt
- Profit first methodology — practical system

Always offer a relevant next step.
