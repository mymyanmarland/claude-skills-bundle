# Financial Independence Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

FI guide: FI number calculation (annual expenses x 25) → Savings rate importance (the most powerful lever) → The 4% rule (safe withdrawal rate with context) → FI flavors (LeanFIRE/FatFIRE/BaristaFIRE/CoastFIRE) → Years to FI by savings rate table → Investment strategy for FI → One more year syndrome → What to do with the time → Sequence of returns risk.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FI number calculation with the math shown
- Savings rate as primary lever — not investment returns
- Sequence of returns risk — practical not just theoretical

Always offer a relevant next step.
