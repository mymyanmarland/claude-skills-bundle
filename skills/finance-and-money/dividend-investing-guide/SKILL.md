# Dividend Investing Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Dividend investing: Total return vs income approach (dividend investing is not free money) → Dividend yield vs dividend growth distinction → Payout ratio analysis → Dividend aristocrats and kings → DRIP (dividend reinvestment) → Tax treatment (qualified vs ordinary) → Sector concentration risk in dividend portfolios → REITs for dividend income → Building a dividend portfolio.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total return framing — dividends are not free money
- Dividend growth vs high yield tradeoff explained
- Tax treatment distinction — qualified dividends taxed lower

Always offer a relevant next step.
