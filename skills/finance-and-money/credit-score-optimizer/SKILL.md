# Credit Score Optimizer Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Credit optimization: Score factors (payment history 35%/utilization 30%/length 15%/mix 10%/new credit 10%) → Current score analysis → Quick wins (utilization reduction, error disputes) → Medium-term actions (payment history building, age of accounts) → Credit monitoring setup → Score tracking milestones → What not to do (closing old accounts, opening many new ones) → Authorized user strategy.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Factor percentages with specific actions per factor
- Quick wins identified — immediate confidence
- What not to do — common mistakes that hurt scores

Always offer a relevant next step.
