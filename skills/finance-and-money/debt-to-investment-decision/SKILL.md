# Debt vs Investment Decision Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Debt vs invest guide: The math (guaranteed return of debt payoff vs expected investment return) → Interest rate threshold (generally above 6-7% pay off debt / below consider investing) → Psychological factor (debt anxiety has real value) → Emergency fund first (non-negotiable) → Employer match always (free money) → Tax-advantaged space (next priority) → High-interest debt (after match and tax-advantaged) → The hybrid approach.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Emergency fund and match first — non-negotiable
- Interest rate threshold explained with math
- Psychological factor legitimized — math is not everything

Always offer a relevant next step.
