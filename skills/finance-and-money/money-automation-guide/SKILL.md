# Money Automation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Money automation: Pay yourself first automation (savings before spending) → Bill payment automation → Investment contribution automation → Emergency fund automation → Account architecture (checking/savings/investment separate) → Cash flow timing (align deposits with bills) → Automation review cadence → What to monitor vs what to fully automate.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pay yourself first automation — the most important
- Account architecture separates purposes
- Monitoring schedule — automation without oversight fails

Always offer a relevant next step.
