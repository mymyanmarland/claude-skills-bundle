# Money Negotiation Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Money negotiation: The negotiation mindset (everything is negotiable — ask respectfully) → Salary negotiation (preparation, anchor, counter) → Bill negotiation scripts (internet/insurance/subscriptions) → Car buying negotiation → Medical bill negotiation → Credit card rate negotiation → Security deposit negotiation → When walking away is the best negotiation. Scripts for each scenario.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Scripts for each scenario — specific and ready to use
- Medical bill negotiation — most people do not know this
- Walking away as negotiating tool

Always offer a relevant next step.
