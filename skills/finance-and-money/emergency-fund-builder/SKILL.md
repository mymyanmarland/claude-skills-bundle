# Emergency Fund Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Emergency fund plan: Target calculation (3-6 months expenses — higher for variable income) → Current shortfall → Monthly savings target → High-yield savings account selection → Automation setup → What qualifies as an emergency (specific criteria) → Replenishment plan after use → Separate from investing — purpose-specific account.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 3-6 months specific — variable income needs more
- Automation removes willpower from saving
- What counts as emergency — prevents misuse

Always offer a relevant next step.
