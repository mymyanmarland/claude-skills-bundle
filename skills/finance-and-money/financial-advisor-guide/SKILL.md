# Finding a Financial Advisor Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial advisor guide: Fee structures (fee-only/fee-based/commission — conflicts of interest) → Fiduciary standard (required to act in your interest vs suitability standard) → CFP vs other designations → Where to find fee-only advisors (NAPFA/XYPN) → Questions to ask before hiring → Red flags → Robo-advisor alternative → When DIY is sufficient vs when advisor adds value.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Fiduciary vs suitability distinction — critical consumer knowledge
- Fee structures and conflicts of interest transparent
- Red flags specific — protects from bad advisors

Always offer a relevant next step.
