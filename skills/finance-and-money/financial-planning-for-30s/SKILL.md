# Financial Planning by Decade Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Decade financial guide: 20s priorities (habits/emergency fund/debt/early investing) → 30s priorities (career income growth/family financial planning/home/accelerate retirement) → 40s priorities (peak earning/catch-up contributions/college/mortgage payoff timeline) → 50s priorities (retirement focus/healthcare planning/estate) → 60s+ (retirement transition/Social Security/distribution strategy) → Each decade: 3 non-negotiable actions.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Non-negotiable actions per decade — actionable
- Decade framing creates urgency at each stage
- Different priorities — not same advice for all ages

Always offer a relevant next step.
