# Housing Affordability Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Housing affordability: Income-based guidelines (housing under 28-30% of gross income) → Total housing cost calculation (PITI + maintenance + HOA) → Rent vs buy calculator inputs → Location cost comparison → Downsizing analysis → Roommate financial impact → Hidden rental costs → Geographic arbitrage opportunity → Housing as investment vs home distinction.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Total housing cost — not just mortgage/rent
- Rent vs buy honest — not always better to buy
- Geographic arbitrage — housing cost variance is huge

Always offer a relevant next step.
