# Teaching Kids About Money Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Kids money guide: Age-appropriate concepts (preschool through teen) → Allowance philosophy and structure → Three-jar system (spend/save/give) → First bank account timing → Earning beyond allowance → Investing concepts for teens (compound interest demonstration) → Credit card education before they get one → Common money mistakes to prevent by teaching early.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Age stages specific — different for 6 vs 14 vs 17
- Three-jar system simple and immediately actionable
- Credit card education proactive — before not after

Always offer a relevant next step.
