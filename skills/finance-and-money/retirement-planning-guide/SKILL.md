# Retirement Planning Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Retirement plan: Retirement number calculation (25x annual expenses rule) → Current savings gap → Required monthly contribution → Account priority (401k match first/IRA second/brokerage third) → Investment allocation by time horizon → Catch-up contribution options (50+) → Social Security optimization basics → Healthcare in retirement → Withdrawal strategy basics.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Retirement number calculation specific — 25x rule with context
- Account priority order with tax reasoning
- Social Security optimization — often ignored early

Always offer a relevant next step.
