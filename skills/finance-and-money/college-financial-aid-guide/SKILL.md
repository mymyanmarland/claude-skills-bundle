# College Financial Aid Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial aid guide: FAFSA completion and timing (file early) → EFC calculation and what drives it → Asset positioning for financial aid (what counts) → CSS Profile requirements → Scholarship strategy (local vs national) → Merit aid negotiation → Appeal process → Community college transfer path → Employer tuition reimbursement.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- FAFSA timing — early filers get more
- Asset positioning legal strategies
- Appeal process — most people do not know you can appeal

Always offer a relevant next step.
