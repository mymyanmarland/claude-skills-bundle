# College Savings Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

College savings: Current college cost and projection → Savings target → 529 plan advantages (tax-free growth, state deductions) → Contribution strategy (lump sum vs monthly) → Investment options within 529 → Non-529 alternatives (ESA/UGMA/Roth IRA) → Financial aid impact of each account type → Overfunding risk → What happens to unused funds.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- 529 tax advantages specific — not just mentioned
- Financial aid impact by account type — important tradeoff
- Overfunding risk and solutions addressed

Always offer a relevant next step.
