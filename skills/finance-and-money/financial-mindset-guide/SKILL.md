# Financial Mindset Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial mindset: Money story identification (what did you learn about money growing up?) → Limiting money beliefs → Scarcity vs abundance mindset in practice → Emotional spending triggers → Financial avoidance patterns → Money and self-worth untangling → Comparison and keeping up with the Joneses → The enough point → Values-aligned spending.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Money story — the root of most financial behavior
- Emotional spending triggers identified specifically
- Enough point — often missing from financial planning

Always offer a relevant next step.
