# Personal Budget Builder Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Budget building: Income mapping (all sources, net) → Fixed vs variable expenses → Zero-based budgeting (every dollar assigned) → 50/30/20 rule vs custom allocation → Budget categories and limits → Tracking method (app/spreadsheet/envelope) → Weekly budget check-in → Monthly review and adjustment → Overspending response protocol.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Zero-based budgeting — every dollar has a job
- Tracking method matched to personality and preference
- Overspending response non-punitive — adjustment not shame

Always offer a relevant next step.
