# Net Worth Tracker Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Net worth tracking: Assets inventory (all accounts, property, valuables) → Liabilities inventory (all debts) → Net worth calculation → Tracking frequency (monthly) → Trend analysis (direction more important than number) → Benchmarking (meaningless vs peers — only vs past self) → Milestone celebrations → What moves the needle most.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Assets and liabilities comprehensive — not just savings
- Trend direction more important than absolute number
- Benchmarking against self only — comparison kills progress

Always offer a relevant next step.
