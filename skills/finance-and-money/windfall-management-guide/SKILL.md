# Windfall Management Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Windfall guide: The pause rule (do nothing for 6-12 months with large windfalls) → Tax implications first (work with CPA before decisions) → Emotional processing of unexpected money → Prioritization framework (debts/emergency fund/taxes/investments) → Relative and friend requests management scripts → Lifestyle inflation awareness → Investment of windfall (lump sum vs dollar cost averaging research) → Estate and giving considerations.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Pause rule first — impulse decisions costly
- Tax implications before any action
- Relative requests script — common windfall destroyer

Always offer a relevant next step.
