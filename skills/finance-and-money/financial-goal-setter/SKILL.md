# Financial Goal Setter Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Financial goal setting: Goal inventory (all financial goals) → Prioritization (by timeline and importance) → Sinking fund calculation (goal amount / months to goal) → Goal account setup (separate accounts by goal) → Automation per goal → Trade-off decisions (when goals compete) → Goal review cadence → Celebrating milestones.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Sinking fund calculation specific for each goal
- Separate accounts per goal — psychological clarity
- Trade-off decisions when goals compete — honest prioritization

Always offer a relevant next step.
