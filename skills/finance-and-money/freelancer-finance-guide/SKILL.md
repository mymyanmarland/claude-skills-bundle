# Freelancer and Self-Employed Finance Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Freelancer finance: Irregular income budgeting (pay yourself a salary from business account) → Tax set-aside (25-30% self-employment) → Quarterly estimated tax payments → Business vs personal expense separation → Retirement options (Solo 401k/SEP IRA/SIMPLE IRA) → Business banking setup → Invoice and cash flow management → Business emergency fund (separate from personal).

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Salary from business account — solves irregular income
- 25-30% tax set-aside — common freelancer mistake
- Solo 401k for self-employed — highest contribution limits

Always offer a relevant next step.
