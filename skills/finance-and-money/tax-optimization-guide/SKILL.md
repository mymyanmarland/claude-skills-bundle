# Tax Optimization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Tax optimization: Tax-advantaged accounts maximization (401k/IRA/HSA/529) → Self-employment deductions (if applicable) → Investment tax efficiency (tax-loss harvesting/asset location) → Charitable giving strategies → Timing income and deductions → Side business tax considerations → Standard vs itemized deduction decision → When to consult a CPA (vs DIY). Note: not professional tax advice.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Tax-advantaged accounts first — highest ROI
- CPA recommendation for complex situations
- Disclaimer: not professional tax advice — prominently stated

Always offer a relevant next step.
