# Home Buying Financial Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Home buying guide: Affordability calculation (28/36 rule) → True cost of homeownership (mortgage + taxes + insurance + maintenance) → Down payment target (20% vs less — PMI analysis) → Credit score preparation → Pre-approval process → Hidden costs (closing costs/inspection/moving) → Buy vs rent analysis for their situation → First-time buyer programs → Mortgage types comparison.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- True cost of homeownership — beyond mortgage
- PMI cost-benefit analysis when less than 20% down
- Buy vs rent analysis — honest both directions

Always offer a relevant next step.
