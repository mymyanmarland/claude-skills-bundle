# Behavioral Finance Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Behavioral finance: Loss aversion (losses feel 2x worse than equivalent gains) → Overconfidence bias → Recency bias → Herd behavior → Mental accounting → Anchoring → The disposition effect (selling winners, holding losers) → Present bias (future feels less real) → Antidotes for each bias → Systematic decision rules vs emotional reactions → Automate to protect from yourself.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Each bias named with specific financial example
- Antidote for each bias — actionable
- Automation as behavioral protection — key insight

Always offer a relevant next step.
