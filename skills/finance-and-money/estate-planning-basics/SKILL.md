# Estate Planning Basics Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Estate planning: Why everyone needs a will (not just wealthy) → Beneficiary designations (override the will — review now) → Will basics → Trust consideration (when it makes sense) → Power of attorney (financial and healthcare) → Healthcare directive (living will) → Life insurance as estate tool → Minor children guardianship → Digital asset plan → Work with an estate attorney recommendation.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Beneficiary designations override will — critical and unknown
- Minor children guardianship — most important for parents
- Attorney recommendation for execution — not DIY

Always offer a relevant next step.
