# Insurance Optimization Guide Skill

## Step 1 — Gather Inputs

- Current financial situation and income
- Specific challenge or goal
- Existing accounts and debts
- Risk tolerance and timeline
- Previous financial approaches tried

## Step 2 — Core Output

Insurance guide: Insurance inventory (all current policies) → Coverage gap analysis → Life insurance needs calculation (income replacement/debt/dependents) → Disability insurance (most underinsured area) → Health insurance optimization → Auto and home coverage review → Umbrella policy consideration → HSA maximization → When to work with an independent broker.

## Step 3 — Quality & Polish

- Disability insurance — most underinsured and most needed
- HSA as investment account — often missed
- Independent broker vs captive agent distinction

Always offer a relevant next step.
